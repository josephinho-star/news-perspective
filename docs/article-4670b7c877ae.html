Taylor Rooks makes rare comments about marriage as she rises in media world

2026-10-13 20:13

Sports broadcaster Taylor Rooks was rarely candid about her July 2025 wedding with husband, Shane Fowler.

The NBA on Prime host and the lawyer were married in a no-phones wedding ceremony with roughly 190 guests — including NBA stars Kevin Durant, Donovan Mitchell and Draymond Green — at Gotham Hall in New York City.

During Front Office Sports’s “Tuned In” summit in New York on Tuesday, Rooks explained that she won’t use her personal life for social media content, despite having a front-facing career.

“I think something that has been really big for me is that I believe in the true separation of church and state,” Rooks, 34, said. “I don’t think the entire world has to know everything about my relationship. That is how I have always been. I think you have to let your private things be private things and you have to let yourself have things that just belong to you.

“I don’t necessarily see my relationship as content… and I don’t knock you if you do. That just really wasn’t — that’s not my ministry. I think that is something that helps too because everybody has their opinions, everybody has things to say. But I feel very blessed and fortunate for the partner that I have.”

Rooks and Fowler kept their relationship and engagement private — and shocked many when they announced their union in a profile with GQ

“He allows me to be myself,” Rooks said at the “Tuned In” summit. “I don’t feel bad if I have to get on a plane and go do this interview because he understands my work — the same way I understand his.

“He is a lawyer. He has an incredibly demanding job. We fell in love with one another because of who we are and neither of us wants to hold the other person back.”

The pair met while Fowler was in his final year at Harvard Law School.

He proposed by orchestrating a scavenger hunt across New York City that concluded with a private dinner at Fini, their favorite pizza spot, Rooks previously told GQ .

Rooks does player features on “Thursday Night Football” on Prime Video.

She also serves as a Board of Advisors member and strategic partner for Boardroom, the sports, media, and entertainment brand co-founded by Kevin Durant and Rich Kleiman.
