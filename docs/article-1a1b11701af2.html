Kevin Durant dishes on Steelers coach Mike McCarthy’s alleged ice cream snack habits after making NBA history

2026-11-26 20:21

Aaron Rodgers’ time with the Pittsburgh Steelers ended abruptly just one day before Thanksgiving. The team cut ties with the veteran quarterback shortly after benching him and following his disparaging comments about coach Mike McCarthy during an appearance on "The Pat McAfee Show" on Wednesday.

Rodgers, who was in his second season with the Steelers, made a series of allegations questioning McCarthy’s focus and work ethic. He claimed the coach had been caught "snoring" during team meetings and recently inadvertently ended a speech with "one, two, three, Packers."

Another allegation came from former Steelers defensive lineman Breiden Fehoko, who claimed that a Steelers player sent him a message reading: "McCarthy has a personal assistant who brings him ice cream sandwiches on demand."

Several sports figures have weighed in on Rodgers’ abrupt departure, with NBA star Kevin Durant among the latest to comment.

"Happy Thanksgiving, hiring a ice cream sandwich assistant for the day, great idea Mike," the Houston Rockets forward wrote on X on Thanksgiving morning.

Durant's post surfaced hours after a 3-pointer in the first half of the Rockets game against the Phoenix Suns on Wednesday, made him the fifth NBA player to score 33,000 career points. Houston went to defeat Durant's former team 116-104, with Durant ending the game with 20 points.

McCarthy addressed the allegations raised by Rodgers, telling reporters the team's front office was ""very in tune with everything that goes on in football operations as far as my job responsibility."

Rodgers acknowledged that his time with the Steelers was over, saying, "That ship has sailed."

Mason Rudolph is expected to start when the Black Friday game against the Denver Broncos kicks off. Rodgers suggested he was unaware he wouldn't be the starter going forward until a teammate alerted him.

Steelers owner and president Art Rooney II responded with a show of support for McCarthy, telling the Post-Gazette that he "expects" McCarthy to "be our coach this season, next season and beyond."

Rodgers expected to enter the waiver wire

The NFL's trade deadline for this season had already passed by the time Rodgers was released, meaning the four-time league MVP will have to go through waivers before another potentially interested team claims him. If Rodgers makes it through waivers unclaimed, he would then be an unrestricted free agent.

Durant addresses confrontation with Dillon Brooks

The Rockets-Suns game also managed to produce some holiday fireworks, as Durant and Dillon Brooks got wrapped up in a heated verbal back-and-forth. While players jawing back and forth isn't a new phenomenon, the exchange between Durant and Brooks raised eyebrows because it happened during a live play.

Durant untucked his Rockets jersey and pushed his forehead up against Brooks' forehead. Referees whistled for double technical fouls, with Brooks leaving the game due to a technical foul he received a few minutes prior to the confrontation with Durant.

Durant address the confrontation after the game, telling reporters, "I mean, I just love basketball. That simple."

"I love to play ball. I love everything about the game, all the details that add up to winning. I care about that and I don't mind if somebody got a grievance with me. I don't mind addressing it right there in the moment. I'm sure he has some. I said some things that he probably didn't like, and he addressed it right there. And I can respect that as a man. And it didn't get farther than that. But if it does get farther than that, I'll be ready for that, too."
