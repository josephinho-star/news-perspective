Alabama Barker accuses ‘sheisty’ friends of stealing $130K worth of valuables — including $50K Birkin and $20K jeans

2026-11-13 13:31

Alabama Barker has accused her “sheisty” friends of stealing more than $130,000 worth of her possessions, and is now calling for the FBI to help her regain ownership of the luxury items.

The 20-year-old daughter of Blink-182 drummer Travis Barker and former model Shanna Moakler took to her TikTok Stories to slam her former friends — while revealing a lengthy list of the pieces she claims they stole, including a black Hermès Birkin bag, a Chrome Hearts stool and several pairs of Chrome Hearts jeans.

“So my black Birkin just happened to end up in a BMW, the same BMW that you drove to my house every time that you came,” Alabama, whose stepmother is reality star Kourtney Kardashian, said, referring to one of the alleged thieves, whom she has not named.

“And his best friend is also a f–king thief, but we’re not even gonna talk about him,” she continued.

She went on to label the two as the “thieves of LA.”

“They steal from people, they’re f–king bums and they flex your money in your face that they have already sold your stuff with. All of my valuables are gone.”

“I can’t even believe it,” she added. “I took care of them like they were like my family. You knew I didn’t have cameras and you’re sheisty, and you took everything from me.”

The influencer then provided screenshots of her friends seemingly trying to sell several pairs of her Chrome Hearts jeans. The pants, notably, resell for around $20,000 a pair.

In a TikTok live, Alabama revealed that she took her complaint to the FBI, but has allegedly “gotten no help.”
