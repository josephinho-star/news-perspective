Women's Feast Week guide: Iowa's 1-2 punch puts away Utah

2026-11-23 23:04

BYU, North Dakota State, Utah and Texas A&M were the last four teams left out of the NCAA tournament in March. Besides the disappointment of missing the field, the four also had something else in common: They lost a key game in November.

For the Cougars, Utes and Aggies, those losses came at Thanksgiving neutral site events. The Bison lost to Nebraska a week earlier, and the Cornhuskers were one of the last teams to make the 2026 NCAA tournament.

November matters. Feast Week matters.

This year's lineup does not include the Players Era event in Las Vegas; it has been expanded to six teams and moved to the week before Christmas. But that doesn't diminish the quality and quantity of Feast Week games that stretch from Puerto Rico to Cancun and from the Bahamas and back to Florida, Texas and California.

Just like last year, many of these contests will have far-reaching implications into March and might be the difference between being in or out of the 2027 NCAA tournament field.

Let this be your guide to the best games, where to find them and what's at stake. And come back after the games are played for more analysis.

All times ET.

North Carolina Tar Heels 61, Texas Tech Lady Raiders 47

Freshman Kate Harpring was North Carolina's leading scorer over the Tar Heels' first four games, but with 39.5% shooting and 3.8 turnovers per game, her efficiency was lacking. Not anymore. After Thursday's 28-point performance in the WBCA Showcase in Kissimmee, Florida, Harpring has scored 58 points in her past two games on 45 shots. Harpring -- the highest-rated recruit in coach Courtney Banghart's tenure at North Carolina -- was the catalyst for the 3-2 Tar Heels at both ends of the court, adding five steals. Perhaps just as important, Harpring has cut down on her turnovers. After having 13 combined in losses to Oklahoma and South Carolina to open the season, Harpring had none against Texas Tech in a team-leading 34 minutes and has only two turnovers in North Carolina's past three games.

USC Trojans 78, Baylor Bears 69

JuJu Watkins' road to recovery appears to be complete. In her best performance since she returned from the knee injury that kept her out all of 2025-26, Watkins scored 36 points while battling a cold Friday to carry the Trojans to their first win this season over a Power 4 opponent. The Bears, led by Taliah Scott's 24 points, were a challenge, leading fourth-ranked USC by 11 after the first quarter. But Watkins, the 2025 national player of the year, scored 21 points in the second half and added 11 rebounds and a career-high six steals for the game, allowing the Trojans to finally put it away in the final two minutes.

North Carolina Tar Heels 78, Alabama Crimson Tide 64

This has been a résumé-building week for the Tar Heels (4-2), who have won four consecutive games after opening the season with back-to-back losses to South Carolina and Oklahoma. At the WBCA Showcase, North Carolina took care of business against Texas Tech on Thursday and Alabama on Saturday. At halftime against the Crimson Tide, the Tar Heels trailed by two before their 24-12 third-quarter advantage put them in control the rest of the way. Freshman star Kate Harpring had her third straight game with at least 20 points, including a 4-for-9 night from the 3-point arc. In their win streak, the Tar Heels have held every opponent to 64 points or fewer. Alabama (6-2), meanwhile, has no more résumé-building opportunities in its nonconference schedule. -- Alexa Philippou

Texas Longhorns 75, Ohio State Buckeyes 61

Even with 10 new players on the roster the formula remains the same for success at Texas. Vic Schaefer's teams always start with defense and rebounding. That's why the Longhorns were able to beat the first ranked team they have played this season despite shooting 35.8% from the field, committing 20 turnovers and star Madison Booker suffering through one of the worst games of her career. Breya Cunningham dominated the game in the post with 24 points and 13 rebounds to help make up for Booker's 3-of-13 shooting and seven turnovers. Ohio State's Jaloni Cambridge, one of the best point guards in the country, had a game-high 27 points but only four in the first half, when she was limited by foul trouble and Texas took control of the game.

South Dakota State Jackrabbits 77, Kansas Jayhawks 70

Perennial Summit League power South Dakota State has played its usual tough nonconference schedule this season, and it's paying off. On Sunday, the Jackrabbits beat the Big 12's Jayhawks at the 28.5 Hoops Invitational at T-Mobile Center in Kansas City, Missouri. The Jackrabbits, who have won 11 regular-season Summit League titles and 13 Summit tournament titles, have made the NCAA tournament 14 times, including the past four seasons.

This season, they previously played Creighton of the Big East (73-71 loss), Texas of the SEC (93-63 loss) and Oklahoma State of the Big 12 (90-80 loss). Those were tough defeats, but they helped South Dakota State be ready for Kansas. The Jackrabbits held the Jayhawks to 41% shooting from the field and 29% from 3-point range. Brooklyn Meyer, in her fifth season with South Dakota State, and Emilee Fox each had 21 points for the Jackrabbits.

"When you play really talented teams, the margin of error is incredibly small," longtime Jackrabbits coach Aaron Johnston said. "That's what helps us get better. You're forced to be at your best in everything you do. We don't shy away from the idea of challenging ourselves."

That will continue for the Jackrabbits, as they will still face Rutgers of the Big Ten and the SEC's Kentucky and Ole Miss. -- Michael Voepel

Iowa Hawkeyes 75, Utah Utes 66

Utah made this a closer game than expected, even taking the lead in the third and fourth quarters before the No. 8 Hawkeyes (6-1) pulled away late behind a 24-14 final frame. Iowa's one-two punch of Ava Heiden (26 points) and Dani Carnegie (27) was exceptional; they became the first Hawkeyes teammates to each score 25 points in a game since Caitlin Clark and Hannah Stuelke did so in the 2024 Big Ten tournament final. Lena Girardi, who this offseason transferred to Utah from Oklahoma State, recorded a career-high 30 points to help the Utes -- who suffered their first loss of the season -- make things interesting. Iowa will face Navy on Wednesday to conclude its time in the Bahamas, while Utah will take on Miami. -- Alexa Philippou

Oklahoma Sooners vs. Virginia Cavaliers

2:30 p.m.; Jacksonville Classic (Jacksonville, Florida)

The ACC/SEC Challenge isn't until the first week of December, so this serves as an appetizer. Virginia's Kymora Johnson and Aaliyah Chavez of Oklahoma are just two of the great guards at this event. Vanderbilt's Mikayla Blakes, Montana State's Taylee Chirrick and Oregon State's Jenna Villa will also be in Jacksonville for two games apiece.

Michigan Wolverines vs. Miami Hurricanes

4 p.m., BDG Live; Baha Mar Hoops Pink Flamingo (Nassau, The Bahamas)

Even just a good showing against the Wolverines would help the Hurricanes, who are bubble-team material this season. Miami guard Gal Raviv is off to a good start but will have Michigan's Syla Swords and Dallas Loughridge to contend with in a major step-up in competition.

Championship game, Emerald Coast Classic

2:30 p.m., FloHoops; (Raider Arena, Destin, Florida)

Michigan State (vs. Alcorn State) and Virginia Tech (vs. UT Martin) will be big favorites to win their openers and meet in the title game. With all of the talent in the Big Ten, the Spartans' Kennedy Blair doesn't garner much attention, but she is filling the stat sheet at the start of her junior season.

South Carolina Gamecocks vs. UConn Huskies

8 p.m., FS1; Hall of Fame Women's Showcase (Mohegan Sun Arena, Uncasville, Connecticut)

This could be the marquee nonconference game of the season, not to mention Feast Week. It's a Final Four rematch and also the first time Geno Auriemma and Dawn Staley will meet following the Gamecocks national semifinal win and their heated postgame exchange. Before the Huskies' losses to Michigan on Nov. 5 and Louisville on Nov. 15, these were considered the two undisputed best teams in the country. UConn is out to prove that still might be the case, and a Huskies win would erase the sting from those losses. UCLA and St. John's are the opener to this doubleheader.

Miami Hurricanes vs. Utah Utes

4 p.m., BDG Live; Baha Mar Hoops Pink Flamingo (Nassau, The Bahamas)

This is the featured matchup of the second day of the Pink Flamingo Championship and a game the NCAA tournament selection committee might look back upon when picking the last few teams in the field. Michigan vs. Utah and Navy vs. Iowa are the other games of the day.

Notre Dame Fighting Irish vs. West Virginia Mountaineers

11:30 a.m., ESPN2; Battle 4 Atlantis (Nassau, Bahamas)

In opening Battle 4 Atlantis games two days earlier, West Virginia will meet Florida and the Irish play Davidson, but this is the biggest game on the schedule in the fifth year of the event. West Virginia is annually one of the best defensive teams in the country and would like to play methodically on offense. The big question is whether Hannah Hidalgo, perhaps the nation's best individual defender, lets them.

Syracuse Orange vs. Wisconsin Badgers

1:30 p.m., FloHoops; Puerto Rico Shootout (San Juan, Puerto Rico)

The middle of three games in three days for the Orange in San Juan, this is one they will likely need to win as a team expected to live near the bubble all season. A quick turnaround awaits with Auburn up next less than 24 hours later.

Georgia Lady Bulldogs vs. Colorado Buffaloes

1:30 p.m., FloHoops; Cancun Challenge (Cancun, Mexico)

The highlight game of a five-game Thanksgiving Day in Cancun might also be the event's best overall matchup. The Lady Bulldogs and Buffaloes could be competing for one of the final spots in the NCAA tournament, adding major significance to this matchup. They each have to play Oregon later in the weekend, another result that could be pivotal for the postseason.

Oklahoma State Cowgirls vs. Washington Huskies

1:30 p.m., BDG Live; Coconut Hoops (Fort Myers, Florida)

Audi Crooks vs. Tilda Trygger, two of the best post player transfers this season. Liv McGill vs. Sayvia Sellers, two of the most underrated scoring guards in the country. Nene Ndiaye vs. Avery Howell, two of the nation's best 3-point shooters in the opening month. The individual matchups are almost as interesting as this meeting between two Top 25 teams.

USC Trojans vs. Saint Joseph's Hawks

1:30 p.m., BDG Live; Baha Mar Nassau Championship (Nassau, Bahamas)

The Hawks are the favorites in the Atlantic 10, but this will be one of the games to determine if they are an NCAA tournament team should they not win the conference. Illinois meets Marquette in the game preceding the Trojans-Hawks, giving ranked Big Ten teams a showcase in the Bahamas.

TCU Horned Frogs vs. Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets

1:30 p.m., ION; Fort Myers Tip-Off (Fort Myers, Florida)

This is a good opportunity to check out Yellow Jackets junior guard Talayah Walker, who has emerged as one of the ACC's best scorers, in what is sure to be a high-paced game against the Horned Frogs, one of the highest-scoring teams in the country.

Fairfield Stags vs. BYU Cougars

2:30 p.m., BallerTV; St. Pete Showcase (St. Petersburg, Florida)

Fairfield will meet North Dakota State the day before, but this matchup will be the big test. The Stags have been one of the most successful mid-majors over the past three seasons, with wins over South Florida, Villanova (twice), St. John's and Richmond in that time. They have also yet to stumble in the Metro Conference tournament, but a win here might allow for some wiggle room should that happen this March.

LSU Tigers vs. Villanova Wildcats

4:30 p.m., ION; Fort Myers Tip-Off (Fort Myers, Florida)

The third game of Friday's quadrupleheader is the best one. The Tigers' nonconference schedule is much better this season than in the recent past, and the Wildcats are the first of four NCAA tournament-level teams LSU will play pre-SEC. The Tigers have eight players averaging double-digit scoring, led by sophomore ZaKiyah Johnson, and will also be able to send waves of defenders at Villanova's Jasmine Bascoe, the leading scorer in the Big East last season.

Mississippi State Bulldogs vs. TCU Horned Frogs

11 a.m., ION; Fort Myers Tip-Off (Fort Myers, Florida)

The Bulldogs represent a second straight tricky opponent for TCU in Fort Myers. Forwards Madison Francis, a 6-2 sophomore, and Favour Nwaedozi, a 6-3 senior, bring size that the Horned Frogs have not seen yet this season.

Kentucky Wildcats vs. South Dakota State Jackrabbits

3:30 p.m., BallerTV; Daytona Beach Classic (Daytona Beach, Florida)

The Jackrabbits have four players in their rotation taller than 6 feet, but none over 6-foot-3. The Wildcats have three 6-5 players in their starting lineup. Yet it's Kentucky that likes to shoot the 3 more often. South Dakota State will have already played Texas, Oklahoma State and Kansas, so it will be tested and is always a tough matchup for Power 4 teams, especially on a neutral court.

NC State Wolfpack vs. LSU Tigers

4:30 p.m. ET, ION; Fort Myers Tip-Off (Fort Myers, Florida)

The Tigers are loaded in the backcourt with Mikaylah Williams, Laila Reynolds, ZaKiyah Johnson and Jada Williams, which makes the timing of this game difficult for the Wolfpack, who are dealing with an injury to star guard Zoe Brooks. NC State will need a big game from forward Khamil Pierre, who is putting up the biggest scoring numbers of her career.

Duke Blue Devils vs. UConn Huskies

7 p.m. ET, Fox; Bad Boy Mower Series (Boston)

The Huskies' loaded nonconference slate continues with a game against the ACC favorites four days after meeting South Carolina. This begins a six-game stretch for the Blue Devils in which they play five top-10 teams.

Championship game, Paradise Jam Reef Division

8 p.m., ESPN+ (St. Thomas, U.S. Virgin Islands)

This is the best of three mini four-team tournaments in St. Thomas. Baylor will be a heavy favorite to advance to the Paradise Jam's final game against either South Florida or Seton Hall.

Iowa State Cyclones vs. USC Trojans

1:30 p.m., BDG Live; Baha Mar Hoops Nassau Championship (Nassau, The Bahamas)

The Cyclones' schedule hasn't been challenging, but their start to the season has been positive considering the massive talent losses they suffered in the portal. How they deal with JuJu Watkins, Jazzy Davidson and Saniyah Hall will illustrate just how well Bill Fennelly has rebuilt the roster in his final season as coach.

Virginia Cavaliers at SMU Mustangs

While not part of any holiday showcase or tournament, this game is notable because it's the first ACC matchup of the season. That's right, a conference game in November. The Cavaliers or Mustangs will be off to a 1-0 start before the ACC squares off with the SEC in the ACC/SEC Challenge, which opens just three days later.
