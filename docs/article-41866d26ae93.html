Ryan Pulock out one to two weeks in latest Islanders injury blow

2026-11-24 19:16

The Islanders will be without a key member of their blue line for their next stretch of games.

Ryan Pulock, who hasn’t played since aggravating a shoulder injury that required offseason surgery Thursday, will miss one to two weeks with the injury.

It’ll mark the second time that the 32-year-old has been forced to sit as a result of his shoulder.

Pulock missed the Islanders’ win over the Red Wings and their loss to the Maple Leafs on Monday, and through 17 games this season, he has collected four points and sits at a plus-7.

“I guess it could always be worse,” Islanders head coach Pete DeBoer said Tuesday after they practiced at UBS Arena. “I mean, I don’t know, he had surgery in the offseason. It’s the second time he’s kinda gone out with it. … There’s a horizon where he’s back for us. That’s good. But it’s a battle for sure.”

That has prompted some shuffling for the Islanders’ blue line in their recent matches, and it continued Tuesday when Logan Stanley — who has meshed with Matthew Schaefer on the top pairing — took a maintenance day.

DeBoer expected Stanley to play Wednesday against the Blues but admitted “you never know with these things.” Stanley missed two games earlier this month with a lower-body injury.

This all unfolded as the Islanders braced for an update on forward Kyle Palmieri, too.

Nearly one year to the day after tearing his left ACL, Palmieri’s right leg got tangled with the post Monday, and he didn’t put any weight on his leg as he went down the tunnel before being ruled out for the game.

Palmieri was seeing doctors as of early Tuesday afternoon when the Islanders finished practicing.
