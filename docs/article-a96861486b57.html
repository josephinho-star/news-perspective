Man accused of lewd act in children’s section of Ross store identified by cops

2026-11-24 19:18

The SoCal sicko who was caught on video browsing clothes at a Ross store with his genitals poking out has been identified.

Santa Ana police said the man is 31-year-old Diego Alejandro Garcia Martinez of Costa Mesa.

Cops released footage last week from a Ross shopper who caught the man in the lewd act in the aisle of the store on Bristol Street in Santa Ana. In the footage, the man is seen standing near clothing racks at the store until the woman crosses the aisle to confront him.
