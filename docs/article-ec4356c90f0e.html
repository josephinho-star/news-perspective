Who qualifies for Costco’s $30-per-share dividend — plus, Meta puts share plan on hold

2026-11-24 19:23

Every weekday, the CNBC Investing Club with Jim Cramer releases the Homestretch — an actionable afternoon update, just in time for the last hour of trading on Wall Street. Stocks are lower after Monday's historic rebound . Tech stocks and the broader AI trade are facing a wave of selling, with Anthropic's drop below its $83 IPO price contributing to the negative sentiment. As the AI trade fell back, defensive sectors like healthcare and staples outperformed. Retail stocks are also having a good session after a number of good earnings reports and a jump in consumer confidence . Meta Platforms' plan to create a new Class C share line is on hold. In an 8-K filing late Monday, the company said it had delayed the plan, which would allow it to raise equity by selling the shares, pending a lawsuit filed by Class A shareholders. The trial is scheduled to begin on April 19, 2027, and Meta said it will not issue any Class C shares while the litigation is ongoing. The shares were intended to give Meta a way to fund its AI infrastructure buildout or make acquisitions without diluting CEO Mark Zuckerberg's voting power. Truist analysts wrote Monday that Meta's hesitancy to issue shares that would dilute Zuckerberg's control may force the company to rely more heavily on debt. Borrowing has become more expensive amid higher interest rates and a growing scarcity of capital for AI-related investments. We would prefer to see Meta scale back some of its ambitious investment plans, and perhaps a tighter regulatory environment in 2027 and 2028 could force the company's hand. We'll keep an eye on how this plays out, but for now, we find the shares attractive at these levels, as evidenced by our small purchase last week. Costco's $30-per-share special dividend went ex-dividend on Tuesday. That means shareholders who owned Costco stock as of Monday's close will receive a special cash dividend payment of $30 per share on Dec. 4. Since the company announced the dividend and reauthorization of its stock repurchase program on Nov. 10, shares have gained about 3%, while the S & P 500 has fallen roughly 3%. Some of Costco's more recent gains may reflect its defensive appeal in a market selling AI-beneficiaries, as well as increased pantry loading amid concerns about recent tech outages. Dell , HP , Workday , Autodesk , and Urban Outfitters report after the close of trading, and Deere reports before the open on Wednesday. The October Personal Consumption Expenditures Index, the second read on third-quarter GDP, and October Durable Goods orders are all out Wednesday.
