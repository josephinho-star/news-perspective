Ariana Grande breaks down in tears during final ‘Eternal Sunshine’ concert before break from spotlight

2026-09-02 05:46

Ariana Grande broke down in tears while saying goodbye to fans at the final show of her “Eternal Sunshine Tour” Tuesday night — ahead of her planned break from the spotlight.

The “thank u, next” singer, 33, wrapped her 41-show tour at the O2 Arena in London, closing out a 10-night run in the city after stops across the US and Montreal.

“This has been one of the most beautiful, extraordinary experiences of my life and I just want to express my gratitude without sobbing completely,” Grande said through tears, according to video posted by Pop Base on X.

“But really, thank you from the bottom of my heart. It’s been so special, and I’ll cherish this experience always,” she continued. “I have the best fans in the entire world, and I always have.”

Grande also praised her fans for the elaborate outfits and messages they brought to the shows.

“I love your outfits, I love your faces, I love seeing you, I love connecting with you in this way,” she said.

“Thank you for all of your effort for making this so special. All your messages and your costumes and your wardrobe and makeup is just so beautiful.”

“I have loved every minute of this with you all and I just want to make sure I express how grateful I am,” she added before telling the crowd, “Thank you and I love you so much.”

“She looks forward to finishing the tour and ending it on a high note, both healthily and happily, and then taking a much-deserved break from public-facing work and appearances, which has led to endless, ongoing public scrutiny,” her rep said.

Grande later told fans that the decision was not made suddenly.

“The announcement that was made yesterday was not a reactive or impulsive thing. It is something that I had decided to plan,” she said during a Chicago concert, adding that she had planned it “quietly, a long time ago.”
