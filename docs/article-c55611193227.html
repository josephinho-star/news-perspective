David Bailey’s Jets promise of change feels different than other top draft picks

2026-09-08 18:00

David Bailey grew up in Southern California, then went to college in Northern California before transferring to school in West Texas.

So, you’ll have to forgive him if he is unaware of Jets history. If you mention the Mud Bowl or the Fake Spike to the 23-year-old, you’ll probably get a blank stare.

Bailey, who was selected No. 2 overall by the Jets in April, is not concerned with the team’s past. He’s out to change its future. When asked about his goals for the 2026 season, he does not mention sack totals or awards.
