OnlyFans star Anya Lacey defends conservative, tradwife values — despite her racy content

2026-09-12 14:49

A conservative OnlyFans star who preaches the “tradwife” gospel is facing a holy war from critics who claim her wholesome marriage goals are antithetical to her raunchy internet hustle.

Anya Lacey, in the top 0.01% of earners on the site, where she posts nude snaps and videos of her having sex with multiple partners, insists she is a God-fearing traditionalist who “100% backs Trump.”

“I had so many people saying … ‘Why are you pretending to cater to conservative men?’ I am not pretending. I don’t know why it’s so unfathomable that somebody can do a job, but then also just have opinions that aren’t typical with that job,” she told The Post.

But critics say her conservative views are a gimmick for attention — and money.

“Selling sex and ‘traditional’ shouldn’t be in the same sentence when it comes to marriage,” one said on Instagram.

“Doesn’t Christianity teach to not cause your fellow brothers and sisters to fall into temptation? Kind of hard when you have all your subscribers lusting after all your goods,” another added.

“Very clever marketing by the content provider, creates a submissive pseudo religious persona then profits off men,” someone else wrote.

The dirty blonde beauty, who boasts over 1 million TikTok followers, insists she’d never endure all the hate if she wasn’t fully committed to those ideologies, “considering how much censorship and how many problems conservatives run into on social media.

“I’ve had accounts taken down, I’ve had relationships … friendships fall through … I don’t do a lot of collaborations with [OnlyFans] girls, because it’s an industry very full of very liberal leftist women.”

Lacey, a supporter of President Trump, said she realized her values aligned with those of conservatives during the COVID-19 pandemic.

“I kind of went point by point by point on all of my opinions and realized that I agreed with the conservative party on a lot of things and that’s when I started rooting for Trump,” she said.

The Chicago-native, now based near Palm Beach, Fla., launched the website dateanya.com last year. Her goal is to become a traditional wife, which she defines as “seeing your husband as the head of the household and then God being above all of that.”

There is also a “Date Me” section where interested men can fill out an application with questions such as “Can you change a tire and handle basic home fixes?” and “Do you believe in God?”

“Also some political questions … like what are your positions on the first amendment, second amendment,” added Lacey, who has already gotten thousands of applications.

Lacey, whose parents are from Belarus and was raised Russian Orthodox, also offers tips such as “Guard your reputation. Men marry women they’re proud to introduce to family,” “Reward effort; don’t nag initiative out of him” and “Exclusivity before intimacy.”

“I need to see that you’re a monogamous person, that you are willing to commit to me and only me … before we get to any kind of sexual intimacy,” she said.

Contrary to critics’ assumptions, she noted it’s not difficult to date conservative men as an OnlyFans star.

“I think it was Michael Jordan that said, ‘Republicans buy sneakers too,’ right? … It’s the same exact thing with explicit content. Republican conservative men, they buy explicit content as well.

“I think there’s a little bit more shame around it … but it still happens,” said Lacey, who launched her OnlyFans account at 18 while working as a waitress, but wouldn’t reveal her earnings.

Although she can date conservatives, it’s harder to find a husband since they “wouldn’t want to bring me home to their moms,” she added.

Lacey was even invited to Mar-a-Lago for a Christmas event in December through a local political action committee, where she saw the Commander-in-Chief from afar.

“I saw him and Melania across the hallway. Unfortunately, I didn’t get to shake his hand or anything. I probably would have been a melted puddle on the floor. But maybe one day.”

Besides meeting the president, Lacey’s future plans include continuing to create explicit content — but she is willing to give up her lucrative OnlyFans career for the right person.

“My dream is basically 100 acres, being able to have my eight, nine children, homeschool them,” she said.

“And if a man comes along and says, ‘Hey, I’m willing to provide all of that for you, you just have to stay home, take care of the children,’ I’m more than happy to do it.”
