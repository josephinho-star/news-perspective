Jensen Huang’s lover was in contact with Chinese spy agency officer during affair with ex-Nvidia chief: report

2026-11-23 09:30

Jensen Huang’s lover was in direct contact with China’s spy agency while she was having an affair with the now-former Nvidia chief, a bombshell report claims.

Alice Huang – no relation to the founder of the AI company – is believed to have been recruited by Beijing’s Ministry of State Security (MSS), according to US intelligence, and she was in contact with an agent between spring 2025 and this fall, the New York Times reported, citing sources familiar with the intelligence assessments.

It’s unclear, however, if Alice Huang passed on any insider information to the agent while having her tryst with Jensen Huang. Neither the White House nor the State Department has commented on the allegations.

However, President Trump was warned about Alice Huang’s alleged links to Beijing in June 2025 – which would’ve been just months after the first contact between the honeypot and official was established.

The Times report also claims US intelligence agencies have uncovered evidence that suggests that Alice Huang’s business partner and husband, Kang Wei, worked with the MSS, heightening suspicions that Jensen Huang was the target of an intelligence operation.

Kang Wei, who ran an extensive Beijing-backed AI data center empire in China and secured investments worth more than $9 billion from 2021 to 2024 as part of a government-backed push to build out AI infrastructure in China, has told people he introduced his wife to Jensen Huang.

Authorities continue to investigate Alice Huang and Kang Wei’s role in a network of companies which have been accused of smuggling Nvidia-made AI chips to China, violating US export controls.

Megaspeed, a Singapore-based data center firm accused of export control violations, strongly denies any wrongdoing, previously claiming that the allegations are “not supported by verifiable evidence.”

Shipping logs have been recovered as evidence that links Alice Huang and Kang Wei to the network of companies under investigation, according to the Times report.

China has strongly denied claims Alice Huang was committing corporate espionage.

“I hope the Board of Nvidia rehires Jensen Huang,” Trump wrote in a Truth Social post Sunday night. “It would be the best thing for the Company, and also, for the U.S.A.”

The latest show of support came just two days after Trump said he’d love Huang to “be given a second chance.”

“You know, we’re a country of second chances,” Trump told reporters in Washington Friday.

“And he put us in a position where we are literally 10 years advanced over other companies.”

“And, I think the company [Nvidia], if they were smart, they’d take that brilliant man, accept an apology and let him go back to work.”

Trump has always denied knowledge of Alice Huang being a Chinese spy.
