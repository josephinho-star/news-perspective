Drake Maye’s wife shares Europe travels before brutal Patriots injury in Munich game

2026-11-15 17:05

Ann Michael Maye is using the NFL’s international games as an excuse to hop around Europe.

Ahead of the Patriots-Lions Sunday morning clash in Munich, the wife of quarterback Drake Maye — who exited the game due to an ankle injury — found some free time to travel to Paris and Vienna before landing in Germany.

Ann Michael detailed her travels in various Instagram posts and stories throughout the weekend, starting out in France.

The 23-year-old posted a photo of her in front of a sparkling Eiffel Tower while wearing a white button-up shirt with feather-trimmed cuffed sleeves paired with a black skirt and tall black boots.

“Pinch me I’m dreaming!!!✨✨✨” Ann Michael captioned the post.

Her husband went on to comment “Okkkkkk”

Ann Michael appeared to travel with other wives of the Patriots, including offensive tackle Caleb Lomu’s wife Kitty and tight end Eli Raridon’s wife Anna.

The three shared pictures wandering the streets of Paris, exploring the Palace of Versailles and the Louvre.

Less than 24 hours before kickoff, Ann Michael also shared a photo of the trio in Vienna, Austria, taking in all the Christmas markets.

“Give me all the Christmas markets 😍” she wrote.

Finally, she landed in Munich and posted a photo of her and Kitty bundled in tan winter coats, scarves and sunglasses for the game at Allianz Arena.

“Last stop! Go Pats!” she posted in an Instagram story.

Maye, 24, and Ann Michael grew up in North Carolina together and went on their first date after the former asked her to a high school football game.

“I cried before our first date,” Ann Michael told PEOPLE. “I told my mom I didn’t want to go, and I went, and it was great.”

The couple got engaged in January 2025 and tied the knot months later in June.

Ann Michael has since collected a large following on Instagram and TikTok, and she’s known for her baking and cooking videos.

She drew more than 1,000 people at her first public meet-and-greet at Market Basket in North Andover, Mass., last month.

The Patriots (5-4) are looking to bounce back against the 6-2 Lions after a big loss to the Packers last week.
