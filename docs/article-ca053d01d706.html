Pat McAfee comes to Josh Allen's rescue, Dianna Russini's new show gets roasted & DOGS!

2026-11-10 20:48

'Nightcaps' is a daily column that catches you up on the biggest sports, news and culture stories you may have missed while pretending to do work at your desk. It runs Monday-Friday on OutKick.com.

Folks, I am counting my lucky stars over here.

Over the weekend, my husband was pulling his work trailer out of our garage when — unbeknownst to him — the garage door got hooked on a 32-foot ladder tied to the top of the trailer.

You can probably already guess how this ends. The frame was busted, and the whole garage door was ripped off its tracks and bent all to hell. Disaster.

Of course, home ownership is a constant string of unexpected expenses, but we thought for sure this was going to be a few-thousand-dollar mistake. Not great anytime but especially not great two months before our Costa Rica trip.

We braced our wallets for impact.

But in a remarkable stroke of good fortunate, a technician came out this morning, saved the garage door, put it all back together again and added a new seal ... for $175! Consider my flabbers gasted. I thought I was being punked.

Anyway, this isn't an ad for an overhead door company. But it is an explanation for why I'm in one heck of a good mood today. I feel like I just hit the lottery.

So crack yourself a celebratory beer on my behalf, and let's get to some Nightcaps.

Pat McAfee saves Josh Allen from potential PR disaster

Man, what an absolute snoozer of a Monday Night Football game that was. Carson Wentz... woof.

You know, in past years, it would make me absolutely sick to see the Bills dominate like that. As a Dolphins fan, I mean. But this year? The tank is on, and my stress is gone.

It's such a freeing feeling to have zero expectations whatsoever.

Besides, at least Josh Allen is likable. So likable, in fact, that the families of opposing players are cheering for him and hounding him for photos.

After last night's game, the son of Vikings' long snapper Andrew DePaola took off his dad's jersey to reveal a Bills shirt underneath.

Unbelievable stuff. If you're DePaola can you even be mad, though? I mean, he's a former MVP and, well, you're a long snapper.

No offense to long snappers, of course.

The kid is one thing. But when DePaola's WIFE chased Josh down for a selfie... yikes.

Now I'm not a WAG, so I don't know the etiquette. But is it standard for players' wives and girlfriends to be out there fan-girling? To me, it feels very — I don't know — unprofessional? Sort of like media members taking selfies with players or asking for autographs — which does happen sometimes, but it is taboo.

Anyway, while we're on the topic of Josh Allen, how about Pat McAfee absolutely saving his butt during the pregame show?

In case it wasn't immediately apparent, Josh approaches McAfee during a live hit, and before Josh can say anything, McAfee lets him know over and over they are live on television.

This is akin to warning your buddy he's on speakerphone or that you're talking to him through the Bluetooth in your car.

Of course, that's not to assume Josh was going to say anything offensive or vulgar. Maybe he was going in with a simple, "Good evening, gentlemen, lovely weather we're having!"

But more than likely McAfee was anticipating something a little more colorful. So good on him for saving his friend from the hot mic.

The internet has enough to be fake outraged about without Josh Allen dropping an F-bomb or a d-ck joke or something.

Dianna Russini is back doing... whatever this is

Look, we knew Dianna Russini would eventually land on her feet after that whole Mike Vrabel scandal. It was just a matter of which outlet was going to be brave enough to hire her.

Enter BetUS. Whatever the heck that is. I guess one of the 472 options for online sportsbooks we have these days.

She's getting a show called "The Russini Report," which debuts tomorrow. And I've got to be honest with you guys — the production value could be better.

Apparently, I'm not the only one who thinks so.

I know Bobby Burack wrote a whole column on this yesterday, but the way in which this woman is just blatantly rejecting all accountability for her role in the affair is mind blowing.

PEOPLE did an interview with her, and during the interview, she repeatedly played the victim card. And at one point, she said she hoped her resiliency could be an inspiration to other women.

I think Dustin here says it best.

We can make fun all we want, by the way, but the fact is this show is going to do NUMBERS. At least the first few episodes will. Because we'll all tune in out of sheer morbid curiosity.

But hey, whatever cashes those checks, I guess.
