Lane Kiffin’s daughter stirs ‘broken heart’ breakup rumors with LSU star

2026-10-12 21:55

LSU head coach Lane Kiffin’s daughter, Landry Kiffin, made waves online over her TikTok videos while visiting Dallas this past weekend.

Some believe Landry, a student at Ole Miss, hinted that she and her boyfriend, LSU linebacker Whit Weeks are on the rocks in a video of her lip-syncing to the song “Beware” by rapper Big Sean.

Landry specifically mouthed the lyrics: “You should beware, beware, beware of a woman with a broken heart.”

The Fifth Year founder also liked a bunch of comments such as, “So beautiful! Scaring me again with the sound” and “I just lost my bitch too girl.”

Landry also replied to a comment that said, “I was gonna be you and my bf was gonna be Whit for Halloween.. guess I’ll return my fit.”

She responded, “Wait great costume tho.”

In other TikTok videos, Landry shared that she was visiting one of her girlfriends in Dallas.

The pair attended the Oklahoma Sooners’ 27-24 win over the University of Texas and on Saturday.

“We’re going to spy on Texas today,” Landry said, adding in her caption, “Scouting out the competition.”

Landry and Weeks have yet to address breakup buzz.

They made their relationship public in a post on Instagram last September.

No. 10 LSU suffered a heartbreaking 35-34 overtime loss to unranked Kentucky at Kroger Field in Lexington on Saturday.

Weeks hasn’t played since he sustained a fracture in his left leg in the Tigers’ 35-6 win over Texas A&M on Sept. 26.

The fourth-year senior, a team captain, underwent surgery and is out indefinitely.

Weeks is expected back at some point during the season, according to CBS Sports.
