Netanyahu threatens to expose Mamdani at UN in feisty video message: ‘I’m going to tell the truth about you’

2026-09-22 14:41

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu slammed Mayor Zohran Mamdani and vowed he’d “tell the truth” about the mayor when he’s in town to address the UN General Assembly later this week.

“Shame on you, Mr. Mamdani. Shame on you for supporting the Hamas terrorist monsters who butchered our people. Shame on you for inciting riots against New York Jews,” Netanyahu said in a fiery video message on X.

Mamdani in July called for New Yorkers to protest Netanyahu’s visit, though he’s now trying to claim he didn’t say it. He’s also backed off his campaign promise to personally arrest the prime minister, admitting he lacks the political power.

“I’m coming to the UN. I’m going to tell the truth about our heroic soldiers and I’m gonna tell the truth about you,” Netanyahu vowed.

Netanyahu’s video message follows Hizzoner’s renewed calls that the Israeli leader should be arrested when he arrives in the Big Apple, accusing him of perpetrating a “genocide” in Gaza.

Following a friendly sit-down with President Trump at Gracie Mansion Monday, Hizzoner told CNN on Monday – during an interview that wasn’t on his daily schedule – that he believes the International Criminal Court should intervene.

“When it comes to Benjamin Netanyahu, I have described him as I believe him to be — a war criminal and an architect of a horrific genocide of the Palestinian people,” Mamdani said.
