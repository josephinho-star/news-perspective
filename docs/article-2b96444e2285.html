Dianna Russini working on a tell-all book after Mike Vrabel photo scandal: Boston sports radio host

2026-11-21 01:03

Dianna Russini might be working on a tell-all book.

On Friday, Boston sports personality Jim Murray claimed Russini is working on a book that will delve into the fallout from her alleged relationship with Patriots coach Mike Vrabel.

“I have a little nugget for you,” Murray said on 98.5 The Sports Hub’s “Felger & Mazz” on Friday. “You remember Teddy Atlas, the boxing commentator? He has an annual event around Thanksgiving. It was held last night in New York, and she was there. A friend of mine was chatting with Dianna Russini, and she’s got a book coming out … She said she’ll be talking about everything that’s gone on, is what she told my friend.”

“She’s come out with this podcast. It’s been one big nothing these last couple of weeks. Maybe [Vrabel] knows that a book is coming … She’s going to further expound on this whole situation.”

Murray’s comments come just over a week after Russini returned to the sports media scene, launching her new show “The Russini Report” on BetUS TV, seven months after her departure from The Athletic amid the Vrabel controversy.

Russini opened up on the “nightmare” photo scandal — first reported by Page Six in April — during an interview with People before her new show hit the airwaves.

“The best way to describe what the last six months were like for me is heartbreaking,” Russini said. “At the beginning of this, it was a nightmare.”

She said she and her husband, Shake Shack executive Kevin Goldschmidt, deleted their X accounts after “lies” about the situation “became so out of control.” Russini has since returned to X and has posted about NFL news.

“This was a runaway train, and there was nothing I could do to stop it,” she told People. “Once we realized nothing was going to stop this, it became unhealthy for us to read it because it wasn’t helping us heal. It was piling on and adding to what we were already dealing with.”

Russini is also set to be a contributor on the “Nobody’s Safe” podcast with host Jon Weiner, who is popularly known as “Stugotz.”

Her return to the spotlight comes as the Patriots’ season is falling apart.

New England was blown out by the Lions in Germany to fall to 5-5 on Sunday. And it appears the Patriots will be without their star quarterback, Drake Maye, likely for the rest of the season after he fractured his fibula.

Vrabel has come under increased scrutiny as the Patriots’ season spirals.

The coach raised eyebrows with a bizarre postgame press conference after the latest loss, declaring that “winners don’t bulls–t and complain” when things go wrong.
