Mina Kimes cracks Harrison Butker joke about men in the kitchen with callback to kicker's commencement speech

2026-11-25 16:03

It's been well over two years since Kansas City Chiefs kicker Harrison Butker delivered his highly criticized commencement speech regarding the important role of wives and mothers being "homemakers," yet it still occupies space inside the mind of Mina Kimes.

The ESPN personality recently joined the 'Pablo Torre Finds Out' podcast alongside celebrity chef David Chang to recreate Andy Reid's infamous mac and cheese recipe. With Chang being a professional chef, he did the heavy lifting in the kitchen while Kimes sat down and watched his craft.

Seeing Chang cook in the kitchen triggered Kimes' brain to make a joke about Butker.

"On a side note, how triggered do you think Harrison Butker would be if he turned this on and he saw a bunch of men cooking for me?" Kimes asked, which drew far too long of a laugh out of Torre. "Just blast this in the Chiefs' facility."

Kimes' joke drew pushback from some on social media, which she addressed by writing, "Damn I thought jokes were legal again" on X.

Butker delivered his graduation speech at the Catholic college in May 2024. He never specifically mentioned anything about his wife, or women in general, spending time in a kitchen or even cooking, but his praise for his wife being the glue to their family was extremely triggering for some.

"I can tell you that my beautiful wife, Isabelle, would be the first to say that her life truly started when she began living her vocation as a wife and as a mother," Butker said in part of his speech.

"I'm on the stage today and able to be the man I am because I have a wife who leans into her vocation. I'm beyond blessed with the many talents God has given me, but it cannot be overstated that all of my success is made possible because a girl I met in band class back in middle school would convert to the faith, become my wife, and embrace one of the most important titles of all: homemaker.

"She is a primary educator to our children. She is the one who ensures I never let football or my business become a distraction from that of a husband and father. She is the person that knows me best at my core, and it is through our marriage that, Lord willing, we will both attain salvation."

In August 2024, roughly three months after his commencement speech, the Chiefs re-signed Butker to a four-year contract worth over $25 million.

The 31-year-old kicker has struggled mightily in 2026, having made just 14 of his 21 field goal attempts and missing an extra point, while Kansas City sits at 8-2 on the season.
