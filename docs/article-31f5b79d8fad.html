Courtney Love, 62, shocks fans with ‘insane’ photo showing off ‘skin glass facial’: ‘No filter’

2026-11-27 01:36

On Wednesday, Courtney Love, 62, posted an unvarnished selfie that amazed her fans.

“No filter on my skin 💧🤌 . It’s insane,” she wrote alongside the pic. “I had Korean Skin Glass facial today with @dr_marwaali & my @dr.costas.papageorgiou Best skin doctor in London.”

Many of the comments were gushing.

“Courtney has insane aura, so charismatic and pretty,” wrote one, while another noted, “Beautiful, my dear! 😍😍🫶🏼.” “The glow is glowing,” designer Marc Jacobs wrote, while model Kristen McMenamy gushed, “WOAH 🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥.”

Not all of the comments were complimentary, as some noted that the former Hole frontwoman may have had some previous touch-ups. “I love her, but that’s good plastic surgery. And then good skincare,” wrote one follower. “No filter but hella surgeons,” another chimed in.

Love, who was previously married to Kurt Cobain, has copped to getting a nose job early in her career.

“I tried out for a movie starring Justine Bateman back in the ’80s and I didn’t get the part,” she shared. “It was about an all-girl rock band… you know who got that part? Julia Roberts got that part [in ‘Satisfaction’]. It was her first part.”

“I was an actress in the ’80s but, well, let’s just be real, I had a really big nose,” she continued. “That schnoz was not taking me anywhere but radio.”

Kimmel confirmed that the “Man on the Moon” star did, in fact, get a nose job to remedy said “schnoz.” “Yeah, I got my nose fixed,” Love said, adding that her new nose was a game changer. “In six months, the whole world changed,” she said. “It just happened… I was 20 when I did it.”

“There are a lot of wonderful actresses who are getting older and look fantastic,” she shared. “I want to be in that club. To me, aging gracefully is to let it happen and accept it. In my experience, fighting it always seems to backfire and make people look ridiculous. You see actresses get work done and it makes them unrecognizable. I look at these actresses like Diane Keaton, who has never had anything done, and I think that’s cool. It’s sexy, really.”
