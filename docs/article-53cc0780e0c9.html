‘Knots Landing’ star Donna Mills, 85, reveals bizarre OnlyFans requests she refuses to fulfill

2026-10-24 19:23

“Knots Landing” star Donna Mills won’t fulfill OnlyFans requests to see her brush her teeth.

In a clip from an upcoming interview on “Nightline” shared with People, the actress shared that she gets requests for workout videos, as well.

“I do the Peloton,” she said in the clip. “Just, you know, they’re the things of everyday life: having a cup of coffee, you know. They say they like it when you brush your teeth … I’m not going to brush my teeth.”

Mills emphasized that she embraces subtlety when it comes to her content.

“I think it’s sexier to be not just totally naked, you know, and have a little imagination about it,” she said, recalling a photoshoot with Playboy.

“One of the pictures in Playboy, which I think is one of the sexiest things, is me in the shower, in a dress.”

Mills shared that the unpublished photos would be “the most appealing” to her subscribers “because they’re from the shoot, the same shoot.”

The “Play Misty for Me” star told “Nightline” she wasn’t sure what she’d charge for a throwback image like that, guessing “maybe $100.”

The interview will air in full on Friday evening’s episode of “Nightline.”

Mills, 85, launched her account on the subscription-based site in July, telling Page Six at the time that she had no fears going into it.

“Honey, I’ve been in this business over 50 years…I’ve seen every trend come and go,” she told us.

“When something new comes along that lets me talk directly to the people who’ve stuck with me since ‘Knots Landing,’ why wouldn’t I jump in? My fans are going to see the real me: the good hair days, the bad hair days and everything in between.”

She added, “People keep asking me why now. Why not now? Life’s too short to sit on the sidelines. My fans have given me everything, and this is my way of letting them in a little closer.”

Creators Inc CEO Andy Bachman added, “Donna called me and said two words: ‘I’m in.’ That’s who she is — no hesitation.”

When asked by TMZ that same month if she’d consider baring it all, Mills said, “Who knows? My first answer would be, oh, no, I won’t do that. But then my second answer is, I don’t know.”

She continued, “I don’t want to do anything that’s salacious, you know what I mean? That isn’t my personality. That isn’t what I feel good doing. But if it’s cute and sexy … we’ll see.”

Bachman claimed during an August interview with Mills on the “Creators Inc Podcast” that the TV star had generated “many six figures” in the first month on the site — driven in part by bizarre “foot kink” requests involving her “stomping on grapes.”

“I’m gonna do it,” she playfully divulged. “I tried to shoot it myself. But you can’t shoot something while you’re stomping, it just doesn’t work.”
