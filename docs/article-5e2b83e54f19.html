A list of the best College Gameday signs at LSU during show's 500th episode

2026-09-05 17:33

One of the best Saturday morning traditions during college football season is not only watching ESPN's best program on the network, College Gameday, but seeing the hilarious, savage, and unique signs fans put together each week. For the historic 500th episode of Gameday at LSU, Tigers fans did not hold back. They poked fun at former QB and Heisman winner Jayden Daniels, the legal battle of allowing NFL players to come back to play for LSU, mascots caught some shade, and more.

Here are some of the best from this morning's program:

"Dabo Supports Lindsay Clancy"

Clemson Head Coach Dabo Sweeney was on the receiving end of some savage signs, including a brutal declaration by an LSU fan as it pertains to the Lindsay Clancy case. I wouldn't take Sweeney as someone to be dressed in pink and joining forces with left-wing feminists outside the courthouse, but this shows that almost nothing is off limits during Gameday.

"Dabo 3 Putts"

The worst thing a golfer can be known for is being a constant three-putter on the green. LSU fans just hitting Sweeney where it hurts.

"Jayden Don't Sue Me I'm Only 7"

Former LSU transfer, Heisman Trophy winner, and current Washington Commanders quarterback Jayden Daniels also caught some friendly fire from LSU fans, including a young fan who poked fun at Daniels and his lawyer, Adam Kenner, sending a cease-and-desist letter to LSU, demanding that the school not assign the number 5 to anyone else.

"Happy *00th Gameday"

Another Daniels specific sign was caught in 4K omitting the "5" from "Happy 500th Gameday" in honor of the show's historic episode and showing sarcastic respect to Daniels. Well played, young man.

"Most Valuable Plantiff"

LSU Head Coach Lane Kiffin is no stranger to controversy. He left Ole Miss in rough fashion and now he's leading the #1-ranked transfer class into more controversy as local courts have opened the door for mutiple NFL players to return to college football. Two of those players were not added to the LSU roster before Saturday's game against Clemson.

"My Other Sign Was Ruled Ineligable"

In a similar vain, a fan poked fun at the NCAA's eligibility rules as it pertains to their other sign that unfortunately didn't make the cut.

"You Can't Spell Lawsuit Without LSU"

Last but not least, LSU fans humorously highlighted the ongoing controversy and legal battle about eligibility for NFL players returning to play for LSU.

Personally, this is always a fun "Where's Waldo"-type of entertainment on College Gameday, which I look forward to weekly. As for today's edition, well done, LSU.
