Kim Kardashian turns heads in trompe l’oeil Schiaparelli at ‘All’s Fair’ Mexico City premiere

2026-10-28 17:27

Kim Kardashian’s favorite Skims trick went couture in Mexico City.

The actress arrived at the “All’s Fair” Season 2 premiere at Soho House on Tuesday in a trompe l’oeil Schiaparelli gown with some pointed details.

The ivory sculpted silicone bodice plunged low in front, with molded faux-nipples over Kardashian’s chest. Below the waist, layers of glossy fringe revealed peeks of sheer polka-dotted mesh underneath.

She and stylist Law Roach kept the focus on the dress with matching platform sandals and subtle hoop earrings, eschewing the star’s usual flashy diamonds.

Roach pulled the look from Schiaparelli’s fall 2026 couture collection, which creative director Daniel Roseberry showed at Paris’ Petit Palais in July.

Roseberry developed the collection’s skin-like silicone with help from a workshop outside Paris best known for making lifelike prop babies for film sets.

Kardashian, 46, has been perfecting the perk for years. In 2023, she launched Skims’ Ultimate Nipple Bra, pitching the padded push-up as a way to look cold no matter the weather.

Schiaparelli’s anatomical illusions have already made the rounds in Kardashian’s inner circle. At the Met Gala in May, her sister Kylie Jenner wore a Schiaparelli gown that appeared to be slipping off, exposing a corset complete with faux nipples and a belly button.

Bella Hadid took the look for a spin in January, wearing a red silk Schiaparelli halter gown with a sculpted bodice to “The Beauty” premiere.

Zendaya got first dibs on the collection in July, when she hit the London premiere of “The Odyssey” in a molded white silicone bustier and illuminated fringe skirt. The look went from runway to red carpet in a matter of hours, with Roach personally escorting it from Paris to London.
