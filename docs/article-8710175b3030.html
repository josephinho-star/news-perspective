Wyndham Clark proposes to Emily Tanner during romantic outdoor getaway

2026-11-16 00:20

Wyndham Clark was very clear about wanting to propose to his girlfriend, Emily Tanner, a few months ago.

And the 2026 U.S. Open champion did exactly that.

Tanner posted an Instagram video on Sunday afternoon that showed Clark getting on a knee and popping the question, seemingly while the couple was on a hike.

“My forever 🥹💍,” the post was captioned.

Clark was asked about his upcoming plans for the offseason after winning the BMW Championship in August.

“I’m playing a tournament in Japan; hopefully get engaged and then going to South Africa for a tournament as well,” he responded.

He later said that he didn’t mean to spoil the surprise about his proposal in that moment, but Tanner knew it was coming eventually.

“She knows it’s happening. So it’s not that big of a surprise. But now, I’ve really got to try to disguise when I’m gonna do it,” Clark said.

It seems that he did a great job of disguising it, as Tanner looked genuinely surprised when he asked her in the video.

These two only began dating in early 2026, but Tanner has been by Clark’s side this entire year (which has been the biggest and most successful year of his golf career).

Tanner has done a great job documenting Clark’s golf journey, and will surely document as the couple begins planning their wedding.
