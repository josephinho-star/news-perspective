Troy Aikman pokes fun at ESPN contract standoff during Bills-Vikings Monday Night Football broadcast

2026-11-10 05:15

Monday Night Football has recently felt like The Troy Aikman Show as the lead analyst wrestles with ESPN for a new contract, a stalemate that has reportedly created friction with booth partner Joe Buck.

Aikman nodded to the standoff during the Week 9 matchup between the Buffalo Bills and Minnesota Vikings.

Eyeing Justin Jefferson's heavily iced-out diamond chains, Aikman deadpanned, "I'm guessing that's insured. I cannot imagine wearing that on a football field. Can't imagine wearing it, period."

He then cracked that he might buy something similar, "if somebody would loan me some money ... "

ESPN paused negotiations after Aikman's camp sought a market-resetting extension near $30 million per year. Tensions reportedly deepened because Aikman expected a deal before reaching his walk year, especially after Buck secured a six-year, $108 million extension. With contract talks tabled, sports television’s longest-running broadcast partnership faces an uncertain future beyond this season.

Prior to Monday, Aikman, 59, hadn't let his expiring deal or Bristol's boardroom standoff bleed into the broadcast.

Whatever ESPN pays Aikman, it wasn't enough to sit through sixty minutes of Minnesota's offense. The Bills beat the Vikings, 23-6.

The Vikes benched Carson Wentz with four minutes left, turning to backup Max Brosmer. Wentz managed just three passing yards in the first quarter, finishing with an 8.9 passer rating, 81 yards passing and three turnovers (two INTs, lost fumble). He fell to Buffalo's pass rush and turned Justin Jefferson into a ghost. Jefferson finished with three catches for 58 yards.

Aikman didn't sugarcoat Minnesota’s cratered offense. "I don't know how you fix it," he said.

Lost in the collapse was a valiant effort by Minnesota's defense, which sacked Josh Allen four times.

The win moves the Bills to 7-1 before heading to East Rutherford to face the New York Jets, while the 6-2 Vikings must sort out their quarterback mess before a Week 10 trip to Lambeau Field against the Green Bay Packers.
