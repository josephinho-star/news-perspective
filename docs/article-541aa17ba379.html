NFL Week 9 early predictions, odds, picks: Broncos vs. Panthers, Cowboys vs. Colts

2026-11-02 20:51

The leaves have changed, and the calendar has turned to November.

As we enter a new month, we know just about everything we need to know about every team in the NFL.

Week 8 was the worst we’ve had this year, as both wagers we targeted did not experience any closing line value, with the Bears still to play on “Monday Night Football” and the hook of +3.5 still available.

We move on past Week 8 and take an early look at Week 9 and look to beat some of the opening lines that are highly intriguing from a betting perspective.

NFL Week 9 early predictions

Broncos -2.5 (-124, Bet365) vs. Panthers

The Denver Broncos appeared to unlock their offense a bit last week when Jonah Coleman was unleashed against the Chiefs.

Meanwhile, the Panthers look broken, and I don’t see Bryce Young pulling this offense together against an elite Denver defense.

The Broncos are rated No. 8 in defensive DVOA and they’re allowing just 4.9 yards per play in their last three games, eighth best in the NFL.

The Panthers are 18th in total DVOA and I think they’re even worse on the field than that ranking suggest.

Target the Broncos to win this one big with under a field goal beginning to evaporate from the betting market.

Cowboys -5.5 (-102, Polymarket) vs. Colts
