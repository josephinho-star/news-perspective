Loon plops out of burning two-story home during standoff with police: wild video

2026-11-28 00:13

A wacko awkwardly slid out of a burning home he allegedly set ablaze during a bizarre standoff with police in Massachusetts on Friday, wild video shows.

The dramatic scene unfolded in Lynn, about 12 miles north of Boston, when cops responded to a reported assault inside the home, according to a news release from the Lynn Police Department.

Footage from the quiet, tree-lined street shows plumes of smoke billowing out of the attic window as the alleged firebug makes his escape out of the top-floor window, Boston 25 shared on X.

The shirtless suspect creeps out of a small attic window before he loses his grip and crashes onto the roof, landing on his butt.

Appearing to be in pain, he takes a few seconds to recoup and launches himself back into the home through a second-floor window.

Once firefighters got the blaze under control, the maniac was taken into custody, police said.

A neighbor, who woke up to the smell of smoke and saw cops with guns drawn outside the home, said he was close with the family, including the suspect whom he described as a “good guy.”

“I saw him hanging outside his attic window here and he let go, landing on the roof and as soon as he landed on the roof he went into the second-floor window and that was the last I saw of him until they put him on the stretcher,” neighbor Michael Hogan told NBC10 Boston.

Hogan said the man has a wife and small children who are “super neighbors” that were always willing to lend a hand.

“So I heard he said he had a gun and I didn’t want to believe that because like I said, I was very close with him,” Hogan explained.

“Not excusing what he did, but I still love him,” the neighbor added.

The police department confirmed no one else was inside the home during the fiery standoff — but it remains unclear how many people, if any, were present when the initial call came in.

No injuries were reported from the chaotic scene.

The Post reached out to the City of Lynn Police Department for more information.
