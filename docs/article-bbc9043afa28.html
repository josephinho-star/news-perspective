Last-minute ticket prices for ‘30 Rock’ Radio City show are dropping

2026-10-05 21:32

“30 Rock” is heading a block north of 30 Rock for one night only.

On Sunday, Oct.11, the beloved, postmodern sitcom will celebrate the 20th anniversary of its pilot airing on NBC with a one-night-only, never-to-be-televised show at NYC’s Radio City Music Hall.

Stars Tina Fey, Alec Baldwin, Tracy Morgan, Jane Krakowski, Jack McBrayer and a few other key cast members are confirmed to appear.

And, best of all, ticket prices are dropping — just like “my single “My single is dropping” is dropping — to catch the all-star cast live in the Big Apple.

When our team last reported on how much the cheapest seats cost for the exclusive Midtown affair, they started at $441 including fees on SeatGeek.

A little over a month later, tickets can be found for as low as $334 including fees on Vivid Seats.

If you aren’t a “math guy really,” that’s a cool $107 in savings.

Now that’s worthy of a mandatory Liz Lemon party.

“Listen up tens, a five is speaking!” Fey said in a press release when the event was announced. “Please join us to celebrate the 20th anniversary of the show Tom Shales once said ‘needs a better premise and funnier dialogue … and most of all a more commanding performer in the starring role.’”

As of now, no special guests outside of the regular cast has been announced but the show’s Instagram teased potential appearances from Steve “how do you do fellow kids” Buscemi, Jon Hamm, Will Forte, Matt Damon, Tom Hanks and many more.

Don’t miss out (or keep an eye on prices in case they keep going down) because as we all know “regrets are for horseshoes and handbags.”

For more information, our team has everything you need to know and more about the “30 Rock” 20th anniversary show at Radio City live below.
