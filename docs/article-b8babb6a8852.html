Why Japan’s hypersonic and underwater weapon plans might worry China

2026-08-31 12:00

Beijing has hit out at Tokyo’s moves to arm submarine drones with long-range missiles and test hypersonic weapons in Australia

China’s defence ministry spokesman Zhang Xiaogang highlighted the two initiatives on Thursday, saying Japan had “openly expanded its military arsenal”.

Zhang said the “dangerous” moves had a “striking resemblance” to Japan’s pre-World War II militarisation, though he did not explain why these particular weapon programmes could pose challenges for the PLA.

Japan has rapidly expanded its long-range strike capabilities since adopting a “counterstrike capability” in its 2022 security strategy. Under the policy, Tokyo says it could strike military targets in an adversary’s territory for self-defence following an armed attack, while pre-emptive strikes remain prohibited.
