As China embraces AI, only 24% of US pharma execs bet on it for drug-making

2026-09-09 06:00

Study finds 50 companies had fully, partially or planned to scale AI, tech which could slash drug discovery costs by US$26 billion globally

“It’s only through that translation that companies can turn AI-driven speed into profit, and patients into real beneficiaries,” Yung added.

Some 72 per cent of respondents said their companies were either “scaling” or had “fully scaled” AI across research and development, and no company reported having no plans to adopt the technology, the survey found.

Yet that enthusiasm comes with a caveat. Only 24 per cent of respondents expect AI to deliver a significant improvement in drug success probability, while 56 per cent expect only a moderate uplift, “not a wholesale revolution,” according to the survey.

Another 34 per cent said human biology, clinical judgment and execution will remain as bottlenecks, regardless of how much AI is deployed.
