Martha Stewart’s top-rated cookware and home decor is deeply discounted for Way Day

2026-10-25 13:00

It’s a good thing to shop home essentials for less.

We love all things Martha Stewart, from her chic phone cases to everything she makes to keep our kitchens and homes in style.

So, when we noticed massive deals on her signature Wayfair line of furniture, home decor and linens during Way Day — the big annual sale event running Oct. 24 to 26 — we knew what we were shopping this weekend.

One of the best kitchen picks is this refit-ready Martha Stewart 14-Piece Non-stick Cookware Combo Set that’s under $135 during the sale event.

With heavy-gauge aluminum construction, enamel exteriors and an oven-safe rating, this gorgeous set is also made with a PFOA-free platinum coating. The gold details are chef’s kiss, plus shoppers love it too.

Two frying pans, a Dutch oven, utensils and more make the set a one-and-done buy or absolutely lovely gift for anyone whose kitchen needs an upgrade.

“This set is exactly what I was looking for! They are very nice and match my kitchen nicely!” raves one shopper.

“Beautiful cookware set, nonstick ceramic interior with enamel bottoms,” reads another five-star review.

Of course, if you’re in the market for furniture, linens or other home decor, plenty of other top-rated Martha Stewart line styles are on sale, from sheets to bathmats. Way Day only runs through Oct. 26 — so shop fast for smart buys the domestic doyenne would certainly approve of.
