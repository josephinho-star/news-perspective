When does ‘The Love Hypothesis’ come out? How to watch for free, cast

2026-09-23 04:01

They’ve got real chemistry.

Lili Reinhart and Tom Bateman are bringing one of BookTok’s most popular titles to life in “The Love Hypothesis,” a new movie premiering on Prime Video today, Sept. 23.

Reinhart plays Olive Smith, a PhD student who impulsively kisses her department’s most intimidating professor, Adam Carlsen (Tom Bateman), to prove to her best friend that her love life is completely fine and she’s completely over her former crush.

Watch 'The Love Hypothesis' for free

- When: Sept. 23, 3 a.m. ET

- How to watch for free: Prime Video (30-day free trial)

- TV Channel: Streaming exclusive

What begins as a desperate act turns into a fake relationship with rules, boundaries, and a mutually beneficial agreement. When their arrangement begins to blur the lines between real and fake, Olive is forced to test her most daunting hypothesis yet: that love might actually be worth the risk.

The rom-com is based on Ali Hazelwood’s 2021 novel of the same name (which infamously began as Star Wars fanfiction).

When does ‘The Love Hypothesis’ come out?

“The Love Hypothesis” comes out on on Prime Video in just a few hours, on Sept. 23, at 3 a.m. ET.

Where to watch ‘The Love Hypothesis’ for free

“The Love Hypothesis” is exclusive to the Prime Video streaming service, so you’ll need a subscription to watch the new movie.

‘The Love Hypothesis’ cast

Tom Bateman and Lili Reinhart star in “The Love Hypothesis” as Dr. Adam Carlsen and Olive Smith, respectively.

Joining them are Rachel Marsh as Anh, Jaboukie Young-White as Malcolm, Nicholas Duvernay as Jeremy and Arty Froushan as Tom.
