Bowl, CFP projections: Big shake-up to the postseason picture after a wild Week 12

2026-11-23 22:20

Where do we begin? It was a week in which we saw only one ranked matchup on the schedule, but sometimes when a week doesn't look too exciting on paper madness ensues nonetheless. And well, we saw just that.

No. 4 UCLA took its first loss of the season in a game that went into triple overtime, leaving No. 2 Utah as the lone remaining unbeaten FBS team. No. 8 Indiana and No. 14 Ohio State also lost on the road, along with No. 16 Kentucky, which was upset by Missouri.

Top-ranked Georgia clinched a spot in the SEC championship game with a dominant win over No. 21 South Carolina, making it the Bulldogs' sixth consecutive appearance in the game.

Following all of that, we take a look at how the postseason picture shifted after Week 12.

New to the 2026 season, the Power 4 conference champions will receive automatic bids to the College Football Playoff along with the highest-ranked Group of 6 team regardless of whether it is a league champ. The remaining slots in the 12-team field will go to the next seven highest-ranked teams. Notre Dame is guaranteed a spot in the field if it is among the top 12 teams.

The four highest-ranked teams (not necessarily conference champions) will be awarded first-round byes. The other eight teams will meet in first-round games at the campus sites of seeds 5 through 8.

The festivities of bowl season kick off with the Cricket Celebration Bowl at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta on Dec. 12 and wrap up with the CFP National Championship game at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas on Jan. 25.

ESPN bowl gurus Kyle Bonagura and Mark Schlabach will make their predictions for all the postseason matchups every week of the season until the actual matchups are set Dec. 6.
