Judge who ousted Turkey’s main opposition leadership promoted to top court

2026-11-25 16:10

Turkey’s Council of Judges and Prosecutors (HSK) on Wednesday appointed Murat Öztürkten to the Supreme Court of Appeals, six months after a panel he led overturned Özgür Özel’s 2023 election as leader of the then-main opposition Republican People’s Party (CHP) and removed Özel and his team from office.

Öztürkten was one of five people the council selected by secret ballot to fill vacancies on Turkey’s top appeals court, where Özel’s challenge to the ruling is pending.

Öztürkten headed the 36th Civil Chamber of the Ankara Regional Court of Appeals, which on May 21 declared two CHP meetings, in November 2023 and April 2025, invalid from the outset. The panel ordered Özel and his leadership team removed and his predecessor, Kemal Kılıçdaroğlu, and the previous leadership reinstated pending a final decision.

The appointment was a government reward for a ruling that disregarded the delegates’ vote, Murat Emir, a deputy leader of the main opposition New Party’s parliamentary group, alleged in a post on X. He described the appointment as part of what he called a “system of loyalty” at the judicial council.

Özel had defeated Kılıçdaroğlu in a vote by party delegates at the November 2023 meeting, ending Kılıçdaroğlu’s 13 years as chairman. Former Hatay Mayor Lütfü Savaş and several delegates challenged the vote in court, alleging irregularities. Özel and his allies denied wrongdoing.

The case reached the Supreme Court of Appeals on July 17, and the court’s Third Civil Chamber began a preliminary review on July 26.

Özel and his supporters left the CHP in July to found the New Party, which became the largest opposition group in parliament. Özel received all 1,154 valid votes when delegates elected him chairman at the party’s first convention on November 14.

The CHP withdrew its own appeal on September 14. A lengthy appeal could delay the party’s plans for its next convention, CHP spokesperson Müslim Sarı said in announcing the decision.
