Georgia officer shoots 13-year-old after traffic stop linked to earlier shooting, police say

2026-11-23 11:23

Published Nov. 23, 2026, 6:23 a.m. ET

See more of our coverage in your search results.

A Georgia police officer shot and wounded a 13-year-old Sunday night while conducting a traffic stop linked to an earlier shooting, according to authorities.

The teenager is suspected of being involved in a Jones County robbery and shooting that left one man with a gunshot wound.

Jones County Sheriff Butch Reece said the teenager had met with four men from Macon for a gun sale on Etheridge Road when the group allegedly tried to rob the teenager. The teenager then opened fire, shooting one of the men.

Authorities said the teenager fled the scene before being tracked down to the Milledgeville Mall parking lot, where Milledgeville police attempted to stop a vehicle determined to be linked to the Jones County shooting.

During the traffic stop, an officer shot the teenager, who sustained a single gunshot wound, Milledgeville Police said. A firearm was also recovered at the scene.

The teenager was alert, conscious and speaking following the shooting and was transported to a Macon-area hospital for medical treatment, where the juvenile was listed in stable condition, Milledgeville police said.

The circumstances surrounding the shooting were not immediately clear, including whether the teenager attempted to shoot the officer.

Milledgeville police said the Georgia Bureau of Investigation has been asked to respond and will take over the investigation.
