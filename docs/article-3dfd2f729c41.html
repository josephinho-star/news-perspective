World famous Fiona the hippo under 24-hour medical supervision after eating knitted hat

2026-11-18 00:05

One of the world’s most famous hippos, Fiona, is under round-the-clock medical supervision after accidentally swallowing part of a knitted hat tossed into her enclosure, zoo officials said.

The cap was tossed into the air and struck by a ceiling fan, flinging it through a tiny one-foot opening at the top of Fiona’s habitat at the Cincinnati Zoo on Nov. 8, according to CNN.

“Fiona ingested an object that a visitor accidentally tossed into her habitat. Our veterinary team and hippo care staff are monitoring her around the clock and doing everything they can to help her pass the object safely,” the Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden said in a Facebook post.

“Her care team is cautiously optimistic that she will pass it and return to her usual self,” the zoo continued.

“Fiona will remain off habitat until this is resolved. We will share updates as we have more information.”

Zoo staff dubbed the freak accident a total long shot, saying that they could not replicate it if they wanted to.

“We probably couldn’t make it happen again if we tried,” a Cincinnati Zoo spokesperson told CNN.

Wildlife experts fear the hat could block Fiona’s digestive tract and trigger life-threatening sepsis, though they remain hopeful the winter accessory will pass through without issue.

“Her care team is cautiously optimistic that she will pass it and return to her usual self,” the zoo wrote.

It is not uncommon for captive animals to accidentally eat items dropped into their enclosures.

“The major concerns regarding Fiona ingesting a cap are primarily that it doesn’t cause an obstruction in her digestive tract which could lead to impaction and damage leading to serious infection and sepsis,” Ron Magill, a goodwill ambassador for the Zoo Miami Foundation, told CNN.

“Zoo Miami has had to surgically remove items such as a collapsible water bottle from a Komodo dragon and sunglasses from an alligator among many other things, including hearing aids and cellphones,” Magill added.

Fiona, now 9 years old, gained widespread social media fame after her premature birth in 2017, when she weighed just 29 pounds — far below the average newborn hippo weight of 100 pounds. Now, she weighs 2,400 pounds, the Associated Press reports.

Fans followed the ungulate’s progress online and became enthralled with her impressive recovery.

She is the primary attraction at the Cincinnati Zoo, and has her own brand of merchandise for sale in the zoo’s gift shop.
