Haunting final video shows devoted California dad moments before plunging 500 feet to his death after son’s mishap on mountain

2026-11-24 19:15

Footage shared on his Instagram Story shows the father, dressed in a neon yellow-green jacket and a gray baseball cap, making his way up the slope with his son climbing just ahead of him.

Guillermo Alejandro Batres tragically died after this clip was taken while hiking Mount Baden-Powell with the boy Sunday, who slipped on the ground.

“As Guillermo tried to help him up, he slipped on ice and fell approximately 500 feet down a steep gulch.

“Guillermo died in the fall, leaving his family shocked, grieving, and facing an unimaginable loss,” his niece, Jennifer, wrote in a GoFundMe.

“This fundraiser is being created to help Guillermo Alejandro Batres’s family with funeral expenses and other financial expenses during this incredibly difficult time,” the fundraiser continued.

The GoFundMe for Batres has raised over $9,350 of its $12,000 goal. Batres was able to save his son before he fell.

Batres was a United Endurance Sports Coaching Academy ultra running coach who received his certification on July 3, 2025, according to his Instagram.

As a UESCA running coach, he was able to help prepare athletes for long races — even longer than marathons — such as 50-mile, 100-mile and multi-day running events.

Mount Baden-Powell is located in the San Gabriel Mountains in Los Angeles County and is considered to be one of the more notorious hikes once ice or snow forms during the winter.

Among the three victims was a 19-year-old college freshman named Marcus Alexander Casanova who attended Santa Clara University and was a Seal Beach resident.
