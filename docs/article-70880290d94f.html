Five EU leaders: Europe must spend better, not just more

2026-09-18 02:00

Mette Frederiksen is prime minister of Denmark. Friedrich Merz is chancellor of Germany. Rob Jetten is prime minister of the Netherlands. Christian Stocker is chancellor of Austria. Petteri Orpo is prime minister of Finland.

In a more dangerous and uncertain world, a strong Europe must be ready to make bold choices. In a world of real fiscal constraints, Europe cannot fund new priorities by simply adding them to old ones. We must make choices.

Europe’s next long-term budget is about much more than money. It’s about political priorities. The next Multiannual Financial Framework will determine how Europe spends its common resources well into the next decade.
