Hero driver who tried to stop Oregon school shooter shows bullet hole-riddled truck: ‘Holy s–t’

2026-10-16 13:05

The driver was the first person to call 911 around 1 p.m. Thursday to raise the alarm after “being shot at multiple times by a person with a long gun” by the evil teen who then shot up Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, killing three young students and a teacher.

The stunned, unidentified driver showed how his Toyota Tacoma was riddled with bullet holes that smashed his windows.

“Boom boom, through my canopy, shattered out the back window,” the driver said in a video recalled of the terrifying volley of shots fired at him.

“Shattered out that f–king window,” he added, alluding to the car’s rear window before panning the camera to his shattered windshield.

“[The bullet] went right through the f–king windshield. Right above my f–king head. Holy s–t.”

At least six large bullet holes pierced the driver’s red pickup truck.
