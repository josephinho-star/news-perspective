Russell Brand seen living it up at 100-person ‘going-away’ party on Florida beach before rape trial: report

2026-11-10 20:44

Russell Brand reportedly threw a massive party before he appeared in court for his rape and sexual misconduct trial.

In photos obtained by TMZ Tuesday, the comedian posed alongside 100 guests at a party hosted on Sept. 27 at Grayton Beach in Florida.

An insider told the outlet that he had invited friends, including people from his church and Path of Grace Recovery, an all-women’s sobriety group, to attend.

According to the outlet, Brand’s wife, Laura, and their three kids were also in attendance.

While the gathering wasn’t labeled as a “going-away party,” an attendee claimed it felt like one because the “Forgetting Sarah Marshall” star, 51, was about to head to court and allegedly told attendees, “I might be going away for a long time.”

During the gathering, people prayed, and the actor allegedly told guests he was trusting in God, with the insider noting he showed no signs of concern about the trial, per the source.

A rep for Brand wasn’t immediately available to Page Six for comment.

The “Get Him to the Greek” star reported to London’s Southwark Crown Court on Oct. 12 for a nearly four-week trial in relation to rape and sexual assault claims.

One woman told the court how Brand raped her at his London home in 2009 after they met at his comedy show at the Hammersmith Apollo theatre. The woman agreed to consensual sex before she was raped anally.
