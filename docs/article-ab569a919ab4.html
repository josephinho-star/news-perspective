Alex Warren takes hard fall during concert: ‘They told me to break a leg’

2026-09-05 08:32

A world-famous pop star has taken a painful tumble in front of a packed stadium of fans – and it was caught on camera.

Alex Warren was performing at Rod Laver Arena in Melbourne on Friday night as part of his Finding Family on the Road world tour when an attempt to climb down from the stands failed miserably.

The British pop singer, dressed in a blue shirt and cream trousers, leapt onto recording equipment placed in a side tunnel and landed on two feet only to tumble it moments later.

Warren didn’t shy away from the embarrassing encounter. Instead, he decided to share a clip of the side-splitting incident on his Instagram.

His very tongue-in-cheek caption read: “They told me to break a leg before the show.”

Fans also joined in the ridicule.

“Ok, bubble wrap added to the dressing room rider,” one person commented.

“Should’ve gotten up to the camera and said ‘pls buy my new album,’” joked a second.

“You can’t park there,” quipped a third.

The clip received 346,000 likes and 2,600-plus comments.

The awkward moment appears to have taken place at the premiere of Guy Pearce and Claire Foy’s new film Ink on Wednesday evening, but only started gaining traction on social media on Friday.

Neither Dobrev or the Venice Film Festival have commented.

Many described their actions as “rude” and “unnecessary.”
