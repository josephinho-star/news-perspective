Ogee’s Lid Sculpt isn’t just an eyeshadow – It’s a new category of eye color

2026-09-17 11:32

There’s a new way to think about eyeshadow. Ogee’s Lid Sculpt Liquid Eyeshadow is making the case that eye color can do far more than simply add pigment.

Forget everything you know about traditional eyeshadow. Ogee’s latest launch, Lid Sculpt Liquid Eyeshadow introduces what the brand describes as a new category of eye color — one where high-impact color meets skincare benefits. Designed to blur the line between makeup and treatment, the formula is created to visibly lift and smooth the look of the eyelids while delivering long-lasting, crease-free color.

The crux of Lid Sculpt is a skincare-infused, liquid-to-powder formula that applies effortlessly and sets quickly to a weightless finish. The result is saturated color without the heavy feel that can come with traditional shadow. Once applied, the formula is designed to remain crease-free and budge-proof for up to 12 hours, making it an easy choice for everything from a quick morning makeup routine to a full day out.

The formula is powered by essence of white flowers, a key ingredient intended to help visibly lift and smooth the appearance of the eyelids. According to Ogee, the lifting claim is based on an in-vitro study conducted on the key ingredient. Clinically backed for 12-hour wear, Lid Sculpt is designed to adapt beautifully across eye shapes, ages, and textures.

A metallic shade for every mood

Lid Sculpt launches in 10 metallic shades, offering a spectrum of wearable and statement-making color:

- Jasper – a rich, earthy metallic

- Sunstone – a luminous warm-toned hue

- Copper – a classic glowing metallic

- Tiger Eye – a dimensional, golden-brown shade

- Rose Gold – a soft, luminous pink metallic

- Nickel – a cool-toned metallic

- Obsidian – a deep, dramatic shade

- Jade – a jewel-toned green

- Amethyst – a vibrant purple

- Lapis – a striking blue

Whether swept on as a sheer wash or built up for more intensity, the quick-setting formula is designed to make application simple. The key is speed: prep the eye area with a favorite eye cream or balm, then sweep a light layer of Lid Sculpt onto the lids and blend immediately.

The brush that makes blending effortless

For an even more seamless application, Ogee is also introducing the Eye Blender Brush. Designed to work with cream, liquid and powder formulas, the ultra-soft brush diffuses color for an effortless wash across the lids.

Like the rest of Ogee’s approach to beauty, the brush is vegan and eco-friendly, pairing performance with the brand’s emphasis on more conscious beauty.

Lid Sculpt reflects the philosophy behind Ogee: makeup doesn’t have to be separate from skincare. The brand’s certified organic, high-performance collection is designed to blur that traditional divide, creating products that combine beauty performance with skin-focused ingredients.

Ogee’s organic collection is certified by NSF, a stringent standard for personal care products. According to the brand, NSF certification requires a minimum of 70% organic ingredients within each formula, compared with certifications that may require substantially less. The certification also reflects Ogee’s focus on using natural and organic ingredients while avoiding harsh chemicals, synthetic fragrances and artificial dyes.

The brand’s products are certified organic, non-toxic, cruelty-free, GMO-free, not tested on animals, gluten-free and made in the USA.

With Lid Sculpt, Ogee is taking a familiar makeup staple and giving it a distinctly modern purpose: color that performs like makeup while bringing skincare thinking to the eyelids.
