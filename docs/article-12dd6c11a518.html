Trump Moves to Bar Three News Outlets From White House

2026-09-19 13:01

President Donald Trump says he is banning CNN, MS NOW and Politico from the White House over coverage he calls “fake news,” though questions remain about how the restrictions would be enforced and whether they could face legal challenges. Bloomberg News White House Correspondent Jeff Mason joins David Gura and Christina Ruffini on Bloomberg This Weekend and discusses how this move comes as Trump also faces protests over the future of the Kennedy Center and prepares to address the UN General Assembly with the Iran war expected to be a major focus.
