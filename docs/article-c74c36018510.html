Katie Holmes sizzles in bikini as she kisses boyfriend during Lake Como getaway

2026-09-13 18:00

Katie Holmes is living her best life with her new boyfriend.

The 47-year-old actress was recently photographed enjoying a day on a luxury yacht while on vacation in Lake Como in Italy with her boyfriend, Jason Bard Yarmosky.

In the photos, Holmes and Yarmosky are seen sunbathing on the back of a yacht as they cuddled, and later took a dip in the lake to cool off.

She was spotted in a black bikini with a halter neckline and gold sparkles, with matching bottoms, while Yarmosky showed off his abs in a pair of black swim shorts.

Holmes smiled as she sat with her arms around the New York City-based artist, before sharing a kiss with Yamosky as she climbed out of the water and back onto the boat.

Holmes and Yamosky first sparked romance rumors in July, when they arrived to a screening of "The Invite" together, and were photographed holding hands.

A source told People at the time that the two "looked very cute" and that "he was whispering in her ear, and she was laughing."

They continued attending events in New York and posing together on the red carpet, before going Instagram official earlier this month. Holmes posted a photo of the two of them together, with the caption, "Happy Birthday to the best of the best…❤️"

"ON THE GRID?! Happy for you queen ❤️❤️❤️" one fan wrote in the comments section. Another added, "Nice to see you look so happy. Happy Birthday to him."

He has since shared photos of the two of them on their Italian vacation, including a few of them on the boat, and one of Holmes in a chic black gown with a deep plunging neckline.

Prior to going public with Yarmosky, Holmes briefly dated Bobby Wooten III in 2022 and Emilio Vitolo Jr. from 2020 to 2021. Most notably, she dated actor Jamie Foxx for six years, from 2013 to 2019.

Her most high-profile relationship was with actor Tom Cruise, who she was married to for nearly six years, from November 2006 until she filed for divorce in June 2012.

During their relationship, they welcomed their daughter, Suri Cruise, in April 2006.

"I love her so much," she told InStyle in March 2020. "My biggest goal has always been to nurture her into her individuality. To make sure she is 100 percent herself and strong, confident, and able. And to know it. She came out very strong — she's always been a strong personality. She'll pick an activity and work her butt off until she's really good at it. Then she's like, ‘OK, I'm going to try the next thing.’ She's very focused and a hard worker."
