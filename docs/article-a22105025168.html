Titans' Jaylen Harrell allegedly 'assaulted' rideshare driver before Tampa car accident, police say

2026-11-22 00:40

Tennessee Titans defensive lineman Jaylen Harrell was riding as a passenger in the back seat of a white Kia Sorento rideshare "when he reportedly assaulted the driver while the vehicle was in motion," Tampa police told OutKick.

Tampa police, in providing preliminary information about the car accident Harrell was involved in, said that the driver, a man in his mid-30s, swerved, striking a second vehicle before colliding with a utility pole. Upon impact, Harrell was thrown forward, striking the windshield and losing consciousness, Tampa police said.

The 24-year-old was transported to the hospital with "serious injuries" and remains under medical care. Tampa police said that the rideshare driver sustained minor injuries and was treated at the scene, while the driver of the second vehicle, a woman in her early 50s operating a Toyota Highlander, was uninjured.

The Titans said in a statement Saturday afternoon that Harrell was hospitalized in a serious car accident.

"Yesterday, Jaylen Harrell was involved in a serious car accident that resulted in him being taken to the hospital in his hometown of Tampa, Florida. We have been in constant communication with Jaylen’s family and are still working to gather more information," the Titans said in a statement to OutKick.

"At this time, Jaylen remains in the hospital and continues to receive care. We ask that Jaylen and his family’s privacy be respected at this time. We will provide an update when appropriate."

The 24-year-old hasn't played for the Titans this season after he tore his ACL during the preseason in August and was placed on season-ending injured reserve. The 2026 season would have been his third in the NFL after being drafted in the seventh round of the 2024 NFL Draft out of Michigan.

He had played in all 17 games in each of his first two seasons and started two of them in 2025. In his career, Harrell has 38 total tackles, 4.5 sacks, seven tackles for loss and one forced fumble.

Harrell was an integral part of Michigan’s national championship-winning team in 2023, recording 31 tackles, 9 tackles for loss, 6.5 sacks and 2 forced fumbles across 15 games that season.

James Harrell, Jaylen's father, played in the NFL for eight seasons. He spent seven seasons with the Detroit Lions and one with the Kansas City Chiefs.
