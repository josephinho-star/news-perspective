Star Wars Zero Company is a must-play strategy game at 20 percent off

2026-10-21 18:30

If you’ve been curious about Star Wars Zero Company, the XCOM-like strategy game set in the Star Wars universe, Amazon has both the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series S/X physical editions on sale for $47.99 and $49.99, respectively. The game usually sells for $59.99. While I’m not usually enthused by turn-based strategy games, I’ve been impressed enough with the story and characters to keep playing, and any fan who’s familiar with the world surrounding the battles should make the experience more immersive.

The character-driven strategy game won me over with impeccable vibes, and it’s even more appealing with a discount.
