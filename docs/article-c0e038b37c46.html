US warns EU leaders against Big Tech tax

2026-11-26 11:21

BRUSSELS — The Trump administration is warning EU leaders against taxing Big Tech as they gather in Brussels to consider new options to fund the bloc’s next budget.

“We oppose the EU proposal for an EU-wide digital levy just as we have opposed (and continue to oppose) other digital services taxes that discriminate against US companies while hurting consumers as well,” a spokesperson for the U.S. State Department told POLITICO in a statement, reiterating Washington’s years-long opposition.

European Council President António Costa on Saturday proposed reviving talks on a European digital tax to help finance the EU’s €2 trillion long-term budget and unlock a deal on its final size.
