Jon Gosselin’s wife says son Collin is ‘changing the world’ with his tell-all memoir about mom Kate

2026-10-31 22:41

Jon Gosselin’s wife, Stephanie Lebo, has praised her stepson, Collin, for his tell-all memoir, detailing his alleged abusive childhood at the hands of his mother, Kate Gosselin.

“Collin, you are changing the world, and I hope you truly understand that,” she wrote in an Instagram post shared on Friday. “You’ve turned some of the darkest chapters of your life into something that will help others. You spent so many years fighting to be heard, and now the world is listening.

“You are kind, intelligent, strong, and so incredibly loved. You are SEEN. You are HEARD. And most importantly, you have nothing left to prove to anyone. You never had to earn love, kindness, or a place to belong. You deserved them all along. ❤️”.

Lebo, 39, added that an “entire village” is standing behind Collin, and she is “beyond honored to be part of it.”

“Keep shining, Collin. The world is listening, and I couldn’t be prouder of you! 🥹❤️,” she concluded. “I love you… WE ALL love you.”

The missive ran alongside photos including Collin holding his book, a screenshot of his book on the NYT bestseller list, and her signed copy.

Collin, 22, starred in the TLC reality series “Jon & Kate Plus 8,” which ran between 2007 and 2017. The show followed his parents as they raised their 8 children — a pair of twins and sextuplets, including Collin. The couple separated in 2009.

Collin has been estranged from his mother since 2016, when she sent him to live in a behavioral health institution at age 11 for alleged behavioral issues.
