Nearly half of Taiwanese lack confidence in US defence commitments, survey finds

2026-09-21 12:00

‘Deeply worrying’ result comes as US$14 billion arms package to island stays stalled ahead of this week’s Xi-Trump summit in Washington

Conducted by the newspaper since 2010, the annual survey also found fluctuating expectations about direct American military support.

Two years ago, 49 per cent believed the US would send troops to help defend Taiwan, compared with 42 per cent who thought it would not. Last year, 49 per cent said Washington would not intervene.
