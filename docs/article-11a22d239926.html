Britney Spears hopes to be the ‘best mommy’ as she celebrates her sons’ birthdays

2026-09-13 20:01

Britney Spears expressed her hopes to be the “best mommy” she can be as she celebrated her sons’ birthdays.

The mom of two took to Instagram over the weekend to pen heartwarming tributes to Sean Preston, whose 21st birthday is on Monday, and Jayden James, who turned 20 on Saturday.

Dedicating a post to her youngest son, she wrote, “Happy birthday to my handsome young boy oh dear lord we have this beautiful thing called life in front of us.”

“It’s such a beautiful delicate thing,” Spears, 44, added. “I hope I can be the best mommy in the world every day of ur life!!!!”

The Grammy winner included a photo of Jayden during a photoshoot and another of him as a child.

An insider told Page Six in April that Preston and Jayden helped their famous mother get back on her feet and into treatment.

“It’s just been really great for them to spend all of this time with their mom,” our insider shared.

“Britney loves them both equally. They are her whole world,” the source continued, referring to the trio’s quality time as “an absolute gift” and “the biggest joy” for Spears.
