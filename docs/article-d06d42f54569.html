Stocks making the biggest moves midday: SpaceX, Lumentum, Diamondback Energy, J.B. Hunt & more

2026-09-16 17:26

Here are some of the companies making headlines in midday trading. Artificial intelligence infrastructure — Shares of companies involved in the AI buildout jumped following several losing sessions. AI cloud computing play CoreWeave jumped more than 4% and Nebius rose nearly 5%, putting both stocks on track to snap a five-day slide. Photonics plays Lumentum and Coherent rose 8% and 6%, respectively, while Dell 's stock advanced 5%. SpaceX — Shares of Elon Musk 's rocket company advanced 5%. SpaceX announced the next test for its Starship rocket, set for launch Sept. 22. In this flight, the company also plans to deploy Starlink V3 satellites. Honeywell Technologies — The developer of automation solutions saw shares jump almost 3%. In a presentation at Morgan Stanley's Laguna Industrials Conference, leadership said that strong second-quarter orders and a solid start to the third quarter are supporting a favorable setup heading into year end and 2027. FTAI Aviation — Shares added 5% after the supplier of aircraft engines announced that its board approved a $500 million share buyback program . SK Hynix , Intel — Shares of the chipmakers rose after a Reuters report that SK Hynix was in talks with Intel to manufacture memory chips in the U.S. for the first time. SK Hynix's stock added 2%, while Intel's stock jumped 5%. SK Hynix said that no decisions have been made regarding any partnership with Intel. J.B. Hunt Transport Services — Shares of the transportation and logistics company slid nearly 13% after the company warned that its earnings may tumble in the third quarter between 5% to 10%, compared with the previous three-month period. J.B. Hunt said the decline has been due to internal adjustments to account for rising rates of purchase transportation. Expedia — Shares of the online travel company slipped about 1% after Morgan Stanley downgraded the stock to underweight. Analysts at the bank said shares have a weak risk/reward profile, particularly with greater exposure to a potentially weaker consumer. Energy stocks — U.S. oil prices fell more than 3% after a report said energy inventories rose last week, sending energy stocks lower. Shares of Diamondback Energy 's declined 8%, while Occidental Petroleum 's stock was off 5%. Shares of ExxonMobil lost nearly 3%, and Devon Energy 's stock dropped 4%. — CNBC's Nick Wells and Darla Mercado contributed reporting.
