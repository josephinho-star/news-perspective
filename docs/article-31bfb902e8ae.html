Scott Jennings praises Trump’s 1,300-word rant against Bill Maher: ‘Miraculous’

2026-10-27 17:02

Conservative pundit Scott Jennings praised President Trump’s stunning 1,300-word rant about late-night comedian Bill Maher as “miraculous” during an appearance on CNN Monday night.

In his record-breaking Truth Social post Sunday night, the commander-in-chief, 80, blasted Maher, 70, for criticizing his low approval ratings after their most recent White House dinner together.

On CNN’s “NewsNight” with guest host Michael Smerconish Monday night, Jennings, 49, defended the eyebrow-raising post, saying the president is “very loquacious.”

“It’s sort of miraculous that the president of the United States issues such detailed missives about all sorts of things going on in our culture,” Jennings said.

He argued the post – which branded Maher a “complete, total loser” and a “sleazebag” who “looked like hell” during their recent dinner – was just an example of the president’s candor with the public.

“He [Trump] does the same thing in front of the press, when they come and ask him questions, he goes on at length about things. You can call Donald Trump a lot of things; one thing you can never say about him is we don’t know what’s on his mind,” Jennings said.

Leftist CNN commentator Van Jones, 58, cut in, quipping, “What’s left of his mind.”

“Oh, come on,” Jennings shot back. “And by the way, I have to say, I kinda like Bill Maher. I mean, Bill Maher has been basically a rational actor. I used to not like him that much but he has become a rational actor trying to tell the left, like my buddy Van here, pull them back from the brink of insanity, a very rational guy, so I don’t know why he’s so upset with him.”

During his HBO show “Real Time” on Friday, Maher claimed the president “has the lowest approval rating of any midterm candidate ever” and joked that his four-hour dinner with Trump on Oct. 15 lasted longer than Katie Zacharia’s five-day stint as press secretary.

Maher also applauded Trump’s behavior during their dinner, saying, “When he’s in that White House, he looks you in the eye, he never interrupts, he never gets mad, he listens.”

“Yeah, I did it again, b–ches,” Maher said, in response to critics who slammed him for meeting with Trump. “I know! I’m the bad guy because I go and say face-to-face to the man the things you write in articles he’ll never read, but okay. You know what? F–k you. I like these dinners.”

But Trump sounded off on the comedian for criticizing his approval ratings in his longest-ever Truth Social post, more than twice as long as the previous record-holder, according to Factbase.

“Let’s talk about this COMPLETE AND TOTAL LOSER, Bill Maher,” the president wrote. “He’s got a Low Rated Show on equally Low Rated HBO, which was once, long ago, a Network of Quality and Excitement — But that’s all gone. It’s not ‘HOT’ anymore!”

He then attacked producer Chuck LaBella, who worked with Maher on his former ABC show “Politically Incorrect.” Trump claimed LaBella had repeatedly pushed the president to set up the White House dinners with Maher.

“Maher’s friend and business associate, Chuck LaBella, was also a friend of mine, worked on The Top Hit Show, The Apprentice, as a ‘gofer’ and, because of the success of that show, Number One and Top Rated on many weeks, with the Finale’ being among the Top Shows of the Year, he was automatically and quickly brought up through the Ranks of NBC, along with everyone else associated with that Historic ‘Happening,’” Trump said.
