Ken Urker’s family extends olive branch to Gypsy Rose Blanchard with funeral invite

2026-10-06 12:06

Ken Urker’s family has extended an olive branch to his ex-fiancée, Gypsy Rose Blanchard, by inviting her to his funeral in his native Texas.

Despite Urker’s sister Autumn claiming her family is “disgusted” with Blanchard for her public statement following his death, insiders told TMZ on Monday there is “no tension” between the reality star and her ex’s family.

Additionally, sources said that Urker’s parents don’t blame Blanchard for their son’s death, and that his mom, Raina, actually likes the convicted murderer.

Blanchard, 35, is reportedly “open” to attending the funeral but also wants to hold one in Louisiana for her family members who can’t travel to Texas.

The “My Time to Stand” author, Urker’s mother, two of his sisters and his brother came together in Louisiana to pay their final respects, TMZ reported.

Blanchard and Urker’s 1-year-old daughter, Aurora, also attended. However, the outlet reported that Autumn was not present.
