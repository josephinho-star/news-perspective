‘Days of Our Lives’ actor Robert Kelker-Kelly died ‘suddenly’ after cardiac arrest: police

2026-10-13 20:09

“Days of Our Lives” actor Robert Kelker-Kelly died suddenly after suffering cardiac arrest at his Missouri home, according to law enforcement.

“A police report was completed, as is standard in all sudden death investigations,” a representative for the St. Peters Police Department told Page Six.

In a police report obtained by Page Six on Tuesday, Kelker-Kelly was found “unresponsive” in his bedroom after complaining of chest, neck and left arm pain earlier that evening.

The actor’s wife, Allison Kelly, told police she picked him up from Spirit Airport around 7:30 p.m. on Oct. 3 when he began experiencing the pain during their drive home.

According to the report, Allison asked her husband if he wanted to go to the hospital, but he declined and said he was going home to lie down.

Once they arrived home, Kelker-Kelly changed his clothes and got into bed, but continued experiencing pain.

He then asked Allison to get him an aspirin from the kitchen. When she returned to their bedroom, she discovered the 62-year-old actor “unresponsive and not breathing.”

“Upon arrival, I contacted the reporting party, identified as Allison Kelly. Allison stated she discovered her husband, identified as Robert Kelly, unresponsive in their bedroom. Allison stated she called 911 and began CPR,” the responding officer wrote.

“Robert was lying in bed on his back with his head facing east and his feet facing west. Robert was wearing a black tee shirt and grey boxers at the time of contact. The St. Charles County Ambulance District (SCCAD) arrived on scene and began providing medical attention for Robert who was unresponsive and not breathing,” the report stated.

Paramedics from the St. Charles County Ambulance District arrived and begun lifesaving measures, but the actor was pronounced dead at 9:09 p.m.

The report also identified Allison as Kelker-Kelly’s spouse, a detail that had not been widely publicized.

Allison told investigators that her husband had no known medical conditions and had not undergone any serious injuries or surgeries in the previous year.

She also said he did not smoke cigarettes regularly, although he occasionally smoked cigars, and did not use illicit substances.

The St. Charles County Medical Examiner’s Office was contacted following his death and determined that no further investigation by its office was necessary, according to the report.

Police classified the incident as a “sudden death” and have closed the case.

As previously reported, police and emergency medical personnel responded to the residence around 8:16 p.m. local time after receiving a report of an unconscious man who was not breathing, according to TMZ.

Kyle Gaines, the division chief and public information officer for the St. Charles County Ambulance District, told Page Six, “First responders and paramedics responded to a call … for an adult male patient for a cardiac arrest.

“They were dispatched where paramedics and first responders provided advanced life-saving protocol techniques. Unfortunately, the patient was pronounced deceased on scene,” Gaines said.

Dispatch audio previously obtained by Page Six revealed the desperate attempt to save Kelker-Kelly’s life.
