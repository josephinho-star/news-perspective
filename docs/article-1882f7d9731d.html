Boss of Orbán-backed MCC Brussels quits, saying staff have not been paid

2026-08-31 08:45

The head of the EU’s most prominent right-wing think tank, MCC Brussels, has resigned, accusing its financial backers of failing to pay staff.

In a letter to the board of the Mathias Corvinus Collegium on Monday, seen by POLITICO, Frank Füredi said he would resign as MCC Brussels’ executive director with immediate effect, a decision “executed for severe cause attributable exclusively to the employer.” Contractual obligations, he said, had been “irreversibly broken by the employer’s persistent failure to provide the contractually agreed funds.”

MCC Brussels was founded in 2022 with the support of the Hungarian government of then-Prime Minister Viktor Orbán — and Füredi’s resignation comes amid a growing rift between the organization and the new government.
