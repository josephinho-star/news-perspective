Chase adds Qantas and Cathay Pacific as transfer partners. These two credit cards can help you access them

2026-11-20 19:13

The Chase Ultimate Rewards® program unveiled two new airline transfer partners on Nov. 18, adding to its previous list of 14 airline and hotel programs.

Now, eligible Chase cardholders can transfer Ultimate Rewards® points to Qantas Frequent Flyer and Cathay Pacific at a 1:1 rate — meaning the number of points transferred stays the same. The last time Chase added an airline transfer partner was in 2021, with the addition of Air Canada Aeroplan.

CNBC Select dives into what Chase credit cardholders should know about the recent transfer partner additions and how to take advantage of them.

Qantas Airways predominantly serves Australia and the South Pacific, while Cathay Pacific Airways is focused on Hong Kong and Asia.

A nice perk these two airlines share is that they're both part of the oneworld airline alliance, which also includes popular options like American Airlines and Alaska Airlines. You can search flights via oneworld's flight search tool and compare the cash price between all the member airlines. Since each loyalty program sets its own prices, you can view the award price on each carrier's website.

Plus, sometimes airlines reserve more or higher-quality seats for their frequent flyer programs.

Anyone with an eligible Chase card can take advantage of these new partners. Two popular options include the Chase Sapphire Preferred® Card (see rates and fees) and the Chase Sapphire Reserve® (see rates and fees).

The Reserve's $795 annual fee may be startling at first, but it offers over $5,000 in value in the first year. The card features a 100,000-point welcome bonus after spending $6,000 on purchases in the first three months and is loaded with perks, including an incredibly flexible $300 annual travel credit. You'll also get access to Chase Sapphire Lounges by The Club, a premium collection of airport lounges.
