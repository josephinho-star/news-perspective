‘Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma’ Comes to Digital, But When Is the ‘Camp Miasma’ Streaming Release Date?

2026-09-19 17:00

If you’re gay and love horror movies, I have great news: Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma—the new movie from director Jane Schoenbrun, that serves as a follow up to their 2024 beloved critical hit, I Saw the TV Glow—is now available to buy and rent on digital platforms to watch at home.

Written and directed by Schoenbrun, Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma takes a meta approach to the slasher genre. Hannah Einbinder stars as Kris Williams, a “Sundance darling” queer filmmaker who has been hired to reboot a fictional, problematic, cult-classic horror franchise from the ’80s known as Camp Miasma. (Basically, Friday the 13th.) Searching for inspiration, Kris tracks down the film’s original final girl, Billy Presley (played by Gillian Anderson), who has retreated from public life and lives as a recluse on the abandoned camp ground where the first film was shot.

If you didn’t get a chance to watch this weird, wonderful, queer horror romance in theaters, now is your chance to watch it at home. And hey, if it gets too real, you can always turn it off.

Where to watch the Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma movie on streaming:

As of Friday, September 18, Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma is now available to buy or rent on digital platforms. You can buy and rent digitally on Amazon Prime, Apple TV, Fandango At Home, and more. The price may vary depending on which platform you use to purchase the film, but on Amazon Prime the movie will cost $24.99 to buy, and $19.99 to rent. If you rent the movie, you’ll have 48 hours to view after hitting “play.”

Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma is not yet streaming on a subscription-based streaming platform like Netflix or HBO Max. However, because Camp Miasma is being released via Mubi, which has it’s own subscription-based streaming platform focused on international titles, arthouse, and independent film.

Prime has the option to add Mubi as an ad-on. For $14.99 per month, you can add hundreds of independent films as a part of your subscription, with Prime currently offering a free trial of Mubi.

When will Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma movie be streaming free on Mubi?

The Camp Miasma streaming release date on Mubi has not yet been announced, but we can guess that Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma might be streaming on Mubi around late November 2026.

Another recent Mubi release, Sound of Falling, began streaming on Mubi a little over three months after it opened in U.S. theaters. If Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma follows the release pattern, you might see it on Mubi around late November 2026, aka about three months after it opened in theaters.

That said, this is all speculation. The streaming release of Mubi films varies by title. If you want to watch the Camp Miasma movie online ASAP, you can buy or rent it on digital.
