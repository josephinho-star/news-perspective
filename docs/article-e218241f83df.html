Rays vs. Brewers Game 2 prediction, props: Drew Rasmussen primed to dominate in World Series

2026-10-24 20:00

The Rays began this 2026 World Series with a curveball.

The AL champs opted to push ace Drew Rasmussen back one day to get him extra rest, opting instead to start Nick Martinez in Game 1.

The gambit did not pay off Friday, with the Rays losing 3-2 in Milwaukee.

Ramussen will now be starting Game 2 on five days of rest against Brewers righty Logan Henderson, who is making his third postseason start.

The Brewers are -118 ome favorites at FanDuel on Saturday.

Rays vs. Brewers prediction

Rasmussen was overshadowed a bit over the course of the regular season.

At one point in the middle of the season, he was in the mix with Cam Schlittler and Dylan Cease for the American League Cy Young odds before it became a two-man race.

By the time the odds closed, he was 100/1 to win it, but that number doesn’t reflect just how great he pitched over the full season.

The Rays ace has been lights out going back to September.

Rasmussen has allowed just six earned runs over his last 46 ⅔ innings pitched, including three earned runs over his playoff-high 21 innings in his three October starts.

More importantly, he’s been able to pitch deep into games without trouble in the playoffs.
