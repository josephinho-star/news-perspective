Best Buy sells Blu-rays online and in-store again

2026-11-18 19:31

Best Buy is selling Blu-rays directly through its website and in physical stores again after exiting Blu-ray and DVD sales in 2023.

The Best Buy website’s movie section currently lists 15 Blu-rays (including one two-pack) that are “sold & shipped by Best Buy.” New titles available include The Odyssey (2026), Project Hail Mary (2026), Supergirl (2026), Super Mario Galaxy (2026), and Obsession (2025). Best Buy is also selling some older titles on Blu-ray, including It’s a Wonderful Life (1943), Saving Private Ryan (1998), and The Godfather (1972).

Most of the Blu-rays are available for purchase online and can be delivered to an address or picked up at a Best Buy store within a few days. As of this writing, The Odyssey appears to be in stock at Best Buy stores, as it’s currently listed as available for pickup within as little as an hour.

Ars Technica reached out to Best Buy to ask whether customers can expect the store to continue selling Blu-rays moving forward.

In October 2023, Best Buy announced that it would stop selling Blu-rays and DVDs online and in stores starting in 2024.

“To state the obvious, the way we watch movies and TV shows is much different today than it was decades ago. Making this change gives us more space and opportunity to bring customers new and innovative tech for them to explore, discover and enjoy,” a Best Buy spokesperson said in a statement issued at the time.

Last year, the Best Buy website started selling Blu-rays and DVDs from third parties via its online marketplace.

Numerous other companies have reduced or ended their Blu-ray and/or DVD businesses in recent years. Target, for instance, only sells the discs in stores during special occasions (like holidays or for big releases) now. Disney outsourced its DVD and Blu-ray business to Sony, which subsequently stopped selling recordable Blu-rays to people, and DVD rental company Redbox went out of business.
