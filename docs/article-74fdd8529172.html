The lululemon holiday collection is here, and it’ll be on everyone’s wish lists

2026-11-16 21:07

We’re breaking a sweat adding these new styles to our carts.

The lululemon holiday collection just dropped, filled with fresh, luxe-looking hues and cool-weather designs worth gifting — or keeping.

Whether the sporty gear inspires you to get active or to add a little extra flair to your athleisure wardrobe, you won’t want to miss the limited-edition pieces. From the cult-favorite belt bags stars love to buttery-soft sweatshirts, the drop includes something for everyone.

Scroll on for a few top picks, including the comfy faux fur mules shoppers say they never want to take off.

The remedy for winter blues? A big, fluffy and ultra-soft sweatshirt. This elevated yet sporty style is already garnering rave reviews, like, “Big cozy is right! This sweatshirt is beyond comfortable!”

Made with a cotton-blend fabric, the style comes in four colors — including the viral Fuchsia Taffy Tone.

Whether you’re running laps or errands, the Define Track Pants will help you do it in style. The breathable, four-way stretch fabric and moisture-wicking technology mean you’ll feel as cool as you look. Plus, there’s even a card sleeve hiding among the zippered pockets.

Hurry, though, the style is already selling out — as is the matching Cropped Track Jacket.

Celebrities love lululemon’s top-rated belt bags just as much as the rest of us. And the winter collection’s new colors are a delight to own (or gift). This smoky lavender almost reads like a neutral, while other shade options range from deep plum to green.

Available in three versions for different bust sizes, the Wunder Train bra blends fashion and function. The holiday drop features new shades — including the buzzy fuchsia hue — all made from lululemon’s fastest-drying fabric. They’re so beloved, one reviewer even admits to owning the bra in 10 colors.

Speaking of covetable shades, the new Cranberry Brown colorway is drop-dead gorgeous — and very on-trend. Not to mention, countless stars wear the Align Leggings.

The best-selling Define Jacket has also earned legions of famous fans over the years. This updated silhouette is slightly cropped, so it pairs especially nicely with baggier pants. Besides the seasonal shades, the gold zipper definitely elevates the beloved style.

Speaking of cozy, the new faux fur version of the iconic WayDay mule is so good, fans say they never want to take them off. “So adorable, so comfortable!” reads one recent review. Available in cream or black, they’re also ultra-giftable.

Zip it up or zip it down, gift it or keep it; this SuperLoft hoodie with thumbholes is another classic that fans buy in multiples.

Hey, low rider, these bottoms are made for you. Promising buttery softness and a weightless feel, they have an adjustable hem with a built-in tie that can be cinched or left loose.

Equally perfect for morning jogs or coffee runs, this jacket offers a cozy fleece lining. In addition to being water-resistant, UV-protective and windproof, it also has a slightly relaxed cut, so it can be layered over other pieces. (Size down if you prefer it fitted!)
