Where to watch ‘Gilmore Girls: A Year in the Life’ after it leaves Netflix

2026-11-20 19:00

It feels like we’re back in Stars Hollow because Gilmore Girls is having a moment.

Amy Sherman-Palladino’s beloved mother/daughter dramedy starring Lauren Graham and Alexis Bledel originally aired for seven seasons from 2000 to 2007 on The WB and CW. The cult classic eventually returned for a sequel series on Netflix, 2016’s Gilmore Girls: A Year in the Life, which took place nine years after the events of the original show’s series finale.

Fast forward to 2026 and the show that helped launch the careers of Melissa McCarthy, Jared Padalecki, Milo Ventimiglia, Matt Czuchry, Chad Michael Murray, Adam Brody, Krysten Ritter, and so many more is back in the zeitgeist with the new documentary Drink Coffee, Talk Fast: A Documentary about Gilmore Girls.

Now available to rent on various streaming platforms, the doc doesn’t include Sherman-Palladino, Graham, or Bledel, but it does feature interviews with fan favorites like Scott Patterson, Kelly Bishop, Jared Padalecki, Keiko Agena, Chad Michael Murray, and Vanessa Marano. The original Gilmore Girls is now streaming on Hulu and Disney+, and Gilmore Girls: A Year in the Life is currently on Netflix, but unfortunately, the sequel series is set to exit the platform.

Where can you watch Gilmore Girls: A Year in the Life after it exits Netflix? Here’s everything you need to know.

Is Gilmore Girls: A Year in the Life Leaving Netflix?

Yep. Sadly, the last day to watch the sequel series on Netflix is Tuesday, November 24.

Where To Watch Gilmore Girls: A Year in the Life After It Leaves Netflix:

When one streaming door closes, another opens! Gilmore Girls: A Year in the Life debuts Wednesday, November 25 on Hulu and Disney+ (for bundle subscribers).

Is Gilmore Girls Streaming on Netflix?

Nope. But you can watch all seven seasons of the series on Hulu and Disney+.

How To Watch Gilmore Girls and Gilmore Girls: A Year in the Life on Hulu:

If you’re new to Hulu, the basic (with ads) price is $11.99/month. If you want to upgrade to Hulu ad-free, it costs $18.99/month.

If you want to stream even more and save a few bucks a month while you’re at it, we recommend subscribing to one of the Disney+ Bundles, all of which include Hulu. These bundles start at $12.99/month for ad-supported Disney+ and Hulu and goes up to $32.99/month for Disney+, Hulu, and Max, all ad-free.

Where To Watch The New Gilmore Girls Doc Online:

The new doc is now available to rent or purchase on Apple TV, Amazon, and additional streaming platforms.
