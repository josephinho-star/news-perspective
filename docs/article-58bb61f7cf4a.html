Live Q&A: SCMP answers your questions about Washington Xi-Trump summit

2026-09-25 05:55

China Editor Wendy Wu tackles reader queries in this live question-and-answer session, after the two leaders’ closely watched summit

This live article is freely available to our registered users. Please log in or create an account.

Chinese President Xi Jinping and his US counterpart Donald Trump have met in Washington for an eagerly anticipated summit, during which the leaders discussed AI cooperation, their countries’ ties and more.

To help South China Morning Post readers unpack the events and figure out what it all means, SCMP’s Wendy Wu will answer questions from readers in this live question-and-answer session.

Wu joined the SCMP in 2015 to report on China. Her journalism career spans two decades, covering a range of topics including diplomacy and economy. Based in Beijing, she is China Editor of the SCMP, following her stint as Political Economy Editor.

The session will last for around 60-120 minutes.
