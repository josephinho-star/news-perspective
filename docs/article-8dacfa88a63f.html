Mariah Carey sparks concern amongst fans with overly ‘stiff’ performance: ‘Rest or retire’

2026-11-18 19:43

Carey, who stunned in a gold sequin gown, had to be assisted by one of the backup dancers as she walked to the center of the stage.

Then, four of the dancers surrounded the pop star as they helped her lean back and lift her leg.

Carey, 57, did not move much on stage without assistance from her dancers, which caused fans to wonder if something was wrong with her physical health.

“This is sad … rest or retire and focus on you girl,” one fan commented on the video.

“She’s only 57 years old. Why she acting like she’s 80? 😂,” added another.

“The fact that she needs assistance in this manner and making it like it’s part of the performance is so telling that something is not right,” a third user said.

“I want my money back, and I wasn’t even there. She lip-synced. She is like a robot.😪🤣,” wrote a fourth netizen.

“If you watched other recent videos of Mariah, you’ll notice that something seems to be wrong with her,” a fifth fan claimed.

“It gives me the same feeling I got from Britney during her conservatorship. Something is seriously wrong behind the scenes, something we know nothing about. I’m afraid this could end in tragedy,” said another person.

Page Six has reached out to Carey’s reps but did not immediately hear back.
