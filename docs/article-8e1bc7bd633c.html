Iran, UAE leaders discuss de-escalation as oil supply worries spike

2026-09-12 13:59

President and crown prince meet for first time since tensions intensified after Iran hit back at US allies following US-Israeli strikes

The meeting, confirmed by the Iranian embassy in New Delhi, brought the Iran and UAE leaders face to face months after bilateral frictions intensified sharply in late February, as US-Israeli strikes saw Tehran hit back at American allies in the region.

The crown prince, Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, said in a social media post after the meeting that the two sides discussed regional and international issues, “along with the importance of supporting de-escalation efforts, reducing tensions, and achieving stability in the region”.

Retaliatory strikes across the Gulf region saw Iran launch sustained ballistic missile and drone attacks against the United Arab Emirates that damaged energy infrastructure, ports and commercial facilities across Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
