Julia Stiles supported by ‘Save the Last Dance’ co-star Sean Patrick Thomas for ‘DWTS’ ‘Super Bowl Night’

2026-10-14 14:08

Julia Stiles’ “Save the Last Dance” co-star Sean Patrick Thomas supported the actress as she honored their 2001 movie on “Dancing with the Stars” Tuesday.

For “Super Bowl Night,” Stiles, 45, and her pro partner, Ezra Sosa, performed a hip-hip inspired jazz routine to Jennifer Lopez’s song “Jenny from the Block,” with some callbacks to Stiles’ moves from the beloved film.

During Stiles’ epic solo part of the dance, Thomas, 55, could be seen in the background over Sosa’s shoulder, holding his jacket just like his character, Derek, does in the movie.

In a clip shared by the ABC show’s Instagram account, Thomas explained he “was over the moon” to be in the audience supporting Stiles.

“We had a great time doing ‘Save the Last Dance’ back in the day, and she brought it all the way back here tonight,” he said.

Stiles and Sosa’s performance got rave reviews from the judges, with Bruno Tonioli declaring it was the pair’s best dance yet.

“That was so great,” said Carrie Ann Inaba. “It’s so wonderful to see you dancing like that with that confidence and that kind of grit, girl.”

Derek Hough invited Stiles over for a hug, before he told the actress, “We all were waiting for that. Were we not?”

“And you did not disappoint,” Hough added. “Your charisma. Your performance. All of it. Wonderful.”

Up in the skybox, Stiles gushed to co-host Juliane Hough that she’s “been wanting to do that for 25 years.”

The judges then gave Stiles and Sosa a 33 out of 40.

Stiles said she was feeling “so good” about the score, while Sosa explained that the actress has “worked so hard” on the dance show.

“I’m just seeing someone live out their dream,” he added, “and it means the world to me.”

Stiles famously played aspiring ballet dancer Sara Johnson in “Save the Last Dance.” Thomas played her on-screen romantic partner.

“I think it’s a bit of a disadvantage,” she told Entertainment Weekly. “To be honest, I stopped taking dance classes after that movie because people expected me to be a great dancer.”

Instead, the “10 Things I Hate About You” star said she’s more of a “home dancer.”

“I love dancing at home, and part of my reason for coming on this show was to find the joy in dance again … find my confidence in dance again,” Stiles explained.

“Making that movie, yeah, it was great training and it was a wonderful opportunity, but I felt very, very self-conscious and kind of tense,” she added. “And I want to let that go.”

“Dancing with the Stars” airs Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET on ABC and streams on Disney+.
