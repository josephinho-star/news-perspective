This bodysuit rivals SKIMS’ bestselling shapewear — And it’s under $30

2026-09-09 14:29

One thing’s for sure: Kim Kardashian knows her shapewear. But what if we told you you could achieve the same snatched look for less?

The reality star makes the most stunning skintight bodysuits and tops with SKIMS. Although we do think they’re worth the splurge, we found a designer lookalike that made us do a double take.

The SUUKSESS Backless Cami Bodysuit hugs the body in all the right places, and it’s under $29 on Amazon right now.

This sultry one-piece features an open back and cheeky thong, made in a high-stretch, breathable fabric that conforms to every shape and size. Plus, its double-layered, not sheer, so you can wear it by itself.

Shoppers can customize the fit thanks to adjustable spaghetti straps. Expect to feel slight compression while wearing to really achieve that hourglass figure.

The style comes in 26 neutral and bright colors, including brown, sapphire, army green and nude.

Pair it with a bomber jacket and jeans for fall, or wear it by itself with trousers and some jewelry for added flair.

One buyer rhapsodized, “Obsessed and just ordered more. The fit is so good. I’ve never really been able to wear things like this, but this one doesn’t cut you off anywhere! I don’t need a bra, it’s super lightweight and buttery smooth and soft.”

Another raved, “Beautiful bodysuit! Love, love, love the material. It’s buttery soft! The fit is great for a long or short torso, due to the adjustments. Love that you can tighten the straps as well.”

Save SKIMS for an upcoming holiday gift, and in the meantime, shop SUUKSESS Backless Cami Bodysuit while it’s on sale for 26% off.
