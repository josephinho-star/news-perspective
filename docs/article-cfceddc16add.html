Celtics set franchise mark with 42-9 lead over Mavs after 1 quarter

2026-11-16 00:24

BOSTON -- The Celtics led the Dallas Mavericks 42-9 at the end of the first quarter Sunday, marking the franchise's largest lead at the end of the period in franchise history.

It's the best scoring margin by any team in any quarter since 1987, when the Los Angeles Lakers outscored the Sacramento Kings 40-4 in the first quarter. It's also tied for the third-best mark by any team in a quarter in the shot clock era, according to ESPN Research.

The Celtics have led by at least 40 points in two other games this season, against Charlotte on Nov. 6 (46 points) and Chicago on Oct. 26 (40 points).

Playing on four days' rest, the Celtics sprinted out to a 24-4 advantage, with Derrick White (11) and Jordan Walsh (eight) scoring 19 of those points.

The Mavericks, who lost in Cleveland on Saturday, looked sluggish throughout the opening period Sunday, missing 16 of their 18 field goal attempts and going 0-for-8 from the 3-point line. By contrast, Boston shot 59% and knocked down seven 3s.

The Celtics took a 67-37 lead into halftime.

The Associated Press contributed to this report.
