2 men rescued after private plane makes emergency landing in Black Sea off Turkey

2026-09-09 14:24

Two Belgian men were rescued on Wednesday after a small private plane flying from Georgia to northern Turkey made an emergency landing in the Black Sea following an apparent engine failure, the DHA news agency reported.

The two-seat Aerospool WT9 Dynamic had taken off from Batumi and was headed for Ordu-Giresun Airport when it reported an engine problem and attempted to divert to Rize-Artvin Airport, according to the Interior Ministry and the Rize Governor’s Office.

The aircraft was unable to reach the airport and landed in the water about four nautical miles off the coast of Rize province.

The Interior Ministry said a coast guard vessel, two boats and a diving team were dispatched to the area. Rescuers found the plane floating on the water and removed both men, who were conscious.

The Rize Governor’s Office identified them as Werner Vlasselaer and Christian Peiffer. Rize Governor İhsan Selim Baydaş said the men, ages 55 and 69, respectively, were taken to Kaçkar State Hospital for examination and were not in life-threatening condition.

“They could not get close enough to the airport and had to land in the sea,” Baydaş said. “Our coast guard teams reached the aircraft quickly.”

Baydaş said the men had reported an engine failure and that no other cause had been identified in the initial assessment. The aircraft, which is registered in France, remained afloat after the landing and was being recovered.

An investigation into the incident is underway.
