Thomas Markle, father of Meghan, cut off ankle monitor days after arrest

2026-11-10 20:44

The estranged father of Britain’s Duchess of Sussex has been accused of keeping child sex abuse materials

The 82-year-old estranged father of Meghan, the Duchess of Sussex, cut an electronic monitoring device off his ankle, just days after his arrest for keeping child sexual abuse material in storage units in California, authorities said on Tuesday.

Thomas Markle was in custody awaiting a hearing in federal court in Tampa, Florida, First Assistant US Attorney Bill Essayli said.

He was arrested shortly after he cut off the ankle bracelet at 1.45am, Essayli said.
