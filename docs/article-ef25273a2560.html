At Least 12 Injured in Shooting Near Florida A&M Football Stadium

2026-10-25 14:19

At least 12 people were injured last around 8 p.m. after “a fight broke out” in a crowded area near the Florida A&M football stadium, according to WBAL-TV.

Eleven of the wounded sustained gunshot wounds.

The New York Times reported that six of the wounded were minors. One of the wounded individuals “was believed to be a Florida A&M University student.”

Six of the shooting victims remain hospitalized Sunday morning and one of them is in critical condition.

It was homecoming weekend at Florida A&M, ” one of the nation’s largest historically Black colleges and universities.”

No one has been arrested in connection with the shooting.
