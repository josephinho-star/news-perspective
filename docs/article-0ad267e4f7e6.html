Karl-Anthony Towns’ ‘amazing’ vanilla body wash is on sale for $9: ‘I always got to smell good’

2026-09-14 20:50

He’s at the top of his grooming game.

Following his star-studded wedding, NBA champ Karl-Anthony Towns shared his off-court must-haves with the Strategist last week. And alongside a massage gun and Knicks gear, we spotted a delectable tip for smelling incredible: Dove Men+Care Moisturizing Body Wash in Bourbon Vanilla and Hazelnut.

“I always got to smell good,” the All-Star told the outlet, adding that Dove has “amazing body wash scents.”

Even better, you don’t need a baller budget to smell like the sports icon; there’s a $2 clickable coupon on Amazon right now, making it a $9 body care steal.

Free of parabens and sulfates, the Moisturizing Body Wash is made from 92% naturally derived ingredients, including plant-based moisturizers.

Bourbon Vanilla + Hazelnut is a soft, autumn-ready pick, but it also comes in Blackcurrant + Cedarwood and Coconut + Guava for any scent preference — perfect for gifting or keeping.

Growing up, Towns’ mother loved incense and the candles in the house, so the scents “draw [him] back to mindfulness.”

“The shower, which is usually my only quiet time of the day, is special and kind of therapeutic. The rest of the day may be hectic, but I know that moment’s for me and myself and I,” the Knicks player relatably added.

Shoppers rate Towns’ pick highly, writing things like it “smells great and is nourishing for dry skin.”

The same person raved, “I’m buying some fall and winter fragrances and needed a body wash that would complement my boozy, spiced and gourmand scents that I decided on. This body wash did not disappoint. It smells fantastic.”

Another five-star review added that the subtle scent “provides great odor protection,” and “gets compliments regularly.”
