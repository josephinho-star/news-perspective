Mets have interest in Ty France with multiteam battle brewing

2026-11-20 01:10

The Mets are interested in 1B Ty France, who had a big year in San Diego (.865 OPS), and they should be. He’s a better fit than Alec Bohm, who’s drawn interest from the Yankees too.

The Padres would like to bring France back, and the Pirates, Guardians, Diamondbacks, Brewers and White Sox all make sense too.

Righty-hitting catcher Carson Kelly would help the Yankees.

The Yankees expressed interest in perennial 20-20 OF Randy Arozarena. But the Yankees don’t seem ready to do anything big yet.
