Pet owners, this 44% off robot vacuum can clean floors and find your furry friend

Cleaning is one of those annoying duties we all put off.

What if there was a way to automate the most tedious chore in your home and leave it all up to a capable, well-built robot vacuum instead? That’s exactly what the Roborock Saros 10R Robot Vacuum and Mop is, and right now it’s on sale for 44% off just in time for Labor Day weekend.

With an ultra-slim profile that’s designed to maneuver around furniture with ease and 3D sensing technology that can even detect barriers like furniture legs, this vacuum is one that makes your cleaning experience easy enough to never have to monitor.

This Roborock option has a side brush, making it easier to clean tough corners, and 22,000 Pa of suction that’s powerful enough to clean all kinds of flooring, whether it’s carpet or hardwood.

There’s also a tangle-free brush that works well for both human and pet hair, and an auto-cleaning mop, which automatically self-cleans and dries completely so you don’t have to worry about lingering odors from your vacuum.

The whole thing works quietly, too, so it won’t disturb little ones or pets playing. Finally, there’s voice assistant compatibility, and with the Roborock app you can customize schedules and even use the app’s one-click pet search to quickly locate your furry friend — yes, this robot vacuum also functions as a camera for your pets.

Shop the Roborock Saros 10R Robot Vacuum and Mop now at 44% off during its Labor Day sale — you might not see this robot vacuum priced this well again.
