Ciara and Russell Wilson share adorable video sharing pregnancy news with kids: ‘Best moment of all’

2026-09-08 02:07

Ciara and Russell Wilson are over the moon about their recently announced pregnancy — but it turns out that their kids might be the most excited of all about the new arrival.

In an adorable video posted to Instagram Monday by the couple, Wilson gathers the family — Future, 12 (whose father is rapper Future), Sienna, 9, Win, 6, and Amora, 2 — and tells them they need to take a group picture and a video to send to relatives.

“Why?” asks Future.

“Because we gotta send it to them,” states Wilson. He then says, “Ready?” and leads the family in a count of “Uno, dos, tres, quatro, cinco!”

As the kids look confused, Wilson clarifies, “Mommy’s pregnant!”

“Actually?” says Future, then yells, “No way…the speculations were true! I knew it,” as his younger siblings run about screaming with excitement.

The couple termed the surprise “the best moment of all” on their Instagram video.

The couple publicly announced the pregnancy Sunday on Instagram, posting a video of Ciara hanging up white t-shirts on a clothesline representing each member of their family, and ending with a fifth onesie to represent the impending new arrival.

Both mom and dad captioned the post, “One more person to Love 🖐🏽🥺🖤.”

Wilson has not been shy about wanting to continue to expand the family, having asked his wife about another baby “shortly after” baby No. 4 joined the family.

“He was like … ‘So, cinco?’” Ciara related to Us Weekly in June. “And I [was] like, ‘Wait a minute now, like, slow it down, sir. We just had a baby here. Let’s be in this moment here, sir.’”

However, she told the outlet: “I love being a mom. It’s my favorite job of all. And I couldn’t stand here and tell you, like, that I don’t see that happening one day in our lives to have cinco.”
