Marine Le Pen and Jordan Bardella struggle to stay on the same page

2026-09-04 02:00

PARIS — Jordan Bardella spent months preparing to replace Marine Le Pen as France’s far-right presidential candidate. Now he has a different challenge: falling back in line behind her.

In just a few days, the two leaders of the National Rally have struck competing notes on sensitive issues, from pensions and the high-profile departure of a top adviser to a long-standing proposal by Le Pen to ban hijabs.

Once seen as his party’s most likely candidate, the 30-year-old member of the European Parliament had to step aside in early July after a court shortened Le Pen’s sentence for embezzlement of EU funds, allowing her to run.
