Make VR your reality with a discounted Meta Quest 3S for Black Friday (and Golf+ for free!)

2026-11-26 10:26

Forget “GTA6” and “Ocarina of Time,” if you really want to step up your gaming you need a Meta Quest 3S. The best-selling VR headset may not be a new release this holiday season, but there’s no gaming experience quite like it out there — and it’s 15% off right now for Black Friday.

Don’t just pretend to be the main character. Strap on the Meta headset and step into their shoes. When I first entered the world of Meta Quest, I was blown away by the experience of being fully immersed in the survival horror world of “Resident Evil 4.” I almost crawled out of my skin in fear while exploring the sewers of Derry, Maine in an app promoting the movie “It.” Everything I have ever played on my headset has made me feel like kid again discovering the world for the first time.

The headset’s internal processor, Snapdragon XR2, helps every game and app run smoothly and lightning-fast. This latest version of the Meta Quest has more system storage and lasts longer between charges.

It’s time to move on from those days couch-rotting playing Playstation and Nintendo. With a Meta Quest, you have access to so many games that can actually help you reach your daily fitness goals like my favorite, “Beat Saber,” which challenges you to work out with a light saber and keep up with the rhythm.

Longing for the links? This deal comes with Golf+ pre-installed, which is one of the best sports games available on the system. But don’t take my word for it. According to our Commerce Partnerships Manager Frank Massaro, “Golf+ is the official reality game of the PGA Tour, so the effort put into bringing the courses to VR is very apparent from the first time you put on the headset.

Golf may very well be the hardest game to play. While easier to hit the ball, every facet of the game is properly mapped and coded into this simulation. This is honestly the next best thing if you can’t get to your local muni, top golf or 5iron golf to play. I found myself just going to hit the range and hitting golf balls for hours on end, much like I do when the weather is nice!

A Meta Quest headset is more than just a gaming console. It’s a whole lifestyle upgrade and now it’s even more affordable for the holiday shopping season.
