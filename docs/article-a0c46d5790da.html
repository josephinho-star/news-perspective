Amon-Ra St Brown kicks off Thanksgiving with ridiculous one-handed grab, stanky leg celebration

2026-11-26 20:11

Amon-Ra St. Brown came to play on Turkey Day.

The Detroit Lions wide receiver wasted no time in showing off his catching abilities against the Chicago Bears in their Thanksgiving showdown. He was also sure to send a message to the Bears' head coach, Ben Johnson, after the fact.

Barely two minutes into the contest, Detroit quarterback Jared Goff threw a pass to a streaking St. Brown running toward the sideline. Most wideouts wouldn't have been able to haul in the pass, but St. Brown is not like most wideouts, and snagged the football out of the air with just one hand.

After making the one-handed snag for a pickup of 27 yards, St. Brown got up off of the turf and did the stanky leg dance, which was undoubtedly a shot at Johnson, who has pulled out the same dance move inside the Bears' locker room following wins this season.

St. Brown ultimately picked up 46 receiving yards on Detroit's opening drive of the game, although the Lions weren't able to find the end zone after his miraculous catch and settled for a 24-yard field goal.

The Bears-Lions matchup in Detroit is serving as the kickoff to one of the best Thanksgiving Day slates in NFL history.

After the 8-2 NFC foes handle their business, the Philadelphia Eagles and Dallas Cowboys match up in an important clash in the lowly NFC East, with both teams holding losing records still somehow alive in the divisional race.

The Thanksgiving nightcap will feature Josh Allen and the Buffalo Bills playing host to Patrick Mahomes and the Kansas City Chiefs in one of the most-anticipated matchups of the NFL season thus far.

Weather, as it typically does in late November, could play a factor, with rain and high winds in the forecast in Upstate New York.
