A.J. Brown suffers apparent injury in Patriots debut against Seattle Seahawks in Week 1

2026-09-10 02:55

A.J. Brown’s highly anticipated debut with the New England Patriots was interrupted by an apparent injury in the third quarter of Wednesday’s NFL season opener.

Brown appeared to suffer the injury while trying to make a catch at Lumen Field against the Seattle Seahawks. The game was billed as a rematch of Super Bowl LX, which Seattle won in February by defeating New England 29-10 to claim the franchise’s second Vince Lombardi Trophy.

New England led 10-0 when Brown exited the game Wednesday.

Brown went down after a Seahawks defender rolled into him and remained on the field after the play. After medical personnel tended to Brown on the field, he made his way to the sideline without assistance. He later entered the blue medical tent before eventually heading to the locker room wearing only one cleat.

Brown was later ruled out for the remainder of the game because of what was described as an ankle injury. Seahawks quarterback Sam Darnold left the game in the first quarter and was later ruled out. Drew Lock replaced Darnold under center for Seattle.

New England took a 10-3 lead over Seattle into the fourth quarter.
