Ella Langley hits back at haters ‘picking on’ her in concert rant after beating Mariah Carey’s record

2026-10-01 12:11

“I don’t think I’ve ever had more opinions on myself in my life than I have in this last year,” the country music singer told the crowd at her Sept. 25 concert in Columbus, Ohio, per video shared to TikTok.

“The way I look, the way I speak, the way I dress, the way I move, the way I sing, the way I write,” she added.

Langley, 27, said the constant criticism “bothered” her at first.

“I was like, ‘What am I going to do about this? If this is what I’m signing up to do, I don’t know about this. I signed up to sing music and I love to do what I do, but if this is what it’s going to be the whole entire time, I mean s–t, I don’t know if I’m strong enough for this,'” she recalled onstage.

“I started to think about the girl that they’re kind of picking on is the girl that got me where I’m sitting here today,” the “Choosin’ Texas” singer continued.

“That’s the girl that rode in a 15-passenger van night after night after night. She rode in a Honda Accord night after night after night, chased her dream, living on bar food, living on tip jar money.”

Langley said she “didn’t have anything” but a “dream and the ability to get up and do it every single day.”

“So I’m going to tell you right now. I am proud of that girl that everybody seems to be picking on all the time,” she concluded.

The “You Look Like You Love Me” songstress’ rant came days after her single, “Choosin’ Texas,” became the longest-running No. 1 single on the Billboard Hot 100 list.

The song surpassed Carey’s “All I Want for Christmas Is You,” which spent 22 weeks atop the chart.

After the historic achievement, Langley spoke out, saying she was speechless and couldn’t “find the words to help explain what’s going on in my brain today.”

“Thank you so much. Thank you for loving this song. For listening to it. I don’t even know what to say at this point,” she said in a Sept. 22 Instagram video.

“I’m running out of hyperboles to describe how I feel, so I will just let my dog in the background show you his excitement.”

Carey, 57, has not publicly reacted to Langley beating her record.
