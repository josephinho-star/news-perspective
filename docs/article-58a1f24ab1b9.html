Giants' JJ McCarthy could have bigger role in offense, John Harbaugh indicates

2026-10-15 22:28

J.J. McCarthy might not be making a giant impact in New York just yet, but that could change soon.

McCarthy played five snaps in the New York Giants' 27-27 tie with the Washington Commanders on Sunday, and head coach John Harbaugh indicated more snaps could be coming for the 23-year-old.

"The benefits of it? It’s a change of pace," Harbaugh said Wednesday at a press conference. "What you saw is probably not all we have for him right now. Whether or not he’s out there or not will be determined based on whatever happens. But we’ll see if it’s something that can help us."

The head coach said the team is always looking for opportunities to improve and wants to explore all avenues.

"One thing about it, to me, it’s like, what’s the benefit of not doing everything you can to use all the guys you have? We’re always going to be searching for opportunities to use our players, give them an opportunity to help us win games. I think we’d be negligent if we didn’t try to do that," Harbaugh said.

One of McCarthy’s plays was a screen pass to Malik Nabers that was called back because of a penalty. Three of his plays involved using the threat of his running ability to spark the struggling running game.
