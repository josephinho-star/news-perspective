California Hard Rock Hotel transforms into ‘Haunted Manor’ for Halloween with 60K square feet of terror

2026-10-19 21:06

A Hard Rock Hotel location in California is transforming into an insane 60,000-square-foot spooky night of terror for Halloween.

Hard Rock Hotel San Diego is hosting an event called “Haunted Manor” on Halloween, taking over 60,000 square feet of the venue with numerous DJs and spooky rooftop dancing.

“With over 15 DJs spanning 3 eerie floors, 4 massive dance floors, and 7 chilling rooms, it’s the ultimate haunt for beats & treats this Halloween,” Hard Rock said.

The event will run from 9 p.m. to 2 a.m. and will have five different experiences such as “The Graveyard,” “The Haunted Ballroom” and “The Forbidden Library.”

Some of those experiences such as the “The Forbidden Library” will have a Halloween themed lounge with “music, cocktails, and a dark, mysterious atmosphere.” Another area will have a pool and a main party floor with downtown views.

One section of the Manor is called “The Secret Door” where guests can have a “hidden experience for those curious enough to step through and discover what’s beyond.”

Tickets to the event start at $91.05, including fees, and people can get expedited entry through a fast pass. VIP passes are also available, with top-tier prices reaching up to $3000.

Haunted Manor is only for adults 21 and re-entry will not be allowed.

Aside from Hard Rock’s Haunted Manor, there are other Halloween events that people can attend such as the San Diego Zombie Crawl.

San Diego Zombie Crawl is a massive Halloween bar hop where one ticket allows guests entry to nearly 25 different Gaslamp Quarter bars and clubs.

The Zombie Crawl takes place across four dates –– with October 31 being the last –– where people are dressed up in costumes ready to party.
