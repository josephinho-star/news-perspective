NHL Power Rankings: Updated poll, plus each team's points vs. preseason projections

2026-11-14 12:27

Closing in on the quarter mark for NHL teams -- which is exactly 21 games, thanks to the expanded 84-game schedule -- and some teams have emerged as clear Stanley Cup contenders while others have cropped up as front-runners for strong positioning in the draft lottery.

The Hurricanes, who are No. 1 in the Power Rankings again, are in that top group. But just how good have they been, based on expectations?

This week, alongside our new 1-32 poll, we examine how each team has done compared with their preseason over/under point total. And there are some shockers!

How we rank: A panel of ESPN hockey commentators, analysts, reporters and editors sends a 1-32 poll based on the games through Wednesday. Those ballots are combined to come up with the rankings you see below.
