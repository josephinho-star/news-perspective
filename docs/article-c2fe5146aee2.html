Is Sports Betting Legal in Missouri?

Sports betting is legal in Missouri. Legislation was passed by the government and overseen by the Missouri Gaming Commission, effective Dec. 1, 2025. As of September 2026, there have been crackdowns on prediction markets and unlicensed operators. You must be 21 years of age to bet on sports in Missouri and physically located in the state to place the wager.

Missouri Sports Betting Laws and Timeline

Missouri has worked on passing sports betting since 2022, but finally got it approved via Amendment 2 on November 5, 2024. The Missouri Gaming Commission opened license applications in the middle of 2025 before officially launching on December 1, 2025. Recently, the Missouri Gaming Commission has started sending cease-and-desist orders to prediction markets offering sports events contracts.

College Sports Betting Rules in Missouri

College sports wagers are permitted in Missouri, including bets on teams that are located within the state. You can also bet on college player props, but they cannot be from an in-State team. This means you can wager on out-of-state college player props.
