Tanker captain, officer arrested over cargo ship sinking off İstanbul

2026-09-04 07:46

A Turkish court has arrested the captain and third officer of an oil tanker that collided with a cargo ship off İstanbul, causing it to sink and leaving all 10 crew members missing, Turkish media reported Friday.

The arrests came late Thursday, a day after the empty oil tanker ALSU struck the Tuğberk İmamoğlu, which was carrying rolled steel, in the Sea of Marmara before dawn on Wednesday.

A court in Silivri, on the western edge of İstanbul province near the site of the collision, ordered the arrest of the two officers, while four other suspects were released under judicial supervision, the DHA news agency reported. No information was immediately available about the charges against them.

The Tuğberk İmamoğlu sank within minutes in waters approximately 900 meters (3,000 feet) deep, some 18 nautical miles off Silivri. All 10 crew members remain missing.

Although investigators have yet to recover the cargo ship’s voyage data recorder, or black box, a preliminary expert report prepared for the Silivri Chief Public Prosecutor’s Office identified multiple breaches of safety procedures aboard the tanker.

“The fundamental root cause … resulting in a catastrophe was the deficiencies and failures on the bridge of the M/T ALSU,” the report said, according to Agence France-Presse, which obtained a copy.

The report also identified shortcomings aboard the Tuğberk İmamoğlu. Its findings remain preliminary pending the recovery and examination of the voyage data recorder.

Search-and-rescue operations for the missing crew members continued Friday.

Turkey’s state-run Anadolu news agency said nine ALSU crew members, excluding the cook, had been detained and questioned by police. Six of them appeared in court Thursday, with two arrested and four released under judicial supervision.
