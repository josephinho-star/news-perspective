Jets takeaways, report card from NFL Week 10 loss to Bills

2026-11-16 00:13

The Jets lost their fifth straight game Sunday, a 31-12 loss to the Bills that drops them to 2-8 this season. Here are some takeaways from the game:

Take flight with the Jets

Text with Brian Costello all season as he brings Sports+ subscribers the latest Jets intel from on the field and off.

SIGN UP NOW1. The Jets now have gone nine straight quarters without scoring a touchdown. That seems like it should be an impossible feat in today’s NFL. They had 376 yards in the game yet could not reach the end zone. It is the same story week after week. They move the ball but then stall when they get into their opponent’s territory. They went 0-for-2 in the red zone Sunday, continuing what has been a trend for weeks. It is perplexing, and they do not seem to have any solutions. Maybe they will break through against the Chargers or the Dolphins, two teams that have a combined one win between them (against the Jets, of course), but it seems like this is going to be the defining quality of these Jets.

2. With the way the Jets can’t score touchdowns, just imagine where this team would be without kicker Jason Sanders. He booted another four field goals Sunday and is 25-of-28 this season. I was dead wrong about Sanders in training camp; I thought the Jets made a mistake by giving the job to Sanders and letting go of Nick Folk, who was cut last week by the Falcons. Sanders has been the team’s most reliable player, and he might be the team MVP right now.

3. The offensive struggles stood out in this game, but let’s not give the defense a pass. They again collapsed in the second half, giving up 21 points in the final two quarters. They were killed by big plays. Josh Allen had throws of 54 and 45 yards, and James Cook III raced through the defense for a 74-yard touchdown that iced the game. The Jets defense has now given up 158 points in the last five games. The unit needs a complete overhaul this offseason.
