This tech stock has been nearly cut in half this year. I think Wall Street is getting it wrong

2026-11-19 19:08

Today I'm looking at a company with one of the coolest names in tech: AppLovin . The Palo Alto, California-based company operates an AI-driven digital advertising platform that helps businesses reach customers and mobile app developers make money from ads. Its AXON technology matches advertisers with potential customers to boost the effectiveness of their campaigns. Key points Revenue growth is accelerating again. AppLovin's AI technology could help it fend off disruption and expand into e-commerce. Exceptionally high profit margins and strong cash flow are fueling aggressive share buybacks. I've long believed digital advertising is one of the best business models in technology. Just look at the success of Meta and Google. The stock has come under considerable pressure, falling roughly 46% from its 52-week high. Part of the concern has been that advances in AI could disrupt AppLovin's advertising business, potentially allowing competitors or advertisers to bypass its platform. But I see AI as an opportunity for AppLovin, not just a threat. The company is already using AI to improve advertising performance, and that advantage could help it win more businesses as it expands into e-commerce. Why I'm buying Revenue growth is picking up speed In the second quarter, revenue growth decelerated to 53% year over year. For most companies, that would be an extraordinary number. But for AppLovin, it was enough to raise concerns that its best days were behind it. Fast-forward 90 days, and the company reported third-quarter revenue of $2.18 billion , up 55% from a year earlier and ahead of Wall Street's expectations. Net revenue per installation increased 55%, while installation volume grew just 2%. That tells me AppLovin's AI models are becoming more effective, allowing advertisers to generate better results and pay more for those outcomes. E-commerce opens up a new market Advertisers are increasingly using AppLovin's technology to reach potential customers, creating an entirely new growth opportunity beyond mobile games. Consumer advertiser spending grew at a triple-digit rate from a year earlier and increased by double digits from the previous quarter. Wall Street is expecting revenue growth of roughly 25% in 2027. I'm modeling 30%, in line with management's long-term growth target, and I think there's potential to do even better as e-commerce gains traction. Exceptional profitability supports aggressive buybacks In the third quarter, AppLovin reported a net income margin of 66%. To put that in perspective, the typical S & P 500 company generates a net margin closer to 10% to 15%. AppLovin's adjusted EBITDA margin was even higher, at 84%. APP YTD mountain AppLovin Corporation, YTD Those margins translate into enormous cash generation. The company produced approximately $1.4 billion in free cash flow during the quarter, giving it plenty of flexibility to reinvest in its business and return capital to shareholders. Management is doing just that. AppLovin repurchased shares aggressively during the quarter, at an average price of roughly $341, and its board expanded its buyback authorization by $3.2 billion. Why now? Despite the recent rebound, there's still meaningful upside as AppLovin's growth accelerates. There's also been a positive development on the regulatory front. The SEC has reportedly closed its inquiry into AppLovin's data-collection practices without recommending enforcement action, which removes a source of uncertainty for investors. My 12-month price target is $545, based on a blend of earnings and EBITDA valuations. That represents roughly 39% upside from the stock's current price of $393. Bottom line AppLovin's AI models need to keep improving, and its expansion beyond gaming is still pretty new. But I believe the company can turn AI disruption into an advantage. Combined with accelerating revenue growth and aggressive share repurchases, that makes this one of the more compelling opportunities I see in technology right now.
