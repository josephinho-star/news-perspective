Nigel Farage exploring Ukraine visit, Reform officials say

2026-09-08 02:00

LONDON — Nigel Farage’s critics have long accused him of having a weakness on Vladimir Putin. Now Reform UK wants to tee up a visit to Ukraine.

Two party officials told POLITICO on Monday that discussions have been held between the party and Ukrainian contacts about the prospect of a trip for the Reform leader.

Farage’s opponents often hurl at him comments he made back in 2014 that he “admired” the Russian president as an “operator.” Then, a decade later, he triggered a mid-general election backlash by claiming it was the West that “provoked” Moscow’s invasion of Ukraine.
