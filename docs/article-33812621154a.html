Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando set to wed this weekend in Southern California: report

2026-11-20 01:19

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are set to walk down the aisle!

Sources told TMZ on Thursday that the musicians will exchange vows in a ceremony in Southern California this weekend.

Further details about the reported nuptials are said by the outlet to still be “under wraps.”

Reps for Cyrus and Morando did not immediately respond to Page Six’s requests for comment on Thursday afternoon.

The development comes months after the “Hannah Montana” alum’s mom, Tish Cyrus, seemingly hinted that the duo may already be hitched.

According to the Daily Mail, during Miley’s Hollywood Walk of Fame ceremony in May, the “Flowers” songstress, 33, was posing for photos with Tish and her younger sister Brandi Cyrus, when Tish reportedly blurted out, “We’re gonna bring the husband.”
