Chad Lowe lists $3M Los Angeles home for sale after daughter Fiona’s tragic death

2026-10-31 20:06

Chad Lowe and his wife Kim Painter put their home up for sale, less than three weeks after the death of their 13-year-old daughter, Fiona.

The spacious 3,239-square-foot home already has an offer from a prospective buyer for $3.249 million.

According to real estate records per TMZ, the couple has accepted the offer, but it’s “contingent on certain conditions being met.”

The sprawling Mar Vista home has 4 bedrooms and 3 baths, vaulted ceilings, and Spanish-style architecture, and the Lowes bought it for $3.15 million in March 2025.
