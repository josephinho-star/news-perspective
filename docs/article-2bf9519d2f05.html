How to watch Commanders vs. Giants live for free on Thursday Night Football

2026-11-12 21:15

A rematch of the 2026 season’s strangest game is going down tonight.

At 8:15 p.m. ET, Jayden Daniels’ Washington Commanders face J.J. McCarthy’s New York Giants at East Rutherford’s MetLife Stadium in a Thursday Night Football showdown.

As fans likely recall, the NFC East rivals met four weeks ago at Northwest Stadium and tied 27-27 in a truly bizarre fashion. First-half extra point and field goal kicks were missed by both clubs, a 13-point deficit was erased and New York didn’t go for two when they needed to, all culminating in a disappointing — sometimes head-scratching — overtime where neither team could capitalize.

“This is for all the marbles. It’s a tie right now. This is the tiebreaker,” Giants head coach John Harbaugh said, ahead of tonight’s contest. “It’s like going to the second half tied; you have a chance to go win the game right now and be one up on these guys for the rest of the season. So, it’s a big game.”

Oddsmakers give New York a 1.5- to 2.5-point edge coming into this evening’s “tiebreaker.”

Also working in Big Blue’s favor is that tonight is the big “White Out” game where the team wears their vintage white uniforms and fans will be doing the same to “flood the stands in white.”

Expect a rowdy battle that…hopefully isn’t as strange as the last one a month ago.
