Judi Dench, 91, reveals heartbreaking reason she can’t act anymore

2026-10-22 14:15

The Oscar winner added, “I can’t do a film any more. I can’t read any more.”

Noting that she could not see host Emma Barnett’s “face at all,” Dench revealed, “I go up to lots of the wrong people and give them an almost hug and say, ‘Oh sorry, didn’t realize.'”

The Golden Globe winner continued, “They said this summer was absolutely sensational with bluebells; I couldn’t see one.

“But I know what my trees look like, and part of it is in my memory and part of it is about hopefully what I can make out.”

The “Shakespeare in Love” star’s heartbreaking health update comes more than a decade after her age-related macular degeneration (AMD) diagnosis in 2012.

Earlier this month, however, she blasted retirement rumors while railing against being called a “national treasure.”

“[While aging], you have to contend with people asking, ‘When are you going to retire?’ or, ‘Isn’t it about time you put your feet up?’ No, it bloody well isn’t,” Dench snapped in an Oct. 9 Times interview.

She shared her wish to “banish” the words “retire,” “vintage” and “old.”

Dench said, “I want to be like Newton Blick, who died in his dressing room after a performance. I want to be one of those women who throw themselves out of planes when they’re 100.”

The actress’ mom, Eleanora Olave Dench, also suffered from AMD.
