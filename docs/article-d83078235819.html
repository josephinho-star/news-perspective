Hasan Piker complains the far left has less leverage with Democratic Party because the Dems won too many seats

2026-11-20 00:30

Far-left streamer Hasan Piker lamented that Democrats were so successful in the midterms they aren’t as dependent on their far-left flank.

The Democrats won a resounding victory in the midterms, which surprised not only the Republican Party, but the far left. Just a month before the midterms, Piker had talked about using the far left to pressure candidate James Talarico to push progressive policy, saying, "We are the force that allowed him to win this seat of power, and if he wants to maintain it, he’s got to play ball."

On Wednesday night, however, Piker played a different tune, noting that the Democratic Party was so successful it is no longer as dependent on support from its most left-wing members.

"I think that there was a way that we could possibly get out of this fight with clear concessions on our agenda. We didn't. Part of that was because nobody expected the margin to be so vast," he said.

"If the margin was 10, then we could have made an impact. If the Democratic Party's margin of victory was 10 and not like f---ing 40, OK, then yeah, we could have had a big caucus, a socialist caucus, a fighter caucus, whatever you want to call it, that could potentially bully the party to be more responsive to the needs of the masses."

Piker also hit back at some critical comments from his fans.

"’This is you at your worst,'" he read aloud one comment before responding. "Why? Because I have to explain to you as an adult who is a political pundit how, you know, how change never really happens in Washington, D.C.?

"It hurts your feelings to hear someone explain to you why these things happen while simultaneously telling you how much I oppose it? Does it hurt your feelings to hear how this goes?" he asked. "It's just reality.

"I'm going to continue to explain how the sausage gets made, even if that hurts your f---ing feelings, because you're too dumb to understand."

Republican consultant Mary Marslender reacted to Piker's commentary.

"Hasan Piker is furious that Democrats won too many seats because it weakened the (Democratic Socialists of America's) ability to hold the Democratic Party hostage. Piker’s complaint reveals that the far left prefers narrow majorities so it can force its destructive socialist experiments on our country and European-style street chaos," she told Fox News Digital.

Democratic strategist and co-host of "The Huddle" podcast Dan Turrentine told Fox News Digital, "There is the world as Hasan Piker wished it would be, and the real world. Socialists are utterly irrelevant in the next Congress. They are not the majority of America, nor are they the majority of the Democratic Party.

"In fact, in the next Congress, socialists will be a tiny 2.7% of the House Democratic caucus and there will be only one in the Senate, Bernie Sanders."
