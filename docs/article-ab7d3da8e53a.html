Halle Berry styled a Chanel jacket with these on-sale jeans

2026-09-24 14:57

When it comes to fall street style, Mother knows best.

Halle Berry stepped out in Manhattan this week for the Through Her Lens luncheon, an annual event co-hosted by Chanel and Tribeca Film Festival to support emerging filmmakers.

The “X-Men” star cut a chic figure in a cropped, stand-collar jacket from Matthieu Blazy’s debut Chanel collection, paired with a coordinating tan handbag. But her jeans — Mother’s Bookie Sneak style in the Buckaroo wash — are surprisingly affordable in comparison.

The best part? We spied the classic denim for 25% off, making it easy to slide into the A-list-approved pair.

Jeans never go out of style, so we don’t mind investing in timeless cuts like the Bookie Sneak. With a mid-high waist and slight bootcut, Berry’s pair is easy to dress up or down — plus, the fabric has a touch of stretch for comfort. (They’re not on sale, but Mother’s site also offers two additional washes.)

The zip-fly style comes in sizes 23-34 and has subtle fading.

Berry paired hers with heels — just in time for falling temperatures on the East Coast — but the universally flattering style is perfect for day-to-night wear.

Mother, which is beloved for its denim (but also makes a full range of apparel), has been spotted on stylish stars from Taylor Swift to Kate Middleton — not to mention its iconic campaign with Martha Stewart earlier this year.
