Accused Penn State cocaine ringleader is being held unlawfully without bail as traffickers to be tried together: attorney

2026-09-02 02:41

Long Island-native Agostino Abbatiello’s defense team filed the motion on Tuesday, a day before their client and five other alleged members of the drug ring were set to appear in court for a hearing.

Abbatiello’s attorney, Philip Masorti, claimed that the 24-year-old frat bro was being unlawfully detained, and requested that his hearing be delayed.

Masorti pointed out that of all the 13 alleged co-conspirators in the drug-trafficking scheme, Abbatiello was the only one that was flat out denied bail.

“He has no criminal record, no history of flight, and, after a two-year investigation, the motion states that law enforcement never asserted that he used violence, threatened violence, or was seen with a weapon,” Masorti said in a statement to CBS News.

A Court of Common Pleas in Centre County said that Masorti’s motion was “moot” and that Wednesday’s hearing would continue as planned, according to the report.
