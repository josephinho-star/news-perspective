Jay-Z to headline Super Bowl LXI halftime show in Los Angeles

2026-10-27 02:29

Jay-Z is headed to the Super Bowl stage.

The music mogul will headline the Super Bowl LXI halftime show, Apple Music announced during halftime of Monday’s Dallas Cowboys-Philadelphia Eagles game.

Super Bowl LXI is set for Feb. 14, 2027, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California.

"GOAT’s out of the bag," Apple Music wrote in a post on X, tagging Jay-Z.

The announcement comes after reports that Jay-Z was in the running for the coveted performance. Puck News previously reported that the rapper had been selected over Miley Cyrus and Justin Bieber, as OutKick reported last week.

Jay-Z’s entertainment company, Roc Nation, has partnered with the NFL on the halftime show since 2019.

The performance will come months after the 25-time Grammy winner wrapped up his Jay-Z 30 Tour, which celebrated the 30th anniversary of his debut album, "Reasonable Doubt."

His wife, Beyoncé, headlined the Super Bowl halftime show in 2013 and joined Coldplay and Bruno Mars during the 2016 performance.
