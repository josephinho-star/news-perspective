Rams star Aaron Donald sends a loud and clear message Packers Micah Parsons

2026-11-23 00:38

Aaron Donald knows what it’s like to not play football for a long period of time.

The three-time NFL Defensive Player of the Year winner missed nearly three years of action when he came out of retirement to join the Los Angeles Rams this August. But apparently, there’s one defensive player he’d prefer to see nowhere near the field on Wednesday night.

Micah Parsons.

With the Los Angeles Rams preparing to host the Green Bay Packers in the NFL’s first Thanksgiving Eve game at SoFi Stadium, Donald had a hilarious message for his fellow defensive superstar, who is expected to make his 2026 season debut, after suffering a severe left knee injury—a torn ACL along with meniscus damage—during a non-contact play on Dec. 14, 2025 against the Denver Broncos.

“I wish he would just stay out one more week, man,” Donald joked Saturday when asked about Parsons. “He could come back next week or something, man.”

The Rams’ injury-riddled offensive line probably agrees with him.

Donald, a three-time NFL Defensive Player of the Year, knows exactly how disruptive Parsons can be. The Packers pass rusher is among the few defenders capable of wrecking an offensive game plan before the quarterback finishes his dropback.

And Donald would be perfectly happy watching him do that to somebody else.

Despite the jokes, Donald made it clear he has enormous respect for Parsons, whose rare combination of speed, power and relentless pursuit has made him one of the NFL’s most feared defenders.

“I’m a fan of Micah. I think he’s one hell of a football player,” Donald said. “Fast, explosive, plays with an unbelievable motor.”

Then came another playful attempt to extend Parsons’ recovery.

“Micah, if you want to take one more week to get yourself 100 percent, make sure everything’s where it needs to be, I’d like to see you on TV.”

The Rams’ playoff hopes also wish he would wait another week before he returns to the field.

Los Angeles enters Wednesday’s showdown at 6-4, tied with Green Bay in a crowded NFC wild-card race, with postseason positioning hanging in the balance.

Parsons’ return would provide another headache for a Rams offensive line already battered by injuries.
