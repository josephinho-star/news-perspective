Alec Bohm signing with Royals after Yankees interest in MLB free agency

2026-11-25 16:26

The Yankees’ interest in Alec Bohm proved to be just that.

The infielder agreed to a two-year, $20 million with the Royals, The Post’s Jon Heyman confirmed, after The Post exclusively reported that the Yankees had interest in the veteran infielder.

Bohm, 30, figures to be the Royals’ third baseman after the team sent Maikel Garcia to the Mariners earlier this offseason in a trade that netted them starter Bryce Miller.

The former No. 3 overall pick in the 2018 NFL Draft held interest to the Yankees as a potential righty bat who could platoon with Ryan McMahon at third base.

Bohm slashed .292/.333/.483 with seven homers against southpaws last year.

The Yankees are considering upgrading over McMahon or at least finding a platoon partner due to his struggles against lefties.

The question hanging over the Yankees’ offseason is just how much owner Hal Steinbrenner will allow them to spend and how that affects their offensive pursuits.

Heyman reported earlier this month there had been hope the Yankees could land him for about $10 million.

The Yankees will instead continue their hunt for their first bat in an offseason focused thus far on pitching additions, specifically in the bullpen, and Garcia being traded and Bohm being signed means there’s now even fewer options available.

The Royals, meanwhile, continue to retool a last-place roster.

The Royals finished 22nd in runs scored last year and 16th in OPS, and swapping Garcia for Bohm while adding Miller is a set of transactions seemingly made with the goal of raising the team’s offensive floor and pitching, while worsening the infield defense.

Bohm slashed .249/.315/.383 last year with 15 homers and 88 RBIs, and has averaged roughly 15 homers and 83 RBIs over the last five seasons.

His 88 RBIs would have led Kansas City last season and he also has experience playing first base, giving the Royals positional flexibility.
