Bears vs. Panthers prediction: NFL Week 1 picks, odds, best bets Sunday

2026-09-13 11:30

The Bears and Panthers open their respective 2026 NFL seasons in Charlotte with high hopes that they each can repeat the success they enjoyed in 2025.

While both teams ended their seasons in similar fashion — last-minute home playoff losses against the Rams — they also vastly exceeded expectations and raised the standard for their fanbases in the process.

Sportsbooks opened this spread at Bears -2.5, and it was bet up to Bears -3 at BetMGM.

The Over/Under opened at 45.5 and moved up to 47 as we approach kickoff.

Bears vs. Panthers odds, prediction

There are a lot of parallels between these two teams, especially at quarterback.

Bryce Young was the No. 1 overall pick in the 2023 NFL Draft; Caleb Williams was the top pick in 2024.

Both players were overshadowed by quarterbacks in their respective drafts who took the league by storm as rookies — C.J. Stroud in 2023 and Jayden Daniels in 2024.

Last season, both players finally came into their own and led their teams to division titles.

The enduring images from the Bears last season were sensational throws from Williams and game-winning comebacks. But those highlights obscure their flaws.

The defense, for example, led the NFL in interceptions, but ranked 29th in total yards and 23rd in points allowed. The Bears needed those amazing plays on offense to balance out their boom-or-bust defense.

For Carolina, Young played his best season to date, and Tetairoa McMillan looks like a stud at receiver, but their offense still managed just 18.3 points per game.

The Panthers have been the best team in the league as a home underdog since the 2024 season, with a 10-5 record against the spread.
