'Reacher' star Alan Ritchson and wife Catherine splitting up after 2 decades of marriage

2026-09-13 00:27

Alan Ritchson and his wife have decided to go their separate ways.

In a joint Instagram post shared on Saturday, the 43-year-old actor and his wife Catherine announced they have "decided to end our marriage and continue alongside one another in a different way" after 20 years together.

"We grew up together. We built a life and a family together. And we are raising three incredible boys who will always be the center of our world," they wrote. "Nothing can diminish what these years have meant to us or the love and respect we will always share."

The post went on to say that the two plan on moving forward with "friendship, grace and a commitment to becoming the strongest co-parents we can be."

They added that in addition to being good co-parents, the two will also allow one another the "room to grow into the people we are becoming."

"Change is hard enough without the whole world weighing in," they added. "We hope, especially for the sake of our boys, that you will allow our family to move through this chapter with privacy and compassion."

The statement concluded with the estranged couple thanking their followers for "supporting us as we continue to make the health and happiness of our family a priority."

TMZ reports that according to court documents, Ritchson filed for divorce in December, noting they reconciled in March, before announcing their separation in September.

The two met as teenagers in high school, but broke up after some time together. They reconnected while in college and later got married in May 2006.

Throughout their 20-year marriage, the two welcomed three sons; Calem, Edan and Amory.

Fox News Digital reached out to representatives for Ritchson but did not immediately hear back.

Ritchson's first significant role in Hollywood was in the hit TV show "Smallville," later appearing in "Blue Mountain State," "The Hunger Games: Catching Fire," "Titans" and others.

His career-defining role came in 2022 when he landed the role of Jack Reacher on the Amazon Prime show "Reacher."

"It’s funny: I've shown my kids the show," he told GQ in January 2024. "I let them watch season one – but I wouldn’t let them see the naked guy hanging crucified on the wall [who had his bollocks chopped off and was forced to swallow them whole], but everything else was… Within context, it all made sense."

He added: "They loved it, man. For me, it’s not ‘Dad TV’, it's ‘family TV’. I walk down the street and little ladies on their walkers are like 'Reacher… Reacher…' It’s such a misnomer to me to qualify this as ‘Dad TV’. But I am a father and I, too, love the show. Maybe it does work.
