Irina Shayk is all laced up in daring Dolce & Gabbana on the Venice Film Festival red carpet

2026-09-07 14:46

Irina Shayk is never afraid to make a statement on the red carpet.

The model attended the premiere of “The Echo Chamber” at the Venice Film Festival 2026 Sunday, dressed in head-to-toe Dolce & Gabbana.

Shayk, 40, borrowed from the brand’s past, slipping into a satin dress featuring enormous cutouts on both sides, held together by laces. She added black satin briefs underneath, styled with knee-high socks and pointed-toe satin stilettos.

For an extra bit of bling, the Victoria’s Secret model covered her neck with a silver crystal mesh choker.

The look was part of the Italian brand’s spring/summer 2023 collection, which was created in collaboration with Kim Kardashian.

The reality star curated a collection of reworked archival looks — mostly rendered in black, white, leopard and metallics — for the runway show; Shayk’s style in particular is one of D&G’s most iconic, based on a design first modeled by Gisele Bündchen in 2003.

Off the red carpet, Shayk was pictured at the film festival in another throwback all-black look: a heavily beaded cropped jacket and pleated trousers from Givenchy’s 2015 collection, designed by her close friend Riccardo Tisci.

“So special to wear @riccardotisci17 Givenchy couture in Venice — a piece of fashion history brought back to life,” Shayk captioned an Instagram post of the look. “Thank you for creating something so extraordinary and making me feel incredible wearing it.”

The Venice Film Festival runs through Sept. 12 and has seen tons of other A-listers including George and Amal Clooney, Laura Dern and Robert Pattinson as well as fellow models Kendall Jenner, Vittoria Ceretti, Stella Maxwell and more.
