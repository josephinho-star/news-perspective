Bunnie Xo, 46, passionately kisses 24-year-old boyfriend Dylan Wolf after defending age gap backlash

2026-10-06 23:51

Bunnie Xo was spotted “making out like crazy” with her much younger boyfriend, Dylan Wolf, after defending their age gap.

The “Dumb Blonde” podcast host, 46, passionately wrapped her arms around the “Calabasas Confidential” star, 24, at The Barrel sports bar in Los Angeles on Sunday, according to photos obtained by the Daily Mail.

An eyewitness told the outlet that the couple was “increasingly affectionate” throughout their time at the bar, describing Wolf as the “aggressor.”

According to the spy, Wolf initiated the PDA, and Bunnie — born Alisa Andrea DeFord — was “happy to go along with it.”

The source noted that the duo appeared “very comfortable” with intense chemistry, though they “rarely talked.”

Bunnie and Wolf were also spotted holding hands and kissing while at The Crabby Crab in Sherman Oaks, California, on Oct. 1.

At the time, she was seen kissing Wolf at her ex’s Goodnight Nashville bar in Nashville, Tennessee, on July 4.

Following the night out, Bunnie reassured her podcast listeners that Jelly Roll — born Jason Bradley DeFord — knew about her steamy makeout session and had “no issue” with it.

“I also feel very safe there,” she said. “I also feel protected there. That’s our home. If I’m gonna land at any bar, I’m gonna do it at Goodnight Nashville.”
