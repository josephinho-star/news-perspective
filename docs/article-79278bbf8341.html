Sofia Richie Grainge swears by these hair growth supplements — and they’re on sale for Labor Day

“I actually didn’t end up with any postpartum hair shedding,” she told InStyle last year. “The Nutrafol Postpartum worked wonders for me, and my hair has honestly never been healthier.”
