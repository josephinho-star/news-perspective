Nick Bosa’s girlfriend shares steamy bikini post — then deletes it

2026-11-19 19:10

Ella Ailiff has quickly become one of the NFL’s most prominent wives and girlfriends.

Ailiff has been dating San Francisco 49ers star edge rusher Nick Bosa. The combination of Bosa being an elite talent and playing for San Francisco — which is one of the league’s best and most successful franchises — has turned Ailiff into a household name.

She also does a great job of showcasing her life on social media, particularly her own modeling career.

Ailiff posted one video of what looked to be a recent modeling shoot to her Instagram story on Wednesday. She was laying down on the sand while wearing a white bikini, in what looked to be a behind-the-scenes post.

However, Ailiff ended up deleting the post at some point afterwards.

It’s unclear why Ailiff made this decision. It doesn’t appear that whatever shoot this was for has gone live yet, which might mean she was told that she should not be sharing footage from it.

Bosa and the 49ers are heading to Mexico City to take on the Minnesota Vikings this weekend.

Perhaps Ailiff will want to watch Bosa in person or perhaps she would prefer to watch that contest from the comfort of her own home.
