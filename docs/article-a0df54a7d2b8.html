Escaped horse wanders to a Virginia McDonalds before leading police chase through neighborhood

2026-11-24 06:39

A runaway horse slipped out of its Virginia farm and trotted down to the local McDonald’s before leading police on an early morning chase.

The escaped equine, identified as a 6-year-old stallion named Rollo, allegedly rolled under an electric fence before making the surprise appearance at the Golden Arches in Colonial Heights, Va., on Nov. 17, WAVY 10 reported.

Rollo – enjoying his newfound freedom – appeared to be “Lovin’ it” until the law caught up to him.

Police initially failed to stop Rollo as the four-legged suspect led them on a pursuit through the neigh-borhood.

One attempt saw Rollo calmly walk through an ill-attempted blockade as an officer held a lasso trying to wrangle the horse.

Officers with the Colonial Heights and Virginia State Police eventually cornered Rollo using a brick wall and their cars to block any further escape attempt.

“Using a little teamwork, some lassos, and the power of friendship, everyone worked together to safely corner and wrangle our four-legged runaway away from traffic,” the Colonial Heights Animal Control said.

Rollo became mildly upset with his capture but was calmed by an officer who used his “horse-whispering skills” until someone could bail the horse from police custody.

“Once the majestic stallion was safely contained, Officer Fritts stepped in with perhaps the most important tool of all: a few whispered sweet nothings, a gentle touch, and some well-timed horse whispering to calm him down until he could be reunited with his rightful owner,” animal officials added.

Rollo was brought back to his home at the nearby ranch he had escaped from.

The horse’s owners fixed the electric fence and made it more secure to halt any further escapes from Rollo, WAVY 10 reported.

“It takes teamwork to keep our community safe, and sometimes that teamwork comes with four legs, a mane, and absolutely no interest in following traffic laws,” animal control said.
