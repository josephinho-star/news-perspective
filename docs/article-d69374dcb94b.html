Billie Eilish mocked for hosting brother Finneas O’Connell’s wedding on ‘stolen land’

2026-08-31 01:30

Billie Eilish is being mocked for officiating her brother’s wedding on what she may refer to as “stolen land.”

The singer, 24, hosted 29-year-old Finneas O’Connell’s marriage to longtime girlfriend Claudia Sulewski, 30, at the luxurious San Ysidro Ranch in Montecito, Calif. Saturday.

One person wrote on X: “Was this on stolen land?” Another posted: “She is on stolen land.” Another added: “On stolen land no less.”

The Chumash historically inhabited a vast swath of California’s Central and Southern coasts, including the San Ysidro Ranch area.

The Santa Ynez Band of Chumash Indians says Chumash territory once covered roughly 7,000 square miles, stretching from Malibu to Paso Robles and inland to the western edge of the San Joaquin Valley.

Historical documentation for the Montecito area also identifies it as Chumash territory before European settlement.

The property later developed into the San Ysidro Citrus Ranch. A sandstone packing house was constructed in 1889, and plans for a small hotel were drawn up several years later.

That hotel opened in 1893 and eventually became a hideaway for some of the world’s most famous names: Sir Winston Churchill stayed there with his family, while Vivien Leigh and Laurence Olivier married at the ranch. Additionally, John F. Kennedy and Jacqueline Kennedy honeymooned there, and one of its cottages is now named the Kennedy Cottage.

Beanie Babies billionaire Ty Warner purchased the property in 2000 and later poured about $150 million into renovating it, according to Architectural Digest.

O’Connell and Sulewski’s wedding added another celebrity chapter to the ranch’s history.

The pair met through a dating app in 2018, according to People, and O’Connell said he was so taken with Sulewski that he began writing his song “Claudia” the same day they met. They bought a home together the following year and became engaged in September 2025 after seven years of dating.

Sulewski, an actress, YouTuber and founder of skincare brand Cyklar, wore four custom Oscar de la Renta looks during the wedding weekend.

The celebrity-filled guest list reportedly included Justin and Hailey Bieber, Kristen Bell and Dax Shepard, Nina Dobrev, Emma Chamberlain, Jason Sudeikis and Rachel Sennott. Eilish attended with boyfriend Nat Wolff.

During her dramatic acceptance speech for her and O’Connell’s Grammy for Song of the Year for “WILDFLOWER” in February, Eilish said: “No one is illegal on stolen land. It’s hard to know what to say and what to do, but we need to keep fighting and speaking up and protesting. Our voices really do matter.”
