Stocks making the biggest moves midday: Netflix, Xenon Pharmaceuticals, Coinbase, Sandisk, SpaceX & more

2026-09-18 17:58

Here are some of the companies making headlines in midday trading. SpaceX — Shares slid 2% a day after Elon Musk's rocket manufacturer announced it would push back the expected date for its Starship launch to Sept. 28. Earlier this week, the company said it was aiming for Sept. 22. Peers Rocket Lab and Intuitive Machines each tumbled more than 5%. Chip stocks — Shares of chip companies rose on Friday, with the VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) up about 0.6%, heading for a fourth straight winning day. Applied Materials ' stock rose 4%, shares of Lam Research advanced 5% and Broadcom 's stock jumped more than 2%. Memory chip plays also gained, as Sandisk 's stock climbed 7% and Seagate Technology 's rose 4%. Steel Dynamics , Nucor — Shares of both steel producers fell after they delivered third-quarter earnings guidance that showed weaker-than-expected results compared with analysts polled by FactSet's consensus estimates. Nucor said earnings in the steel mills and steel products segments will increase, while they're expected to decrease in the raw materials segment. Steel Dynamics' stock was down more than 4%, while shares of Nucor last traded 6% lower. Netflix — Shares of the streamer declined almost 5% after Wells Fargo downgraded the stock to underweight. Analysts at the bank said that worrying engagement trends and weaker content expected to be released in the second half of 2026 will lead to lower margin expansion in 2027 and 2028. Xenon Pharmaceuticals — The stock sank 30% after the company announced it was pausing enrollment for new patients in its clinical studies for treatments for major and bipolar depression. While the pause is expected to be temporary, it was put in place due to the discovery of neuropsychiatric side effects in the studies. Cryptocurrency stocks — Shares of crypto-exposed companies rose after bitcoin surpassed $80,000, last trading almost 6% higher. Shares of trading platforms Robinhood and Coinbase rose 8% and 11%, respectively, while shares of crypto treasury company Strategy jumped 12%. Macom Technology Solutions — The stock was up 4% after BMO Capital Markets upgraded the stock to outperform. Analysts said Macom's valuation has become attractive following a more than 35% decline from its 2026 highs, even while fundamentals in its data center, industrial and defense businesses have remained strong. — CNBC's Nick Wells and Darla Mercado contributed reporting.
