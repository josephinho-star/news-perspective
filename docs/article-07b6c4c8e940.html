China mocks US ‘congratulations’ over Dominican closure of Chinese-run factory

2026-11-10 20:49

Beijing’s embassy hits back at State Department official Juan Pablo Segura for praising shutdown of the Chinese-operated Cartesa plant

The rebuke came hours after Juan Pablo Segura, who leads the US State Department’s Bureau of Western Hemisphere Affairs, praised Dominican authorities on social media for “taking decisive action to shut down a massive, illegal, Chinese-operated cardboard factory”.

Operating without permits, running an illicit fuel depot and illegally hiring foreign workers “will not stand”, he wrote.
