What Georgia Tech couple really said during viral moment revealed, according to lipreader

2026-11-24 19:36

It’s all still a mystery.

The infamous Georgia Tech couple, Braden Flournoy and Tallulah Wall, seemingly had a disagreement over a third party, forensic and expert lip reader Nicola Hickling of LipReader translated for The Post on Tuesday.

Flournoy started off by saying: “That’s not okay with me at all.”

“Okay,” Wall responded.

“I don’t like him either. I know he’s a piece of s–t, but I value human life,” Flournoy went on before Wall agreed, saying “Okay. So do I.”

“I’m your guy,” he added.

“Are you suggesting I would follow you to the end of the earth? Why the f–k would I do that?” Wall replied. “Do you remember that? Are you serious right now? Do you think I expected you to do that?

Flournoy answered: “I am completely serious. No excuses, that’s it.”

“I believe you,” Wall said to conclude the chatter caught on camera.

While on the “Today” show Tuesday morning, the couple didn’t get into detail surrounding their viral conversation during the Georgia Tech-Wake Forest game on Saturday.

They boiled it down to “more miscommunication than anything” else and Flournoy added there was “zero speculation between us breaking up.”

“That’s why we were like ‘Let’s just go back and watch this football game, even if we’re losing,’” Wall added.

During the fourth quarter of Wake Forest’s 50-29 victory, ESPN cameras found Flournoy and Wall, who was wearing a Georgia Tech headband, in the middle of a tense conversation while sitting in the stands of Bobby Dodd Stadium in Atlanta.

The couple found out they were on television after a stadium employee came up to them and showed the two the viral video.

“[Our phones were] covered in texts being like ‘You were all over the screen. Jesse Palmer was just talking about you,’” Wall said before admitting both she and Flournoy have not watched “The Bachelor.”

The moment served as a hard launch for the couple, who said many of their friends and family did not know they were together.

“Not only were we getting texts, ‘Oh, hey, you were on the screen for a solid couple of seconds.’ It was ‘You have a boyfriend?’” Wall said.

Tuesday marked the couple’s two-month-and-one-week anniversary and the two have plans to spend Thanksgiving together.

In the moment on Saturday, Palmer gave a play-by-play of the dispute before cameras cut back to find them sharing a few kisses.

“He is locked in,” Palmer said of Flournoy. “He is holding her to court right now. He wants an answer, and he is not leaving. They are in each other’s personal space — he is not happy with whatever she just told him.”

Georgia Tech sits with a 3-8 record this season and faces No. 1 Georgia next week.
