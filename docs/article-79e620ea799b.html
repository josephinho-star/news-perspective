A-list Marvel actress goes undercover on Thanksgiving to feed LA’s homeless

2026-11-28 00:30

Florence Pugh, who plays Yelena Belova in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, was spotted handing out meals to the homeless in Los Angeles on Thanksgiving.

The 30-year-old actress was one of dozens of volunteers who served up holiday meals to 2,000 people Thursday at The Midnight Mission’s annual street festival on Skid Row.

The “East of Eden” star wore a white apron with the organization’s logo on it over a black shirt.

Pugh completed her casual look with light-washed jeans and a gray baseball cap, keeping her hair pulled back and donning black gloves.

In photos from her day of volunteering, she scooped mashed potatoes, gravy, green beans, turkey and a dinner roll to pass to a diner.

Other snaps showed the “Midsommar” actress carrying several plastic bags full of supplies in her arms.

In one social media post, The Midnight Mission specifically called out Pugh and Paulson for being among the volunteers handing out Thanksgiving meals to those less fortunate than them.

The organization thanked everyone who made the day possible in another post, which read, “A heartfelt thank you to Sysco, Alison Barker, Dene Anderberg with the LAPD, and Mike Gonzales for donating turkeys, and to Costco for generously providing the side dishes.”

“Because of the generosity of our community, thousands of our neighbors will gather around the table to enjoy a delicious Thanksgiving meal, experience fellowship, and be reminded that they are loved and valued,” the message continued. “Together, we have so much to be thankful for!”

Pugh has played assassin Yelena in several MCU films — 2021’s “Black Widow,” 2025’s “Thunderbolts” and 2026’s “Spider-Man: Brand New Day.”
