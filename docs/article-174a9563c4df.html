49ers get optimistic Kyle Juszczyk update after scary knee injury

2026-10-05 02:14

San Francisco 49ers fullback Kyle Juszczyk suffered what appeared to be a scary knee injury during Sunday’s 24-14 win against the Denver Broncos.

Juszczyk caught a pass by Brock Purdy and was tackled at his knees by Broncos cornerback Jahdae Barron. Upon impact, Juszczyk’s right knee hyperextended backwards, which looked like a gruesome injury.

Juszczyk was down for a while before being helped off the field by 49ers trainers.

Anytime a knee injury occurs, the immediate fear is a torn ACL, which typically takes about a year to recover from.

49ers coach Kyle Shanahan spoke with the media after Sunday’s win and delivered what sounds like a positive update on Juszczyk’s status.

“Juice, I mean, we don’t know for sure. They’re thinking it was probably a knee sprain. His ACL and stuff looked intact. But to find out for sure, we’ll have to find out tomorrow,” Shanahan said.
