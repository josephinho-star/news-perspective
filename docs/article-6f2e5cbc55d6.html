Huckabee Sanders doesn’t rule out future presidential bid

2026-11-15 17:43

Arkansas Gov. Sarah Huckabee Sanders (R) on Sunday left open the possibility of a future presidential run, saying she is focused on leading her state but stopped short of ruling out a bid for the White House.

In an interview with CNN’s “State of the Union” on Sunday, Sanders, who won reelection for her second and final term as governor, was asked about her political future and whether her father, former Arkansas Gov. Mike Huckabee, would support her following in his footsteps by running for president.

“I don’t know,” Sanders said with a laugh. “Given the fact that he knows what that means, I’m not sure he would wish that on me in some cases.”

Her father unsuccessfully sought the Republican presidential nomination in 2008 and 2016, winning the Iowa caucuses during his first campaign. Sanders helped run his campaign operation in 2008.

When CNN host Dana Bash pressed Sanders about her own presidential ambitions, the governor emphasized that she was happy in her current role, but did not completely rule out a presidential run.

“For me, personally, I just got reelected as the governor of Arkansas. I love what I’m doing, and I look forward to holding this job for the next four years,” Sanders said. “My plan is to focus and do as best as I can lifting up the people of my state, moving my state forward, and really just keep my focus there for right now.

Bash asked if Sanders was committing to serving her full four-year term as governor, which would extend beyond the 2028 presidential election.

“Well, at that point I’m termed out anyway unless they change the rules,” Sanders joked. “My max out is in four years, they’re going to kick me out whether I like it or not. But ultimately the simple answer is I like what I’m doing.”

Sanders served as White House press secretary during President Trump’s first term before becoming the first woman elected governor of Arkansas in 2022. Much like other possible contenders with presidential ambitions, Sanders recently released a memoir titled, “Unapologetic: Clarity and Conviction in a World Gone Crazy.”
