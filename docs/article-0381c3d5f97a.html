Robert Saleh has ‘100 percent support’ of owners during 1-9 Titans season

2026-11-23 23:07

Robert Saleh has no concerns about support from the Titans’ ownership despite a 1-9 record to start the season.

Speaking with reporters on Monday, he said that he has “100 percent support” from Titans owner Amy Adams Strunk and down through the organization during his first season with the team. The declaration comes a day after the Titans fell to the Cowboys 51-30 and their only win has come against the Colts back in Week 6.

“There’s an expectation to get better, and this is coming from me and nobody else,” Saleh said, according to the Associated Press. “And I’ll tell you guys the same thing I tell the coaches and I tell players, that the expectation is that you find a way to get better and the people who get better will stay. The people who don’t get better, will not.”

Saleh has been lauded for his work with defenses in the past, but the Titans’ defense has been bad this season, giving up at least 40 points in three straight games following Sunday’s loss, and Tennessee has allowed opposing teams to score on 13 consecutive drives.

The Titans are 30th in the NFL and have allowed 29 points a game on average and are last in yards allowed per game, averaging of 399.8.

Saleh called Sunday’s loss “disgusting, embarrassing football” after the game and he was still plenty critical when he addressed the media on Monday.

“Our young guys are getting a lot of play. We’re getting better in some areas in that regard, but these last couple of weeks, I’m not going to try to sugarcoat anything, it’s been dogs–t,” Saleh said bluntly.

Things won’t get any easier this upcoming weekend when the Titans have to face the highly potent Jaguars offense in Week 12 on the road at EverBank Stadium in Jacksonville. The Jaguars are coming off a disappointing 23-18 loss to the Giants on Sunday.
