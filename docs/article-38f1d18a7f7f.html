How to make Italian stuffing

• John C. told us earlier this week about his mother-in-law's stuffing that he says could be sold as a street drug. Reader begged him for the recipe. Here we go:

Monday - She sautés 3 sticks of butter, 3 celery stalks, 4 lbs of onions, and thyme, salt, and pepper (and any other spices you care to add) in a cast iron pan for an hour (the photo I sent yesterday). She lets that cool, then puts it in a slow cooker with 2 boxes of chicken broth and 2 packages of stuffing mix (at 88 she doesn’t do everything from scratch anymore). She mixes it then lets it sit in the fridge overnight.

Tuesday - She bakes it for 3 hours in the slow cooker on low, adding more broth if necessary, then she lets it cool and it goes back in the fridge.

Wednesday.- She bakes it for 6 hours in the slow cooker on medium adding more broth as necessary, then she lets it cool and it goes back in the fridge.

Thursday - She heats it up and serves it.

No secret ingredients or unique cooking methods, just lots of time and attention. The end result is outstanding.
