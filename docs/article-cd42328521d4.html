Yankees could check in on second-base stars even with Anthony Volpe as incumbent

2026-11-10 03:48

Third base is the Yankees’ real infield issue. But they may take a look at second base, too.

The Yankees aren’t in terrible shape at second with Anthony Volpe, whose stock rose rapidly after making the positional switch from shortstop, perhaps Luis García Jr. — who, believe it or not, played some second as a Nat — and utility player José Caballero.

But they may still check in on Brandon Lowe and Luis Arraez, two stars who are free agents. They’ve always liked Arraez.

The Yankees probably won’t try to retain Jazz Chisholm Jr. The decision not to extend a $23.15 million qualifying offer to the uber-talented former 30-30 guy was telling.
