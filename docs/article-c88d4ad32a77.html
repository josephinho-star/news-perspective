Beyoncé glitters in gold, diamonds and sequins for 45th birthday celebration

2026-09-08 17:50

She’s pure honey.

Beyoncé celebrated her 45th birthday as only she can: with sequins, beads and — of course — diamonds.

Queen Bey was spotted across the pond, attending a Sunday night fête in London alongside her mom Tina Knowles and husband Jay-Zm wearing a golden bronze outfit with some serious impact.

For the Harry’s Bar event, Beyoncé slipped into a micro-mini Roberto Cavalli skirt ($2,395) — trimmed in beaded fringe — and a matching top ($2,995) crafted of sheer chiffon with cut velvet detailing.

Bey accessorized with a gold Jimmy Choo bag ($8,495) in one hand that matched her metallic gold manicure.

Doubling down on sparkle, the singer wore chandelier earrings crafted by jeweler Glenn Spiro. Designed to look like a fishbone, the earrings were made of spessartite garnets as well as yellow, orange and white diamonds.

The birthday girl finished off her look with a pair of red Jimmy Choo sunglasses ($505).

Jay-Z opted for a more casual khaki-colored jacket and matching pants, but he leaned into the metallic theme of the evening, carrying a gold bottle of his Ace of Spades champagne as his accessory of choice.

On Friday, Bey’s actual b-day, the rapper performed the first of two Tottenham Hotspur performances, taking a moment during the show to lead the crowd in a rendition of “Happy Birthday” accompanied by a fireworks display.
