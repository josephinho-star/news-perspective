Oprah’s Favorite Things 2026 list includes editor-loved gifts: Our picks

2026-11-12 14:05

Mariah Carey may say, “It’s timeeee!”, but Oprah is saying, “And you get a gift, and *YOU* get a gift!“

Alas, the holiday shopping season just got a little more exciting: Oprah’s Favorite Things 2026 is officially here!

Announced Nov. 12, this year’s highly anticipated list marks a major milestone — the 30th anniversary of Oprah’s Favorite Things. Three decades after the tradition began, Oprah is once again sharing her handpicked selection of gifts guaranteed to delight everyone on your holiday shopping list.

The 2026 edition features 79 unique gifts, offering plenty of inspiration for finding something special for everyone from the hardest-to-shop-for family member to the friend who seems to have everything. As always, the list is packed with an irresistible mix of the most decadent desserts, ingenious gadgets, and standout finds in home, fashion and beauty.

Whether you’re looking to surprise a loved one, treat yourself, or get a head start on your holiday shopping, Oprah’s Favorite Things is the ultimate gift guide to have on your radar.

Ahead, shop our ultimate favorites from this favorite list.

If you like a matte finish, Anastasia’s velvet lip mousse is perfect. The Trio features Anastasia’s bestselling hush rose color alongside two brand new shades – crimson kiss and twilight fig — and is 20% off with code OPRAH at checkout.

It’s time to give your hands some *rich* treatment. This thick and creamy hand salve contains shea butter, avocado oil and sesame seed. And, you’ll save 20% with — you guessed it — promo code OPRAH.

There’s no place like home. These sequined slippers come in adult and kids’ sizes, making the perfect gift. Plus, the box includes a foam apple like the one that falls from the ceiling during the Sphere’s “The Wizard of Oz” experience.
