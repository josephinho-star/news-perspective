Masked protesters chant 'Charlie Kirk deserved to die' outside TPUSA event at University of Washington

2026-11-20 01:38

Masked activists protesting a Turning Point USA (TPUSA) event at the University of Washington Wednesday celebrated TPUSA founder Charlie Kirk’s assassination.

Since Kirk's murder in September 2025, Turning Point USA has hosted a series of Pick Up the Mic campus events where influencers spark conversations with students to continue Kirk's legacy. Chloe Cole, a detransitioner who advocates against youth gender transition, was slated to speak at the event.

Cole canceled a scheduled speech at the university in May because of alleged violent threats from Antifa.

On Wednesday night, protesters gathered on the lawn outside the event, waving the transgender pride flag and holding signs with messages opposing Cole and Kirk.

Videos circulating online showed protesters who identified themselves as members of the American Party of Labor Puget Sound chanting, "Charlie Kirk deserved to die," and carrying signs that said "Women hold up half the sky, Charlie Kirk deserved to die" and "Kirk's pronouns were was/were."

The group reposted several of the videos to its social media accounts acknowledging the chanting with one post saying, "Guilty!!"

When asked about the protest, a spokesperson for the University of Washington told Fox News Digital, "The safety and security of our entire community, including event participants, observers and passersby, is our top priority. The scheduled event occurred without disruption, and protesters outside exercised their right to free expression."

Theo Wold, a former White House deputy assistant for domestic policy during President Donald Trump's first term, commented on the incident in a statement to Fox News Digital.

"The left is defined by mass-movement politics with roots in the violence and barbarism of the French Revolution and the Red Terror of the Spanish Civil War," he said.

"Elite institutions paper over the growing threat of Antifa Democrats, anarchist direct action and revolutionary violence because they don’t want Americans to understand leftist radicalism for what it is.

"But this pattern of incidents cannot be shrugged away as ‘mere’ intimidation. It is another example of this radical movement and its easy acceptance of violence."

TPUSA and Cole did not immediately return Fox News Digital's request for comment.
