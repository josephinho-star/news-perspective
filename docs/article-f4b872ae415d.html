Nikki Glaser calls out Taylor Swift haters and hypes up Chiefs fans in Sunday Night Football video

2026-09-21 01:54

Nikki Glaser fired up Taylor Swift and Kansas City Chiefs fans in a promo video for the team that aired on Sunday Night Football.

The comedian, who’s a Swift superfan, narrates and stars in the video that’s set to the pop star’s 2017 hit, “…Ready For It?”

“People love a winner — until they win too much,” she notes, referring to the Chiefs’ dynasty. “People want dynasties to die and for chaps to become chumps.”

“And when that downfall finally comes, it hurts like a Tush Push you weren’t expecting,” she continues, referencing both the Chiefs’ disappointing season last year and the Philadelphia Eagles’ football play associated with Swift’s brother-in-law, Jason Kelce.

Glaser goes on, “But without the downfall, you don’t get the thing that people love most of all: The comeback.”

The video also features footage of Swift cheering on her husband, Travis Kelce.

“And it’s safe to say the world’s greatest singer-songwriter is now a permanent fixture at Arrowhead [Stadium],” Glaser says. “And I speak for all Swifties when I say, we want more cutaways.”

Glaser rocks a glittery red jersey with Kelce’s number — 87 — on it, but the name on the back reads “TAYLOR’S HUSBAND” instead of the tight end’s name.

She concludes, “So here we go, Kansas City. Tonight we take on the Colts. Before you know it, we’ll be back on top, and then the rest of you can go back to hating us all over again.”

Swift was present at Arrowhead Stadium Sunday for the Chiefs game against the Indianapolis Colts. She was with her parents, Scott Swift and Andrea Swift, as well as her mother-in-law, Donna Kelce in a VIP suite.

The “Blank Space” singer wore a Chiefs tank and playfully flashed her ring, shouting “My husband!” when Travis scored a touchdown.

Glaser attended Swift and Kelce’s star-studded wedding at Madison Square Garden in New York City in July. Earlier this month, she dished about the “wild” experience during her residency at Caesars Palace Las Vegas.

“I am a huge Swiftie, probably the biggest Swiftie that was at that event. If you’re a Swiftie, you’ll understand. I got the text inviting me to the wedding — I never thought in a million years I’d get invited,” she shared with the audience.

“I don’t even know them, but I got invited, and I’m shaking as I’m opening [the message]. I’m like, ‘Oh my God, I want to tell everyone that I know,'” she continued.

Glaser, 42, lamented she “had nothing to show for it” since all guests had to turn off their phones.

“I go in, and I have no pictures. It sucks so much because it was the best night of my life as a Swiftie, like, the coolest thing I’ve ever seen,” she gushed, “The purest love I’ve ever witnessed, and I have no pictures [and] no evidence.”
