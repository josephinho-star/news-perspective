Mama June’s daughter Pumpkin considering legal action after ‘hardcore’ OnlyFans content gets kid kicked out of private school

2026-08-30 14:04

Mama June’s daughter Lauryn “Pumpkin” Efird is considering taking legal action after a private school pulled her daughter’s acceptance due to the TV star’s OnlyFans content.

The “Here Comes Honey Boo Boo” alum told TMZ that she is weighing her options after a Georgia private school allegedly revoked her 8-year-old daughter Ella’s spot.

Efird told the outlet that the school allegedly believed her social media presence did not align with their values.

The reality star said she was called into a meeting last Friday and administrators allegedly informed her that they were aware of her raunchy content and were withdrawing Ella’s acceptance.

The school also told her they would refund her money.

Efird said she told the school employees: “Now that I know you guys are this judgy, my child doesn’t need to be here anyway.”

The “Mama June: From Not to Hot” star, 26, told TMZ she may file a lawsuit against the school because she believes they should have disclosed any issue with her OnlyFans account before accepting Ella into their program.

Page Six has reached out to Efird and the school for comment but did not immediately hear back.

As we previously reported, Efird fired off on Facebook, saying she was personally upset over how the school allegedly handled the situation by making her continue with the admissions process without once being told her social media was an issue.

“I believe Ella deserved better than to be sitting outside in the front office, completely unaware that she was essentially being rejected from a school she was so excited to attend because of her mother’s social media presence,” she wrote.

“At the very least, the situation could have been handled with more transparency and consideration,” the We TV star added.

Efird revealed that Ella will now be homeschooled.

Earlier this month, the mom of four revealed she would be beginning “hardcore” content on the subscription-based platform, including girl-on-girl action that may turn into a threesome with her boyfriend, Darrin Kitchens.
