Once again, a Big Ten coach has the SEC on his mind. Last weekend, it was Illinois' Brett Bielema, who said his team would be ranked in the top-15 if they were in the SEC.

It's a fair point until you remember that Bielema went ... checks notes ... 11-29 in the SEC over his five seasons at Arkansas. That's a tough sell.

The latest shot at the committee came from defending champ Curt Cignetti, who certainly has more street cred than Beilema. When Cig talks, folks listen. Doesn't mean he's right, but they at least don't laugh.

Thoughts? Look, I don't know who, exactly, Cignetti is singling out here. No. 1 Georgia, which lost to an unranked Auburn team a few weeks ago? No. 6 Texas? The Longhorns have lost to Florida (yuck) and Oklahoma (double yuck). Either way, it's an SEC team.

Here's the problem ... Indiana hasn't exactly faced a gauntlet this season. The Hoosiers have one loss — against Ohio State — and needed overtime to beat Michigan and Minnesota.

The SEC is just better than the Big Ten this year ... by a lot. It's a down year for the Big Ten. It happens. The committee is almost certainly punishing Indiana for the schedule compared to Texas or Georgia. Fair or not, that's what they're doing.

I watched it live and was truly baffled. What in the world is going on here? Some folks on the internet think Skattebo was a decoy. OK? That still doesn't explain why he just sat there for two seconds while Jameis was fighting for his life trying to gain an extra two inches.

But it was still ... odd. Just odd.

My family tradition is to put up decorations the weekend after Thanksgiving. Plenty of ballgames to "watch" while decking the halls and trimming the tree (artificial).

Hey, Zach — The lights go out on Black Friday, because who wants to leave the house that day anyway? By 5:00 our house looks like a used car lot.

So, I have an admission to make that I'm not proud of ...

Our house is decorated for Christmas. My wife yanked all the Halloween stuff down last weekend (against my wishes), so our house was just in purgatory for days. It wasn't decorated for Halloween. We don't have a ton of fall decor. And there were boxes and bins everywhere.

I couldn't take it anymore. I knew I didn't want to make two trips to the attic, so I decided to bring the Halloween stuff up, and take the Christmas stuff down at the same time.

Once the First Lady caught a whiff of those Christmas bins, it was game over. I didn't stand a chance.
