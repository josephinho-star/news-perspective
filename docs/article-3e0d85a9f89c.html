Eagles vs. Jaguars prediction: Trevor Lawrence & Co. clip the birds in London

2026-10-11 09:30

There have been a flood of losses and departures for the Eagles all week leading up to the NFL London Game against the Jaguars.

First Eagles offensive lineman Lane Johnson made the decision to retire midseason. Then the Eagles traded another starting offensive lineman, Cam Jurgens, to the Ravens for future draft picks.

On Friday, the team announced that center Drew Kendall was ruled out with a knee injury in addition to Saquon Barkley and Devonta Smith.

The spread for this game ballooned in the wake of all these developments from Jaguars -3.5 at open to -7.5

Eagles vs. Jaguars prediction

There’s no way to sugar coat the situation for the Eagles. The combination of injuries and playing overseas in London would make this a difficult situation regardless of the opponent.

But the Jaguars aren’t just any team, especially on the defensive side of the ball. The Jags allow the second-fewest points per game (13.3 ppg) in the league trailing only the Vikings.

Quarterbacks have been able to throw on the Jaguars — they’ve allow the fifth-most passing yards and the fifth-highest completion percentage this season — but when passers make a mistake, Jacksonville’s secondary is quick to pounce. The Jags defense leads the NFL in interceptions and has only allowed three passing touchdowns in four games.

Teams can move the ball on the Jacksonville, but their red zone defense is second-best in the NFL and puts the clamps on teams when they need to get stops.
