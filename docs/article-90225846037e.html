‘Ted Lasso’ Season 4 Total Episodes: How Many Episodes Are In ‘Ted Lasso’ Season 4? And Will There Be A ‘Ted Lasso’ Season 5?

2026-09-30 21:20

No spoilers here, but when the end credits roll on Ted Lasso Season 4, Episode 9, “Mae Rides the Bus,” fans of Apple TV‘s Emmy-winning comedy will be anxiously anticipating answers and incredibly curious to see where the show goes next.

After the Lady Greyhounds won a crucial match, Keeley (Juno Temple) shared life-changing news with Roy (Brett Goldstein), and Rebecca (Hannah Waddingham) broke some terribly heartbreaking news to Ted (Jason Sudeikis), Ted Lasso has a lot of ground to cover in its next episode.

The official synopsis for Ted Lasso Season 4, Episode 10 (curiously titled “The Departing Lass” on the press site and “Being Alive” on Apple TV’s streamer) is simply, “Big changes in bunches.” It’s an incredibly vague description, and a title that fans feel could be referencing the unexpected character death in Episode 409, be foreshadowing Ted stepping down as head coach of AFC Richmond’s women’s team, or both.

Ultimately, we’ll have to wait and see what Ted Lasso has in store. So when is the next new episode of Ted Lasso? How many episodes are in Ted Lasso Season 4? When are Ted Lasso episode release dates? What time does the next episode of Ted Lasso come out on Apple TV? How many seasons of Ted Lasso are there? And will there be a Ted Lasso Season 5? We’ve got answers!

Read on to learn more about the future of Ted Lasso, including Season 4’s total episode count.

How Many Episodes Are In Ted Lasso Season 4? Ted Lasso Season 4 Total Episodes:

Despite Ted Lasso Season 2 and Season 3 having 12 episodes each, Ted Lasso Season 4 will consist of 10 episodes, just like Season 1.

Ted Lasso Episode Release Dates: When Does The Next Episode Of Ted Lasso Come Out?

The next new episode of Ted Lasso comes out Wednesday, October 7, 2026. But read on to see how to watch sooner.

When Is Ted Lasso‘s Season 4, Episode 10? When Is Ted Lasso‘s Season 4 Finale?

Per Apple TV, Ted Lasso Season 4, Episode 10 (aka Ted Lasso‘s Season 4 finale) comes out Wednesday, October 7, 2026. Though locked-in fans of the streamer know that episodes are available to watch sooner. Here’s what we mean by that…

What Time Is Ted Lasso On Apple TV?

Apple TV regularly releases new episodes at 9:00 p.m. ET the night before designated premiere dates, so if you’re extra eager to watch Ted Lasso‘s Season 4 finale, keep your eyes peeled for Ted Lasso Season 4, Episode 10 at 9:00 p.m. ET on Tuesday, October 6.

How Many Seasons Of Ted Lasso Are There?

Ted Lasso‘s original three-season run ended in May 2023, but three years later, the show returned with a new iteration/continuation that focused on AFC Richmond Women’s Team. Ted Lasso is about to wrap its fourth season. But who’s to say we couldn’t see more?

Will There Be A Season 5 Of Ted Lasso? Ted Lasso Season 5 Renewal Status:

Ahead of Ted Lasso‘s Season 4 finale, Apple TV has yet to officially renew Ted Lasso for a fifth season, but that’s not to say Ted Lasso Season 5 isn’t a possibility. The Emmy-winning series is one of Apple TV’s most popular original shows. Heck, that’s part of the reason why it returned for Season 4! And without spoiling anything, when Decider spoke with stars Abbie Hern and Tanya Reynolds ahead of the finale, they expressed interest in and hopes for Season 5.

Given the success of the series and the large fanbase, we can certainly see Apple greenlighting more seasons of Ted Lasso, especially since there’s more of the Lady Greyhounds’ story to tell, and Sudeikis told The Hollywood Reporter he revived the series with another three-season arc in mind. So stay tuned for more updates!

Where To Watch Ted Lasso: Apple TV Streaming Info

Since Ted Lasso is an Apple TV original series, you’ll have to subscribe to the platform to watch new and existing episodes.

Apple TV comes with a seven-day free trial for new subscribers and has just one ad-free streaming plan available for $14.99/month.

A bundle with Apple TV and Peacock is also available starting at $14.99/month.
