The “Hitch” star, meanwhile, also fueled speculation when she referred to Gosling as “her husband” during an appearance on “Today Australia” in 2022.

When asked about her time in Australia, she said, “Everybody is amazing, everybody is welcoming us, it’s been so beautiful. My husband Ryan is here … and our children are here, we’re having the best time.”

Additionally, Mendes has occasionally been photographed with a ring on that finger.

The couple started dating in 2011 after starring in the Derek Cianfrance film, “The Place Beyond the Pines.” They have since welcomed two daughters, Esmeralda, 11, and Amanda, 10.

Mendes, who has since pulled back from her acting career to raise the girls, confessed to Women’s Health magazine in 2019 that she didn’t want children until she met Gosling.

“Then it made sense for me to have … not kids, but his kids,” she explained at the time. “It was very specific to him.”
