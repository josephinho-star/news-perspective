Grab a set of AirPods 4 for less than $100 right now on Amazon

2026-09-10 14:31

Apple just announced the latest AirPods 5, which features 50% better noise cancellation than AirPods 4, according to the brand.

That said, the product is only on preorder for now, and there are no intro offers currently available on Amazon. Lucky for you, though, the AirPods 4, which are in many respects just as good, are currently on sale and under $100 on the e-tailer for a limited time only, making it a great time to shop.

These earbuds personalized spatial audio and both improved sound and call quality, thanks to Apple’s redesigned H2 chip.

They’re also dust-, sweat- and water-resistant, which means a cardio workout at the gym is something these AirPods can handle. They’ve also got a lightweight design, making them easy for long hours of wear. Since the case features USB-C charging, you don’t have to worry about buying an additional cable for your new buds.

Make sure to connect your new AirPods to the Find My app, too, so you can keep track of them at all times.

This version of the AirPods doesn’t come with active noise cancellation. For that feature, you have to pony up an extra $50.

Shop the Apple AirPods 4 now at their reduced price while stocks last, and upgrade your music-listening sessions with these under-$100 earbuds.
