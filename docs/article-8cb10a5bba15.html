Trump asks UFC chief Dana White to be his new press secretary: ‘please take the job’

2026-11-07 19:44

The White House role has been empty since Katie Zacharia’s exit after less than a week on the job

US President Donald Trump said Saturday that he has offered the job of White House press secretary to his friend and UFC boss Dana White, an announcement unconventional even for the norm-busting leader and one that nobody in the White House immediately confirmed.

Trump, while he was at his golf club in Florida, posted on social media that he made the offer to “the Great Dana White, a violent Fighter and Warrior” to become the Republican president’s chief spokesman.

Trump said he requested that White bring along several UFC fighters as “assistants”, adding: “We need these people to defend us against the Lunatic Left. Dana will be a Press Secretary like none other. Dana, please take the job!”
