Aaron Judge not giving up on return for wild-card round in Yankees injury twist

2026-09-23 23:19

One day after manager Aaron Boone said he was “not expecting” the Yankees captain to return from a calf injury in time for the American League wild-card series, Judge opened the door for a first-round return.

Judge told ESPN on Wednesday that he’s looking to be ready to go for Game 1, which would be Tuesday night.

“That’s what the plan is,” Judge said. “Try to get my body ready so I’m ready to go.”

The three-time AL MVP, just eight days after returning from a rib fracture that had left him on on the injured list for three-plus months, landed back on the IL on Saturday with the calf strain.

Judge’s comments about a possible playoff return to ESPN echoed what he told reporters in Arizona on the day he returned to the IL.

“I’d like to be back by the postseason,” Judge said Saturday. “That’s definitely the plan.”

The injuries have limited Judge to 66 games, with the Yankees going 41-25 in those contests. They’ve gone 49-42 without him entering Wednesday’s game against the Rays.

Though Boone did pour some cold water Tuesday on the possibility of Judge returning for the wild-card round — likely to be against the rival Red Sox — he did say it wasn’t totally out of the question.
