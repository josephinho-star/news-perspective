Will players in the Gulf establish a new security framework?

2026-10-27 21:14

Officials from Riyadh, Islamabad, Ankara and Tehran met in Iran.

Top officials from the Mecca pact nations – Saudi Arabia, Pakistan and Turkiye – have sat down with Iranian leaders in Tehran.
