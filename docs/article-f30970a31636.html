2026-10-03 23:04

The Skims founder, 45, called the break from dating “great,” noting that she “could focus on [her] kids and projects that [she] really wanted to be involved with.”

They’ve since kept their relationship largely under wraps, with the exception of an occasional social media peek into their sweet moments together.
