Emma Hayes reveals her role in USMNT head coach search: ‘Very thorough process’

2026-11-19 01:45

U.S. women’s national team coach Emma Hayes addressed the role she’ll play in helping to find her new counterpart for the U.S. men’s national team following the departure of Mauricio Pochettino.

Hayes was named as part of the group that will hire the next coach, along with U.S. Soccer CEO JT Batson, chief operating officer Dan Helfrich and Oguchi Onyewu, the vice president of sporting, last week when the federation confirmed Pochettino would be leaving.

Pochettino coached his final match on Tuesday before leaving to take over as the new manager at Manchester United.

“Listen, I’m very embedded into the federation, and I work really closely with JT, in particular, and even more so with Dan as we’re growing to develop the American soccer movement,” Hayes said on Wednesday during a media conference call after the USWNT announced its roster for the Concacaf championship and World Cup qualifier.

“JT asked if I would be involved as a technical voice to be involved with the process. And I feel confident in the group of people that have been put together to create a thorough process to select the next men’s national team coach – my role is merely to support that through a technical lens, knowing I do this job … albeit with the women’s team, and that I’ve coached for a number of years, and can offer some of that insight into the application of what to look for and why.”

The American women are preparing for the Concacaf W Championship quarterfinal against El Salvador later this month, with the winner qualifying for the 2027 World Cup.

Hayes has an impressive resume that runs the course of 25 years, that includes the current position she holds as the women’s national team coach, a job she took in 2024.

Hayes said the search for the new USMNT coach will be a “very thorough process.”

“I think that’s the most important part of it. I feel very embedded in what we’re doing with the federation, so it doesn’t feel unusual to me [to help with the search],” she said. “So, like I’ve said many times, let’s build off the good work that Mauricio has done. I think that’s the important part, and let’s get it right.”

Pochettino said after Tuesday night’s 2-0 win over Haiti in the Concacaf Nations League quarterfinals second leg, that he would not be participating in the coaching search for the U.S. men’s national team’s next coach.

“I think it’s not my job. And for sure they have the capacity and the knowledge to find the right people with the right profile,” he said. “I don’t know how to measure my colleagues and I think it’s not my job.”

U.S. Soccer has plenty of time to make the hire, with the next competitive match not taking place until March, when the United States faces Mexico at SoFi Stadium in the Nations League semifinals.

Steve Cherundolo will run the USMNT’s December camp, which will include a game against Jamaica on Dec. 13 in Fort Lauderdale.
