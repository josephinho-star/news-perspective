Chinese President Xi Jinping will pay state visit to US this week, Beijing confirms

2026-09-21 07:06

Rare airport welcome by Donald Trump will precede talks on trade, tech and other areas of friction during second in-person summit of 2026

“At the invitation of President of the United States of America Donald J. Trump, President Xi Jinping will pay a state visit to the United States from September 23 to 25,” China’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs said in an official statement on Monday.

According to Chinese foreign ministry spokesman Guo Jiakun, Xi will “have an in-depth exchange of views with Trump on major issues concerning bilateral ties and world peace and developments”.
