Orioles agree to $125 million deal with Rays' postseason hero Freddy Peralta in free agency splash: report

2026-11-24 19:29

Freddy Peralta has reportedly parlayed his postseason success into a very large contract in free agency.

After starring with the Tampa Bay Rays in the 2026 postseason, being named the ALCS MVP for his stellar performance against the Cleveland Guardians, Peralta has agreed to a five-year, $125 million contract with the Baltimore Orioles, according to multiple reports. The deal is subject to a successful physical.

The 30-year-old was traded from the New York Mets to the Rays at the trade deadline, and while it took him a little bit to settle in, he found his stride in September and October. Peralta was named the American League's Pitcher of the Month in September.

Peralta struggled in New York, going 5-9 with a 4.99 ERA in 22 starts as the Mets languished in last place in the NL East and became one of baseball's biggest disappointments. He went 5-2 with a 3.25 ERA in 10 starts for the AL East champions, finishing the season 10-11 with a 4.42 ERA in 32 starts overall, covering 169 innings.

In five postseason appearances (four starts), Peralta had a 2-1 record with a 2.95 ERA in 21 1/3 innings pitched. In the ALCS, he went 2-0 with a 0.75 ERA in two starts and one relief appearance against the Guardians, allowing one run over 5 1/3 innings to win the clinching Game 6.

Before he was traded to the Mets in the offseason, Peralta had a very successful eight-year run with the Milwaukee Brewers. In his Brewers tenure, he made two All-Star teams and posted a 70-59 record with a 3.59 ERA in 931 innings.

His strong postseason run coupled with his success with the Brewers made him a desirable free agent.

Peralta will slot at the top of a rotation that lost Trevor Rogers, as the left-handed pitcher agreed to a five-year, $120 million contract with the Chicago Cubs, according to multiple reports. Peralta will join a rotation with Shane Baz, Kyle Bradish and Brandon Young.

The Associated Press contributed to this report.
