Sick SoCal business owner accused of creepy bathroom tactic to perv on customers

2026-10-10 15:35

A creepy SoCal business owner is accused of using sick tactics to spy on customers at his Covina automotive business — including attaching a hidden camera to his shoe to film up women’s skirts.

The Ontario Police Department arrested Young’s Automotive owner Bryce Neighbors, 39, of Upland last week after an investigation involving possession of child sexual assault material.

At his business, police say Neighbors planted multiple hidden cameras inside the bathroom to record unsuspecting customers. Cops also determined he used the hidden shoe camera to record up women’s dresses.

He was booked at the West Valley Detention Center on multiple felony charges, including commercial distribution of obscene material depicting minors and possessing or controlling child sex abuse material.
