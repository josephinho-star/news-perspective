Shop Small Business Saturday for fruit trees, Advent calendars and more

2026-11-28 10:00

Small Business Saturday is a good excuse to check a few names off your holiday shopping list while supporting small businesses. We've rounded up finds from four brands, including an olive tree you can grow indoors, a pickle Advent calendar and a tote made with pieces handcrafted by military spouses.

PlantOGram makes it easy to give someone a tree they can grow at home.

This olive tree from PlantOGram is a Tuscan variety you can keep in a pot by a sunny window. It's a timeless gift symbolizing peace, prosperity and longevity.

A Kesar mango tree is a variety from western India that grows sweet mangoes with golden-orange flesh. It's celebrated for its incredible sweetness and rich tropical flavor.

PlantOGram’s Super Hass is a dwarf variety that grows large, creamy avocados. It comes in three sizes, starting at 1 to 2 feet tall.

GiveThemBeer is a family-owned business based in St. Petersburg, Florida. It started with craft beer gifts and now offers everything from whiskey and wine to specialty snacks, plus more than 20 Advent calendars for adults.

Count down to Christmas with GiveThemBeer's 2026 Advent Calendar. Each door has a full-size can from an American brewery, with picks including dark brews and Belgian-style beers.

The Great American Jerky Tour comes with five bags of beef jerky, each from a different American maker. They come packed in a reusable metal ammo can, making it a fun gift for anyone who loves jerky or spends a lot of time outdoors.

GiveThemBeer’s pickle Advent calendar is a thoughtful gift for the pickle lover in your life. Behind 12 numbered doors, they’ll find surprises like spicy pickles, pickle lemonade and pickle-flavored pistachios.

Tom Arrix started Get Joy after his golden retriever, Cooper, was diagnosed with lymphoma in 2018. The diagnosis led him to take a closer look at what he was feeding Cooper and inspired him to start a fresh dog food company.

Switch up your dog’s dinner with Get Joy’s freeze-dried raw dog food. It comes in beef and chicken recipes that you can serve as a full meal or food topper. Get 50% off your first auto-ship box.

The American Prosperity Shop

Karen Post started the American Prosperity Shop after creating a pillow for America’s 250th birthday, and it now sells American-made gifts and home accessories from artisans and small businesses.

The Freedom 250 tassel has red, white and blue strands with a gold-colored charm commemorating America’s 250th birthday. Hang it from a cabinet pull or tie it onto your favorite bag.

Use the American Prosperity Pillow to surprise someone with a piece of jewelry, add a small accent to your couch or give the tooth fairy a spot to leave a few dollars. It's navy on one side and white on the other, with gold trim, shredded U.S. currency tucked inside and a commemorative America250 charm.

Use Manready Mercantile's American Dream room spray to freshen up your living room. It blends bourbon vanilla and sandalwood with a hint of citrusy bergamot.

Handcrafted from white oak planted at Mount Vernon in 1785 at George Washington's direction, the Roots & Jones George Washington Commander pen is a gift for the history buff on your list. Each pen comes with Washington's initials on the finial, a custom box and a Certificate of Authenticity signed by the 24th Regent of the Mount Vernon Ladies' Association.
