Young Albany Democrats accuse Israel of ‘genocide’ in Gaza — sparking civil war within NY party

2026-09-11 11:30

A Democratic civil war erupted over the Israel-Gaza war after the Young Democrats of Albany County issued a statement accusing the Jewish state of committing genocide in Gaza.

State Democratic Party Chairman Jay Jacobs and other Democrats in the Capital District slammed the Albany Young Democrats’ stand as a wrong-headed smear.

The offshoot Dem group ignited the intraparty fight with a Sept. 3 statement by its executive board.

“Let us make this crystal clear: Benjamin Netanyahu’s Israeli government has committed a genocide in Gaza and gross violations of international law against the Palestinian people,” the statement said.

It made no mention that Hamas started the war by launching a sneak attack on Israel on Oct. 7, 2023 that killed about 1,200 people, including more than 800 civilians and many young people attending a music festival.

In sum, Albany Young Democrats side with the Palestinians over Israelis.

“We recognize the importance of making our position clear, and stand firmly in solidarity with young Democrats across America in fighting for the Palestinian people,” the statement said.

“Young Democrats oppose the genocide in Gaza, the occupation of the West Bank and the Israeli government’s denial of civil and political rights on an equal basis in the territories its military occupies.”

The Young Dems also said giving a “blank check” of billions of dollars in military aid to Israel “has to stop.”

The statement parrots growing anti-Israel sentiment in the party, reflected by New York City democratic socialist Mayor Zohran Mamdani, congressional candidate Darializa Avila Chevalier and “The Squad” in the House of Representatives that includes Reps. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, Rashida Tlaib and Ilhan Omar.

But Gary Greenberg, who served on the Albany County Democratic Committee for 25 years, accused the Young Democrats of siding with Hamas and fomenting Jew hatred.

“They’re basically supporting the policies of Hamas. That’s who the Palestinians are,” Greenberg fumed to The Post Wednesday. “They’re basically siding with the Palestinians who crossed the border and invaded Israel [on Oct. 7, 2023.

“It’s a vile rant against the Jewish community. It’s a Jew hatred statement. The head of the Albany County Democrats Jake Crawford and County Executive Dan McCoy must rescind this vile rant against the Jewish community.”

State party Chairman Jacobs criticized the statement as a smear and false accusation against Israel, and insisted the statement is an “outlier” within the party committee.

Jacobs, who is Jewish and the descendant of Holocaust survivors, said Israel is engaged in a war following Hamas’ Oct. 7, 2023 attack against the Jewish state.

“I disagree with their categorization. Genocide it is not,” Jacobs told The Post. “In war, reprehensible things happen. Wars are not neat things without unintended consequences.”

The Young Democrats’ statement, Jacobs said, “does not take into account the reality, the existential threat to Israel.”

He said there is legitimate criticism of the actions of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s government, such as allowing Jewish settlers in the West Bank.

But Israel is not intentionally trying to wipe out the civilian population of Gaza, Jacobs.

“Look up the definition of genocide,” he said.

The New York State Young Democrats, which does not mention the Israel-Gaza conflict in its platform, had no immediate comment.
