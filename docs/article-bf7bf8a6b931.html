Trump leads GOP midterm convention attendees in pledge to vote, warns them ‘you go to hell’ if they don’t

2026-09-11 02:51

DALLAS — President Trump asked thousands of convention attendees to raise their hand and pledge to vote in the midterms — cautioning them of eternal damnation if they failed to do so.

“I pledge to the greatest president in the history of the United States… I’m going to get my friends, my family, and we are going to vote on Nov. 3, or we are going to vote before that,” Trump began.

After attendees eagerly complied, Trump added: “You know what happens if you don’t vote? You go to hell.”
