Belgian PM’s chocolate gift to Modi doesn’t survive journey to India

2026-09-04 11:39

Diplomatic protocol is like a box of chocolates. You never know what you’re going to get — or whether it’ll survive the flight.

A chocolate replica of New Delhi’s India Gate intended as Belgium’s gift to Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi was damaged on its way to India, Belgian Prime Minister Bart De Wever said Thursday.

De Wever joked about the mishap at a press conference alongside Modi. He had planned to present him with a sculpture “made out of the best Belgian chocolate.”
