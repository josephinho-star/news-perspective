'Landman' star Michelle Randolph rocks white bikini months after going Instagram official with Glen Powell

2026-08-31 23:06

Michelle Randolph is giving fans a glimpse at her off-duty style.

The "Landman" actress shared a mirror selfie to her Instagram Story on Sunday, posing in a white triangle bikini top with a black skirt and a baseball cap.

Randolph, 28, sat on the floor in front of a wood-paneled wall with her knees bent as she held up her phone for the photo. She finished the casual look with black-and-red slide sandals, layered bracelets and rings, while a pair of sunglasses and a glass sat beside her on the floor.

The effortlessly stylish snapshot comes as Randolph continues to draw attention both on and off-screen.

Randolph first broke through in Taylor Sheridan’s "1923," starring as Elizabeth Strafford in the "Yellowstone" prequel. She later joined Sheridan’s "Landman" as Ainsley Norris, the teenage daughter of Billy Bob Thornton’s Tommy Norris.

Randolph’s growing career has brought increased attention to her life off-screen as well, including her relationship with Glen Powell.

The pair first sparked romance rumors in November 2025 after they were spotted dancing together in Powell’s hometown of Austin, Texas. They continued to be seen together over the following months, including during outings in Los Angeles and New York City.

Powell made their relationship Instagram official on July 7, sharing a black-and-white photo of the couple kissing. The social media debut came weeks after the pair had been photographed holding hands and kissing during an outing in Manhattan.

Despite the attention surrounding their romance, Randolph has generally kept her personal life private.

"I think, for peace of mind, those are really important for me to keep separate," she told InStyle earlier this year. "How people perceive you publicly is not who you are."

She echoed that sentiment in a February interview with Bustle.

"I think my personal life is so separate than my work life. And I understand that my job is something public, and I’ve chosen that," Randolph said.

"But I just think for my peace of mind, I just try to keep those things as separate as possible. And it’s hard, but it’s important to me."

Randolph has also spoken about navigating increased attention as her acting career takes off.

In a December 2025 conversation with "Landman" co-star Demi Moore for Interview magazine, Randolph said she has had to learn not to let outside opinions shape how she sees herself.

"I had to really unlearn caring so much about what people thought of me," she said.

The actress added that staying off her phone more and spending time with family help keep her grounded.

"Honestly, I have to just shut a certain part of my brain off, and I’ve gotten better at doing it," Randolph said. "I’ve gotten a lot better at not being on my phone as much, trying to be really present in just my life in general. And my family keeps me really, really grounded."

Randolph said that despite the increasingly glamorous side of her career, life at home remains decidedly normal.

"You go on these crazy press tours and you’re doing all these fabulous things, and then it’s like I go home and my parents are like, ‘Unload the dishwasher, please.’"
