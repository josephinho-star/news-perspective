Kelly Osbourne in a ‘happier place’ after messy Sid Wilson split, brother Jack reveals

2026-08-29 20:45

Kelly Osbourne has come to a “happier place” following her volatile split from Slipknot musician Sid Wilson, brother Jack Osborne revealed.

“Kelly’s living her country life,” Jack recently told the Sun, noting that she’s “living out by the house and she’s just doing her thing, being a mum.”

He added, “She’s in a happier place and she’s got a good routine going. She’s focusing on her and her son.”

A rep for Kelly did not immediately respond to Page Six’s request for comment on Saturday afternoon.

Kelly, 41, and Wilson, 49, parted ways back in March after four years together, with a source divulging to the Daily Mail that they “decided to call off their engagement.”

“Kelly has been struggling following the loss of her father,” the insider noted of the death of Ozzy Osbourne in July 2025. “The grieving process has been incredibly difficult, and she’s been doing everything she can to cope.”

They added that the former couple — who welcomed son Sidney in late 2022 — had been “facing challenges in their relationship for some time.”

“Things were not as they appeared” for the co-parents, the source explained.

“They tried to make it work, particularly for the sake of their child, but ultimately decided that separating is the best path forward,” the insider told the outlet.

“Kelly has endured a great deal over the past year,” they concluded, noting that, “Despite everything, she remains sober and is now focusing on herself and her role as a mother moving ahead.”

Months after the split, the “Osbournes” alum took to social media with what appeared to be a savage diss toward Wilson after he was reportedly fired from Slipknot.

“Wake the f–k up and take some responsibility. I will not take your s–t anymore! I’m done,” she wrote via Instagram Stories earlier this month, before deleting the message.

“I want to be happy and I have a beautiful son to raise. Leave me alone. Let me heal. I can’t protect you from yourself anymore,” she added.

Another post read, “Also while I’m at it can I have my dogs back and all my possessions. Oh yeah and maybe some child support?”

Wilson — who originally met Kelly back in 1999 when Slipknot toured with Ozzfest — proposed to the mom of one backstage during Ozzy’s Black Sabbath farewell concert at Villa Park in Birmingham, England, on July 5, 2025.

Ozzy died at the age of 76 just weeks later, on July 22.
