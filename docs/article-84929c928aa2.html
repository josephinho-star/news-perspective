By-election in Keir Starmer’s old seat expected Oct. 8

2026-09-07 14:36

LONDON — The by-election to replace Keir Starmer as a member of parliament is expected to take place on Oct. 8, two Labour Party officials told POLITICO.

Voters in the central London constituency of Holborn and St Pancras will go to the polls next month to elect the ex-PM’s successor in what is likely to be a tense fight between the ruling Labour Party and insurgent Greens.

On Monday, Labour moved a writ — the parliamentary procedure to trigger a by-election when a seat becomes vacant — and the two Labour Party officials quoted above, granted anonymity to discuss internal discussions, said it will pave the way for the Oct. 8 date.
