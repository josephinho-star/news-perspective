The FBI and USDA were reportedly hacked

2026-11-21 17:42

Friday, it was reported that both the US Department of Agriculture (USDA) and the FBI were hacked. In reporting on the FBI hack, CNN noted that this is the second such attack on the agency in the last few months, this time targeting the National Crime Information Center (NCIC), which federal, state, and local law enforcement use to share information, including warrants and missing persons. President Donald Trump later confirmed the hack in an interview with Fox News. NBC first reported the USDA hack on Friday. According to Reuters:

While the payments database is the known extent of the intrusion so far, the former officials warned that the compromised system could provide a backdoor to more sensitive information, including food safety data and research on animal-borne illnesses, according to the report.

The breaches come following a week of major outages across several sectors, including at the FAA and NSA, which the government has sought to reassure the public were not the result of a cyberattack.

President Trump suggested that the breach of the FBI might not be an entirely bad thing, telling Fox News that, “Maybe we’ll learn about the voter fraud, all the voter fraud that took place,” and “wouldn’t it be great to learn about Russia, Russia, Russia?” referring to investigations into his ties to Russia.

The FBI suffered a breach, reported in late September, that allegedly compromised personal records of “all FBI employees and applicants.” This was followed by a hack of DHS, reported in October, comprising 40,000 employee records. In the wake of these major outages, the White House national cyber director, Sean Cairncross, stepped down this week.
