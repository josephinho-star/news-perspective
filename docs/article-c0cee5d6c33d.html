Beijing doubles down on Type 071 amphibious warships, signalling a Taiwan focus

2026-11-02 10:06

Move challenges US assessments that Beijing lacks the maritime lift and logistical support needed for a large-scale attack on the island

Beijing is accelerating production of its Type 071 amphibious transport docks by expanding construction to a second shipyard, potentially strengthening its ability to move troops and military equipment across the Taiwan Strait in a conflict.

According to Janes Fighting Ships, two of the vessels in Shanghai were launched in August and October but have yet to enter service, while the other two remain under construction.
