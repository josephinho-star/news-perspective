Crush your goals: My lazy hack to hit 10K steps is at its lowest price

2026-11-27 23:30

We know how winter goes — especially in New York City.

There are days when you simply don’t want to leave the house, let alone worry about hitting your step-count goals.

As a health and wellness writer who’s spent about 3 years testing the buzziest products on the market, there’s one simple piece of at-home equipment I swear by again and again: the MERACH UltraWalk W60 Walking Pad Treadmill.

The compact walking pad is the latest version in the brand’s fleet, and it’s currently on sale for its lowest price ever for Black Friday (yes, Nov. 27 is already here). But it’s not just the rare discount that makes this a great time to snag one. The privilege of getting your steps in from the comfort of your cozy living room — not to mention, in front of the TV — is seriously hard to beat.

With just about a month left before the New Year, now is the time to crush those 2026 health goals. And if there’s one thing I’ve learned, it’s that a little walking can go a long way.

There are a few key features that really make this new walking pad model stand out.

The W60 comes with a built-in Bluetooth speaker for music, podcasts and audiobooks, plus customizable RGB lighting that syncs to your tunes. In my experience, the lighting system also makes the walking belt a bit easier to see when the room is dim.

For tracking your progress, the treadmill syncs with Apple Health and Google Fit and connects to the MERACH app, where you can access trainer-led workouts, on-demand classes and real-time performance stats. I love the fact that you can monitor your progress to keep yourself accountable.

Adjusting your workout is also super easy. You can control speed and incline through the app or included remote, with speeds up to 3.6 mph and a 12% incline for a surprisingly challenging burn. Trust me, I’ve worked up a serious sweat on this thing.

The sturdy steel frame supports up to 400 pounds, and the spacious 16.5-by-41.3-inch deck (which MERACH says offers 33% more stride space) makes walking feel comfortable and natural. Compared to other walking pads I’ve tested, this one feels noticeably more stable, too.

As a toddler-mom, though, my favorite feature might be the built-in child lock, which offers a little extra peace of mind. Between the thoughtful safety features and smart extras, the W60 has made me rethink at-home cardio — and my workouts have never felt easier to squeeze in.
