8 students wounded, 1 critically, in İstanbul school knife attack

2026-10-06 10:56

A teenage girl wounded eight girls in a knife attack at an İstanbul middle school on Tuesday, leaving one in critical condition, officials said.

The attack occurred around 8:40 a.m. at a school in Başakşehir, in the northwestern part of the city.

Police detained the suspected attacker, reportedly a 15-year-old 10th-grade student at another school.

Health Minister Kemal Memişoğlu said on X that there were “eight people injured in the incident — one in critical condition, and two in moderate-to-serious condition,” all receiving hospital treatment.

İstanbul Governor Davut Gül said five students had minor injuries and were expected to be discharged later in the day, while one was undergoing surgery.

“The pupil who carried out the attack lives in the same neighborhood and attends a different school. She entered the premises wearing school uniform and stabbed the children indiscriminately,” he said.

Başakşehir District Governor Cemil Özgür Öneği had initially reported seven girls injured and identified the suspected attacker as a high school student.

Last month, a teenager opened fire outside a high school in Turgutlu, in the western province of Manisa, wounding 11 students before being detained.

In April, two school shootings on consecutive days shocked Turkey and raised concerns about school security and access to firearms.

A teenager wounded 16 people at his former high school in the southeastern province of Şanlıurfa. The following day, a 14-year-old attacked a middle school in Kahramanmaraş, killing a teacher and nine students.
