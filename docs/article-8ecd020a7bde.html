Anthropic IPO live updates: AI lab tops $2 trillion market cap after record offering

2026-11-19 19:31

Anthropic opened at $111.50 per share in the artificial intelligence lab's Nasdaq debut on Thursday after the company completed the biggest initial public offering on record.

The stock will trade under the ticker "ANT" after the company raised $81.3 billion in the IPO. The Claude creator sold 980 million shares at $83 a piece on Wednesday, lifting the size and price of its offering.

The 5-year-old company captivated the corporate world with its Claude AI models and products, which have fueled its meteoric rise this year. Anthropic said in its prospectus that it generated $33.24 billion in revenue for the nine months ended Sept. 30, up a staggering 1,200% from the same period last year.

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei co-founded the company in 2021 after he and a small group of researchers defected from OpenAI, now its chief rival. Anthropic has faced sharp criticism over its positions on safety, regulation and national security, including by President Donald Trump and other senior government officials.
