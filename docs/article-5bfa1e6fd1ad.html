Recent workout video indicates Rams’ Myles Garrett can fly

2026-08-29 02:35

It’s well known that Myles Garrett is an athlete out of this world, but is he literally?

The X account @ NFL Films released a video of Garrett working out alongside Houston Texans star edge rusher Will Anderson Jr. where they were working on box jump exercises, but it appeared Garrett looked like he was floating during the workout.

Before Garrett cleared the box set, he was sitting down on a weight bench and was performing a workout that focused on explosiveness.

As he moved off the bench, Garrett immediately exploded into the air, where he seamlessly cleared the box set, with his feet connected as he was gliding in the air.

Garrett, 30, has a reputation of being one of the freakiest athletes in the NFL and is well known for his intense workouts that focus on powerlifting and extreme plyometrics.

With a 6-foot-4 frame and weighing 272 pounds, Garrett has often been referred to as “Big Foot” and “Superman” in the NFL.

During the Houston Texans’ preseason game against the Raiders last week, Anderson was spotted on the sideline and was interviewed on the sideline during the middle of the game, where he talked about finishing his second offseason workout with Garrett and said, “He’s been like a big brother to me.”

Garrett is set to enter his 10th season in the NFL, in what’s already been a Hall of Fame career that’s featured him being named a five-time All-Pro and two-time Defensive Player of the Year winner, most notably last season, when he broke the NFL’s single-season sack record.

The Rams just wrapped up their final game of the preseason, where rookie quarterback Ty Simpson shined on the football field, and are now about to begin the regular season when they play the San Francisco 49ers in Melbourne, Australia.
