Amanda Batula takes aim at ex Kyle Cooke with pointed message after his explosive West Wilson rant

2026-10-30 00:22

Amanda Batula took aim at her estranged husband, Kyle Cooke, with a pointed message after the latter claimed West Wilson was blowing up her “life and career.”

“Kyle realized that when Amanda declined to film the upcoming season of #inthecity that he could no longer control or manipulate her anymore and melted down in West’s IG comments,” one person wrote on the platform, to which Batula responded with the bullseye emoji.

According to Deumoxi, Wilson, 31, also called out Cooke’s alleged behavior against Batula, 35, after one social media user tried to shame him for their relationship.

“I gotta admit, I still like you West,” the comment read. “But Kyle was your boy.”

“My boy’s don’t call their partners ‘dumb ass bitch,'” Wilson responded.

A rep for Cooke, 44, wasn’t immediately available to Page Six for comment.

The drama between the trio escalated after the Loverboy founder left a since-deleted feisty comment under Wilson’s recent Instagram post.

“More like [you’re] a coke head that blew my ex’s life and career up,” Cooke wrote following the news that Batula wouldn’t be returning for Season 2 of the “Summer House” spinoff series.

One insider told Page Six that the show’s producers wanted Batula to return to the series part-time. Additionally, Batula allegedly pushed for West to be on the series, but the network was against the request. His absence allegedly led to her decision.

Another source, however, denied the allegations, stating that the time wasn’t right for them to shoot the show, and a third source claimed that Batula’s salary request wasn’t met.

“In the City” is currently filming, starring Cooke, her ex-pal Lindsay Hubbard, and a few new cast members.

Cooke and Batula, meanwhile, called it quits in January after four years of marriage.
