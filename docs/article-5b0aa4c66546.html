Perez Hilton’s scars visible as celeb blogger steps out in Miami following heartbreaking suicide attempt

2026-11-19 01:46

Perez Hilton was spotted running errands around his Miami neighborhood on Wednesday, more than three months after he attempted suicide during a livestream.

Hilton (real name Mario Armando Lavandeira Jr.) was dressed casually in the photos obtained exclusively by Page Six, wearing a short-sleeved army green T-shirt, baseball cap, and shorts as he stopped to fill his car up with gas.

Hilton is continuing his recovery through an intensive outpatient program while being back at home with his family, his loved ones reported.

“This allows him to receive ongoing therapy and mental health support as he gradually returns to everyday life,” they said on Hilton’s website. “With Thanksgiving approaching, we’re looking forward to spending some much-needed quality time together as a family and enjoying a little peace and quiet during the holiday.”

Miami police were called to Hilton’s residence Aug. 4 after he concerned fans by sharing a livestream in which he appeared to harm himself. Officers transported him to a local hospital where he received medical attention.

He was listed as being in “serious but stable” condition and his family said he had “experienced significant blood loss and sustained additional injuries” — including broken bones — that required surgery.

In September, Hilton entered a residential mental health treatment facility to receive specialized psychiatric and medical care.

While he was there, he reconnected with his family — including his three kids, who fled his home during the incident — through video calls and supervised visits.

Earlier this month, police bodycam footage of the night Hilton attempted to take his own life was released.

In the footage, obtained by Page Six, blood could be seen on the walls of Hilton’s Miami home as officers responded to the scene.

Hilton’s bloody face and body were blurred out as he was shown being placed onto a stretcher and transported to an ambulance.
