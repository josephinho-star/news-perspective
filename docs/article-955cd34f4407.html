Kris Jenner said she ‘wouldn’t go a day without’ these hair growth peptides — and they’re 20% off

2026-09-04 20:43

Keeping up with Kris Jenner’s favorite products?

The iconic momager is such a fan of Omi’s Hair Growth Peptides, she and Khloé Kardashian both became stakeholders in the brand. And right now, the label’s offering 20% off new subscriptions for Labor Day weekend.

“I started taking [them] as a gummy form, two a day, and it pretty much changed my life,” Jenner raved in a sponsored Instagram Story this February.

She also dished about it to Page Six Style when her partnership was first announced, saying she “so impressed” with the science behind the formulas.

“I’ve tried every product under the sun,” she added. “After taking Omi consistently, I noticed my hair feeling thicker, stronger and just healthier overall. I’m obsessed. I became a stakeholder because I believe in it, I use it, and I wouldn’t go a day without it.”

While Jenner’s said she personally takes the chewable gummies, the brand also makes capsules for both men and women.

The reality star also regularly reposts news about the brand; just last week, she reshared a post about the Nourish formula on her Instagram Stories and wrote, “The best hair gummies!!!”

Developed by nutritional expert and author Naomi Whittel and Dr. Rob Kelly, PhD, a research scientist in regenerative hair growth, the gummies are designed to restore keratin peptides to help anchor hair to the scalp and strengthen strands.

Celeb colorist Tracey Cunningham has also teamed up with Omi — and according to the brand’s site, recommends the products to all of her clients.

“If you want thick, strong hair you have to start at your follicles,” she explained.
