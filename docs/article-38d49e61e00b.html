When will Mamdani care about victims of crimes the same way he does about illegal migrants?

2026-10-10 02:37

And by the way New York politicians immediately reacted — with press conferences and outraged tweets the length of “War and Peace” — one would think ICE had made swiss cheese of a sweet grandma as she handed out Werther’s Originals to the neighborhood children.

She used the shooting to call for NYPD Commissioner Jessica Tisch to step down because she had the gall to read “our neighbor’s past interactions with the police” — that is, his long criminal record — instead of condemning the shooting.

Yes, she literally called him “our neighbor.” Mr. Rogers would have been the first person to call the cops on this miscreant.

Where is this energy and outrage when innocent citizens are injured or killed by illegal immigrants, criminals with long rap sheets and the criminally insane?

Because if they poured as much into the innocent as they do illegals, they’d be forced to admit that their own sanctuary and soft-on-crime policies are responsible for most of this death and disorder.

Just ask Tish James, who wrote of Belgal on X that she was, “keeping the victim and their family in my prayers tonight.”

They simply don’t care.

Every day they proudly flaunt their twisted moral framework.

Law breakers get all their love, attention and activism, while regular New Yorkers get the ol’ hand flicked under the chin.

James accused Komatireddy of wanting to go into the subways and “remove people struggling with mental illness,” adding that it was turning “back the clock of progress.”

James and company’s idea of “progress” is having mentally ill lunatics — many of whom have criminal records — roam the subway.

Every morning, I bear witness to James’ “progress” as I step over two to three passed-out druggies on my subway platform.
