‘Nobody Wants This’ and ‘Succession’ star announces pregnancy on Thanksgiving

2026-11-26 20:39

The first shot showed her standing with her husband, illustrator Tyson Mason, who had his arm around his wife.

Lupe, 37, had her baggy red T-shirt pulled up to reveal her growing bump adorned with an adorable hand-painted turkey, a close-up of which was featured on the second slide.

The “Nobody Wants This” star carried the couple’s 2-year-old daughter, Ellis, in her arms as Mason held up the family’s dog. The foursome smiled for the picture while celebrating the cozy holiday at home.

Friends and celebrities alike, including Lupe’s “Nobody Wants This” co-star Jackie Tohn, flooded the comments section with messages of congratulations.

“GORGEOUS FAMILY OF ANGELICS,” Tohn, who ironically plays the estranged wife of Lupe’s onscreen love interest in Season 3, gushed beneath the snaps.

Lupe and Mason welcomed Ellis in August 2024 after the actress filmed Season 1 of the Netflix rom-com while pregnant.

“I had friends be like, ‘You really need to get a facial; you really need to get a [massage],'” she told us on the red carpet ahead of the ceremony.

“I didn’t do anything. I have a 5-month-old, so truly, sleep deprivation is all I did to prepare,” she confessed. “I got three hours [of sleep] last night, so just a lot of caffeine — if that’s a beauty regimen.”

Despite the long nights, the former HBO personality said she was “doing well” and that her postpartum experience was “going good” so far.

“I feel really lucky that I had an easy delivery,” she added.

Lupe married Mason in a courthouse wedding the following month.
