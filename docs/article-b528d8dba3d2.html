Kevin Reddington defends Lindsay Clancy’s ex Patrick against ‘horrific conspiracy allegations’: ‘Stop with the speculation’

2026-09-09 14:57

Defense attorney Kevin Reddington said Clancy’s then-husband, Patrick Clancy, “ought to be able to move on with his life” and not have to continue to be subjected to unfounded conspiracy theories that he was actually the one who strangled their three children and framed Lindsay Clancy for it.

“Stop with the speculation and horrific conspiracy allegations that he was involved with some massive conspiracy to kill his children,” Reddington said Wednesday in an interview on “CBS Mornings.” “It’s horrific what this man has been going through and he’s entitled to move on with his life.”

Here’s the latest on Lindsay Clancy’s murder trial:

Patrick Clancy divorced Lindsay Clancy, moved to New York City and married infertility specialists, Rachel Danis, earlier this year.

He was the prosecution’s first witness in the captivating case that ended with a mistrial on Friday because of a hopelessly deadlocked jury.
