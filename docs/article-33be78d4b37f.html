Is Sports Betting Legal in Ohio?

Yes, sports betting is legal in Ohio. It can be done using mobile apps or in person for anyone over 21 years of age. In Ohio, you can bet on any professional sporting event and collegiate event, but you cannot wager on college player props.

Deposit matches give you a 1:1 match for every dollar you deposit. These are bonus funds, and are not the same as real cash, but can be used to place wagers. Ohio Sports Betting Laws and Timeline

On Jan. 1, 2023, sports betting became legal in Ohio with Governor Mike DeWine signing House Bill 29. The actual signing took place in December 2021, but the first app wasn’t available until January 2023 after being cleared by the Ohio Casino Control Commission. Since the launch, over 10 different sportsbooks have been legalized for online or retail use.

Teams and Markets to Bet On in Ohio

There is no shortage of professional and college teams to bet on in Ohio. In the NFL, you have two teams that you can choose from, with the Bengals and Browns taking center stage. There are also two MLB teams you can wager on with the Guardians in Cleveland and the Reds in Cincinnati. The Cleveland Cavaliers are an option for NBA fans, and the Columbus Blue Jackets give hockey fans an extra incentive. Ohio State is also one of the most popular college football programs in the country.

College Sports Betting Rules in Ohio

The professional level is not the only way to bet on sports in Ohio. Instead, you can bet on college-level games. You can bet on sides, totals, and spreads with Ohio and national college teams. However, you cannot place wagers on player props from college games.
