Orlando Bloom shows off washboard abs while vacationing with daughter Daisy Dove as ex Katy Perry moves on

2026-09-12 23:52

Orlando Bloom displayed his washboard abs, cuddled with daughter Daisy Dove and posed alongside a mystery woman in a new set of vacation photos.

In an Instagram carousel shared Friday, the “Lord of the Rings” star was pictured standing in a body of water with his hands on his hips, showing off his fit physique while staring down the camera.

In another sweet snapshot, he cradled his 6-year-old daughter on his lap while sitting at an outdoor table at dusk, her face turned away from the camera.

A pic showed Daisy Dove standing on what appeared to be a washed up buoy while wearing a sundress — and the actor had drawn a heart around her and scribbled on her face to obscure it.

The grade schooler was also seen sporting a temporary Captain Jack Sparrow tattoo — a sweet nod to the “Pirates of the Caribbean” franchise, in which her father co-starred alongside Johnny Depp — as she waded in a body of water, wearing a bucket hat to shade herself from the sun and the camera.

Yet another snapshot showed what appeared to be Bloom taking a photo of his shadow standing alongside the shadow of a mystery woman on the beach.

Though Bloom gave no hint as to the woman’s identity, the actor, 49, has most recently been linked to Swiss model Luisa Laemmel, 28, as the pair left the Super Bowl arm-in-arm in February. He was later spotted touching down in Italy with her in July.

Bloom captured his vacation upload, “Everything everywhere all at once ☀️.”

The snapshots come just days after ex-fiancée Katy Perry — with whom he shares Daisy — gushed over her new romance with former Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau while addressing her split from the “Troy” star.

“It was probably the hardest time,” she told People earlier this month of her split from Bloom, which they confirmed in a joint statement shared in July 2025.

“My friends were all hanging out back home. I was crying and just feeling far away,” she continued. “But then my angels went to work. They really showed up.”

At the time, Perry, 41, was in the midst of her “Lifetimes” tour. She went on to say that she “got the greatest gift from this tour: I got to meet the love of my life.”

Perry and Trudeau, 54, met while she was on tour, with the pair being spotted taking in a quiet dinner in Montreal before Trudeau was caught cheering her on during her tour stop there.

Perry told People that she’d always hoped for an “epic, legendary love,” and found it in the politician.

“Everything in my life is epic, but this is kind of the missing puzzle piece — then it showed up,” she told the outlet.

Of Bloom, she said in part, “Each relationship is a teacher.”
