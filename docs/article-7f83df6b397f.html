Erika Kirk posts heartbreaking wedding photo to honor husband Charlie on anniversary of his murder

2026-09-10 23:40

Erika Kirk paid tribute to her slain husband, Charlie Kirk, Thursday by posting a wedding photo on social media with a poignant message on the one-year anniversary of his murder.

The black-and-white photo showed Erika, in her wedding dress, and Charlie, in his suit, holding hands during their nuptials.

“…until Heaven,” read the message in the center of the photo, which Erika shared on X.

Charlie, 31, was shot and killed by a sniper at Utah Valley University a year ago Thursday, during one of his signature “Prove me wrong” events at the Orem campus.

Events honoring the influential Turning Point USA founder are being held across the country to mark the one-year anniversary of his death.

Erika, 37, is not planning to mark the anniversary publicly.

“Erika will mark the anniversary of Charlie’s death privately with her and Charlie’s two young children, their family, and close friends,” a family spokesperson said.

“She is so grateful for the continued outpouring of prayers, love, and support as she and her family remember and honor Charlie as the beloved husband and father he was.”
