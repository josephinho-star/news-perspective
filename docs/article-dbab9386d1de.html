Justin and Hailey Bieber pack on the PDA in bed during intimate photo shoot

2026-09-07 20:46

Justin Bieber and Hailey Bieber appeared more in love than ever as they passionately packed on the PDA in bed during an intimate photo shoot.

The couple shared an intense kiss while lying down during their steamy WYouth cover shoot published Monday.

Another photo showed them posing with their heads touching, as the Rhode founder tenderly wrapped her arm around the Grammy winner’s head.

In a third photo, Justin, 32, gently kissed Hailey, 29, on the cheek as she smiled with her eyes closed.

The beauty guru wore a see-through slip dress from Miu Miu, while the pop icon posed shirtless and later in a gray hoodie from Skylrkclothing.

In separate snaps, Hailey flaunted her sheer fit, while cheekily covering up her bosom.

She styled her brunette locks straight and did a light neutral glam for the stripped-down shoot.

As for Justin’s solo snap, he went shirtless and pretended to be asleep with his arms goal posted up.

The “Peaches” crooner and Hailey also answered a series of questions for the outlet, sharing their favorite things of all time.

When asked about their favorite movie, the model named the 2000 Christmas movie “The Grinch” starring Jim Carey, while Justin shared his favorite was the 2006 comedy-romance “The Break-Up” starring Jennifer Aniston and Vince Vaughn.

When asked about their favorite hobby, Justin shared that his was golf, and Hailey admitted she didn’t “have a fun hobby.”

While their answers varied, the power couple agreed that their favorite way to relax was at a spa.

Hailey and Justin tied the knot in 2018 and haven’t shied away from sharing their loved-up snaps via Instagram throughout the years.

They also publicly gush over each other, with the hitmaker dedicating several songs on his 2025 “Swag” and “Swag II” albums to his wife and their son, Jack Blues.

In the album art, the duo also cozied up as Hailey held their 2-year-old son in a rare family photoshoot.
