Presley Gerber found in bedroom of rehab facility: report

2026-09-24 01:54

Presley Gerber was found in a bedroom inside the Santa Monica, California, rehab facility where he died at the age of 27 on Sept. 20, according to a report.

Per TMZ, a source close to the investigation revealed that Gerber’s body was discovered near the bed — but not on it — in the room Sunday night.

The source did not give an exact location, otherwise.

Sources also noted to the outlet that fentanyl has not yet come up in the death investigation.

The only son of supermodel Cindy Crawford and businessman Rande Gerber, he had been candid about his substance abuse and mental health struggles before he died, as well as had his share of public troubles regarding it, including a 2018 DUI which put him on probation for three years.

Last November, Gerber noted that he “had to step back and take care of my own mental health for a bit” while explaining a brief hiatus from social media. He said his intention was to come back “stronger, clearer, more intentional than ever,” and to try and help others suffering with mental health issues.

Gerber even strove to promote a “Mental Health Mondays” movement on his social media platform, in order “to spark real conversations about the stuff most people hide.”

However, source exclusively told Page Six Presley “seemed off” in the weeks leading up to his death, and that he “didn’t seem like himself.“

In addition to his parents, Gerber is survived by his younger sister, Kaia Gerber, who is said to be struggling with the loss of her brother, whom she was extraordinarily close to.

“Kaia and Presley were insanely close,” a source exclusively told us, adding, “I don’t know how she’s going to go on without him.”
