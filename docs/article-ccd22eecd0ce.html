James Cleverly is ready to run for London mayor. Labour is still waiting on Sadiq Khan.

2026-11-25 03:00

LONDON — The Conservative Party has already fired the starting pistol on the race to be London’s mayor. Labour hopefuls can’t get off the mark.

It is still 18 months before London elects a new mayor. But Labour MPs and party officials are fearful that being late to confirm a candidate could leave a “vacuum” for rival parties to dominate the airwaves.

James Cleverly, a former Tory foreign secretary and home secretary, was chosen as the Conservative candidate last Thursday, putting pressure on Labour to get its act together.
