Türkiye aims for 77% of installed capacity to be renewables in 2040

2026-11-05 08:38

Türkiye aims to increase the share of renewables in total installed capacity to 77% from the current 63% by 2040, Energy and Natural Resources Minister Alparslan Bayraktar said Thursday.

Sharing the new national energy road map for 2040, Bayraktar highlighted the rising energy demand and announced plans to boost electrification and the importance of energy efficiency.

He said the government aims for the Turkish economy to achieve 36% electrification by 2040.
