Erdoğan, Putin to meet in Bishkek with Ukraine and Black Sea on agenda

2026-08-29 12:24

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan and Russian President Vladimir Putin will meet on September 1 in the Kyrgyz capital of Bishkek to discuss the war in Ukraine and developments in the Black Sea.

The talks will take place on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) summit, Russian presidential aide Yury Ushakov announced on Friday.

“There are many issues to discuss, and of course, these include the situation in the Black Sea and the Ukraine issue in general,” Ushakov said.

Trade and economic ties between Turkey and Russia are also expected to feature in the talks.

The SCO summit is scheduled for August 31 and September 1 under Kyrgyzstan’s presidency of the Eurasian political, economic and security group.

Turkey has been an SCO dialogue partner since 2012 but is not a full member of the group.

A member of the NATO, Turkey has kept channels open with Moscow and Kyiv while backing Ukraine’s territorial integrity and declining to join Western sanctions against Russia.

Ankara hosted peace talks in 2022, while Turkey and the United Nations brokered a Black Sea grain agreement that allowed Ukrainian exports until Russia withdrew in 2023.

The Black Sea has returned to the center of the conflict as Russian and Ukrainian attacks on ports and vessels disrupt grain shipments.
