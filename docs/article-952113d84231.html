These $75 sneakers are converting walkers into runners — Asics Prime Day deals going fast

So, you hate running? Don’t worry, you’re not alone.

There may not be much research to prove it, but anecdotally speaking, I’d argue there are plenty of people (myself included) who want to get in cardio but don’t always have the motivation to push themselves to go for a run.

Well — what if your running shoes could give you that extra push? Enter ASICS. Content Strategist and Commerce Journalist Victoria McConnell says her ASICS running shoes are “inspiring her” to run for several reasons. And considering these kicks have dropped below $75 for October Prime Day, we’re also feeling inspired to run, not walk, and snag the sale before it ends tonight.

She owns the Gel-Excite 11 and says its bounce is incredible — and so is the comfort. According to the brand, the sneakers feature soft cushioning and shock-attenuating GEL technology for added support.

“I’m not a marathon runner by any means, but my new kicks are inspiring me,” McConnell shared.

McConnell says her ASICS sneakers have become her go-to everyday shoes. While she doesn’t consider herself a runner, she says the comfort of the shoes has motivated her to get outside more often.

“I love wearing them on walks in my neighborhood, and they’re one of the comfiest and most supportive sneakers I own,” she noted.

She also appreciates that they don’t feel overly bulky while still delivering an energized, springy feel: “I love how I have a pep in my step with them (the perfect bounce-back), and they’re not too clunky.”

And she’s not the only one taking notice. Other reviewers have mentioned their ASICS make running “so much more enjoyable,” plus the versatile design makes them equally suited for neighborhood walks and hopping between errands, too.

Your Prime Big Deal Days FAQs, answered

What is Prime Big Deal Days and when is it?

Prime Big Deal Days — fondly known as October Prime Day — is Amazon’s biggest fall sale, offering massive discounts before the holiday rush of Black Friday and Cyber Monday. October Prime Day runs from Tuesday, Oct. 6 to Wednesday, Oct. 7, 2026.

Prime Day vs. Prime Big Deal Days

Prime Day and Prime Big Deal Days are both Prime member-exclusive seasonal sales with massive discounts on rotating bestsellers. Unlike July’s Prime Day, Prime Big Deal Days runs every October as a precursor to the busy holiday season, allowing shoppers to start saving on gift shopping early.

Do you have to be an Amazon Prime member to shop?

October Prime Day is exclusively available to Prime members. However interested shoppers can grab a 30-day free trial to try Prime, and its many benefits with no strings attached, and gain access to exclusive deals and the convenience of fast, free shipping.
