Turkey’s social media ban for under-15s takes effect November 1

2026-10-10 11:29

Turkey on Saturday published regulations banning children under 15 from using social media, with the restrictions set to take effect on November 1.

The rules appeared in the Official Gazette, the government publication used to announce laws and regulations, following parliament’s adoption of a law in April.

Social media providers must “take the necessary technical and administrative measures, including age verification, to implement” protections for children.

“No service likely to produce an addictive effect in children may be offered,” the regulations stipulate.

Turkey joins a growing number of countries implementing restrictions aimed at protecting children and teenagers from risks associated with social media use.

Australia has introduced restrictions on children’s access to social media, while Britain and France are pursuing similar measures.

The measures aim to protect children from risks such as screen addiction, sleep loss, bullying and eating disorders.

The ban comes amid criticism from rights groups over restrictions on freedom of expression in Turkey targeting LGBTQ communities, journalists and the political opposition.

Numerous social media accounts have been blocked in recent weeks.
