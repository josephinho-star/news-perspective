The best Black Friday deals you can still get on AirPods, Macs, and other Apple gear

2026-11-27 23:53

Black Friday is entering the evening, which means one of the best times of the year to save some cash on a new pair of AirPods or the latest Apple Watch is coming to a close. After all, the holiday savings can help offset the unfortunate price hikes from earlier this year, when Apple raised the cost of some of its most popular gear. Some discounts still apply to recent releases like the Apple Watch Series 12 and touchscreen MacBook Pro, too, which are available at their lowest prices to date.

We dug up the biggest price drops on Apple hardware and accessories before black Friday officially ends.

In addition to a variety of discounts across retailers, Apple is offering up to $300 in gift cards with select purchases between November 27th and November 30th. How much you get back is determined by how much you spend, with up to $100 available for some iPhone and Apple Watch purchases, and up to $300 for the 16-inch MacBook Pro. That said, in some cases you may be able to save more by buying from other retailers.

Earbud and headphone deals

The latest AirPods Pro offer improved noise cancellation and richer bass, along with built-in heart sensors that work with Apple’s Fitness app to monitor calories burned across more than 50 workouts. Read our review.

Beats’ fitness-focused earbuds feature ear hooks designed to stay put during even the most intense workouts as well as built-in heart rate monitoring. They also deliver relatively bass-rich sound for their design, along with solid noise cancellation, wireless charging, and AirPods features like adaptive audio thanks to Apple’s H2 chip. Read our review.

- You can buy the AirPods 4 on sale at Walmart for around $89 ($40 off), which is $20 shy of their best price. Apple’s last-gen earbuds lack active noise cancellation, but they still deliver good, relatively bass-rich sound, even if they don’t quite match the newer AirPods 5. They also offer voice call quality on par with the latest AirPods Pro. Read our review.

- The second-gen AirPods Max are down to $419 ($130 off) at Amazon and Best Buy, which is $19 shy of their best price to date (Target also has them for about $10 more). Like the original, Apple’s latest over-ear headphones feature a luxurious build consisting of aluminum, steel, and a mesh fabric, but they offer better sound and noise cancellation. The new H2 chip also enables features like adaptive audio and live translation. Read our review.

The latest Beats Studio Pro feature improved noise cancellation, sound quality, and comfort. They also support lossless audio over USB-C and offer native support for a range of iOS and Android features like other Beats headphones. Read our review.

The Beats 360 are ANC headphones for people who prefer headphones over earbuds at the gym. They’re Beats’ first IP-rated cans, with ear cushions that you can easily remove for cleaning (or style points). They also stay in place during most exercises, while still delivering solid bass. Read our review.

- If you want over-ears and are comfortable going without active noise cancellation, the Beats Solo 4 are on sale starting at $79.99 ($120 off) at Amazon and Target — a new low price. The headphones are a noticeable improvement over their predecessor, delivering more detailed sound and support for lossless audio. They also last up to 50 hours on a single charge, while their memory foam ear cushions make them comfortable enough for long listening sessions. Read our review.

- The Beats Studio Buds Plus are down to around $89.95 ($80 off) at Amazon, Best Buy, and Target, nearly matching their lowest price to date. The wireless earbuds feature a slick, eye-catching transparent option and solid noise cancellation, especially at this price. They’re also exceptionally comfortable, with four ear tip sizes to help you find a secure fit. Sure, you don’t get wireless charging, but they offer IPX4 water resistance and up to nine hours of battery life on a single charge. Read our review.

Apple’s most affordable laptop uses the same A18 Pro chip found in the iPhone 16 Pro. It features a vibrant 13-inch display, surprisingly good speakers, and 8GB of RAM, making it a solid option for students and anyone who needs a laptop for everyday tasks. Read our review.

Apple’s latest MacBook Air features an M5 chip and starts with 512GB of storage, with significantly faster performance than prior generations. It’s still an excellent machine despite the higher base price, especially given the battery life and speakers. Read our review.

- The recently released 16.3-inch MacBook Pro with a PROLED XDR display, 24GB of RAM, and 1TB of storage is on sale starting at $3,099 ($300 off) — a new low price — at Amazon. The redesigned laptop is Apple’s first with an OLED touchscreen, which allows for rich colors and deep blacks. It’s also noticeably thinner and lighter than the non-touchscreen 16-inch MacBook Pro, and features a new Dynamic Island that replaces the notch and provides quick access to controls and notifications. Read our review.

- The 14-inch M6-powered MacBook Pro is on sale starting at $1,749 ($250 off) at Amazon and Best Buy, which is a new low price. The Pro builds on its predecessor with a faster chip and support for Wi-Fi 7. In our testing, it handled multitasking, photo editing, and occasional video editing with ease while still delivering exceptional battery life. It lacks the newer MacBook Pro’s gorgeous OLED display, but its Mini LED screen still looks great, especially given its smooth 120Hz refresh rate. It also offers excellent speakers and a good selection of ports, including three Thunderbolt 4 ports, HDMI, and an SD card slot. Read our review.

The Apple Watch Series 12 introduces AI-powered Audio Intelligence features, including sound recognition alerts, Live Rewind, and Siri Recap. It can also track your heart rate every five seconds, even when you’re not exercising, and offers faster charging than prior models. Read our review.

- The Apple Watch SE 3 is on sale in the 40mm / GPS configuration starting at $179 ($50 off) at Amazon and Walmart, matching its best price to date, while Best Buy is currently $20 higher. Apple’s newest entry-level smartwatch remains our top recommendation for anyone who wants a basic fitness tracker, as it offers all the essentials alongside an always-on display and Apple’s S10 chip, which enables on-device Siri requests. Read our review.

The Baseus PicoGo AM52 is a 10,000mAh magnetic power bank that supports fast 25W Qi2.2 wireless charging and up to 45W wired charging via its built-in USB-C cable. It was one of the fastest wireless chargers we tested, bringing an iPhone 17 Pro Max to 65 percent in an hour. Read our guide to the best Qi2 batteries.

Apple’s 11th-gen iPad offers 128GB of storage — twice that of its predecessor — and runs on a faster A16 chip. It also comes in four colors: blue, pink, yellow, and silver.

- You can buy the last-gen iPad Mini with 128GB of storage and Wi-Fi on Amazon in select colors for $484 ($115 off), which is the best price we’ve seen since Apple’s recent price hikes. It has a smaller screen and a slower chip than the newer model, and it lacks an OLED screen, the Acoustic Display, and water resistance. Still, it’s a great option for reading and everyday use that’s currently more affordable than its $599 successor, with support for Apple Intelligence and the Apple Pencil Pro. Read our review.

- Apple’s latest Magic Mouse is matching its all-time low of $59.99 ($20 off) at Amazon. The wireless mouse features a touch-sensitive surface that lets you scroll through documents and swipe between apps with simple gestures; it also pairs automatically with compatible Macs and offers support for USB-C.

Apple’s entry-level stylus attaches magnetically to supported iPads, though it doesn’t support magnetic charging. Instead, you pair and charge it by connecting it to your device’s USB-C port. It includes tilt sensitivity and Apple Pencil Hover support, but it doesn’t offer double-tap gestures or pressure sensitivity.

The Icemag 3 can wirelessly deliver up to 25W of power to compatible smartphones and up to 35W wired charging via USB-C. The 10,000mAh Qi2 charger can also act as a foldable kickstand, comes with an integrated USB-C cable that serves as a lanyard, and sports a transparent design with colorful RGB lighting.

Designed to pair with a Mac, the latest Apple Studio Display features a sharp 5K Retina display, a great 12-megapixel Center Stage camera that keeps you in frame during video calls, and a good six-speaker sound system with spatial audio. Sadly, it only offers two Thunderbolt 5 ports in addition to a pair of USB-C ports. Read our review.
