‘Seinfeld’ star Steven Hack dead at 68 after ‘unexpected’ medical emergency

2026-11-24 19:33

Hack died after an “unexpected cardiac event,” according to the InterAct Theater Company, which he co-founded.

He was 68 years old.

‘It is with great sadness that we share the news that Steven Hack, a founding member of Interact Theatre Company, recently passed away at home following a sudden and unexpected cardiac event, ” the message reads.

‘Steven was a gifted and versatile performer, a superb graphic artist, and a cherished member of our artistic family,” the message continues. “His creativity and talent helped shape Interact in its earliest years, and his artistry, humor, warmth, and generous spirit enriched everything he touched.”

Hack died of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease at his home, Page Six can confirm.

Atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) is caused by plaque buildup in arterial walls, according to the American Heart Association.

Hack is best known for his extensive work in television. During Season 7 of “Seinfeld,” Hack memorably played a participant in an AIDS walkathon who confronts the character Kramer for refusing to wear a ribbon.

He also appeared on “Desperate Housewives,” “American Horror Story,” and “Agent Carter.”

Hack’s movie roles include appearances in Marvel’s “Fantastic Four” in 2015 as well as “Thor: The Dark World” in 2013.
