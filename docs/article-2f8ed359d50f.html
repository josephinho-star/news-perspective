More NYC mole people spotted as 5 emerge from sewer manhole in latest sighting: cops

2026-11-25 02:51

Five people were seen crawling out of a Manhattan sewer system last Wednesday morning in the latest sighting of the city’s mysterious mole people, cops said.

The crew of subterranean interlopers were seen exiting a manhole near Second Avenue and East 17th Street around 5 a.m., according to the NYPD.

Photos released by cops show the urban explorers donning headlamps, shovels and work gloves after emerging from the city’s depths.

It appeared one women and four men were seen climbing up from the sewers.

The crew got into a red sedan with a female getaway driver, before disappearing into the wind, police said.

There are no arrests, and an investigation is still ongoing.

Their expedition found them underneath a hotel where Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu had been staying during the UN General Assembly, cops and sources said.

Venezuelan citizen Samuel Reveron, one of the pair charged for the alleged incursion, told authorities he had gone into the sewers to “look for valuables that get stuck in the storm drains.”

Experts say that it’s highly unlikely that valuables like gold and silver end up underground.
