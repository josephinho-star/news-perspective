Homeowners, this eufy security upgrade is 20% off ahead of Prime Big Deal Days

2026-09-21 14:38

Owning your first home is exciting — but it comes with its significant list of challenges.

One of the biggest is security, which means constantly dealing with nosy neighbors, porch pirates or uninvited visitors who should probably be nowhere near your lawn. A good home security camera can help you keep tabs on your backyard, porch and more, and Eufy’s Security SoloCam S340 is one of the best.

Plus, it’s currently on sale for 20% off, bringing its price down to $160 for a limited time as part of the early Prime deals ahead of Prime Big Deal Days.

The camera captures footage in up to 3K resolution and offers 8x hybrid zoom, so you can keep track of everything from people approaching your home to tiny animals burrowing in your backyard.

The brand also says installation takes just seven minutes, so you’ll have this camera set up and ready to go in no time. It offers 360-degree coverage with pan-and-tilt functionality, giving you a bird’s-eye view of your outdoor space.

Plus, unlike some other security camera systems, you don’t need to pay a monthly subscription. There’s built-in local storage, which means you can save footage directly on the device.

Other features include two-way audio, so you can speak to visitors — or scare off an unwanted one — using your voice. It’s also built to withstand outdoor weather, from rain and snow to bright sunshine.

Since there’s a solar panel included, there’s minimal maintenance, too. Eufy says two hours of direct sunlight per day is enough to keep the camera continuously powered under typical conditions.

If you’re looking for an easy-to-use security camera that requires little looking after, offers local storage without a required monthly fee and can keep tabs on your outdoor space without missing a beat, the eufy Security SoloCam S340 is worth your money — especially now while it’s 20% off.

Your Prime Big Deal Days FAQs, answered

What is Prime Big Deal Days and when is it?

Prime Big Deal Days — fondly known as October Prime Day — is Amazon’s biggest fall sale, offering massive discounts before the holiday rush of Black Friday and Cyber Monday. October Prime Day runs from Tuesday, Oct. 6 to Wednesday, Oct. 7, 2026.

Prime Day vs. Prime Big Deal Days

Prime Day and Prime Big Deal Days are both Prime member-exclusive seasonal sales with massive discounts on rotating bestsellers. Unlike July’s Prime Day, Prime Big Deal Days runs every October as a precursor to the busy holiday season, allowing shoppers to start saving on gift shopping early.

Do you have to be an Amazon Prime member to shop?

October Prime Day is exclusively available to Prime members. However interested shoppers can grab a 30-day free trial to try Prime, and its many benefits with no strings attached, and gain access to exclusive deals and the convenience of fast, free shipping.

What are the best deals?

From reporting on Amazon sales for years, we can tell you for certain that Amazon’s October Prime Day is the ideal time to save on big-ticket tech, Amazon devices, gift-worthy clothing and beauty and skincare staples, household essentials and many more Amazon best-sellers.

Is it worth it?

Every Prime Day sale is a great chance to score deals on thousands of best-sellers, top tech and appliances and every day essentials, but Amazon’s October event also has great pre-holiday discounts on gifts.
