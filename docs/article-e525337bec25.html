How prediction market promos work

Prediction market promos are welcome offers that prediction market platforms award to eligible new users. These promos usually come in the form of bonus money or trading credits and are awarded after completing registration, making a qualifying deposit or completing a required amount of trading.

While promotional funds themselves may not be withdrawable, eligible proceeds earned from trades using those credits may be withdrawable according to the platform’s terms.

Before signing up, however, always check the full terms. Even simple-looking prediction market promo codes can have minimum deposits, qualifying-trade requirements, expiration dates, geographic restrictions and other eligibility rules.

What types of prediction market promos exist?

There are several common types of prediction market welcome offers, and understanding the differences can help you compare their actual value.

Deposit bonuses have a simple promotional structure: Make a qualifying deposit and receive bonus trading credits.

For example, the Novig promo requires a $10 deposit to qualify for $25 in trade credits, while the featured Polymarket offer requires a $10 deposit to qualify for a $50 trading bonus.

Always determine whether the promotion is a fixed bonus or a true percentage-based deposit match before funding your account.

A trade-and-get offer requires users to complete a specified amount of trading before receiving the bonus.

Kalshi’s featured offer requires $25 in trades to receive $25, while ProphetX requires $50 in trades to qualify for its $75 offer.

These offers may require more activity than a simple deposit bonus, but the reward can also be larger.

Laddered prediction market promos increase in value as users complete specified milestones.

Instead of receiving the entire bonus after one action, you may unlock additional rewards as you deposit or trade more. Check every milestone before participating so you know how much activity is required to earn the advertised maximum.

Some platforms use prediction market referral codes or referral links to reward users for inviting eligible new customers.

The structure varies by operator. A referral program may reward the existing user, the new user or both after certain registration, deposit or trading requirements are completed.

Referral offers should not automatically be assumed to stack with a prediction market welcome bonus, so check the terms before choosing which offer to use.

Contests and limited-time promotions

Prediction markets may also run contests or limited-time promotions around major events such as the Super Bowl, World Series or elections.

Unlike evergreen prediction market bonus codes, these promotions can disappear quickly and may be available to existing customers as well as new users.

When should I apply a prediction market promo?

Once you’ve created your account and made your first deposit, you should use your prediction market promo immediately.

Pay particular attention to the order of operations. If an offer requires you to opt in before depositing, for example, making the deposit first could prevent you from qualifying.

You should also check whether the promotion has an expiration period. If bonus credits must be used within a certain number of days, don’t claim an offer until you’re prepared to complete its requirements and use the reward.

How prediction market promos differ from sportsbook bonuses

When you use a prediction market promo, you’re trading event contracts on an exchange rather than placing a wager against a sportsbook at odds set by the house. That distinction changes how they the best prediction market promos are used.

- Promo size: Traditional sportsbooks frequently advertise larger welcome packages, including hundreds of dollars in bonus bets or deposit-based promotions. Prediction market promos are generally smaller and often revolve around trading credits.

- Types of promos: Sportsbooks offer a broader range of ongoing promotions, including profit boosts, parlay promotions, bonus bets and team- or player-specific offers. Prediction market promotions are more commonly based on deposits, trading activity, credits, referrals or other exchange-related incentives.

- How pricing works: Sportsbook promotions can include boosted odds or special promotional pricing because the sportsbook sets the odds. On a peer-to-peer prediction market, contract prices are driven by trading activity, so the platform generally can’t offer an odds boost in exactly the same way.

- Trading mechanics: Prediction markets allow users to buy and sell contracts, potentially creating opportunities to exit positions before settlement. Sportsbook wagers typically remain open until the bet settles unless the sportsbook offers a cash-out option.

- Promo maturity: Sportsbook promotions have existed for years in mature legal betting markets. Prediction market bonuses are still evolving, with new-user credits, rebates, referrals and trading incentives currently more common than the steady stream of recurring promotions offered by sportsbooks.

Prediction market promo requirements to check before signing up

Checking these requirements before depositing is more useful than simply comparing the headline values of prediction market bonuses.

- Minimum deposit: How much money must you deposit to activate the offer?

- Qualifying trade: Does the promotion require a specific amount of trading before the bonus is awarded?

- Promo code: Do you need to enter one of the listed prediction market promo codes, or does the offer activate automatically?

- Eligible markets: Do all event contracts qualify toward the promotion?

- Settlement requirement: Does the qualifying contract have to settle before you receive the bonus?

- Bonus expiration: How long do you have to use your trading credits?

- Withdrawal rules: Can the bonus be withdrawn, or only eligible proceeds generated from it?

- Location: Is the promotion available where you’re physically located?

- Age requirement: Minimum ages vary by platform and jurisdiction.

- New-user requirement: Most prediction market welcome offers are limited to customers who have never previously registered with the operator.

Prediction market promo FAQs

Are prediction market promos legal?

The best prediction market promos are welcome offers from platforms operating through federally regulated event-contract businesses.

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission oversees U.S. derivatives markets, including designated contract markets and other registered entities involved in event-contract trading.

Prediction markets can therefore operate under a different regulatory framework from state-regulated sportsbooks.

How can I tell if a prediction market promo is legitimate?

Start by verifying the operator rather than focusing solely on the bonus.

Legitimate prediction market promos may include deposit bonuses, trade-and-get offers, referral rewards and other incentives associated with regulated event contracts.

You should verify the operator’s regulatory status, read the promotion’s full terms and confirm that you’re using the official platform before depositing money or providing personal information.

Do prediction market apps offer sign-up bonuses?

Yes. Many prediction market platforms offer some form of prediction market welcome offer for eligible new users.

Depending on the operator, you may receive trading credits after making a qualifying deposit, completing your first trade or reaching a specified amount of trading activity.

Can I withdraw prediction market bonus credits?

Promotional trading credits themselves are not usually directly withdrawable. Eligible proceeds generated from successful trades using those credits may be withdrawable according to the promotion’s terms.

Do I need a prediction market promo code?

It depends on the operator.

Polymarket, Kalshi, Novig and ProphetX currently have prediction market promo codes attached to the featured offers on this page, while FanDuel Predicts does not require a code for its listed sign-up offer.

Can I claim more than one prediction market promo?

You can claim welcome promotions from multiple prediction market operators if you’re an eligible new user at each platform.

But you cannot claim multiple new-user offers from the same operator, and individual prediction market referral codes and welcome offers aren’t stackable.

Are prediction market promos available in every state?

No. Availability varies by operator, and some prediction markets exclude certain states or jurisdictions.

Your physical location can also affect which markets you’re allowed to trade. Check the operator’s current availability and promotional terms before creating an account.
