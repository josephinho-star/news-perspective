How Grimes bonds with new boyfriend over AI safety, ’60s girl groups and anime in quirky new romance

2026-11-27 15:35

Grimes’ unconventional new romance with Elijah Weinkauf is filled with late-night conversations about artificial intelligence, music history and anime.

“They drive through LA on long drives in his beat-up SUV, having long discussions about ’60s girl groups, Katseye and the Beach Boys,” a source exclusively told Page Six.

According to the insider, the “Oblivion” singer and Weinkauf — who produces music under the name VADAKIN — “stay up late discussing music history, AI safety, why so much tech is bad and needs regulating, anime and podcasts.”

Grimes and her new boyfriend have been dating privately, but recently made their romance public when they walked the red carpet hand-in-hand at the “Blade Runner 2099” premiere on Nov. 12.

We’re told the pair fell for each other while working on Grimes’ upcoming album, “PSYOPERA,” which Weinkauf co-produced — including her next single, set to drop in the coming weeks.

The two also “recently DJed together during her viral performance at the Brooklyn gay bar Singers,” our source said, adding that some people backstage began questioning whether they were romantically involved.

Although we’re told Weinkauf is incredibly shy and the couple initially kept things low-key, those around them couldn’t be happier about their blossoming romance.

Unlike Grimes’ SpaceX founder ex Elon Musk, we’re told Weinkauf isn’t interested in politics and is instead focused on his art.

The music producer has also played a significant role in helping the “Genesis” singer rediscover her passion for music following several tumultuous years in the public eye.

We’re told Grimes had become somewhat withdrawn following the drama surrounding her personal life, but the “super supportive” producer has helped bring her out of her shell creatively.

While those in her inner circle admire his talent and good looks, they’re particularly happy to see the singer with someone who treats her with kindness and respect.

Reps for Grimes and VADAKIN did not respond to Page Six’s requests for comment.

They later rekindled their romance before splitting again in March 2022 — the same day news broke that they had welcomed their second child together.

In her March 2022 Vanity Fair cover story, Grimes said a surrogate gave birth to their daughter, Exa Dark Sideræl, in December after she and Musk had gotten back together in some capacity.

“I would probably refer to him as my boyfriend, but we’re very fluid. We live in separate houses. We’re best friends. We see each other all the time. … We just have our own thing going on, and I don’t expect other people to understand it,” she explained to the publication.

Grimes and Musk secretly welcomed their third child, Techno Mechanicus, in June 2022.

The former couple are already parents to son X Æ A-XII, 6. Musk is also a father to twins Griffin and Vivian, 22, and triplets Damian, Saxon and Kai, 20, from a previous marriage to author Justine Wilson.
