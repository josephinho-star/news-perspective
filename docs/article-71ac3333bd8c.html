When Does The Next Season of ‘Mayor of Kingstown’ Come Out? ‘Mayor of Kingstown’ Netflix/Paramount+ Info

2026-10-11 16:00

It’s the perfect time to catch up on Mayor of Kingstown.

Created by Taylor Sheridan and Hugh Dillon, the Jeremy Renner-led series originally debuted in November 2021 on Paramount+. If you’re looking to binge all four seasons, Amazon is currently offering a free trial of Paramount+.

No Paramount+? No problem! Seasons 1-4 of Mayor of Kingstown were recently added to Netflix! It’s now easier than ever to watch before the fifth and final season drops on Paramount+.

What exactly can we expect from the show’s final installment? Per Paramount, the fifth season centers on new series regular Russell Hardy (David Morse), an FBI agent who arrives in Kingstown following the murder of REDACTED (we’ll keep this spoiler-free). “A dedicated lawman, Agent Hardy threatens to disrupt the tenuous balance of power in Kingstown.”

When do we expect Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 to premiere on Paramount+? Here’s everything we know.

How Many Seasons Are There of Mayor of Kingstown?

There are currently four seasons of Mayor of Kingstown.

Will There Be a Season 5 of Mayor of Kingstown?

Yes! In January 2026, Mayor of Kingstown was renewed for a fifth and final season. The show’s final season will consist of eight episodes.

When Does Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 Start on Paramount+?

The official Season 5 release date for Mayor of Kingstown has yet to be announced, but production on the final season ended in early July. For context, production on the fourth season concluded in June 2025, with the season debuting on October 26, 2025.

Where exactly does Mayor of Kingstown fit on Paramount+’s 2026/2027 schedule? MobLand is back; Tulsa King and Dexter: Resurrection will return later this month; and the final season of Yellowjackets premieres in November. The third season of Landman just started production, so that won’t be back until the middle of 2027 at the earliest.

Based on the above information, our best guess is that Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 will debut in December 2026 or early 2027 on Paramount+.

Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 Cast Info:

The final season of Mayor of Kingstown will feature a few new faces. David Morse has joined the cast as seasoned FBI agent Russell Hardy, and Nestor Carbonell will portray Enrique Molina, a “boss who comes to Kingstown to avenge a relative who has gone missing.”

Is Mayor of Kingstown on Netflix?

Yes! As we mentioned above, the first four seasons of Mayor of Kingstown are now streaming on Netflix.

Mayor of Kingstown on Paramount+ Streaming Options:

Paramount+ offers two subscription plans, Essential and Premium. Right now, you can get started with a seven-day free trial on the Premium plan.

The ad-supported Essential plan typically costs $8.99/month, while the ad-free Premium plan (which comes with Showtime titles and live CBS) costs $13.99/month.

You can also add Paramount+ to your Hulu or Amazon accounts for $13.99/month. Both streamers offer free trials for new subscribers.

Is Paramount+ Available on YouTube?

Yes! You can currently purchase Paramount+ on YouTube for $10.99/month for the first three months. YouTube also offers a free trial.
