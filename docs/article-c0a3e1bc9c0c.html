Dad deserves better than another tie — shop our 32 best Christmas gift ideas instead

2026-09-29 21:03

Dads can be impossible to shop for. Many insist they “don’t want anything,” or even worse, just buy themselves whatever they want or need.

And yet, holiday after holiday, the pressure lands squarely on your shoulders to find something useful, unique, funny, or sentimental for your own father figure in the hopes that it’ll make him smile.

This year, we did the digging for you. Whether you’re hunting for cool tech gadgets for Dad or a new cooking essential for all his cooking needs, there’s a gift in here for him.

We made sure to include a wide range of budgets too, so whether you’re looking to spend $50 or $100, there’s an option here for you.

Best gift for the bookish dad: Kindle Paperwhite

If Dad loves curling up with a good book, the Kindle Paperwhite is a must-have. The lightweight e-reader packs weeks of battery life and fits into backpacks, work bags and more. Dad can read while he’s on a plane or on the train during his morning commute.

Best tech gift for dad: Anker Laptop Power Bank

If your father is always complaining about how his multiple devices keep running out of battery — I know mine always is — shop the Anker Laptop Power Bank, which can be used to boost his laptop, smartphone, headphones or any other device that needs a little juice.

Best gift for the whiskey fanatic: Glenlevit Provence + Glasses

I grew up watching my dad pour himself a glass of The Glenlivet as a nightcap. The latest Provence release features a brighter, fruitier flavor and is finished in wine barrels. Pair the bottle with this set of whiskey glasses, which also comes with round ice molds.

Best Gift for Dads who love sleeping in: Pluto Pillow

Pluto Pillows are completely customizable, so you can buy a pillow that’s built specifically for your dad. Just make sure to sneakily ask him about his biggest sleep concerns before you place your order so the pillow is built to his needs.

Best gift for dads with cars: DS & Durga Concrete After Lightning

Good car air fresheners are hard to find, and in my opinion, D.S. & Durga makes some of the best and most unique. This Concrete After Lightning scent smells exactly like what it sounds like: deep notes of earthy grass and summer rain — minus the humidity.

Best gift for the fit dad: Theragun Mini

If Dad suffers from constant aches and pains, a Theragun Mini can help. It’s portable and has an ergonomic handle, which makes it easy to glide over calves, shoulders and more.

Best gift for chocoholic dads: Harry & David Signature Chocolate Truffles

If Dad is always buying chocolate after dinner, this signature set comes with 24 truffles, including dark, white and milk chocolate options for him to indulge in. You can also add extras, like a bottle of wine or a personalized gift card.

Best gift for dads who love music: Bose SoundLink Flex 2

This was ranked as one of our best Bluetooth speakers. The speaker delivers big sound in a portable, compact package and comes with a water-resistant rating. If Dad loves to jam out to his favorite tunes, this speaker is the gift he needs.

Best gift for dads who travel: Paravel Aviator Luggage

Paravel makes some of my favorite high-quality, durable luggage. This Aviator option is the perfect carry-on for that weekend business trip Dad needs to take. It has a hardshell build for durability and 360-degree spinner wheels for when Dad’s running through the airport to his gate.

Best gift for the dads who hate city noise: Apple AirPods Pro 3

Easily one of the best earbuds for travel, workouts or just Dad’s everyday commute, these buds also come with multiple ear tips and have Live Translation, so Dad can converse with his foreign clients without ever missing a beat.

Best gift for the dad who loves to cook: Always Pan 2.0

Whenever my dad visits, he grabs my Always Pan to make me eggs in the morning. While I only have the first edition, the second is even better, now oven-safe up to 450 degrees Fahrenheit. It still serves multiple cooking functions, handling everything from frying to braising with ease.

Best gift for the dad who’s always cold: Vince Classic Cashmere-Lined Leather Glove

These cashmere-lined gloves will keep Dad’s hands warm even during the coldest days. They’re available in three colors and four sizes, so make sure to pick one you know Dad will like.

Best gift for the coffee-craving dad: Keurig K Mini Mate

This compact coffee machine works well for Dad’s office or living room. It’s available in four colors, including a bright green shade. Dad can brew a 12-ounce cup of coffee in the mornings, and it’s even big enough to fit travel mugs.

Best gift for the dad who only drinks tea: Adagio tea starter set

Adagio has amazing loose-leaf tea samples, with options starting at just $1. Whether Dad prefers green tea or berry tea, there’s a loose-leaf option that he’ll like. Each box can brew up to 5 to 10 cups, and you can even buy a couple of samplers to send Dad a larger gift set.

Best gift for the sporty dad: Kuhl Burr Vest

This durable vest is made from 100% cotton weave, so Dad will feel comfortable all day long. There are zippered pockets in case you need to keep anything valuable, and you can shop it in a new Desert Khaki colorway, too.

Best gift for the classy dad: Timex Marlin Quartz GMT

For the watch collector, this limited-edition piece has a beautiful dial with an almost vintage-y sophisticated feel to it. The 40 mm case and polished top ring add to the understated silhouette. The brown leather strap is my favorite part and gives this watch an elevated feel — trust me, it looks far more expensive than the $200 it’s selling for.

Best gift for the Footballer dad: Hisense 50″ Class E6SR Series

Yes, it might seem odd to deliver a TV to your dad on Christmas, but hear me out: this widescreen TV is under $350 and is top-rated on Amazon. I’ve owned Hisense TVs before, and I love the contrast and true-to-life color on them — plus, imagine how many movie nights you and Dad can enjoy this holiday season.

Best gift for the cluttered dad: Teslyar Wood Phone Docking Station

If Dad’s desk is always cluttered, a phone docking station can help. This wooden option can hold his smartwatch, smartphone, glasses, AirPods and then some. It’s small and compact, too, and can make your dad’s desk look far better than before.

Best useful gift for dad: Shark Wandvac

My dad always drops crumbs when he eats, and a hand vac helps mop those crumbs up in a jiffy. The Shark hand vac is one of my favorites because its lightweight design and no-touch waste disposal make this a top pick in my dad’s cleaning arsenal.

Best cool gift for dads: Olfactory At-Home Custom Scent Experience

You don’t need to go to a physical studio to make your own fragrance. In fact, Olfactory lets you create one right at home. The set comes with everything he needs to make his custom scent, along with a 1:1 virtual consultation with one of Olfactory’s team members. Once he has his scent figured out, he can place his order online and have it sent straight to his door.

Best gift for dads who take skincare seriously: medicube Kojic Acid Renew & Smooth Duo

Dads care about skincare, too. This set comes with two products for skin brightening. My boyfriend loves Medicube products, and even my dad is a fan of the toner pads, which is why I know that both the turmeric pads and serum can help dads with sensitive skin feel refreshed in the mornings.

Best gifts for the practical dad: Brita Small 6 Cup Water Filter Pitcher

My dad always complains about drinking tap water when he visits. This option filters out six cups of water at a time and reduces chlorine, copper, mercury and cadmium in tap water. It also improves the taste of tap water, in my opinion.

Best golf gift for dad: Titleist Customized Golf Balls

This set of golf balls can be customized with a message for your dad or his initials. If Dad’s a golfer, this will be a gift he’ll remember every time he heads out for a tee time.

Best gift for the griller dad: Weber Precision Grilling Tool Set, 3‑Piece

This three-piece grilling set comes with tongs, a spatula and a silicone brush. Each has an ergonomic grip and a design built for long-lasting durability. The tools are also dishwasher-safe for easy cleaning.

Best gift for the gamer dad: SteelSeries Aerox 3 Wireless Gen 2 Mouse

If Dad spends his after-hours gaming on the PC, this SteelSeries mouse can keep up with his games without missing a beat. It’s water-resistant, comes with up to 200 hours of battery life and is ultra-light, making it a joy to use day in and day out.

For the health-conscious dad: Apple Watch Series 12

The latest Apple Watch packs even more health features than before, and now offers daily vitals screening and HRV reports every five minutes. If your dad cares about his health and wants to be more proactive about it, the Series 12 is a great companion for Apple users.

For the dad with a 5 o’clock shadow: Harry’s Plus Shave Set

This set comes with a razor, foaming shave gel and three German-engineered five-blade cartridges. There’s even a travel cover to protect each blade, and the whole set is also currently on sale.

For the dad who struggles to wake up: Hatch Restore 3

This alarm clock isn’t just for babies or kids — it’s for anyone who has trouble sleeping or waking up in the morning. It can help Dad maintain a healthier circadian rhythm, sleep more deeply and wake up less crabby in the mornings. It basically simulates a natural sunrise, even during the winter.

For the builder dad: LEGO Marvel Iron Man Mark 3 Collectors’ Edition

If Dad loves building or is a big fan of Marvel — the comics or the movies — the Iron Man LEGO set is worth buying. It takes about six to seven hours to complete, and you can do it even quicker if you accompany Dad during the build. Once he’s done, he can also display his new figurine on his home desk.

For the cigar aficionado: Tommy Bahama Cigar Accessories & Folding Ashtray Set

This chic ashtray set is perfect for the dad who likes to indulge in a smoke from time to time. It’s foldable for when it’s not in use and also comes with a punch cutter to keep his cigar ready for use.

For the dad who loves board games: Catan Board Game (6th Edition)

I love playing Catan with my family. It’s easy to grasp and can even be modified if you want to play with just two people. It’s a strategy-based game, takes about an hour to 1.5 hours to complete and it’s so fun, you can truly play it over and over again without getting bored.

For the note-taker dad: 2027 Moleskine Planner

Dad can keep track of all his appointments in the new year with this planner. It’s available in five colors and comes in both soft and hardcover options. The whole planner is dated, making it easy for Dad to keep track of everything he has going on.
