Man, 61, arrested for allegedly spiking drink of Japanese DJ at Hong Kong nightclub

2026-11-03 02:53

Police detain Hongkonger for alleged drugging attempt at Tsim Sha Tsui venue, which incapacitated Mao Hamasaki and left her in intensive care

A 61-year-old Hong Kong man has been arrested for allegedly spiking the drink of Japanese disc jockey Mao Hamasaki at a Tsim Sha Tsui nightclub over the weekend, leaving her in intensive care.

The police on Tuesday said that after investigations by the Yau Tsim crime squad, officers arrested the suspect in Central on Monday in relation to an alleged drugging attempt at PP Club on Mody Road the previous day.

The man has been detained and is being investigated for allegedly administering poison with intent to injure.
