Costco’s chicken salad is packed with tons of protein, but that comes at a cost

2026-09-23 02:19

One of Costco’s newer food court additions is packing more protein than anything else on the menu, but a dietitian says shoppers should look beyond the eye-catching number.

The Rotisserie Chicken Chef Salad, which Costco highlighted among its new food court offerings this year, contains 59 grams of protein along with 840 calories.

The $7.99 salad includes rotisserie chicken, hard-boiled eggs, bacon bits and shredded cheddar cheese on a bed of romaine lettuce, along with tomatoes and creamy peppercorn ranch dressing.

Jamie Lee McIntyre, a Florida-based registered dietitian nutritionist, told Fox News Digital that protein is only one factor consumers should consider when deciding what to eat.

“That’s just a nutrition attribute,” McIntyre said of protein.

She noted that “a food can be high in protein, but it can also still be high in sodium, saturated fat or calories.”

Regarding this particular Costco menu item, McIntyre said the protein “is coming from some good whole foods, chicken and eggs. There’s veggies in there. So those are some good building blocks of the meal. But I would say, for a lot of people, 840 calories might be a bit much for a light lunch.”

Online reactions to the Rotisserie Chicken Chef Salad have been mixed, with some Costco shoppers criticizing its proportions and others praising its flavor.

“Tons of toppings, not enough lettuce. Salty for a salad. I wouldn’t buy it again, the portions are just way off, and it was a whole thing to assemble,” one Reddit user said.

Another Reddit user was a fan of the salad, particularly the dressing.

“Someone bought me one, and I went back 2 days later to buy another,” someone wrote.
