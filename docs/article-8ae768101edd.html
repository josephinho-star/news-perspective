Election Day 2026: Photos from around the country

2026-11-04 00:00

Election Day 2026: Photos from around the country : The Picture ShowVoters across the country are heading to the polls today to cast their ballots in the 2026 midterm elections.

Against a backdrop of deep political division and with the control of Congress at stake, voters across the country are heading to the polls today to cast their ballots for the 2026 midterm elections. From lines at polling stations to the anticipation and reactions as the results come in, NPR station photographers are documenting the election in their communities.

Voters fill out their ballots at Chicago's McKinley Park Library.

"And damn it, they shouldn't have died."
