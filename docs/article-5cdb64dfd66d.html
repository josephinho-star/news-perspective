Camille Grammer blasts ex Kelsey Grammer for forgetting their daughter’s birthday

2026-10-27 02:36

Camille Grammer called out her ex-husband, Kelsey Grammer, for allegedly not reaching out to their daughter Mason on her 25th birthday.

The “Real Housewives of Beverly Hills” alum, 58, accused the “Frasier” star of forgetting Mason’s birthday — which was on Oct. 24 — in an Instagram post Monday.

“A daughter should never have to wonder whether her father remembers her birthday. Three days later, and still not a call or a text. 💔,” Camille wrote alongside a throwback photo of the actor holding Mason as a baby.

Camille included a clown emoji over her ex’s face in the post.

“You can be a beloved actor to the world, but being a father is a role that requires showing up when the cameras are off,” she continued. “Fame, accolades, and public admiration mean very little if you neglect the people who should matter most.”

Camille explained that she was hurt on behalf of her daughter, writing, “25 is a milestone birthday. Sometimes the silence is deafening, and the disappointment speaks volumes.”

Her post included more childhood snaps of Mason, including one of her smiling on the beach.

In the comments of her own post, Camille took aim at Kelsey’s current wife, Kayte Walsh.

“A stepmother should want to encourage a healthy, loving relationship between a father and his daughter. Sadly, from my perspective, that hasn’t been Mason’s experience. Instead, she has felt increasingly excluded from her father’s life and home.”

Camille continued: “No matter the adult relationships involved, a daughter deserves to feel welcomed, respected, and loved—not pushed to the sidelines. A 25th birthday should be a celebration of love, not another reminder of the distance between a father and his child.”

Page Six reached out to Kelsey’s rep, but didn’t receive an immediate response back.

Camille shares two kids with Kelsey — Mason and their son Jude, 22.

Back in 2018, Mason made rare comments about her relationship with her famous father, noting that her parents’ divorce was the “hardest time” of her life.

“I changed a lot after that,” she reflected in an interview with Genlux magazine. “I had to grow up faster because I had a lot more responsibilities. My dad wasn’t around, so I didn’t see him much, and my mom had such a hard time. I became my younger brother’s mom.”

She added, “My parents still don’t talk. At all. Ever.”

Kelsey, 71, has eight children total. His eldest child is daughter Spencer, 43, whom he shares with his first wife, Doreen Alderman, followed by daughter Greer, 34, whom he shares with ex Barrie Buckner.

After his divorce from Camille, he married Walsh, 47, with whom he shares four children — daughter Faith, 14, as well as sons Gabriel, 12, Auden, 9, and Christopher, 1.

Camille and Kelsey were married from 1997 to 2011. He previously admitted that he started seeing Walsh while he was still married to Camille, sharing during his 2012 appearance on “Jimmy Kimmel Live!” that he met the British flight attendant while flying to the UK.

In May, Camille said she hadn’t spoken to Kelsey in 14 years during her appearance on the “Humble Brad” podcast, adding that they don’t co-parent their two children together, but rather do “parallel parenting.”

The reality star also exposed the brutal text she said she received from the Emmy award-winning actor after their nasty split.

“He sent me a text saying, ‘I feel no remorse for what I am doing,’” she recalled.
