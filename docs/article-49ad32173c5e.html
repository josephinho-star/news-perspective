Zooey Deschanel Says ‘New Girl’ Cast Was “Forced to Drive Home” After Brutal Hours on Set

2026-09-06 11:30

There were plenty of late nights on the set of New Girl — and sometimes the hardest part of the day was simply getting home.

Zooey Deschanel recently recalled that she and her castmates were “forced to drive home” from set because “none of us had rides to work.”

“The hours were insane,” she said while chatting with fellow New Girl star Jake Johnson on Wednesday’s episode of his podcast, We’re Here to Help. “I would drive home from work and be almost like falling asleep.”

It got bad enough that Deschanel would sometimes call her mom for help.

“My mom would wake up in the middle of the night because I’d be like, ‘I’m so tired. I can’t drive home,’” she added. “And she would wake up to talk to me so I’d stay awake. Isn’t that really sweet?”

Johnson agreed. The actor said the schedule was “too much,” and a recent shoot with New Girl alum Damon Wayans Jr. apparently brought some of those memories back.

Johnson and Wayans recently spent a long day filming their upcoming comedy series inspired by The Cable Guy, and Johnson said he was reminded of what happens when a shoot stretches well past a normal workday.

“You get so weird because you’re so overtired,” he said. “So all of a sudden, we’d be in takes again and I was doing stuff really just to make Damon laugh and he’s doing stuff to make me laugh. And I’m like, you only get here after hour 13 when you’re like, ‘I have to go home now.’”

Of course, the long days don’t seem to have left the New Girl cast with too many bad memories. The stars have continued to speak fondly about the sitcom, which ran for seven seasons, and there’s even been some recent talk of bringing it back.

Max Greenfield added fuel to those revival rumors in July when he said that creator Elizabeth Meriwether had written new scripts.

“I’m not gonna play the game,” Greenfield said on the Today show. “As far as I know, it’s happening.”

So, if the New Girl gang really does return, here’s hoping they get a little more sleep this time around.
