The top 10 cars that put other drivers at greatest fatality risk — but protect their passengers

2026-10-25 16:28

These highway behemoths might rule the road in size, but they leave a fatal trail of destruction.

Hines Law reveals the top 10 vehicles that pose the greatest danger to other drivers and pedestrians, based on annual driver fatality rates for all vehicles involved.

The Ram 3500 is the most dangerous pickup for other drivers on the road, but is one of the safest for those driving it, according to a September 2026 report of danger gaps, comparing other driver deaths vs. own driver deaths.

Research examined nearly 200 vehicle models to find which ones put other drivers at the most risk while keeping their own drivers safest. It looked at two fatality rates: how many drivers of a given vehicle die in a crash, and separately, how many drivers of other cars die when they crash into that vehicle.

Both rates are counted per million vehicle-years on the road, so common models and rare ones can be compared equally. The two death rates were combined to reveal which cars pose extra danger to other drivers.

A recent study found that an adult pedestrian hit by an SUV is 44% more likely to die than one hit by a smaller car. For children, that number jumps to 82%.

SUV sizes are being called into question. In the UK, some experts are already calling on the government to restrict the size of SUVs sold to the public. A Hines Law expert said this is unlikely to happen in the US because big trucks are a part of the “culture” here.

Here are the top 10 vehicles that other drivers should be on the lookout for on the road:

Ram 3500 crew cab long bed 4WD

Chevrolet Silverado 3500 crew cab 4WD

Ram 2500 crew cab short bed 4WD

Chevrolet Silverado 2500 crew cab 4WD

GMC Sierra 2500 crew cab 4WD

Ram 1500 crew cab short bed 2WD

1. Ram 3500 crew cab long bed 4WD

Own-driver deaths: 37 per million vehicle-years

Other-driver deaths: 204 per million vehicle-years

Weight: 6,872 lbs, among the heaviest trucks

Liability claims for damage to other people’s property: 93% above the average

The Ram 3500 Crew Cab Long Bed 4WD ranks as the single most dangerous vehicle for other motorists on American roads. The driver of the other vehicle is over five times more likely to be killed than the occupant of the Ram in a crash with this truck. Weighing nearly 6,900 pounds, The Ram also generates 90% more third-party property damage insurance claims compared to average vehicles.

2. The Chevrolet Silverado 3500 crew cab 4WD

The Chevrolet Silverado 3500 crew cab 4WD came in second place. 10 of its own drivers die per million vehicle-years, one of the lowest rates among the cars studied. But other drivers die at nearly 13 times that rate, with 126 deaths. At a whopping 7K+ pounds, it’s the heaviest model on American roads and gets nearly 60% more liability claims than other cars.

3. The Ram 2500 crew cab short bed 4WD

The Ram 2500 crew cab short bed 4WD is the second Ram model to make the top five. An outside driver is more than five times more likely to die than the person driving the Ram itself. The 2500 model weighs over 6.7K pounds, almost the same as the Ram 3500 and its liability claims are also 63% higher than other cars.

4. The Ford F-150 SuperCrew 2WD

The Ford F-150 SuperCrew 2WD is 4.5K pounds, around 2.3K pounds less than the Ram 3500. It’s Ford’s standard best-selling pickup, but other driver is more than 14 times more likely to die than the person behind the wheel of the F-150.

5. The Chevrolet Silverado 2500 crew cab 4WD

The Chevrolet Silverado 2500 crew cab 4WD rounds out the top five dangerous vehicles in America at 6.6K pounds. Its own drivers die at a rate of 21 per million vehicle-years, and other drivers die more than five times as many, at 111 per million vehicle-years.
