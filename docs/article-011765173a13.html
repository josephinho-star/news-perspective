OnlyFans twins raking in 6-figures monthly recruit their disabled mom to join adult platform

2026-10-31 15:50

Twin sisters Julia and Lauren Burch are raking in six figures a month on OnlyFans — and now they’ve helped their disabled mom cash in on the explicit-content platform, too.

“We were aspiring models when we were kids, but we’re short, so we started on social media,” Julia told The Post.

“We love it so much. It’s a lot of work but it’s worth it,” Lauren chimed in.

They each have roughly 2 million Instagram followers and over 400,000 OnlyFans subscribers.

Their mother Pam, 46, has always been their biggest fan — and is now getting in on the action herself.

“My girls are smart and know what they’re doing. I’ve always been so proud of them,” Pam said.

Pam, who also has a son, raised her children as a hardworking single mom in Ontario, Canada, where she still lives most of the year in a van. She homeschooled them for much of their childhood and worked multiple jobs to support Julia and Lauren’s competitive dancing ambitions.

After waitressing for seven or eight years, Pam returned to school and went to work for the school board.

“I became a child and youth care practitioner for the school board,” she said.

But her life took a devastating turn when she was diagnosed with Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease — a rare genetic neuromuscular disorder that causes extreme fatigue in the arms and legs.

Forced to leave her job, Pam was also separating from her fiancé. Her disability payments of about $2,000 a month weren’t enough to cover her expenses, she said.

“OnlyFans seemed like the best next step,” Pam said. “It allows me to work when I can and give my body a rest.”

When Pam launched her own page in 2023, her daughters put their content-creation expertise to work helping her get started.

“We help her take photos and get to take cute pictures for social media around the holidays and things like that,” Lauren said.
