Why Jennifer Aniston isn’t hiding her romance with hynotherapist Jim Curtis — even though insiders say she won’t marry him

2026-08-29 12:00

Jennifer Aniston is not afraid to flaunt her boyfriend Jim Curtis — and their romance is a far cry from how private she was in previous relationships.

Just don’t expect her to see a wedding ring on her finger anytime soon. Or ever.

The “Friends” icon has been dating the hypnotherapist and transformational life coach for more than a year and is blissfully in love, sources tell Page Six.

“They’re still very, very happy,” said a source in the know. “But they have no intention of ever getting married — Jen swears she will never get married again.”

Twice- wed Aniston, 57, normally keeps her personal life under wraps.

But she’s boldly going public with this relationship — on Wednesday, she will interview Curtis at The 92nd Street Y in Manhattan.

The event is billed as “An Evening with Jim Curtis” and celebrating the hypnotherapist’s new book, “The Book of Possibility.” Page Six is told tickets sold out as soon as they went on sale.

The couple were first seen cuddling at a hotel in Big Sur, California, in July 2025. This is the first-long term relationship for the A-lister since her divorce from Justin Theroux in 2018, after three years of marriage. She was previously wed to Brad Pitt from 2000 until 2005.

But insiders say Curtis provides something her movie star exes didn’t.

“This relationship is succeeding on so many levels because it’s got things that Jen’s never had before,” said the source.

“She doesn’t feel like she has to work to impress him or keep him [like she did with Theroux, 55]. She doesn’t feel like she has to compete with other, more famous women who are after him.”

Pitt famously left Aniston for Angelina Jolie, his co-star in “Mr. and Mrs. Smith.”

“And most of all,” the source said, “she never ever questions where she stands with him — he has zero hesitation about letting her know that he is crazy about her and is totally devoted.”

And while he’s not as famous as her exes, dating Aniston has put Curtis in the spotlight.

In June, he appeared on “The Kelly Clarkson Show” on NBC to hawk his book, and insiders said his high-profile romance had undoubtedly given his career a boost.

That newfound fame comes with a price, though — outlets expect him to talk about his girlfriend as well as himself and his own career.

Asked about their “origin story,” Curtis told the “Today” show’s Craig Melvin of Aniston, “We were just introduced by friends … we found out we had mutual friends and we started to just chat and it took a long time.”

He’s also been ushered into the upper echelons of Aniston’s Hollywood life, joining her and her friends like Courteney Cox and Jason Bateman on vacation in July.

The group, which also included actor Pedro Pascal, was spotted enjoying a luxury yacht trip off the coast of Mallorca, Spain.

And this week, Curtis joined Aniston and her former “Friends” co-stars Cox and Lisa Kudrow for dinner at the swanky Tower Bar at Hollywood’s Sunset Tower hotel, Aniston’s favorite LA haunt. Bateman and his wife, Amanda Anka, were also there, along with Will Arnett.

Despite all of this, the source said of Curtis, “He doesn’t play games, doesn’t do hard-to-get, doesn’t try to make himself seem bigger by expecting [Aniston] to shrink herself. He is in no way threatened by her fame and has no illusions that he’s the one who needs to join her world, not expect her to change to suit him. He loves her for exactly who she is.”

On “Today,” When Melvin pointed out that Curtis was smiling and “kind of blushing” while talking about Aniston, Curtis laughed and said, “Yeah, I sure am.”

In November 2022, Aniston admitted that she did not want to marry again, but said she would “love a relationship.”

“Never say never, but I don’t have any interest,” Aniston said when asked by Allure magazine if she would ever tie the knot. “I’d love a relationship. Who knows? There are moments I want to just crawl up in a ball and say, ‘I need support.’

“It would be wonderful to come home and fall into somebody’s arms and say, ‘That was a tough day,’ ” she added.

Curtis, a divorced dad-of-one, often uses a cane and walks with a limp due to a severe, undiagnosed spinal condition he developed at age 22.

Doctors found lesions on his spinal cord and the condition led to chronic nerve damage, muscle spasms and near-paralysis, prompting a long and painful physical recovery.

During his appearance on Clarkson’s show, Curtis said he had used hypnosis to combat his chronic pain, adding, “Transformation work really is changing your beliefs so you can change your reality. You saw me walk out there [on stage]. Just a few years ago I wouldn’t have walked out because I had shame and fear around what I would look like, around my own disability.

“And then when I changed my beliefs, when I started to love myself, when I started to see it as a super power — look what’s possible, I’m here today.”

It’s this positive mindset that has helped Aniston be sure with herself and in their relationship, another friend said.

Last November, “The Morning Show” star spoke about Curtis to Elle for its 2025 Women in Hollywood honors, calling him “quite extraordinary.”

“Hypnotism is one of the many things that he does,” she said.“He’s quite extraordinary, and helps many, many people. He’s very special, very normal, and very kind and wants to help people heal, move through their trauma and stagnation into clarity.”

She added, “It’s a beautiful thing to commit your life to.”

The actress recently finished filming the fifth season of Apple TV + “The Morning Show,” in which she plays TV anchor Alex Levy.

Due next year, it’s been announced as the final season of the series — leaving lots of time to devote to her personal life.

But even as their romance goes from strength to strength, another friend revealed, “Jen also has been clear with Jim that she is never getting married again.

“She has zero interest in that institution and is never going back. He more than understands and his belief in soul mates and spiritual connections makes him more than amenable to the fact that a piece of paper is unnecessary.”
