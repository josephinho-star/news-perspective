Mamdani recovering after ‘successful’ Achilles surgery

2026-11-28 00:33

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani is recovering at Gracie Mansion—the mayor’s official residence—following a “successful” surgery on his injured Achilles, which was caused by a recreational soccer game earlier this month.

“Today, Mayor Zohran Kwame Mamdani underwent a surgical procedure to repair his Achilles tendon,” Joe Calvello, Mamdani’s press secretary, said in a statement.

Calvello confirmed that the procedure started at 11:33 a.m. EST and concluded at 12:42 p.m. EST on Friday.

“The surgery was successful, and the Mayor is expected to make a full recovery,” the press secretary continued. “He was discharged from the hospital and is now resting comfortably at Gracie Mansion.”

“We are extremely grateful for the first-class care the Mayor received from the medical staff today. First Deputy Mayor Dean Fuleihan is leading day-to-day operations today,” he concluded.

Mamdani first appeared injured last week at a morning press conference where he disclosed his injury. The mayor injured his Achilles while playing a soccer game against New York City’s press corps.

He said he forgot the stretch before hitting the field, telling reporters, “I scored a goal soon after getting on the pitch, and I forgot that I was 35 and soon after I tore my Achilles.”

“I will be in a boot for six to eight weeks. I will play soccer in about nine months,” Mamdani added.

Due to the surgery, he will also not be able to attend the rescheduled Macy’s Thanksgiving parade. He remarked that he was scared to have surgery, saying it was his first procedure.

“I’ve been looking forward to attending this parade, and now, as a result of the rescheduling, I will not be able to make it because of the doctor’s orders,” the mayor stated.

Macy’s pushed its Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday, citing a severe storm moving up the East Coast, which is forecast to bring heavy rain and wind. The parade is now scheduled for 8:30 a.m. EST Saturday.
