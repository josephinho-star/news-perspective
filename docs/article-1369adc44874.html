‘Selling Sunset’ star Christine Quinn reveals where she stands with Chrishell Stause after years-long feud

2026-11-18 19:32

“I never had a problem with Chrishell. I don’t hold grudges. It’s not who I am,” Quinn told Us Weekly in an interview published Wednesday.

“The animosity wasn’t from me,” she insisted. “I was willing to be in rooms with her.”

Quinn also revealed that she would have been open to rejoining the cast even if Stause was still involved.

“Even though we had disagreements, I could still show up to work. So I wouldn’t have a problem filming with her. Would it be comfortable? Probably not for her. But she’s doing her own thing [now]. She was on for nine seasons. I don’t think she wants to be on the show anymore.”

Despite having no bad blood with Stause, 45, Quinn — who exited “Selling Sunset” ahead of its sixth season — admitted that she felt “vindicated” by her enemy’s behavior in the seasons after she left.

“I was definitely right about Chrishell,” she boasted. “People’s eyes were opened. I’m like a truffle pig. I can smell a biatch from a mile away.”

The “How to Be a Boss Bitch” author also noted that it was time for several of her former co-stars to leave the show as “the energy was getting dark and vile, and it wasn’t fun for anyone.”

“Some of these girls are just bad leftovers, and if you leave fish out too long, it reeks up your house,” she quipped. “It was time. We needed a mix-up.”

“I can bond and relate to anyone who’s going through a breakup,” she told the outlet. “I never met G. I wish [Chrishell] all the best.”

Stause, 45, and Quinn, 38, were both original castmembers of “Selling Sunset,” which premiered in 2019.

The former soap opera actress came on to the scene as a new agent at The Oppenheim Group, a luxury real estate brokerage, while Quinn was a seasoned top employee.

Stause and Quinn clashed after the latter dished that the former pair had been in couples therapy in an exclusive interview with Page Six in July 2020.

The “Days of Our Lives” alum subsequently called out Quinn on social media, calling her co-star “desperate,” and the two continued to fight until Quinn’s exit.

Stause remained on the show through Season 9 and announced her departure in November 2025.

“I’ve vacillated back and forth with this decision in the past,” she told Bustle at the time.

“I have to be honest — having come from nothing, it’s really hard to turn something like this down.”

“I’ve gotten to a place where I don’t need the show financially,” she noted. “I’m lucky to have other forms of employment because it’s no longer good for my mental health.”
