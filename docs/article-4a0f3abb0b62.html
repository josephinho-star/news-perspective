Michigan Dem Haley Stevens endorses Republican Senate candidate Mike Rogers

2026-11-01 18:51

In a blow to Democrat Abdul El-Sayed’s campaign for the United States Senate in Michigan, his former primary opponent, US Representative Haley Stevens, has endorsed Republican candidate Mike Rogers.

Stevens shocked party officials on Sunday by throwing her weight behind Rogers just two days ahead of Tuesday’s high-stakes US midterm election, which will decide which party runs Congress.

“I am endorsing Mike Rogers for the US Senate. Today, I do not belong to Abdul’s campaign,” she said in a video posted on X after attending a church service with Rogers in Detroit, the state’s largest city.

Stevens’ surprise support for US President Donald Trump’s endorsed candidate in the tightly contested race comes after she was uninvited to a joint event on the same day with El-Sayed, who defeated her by a slim margin in the August primary.

“Who we are as Michiganders is not hate, and [that] is not what we are getting out of our Democratic nominee right now,” Stevens said.

Democrats have pledged to act as a check on Trump if they win control of Congress, including blocking funding for the Iran war and the president’s tariffs.

Polls indicate that Democrats are likely to win control of the House of Representatives while the Senate, which Republicans currently lead by a 53-47 majority, is considered a toss up between the two parties. Michigan is one of a handful of highly competitive Senate races nationwide.
