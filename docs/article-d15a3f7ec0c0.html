Is a $10,800 boat towing bill high for Lake Erie?

The story is that a guy I know from golf trips ran his massive boat up on a sandbar the other night. Three hours later, his yacht was off the sandbar, but it came with a massive bill.

– Brad S. knows this business: A little background: I was in the boating business and am now in insurance. I've had some experience with this kind of issue. The easy answer: It depends. The size of the boat, the distance from the tow boat's berth and how bad the boat is stuck will all factor into the final invoice.

Most marine towing companies will charge anywhere from $300-$600/hr, and that starts from when they untie to when they tie back up. A soft ungrounding can run anywhere from $1500 to $3500. Bottom line: It depends.

Now, there are programs you can join - BoatUS for example - where you pay for an annual membership and they'll pull your sorry ass off the sandbar as part of the annual membership. A lot of captains think, "I'll never need that!" Until they do.

He likely has insurance, and should put in a claim. Insurance carriers are often able to negotiate a better rate for the tow, if he was indeed getting gouged.
