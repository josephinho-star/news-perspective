US delegation walks out as Iran’s Pezeshkian angrily responds to Trump at UN General Assembly - live

2026-09-23 14:43

'Russia's aggressive posture poses a direct threat to European security,' Czechia's Pavel says

Meanwhile, Czechia’s Pavel is speaking now, and he joins in warnings about an emerging “power vacuum” in international politics, where “might replaces right.”

Speaking specifically on Ukraine, he warns that “Russia’s aggression continues to inflict enormous suffering on the Ukrainian people, civilians and civilians.”

“Russia’s aggressive posture poses a direct threat to European security as a whole,” he warns, talking about recent incidents involving Russia, including at Leipzig airport in Germany.

But framing his argument more broadly, he says:

“The consequences of aggression reach far beyond Europe. Russia’s war has undermined global food security, disrupting agricultural production, exports and supply chains. Its effects are felt in my country, but even more acutely in parts of Africa and elsewhere, particularly in countries that depend on stable and affordable food supplies.”

He then turns towards Czechia’s bid to get a non-permanent seat on the UN security council for 2032-2033, and to his reflections on AI as he warns that “our ability to govern this technology is not keeping pace with its development.”.

Russia and Ukraine interested in 'limited ceasefire' on grain and energy, US Rubio says

Meanwhile, US secretary of state Marco Rubio said after meeting with Russia’s Sergei Lavrov that both Russia and Ukraine had expressed interest in a limited ceasefire involving grain and energy.

“We would be open to playing a constructive role in making that happen,” he said, quoted by Reuters.

'We will never bow our head,' Iran's president says, as he says country 'ready for dialogue' but without threats

Pezeshkian goes back to Trump’s speech from yesterday, as he says that “everything uttered about our country goes against the very essence” of the UN charter.

“He must know that the resistance of the Iranian people will only increase in the face of sanctions, increased pressure, increased bullying.

We will never bow our head or bend at the knee.

Iran must be understood by Mr Trump and those who seek to bully us; that we are ready for dialogue and diplomacy and negotiations - without accepting the language of force.”

'We will stand in defence of our beloved Iran until our last breath,' Iran's Pezeshkian says

Pezeshkian then says he wants to say “a few words” to those who “carried out aggressions against our country.”

“They have tested the strength and the steadfastness of Iran, and they have learned that Iran cannot be made to surrender. Iran has never, ever been an aggressor, has never started a war, and has always shown her commitment to the negotiating table.

They impose the war on us but we have proven that we’re not afraid of fighting, of war, that is defensive in nature, and we will stand in defence of our beloved Iran, our homeland, until our last breath.”

He draws comparisons between Iran and Israel in particular suggesting double standards as he says:

“The nuclear weapon is in Israel, but the inspectors in Iran. Israel kills, but Iran is subject to sanctions.”

“We cannot let some have free access and gain their interest from a waterway, while at the same time using that waterway to impose their aggressions upon us, to impose insecurity upon us, to forbid us access to our own waterways.”

He also warns that any “expansion of warfare will not result in security.”

He then says the situation in Palestine remains “an open wound on the conscience of the international community,” and “a symbol of the failure of international mechanisms to prevent civilian suffering.”

“We cannot speak of peace while ignoring Palestine. Lasting peace in West Asia is impossible without justice for Palestine,” he says.

Pezeshkian says the US often labels Iran as terrorists, while “we only seek to live independently.”

“Those who are committing terrorism, and committing crimes – are they they protectors of peace and stability? None of the international laws have been adhered to by them,” he snaps.

He says that “for the past two centuries” Iran “has not attacked any country of any territory,” and focuses on “defensive” military power.

He says Iran only wants nuclear technology for energy and “for progress,” and not for military reasons.

“We will not bow our head and give up the right that is inherently ours. We say it clearly no nuclear weapons and no limitations to peaceful nuclear technology. Our peace will not come in the field of battle. They must give the needed security to the people and nation of Iran.”

US Rubio meets Russia's Lavrov on the sidelines of UN general assembly debate

Away from the general assembly room, there is also lots of activities on the sidelines of the main debate.

For example, US secretary of state Marco Rubio is meeting Russia’s foreign minister Sergei Lavrov.

Earlier today, Kremlin spokesperson Dmitry Peskov told reporters that Russia believed there were no ground for Ukraine peace talks, but claimed it was open to them in principle.

He opens focusing on his concerns about social media platforms, as he says it is a fundamental "threat to our democracies that I do not believe is receiving the interest it deserves.”

He particularly talks about troll farms and other actions that seek to undermine “the fundamental principle of democracy: one person, one voice, one vote.”

He then turns to talking about Romania’s reforms over the last 37 years, showing “the difference between a communist system that oppressed them and stifled their initiative and freedom of choice, and a democracy that rewards work, innovation and personal responsibility.”
