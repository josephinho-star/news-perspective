Special education teacher calls for moms to ‘stop giving birth’ to boys to escape ‘patriarchal hell’

2026-11-05 19:05

A Maryland special education teacher called on women to “stop giving birth” to boys and “start aborting male fetuses” in a deranged series of social media posts, according to reports.

Dana L. Zentgraf — a teacher at Montgomery County’s Clopper Mill Elementary School — advocated the mass culling of newborn boys in a series of October Facebook posts, arguing their extermination was the only way to free America from “patriarchal hell,” the Free Beacon reported.

“I think we should start aborting male fetuses (or not having kids at all right now) and put an end to female oppression once and for all,” Zentgraf reportedly wrote in one post.

“They are all menace to society and we need to stop giving birth to them,” she wrote in another. “If it’s too late to abort, raise him like your life depends on it because it does. All of our lives depend on it.”

The posts were made by a Facebook user named “Da Graf,” which the Free Beacon linked to Zentgraf after she apparently posted using her real name on her school’s page.

And that Da Graf account — which featured a photo of a young girl alongside a Palestinian flag — was filled with other twisted screeds insisting all boys were fated to exploit women, and calling on mothers to exterminate them.

“If I was in my reproduction era, I would have one of those early blood tests to see if the fetus is a boy or a girl and I would abort it if it was a boy,” one post wrote. “I don’t want to give birth to my own oppressor and I feel like the only way out of this patriarchal hell is for there to be less men on this earth.”

In others, she explained her dark philosophy, claiming even mothers who insist they’re raising their sons well are really just breeding future misogynists.

“I have news for the mothers of boys who believe they raised their sons ‘right’: In the world we live in, raising a boy right at home does not guarantee that he will be seen or treated right outside those doors,” she wrote.

“Once he steps outside your home, he enters a patriarchal world that has taught men, in ways both subtle and overt, that they hold power over women,” she said. “It’s your son too. It’s all of them.”

The posts were alarming as is, but especially so given Zentgraf’s position caring for young children — presumably boys among them — with her duties focusing on teaching kids with emotional, mental, learning and physical disabilities.

Neither Zentgraf, Clopper Mill Elementary or Montgomery County Public Schools responded to requests for comment.

The school district has a history of adopting lefty policies, including dropping nearly $500,000 in 2020 for an “anti-racist audit,” while in 2023 it limited parents’ access to a board meeting where their ability to opt children in or out of gender identity classroom discussions was being weighed, the Free Beacon reported.

Zentgraf’s social media ire wasn’t just restricted to men — she referred to the US as “AMERIKKKA” in a play on the racist Ku Klux Klan’s acronym, and called for insurrections over voting.

“Anyone who loves America this much is racist and nothing will ever change my mind,” she wrote alongside a photo of an SUV bedecked in American flags.

And some women were also among her targets — with Zentgraf outright dismissing anyone who had any sympathy for men.

“Upon meeting another woman, I will almost immediately find a way to say something mean about a man or men in general to figure out whether or not she’s a safe person to be myself around,” she wrote. “The last thing I need in my life is a male centered woman.”

Zentgraf’s Facebook account appeared to have been taken down after the Free Beacon report was published.
