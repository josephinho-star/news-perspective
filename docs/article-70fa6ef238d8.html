Hong Kong man falls from luxury housing complex, striking domestic helper below

2026-10-31 15:28

Both victims conscious when taken to hospital after man falls at 1 Austin Road West and lands on podium, hitting Filipino woman

A Hong Kong man believed to have accidentally fallen from a height struck a domestic helper on the podium of a luxury housing estate in West Kowloon on Saturday, with the pair sent to hospital.

Police said they received a report at 7.43pm that the man had fallen at 1 Austin Road West and landed on the podium, hitting the woman.

A police spokesman said both were conscious at the scene, with the man taken to Queen Elizabeth Hospital and the woman sent to Kwong Wah Hospital.
