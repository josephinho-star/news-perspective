Why is surrogacy in China proving so difficult to ban despite the crackdown?

2026-09-10 07:56

Undercover investigation leads to allegations of two labs operating illegally, despite the nation’s tough stance against the practice

On Wednesday, the health bureau in Changsha, Hunan province, said six people were detained after a tip about a “laboratory that illegally uses assisted reproductive technologies”, according to state news agency Xinhua.
