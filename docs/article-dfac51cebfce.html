Model stabbed to death by abusive ex — who then posted sick pic of body to her Instagram

2026-11-17 15:22

A Russian model was stabbed to death by her abusive ex – who then killed himself after posting a sickening image of her body on her Instagram, according to reports.

Anastasia Drozdova, 28, had finally had enough of on-off boyfriend Dmitry Zadvornykh, 38, and dumped him in September, initially hiding out in Turkey before returning home to Moscow, according to Russian outletREN TV.

Zadvornykh ambushed her at her rented apartment Saturday — stabbing her eight times before slashing her throat, REN reported.

The deranged killer then used his ex’s phone to upload a snap of her lifeless body to her Instagram Stories, along with a twisted message for her followers to see.

“You mocked the wrong person and paid the price. Goodbye,” wrote Zadvornykh, a laser hair removal specialist with a criminal record.

Zadvornykh then drove to a shooting club around eight miles from the home where he shot himself. He later died of the injury in a hospital.

Zadvornykh had previously been abusive and his ex fled to Turkey after dumping him, according to friends.

The jealous ex even filed a missing persons report, before realizing she was back in Moscow, the report said.

“When she returned to Moscow, he would start messaging her again,” a friend said, according to NeedtoKnow.

Authorities have confirmed they are treating it as murder.
