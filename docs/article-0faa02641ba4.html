This $29 Luseta Beauty Hair & Body Mist Set makes my hair smell amazing

2026-09-18 18:03

I have a soft spot for beauty products that make me feel instantly more put together, and a good hair mist is one of those little luxuries I can never resist.

Lately, I’ve been reaching for the Luseta Beauty Hair & Body Mist Set, and at just $29, it feels like an especially easy addition to my routine.

The concept is simple: these lightweight fragrances are designed to be used on both hair and skin, giving me an easy way to freshen up from head to toe. I love having something I can spritz on after getting ready, before heading out, or whenever my hair needs a quick fragrance refresh. Luseta describes the collection as being made for everyday layering, on-the-go touch-ups and all-day freshness.

- Makes hair smell amazing

- Four scents to choose from

- Some scents may be too sweet

- Doesn’t last as long as perfume

The set gives me four fragrance personalities to choose from: Rich Mellow, Brazilian Sun, Love Bouquet, and Vibrant Blossom. Each one has its own mood, which makes picking a scent part of the fun.

Rich Mellow is the one I’d reach for when I want something warm and cozy. It combines mandarin, bergamot and passion fruit up top with jasmine, ylang ylang, grape and neroli, before settling into amber, musk, vanilla and caramel. Brazilian Sun takes me in a completely different direction, with orange, mandarin, coconut and hazelnut alongside floral notes and a sweet base of vanilla, amber, musk, caramel and cacao.

Then there’s Love Bouquet, which has a romantic floral profile built around mandarin, bergamot, watermelon, jasmine, rose and peony. Vibrant Blossom is the freshest-feeling option to me, pairing grapefruit, mandarin and bergamot with rose, jasmine and freesia, followed by musk, vanilla and cedar.

For my hair specifically, I like the idea of having fragrance without reaching for my regular perfume every time. A quick mist gives my hair that freshly scented feeling, and because the product is also intended for the body, I can coordinate the fragrance across both. It’s that finishing step that can make getting ready feel a little more special.

The formula also includes glycerin, orange peel oil, grapefruit peel oil and lavender oil. Luseta says its formulas are salon-quality, made with naturally derived ingredients, suitable for all hair types and free of sulfates and parabens.

For $29, this is an easy beauty find for anyone who loves a good-smelling hair product but doesn’t want to spend a fortune. I especially like that there are four distinct scent directions, so I can pick one based on my mood rather than committing to a single fragrance.

For me, the biggest selling point is still the simplest one: I spray it through my hair, and my hair smells amazing. At $29, that’s a beauty treat I’m very happy to keep within arm’s reach.
