Travis Kelce makes new comments about Taylor Swift wedding: ‘Best night of my life’

2026-09-11 18:01

Travis Kelce declared his wedding to Taylor Swift was “the best night of my life” two months after the nuptials.

“Marrying Taylor was more than I could have ever dreamed it to be,” he said in a preview clip from his interview with ESPN’s Chris Berman airing Monday before the Kansas City Chiefs’ game against the Denver Broncos.

Kelce married Swift, also 36, on July 3 at Madison Square Garden in New York City.

Over 1,000 guests were present, from Kelce’s teammates and coach, Andy Reid, to Swift’s celebrity pals including Selena Gomez, Karlie Kloss, Lena Dunham, Sabrina Carpenter, Ed Sheeran, Zoë Kravitz and Mariska Hargitay.

Kelce broke his silence on the wedding at a Chiefs training camp press conference on Aug. 12, where he publicly called Swift his “wife” for the first time.

“I appreciate everyone who came out and celebrated and had fun with us. That’s about all I really got from that night. It was a crazy night. It was full of a lot of celebration,” he said.

“Me and Taylor are so thankful for everybody that showed up, from the vows to just seeing everyone, to how much fun we had throughout the entire evening and hearing that kind of be relayed through everybody’s stories of the night,” Kelce shared.

But Kelce did reveal he had one regret from the “magical” night.

“I wish it could have lasted even longer than it did. It felt like it just happened in the blink of an eye,” he said.

Kelce added, “They kicked us out. Well, once the drinks stopped being served, everyone just starts to, you know, mosey out.”
