Padres blast elevator music during Brewers practice — returning the favor from Milwaukee’s troll job

2026-10-06 03:51

Both the Milwaukee Brewers and San Diego Padres continue to troll one another.

While the Brewers were practicing on the field at Petco Park on Monday, the stadium had the cool, calm sound of elevator music blasting from the speakers.

Back Milwaukee, when the Padres were taking batting practice on Saturday, American Family Field was blasting “monotonous” music off the speakers.

San Diego did their best to attempt to drown out the music, as they had a Bluetooth speaker playing music next to their batting cage.

But for the Padres, the attempt to drown out the sound didn’t work as they found themselves down 2-0 in the NLDS.

In both games, the Padres lost in heartbreaking fashion.

On Saturday, San Diego blew a 2-2 lead in the seventh inning from a solo Wilson Contreras home run.

In the ninth inning of that game, Ty France thought he had a go-ahead two-run homer, until the ball bounced off the roof of the stadium and landed in Dustin Harris’ glove.

On Sunday, superstar closing pitcher Mason Miller took the mound with a 3-2 lead in the eighth, in an attempt to make a two-inning save.

Miller would give up two walks in the ninth, and then gave up a go-ahead two-run single to Jackson Chourio, resulting in the Padres falling 4-3.

After the game was over, speculation arose that Miller was tipping his pitches. On Monday, his manager, Craig Stammen, denied any possibility of his closer being predictable.

Game 3 of the NLDS is set to begin at 6:30 p.m. PT when Nick Pivetta faces off against Dustin May.
