15 hospitalized in potential Thanksgiving food poisoning at Florida medical center

2026-11-27 23:47

Fifteen people were hospitalized due to possible food poisoning from Leon Medical Center headquarters’ Thanksgiving event, according to Miami-Dade Fire Rescue officials.

Miami-Dade Fire Rescue told a local NBC affiliate that crews responded to reports of potential food poisoning before the call was shifted to a Level 1 mass casualty incident. The agency continued that it initiated medical treatment before 15 people were hospitalized.

The center is located in Doral, Fla., where local police told The Hill that Leon Medical Centers was hosting its annual Thanksgiving luncheon before attendees began showing signs consistent with possible illness. Doral Police stated the illness’s source remains unclear.

“Leon Medical Centers contracted an external caterer to provide a Thanksgiving luncheon for its staff at its Doral Headquarters,” the company wrote in a statement provided to CBS News. “Some of the staff began to experience gastrointestinal symptoms shortly after the luncheon and required treatment.”

“We continue to work with the caterers to determine, if possible, the cause and continue to provide support to our staff and their families,” the center said. “No patients are affected by this incident which was limited to the Company’s headquarters facility.”

“Please note that this did not occur at any of the Leon Medical Centers healthcare facilities,” it concluded.

Food poisoning occurs when people ingest certain germs – such as Salmonella or E. coli – which can cause a variety of mild to serious symptoms, according to the Centers for Disease Control (CDC). While some germs take days to cause illness, other germs can make individuals sick within a few hours.

Common symptoms include nausea, vomiting, stomach pains or cramps, diarrhea or having a fever.

The CDC recommends that individuals experiencing diarrhea or vomiting drink lots of water to avoid dehydration.

Miami-Dade Fire Rescue did not respond immediately to The Hill’s request for comment.
