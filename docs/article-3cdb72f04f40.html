Rachel Stuhlmann is back turning heads at the US Open, Xandra Pohl reading in a bikini & UNC wins in Ireland

2026-08-30 13:41

It's the final Sunday of August, and we're getting things started this week with a Sceencaps favorite, Rachel Stuhlmann. She's a former tennis player turned influencer who OutKick dubbed the world's No. 1 tennis influencer.

It's a title she didn’t take lightly when it was first handed out a few years back. She went all in on doing her part to grow the game of tennis through social media content, and I'm happy to report she's still at it.

Rachel, who now considers herself to be the "World’s 1st Tennis Influencer," is back at the U.S. Open churning out content.

She's on the court. She's at the parties. And she's even teaming up with retired tennis player Genie Bouchard.

Wherever tennis needs her is where you'll find Rachel Stuhlmann. She isn’t just handing out tips on a random court far away from the action. She's on the ground at events and that's intentional.

"I've been creating tennis content for nearly a decade, long before sports creators became as common as they are today. I think that's where the "OG" nickname comes from. I've been fortunate to help pioneer this space and watch it grow," she told the Daily Star earlier this summer.

"The way I stay ahead is by continuing to evolve. I don't just post content for the sake of posting - I think strategically about storytelling, what fans actually want to see, and how I can bring them closer to the sport."

Rachel added, "Because I work across so many areas of professional tennis, whether it's hosting, media, partnerships, or content strategy, I have access to stories and experiences that most fans don't get to see. That's what I try to share."

The strategic storytelling is some of the best strategic storytelling you're going to find, especially when it comes to the sport of tennis.

Well, the official charge, according to WTSP, was child neglect. The breastfeeding of her 2-month-old infant while being too intoxicated to care for the child and a 2-year-old toddler played a role in the arrest.

The Largo Police in Clearwater, Florida were called to an Extended Stay America hotel last week after a witness alleged Tamisha Brodie, 32, "had fallen while holding her baby and became unresponsive multiple times.

The affidavit states that she showed signs of severe intoxication. She had slurred speech, glossy eyes and a strong odor of alcohol. Investigators found empty containers in her room. The kids reportedly had full diapers.

There was also no formula for the baby found. Mom was taken to Largo Medical Center for evaluation. Deputies said she had a blood-alcohol content of .295.

The infant, who she had allegedly been breastfeeding while intoxicated, was taken to a children's hospital. There was no mention of the 2-month-old's blood-alcohol content, although I think it's safe to assume that the infant wasn't able to drive.

The mother of the year nominee had her bond set at $15,000.
