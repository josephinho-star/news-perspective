Panthers vs. Browns prediction, best bets: Bryce Young flops in the Dawg Pound

2026-09-27 11:45

Is Bryce Young making the next step toward being elite?

He’s been a superstar through two weeks, throwing six touchdown passes and posting 648 passing yards in that time.

He now gets a Cleveland Browns squad that was relatively lucky to get through a hapless Buccaneers team.

The issue for me isn’t that the Browns are good – they’re not – and Deshaun Watson isn’t having a rebirth either.

Carolina has smashed early on this year, but with starting linebacker Devin Lloyd out and Jalen Coker banged up and listed as questionable, my model continues to favor the Browns.

We have Cleveland as just a half-point underdog at home.

Add in that Young hasn’t yet shown to be the same player on the road and we have a much different Carolina team.

In his career, Young is a significantly different player at home vs. on the road: He owns a 58.7 percent completion rate on the road vs. 64.8 percent at home, with a 78.5 rating against an 87.2 rating.

Given Young’s regression on the road and the Panthers’ defensive holes thanks to injuries and we could be looking at the Quinshon Judkins show.
