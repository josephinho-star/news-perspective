Taylor Swift wore an expected accessory on her wedding day

2026-10-30 17:12

The pop superstar slipped into sneakers for part of the reception after exchanging vows with the Kansas City Chiefs tight end at Madison Square Garden in New York City on July 3, a source exclusively told Page Six.

“Taylor changed dresses between the ceremony and reception. She wore sneakers during part of the reception and wore her hair down,” the insider said.

The Grammy winner, 36, wore a custom Christian Dior Haute Couture gown designed by Jonathan Anderson for the ceremony, completing her bridal look with personalized Christian Louboutin shoes and Cartier jewelry.

However, Swift wasn’t just focused on her wedding look.

The singer also made an effort to personally connect with guests throughout the star-studded festivities, which drew more than 1,000 people – an impressive mix of family members, longtime friends, teammates and celebrities in attendance.

“Despite the number of people, she really made a point of greeting guests throughout the celebration and made everybody feel so welcome,” the source told us.

The insider shared additional details about the elaborate wedding setup, from the castle backdrop where Swift and Kelce exchanged vows to the various rooms featuring games and prizes for guests.

The couple tied the knot in a ceremony officiated by Adam Sandler, with Swift’s brother, Austin, serving as her man of honor and Kelce’s brother, Jason, standing beside him as best man.

Their highly anticipated nuptials transformed Madison Square Garden into an extravagant wedding venue, complete with elaborate decorations, food stations and carnival-style entertainment.

At around 7:30 p.m., the Garden’s exterior lit up with pink digital screens reading “JUST&T MARRIED!”

Kelce, 37, later described the wedding as the “best night of my life,” telling reporters in August that he was grateful to everyone who came out to celebrate with them.

Since saying “I do,” the newlyweds have been adjusting to married life while balancing their demanding careers.

As Page Six previously reported, the couple has been navigating their new normal as husband and wife while figuring out how to spend quality time together amid his NFL commitments and her busy schedule.

“Taylor and Travis spent so much time together in the months leading up to the wedding, and they were pretty much inseparable,” a source exclusively told Page Six at the time. “Now they’re settling into this new rhythm as a married couple and really enjoying what that feels like.”

Last month, Page Six revealed that the couple isn’t planning to settle down in just one place. While Travis’ Kansas home serves as their primary residence during football season, the newlyweds plan to split their time between several properties across the country, depending on their schedules.

The couple’s love story began in 2023, when Swift was first spotted cheering Kelce on at a Kansas City Chiefs game that September.

After nearly two years together, they announced their engagement in August 2025 with a playful Instagram post that read, “Your English teacher and your gym teacher are getting married,” ending the caption with a dynamite emoji, seemingly referencing their “TNT” nickname.
