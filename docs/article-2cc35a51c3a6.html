Lovesick suitor tried to put ‘hex’ on OnlyFans superstar: ‘What the f–k is this?’

2026-09-19 14:34

OnlyFans superstar Kit Barrus says a lovesick pal who couldn’t escape the friend zone tried a little black magic to change her mind — by placing a “love hex” inside her home.

And she wasn’t taking any chances.

“I threw it away!” Barrus, 28, told The Post. “I was terrified it would work and I’d get feelings for him.”

The bizarre discovery came after Barrus found a beaded necklace — that said FOR KIT — secretly wedged inside a table following a get-together at her home, she said.

“I remember looking at it and thinking, ‘What the f–k is this?’”

The pretty content creator immediately suspected her smitten friend, who she said had a longtime fascination with witchcraft and the spirit world.

“I was in his dorm room, I saw all the books on spirts and spells,” she said.

With his witchy ways in mind, Barrus promptly Googled what he could have been trying to do.

“Apparently there’s this thing where you make someone a piece of jewelry and while you’re making it, you think about them and put your intentions into it and then you leave it where they live so the energy is supposed to flow into them or whatever,” she explained her findings.

Online, aspiring sorcerers can find countless blogs and tutorials for “enchanted” jewelry and spells for just about anything.

But people have been leaving the magic to the pros in recent years — hiring Etsy witches for reasons ranging from solidifying a promotion to cursing an ex-lover.

Of course, the online witches are summoned for love spells as well.

The pair’s relationship had started innocently enough.

Barrus and the mystery man attended college together in Southern California, where both were pursuing math degrees and regularly crossed paths.

“We were friends, but he wanted more,” Barrus said. “We did some things together, and it was casual, but then he wanted to move in a romantic direction, and I knew he wasn’t for me.”

Barrus said she made her feelings crystal clear.

“I was honest, and repeatedly told him the same thing,” she recalled. “it’s not going to happen.”

Barrus — who draws roughly 10,000 OnlyFans subscribers a month and has amassed some 500,000 Instagram followers — believed the would-be Romeo had finally accepted that romance was off the table.

Then came poker night.

The man joined a casual game at Barrus’ home and, to her relief, seemed perfectly content keeping his distance.

“He sat in the corner, and I was pleased he’d seemed to finally come around,” said Barrus, whose globe-trotting content includes hiking, mountain climbing and sailing in the buff.

But days later, while cleaning, she found the eerie offering.

That’s when Barrus’ mind went straight to her would-be beau.

“He put some kind of powerful energy into it to make me fall in love with him,” she said.

Whether it did or didn’t, Barrus wasn’t interested in finding out.

She tossed it, and gave her home a spiritual scrub.

Whatever spell her smitten suitor may — or may not — have been trying to cast, Barrus said it fizzled on the spot.
