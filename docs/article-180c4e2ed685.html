Netflix actor dead at 38 after jumping off Santa Monica pier, as cops launch suicide probe

2026-11-19 19:17

Alexander Haskin has died after jumping off a Southern California pier. He was 38.

The background actor, who appeared in several shows, including “You,” leapt off the Santa Monica Pier around 4 p.m. local time, authorities announced in a statement obtained by Page Six.

His body was located by a search-and-rescue team within the hour, and life-saving measures were unsuccessful.

According to the Santa Monica Police Department, Haskin was alone before the incident and didn’t interact with others before jumping.

Authorities claimed there were no suspicious circumstances surrounding Haskin’s death, which is being investigated as a suicide.

Haskin’s identity was revealed after SMPD announced a man had died after they responded to a call about a man in the water on the west end of the pier.

Alexander’s father, Dana Haskin, exclusively told the California Post that he last talked to his son earlier in the day.

While the patriarch refused to elaborate on the conversation, he claimed the conversation was brief and “brings up more questions” for their family.

Dana added that Alexander wasn’t visibly distressed before the incident and looked forward to spending the holidays with his family.

Dana also noted that the “Rookie” background actor could swim, and he believed the decision to jump was rash and emotional.

“In the heat of the moment, who knows what a human brain can do?” Dana told the outlet. “It’s still a big mystery to us … we keep asking why.”

Before acting, Alexander was a military police officer in the Air Force and a personal trainer after graduating from Long Beach City College in 2010.

Aside from his father, Alexander is survived by his sister and three brothers.

Authorities have requested witnesses at the scene come forward with information related to the incident by contacting the Santa Monica Police Department at 310-458-8491.
