Meta reports third-quarter revenue beat and issues strong guidance

2026-10-21 23:45

Meta reported better-than-expected revenue in its third-quarter earnings report on Wednesday and issued strong guidance for the current period. The stock rose about 2.5% in extended trading.

- Earnings per share: $3.83

- Revenue: $64.60 billion vs $63.37 billion

It's not clear if earnings were comparable to the $5.75 per share expected by analysts.

Revenue jumped 26% from a year ago.

"We hit major milestones this quarter toward delivering personal superintelligence for everyone - including launching Muse and Muse Wonder, our first frontier model," said CEO Mark Zuckerberg in the earnings release.

For the fourth quarter, Meta said it expects revenue of between $73 billion and $76 billion, or $74.5 billion at the middle of the range. Analysts expected $73.73 billion, according to LSEG.

The initial success of the Muse AI personal assistant tool that debuted in September helped Meta end September with its best month on Wall Street since 2022.

Meta isn't the first to create a digital assistant that can organize people's email and scan the web for headphone deals, but the company's empire, which includes Facebook and Instagram, has helped introduce AI agents to the masses. At the same time, Meta and its AI chief Alexandr Wang have continued rolling out new foundation models and related updates at a speedy clip, including Muse Wonder, which was released last Monday.
