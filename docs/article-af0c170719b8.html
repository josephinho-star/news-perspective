North Carolina Sports Betting Laws and Timeline

North Carolina sports betting first started gaining traction around 2019 when tribal lands accepted sports betting. In March 2021, in-person bets were allowed. On June 14th, 2023, House Bill 347 was signed into law by Governor Roy Cooper. March 11th 2024, saw mobile and online sports betting become live.

College Sports Betting Rules in North Carolina

You can bet legally on college sports in North Carolina. This includes options on spreads, totals, and sides. Unlike other states, you can bet on player props in North Carolina for college athletes.
