Retailers like Macy’s have up to 80% off diamonds, designer bags and more

2026-11-17 15:24

You’re going to be dripping in designer this Black Friday with all the luxe early deals already live.

Major retailers like Macy’s got a head start on the big event, which takes place on Friday, Nov 27. Ahead of Thanksgiving, you can save up to 80% off everything from diamonds to designer handbags across your favorite brands.

Donna Karan, Michael Kors and Kate Spade are all fair game, granting you the opportunity to score the perfect accessory to carry to an upcoming holiday party. Treat yourself, or avoid the post-Christmas procrastination and shop styles for your loved ones.

Below, we gathered some of our favorite luxury early Black Friday deals before the major markdowns to come in a few weeks.

Post readers can’t get enough of Macy’s tennis bracelets, especially while the diamonds are nearly 80% off. This lab-grown style comes in regular, gold-plated and rose-gold sterling silver, with 1 carat diamonds.

You can’t have a tennis bracelet without its matching necklace. This one is 75% off for a limited time and is strung with stunning lab-grown white sapphires.

This spacious work tote is both stylish and functional, with ample storage to carry all your essentials. It’s made out of supple saffiano leather with an interior zip pocket to stash your keys or wallet.

Seeking a smaller bag? This mini style is made out of luxe, 100% cow leather and comes in three colors: umber, amber and black/gold.

These boots were made for walking straight into winter. A sleek pair of knee-highs elongates your legs while keeping your body warm in colder temps.
