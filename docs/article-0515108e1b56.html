Matthew Broderick lived at Warren Beatty’s home for 3 months as ‘victim’ to the ‘longest meeting ever’

2026-11-04 18:27

Matthew Broderick claims he may have been the “victim” of the “longest meeting ever” with Warren Beatty ahead of filming their 2016 rom-com “Rules Don’t Apply.”

During an Oct. 22 appearance on the new podcast “Nepo, Baby!,” Broderick, 64, recalled heading to Los Angeles to meet with Beatty, 89, for what was only meant to be a day, but ended up lasting several months.

“I remember I was early, driving up the hill to Mulholland, so nervous. I had to pull over and wait at a curb for a while, just sweating,” he said.

The “Ferris Bueller’s Day Off” actor said Beatty was wearing a white shirt and white pants when he first met him at the house, adding that he “looked like Warren Beaty.”

He also noticed burn marks on the filmmaker’s face from having things burned off ahead of filming, which he said was “good” because he “looked more human.”

“We had a really good dinner. We didn’t talk about the movie for a long time. Then he gave me the script, … and I was put in a room to read it,” Broderick said.

He went on to explain that by the time he finished reading the script, it was already late, and after discussing the movie, Beatty offered to let him stay the night when he said it was time to head back to his hotel.

The “Lion King” star said he was “creeping” around the house the following morning, hoping to quietly slip out, when he ran into Beatty making oatmeal in the kitchen.

Broderick then tried to use going to the gym as an excuse to leave, only for Beatty to point out that they had a gym at the house.

“I don’t remember when that ended, but the reason I say it might be the longest meeting ever is I did the movie and stayed there. He had a little guest house,” Broderick revealed.

He went on to joke, “That meeting, in a way, went on for, like, three months because I never left. I never really left Mulholland.”

“Rules Don’t Apply” was directed and co-written by Beatty, who also starred in the film alongside Broderick, Lily Collins and Alden Ehrenreich.

Notably, the cast also included Beatty’s wife, Annette Bening, whom he married in 1992 and with whom he shares four children.

Broderick, for his part, tied the knot with Sarah Jessica Parker in May 1997 in a surprise ceremony at a Manhattan synagogue.

The couple share three children: son James, 23, and 17-year-old twin daughters, Tabitha and Marion.
