South Korean killer’s drone escape from Philippine prison foiled by test footage

2026-09-16 05:10

Convicted killer Park Wang-yeol reportedly spent some US$74,000 on his planned drone-assisted prison break

Park Wang-yeol, 47, was sentenced in 2022 to 60 years behind bars for the killing of three South Koreans in a sugar cane field outside Bacolod City in 2016, a crime that earned him the nickname “sugar cane field killer”.

This was not Park’s first escape attempt. He had already escaped custody twice, in 2017 and again in 2019 while being transported from a court hearing.
