Christa Pike spoke about botched executions weeks before surviving lethal injection: ‘My worst nightmare’

2026-10-02 16:46

Pike, 50, revealed fears she would suffer the same fate as Tony Carruthers, another Tennessee death row inmate whose lethal injection execution had to be called off in May.

“The concerns I have on our lethal injection method are simply that I do not have good veins, and I’m worried about our physician not being qualified to start a central line on me,” Pike said in a recording obtained by the New York Times.

“Tony Carruthers lived out my worst nightmare. Detail for detail. And I don’t want to go through that.”
