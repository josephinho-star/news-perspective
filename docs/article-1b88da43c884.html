Tragic update in search for missing teenage Marine who vanished from California military base

2026-11-21 17:50

The search for a missing 19-year-old Marine has ended in tragedy after authorities confirmed his body was found inside an abandoned building just minutes from where he was last seen.

Marriner Richards, who was reported missing Nov. 7, was discovered Friday in Twentynine Palms, ending an extensive search involving his family, local residents and private investigators.

The grim discovery came just minutes from both the Tortoise Rock Casino, where Richards was last seen, and the nearby Marine Corps Air Ground Combat Center, where he was stationed.

Richards was last spotted at the casino on Nov. 6. Investigators said he reportedly left the property on foot around 11 p.m., but what happened after that remained unclear.

The San Bernardino County Sheriff’s Department said deputies responded to the Marine Corps base after military police reported Richards missing.

Richards’ body was found approximately three miles from the casino and five miles from the sprawling Marine Corps base, according to the San Bernardino County Sheriff’s Department.

Authorities said the discovery was made around noon Friday near Adobe Road, as family members and community volunteers joined law enforcement in searching the desert community.

The sheriff’s department later confirmed Richards’ identity and determined that he died by suicide, according to ABC News.

The discovery came as volunteers were preparing for another coordinated ground search, with family members, private investigators and local residents combing the rugged desert terrain for clues.

Richards’ disappearance had sparked an outpouring of support from the community, with dozens of people joining the effort and others traveling from Utah to help his family, KESQ reported.

His father, Brock Richards, previously said surveillance footage from the casino showed his son leaving around 5 a.m. and walking along nearby Adobe Road at 5:34 a.m.

“Richards was last known to be on foot, and no associated vehicles were reported,” the sheriff’s office said in a statement.

The Morongo Basin Sheriff’s Station had issued a public appeal for information Nov. 11, asking anyone who might have seen the teenager to come forward.

Richards’ parents, Brock and Brooke, traveled from Utah to California to take part in the search. A GoFundMe campaign was established to help cover search expenses and allow them to remain in the state while looking for their son.

The investigation into his death remains ongoing.
