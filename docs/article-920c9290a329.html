Big bags are back: This Marc Jacobs tote is 60% off during Nordstrom Rack’s anniversary sale

2026-09-22 17:42

Big bags are making a comeback for fall, and you’re in luck because some of our luxury faves are now available at a discount.

To celebrate the 125th anniversary of Nordstrom Rack, the retailer dropped a special birthday sale that lasts through October 4, with up to 65% off designer brands.

Among the hottest deals is the Marc Jacobs On Your Marc Tote, now 62% off for a limited time.

“This fall, accessories are becoming oversized again, including the handbag,” celebrity stylist Jazmin Whitley confirmed. “This tote is perfect for fall because it’s an easy way to carry all your essentials and still look chic.”

The bag is spacious enough to store all your essentials inside, while looking luxe on the outside with pebbled leather and logo hardware. It also features an easy magnetic closure and comfortable top carry handles.

“I love to style a tote like this with a cropped knit and a cigarette pant,” Whitley suggested. “We’re going to be seeing way more maximalist ‘What’s in my bag’ content, and that makes this tote the perfect fall accessory.”

The bag is available in black and whiskey, and both colors are on sale during Nordstrom Rack’s anniversary event.
