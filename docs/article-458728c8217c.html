Belkin’s kid-friendly wireless headphones are 25 percent off

2026-09-02 14:58

Verge readers can get a discount on Belkin’s SoundForm Mini 2 wireless headphones. The kid-focused on-ear headphones feature an 85-decibel volume limit and easy controls, and a mode that lets them beam audio (wired or wirelessly) to a second pair without extra hardware. Readers can use the code VERGE25OFF to save 25 percent on up to two pairs of SoundForm Mini 2 headphones.

The $29.99 model without the zip-up carrying case gets knocked down to $22.49. Alternatively, a more limited selection of colors are currently available that include a case, which is $29.99 instead of $39.99 once the offer code is applied in the cart.

While these don’t deliver noise cancellation, they should be good enough for most situations. They can last up to 55 hours on a single change, and they can fold up neatly to fit into a variety of bags. The SoundForm Mini 2 are made for ages 3 and up, and feature an adjustable headband and cushioned earcups. They’re pretty no-frills, though the audio-sharing mode I described above is a great way around having to buy some sort of audio splitter or wireless transmitter to ensure multiple kids are entertained.
