Calum Ritchie returns from his Islanders demotion with something to prove

2026-11-26 11:40

Watching from above, it wasn’t at all obvious before Calum Ritchie left the bench in Toronto last month that anything serious had happened. He knew right away, though, “that it was pretty bad,” after taking a hit from Nick Blankenburg that resulted in a lower-body injury.

That was five weeks ago, with a pit stop in Hamilton before Ritchie finally got back in an Islanders uniform on Wednesday evening against the Blues. The one-game AHL stint was mostly a function of wanting Ritchie to get back up to speed post-injury, though the organizational need created by Kyle Palmieri’s injury trumped that.

And, if absence has a way of clarifying one’s standing, this one seemed to drive home that, while Ritchie is still considered a foundational piece for the Islanders’ future, his presence in the lineup right now wasn’t so important that they couldn’t afford to have him do a de facto rehab stint in the AHL.

The Islanders didn’t plan on Ritchie being in the minors for any serious amount of time, but it was interesting, too, to hear coach Pete DeBoer say Wednesday morning that “in a perfect world, he might’ve had a little more runway there to get a few more games.”
