Michael Douglas reveals how ex Jeanne Tripplehorn reacted to his bombshell affair reveal

2026-10-08 17:46

Michael Douglas got Jeanne Tripplehorn’s approval before he wrote about their affair in his explosive new memoir.

“I talked to her before I put it in the book,” he told Page Six in an exclusive interview, before assuring that “she was fine” with it.

In “One Helluva Ride,” the actor revealed he had conducted a torrid fling with Tripplehorn, 63, while filming “Basic Instinct.”

At the time of the film’s release, many assumed that Douglas was having an affair with another female co-star, Sharon Stone, due to their steamy scenes together.

“The truth is, I fell very hard for Jeanne despite our nearly 20-year age difference,” the Oscar winner —who was married to his first wife, Diandra Luker, at the time — shared in the book.

“In fact, I was ready to officially leave my wife for Jeanne. I was crazy about her.”

The “Wall Street” star noted, however, that he knew ditching nearly two decades of marriage and jumping right into another serious relationship would be the “worst thing” for him.

“My relationship with Jeanne lasted through production, then we went our separate ways,” the 82-year-old wrote in the book.

“But I will always think of her as an amazing actress and a truly terrific woman that I was very fortunate to spend time with.”

Douglas eventually divorced Luker, and he’s been married to Catherine Zeta-Jones, 57, since 2000. Tripplehorn went on to marry actor Leland Orser, 66, in 2000.
