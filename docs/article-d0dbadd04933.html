CENTCOM's Thanksgiving message to troops 'is one of deep gratitude' for their 'exceptional work' protecting US

2026-11-26 11:37

U.S. Central Command spokesperson and U.S. Navy Capt. Tim Hawkins told Fox News Digital that his message to the more than 50,000 service members stationed across the Middle East this Thanksgiving is "one of deep gratitude for the exceptional work they're doing."

"Right now, they're focused on a variety of missions, including helping protect shipping moving through the Strait of Hormuz, an international waterway. They're also maintaining America's ironclad blockade against Iran. Highly effective," Hawkins said. "And they're also protecting America and Americans from terrorists. So a lot of hard work. We're very grateful for their outstanding focus, commitment, and dedication to the mission."

Hawkins also said CENTCOM is "grateful" this holiday for the families of America's men and women in uniform.

"We have a highly motivated team deployed to the Middle East, but as many of us know in uniform, military service does not come without great personal service, great personal sacrifice, and great personal cost. And that cost isn't just unique to our service members, it is also shared by the family members back at home who are waiting," Hawkins told Fox News Digital.

"They're praying. They are holding down the home front during a very, very challenging period of time," he added. "So I want all of them watching to know they are in our thoughts and we are also grateful to them and we wish them a happy Thanksgiving."

CENTCOM has played a key role in Operation Epic Fury against Iran, which was launched on Feb. 28.

"Of course, a lot of folks focus on Iran, and that has been a primary focus, but we've been doing other things, continuing to ensure that ISIS doesn't reconstitute in order to protect America and Americans," Hawkins said about the Islamic State terrorist group.

CENTCOM said in September, following the departure of U.S. forces and equipment in Iraq, "Combined Joint Task Force-Operation Inherent Resolve (CJTF-OIR) remains operational and is now headquartered in Jordan as U.S. forces continue the counter-ISIS mission in Syria alongside Coalition partners and the Syrian government."

"We've also been working shoulder to shoulder with our partners and defending against Iranian aggression," Hawkins told Fox News Digital. "There have been around 10,000 or more projectiles fired at innocent civilians, Gulf neighbors, and our service members have stood shoulder to shoulder with our regional partners and protecting not only our forces, but the civilians around.

"And of course, as you have seen in the Strait of Hormuz, traffic is flowing through, and that's because of the great commitment of our service members in protecting international shipping," he added.
