Kalshi moves into a new kind of gambling: the stock market

2026-11-17 15:35

Kalshi is getting ready to offer stock trading on its platform, according to reports from The Wall Street Journal and CNBC. The company reportedly plans to use its acquisition of broker FB Securities to launch equities and exchange-traded funds “within the next two months,” pitting the prediction market against rivals like Robinhood.

The rumored expansion into stock trading comes as Kalshi faces a growing number of legal battles over its prediction market platform across the US. New York, Nevada, New Mexico, Washington, Connecticut, and several others have accused Kalshi of operating illegally in their states, while Utah recently filed criminal charges against Kalshi and its CEO.

At the same time, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission is pushing back on state efforts to regulate prediction markets like Kalshi and Polymarket, arguing that it has the “exclusive” authority over them. Nine Democratic senators wrote a letter to the CFTC this week, urging it to stop interfering with state bans on prediction markets.
