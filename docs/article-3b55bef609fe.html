The lonely crusader against Gianni Infantino

2026-09-05 02:01

Lise Klaveness was calling out Gianni Infantino before it was cool.

The president of Norway’s soccer federation has been a longtime thorn in the side of FIFA’s president, pushing vociferously for more transparent governance at the summit of global soccer. Her concerns, which date back to the World Cup in Qatar in 2022 and its questions about migrant labor and human rights, found a much wider audience this summer after Infantino’s secret plot to sell off parts of the tournament exposed the risks of FIFA’s highly centralized leadership.

The 45-year-old former international soccer player, attorney and judge recently told POLITICO that not only must Infantino depart FIFA but the system he constructed be totally rebuilt. Sipping black coffee recently in Le Café Lacoste at Monaco’s tony Méridien Beach Plaza hotel, the flame-haired Klaveness outlined five areas where that should start, including committee reform and reducing the president’s executive power.
