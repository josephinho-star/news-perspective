Carson Benge makes Mets rookie history with 20-20 campaign: ‘Something special’

2026-09-26 02:56

WASHINGTON — Carson Benge’s ascent has been one of the compelling reasons to watch the Mets for much of this season’s second half.

Friday night, the 23-year-old outfielder homered in the fifth inning of the Mets’ 7-6 loss to the Nationals. The blast was his 20th and made him the first rookie in franchise history to join the 20-20 club — homers and stolen bases in a season.

Ronny Mauricio is scheduled to start Saturday in what could be his farewell to the Mets.

Once a highly regarded prospect, the 25-year-old infielder has largely seen his major league opportunities dissipate because of injuries. He will be a non-tender candidate this winter in his first season of arbitration eligibility.

In 18 games for the Mets this season he owns a .454 OPS.

“He’s definitely missed a lot of time,” Green said, noting the ACL tear that cost Mauricio the 2024 season. “He’s come back. The athleticism returned. I don’t see any residual effect of that. So, from my vantage point, people come back from injuries all the time. Does it make life harder? Sure. It makes things tougher.

“Does he have every opportunity out in front of him to still turn in a good career? Sure. He does. I think the path forward for him is you know impact the baseball consistently, and if you can minimize the chase.”

Mauricio spent six weeks on the IL this season with a thumb fracture. Following his rehab he spent most of the season at Triple-A Syracuse.
