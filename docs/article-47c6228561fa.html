Incoming NY Rep. Anthony Constantino says he wants ‘every gay American’ to join the GOP

2026-11-23 01:10

Incoming Congressman Anthony Constantino said politically realigning America’s gay voters “to be Republican” is one of his top priorities after winning New York’s 21st District.

“Now that I’m elected, one of my big goals is to get every gay American to be Republican,” Constantino pledged in a brief video posted to X on Sunday evening.

The 44-year-old entrepreneur did not elaborate on how he planned to swing the country’s gay voting bloc in the video, but promised he would say “more on this soon.”

The incoming Republican representative’s pledge to turn LGBTQ voters towards the GOP would require a wild shakeup to the voting habits of many gay and lesbian Americans, who historically tend to support more liberal candidates.

Nearly 87% of LGBTQ voters supported Democratic candidates in House races during the Midterm elections, according to the NBC News Exit Poll.

A measly 12% of gay, lesbian and trans voters supported Republicans in the same races – numbers that mirror voting habits in the 2024 presidential election, according to the poll.

But in an interview with The Post, Constantino said he believes the Democrats aren’t a party “that’s safe for the gay community.”

“What I do know is that Democrats are against the gay community. And they are willing to endanger their lives,” Constantino said in a phone interview.

Constantino said that “hardworking” and “high-income” gay voters would be perfectly served by the Republican Party, saying the switch is “just something that makes sense.”

“The Republican Party is for things that are good for the gay community, like low taxes, security, safety,” the congressman-elect said.

It’s unclear how many gay voters cast their ballots for Constantino – a Trump-endorsed sticker manufacturer – as he beat Democrat Blake Gendebien by a slim margin of 50.8% to 49% in one of the Republican Party’s few wins in New York.

The Republican’s ambitious commitment to win over gay voters is his latest outrageous pledge. He previously called on President Trump to free disgraced music mogul P Diddy.

“I think President Trump should consider freeing Diddy. He’s a historic New Yorker that did great things for New York state. I think we should consider it,” Constantino said on election night.
