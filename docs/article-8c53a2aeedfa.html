How to watch ‘Percy Jackson’ Season 3 premiere for free: Release date, time

2026-11-20 01:00

‘Percy Jackson’ Season 3 release date

“Percy Jackson and the Olympians” Season 3 premieres on Nov. 20.

What time does ‘Percy Jackson’ come out?

New episodes of “Percy Jackson” come out at 12 a.m. ET.

How to watch ‘Percy Jackson and the Olympians’ for free

‘Percy Jackson’ Season 3 cast guide

Walker Scobell returns as the titular Percy Jackson, the young demigod son of Poseidon. Leah Sava Jeffries and Aryan Simhadri are also back as Percy’s best friends, Annabeth Chase and Grover Underwood.

Charlie Bushnell also reprises his role as Luke Castellan and Tamara Smart, as Thalia Grace, is a series regular this season after recurring in Season 2.

The large ensemble cast is rounded out by returning guest stars including Lin-Manuel Miranda (Hermes), Adam Copeland (Ares) and Jason Mantzoukas (Dionysus / Mr. D).

Some new faces joining the cast this season include Levi Chrisopulos and Olive Abercrombie as young demigod siblings Nico and Bianca di Angelo; Dafne Keen as the goddess Artemis; David Costabile as Dr. Thorn; Kate McKinnon as Aphrodite; and Ming-Na Wen as Hera.

‘Percy Jackson’ Season 3 release schedule

“Percy Jackson” will air over four weeks, marking a change from last season. Here’s when you can expect to see new episodes drop at 12 a.m. ET on Disney+.

- Season 3, Episode 1: “My Rescue Operation Goes Very Wrong” – Nov. 20

- Season 3, Episode 2: “An Old Dead Friend Comes to Visit” – Nov. 20

- Season 3, Episode 3: “I Learn How to Grow Zombies” – Nov. 27

- Season 3, Episode 4: “We Visit the Junkyard of the Gods” – Nov. 27

- Season 3, Episode 5: “I Have a Dam Problem” – Dec. 4

- Season 3, Episode 6: “I Wrestle Santa’s Evil Twin” – Dec. 4

- Season 3, Episode 7: “I Put on a Few Million Extra Pounds” – Dec. 11

- Season 3, Episode 8: “The Gods Vote How to Kill Us” – Dec. 11

What book is ‘Percy Jackson’ Season 3 based on?

“Percy Jackson and the Olympians” Season 3 is based on book three of the series by Rick Riordan, “The Titan’s Curse.”

There are five books in the original “PJO” series, plus a number of sequels and spin-offs.
