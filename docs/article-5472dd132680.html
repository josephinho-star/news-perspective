Conservative Christian leaders turn on Sophie Cunningham after topless Maxim photo shoot

2026-11-25 16:08

This isn’t the Sophie Cunningham they thought they were supporting.

Some conservative Christian figures had issues with the WNBA star’s decision to pose topless for Maxim Magazine — in which she also opened up on her “Christ-centered’ plans for the future — after celebrating her for defending women in the transgender sports controversy.

According to the Christian Post, Robert P. George — a Princeton professor and conservative Catholic scholar — leveled criticism at Cunningham on Facebook, despite her “courageous expressions of patriotism and gender sanity.”

“I can no longer, however, share the view of those who urge young women to regard her as a role model,” George wrote, according to the publication.

“I wouldn’t want my own daughter and granddaughters to do that. Her pornifying herself in photographs for Maxim magazine makes that impossible. I’m mystified as to why she would do such a thing.”

Despite mostly being a WNBA role player, Cunningham has become one of the faces of the league — known for her bold fashion arrivals at games and fierce competitiveness on the court alongside Caitlin Clark with the Fever.

Her notoriety jumped to a new level when she spoke out against transgender athletes playing in women’s sports in an ESPN profile.

“Typical. There is such a persistent lack of discernment, even (and often especially) among leaders in the evangelical world, we automatically praise and platform any celebrity who can even pronounce the name Jesus without giving them time to see if their profession has any fruit,” evangelist Justin Peters wrote, according to the Christian Post.

“It always, always comes back to bite us. No matter how many times we see this same movie play out, we never learn.”

In the Maxim Q&A, Cunningham opened up on how she views her religious-focused future with a husband.

“I have a very high standard for my future husband,” Cunningham said. “He’ll be someone who is Christ-centered and can lead me in my walk with Jesus. Someone who’s friendly, confident, and secure. Someone who is taller than me and athletic and just a good human with similar morals and values. With what I do for a living, I have to have someone who’s confident, secure, and able to kind of shoot the shit a little bit because that’s who I am. I don’t live a typical lifestyle.

“I hope one day to kind of have more of a traditional lifestyle of being in one spot, having a husband and kids and whatnot. But I prayed for this life, and I’m still having fun with it. But when it comes to a guy, I want someone who could push me in my faith, someone who is loyal, and someone who also loves every little piece of me.”

Cunningham opened up on the decision to be featured on the magazine’s cover — and noted how Maxim has changed from when it routinely featured female celebrities in provocative photo shoots.

“Well, it was so funny because I guess I just, in my own world, I don’t know, I just wasn’t exposed to Maxim. Maxim reached out to me, and I sent it to my agents. And at first, like, absolutely not,” she said on her “No Hesitation” podcast.

“Like, what are you doing? And I was like, what do you mean? And they did not know that Maxim rebranded. And so they were thinking of the old Maxim, which is like a, you know, it’s a man’s magazine, I’m gonna say that. And I was like, oh my gosh, guys, I would never ask to do that. And so once they found out they rebranded, we actually shot this in Indy. I had the best time. It was absolutely amazing. And a fact, those leather pants right there, I’m pretty sure were around $27,000. And it took four people to get me in them.”

Still, the one shot of Cunningham — with her hair covering her bare chest — caused issues with her newfound fans.
