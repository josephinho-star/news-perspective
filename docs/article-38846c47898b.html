WWE Sunday Night's Main Event match card, predictions and more

2026-09-06 13:46

For at least one night, WWE’s Saturday Night’s Main Event will transform into Sunday Night’s Main Event as the showcase will take place in Atlanta on Labor Day weekend.

There are only four matches on the card, but each comes with heightened stakes.

Sunday Night’s Main Event is also set to feature a live performance from hip-hop artist Quavo, who will debut an unreleased track from his forthcoming album "QROMELIFE."

Read below for the match card and predictions on what will happen at the State Farm Arena on Sunday night. The event begins at 8 p.m. ET and can be seen on Peacock.

Charlotte Flair, Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley vs. Jade Cargill, Michin and B-Fab

A six-woman tag team match will take place to add to the longstanding feud among Charlotte Flair, Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley and Jade Cargill, Michin and B-Fab. It feels as though these women have been battling for months. Paxley was added to the fray when she made her SmackDown debut to back up Flair and Bliss. But Cargill, Michin and B-Fab have continuously been scrapping with Flair, Bliss and Paxley with Cargill and Flair seemingly fighting for the top contender spot to Chelsea Green’s women’s title.

Prediction: Flair, Bliss and Paxley put away Cargill, Michin and B-Fab.

Baron Corbin vs. Lil Yachy

Baron Corbin returned to WWE and made an immediate impact. He earned a United States Championship shot at SummerSlam and defeated Trick Williams to win the title. Williams has been clawing his way back to the top, eyeing a title return. But he hasn’t been the only who has messed with Corbin. Lil Yachty, Williams’ valet, has kept nipping at Corbin’s heels. He’ll have a chance to help Williams in his match against Corbin. If Lil Yachty lasts at least five minutes in the ring with Corbin, Williams will get a rematch for the U.S. title. Expect some kind of shenanigans to take place in this match.

Prediction: Lil Yachty holds out for as long as he can against Corbin

Cody Rhodes vs. Randy Orton

It’s the long-awaited grudge match between WWE superstars Cody Rhodes and Randy Orton. Rhodes owes Orton another lick after getting punted in the head following their WrestleMania 42 Night 1 main event brawl. Rhodes won that match to retain the Undisputed WWE Championship. Orton got the last word with the kick that left Rhodes’ eye swollen. Since Orton’s return, the two have brawled constantly. Rhodes even went for a kill shot at one point. It caused SmackDown GM Nick Aldis to tell them if they continued to fight each other, they would never get a shot at a WWE championship again. It led to some harsh words from Orton, trying to coax Rhodes into hitting him. It didn’t work, but you could feel the tension between the two men building. This match will turn into a good ol’ fashioned slobber knocker.

Prediction: Cody Rhodes comes out on top in this one.

Bron Breakker vs. Oba Femi

There’s no tangible title on the line in this one – there’s only perception. Who is the future of WWE? Bron Breakker has been inside his own head for weeks as he grappled with Oba Femi’s rise in WWE. Breakker was on the mend as Femi defeated Brock Lesnar at WrestleMania 42, won the King of the Ring Tournament and seemingly ended Lesnar’s career at SummerSlam. Breakker failed to dispatch Seth Rollins and only became a tag team champion after Logan Paul got hurt. Breakker has shown that he still has that dog in him but Femi’s rule over the Raw roster has been cataclysmic for his opponents. Both men could very well be champions in the future, but for now, they fight to be the face of what is to come down the road.

Prediction: Breakker needs a win here to go back to the days in which he was considered the man with the loudest bark and the biggest bite. Breakker wins in what might be an upset.
