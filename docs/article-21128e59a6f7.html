Lindsay Hubbard is dating ‘Below Deck’ star Joe Bradley, her ex Carl Radke confirms

2026-10-15 18:38

Carl Radke is spilling the tea on his ex Lindsay Hubbard’s rumored romance with “Below Deck Mediterranean” star Joe Bradley.

The “Summer House” star, 41, was asked during an exclusive interview with Page Six’s “Virtual Reali-Tea” Thursday whether he and his former fiancée would ever rekindle their relationship.

Radke insisted the two are better off as friends, adding that Hubbard, 40, is already “talking to somebody else.”

When co-hosts Evan Real and Danny Murphy asked whether that person was Bradley, whom Hubbard has recently been romantically linked to, Radke smiled and teased, “Quite possibly.”

He went on to gush that Bradley “is a good-looking man” and “really cool.”

Radke, who was promoting the New York Road Runners nonprofit behind the NYC Marathon, added, “I know the ladies like him. He’s got the ridiculous blue eyes all the ladies love.”

Reps for Hubbard, Bradley and Bravo did not immediately respond to Page Six’s requests for comment.

Rumors began swirling about Hubbard and Bradley — not to be confused with the “Southern Hospitality” star also named Joe Bradley — earlier this week.

An eyewitness alleged to DeuxMoi that the reality stars had been spotted kissing while filming “In the City,” Bravo’s “Summer House” spinoff.

Bradley’s castmate Captain Sandy Yawn subsequently told Page Six she “felt [a] vibe” between Hubbard and Bradley, leading her to believe there was “some truth” to romance rumors.

On Tuesday, Us Weekly claimed the pair were “hooking up” after they “met at a Bravo event and hit it off.”

The outlet alleged Hubbard and Bradley have been an item “for a little over a month now” — but are “having fun” and “not dating.”

Bradley has previously been linked to his co-stars Kayley Smith and Victoria “Vee” SanJuan.

While Radke is “focused on [his] career” and didn’t give us any dating updates, he did share that he and Hubbard were “wingman-ing” for each other while filming the “Summer House” and “Below Deck Med” crossover in Greece in June.

The episodes are expected to air next year as part of “Summer House” Season 11 and “Below Deck Med” Season 12.
