LeBron on climbing to third all time in assists: 'Something I didn't foresee in my career'

2026-11-16 00:24

PHILADELPHIA -- As LeBron James leaped for a contested layup over the outstretched arms of Indiana Pacers forward Pascal Siakam and guard Aaron Nesmith on Sunday, James turned and passed the ball to Sixers guard Tyrese Maxey for a 3-pointer.

It was a pass that only James knew was coming, and it was one of eleven assists in Sunday's 131-124 win over Indiana that moved him past Hall of Fame point guard Jason Kidd for third on the NBA career assists list with 12,092.

"It's super humbling. It's something that I didn't obviously foresee in my career," James said. "But I knew assists meant a lot to me. Just the movement of the ball, being able to find my teammates and put the ball on time and on target."

James finished Sunday's game with 15 points, 11 assists and seven rebounds. The eleventh assist, which pushed James past Kidd, came in the fourth quarter on a pass to guard VJ Edgecombe. James has thrived as a passer against the Pacers this year, posting his top two assist totals of the season against them -- including 10 against Indiana on Oct. 28.

Throughout the night, James had a handful of highlight-worthy dimes. There was a no-look pass to a cutting Jaylen Brown that drew roars from the crowd at Xfinity Mobile Arena, as well as the pass to Maxey. James said that his new teammates adjusted to his passes by necessity, after James "hit them in the head a couple times," with passes throughout training camp.

"You can ask VJ [Edgecombe] for sure. I hit VJ a couple times," James said with a smile.

But he and Maxey have trained together in the offseason for the last six years, so they have a different level of chemistry, James said. But even Maxey wasn't sure that the pass in the first quarter was coming. He stood ready in shooting position but then dropped his hands as James rose before quickly adjusting as the ball headed toward him.

"I knew once I got his defender to stare at me, I was going to 'Rese," James said. "It caught 'Rese by surprise. He was like, 'Yo, I didn't even believe that you was gonna pass the ball.' So you know, I just tell my guys, I mean, 'Always just pay attention to me when I got the ball because I definitely see plays develop before they happen.'"

A few minutes before that play, Maxey and James had a failed connection. On the opening possession of the game, James threw a bounce pass to a cutting Maxey, who missed the layup.

"I missed at the rim because ... I was just jogging through and I was not playing to get the ball," Maxey said. "And the ball hit me in my chest, and I looked up; I was at the rim, so I laid in, and I missed it. But it's just like plays like that where you need to be ready at all times."

James could continue to climb the charts this season, as he is 461 assists away from passing point guard Chris Paul for second all time. The top spot, held by former Utah Jazz point guard John Stockton, is a ways away at 15,062 assists.

For the season, James is averaging 15.1 points, 7.2 rebounds and 6.9 assists per game. The Sixers play the Nets in Brooklyn on Monday. James hasn't played both games of a back-to-back this season.
