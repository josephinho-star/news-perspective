‘1000-Lb. Sisters’ star Amy Slaton’s ex-husband Michael Halterman arrested for child abuse

2026-11-27 15:38

Michael Halterman — the ex-husband of “1000-Lb. Sisters” star Amy Slaton — has been arrested for allegedly abusing a child, Page Six can confirm.

Halterman was booked at the Webster County Detention Center in Kentucky on Wednesday just after 1 p.m. and charged with fourth-degree assault involving child abuse.

The 43-year-old’s bail was set at $2,500, and he was still in jail at the time this story was published.

In Kentucky, the charge is considered a Class A misdemeanor — the state’s most serious misdemeanor classification — alleging that someone intentionally or wantonly caused physical injury to a child.

The base offense of fourth-degree assault carries a maximum potential sentence of 12 months in prison and a fine of up to $500. However, additional circumstances can affect the potential penalty.

Halterman and Slaton, 39, share two sons: Gage, 6, and Glenn, 4. It’s not currently known whether either was involved in the incident.

The former couple had a wedding in March 2019 after eloping two years prior in June 2017.

Halterman filed for divorce in March 2023, listing their date of separation as February 2023.

He asked for temporary joint custody, claiming “both parties have been in a caregiver role for the children since birth.”

The judge overseeing the case ordered temporary joint custody, telling the exes to remain at least 500 feet apart and communicate about parenting only through a court-approved app.

Slaton later claimed on the TLC reality series that she was eventually awarded primary custody, with Halterman getting supervised visitation.

After their divorce was finalized in September 2023, Slaton claimed on the show that their final custody arrangement stated she would have the boys 70 percent of the time and Halterman the other 30 percent.

The following September, the mother of two and her now-husband, Brian Lovvorn, were arrested at a zoo in Tennessee after marijuana and psychedelic mushrooms were discovered in their car, which her sons were also in.

The reality personality was initially charged with two counts of child endangerment and two counts of possession of a controlled substance but accepted a plea deal in December 2024 that led to the dismissal of the child endangerment charges.

Slaton received a suspended sentence and was ordered to pay fines. She also had to complete drug and alcohol as well as parenting classes.

Reps for Halterman and Slaton did not immediately respond to Page Six’s requests for comment on his arrest.
