Stream It Or Skip It: ‘Dig’ on Peacock, where Amy Poehler ends up in a hole with Hugh Laurie

2026-11-23 22:30

Buried at an archeological dig site is a treasure trove of laughs, right? Right?? That’s what Dig, a new ten-episode Peacock comedy from Amy Poehler and Michael Schur, intends to discover. Dig is based on the 2023 novel “Excavations” by Kate Myers.

DIG ON PEACOCK: STREAM IT OR SKIP IT?

Opening Shot: Through quavering VHS image lines and a 1983 copyright, we see the intro of a PBS-style show called Adventures in Time, where a young version of Dr. Neville Wycombe asks, “What is archeology?”

The Gist: Hugh Laurie plays Wycombe in the present day of Dig, where he still leads yearly expeditions to the same 12th-century BCE site, and staffs it with bright-eyed graduate students high on Wycombe’s academic mystique and the idea of partying in Greece for the summer. Elise (Poehler) was part of Wycombe’s digs as a student, and she’s here as an adult, serving as the professor’s chief of staff and heir apparent, if his ego would only get out of the way. Elise wrangles newbies like Patty (Fina Strazza) as she greets returning preservationist Clare (Antonia Thomas) and Dylan (Geraldine Viswanathan), another veteran of the digs with whom she has personal drama.

“How did I get to that point in my life where I think everyone works for me?” Elise wonders about the thought processes of the youngs volunteering at the site, and Wycombe warns her to be more gracious. (To us, the line also sounds like a topic launcher on a podcast, which might speak to the style of humor in Dig.) Beyond her gelling with new staff, though, Elise is also dealing with a potentially huge development in the field of archeology. What if their dig site actually uncovered something historic? And what if that discovery was stolen by the wrong people?

What Shows Will It Remind You Of? Parks and Recreation, Brooklyn Nine-Nine: With Poehler and Schur aboard, this is the kind of quirky ensemble and novel setting Dig is aiming for.

Our Take: A big hole in the ground? Treasures and history to be discovered? And a handful of the disparate and slightly eccentric personalities who are to be found gracing an archeological dig site? There’s GOTTA BE laughs in this dirt!

This seems to be the promise of the premise that Dig is working with, where we didn’t necessarily LOL initially, but we definitely understood the potential the series holds. It might take time for the laughs to be unearthed. But we feel like they are in there, especially as Poehler’s Elise builds around her with allies, and Dig lets Hugh Laurie lean into an egocentric goof version of his bad guy from The Night Manager.

Sex and Skin: There’s a lot of “Cool for the Summer” hookup tendencies swirling around with the entire staff of this Dig. Not to mention the arrival of a suitcase full of sex toys.

Parting Shot: In the middle of fighting about their own problems, Elise and Dylan make a tantalizing discovery. Their first thought? Don’t tell anyone else.

Sleeper Star: Dylan, her character, calls it her “chaotic energy,” and for us, former Decider Woman We Love honoree Geraldine Viswanathan is bringing much-needed amounts of that to Dig. In Viswanathan’s hands, Dylan is funny and unpredictable, a wild card we can trust.

Most Pilot-y Line: Claire has a way with a backhanded compliment. “Always was so whimsical! It’s like you’re from a fairy tale about neglected children lost in a forest!”

Our Call: STREAM IT! Dig might uncover a little more laughter as it ramps up its on-site archeological community and all the ways these people figure to mess with each other.
