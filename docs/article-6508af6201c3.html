Stock futures rise as Nvidia gains on earnings beat and solid outlook: Live updates

2026-11-18 10:49

Stock futures climbed Wednesday morning after the S&P 500 fell for a fourth straight day. Traders also weighed Nvidia's better-than-expected earnings report.

Nasdaq-100 futures were up 1%, while S&P 500 futures added 0.58%. Dow Jones Industrial Average futures were up 0.32%.

Shares of Nvidia were almost 3% higher in premarket trade, after jumping 4% in extended trading after the company's profit and revenue in the third quarter beat analysts' estimates. The company also lifted its revenue guidance for the fiscal year ending January 2028, calling for 75% growth year over year.

"The fact that they gave guidance is huge," said Gene Munster, managing partner at Deepwater Asset Management, on CNBC's "Closing Bell: Overtime." "That is a very bullish sign. I expect the stock to move higher based just on that alone."

Neocloud companies also rose in extended trading. CoreWeave advanced 8%, while Nebius Group gained 10%.

During the regular trading day, the Dow dropped 1.6%. The S&P 500 slipped 1% and notched its lowest closing level since July 29, while the Nasdaq Composite shed 0.7%. During the day, the tech-heavy index touched its lowest level since April.

Looking ahead, traders are awaiting building permits and housing starts data. The Federal Open Market Committee's minutes from its October meeting will also be out in the afternoon. Federal Reserve policymakers last month decided to keep the overnight funds rate in a range of 3.75% to 4%.

On the earnings front, Lowe's, Target and TJ Maxx-parent TJX Cos are slated to report Wednesday morning.

Later this week, investors are awaiting a blockbuster initial public offering from Anthropic. The AI giant's shares are expected to be priced above the current range between $70 and $78, multiple sources told CNBC's David Faber on Tuesday.

Across the Atlantic, stock markets were mixed on Wednesday, with the pan-European Stoxx 600 hovering just below the flatline in morning trade. The U.K.'s FTSE 100 was 0.34% down, with France's CAC 40 sliding almost 0.1%, but Germany's DAX advanced 0.15%.

Most Asian markets closed in positive territory after a choppy trading session. South Korea's Kospi led gains in the region as chip heavyweights gained ground, ending 1.99% higher at 5,067.44, while Japan's Nikkei 225 gained 0.28% to close at 59,024.28,

Hong Kong's Hang Seng Index was 0.87% lower in its final hour of trade, while the CSI 300 on mainland China advanced 0.18% to 3,983.77. Australia's S&P/ASX 200 slipped 0.69%, marking a fifth straight day of losses.
