South Korea rushes to reissue passports to President Lee, wife after data leak

2026-11-03 03:00

Police also launched an investigation into the breach that also exposed personal information of ministers and senior officials

“The foreign ministry is swiftly taking measures to ensure the smooth reissuance of passports for all affected individuals, including the president and first lady,” ministry spokesman Park Doo-soon said during a briefing.

Police have opened a preliminary investigation into the leak, which involved personal information belonging to the presidential couple, cabinet members, senior officials from the presidential office and the foreign ministry, security personnel and reporters travelling with the presidential delegation.
