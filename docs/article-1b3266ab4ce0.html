How Francisco Lindor, Mets teammates reacted to $450 million Tarik Skubal coup

2026-11-23 00:38

The Mets, including a pair of members of the starting rotation, were thrilled about Tarik Skubal’s free agency decision.

Francisco Lindor, Christian Scott and Zac Thornton all reacted to the news of Skubal’s new nine-year, $450 million deal Sunday, as Scott and Thornton — both projected to be starters in 2027 if there’s a season — both shared an SNY graphic on Instagram, and Lindor posted a handful of grinning emojis on his Instagram Story.

Scott also shared a GIF on X that captured Seinfeld characters celebrating.

The Mets were viewed as the favorite to land the two-time Cy Young Award winner, and they beat out the Dodgers and Padres, among other teams, to add another mega-deal to their payroll.

It marked the second time in three years owner Steve Cohen delivered the splash of free agency, as the Mets also signed outfielder Juan Soto to a $765 million deal in December 2024.

Skubal, who was dealt from the Tigers to the Dodgers ahead of the trade deadline and his looming free agency, pitched to a 2.72 ERA across 158 ⅔ innings last year, and his deal with the Mets set a record for the largest by a pitcher in MLB history.

The contract has $30 million in deferrals and doesn’t include any opt-outs or option years.

So wherever the 2027 season begins, with a lockout expected to start when the current collective bargaining agreement expires Dec. 1 at 11:59 p.m., the Mets will unveil a rotation that includes Skubal and Nolan McLean as their aces.

Scott and Thornton will also likely begin the season at the MLB level — pending any other moves by the front office — after promising 2026 campaigns, while Sean Manaea also remains under contract and prospect Jonah Tong remains a possibility.
