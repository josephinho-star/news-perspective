Get ready for Amazon’s October Prime Day: Big Deal Days 2026 dates, early deals

2026-09-15 05:00

It’s almost the prime time for fall discounts.

Amazon’s October savings event, Prime Big Deal Days, is back next month — and bringing plenty of can’t-miss sales across categories.

Whether you missed out on July’s Prime Day or have been waiting for markdowns on some of your favorite Amazon finds, it’s time to start making your shopping list. Read on for everything we know so far about the annual deal event.

When is October Prime Day 2026?

This year’s Prime Big Deal Days will run from Tuesday, Oct. 6 to Wednesday, Oct. 7. (Pro tip: The two days will go by fast, so we recommend shopping early to seize the best sales!)

Do I need to be a Prime member to shop Prime Big Deal Days?

You’ll need an Amazon Prime membership to access the biggest October Prime Day deals. If you’ve never signed up before, you can even unlock a 30-day free trial (and take advantage of other exclusive perks, including streaming everything Prime Video has to offer).

What competing Prime Day sales we expect

While many smaller retailers are saving their biggest fall deals for Black Friday, in the past we’ve seen big box stores like Target, Best Buy and Walmart offer concurrent discounts during October Prime Day.

What to buy during October Prime Day

It’s a great time to stock up on big-ticket items at a major markdown. Amazon’s own devices — including Echo speakers, Fire tablets, Kindle readers and more — are usually safe bets during the e-commerce giant’s sale events.

And here at Page Six, we’re of course always tracking the best makeup and skin care deals. In past years, several star-approved premium brands have reached Black Friday-level prices — making it an ideal time for a beauty bag refresh.
