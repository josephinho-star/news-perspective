Mitchell Robinson is off to troubling Celtics start after bolting Knicks for $47 million deal

2026-11-04 18:35

The grass hasn’t been greener for Mitchell Robinson, even if his jersey is.

The former Knicks center is off to an inauspicious start with the rival Celtics after bolting in the offseason for a three-year, $47.4 million pact.

Robinson is averaging 6.2 points but just 4.4 rebounds and 0.7 blocks per game through six contests with a disastrous minus-42 rating when he’s on the court.

There has been speculation that the 7-foot, 240-pound big man reported to training camp in underwhelming physical shape, according to The Boston Globe.

“None of us have met the expectations, myself included,” Celtics coach Joe Mazzulla said recently, per the outlet. “I have to be better, and it goes down the line. I like where Mitch is at from a mentality standpoint. I like his approach and his work ethic into doing the things that we’re asking to do.

“It’s obviously a little bit different, but none of us have met our own expectations, and we just have to keep working toward it. As long as we keep that mindset, not be distracted by the result, we got a chance. He’s somebody that takes pride in that process of getting better, and I’ve enjoyed working with him.”

The Knicks did not attempt to retain Robinson after he helped with the franchise’s first title in 53 years in June and the Celtics swooped in to add some needed size to their frontcourt.

Six games is obviously an extremely small sample size, but there have been some worrisome signs.

Robinson posted a minus-16 rating in Monday’s road loss to the Rockets and the Globe noted that he struggled on defense, “isn’t moving as well as expected” and not contributing on offense.

His teammates reportedly are having “major trouble” finding him for alley-oops.

Robinson’s points per game is actually slightly higher than his 2025-26 average, but his rebounds are notably down from 8.8 per game to 4.5 and his block have dipped from 1.2 to 0.7 per game.

The Celtics have a net rating of minus-18.8 when he’s on the court compared to compared to plus-19.4 when he’s off the court, per screwball.com.

The Knicks were better with Robinson on the court in each of the last two seasons per that metric, and his minus-38.2 net is the worst of his career.

“I would say for his offensive rebounding impact, we have him a little bit further away from the rim, setting screens and playmaking for us on the perimeter as part of our offense,” Mazzulla said recently, per the Globe. “I think that has a little bit to do with it.

“But as far as the two-man game, it’s just something to work on.”

The Celtics have managed a 5-2 start despite Robinson’s issues with their new system, and have a soft stretch coming up with three games against Milwaukee (twice) and Charlotte before a storyline-filled clash with ex-star Jaylen Brown and the 76ers next Tuesday.
