Multiple planes diverted amid bomb threats to commercial flights across Europe – live

2026-11-05 09:54

Two more planes affected by bomb threats, Italian media report

And the Italian news agency Ansa reported that two more planes with links to Italy were affected by bomb threats this morning: that’s a WizzAir service from Milan Malpensa to Warsaw, which reportedly landed in Vienna, and a Finnair flight from Helsinki to Rome Fiumicino.

This morning’s incident will once again raise questions as to whether there is any coordination behind the bomb warnings in recent days.

Four out of eight incidents in the last few days targeted planes flying to or from Italy: three to Rome – two from Dublin and one from Lisbon, and one from Milan Bergamo.

Flightradar24, one of the largest flight tracker websites in the world, also said that “no aircraft have departed from Rome Fiumicino Airport (FCO) in the past 30 minutes.”

The plane crossed the English Channel on a normal flight path to Rome before veering south-west of Paris and circling Chartres airport and diverting towards Évreux.

Curiously, in the last few minutes it flew right past the airport in Évreux without touching down.

Morning opening: Another bomb alert on a European flight

An Aer Lingus flight from Dublin to Rome has been diverted to a major military base in Evreux in France after a bomb alert, the French prime minister’s office said.

The PM’s office said the government was “monitoring the situation closely.”

The flight, EIN40G, is operated by an Airbus A320. It’s currently circling above France and should start its descent to Evreux shortly.

The incident comes just two days after seven planes across Europe were searched or diverted after malicious hoax bomb alerts.
