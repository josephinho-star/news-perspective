LSU's Fulwiley arrested after allegedly fleeing scene of traffic stop

2026-11-16 00:24

LSU guard MiLaysia Fulwiley, one of the ninth-ranked Tigers' leading scorers, was arrested after she allegedly drove her car into a police vehicle while attempting to flee from a traffic stop.

LSU's athletic department said in a statement that officials "are aware of the situation and have no further comment at this time as it's an ongoing legal matter."

The arrest, first reported by WBRZ TV in Baton Rouge, occurred Saturday night as LSU's football game against Texas was ending in sold-out, 102,000-seat Tiger Stadium.

Authorities allege that an officer tried to stop the 21-year-old Fulwiley after observing her weaving through traffic and that she drove away from the scene, the right front side of her vehicle striking a police vehicle. She allegedly sought to cut through a store parking lot before her vehicle was blocked by responding police.

Louisiana State Police said an officer suffered minor injuries during the incident.

Fulwiley was booked into East Baton Rouge Parish Prison on charges including aggravated battery, flight from an officer, aggravated assault and resisting an officer.

She addressed the incident in a social media post, writing that she "would NEVER in my life hit a police!"

"WHY would i hit a police and run knowing who i am and what i do!" Fulwiley wrote. "PLEASE DONT BELIEVE EVERYTHING YOU SEE!"

Fulwiley, who is averaging 17.8 points per game this season, scored a season-high 23 points during LSU's 101-37 victory over Denver on Friday.

LSU is scheduled to play at home Monday night against Troy. Fulwiley's availability for that game was unclear as of Sunday afternoon.

Information from The Associated Press was used in this report.
