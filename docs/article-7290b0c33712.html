Stock futures rise slightly as market tries to recover from Nvidia-led decline: Live updates

2026-11-11 03:58

Stock futures rose slightly on Tuesday night following a losing session in which Nvidia, coupled with hotter-than-expected inflation data, pressured the broader market.

Dow Jones Industrial Average futures gained 88 points, or 0.2%. S&P 500 futures climbed 0.2%, while Nasdaq-100 futures advanced 0.4%.

In Asia, Japan's Nikkei 225 traded 0.85% higher on Wednesday. South Korea's Kospi was up 1.22%, while Australia's S&P/ASX 200 added 0.23%.

Hong Kong's Hang Seng Index is down 0.66%, while the CSI 300 is down 0.39%.

The Dow pulled back more than 150 points in regular trading, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite shed 0.4% and 0.7%, respectively. At the center of the decline was Nvidia. The chipmaker pulled back 5.5%, as CEO Jensen Huang's affair with an alleged Chinese spy weighed on the stock for the second consecutive session.

Nvidia's two-day slide pulled the company's market cap down to around $4.07 trillion, its lowest since late March.

Equities also fell on Tuesday after the latest U.S. inflation data solidified expectations for a December Federal Reserve rate hike. The consumer price index increased by 0.4% month over month in October, topping expectations.

Expectations for a quarter-point rate increase next month rose to about 90% following the release, per the CME Group's FedWatch tool.

"Despite some technical uncertainty, higher than desired levels of inflation should keep the Fed in a stance of dissatisfaction with the degree of progress on inflation remaining lackluster, so hiking the Fed Funds policy rate in December is our and the market's base case," wrote Rick Rieder, CIO of global fixed income at BlackRock. "The question now is more of one of the extent of the hike."

Comments from Fed Governor Christopher Waller also led traders to price in greater chances of a quarter-point move.

Traders on Wednesday will keep their eyes on the artificial intelligence trade, after CoreWeave reported third-quarter revenue that beat analyst expectations. The report initially sent the neocloud stock slightly higher.

The U.S. bond market will be closed on Wednesday for Veterans Day.
