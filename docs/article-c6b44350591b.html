Pau Gasol, Vanessa Bryant shock world by placing bid on one of Mark Walter’s teams

2026-11-19 01:34

A potential suitor to purchase the Sparks has been found.

On Wednesday, Tom Friend of Sportsbusinessjournal.com reported that former Lakers star Pau Gasol and Vanessa Bryant are joining forces to place a bid for the WNBA team.

Friend reported that Gasol declined to speak on the matter through a spokesperson and said, “Gasol16 Venture is always open to exploring investments.” One of Bryant’s representatives said, “She has not personally had any conversations with the Sparks.”

Bryant denied the report and said “False” on the rumored bid that was reported by The Sports Business Journal.

“Another rumor out today… I’ve never had any conversations about being an NBA or WNBA team owner. If that was true, I would announce it,” Bryant wrote on an Instagram post.

“I’m just over here undecided on what seasonal coffee flavor I like and over caffeinated…😊😳”

The relationship between Gasol and Bryant dates back to 2008, when he and her late husband, Kobe Bryant, were Lakers teammates.

Kobe and Gasol were one of the NBA’s most dominant duos from 2008-2014, when they went to three consecutive NBA Finals from 2008-2010 and won back-to-back titles in 2009 and 2010.

The Bryants and Gasol remained close friends after their time on the court and after Kobe’s passing in 2020.

According to Friend, it’s unknown if the Sparks will continue playing at Crypto.com Arena or will look to play elsewhere.

A source told him that a second bidder might relocate the franchise to the Intuit Dome in Inglewood. An undisclosed group of investors that was put together by the women’s sports-centric management firm Swish Partners “would entertain moving the team.”

Friend added that new Padres owner Jose E. Feliciano and his wife, Kwanza Jones, are considered a third bidder to purchase the team but were unavailable for comment.

Feliciano and Sparks owner Mark Walter were once business partners when they had a stake in Chelsea FC.

The looming Sparks sale is just one of many sports enterprises to be added in the fire sale from Walter this past year.

Last summer, Walter shockingly sold the Lakers to former Disney CEO Bob Iger and entrepreneur Josh Kushner at a record-breaking $12.5 billion. He followed up the sale by moving off his shares of Chelsea FC less than a week later.

After Walter announced the Sparks were up for sale, Sportico reported on Nov. 2 that he was putting his Cadillac F1 team up for sale with an asking price of $3 billion.

Walter’s decision to offload pieces of his sports empire this past summer has raised some eyebrows. The Wall Street Journal reported that his name was dropped in a class-action lawsuit.

Walter’s empire, TWG Global, has ownership in motorsports, the Professional Women’s Hockey League and the Dodgers.

In an exclusive with Jack Harris of the California Post on Oct. 28, Walter expressed his commitment to owning the Dodgers and said, “We’re going to keep the team no matter what.”
