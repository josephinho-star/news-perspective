Worried about the pending blackout tomorrow? You can have this generator in time

2026-11-16 20:50

Is the world ending? We don’t know, but our Shopping section is still on Amazon (naturally).

With Nov. 17 approaching, concerns about a possible nationwide blackout are spreading across social media. For Americans who want to be prepared for unexpected power outages, there’s a home backup option worth checking out: the Westinghouse 12,500-Watt Dual Fuel Home Backup Portable Generator, available on Amazon with delivery as soon as tomorrow for eligible customers.

But first, what’s actually happening on Nov. 17?

Why is everyone talking about a Nov. 17 blackout?

Rumors of a major blackout on Nov. 17, 2026, have been circulating online, with some posts warning of widespread power and internet disruptions. The speculation has gained traction amid recent technology outages and renewed conversations about America’s critical infrastructure.

Customers of Chase, Bank of America and Wells Fargo were experiencing outages, with many reporting trouble accessing their accounts and checking balances. The disruptions came just days after major technical problems affected Microsoft services and nationwide air travel.

However, there’s an important distinction between online predictions and confirmed events. There is no verified announcement of a nationwide power shutdown tomorrow.

What is confirmed is that FEMA and the Federal Communications Commission have scheduled a nationwide Emergency Alert System test for Nov. 17 at approximately 2:20 p.m. Eastern Time. The test will briefly interrupt radio and television programming, but it is not a warning that the electricity grid will be shut down.

Still, unexpected local outages can happen because of severe weather, equipment failures and other emergencies. Having a backup power plan can provide valuable peace of mind.

This Westinghouse generator could be a timely backup option

For households looking to prepare, the Westinghouse 12,500-Watt Dual Fuel Home Backup Portable Generator is worth a closer look.

Available through Amazon, this portable generator offers two fuel options: gasoline and propane. That flexibility can be particularly useful during an extended outage when one type of fuel may be difficult to obtain.

The generator delivers up to 12,500 peak watts and 9,500 running watts on gasoline, providing substantial backup power for essential household needs.

It also features remote electric start, allowing users to start the generator using an included remote key fob rather than relying exclusively on a manual pull-start.

Its transfer-switch-ready capability offers another practical advantage for homeowners looking to connect backup power to their home’s electrical system through a properly installed transfer switch.

Can you *actually* get it delivered before Nov. 17?

Here’s the appealing part: Amazon’s listing may offer delivery as soon as Tuesday, Nov. 17, depending on the buyer’s location and available inventory.

That means shoppers who have been considering a backup generator may still have an opportunity to get one delivered before the date generating so much online attention (pun intended).

A little preparation can go a long way

Nobody should panic over unverified blackout predictions. But preparing for ordinary power outages is a sensible step for homeowners.

If you’re considering a portable generator, remember that it must always operate outdoors, far from windows, doors and vents, because generator exhaust contains deadly carbon monoxide. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions and use a properly installed transfer switch when connecting it to household wiring.

If you don’t mind waiting just a few days, this top-selling Jackery Solar Generator Power Station offers backup electricity when the regular power grid goes down.

Whether Nov. 17 passes without incident or your area experiences an unexpected outage another day, one thing’s for sure: having a reliable backup power plan can make a difference.
