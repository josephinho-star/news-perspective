Skip Starbucks: Score $200 off Breville’s viral espresso machine for Prime Day

2026-10-06 12:00

Ditch the $7 lattes and sip this deal.

October Prime Day 2026 is finally here. The shopping extravaganza, running from Oct. 6 through Oct. 7, is giving shoppers just a short window to score some of the best deals of the season on their favorite brands.

Plus, Prime members can get free same-day or next-day delivery on select items, so your order could be at your doorstep in no time. Among the top-rated appliances on sale is none other than the cult-favorite Breville Barista Express Espresso Machine.

This “barista-quality” espresso machine is nearly $200 off for October Prime Day and is loved by thousands of coffee shoppers for its smooth grinding experience, gorgeous stainless steel construction and range of features that give you precise control over your cup.

And yes, with its powerful steam wand and precise temperature control, it can make everything from lattes and cappuccinos to espresso and more. Some even rave that it’s “the last espresso machine you’ll ever need!” — and honestly, having one on your countertop is a statement in itself.

Both espresso novices and seasoned coffee aficionados have put this machine to the test — and loved it. Several reviewers say they’ve had their Barista Express for years, and it’s held up beautifully.

“It’s solidly built, looks fantastic and most importantly, delivers the kind of espresso quality I remember serving in my own café,” one reviewer shared. ” Add some good freshly roasted beans and this little beauty can turn your kitchen into a very respectable coffee bar.”

Better yet, Breville is throwing in two free bags of specialty coffee with the purchase and registration of any Breville coffee machine — which means you should be all stocked up to start brewing right away.

Your Prime Big Deal Days FAQs, answered

What is Prime Big Deal Days and when is it?

Prime Big Deal Days — fondly known as October Prime Day — is Amazon’s biggest fall sale, offering massive discounts before the holiday rush of Black Friday and Cyber Monday. October Prime Day runs from Tuesday, Oct. 6 to Wednesday, Oct. 7, 2026.

Prime Day vs. Prime Big Deal Days

Prime Day and Prime Big Deal Days are both Prime member-exclusive seasonal sales with massive discounts on rotating bestsellers. Unlike July’s Prime Day, Prime Big Deal Days runs every October as a precursor to the busy holiday season, allowing shoppers to start saving on gift shopping early.

Do you have to be an Amazon Prime member to shop?

October Prime Day is exclusively available to Prime members. However interested shoppers can grab a 30-day free trial to try Prime, and its many benefits with no strings attached, and gain access to exclusive deals and the convenience of fast, free shipping.

What are the best deals?

From reporting on Amazon sales for years, we can tell you for certain that Amazon’s October Prime Day is the ideal time to save on big-ticket tech, Amazon devices, gift-worthy clothing and beauty and skincare staples, household essentials and many more Amazon best-sellers.

Is it worth it?

Every Prime Day sale is a great chance to score deals on thousands of best-sellers, top tech and appliances and every day essentials, but Amazon’s October event also has great pre-holiday discounts on gifts. Already wondering when Amazon’s next Prime Day is? We expect Prime Day to return in summer 2027, but anticipate just-as-good (if not better) deals coming this Black Friday and Cyber Monday.
