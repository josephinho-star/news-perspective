Brian Hickerson has dropped 40 pounds since Hayden Panettiere’s death: report

2026-09-15 14:56

Hayden Panettiere’s ex Brian Hickerson has lost 40 pounds since the actress’ death, according to a new report.

Hickerson is allegedly “extremely heartbroken” and grieving over Panettiere’s Aug. 16 passing at 36, sources close to Brian and his brother, Zach Hickerson, told TMZ on Monday.

The outlet noted that the siblings’ recent outing — which ended in Zach’s arrest for disorderly conduct — was meant to get Brian out of the house.

That same month, Brian called his behavior “terrible” and confessed, “Never in my life did I ever get taught what domestic violence was, right? Maybe if I did at an early age, maybe I wouldn’t have done what I did. We don’t know. It’s one of those things you think about.”

During his scuffle over the weekend, Brian flung a chair, was dragged out of the establishment by another man and claimed, “GPD leaked it when my girlfriend died.”

Zach allegedly yelled obscenities and was detained.

He spoke out post-arrest, telling TMZ, “It’s just super frustrating all that we’ve been through and all the rumors that get started and then people believe them and then they attack as a result of the rumors.”

Zach added that it “pisses [him] off” when people “fill in” their own theories about the tragedy.

In Brian’s only statement on the tragedy, his lawyer wrote, “Hayden’s death remains under investigation and it is important to allow that investigation to proceed. As has been publicly reported, police confirmed there were no signs of foul play.

“Out of respect for Hayden’s loved ones and the ongoing investigation, there will be no further comment at this time.”
