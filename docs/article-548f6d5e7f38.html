Eyewitness gives the most detailed account of Travis and Taylor’s wedding yet: Cocktails, ‘castle’ and who did karaoke

2026-09-02 20:42

Two months after Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce said “I do” at Madison Square Garden in front of 1,000 of their closest friends, somehow not one picture has leaked.

While that’s testament to the couple’s ultra-tight security, one eyewitness has spilled previously unrevealed details from the celebrity wedding of the year (perhaps decade) to Page Six.

Our source told us, for the ceremony Swift made her way down the aisle from the arena’s sixth floor — in an area normally home to a concession stand — before changing into a shorter party dress for the reception.

The later festivities took place on the opposite side of the venue, where a “castle” had been constructed for the ceremony.

“The area where the ceremony was kind of small,” our source said. “They left the rest of the arena open, where people could walk around and sit down.

“There were areas to sit, there were food stations, there were bars, there were carnival games. I mean, it was a spectacle.”

Guests at the July 3rd event were treated to three specialty cocktails: “The Gym Teacher,” “The English Teacher” and “The T&T,” each served with its own signature ice cube.

“The T&T” — a tequila-based concoction — came with shot-glass-shaped ice, while “The English Teacher” concealed a flower.

Although the public have not seen any of it, the whole thing was captured by multiple camera crews — tantalizingly raising the possibility it could eventually surface.

“They had camera people running around, so I have to imagine, at some point she’s gonna release something,” the source told Page Six. “I hope she makes a Netflix special or something, because there’s no way I can describe it to you. It felt like you were at a movie set.”

While other outlets have reported the celebration eventually ran out of champagne, our source insists the bubbly — specifically Jay-Z’s Ace of Spades — was flowing in abundance all night.

During the reception, Swift took to the stage and called some of her famous friends up for karaoke. Ed Sheeran declined the invitation, but Stevie Nicks, Avril Lavigne and Paul McCartney all took their turns at the mic.

Swift herself called it a night around midnight, but the DJ kept the crowd dancing and the party continued until 4 a.m.

Swift and Kelce began dating in the summer of 2023, after the Kansas City Chiefs tight end famously revealed that he had tried — and failed — to meet the pop superstar at her Eras Tour stop in Kansas City.

Their relationship quickly became one of the most high-profile romances in the world, with Swift frequently cheering Kelce on from the stands at Chiefs games.

The couple announced their engagement in August 2025, and eight weeks ago transformed Madison Square Garden in the center of New York City completely for the wedding, but still managed to keep complete secrecy.
