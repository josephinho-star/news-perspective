Heroes, zeros from Giants’ win over the Cardinals: Jacoby Brissett faded when it mattered

2026-10-05 02:51

Heroes, zeros and the full blitz from the Giants’ 36-24 win over the Cardinals on Sunday:

Deonte Banks produced the first pick-6 of his career to seal the Giants’ third win in four games to start the season. The young cornerback picked off Cardinals quarterback Jacoby Brissett at the 2-yard line and returned it 98 yards for the clinching touchdown as time expired. It came only four plays after he was whistled for a questionable pass interference call that gave Arizona the ball at the Giants 4-yard line.

Jacoby Brissett started hot, but he faded as the afternoon went on. He threw three interceptions, including one right to Banks on the final play of the game. In the second half, the veteran threw for only 90 yards and was 11-for-20 passing.

Malik Nabers busted out, catching six passes for 112 yards and his first touchdown of the season. His 25-yard scoring grab was the first time he found the end zone in over a year, since Sept. 14 of last season. Nabers suffered a torn ACL on Sept. 28, 2025.

18 Years since the Giants started 3-0 at home.

“You know who else came through? Tae Banks. After a crazy pass interference penalty. I thought you had to touch the guy for it to be pass interference?”

— Giants coach John Harbaugh
