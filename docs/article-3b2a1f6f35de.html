‘Forgotten Island’ Comes to Digital, But When Will ‘Forgotten Island’ Be Streaming on Peacock?

2026-10-26 15:30

Looking for something to watch with your kids this week? Forgotten Island, the latest critically-acclaimed animated movie from DreamWorks, is coming to digital platforms to buy and watch online starting tomorrow.

Written and directed by Joel Crawford (who also directed Puss in Boots: The Last Wish) and Januel Mercado, Forgotten Island tells the story of two best friends in the Philippines in the 1990s, who get themselves trapped in the magical, mysterious island called Nakali. This would be cool, if it weren’t for the fact that there are bunch of demons living on the island who feed on the friends’ memories. They have got to get out of there before they forget everything!

The Forgotten Island voice cast includes H.E.R., Liza Soberano, Dave Franco, Jenny Slate, Manny Jacinto, Dolly de Leon, Jo Koy, Ronny Chieng, and Lea Salonga. Although the film was well-liked by critics, it didn’t connect with audiences in theaters, and earned only $63.2 million at the worldwide box office, failing to make back it’s estimated $80 million production budget. But perhaps the movie will do better on digital. That said, before you spend $25 on the movie to watch it at home, you should know that we do expect to see Forgotten Island streaming on Peacock for free soon.

Where to watch the Forgotten Island movie on streaming:

Beginning on Tuesday, October 27 at 12 a.m. Eastern Time (which is also October 26 at 9 p.m. Pacific Time) Forgotten Island will be available to buy and rent digitally on Amazon Prime, Apple TV, Fandango At Home, and more. The price may vary depending on which platform you use to purchase the film, but on Amazon Prime the film costs $29.99 to buy a digital copy, and $24.99 to rent. If you rent the movie, you’ll have 48 hours to watch the movie after hitting “play.”

Forgotten Island will also be released on DVD, 4K UHD, and Blu-ray on December 1, 2026.

Forgotten Island is not yet streaming on any subscription streaming services, but we do expect to see Forgotten Island streaming on Peacock soon, because it is a Universal movie and Peacock is the streaming service owned by Universal.

Peacock currently offers two subscription types: Premium with ads and Premium Plus ad-free. Peacock Premium costs $12.99/month, while Premium Plus costs $19.99/month.

You can save a bit by subscribing to one of Peacock’s annual plans, which give you 12 months for the price of 10. These cost either $129.99 with ads or $199.99 without ads.

Peacock Premium Plus is also available to subscribe to via Prime Video with a seven-day free trial that you can’t get by subscribing directly on Peacock.

When will the Forgotten Island movie be streaming on Peacock?

You might see Forgotten Island streaming on Peacock around late January 2027.

Forgotten Island streaming release date on Peacock has not yet been announced, but you will likely see the Forgotten Island streaming on Peacock later this year. Another Universal release, Disclosure Day, will begin streaming on Peacock about four months after the movie opened in theaters.

If Forgotten Island follows a similar release pattern, you might see the movie on digital in about a month, around late October 2026, and streaming on Peacock around late January 2027. That said, other Universal movies have taken just three, or even two months to come to Peacock, so you may see Forgotten Island on Peacock even earlier, around December 2026.

When will the Forgotten Island movie be streaming on Netflix?

You might see Forgotten Island streaming on Netflix around late May 2027.

Forgotten Island will likely stream on Netflix eventually, four months after it streams on Peacock, as part of Universal’s deal with Netflix that sends Universal animated movies, including DreamWorks and Illumination, to Netflix for a limited period. If our prediction for the Peacock release Forgotten Island is correct, then you might see Forgotten Island streaming on Netflix around late May 2027.

But this is all speculation. If you want to watch the movie right away, you can buy or rent the film online starting tomorrow.
