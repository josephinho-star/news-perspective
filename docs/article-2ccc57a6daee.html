Bella Hadid channels Britney Spears in sheer black lace at Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show 2026 after-party

2026-10-19 15:40

Gimme more.

Bella Hadid partied the night away after the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show 2026 runway, looking to the early aughts for her inspiration.

Channeling Britney Spears, the blond model slipped into a completely see-through black lace minidress with black underwear underneath for the after-party at the Bird Streets Club in West Hollywood.

The look was skin-tight, boasting long sleeves and a mock neck — just like the original Dolce & Gabbana look Spears memorably wore to the 2001 VMAs.

While Spears opted for more full-coverage boyshorts on the bottom and a balconette bra up top, Hadid leaned into her VS bombshell vibe, slipping into a barely-there string thong with her underwire bra.

She was spotted entering the party in the look, blond hair blowing in the wind behind her.

Hadid wore black pointed toe pumps and sunglasses, carrying a tiny metallic bag in one hand.

It was a big night for the 30-year-old model, who not only walked in the LA lingerie spectacular, but also opened the show.

She wowed in a totally floral winged look and later changed into an all-white outfit that included oversized pleated wings and matching swing dress over her lingerie.

On Sunday night, Hadid was joined by her sister Gigi on the runway, as well as Victoria’s Secret veterans like Ashley Graham, Alessandra Ambrosio, Adriana Lima and more.
