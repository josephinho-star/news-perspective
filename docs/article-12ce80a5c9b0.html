Not cooking? Where to get a great Thanksgiving meal in LA — for as little as $30.95

2026-11-20 19:19

While some of LA’s swankier restaurants are charging well north of $100 per person for the holiday, diners can tuck into a traditional turkey dinner with all the trimmings for a fraction of the price.

From a century-old Scottish pub to a beloved Burbank diner hungry patrons line up for their annual turkey fix, there’s no shortage of affordable alternatives to slaving away in the kitchen on Nov. 26.

Leading the pack is The Tam O’Shanter, the historic Scottish pub in Atwater Village that has been feeding Angelenos since 1922.

A neighborhood favorite for locals who prefer to leave the cooking to the pros, the eatery is dishing out a full holiday roasted turkey dinner for just $36. The feast includes stuffing, mashed potatoes, vegetables, candied cranberries, and turkey gravy. Meat lovers can add in thinly sliced prime rib for an additional $16, while candied yams will set diners back just $6.

Perhaps the biggest Thanksgiving steal in LA is hiding in Burbank.

Tallyrand, the beloved old-school diner, which has been around since 1959, is offering a holiday meal for just $30.95, complete with your choice of turkey or honey-baked ham, mashed potatoes, stuffing, gravy, cranberry sauce, sourdough bread, yams and pumpkin pie. A true holiday staple, Tallyrand reportedly roasts over 3,000 pounds of turkey every year for Thanksgiving.

Regulars warn that fans routinely line up for 45 minutes or more to snag a table. “My friends and I go there every year. Small groups, no reservations,” one diner wrote on Reddit. “Each year for our group of 3 has been about 45 minutes. We plan on it so we visit.”

Even in Beverly Hills, Thanksgiving dinner doesn’t have to come with a three-figure price tag. Lawry’s The Prime Rib, the legendary steakhouse on La Cienega Boulevard, is offering a $49 roasted turkey dinner that comes with its famous spinning bowl salad, mashed potatoes, stuffing, gravy and cranberry sauce.

Those unwilling to visit Lawry’s without indulging in its namesake dish can get two slices each of prime rib and turkey for $63.

Nothing says Thanksgiving on a budget quite like Norms. The beloved Southern California diner chain, which has been feeding hungry Angelenos since 1949, is offering a holiday feast that works out to as little as $20 per person for those willing to eat at home.

For $99.99, families of four to five can dig into either roasted turkey breast or brown sugar-glazed ham, along with mashed potatoes, stuffing, gravy, and cranberry sauce from the comfort of home.

For diners who want to skip the bird altogether — or just soak in a piece of LA history — the Original Pantry Cafe just reopened this week after a high-profile closure last year.

The legendary century-old diner at 9th and Figueroa has long been synonymous with big portions, fluffy pancakes, sourdough toast and no-frills comfort food. The diner reopened on Thursday to crowds of ecstatic fans who braved the rain to be among the first in line to get through the doors.
