Dinner hack: This under-$30 kitchen gadget makes meal prep almost too easy

2026-09-18 20:51

There’s one kitchen gadget that can make cooking a whole lot easier: a good immersion blender.

The Fresko Immersion Blender Handheld is one of the best out there, and it’s available on Amazon for 40% off right now, making it a great time to buy.

This under-$30 immersion blender has an anti-scratch design and a high-quality titanium-coated steel blade that offers corrosion resistance, according to the brand.

There are variable speeds available, too, allowing you to blend everything from delicate sauces to thicker, more heavy-duty mixtures. The handle itself is grippy, and the brand says its button is designed to reduce wrist fatigue over long sessions. It’s easy to clean, too. The head detaches for hand-washing — that said, don’t pop it in the dishwasher.

If you want the same blender with additional accessories, you can shop the Fresko Immersion Blender Handheld 4-in-1 version, which is seeing an even bigger sale on Amazon. It’s now a massive 57% off for a limited time.

This option comes with a blending arm, 24-ounce beaker, food chopper and egg whisk. The parts are again detachable for easy cleaning.

Whether you’re a home cook blending sauces and making smoothies or a new mom preparing baby food, Fresko’s immersion blender is a great product to buy and one that’s built to stand the test of time.
