North West cosplays Natalie Nunn for Halloween — and the ‘Bad Girls’ star responds

2026-10-22 13:57

Natalie Nunn was elated that Kim Kardashian and Kanye West’s daughter North West dressed as the Zeus Network star for Halloween 2026.

The “Bad Girls Club” alum took to Instagram Wednesday to repost the 13-year-old’s TikTok of herself transforming into Nunn — including adding a long prosthetic chin.

“I GOTTA GET @northwest her baddie chain now! ❤️❤️❤️❤️,” the reality star captioned the post. “SHE GETS A BADDIE CHAIN HAPPY HALLOWEEN MONTH!!”

In North’s TikTok, the teen raps along to Nicki Minaj’s verse on Lil Uzi Vert’s track “Endless Fashion,” in which the “Anaconda” emcee name-drops Nunn in the song by mentioning her chin.

“Ayo, even if my name was Natalie Nunn, you bitches still couldn’t chin-check me,” Minaj raps.

After North said that verse, she transitioned into Nunn, rocking a black sleeveless top that had “Zeus” written on it in white letters, a black leather pleated skirt, knee-high tights and sneakers.
