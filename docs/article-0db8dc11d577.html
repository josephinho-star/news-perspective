Watch: Cruise ship overturns after collision with tanker in New Orleans

2026-11-14 11:30

About 130 people were rescued from a cruise ship after it was struck by a tanker while docked in New Orleans, Louisiana on Friday.

Footage shows the American Heritage taking on water following the collision, which left 32 people in hospital with non-critical injuries.

"We have zero people unaccounted for, which truly is a miracle on the Mississippi," New Orleans Mayor Helena Moreno told reporters during a press conference.

The cause of the collision remains under investigation, with a possible steering fault suspected.
