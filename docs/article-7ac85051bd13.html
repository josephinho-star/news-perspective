Spencer Pratt: LA’s ‘Dumb and Dumber’ mayoral election was a humiliation ritual — and everybody lost

2026-11-06 23:36

In every election, there are winners and losers … except in the 2026 LA mayoral election: Everybody lost.

Clearly, Nithya Raman didn’t win the election, so much as Karen Bass lost it. And with the inept, stuttering and incompetent Nithya Raman ascending to the top spot at City Hall, we all lose.

We’ve lurched from an old-school incompetent communist activist to a new-school incompetent socialist carpetbagger; it’s a distinction without a difference.

Los Angeles needed radical change, not a change in radicals.

Many of my fellow Palisadians chose to endure an even more insufferable loss. As predicted, most of my neighbors held their noses and sacrificed their dignity by voting for the woman who let their entire town burn to the ground, only for her to lose anyway; this cynical political gambit was all for naught.

This election wasn’t an election … it was a humiliation ritual.

The reality for us in the Palisades, who endured Bass’ greatest failure, is that we represent but a fraction of a minority in Los Angeles, and nobody really cares about us. They all have their own problems, and their own reasons to hate Karen Bass and I heard them often throughout the campaign.

South Central hates Bass because she abandoned them in favor of criminal migrants. Boyle Heights hates her for the warehouse fire. DTLA hates her for the bums. Hollywood hates her for the restaurant collapse and water main breaks. When Raman presides over the same intractable issues in LA; they will hate her all the same. Everybody is a loser in this election.

Even Nithya Raman lost … she just doesn’t know it yet. She has taken the wheel of the Titanic, after the ship has already snapped in half.

Candidly, throughout my campaign, as I drove around LA and was fully immersed in the filth, blight and rot that has permeated the city, I couldn’t help but wonder if our problems were even fixable at this point. To a certain degree, there are so many issues that cannot be addressed by any mayor.

The rot is so deep, we need radical interventions — and we cannot do any of it without the help of the federal government, just as we did in the Palisades after the fire.

These daily water main breaks cannot be patched; we need the Army Corps of Engineers to rip up the entire 7,000 mile trunkline system root and branch. It will cost $25 billion and cannot be managed by sometimes-incompetent local or state employees.

We also need the FBI and DEA to do sweeping raids against the cartels operating in broad daylight throughout Skid Row and MacArthur Park. We need a surplus US military installation to house all the homeless drug addicts who haunt our streets; no number of “Inside Safe” initiatives can scratch the surface of the problem. We need to eliminate all of the illegal street vendor stands, citywide.

Nobody in Los Angeles has the capacity to fix these problems, and Angelenos don’t have the stomach to endure them, either. Even conservatives in Los Angeles are too squeamish to address these issues head on, even though they know it’s all destroying their city; they ultimately wish someone could just magically solve these things while they’re asleep, so they don’t have to see it.

And if even conservatives are too soft to do what’s required to salvage our city, then Raman’s communist base will go apoplectic were she to even sniff a policy that would effectively crack down on crime and blight; she will be relegated to simply making overpriced bike lanes and occasionally painting a crosswalk for her cringe TikTok videos.

Ultimately, I want Raman to succeed. I don’t want her to succeed with her socialist agenda, but I want her to succeed in cleaning up the city.

I can appreciate the fact that I am outnumbered significantly by the left wing in Los Angeles. The majority of Angelenos want leftist policy here, and we have to respect that.

A great first step for Raman would be to replace LAPD Chief Jim McDonnell with Assistant Chief Emada Tingirides. This would be a brilliant chess move that would not only satisfy the radical left base by elevating a black female, but Tingirides is not some DEI hire … she is a stud. She was one of the Top 3 names on my short list for LAPD chief, because she’s so highly respected within the department.

I’d like to say that I’m happy the Bass got fired, but I’m not. While there is some cosmic justice in seeing some shred of accountability, there’s not much to be happy about. Everybody loses in the Dumb and Dumber election.

We in the Palisades had no shot at getting justice with Bass in office; she was never going to get to the bottom of her greatest failure. But I have very little hope that Raman will care about us either.

The one upside with the election result is that, while Bass has thus far been “too busy” to answer to Congress for the Palisades fire … in six more weeks, she’ll have a whole lot of free time to go answer some questions.

I’ll even pay for her flight to DC.

Spencer Pratt is a former candidate for mayor of Los Angeles.
