‘Marshals’ star Luke Grimes admits he was ‘worried’ about doing ‘Yellowstone’ spinoff without on-screen wife

2026-11-17 05:42

Published Nov. 17, 2026, 12:42 a.m. ET

See more of our coverage in your search results.

Luke Grimes initially struggled to imagine his “Yellowstone” character’s future without his on-screen wife — and now “Marshals” is exploring whether Kayce Dutton could find love again.

Speaking to ScreenRant, Grimes revealed that reprising his role without Kelsey Asbille, who played Monica Dutton throughout “Yellowstone,” was one of his biggest concerns about the spinoff.

“Marshals” picks up after the events of “Yellowstone,” with Kayce and his son, Tate, grieving Monica’s death.

Speaking to ScreenRant, Grimes admitted that one of his biggest concerns before agreeing to a “Yellowstone” spinoff was doing it without Asbille.

“The thing I was most worried about doing ‘Marshals’ was doing it without Kelsey. That’s legitimate. Like I just couldn’t wrap my head around Kayce without Monica. That was the story. The rest of ‘Yellowstone’ was about people fighting over land, and I just loved my wife,” Grimes said. “I had my own thing going on, and so I didn’t know what that would even look like.”

Grimes continued, “But then once I sort of, you know, could wrap my head around the fact that that was going to open up all these new storylines, like, we just have to make sure we do honor that and don’t try to skip over it or pretend it didn’t happen.”

Now in its second season, “Marshals” is exploring Kayce’s growing bond with fellow U.S. Marshal Andrea Cruz, played by Ash Santos, raising the possibility of romance as he continues to grieve Monica.

Speaking to ScreenRant, Grimes addressed fans’ concerns, saying that they are meant to feel that way.

“They’re not wrong. They’re meant to feel that way. I’ve always loved a will-they, won’t-they kind of a storyline. I don’t know where it’s going, but they’re kindred souls, and I think the way that we’re sort of building that is really beautiful,” he began. “And you don’t see it much these days, where, you know, they’re forming a really amazing friendship first.”

One detail remains unresolved: when Kayce might be ready to remove his wedding ring. Grimes said he has discussed that moment with executive producer and showrunner Spencer Hudnut.

“But I will say he’s still wearing the ring, and … Spencer and I have talked about, like, when do I take it off? Are we going to make this a thing or is it just going to not be there one day? So we’re still trying to figure that out,” Grimes said.

“Marshals,” a “Yellowstone” spinoff, follows Kayce Dutton as he joins an elite unit of U.S. Marshals in Montana, drawing on his experience as a cowboy and former Navy SEAL to fight crime while balancing family responsibilities.

The series airs Sundays on CBS and streams on Paramount+.
