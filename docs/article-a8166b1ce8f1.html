Is ‘Survivor’ On Tonight?

2026-09-23 17:45

Grab your buff and a seat on the couch, because it’s almost time for the return of Survivor!

On the heels of Survivor‘s milestone 50th season concluding in May, Survivor 51—which starts the hit reality competition show’s “Open Era”—kicks off with a two-hour premiere. However, the fans shouldn’t prepare for that two-hour time commitment on a weekly basis. Episodes following the start of Survivor 51 will clock in at 90 minutes.

Survivor 51 welcomes 21 castaways to compete in a season that has earned “permanent uncertainty” as its catchphrase. If that doesn’t have you on the edge of your seat, I don’t know what will!

Wondering if Survivor is on tonight? Who is competing on Survivor 51? Here is what we know.
