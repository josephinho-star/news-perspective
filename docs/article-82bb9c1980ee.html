Mom died after BBL surgery from troubled ‘Lip King’ — having texted mid-op: ‘OMG it kills’

2026-11-18 19:40

Alice Webb, 33, collapsed and died in Sept. 2024 hours after getting the BBL procedure from Jordan James Parke, a man who bragged about spending more than $150,000 to look like Kim Kardashian, a court hearing into her death was told this week.

Parke was arrested on suspicion of gross negligence manslaughter — but then also died months later, before he could be charged, police told the inquest, according to the BBC.

Webb seemed “absolutely fine” the day of the $2,600 BBL surgery at a friend’s house in Gloucester — but started sending alarming messages mid-op, her sister-in-law Morgan Townsend said in court.

“She was communicating with me on the phone whilst she was having it done,” Townsend told the court, according to the Sun.

“She said ‘OMG it kills.'”

“The last message from Alice … was just one word – ‘painful.'”

Parke called emergency services to report the mom of five — whom he’d previously performed breast surgery and liposuction on — had “passed out” and “looks like she’s asleep.”

“She had basically had a procedure, a filler procedure, and passed out,” he was heard saying in the call played in court.

“She’s literally just had it done. She has had bum filler.”

Parke — who’d previously been banned by other UK towns from performing such surgeries — appeared “unbothered by the situation,” a responding paramedic, Allanah Kingston, said.

The only first aid he had on hand were bandages, a cop told the coroner.

The mom died hours later in a hospital, with a “gel-like material” found in her lungs, brain, heart, thyroid gland and kidney, pathologist Dr. Stephen Leadbetter said in court.

This could have been from damage to a blood vessel during the BBL procedure, Leadbetter said, while stressing he could not identify “a specific entry point.”

The inquest into the cause of Webb’s death is expected to continue for the next few days.

Parke, a social media influencer who also appeared on reality TV, was initially arrested after Webb’s death on suspicion of manslaughter but was not charged, an officer confirmed in court.

He died suddenly in February 2025 at the age of 34 after doctors had warned him that the dozens expressed concern about his plastic surgery habit, on which he reportedly spent hundreds of thousands of dollars.

It was not immediately clear how he died, but reports at the time said he had also recently had more surgery.
