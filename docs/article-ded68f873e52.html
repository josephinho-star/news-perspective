Hackers break into US Air Force cloud computing platform: Report

2026-11-27 13:54

Hackers breached the Air Force’s main cloud computing platform in recent weeks, risking sensitive data, according to a new report from Reuters, the latest in a string of cyber incidents and outages at government agencies.

Reuters reported Friday that according to three sources, hackers broke into the Air Force’s Cloud One platform, which hosts sensitive tactical, personnel and logistics data.

Two of the sources told Reuters the hack had been found in the past month or so, as the system was being upgraded. Reuters reported that it had not uncovered who the hackers are, how far they had gotten into the system and if data was swiped from Cloud One.

The reported hack follows other breaches in recent days.

The CIA said Tuesday that some of its unclassified systems were exposed in a hack. The Office of the Director of National Intelligence said its systems were also impacted by the breach.

The Treasury Department’s Financial Crimes Enforcement Network said Thursday it’s investigating an intrusion into some online systems.

Federal agencies have also been hit by outages that spurred widespread fears of malicious activity.

Last week, the National Security Agency faced a network outage, disrupting a classified internal network.

The Federal Aviation Administration issued a rare nationwide ground stop earlier this month, lasting for over four hours. Authorities pointed to a router in Arkansas and a Texas-based radar site as the two probable reasons behind the outage.

On Sunday, Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton said that Americans should get ready for upgrades to aging infrastructure and multiple systems. He advised that there would be “bumps in the road” in the wake of the recent tech failures.

“The American people should be prepared for a great deal of investment in upgrading our systems,” he told CNN’s Dana Bash. “They should be prepared for investment in cybersecurity, and they should be prepared that any time there is a step change in technology, a step change in both compute and applications, there will be bumps in the road.”

The Hill has reached out to the Pentagon about the reported Cloud One breach.
