Jenny McCarthy living in a tent while battling ‘paranoid’ fears after mold poisoning diagnosis

2026-11-01 22:00

Jenny McCarthy's two-year health nightmare left her physically exhausted, emotionally shaken and so fearful of mold exposure that she traded her home for a tent in a desperate attempt to heal.

The former TV host revealed that her ordeal began in 2024 and spiraled into a series of painful infections, surgeries and unexplained symptoms during an episode of "The Jenny McCarthy Wahlberg Show."

"Okay, I have been dying to tell you this story because the last two years of my life have been absolutely fricking insane. Insane, okay?" McCarthy told her viewers. "Some of you guys already know that I’ve been dealing with mold and mycotoxin poisoning. I’ve talked about pieces of it before."

McCarthy claimed the whole ordeal began in 2024 with a toothache.

"And somehow that led me down a rabbit hole of doctors, surgeries, teeth being pulled, mysterious growths popping up in places you are not expecting, implants literally falling out, and eventually realizing my immune system was fighting something so much bigger," she said.

McCarthy's health spiraled into a series of medical complications, including severe dental infections, repeated surgeries, jawbone damage and recurring eye cysts.

The former "The View" co-host also battled debilitating leg cramps, facial swelling, worsening food allergies and insomnia before undergoing testing that she said revealed mold poisoning. McCarthy claimed the condition had compromised her body's ability to fight infections, leaving her physically and emotionally exhausted.

"At one point, you know, I was just so desperate to heal that I literally was living in a tent," she revealed. "That’s right, living in a tent!"

After repeated procedures and inconclusive attempts to resolve her health problems, McCarthy said she experienced significant medical fatigue and became increasingly fearful of potential environmental triggers. She claimed she felt "like a freaking paranoid schizophrenic."

"I thought everything was poisoning me at this point, because look at me, I was a freaking hot mess, okay?"

McCarthy's functional medicine doctor eventually suggested testing her for mold after observing her repeated health problems. She initially dismissed the idea because she believed her home was clean.

Around Christmas, she said the doctor contacted her urgently with test results indicating significant mold-related concerns.

"[My doctor] was like, ‘No, I need to talk to you now, you better get on the phone.’ So I get on the phone with her, and she says, ‘Jenny, you’ve got serious mold. You’ve got the type of mold that causes seizures. If this thing crosses the blood-brain barrier, I’m going to be talking to you in hospital. You’re going to start seizing,'" McCarthy recalled the doctor telling her.

McCarthy said she underwent an extensive treatment regimen after being diagnosed with what she described as mold and mycotoxin poisoning. Her protocol included daily infrared sauna sessions, numerous vitamins and supplements, a powdered drink, nasal spray treatments and lymphatic castor oil packs.

"I’m still in my mold detox, you guys," she revealed. "I’m still in it, like, I’m knee-deep in it. I’m in month seven of detox, and I’m too paranoid to honestly live anywhere else other than a tent."

McCarthy said the physical toll was only part of the nightmare, admitting that the experience had left her struggling to shake the fear that mold was lurking everywhere.

"I'm still in the traumatic part. I'm not even in the post part yet because I feel like now everything is mold," she said. "I can detect mold honestly like a mold dog. Like I can walk in a building, I have to walk out of it immediately."
