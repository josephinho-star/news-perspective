Vanessa Trump marks major milestone with daughter Kai amid cancer battle

2026-08-31 14:38

Vanessa Trump helped daughter Kai shop for her new college apartment amid her cancer battle.

“I am totally exhausted from going to her apartment and coming back and forth and moving everything,” Vanessa said while the pair were at Target in Kai’s Instagram video shared on Sunday.

Kai, who just started her freshman year at the University of Miami, explained that her mom “drove to Miami for no reason yesterday” because the 19-year-old “messed up the furniture delivery.”

“It’s OK. It was during rush hour too, so it was fun times,” Vanessa, 48, sarcastically said, adding, “What we do for our children, right?”

Vanessa listed everything Kai needed from Target, including cleaning supplies, because the teen, according to her mom, will “be cleaning her own apartment.”

“No, I’m calling her down. ‘Hey mom, my bathroom is dirty,'” Kai joked, as Vanessa replied, “She is going to learn how to do that, or else it’ll just stay a mess.”

Kai captioned the video, “Back-to-school shopping with Mom! We spent HOURS shopping for everything I could possibly need for my apartment and getting ready for this new chapter of college life!”

She added, “So thankful my mom was there to do it all with me. She truly is the BEST mom, and I’m so grateful for her.”

“I am super excited to be a Cane and represent the University of Miami,” she wrote via Instagram at the time. “Gooo Canes!”

Kai is the eldest of Vanessa and Donald Trump Jr.’s five children. The former couple, who also share Donald III, 17, Tristan, 14, Spencer, 13, and Chloe, 12, got divorced in 2018.
