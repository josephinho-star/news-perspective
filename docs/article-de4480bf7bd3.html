Paige Spiranac admits she ‘dabbled in college’ when asked about dating women

2026-09-11 15:05

Things got personal when golf influencer Paige Spiranac and comedian Andrew Santino faced off in a 1v1 match play.

During her YouTube series, “Paige Meets Her Match,” Spiranac was candid on the topic of dating women during her college days at Arizona and San Diego State.

The conversation on the course took a turn when Santino asked Spiranac who she would choose to take back to her cabin at the Celebrity Invitational, under the condition that everyone is single.

“Sarah Winter … she’s the best looking one there,” Spiranac said of the Canadian golfer.

“Really? You’re a ladies lady. Have you ever dated a girl?” Santino asked.

“I dabbled in college … Hooked up,” Spiranac responded, adding that she didn’t seriously date women.

Asked whether she could date a woman, Spiranac said, “I think so.”

“I’m a huge fan. Women are fantastic, I get it.” Santino said. “… Guys are disgusting. Have you ever seen a guy naked out of the shower? It’s repulsive behavior.”

Spiranac and Santino played the front nine in Part I of the two-part special — and they placed a wager.

If Santino wins, Spiranac agreed to pay for the most expensive meal of his choosing at his restaurant.

If Spiranac wins, Santino agreed to have her as a guest on his “Whiskey Ginger” show under FanDuel.

They play the back nine to find out who takes the win in Part II debuting Sept. 17.
