When Does ‘The Summer I Turned Pretty’ Movie Come Out? Everything We Know About ‘TSITP’ Movie

2026-11-10 14:00

Get in, The Summer I Turned Pretty fans! We’re going back to Cousins Beach! Eventually…

It’s been over a year since The Summer I Turned Pretty‘s third and final season ended, and Belly (Lola Tung) and Conrad (Christopher Briney) fans finally rejoiced. But before viewers had time to process the series finale, Prime Video announced that the beloved franchise inspired by Jenny Han’s bestselling book series was expanding with The Summer I Turned Pretty: The Movie.

“…The global hit series The Summer I Turned Pretty is set to conclude its story with a feature film installation directed by Jenny Han, and written by Han and Sarah Kucserka,” a 2025 press release from Prime Video read, noting that the surprise news was revealed at the series finale celebration in Paris, France. No big deal!

While Season 3, Episode 11 was incredibly touching, fans who read Book 3, We’ll Always Have Summer, were let down by a major change to the ending and several omissions of popular book moments. But fear not, Han knows what she’s doing! She just needed more time and resources to bring her vision to life!

So when does The Summer I Turned Pretty: The Movie come out? When is TSITP movie release date? Is TSITP movie done filming? What’s the movie about? And who’s in TSITP movie cast? Here’s what we know about Han’s highly anticipated film.

When Is TSITP Movie Coming Out? The Summer I Turned Pretty Movie Release Date Info:

While an official TSITP: The Movie premiere date has yet to be announced, back in 2025, before Han wrote the script, she explained, “…We don’t know when it’s coming out, but not next year,” meaning not 2026.

Earlier this year, when asked for a release date update, star Gavin Casalegno said, “Hopefully it will be out next year,” so although fans were hoping for a very on-brand release timed to Christmas, it sounds like TSITP fans will be returning to Cousins Beach for the feature film in 2027 — and based on past release dates, Prime Video could be aiming for June or July. After all, it’s not summer without TSITP.

Where Is The Summer I Turned Pretty Filmed? Is The Summer I Turned Pretty Movie Done Filming?

According to Deadline, the movie began filming in Wilmington, North Carolina, on April 27, 2026. In the months following the news, as fans flocked to filming locations to witness the magic of Han’s world firsthand and safety concerns rose, Prime Video released a statement asking people to respect the privacy of the film’s cast and crew while they were on set. During a July 2026 interview, Casalegno confirmed TSITP: The Movie had finished filming, and though filming took place in North Carolina like the series, in mid-June, CBS News also reported that Tung and Briney were seen on the streets of downtown Plymouth, Massachusetts.

What Is TSITP Movie About? How Do The Summer I Turned Pretty Books End?

While specific TSITP details are still under wraps, when the film was announced in 2025, Han teased, “There is another big milestone left in Belly’s journey, and I thought only a movie could give it its proper due. I’m so grateful to Prime Video for continuing to support my vision for this story and for making it possible to share this final chapter with the fans.”

Those who’ve read TSITP books, particularly Book 3, We’ll Always Have Summer, know the Season 3 finale omitted several crucial scenes, storylines, and an epilogue. No spoilers here, but if you want some idea of what to expect from the movie and what that major milestone in Belly’s life could be, read our TSITP Book 3 ending explained article.

During a Teen Vogue interview in September 2025, Han also said, “Writing the movie has been actually joyful to me. I would say that I know that the fans want to see Belly and Conrad together as a couple, and I guess I could say that that’s something I can promise to them, and it’s partly why I’m doing it, is to be able to experience that with them in the present time.”

Aside from Conrad and Belly’s endgame, TSITP Season 3 finale also set up several new storylines and relationships that can be explored in the movie, including “Christmas in Paris,” Steven/Taylor/Denise’s move to San Francisco, a potential Jeremiah and Denise romance, Jere’s culinary career, Laurel and John’s connection, and more. So stay tuned!

Cast Of The Summer I Turned Pretty Movie:

We already know Tung, Briney, and Casalegno are in the film, and eagle-eyed TSITP fans spotted Sean Kaufman and Rain Spencer on location, so we know Steven and Taylor will be back. Jackie Chung, Colin Ferguson, and Tom Everett Scott will likely return as Laurel, John, and Adam. And while there’s no confirmation yet, there’s also potential for new cast members and returning characters like Denise, Redbird, Anika, Agnes, and Cam Cameron. Stay tuned for updates!
