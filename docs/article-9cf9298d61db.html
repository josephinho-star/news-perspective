Fake 49ers player Daejon Love texts with alleged victims revealed: ‘A lot of darkness’

2026-08-31 17:43

The Daejon Love story continues to unfold as female victims have started to speak out against him.

One of Love’s alleged victims, Karma Rose, took to her Instagram account @jaderisetwhloha and spoke about her interaction with Love and how she believes there is more to this story than him scamming women out of money.

“I know everybody is joking about these things with Daejon and how he pretended to be a 49ers [player] but there’s really a lot of darkness behind that,” Rose said on Instagram.

“He definitely tailored to each individual female that he interacted with, and decided what he was going to be benefiting from, whether it be unconsensual sexual gratification that gets met.”

Love is accused of scamming at least 26 women out of $1.3 million, but Rose went on to say she believes the number is higher.

“People’s physical, mental, emotional health was taken for granted,” Rose said. “Like s—t was really going on that can affect somebody’s daily life. People could be losing their lives over things like this.”

After the video ended, Rose revealed a screenshot of text messages exchanged between the two, where he said he was on his way to Oregon.

In the final part of the Instagram thread, she released a video of him speaking to her, where he said he had just finished eating dinner and told her, “In all seriousness, I don’t think we’re all on the same page.”

Before Love could reveal his reasons, the video was cut short.

The X account @LakeShowYo separately posted a photo of a text message thread between Love and another victim, who was accusing him of not actually being in the NFL.

The alleged victim asked him to send her his highlights from college to prove that he was real, to which Love replied, “I got hurt my college career. Only had one year. Then killed it at a private tryout.” He went on to suggest that he could show her some of his “team-issued” 49ers gear.

The woman would go on to argue with Love, calling out the fake 49er for having “no proof” of having a football career.

To which he replied, “Why lie about playing football?”

This story only continues to deepen as more people come forward.
