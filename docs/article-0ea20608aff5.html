Former Disney heartthrob proves he’s still got it with shirtless display amid legal troubles

2026-11-04 18:59

A ripped Erik von Detten was spotted putting up Christmas decorations outside his California home during a rare sighting on Wednesday.

The former Disney star bared it all, going shirtless as he hung up Christmas lights and climbed on his roof to secure the adornments.

In other photos, the hunky 90s heartthrob was seen talking on the phone in the sun, showing off his rock-hard abs and shredded chest.

The “Princess Diaries” actor was also pictured driving his golf cart, with his muscular arms on full display for the entire neighborhood to see.

The rarely-seen star was last spotted in 2024 at the premiere of the movie “Junction” in Los Angeles.

The recent sighting comes as he is being sued for alleged elder abuse and fraud. The lawsuit was filed by Norman Greenberg, 97, and Sheila Greenberg, 85, who claimed that they suffered $900,000 in damages while working with von Detten and his former employer, Rosland Capital, per TMZ.

The company, which bought and sold precious metals, is now in bankruptcy. The couple claimed that Rosland Capital exploited elderly people’s fears by trying to sell them on the stability of gold.

The “Leave it to Beaver” star worked at the company as a senior account representative and sales agent.

The couple claimed to have spent around $2.1 million on coins allegedly worth only $1.3 million.

Von Detten stepped away from the spotlight in order to make more money to support his large family, seemingly by working for Rosland Capital.

His last role was in the 2024 Lifetime movie, “My Acting Coach Nightmare,” where he played Mark, the boyfriend of lead character Lisa, played by Samaire Armstrong.
