Meghan Markle spotted in UK for first time since bombshell move with Prince Harry

2026-09-09 14:54

Meghan Markle broke cover in the Cotswolds on Friday, Page Six can confirm.

We hear the Duchess of Sussex had tea with what looked to be a fellow mom at Soho Farmhouse, a members-only retreat.

The outing marked the first time the “Suits” alum, 45, has been spotted in the United Kingdom since moving back with husband Prince Harry.

“She seems to be starting to socialize and going out and about,” a source told us, noting that “lots of the moms go to [Soho Farmhouse] to relax and unwind at the end of the week.”

The 100-acre club, notably, has a spa, 40 guest cabins and an indoor-outdoor pool.

The countryside hotspot is star-studded, with David Beckham, Victoria Beckham, Paul McCartney and more celebs regularly frequenting.

Markle’s rep has yet to return Page Six’s request for comment.

The “Deal or No Deal” alum’s Cotswold sighting fuels speculation that she and Harry, 41, are house hunting in the posh UK area.

Last month, Page Six reported that the Duke of Sussex knows the area “very well.”

“I was told by a friend of Harry’s earlier this year that he was going to go house hunting and potentially in the Cotswolds near [King Charles and Queen Camilla’s] Highgrove [House residence],” royal commentator Tom Sykes exclusively told us in August.

“Obviously, that’s where he grew up, living with his father and brother there, and has happy memories.”

As Page Six previously reported, Harry and Markle arrived in England with their two kids — Prince Archie, 7, and Princess Lilibet, 5 — two weeks ago.

Last week, we heard a school warned parents in the area about how “from time to time, some families joining the school may attract a greater level of public or media interest.”

The message reminded readers “not to photograph or film other children without permission, or share photographs, information or details about pupils or their families on social media,” per royal commentator Kinsey Schofield.

The note was not sent by the school Archie and Lilibet will attend as their parents “approached multiple” places before making their selection, ultimately choosing a school Page Six has decided not to name for privacy reasons.

The young royals have not been seen since the bombshell move, but Harry surfaced for the first time in a photo at Tower Bridge Studios in London on Monday.
