Republican senators criticize Nvidia’s decision to reinstate Jensen Huang

2026-11-23 21:09

Republican lawmakers roundly criticized the Nvidia board’s decision to reinstate chief executive Jensen Huang, even after Donald Trump said he spoke “directly and indirectly” with board members and “convinced them” to bring him back.

Pete Ricketts, a US senator from Nebraska, wrote on X that Huang’s “behavior does not support reinstatement”.

Ricketts, who sits on the Senate foreign relations committee, said Huang still needed to testify before Congress – and Nvidia still needed to answer questions about national security concerns.

John Curtis, a senator from Utah on the Senate commerce committee, noted that Nvidia’s board relied on information from a government official to reinstate Huang.

“Nvidia’s board says it relied on input from senior US officials to conclude that bringing Jensen Huang back posed no national security risk … Who in our government weighed in? What did they tell the board? And on what basis did the board conclude there was no risk?” Curtis asked.

John Cornyn, an outgoing senator from Texas, posted several times on social media, questioning whether the FBI conducted a defensive briefing on Huang, whether that information was ignored, Nvidia’s corporate governance, and more.

Tim Scott, the Republican senator for South Carolina who heads the Senate banking committee, set a date of 3 December for Huang to testify before the panel.

According to a notice sent by the committee, Huang is scheduled to be the only witness at the hearing, called “Protecting America’s AI Advantage: Oversight of US Export Controls”.

A chorus of Democrats joined with Republican criticizing Huang’s return to head the company.

Elizabeth Warren, a senator for Massachusetts who is likely to be the Senate banking chair when Democrats take control of the chamber in January, called the decision “reckless” and urged legislation to ensure stronger chip export controls.

“Jensen Huang’s return only increases the urgency of passing bipartisan export control bills into law and making sure Nvidia does not lobby to kill that legislation and put our national security at risk,” Warren said.

Warren’s call was echoed by Senate Democratic leader Chuck Schumer.

Hakeem Jeffries, the incoming speaker of the House, said that Congress should include “ironclad” legislation in the annual defense bill to prevent AI technology from reaching China.

The lawmakers’ comments came as Trump said on CNBC that he directly influenced the decision.

“I’ve been speaking to the board directly and indirectly, and I felt very strongly about it,” Trump said. “And I felt more strongly as I saw what was happening with the company, because that was, to me, a very important part of our country.”

He added: “I convinced them.”

Huang resigned from Nvidia earlier this month after reports of extramarital affairs including with a woman who US intelligence believes to be a Chinese spy. When the chipmaker announced on Monday that he had been reinstated, Nvidia shares jumped 33%.

The president said in the CNBC phone interview that the company’s board asked for assurances on national security risks, which Trump said the administration gave them.

“They did ask for assurance on the national security threat,” the US president said. “And we gave him assurance at least with everything we know, and we know a lot.”

Trump also said he spoke with Huang, with whom he has a close personal relationship, on Sunday night, before Nvdia’s board announced his reinstatement.

He said that it would be a “be a far bigger threat” to lose Huang, and that the board had “acted really responsibly” in deciding to reinstate him. Trump added that he was “very, very happy” that the company “had the courage to do the right thing”.

After his announcement, Trump posted a photo on Truth Social depicting Huang prevailing by holding up a red bra.

The image appeared to be an altered version of the famous photo of Trump holding up his fist after surviving an assassination attempt in 2024, which he and many supporters have used as a symbol of defiance. In this version, Huang’s face is superimposed over Trump’s, the Nvidia flag is superimposed over the American flag, and Huang is holding a red bra in his raised fist.

Edward Helmore contributed reporting
