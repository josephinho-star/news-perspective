Giants’ Greg Newsome II thinks he has what it takes to keep Goliath Cowboys receiver tandem in check

2026-09-05 23:54

The best way to straighten out the side-eyed reactions to Greg Newsome II claiming the Giants have “the best cornerback room in the league” is to prove it against arguably the NFL’s best receiver duo.

And that’s the season-opening challenge that the Cowboys’ 2,500-yard pairing of CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens poses Sept. 13.
