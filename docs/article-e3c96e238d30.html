Rep. Nancy Mace calls for ‘serial killer’ mom Lindsay Clancy to be ‘publicly executed’ in a ‘warning to women’

2026-09-15 10:46

“Her children are dead, she should be dead too,” Mace, a 48-year-old mother of two, told TMZ on Monday about Clancy, who is likely facing a new trial for allegedly killing her three young kids after her last trial ended in a hung jury earlier this month.

“She should get the death penalty. It should be public. It should be a public execution,” Mace said, saying she “deserves nothing less.”

“It could be by firearm, it could be electric chair, I don’t really care,” the outgoing lawmaker added. “She’s a serial killer.”

Mace later said such an execution would serve as a “warning to women everywhere — you don’t kill your kids.”

Her defense team had argued that she should be found not guilty by reason of insanity. Eleven jurors agreed — but one holdout kept them from a unanimous decision.

“It’s a very sad situation,” Trump told reporters Sunday. “I certainly heard that, but look, it’s very sad. There is no winner there. There’s no win no matter what you do. Three children are dead.”

Mace blasted Reddington’s plea as a “nasty, ugly” publicity stunt.
