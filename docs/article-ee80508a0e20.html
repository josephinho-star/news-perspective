Proud gamer girlfriends prep GTA 6 gift baskets for boyfriends — complete with Vaseline, deodorant and energy drinks

2026-11-18 19:25

After 13 years of waiting, millions of husbands and boyfriends are just hours away from being permanently glued to their screens, and proud girlfriends are celebrating with bizarre gifts.

Across social media, girlfriends are showing off surprise “GTA VI survival baskets” filled with snacks and energy drinks to fuel the couch potato gaming session.

Hygiene essentials like deodorant and dry shampoo are also tossed in so gamers won’t have a single reason to get off the couch.

From custom game covers featuring what appear to be AI-generated pictures of couples, to toy cars and PlayStation gift cards, the viral care packages have sparked a huge debate over who’s a thoughtful girlfriend and who’s a so-called “pick me.”

TikToker @armoneey__ racked up over 10,000 likes on a GTA 6 basket for her man, which included a custom GTA game cover of the couple alongside snacks, energy drinks, a PlayStation gift card, dry shampoo, deodorant, and Liquid I.V. for hydration.

Commenters called her a “bird” and left remarks like “chirp chirp,” referring to slang describing “a woman who will give up her self-dignity for the purpose of supporting a man or being with a man.”

Fellow TikTok user Madisyn Kuehn also shared her care package, complete with a pack of Hot Trucks toy cars for her boyfriend, Vaseline, and an AI-generated custom cover depicting the pair.

“This is your sign to create a GTA 6 release basket for your boyfriend! Fill it with some snacks or treats, maybe a few things he’s been needing, and then, of course, a gift card for the game,” Kuehn captioned her post.

“Getting all this for him to cheat on you with Lucia,” one TikTok user commented on a gift basket video, referencing the game’s female protagonist. “No man deserves this,” another snarked.

“Why are we making them a basket when they are going to be on the game, not spending time with us?” another girlfriend questioned on TikTok. “Maybe they need to make us one.”

Instagram comedian Darren Conway posted a reel to his 144,000 followers, admitting he keeps seeing GTA baskets online and wants to know how to score one for himself.

“Nah, I’m a grown man so I bought myself the game — a new controller and a new headset,” one follower replied.

Many social media users insist these thoughtful partners deserve engagement rings. TikTokker @LILEVIEX posted a video assembling a GTA basket, adding a matching pajama set “because he deserves to be nice and comfy while he plays.”

She also threw in a fresh pair of Nike socks, two packs of cookies, cologne, shower gel, a face mask, and more.

“He needs to put a ring on your finger,” one commenter insisted.

After a grueling 13-year wait, Rockstar Games’ blockbuster drop is set to smash records as one of the biggest media launches ever — racking up an eye-popping $1.5 billion before even touching store shelves.
