Jax Taylor reacts to backlash over cancer diagnosis after ex Stassi Schroeder’s shady reaction

2026-11-24 19:41

Jax Taylor is asking for grace to “demonstrate growth and change” amid his medical issues, following a shady reaction to his cancer diagnosis from ex-girlfriend Stassi Schroeder.

“Jax is aware of the noise online but is choosing to tune it out and focus on his health, his son and the people who have shown up for him,” a source close to the “Vanderpump Rules” alum — who shares 5-year-old Cruz with estranged wife Brittany Cartwright — told Page Six exclusively.

“Jax is fully aware that his past has given people reasons to question him,” our insider continued. “But mocking someone’s cancer diagnosis says far more about the character of those doing it than it does about him.”

Our informant noted that this coming Saturday, Nov. 28, will mark two years since Taylor, 47, got clean and sober, wondering, “At what point do people stop holding him hostage to his past and allow him the opportunity to demonstrate growth and change?”

“You don’t have to like someone to have compassion for what they’re going through,” our source argued.

The “Valley” alum — who is navigating a contentious divorce with Cartwright — has been “very intentional about who he keeps in his life” over the past year, with our insider adding that he is “recovering well and getting stronger each day” following his recent operation.

Taylor disclosed his “private” health battle last week, telling his Instagram followers he “ended up in the hospital with kidney stones” back in July, at which point a “CT scan also showed a tumor on [his] left kidney.”

After “many tests and appointments,” Taylor said he was told he had cancer, which “broke” him because he lost his father to stage 4 esophageal and throat cancer in December 2017.

“It put me in a really dark place. The hardest part was the waiting. Knowing there was a tumor growing inside me while trying to go about my life like everything was fine,” he confessed, noting he had just undergone a “long, five-hour surgery” to remove the tumor, which was “a lot larger” than his medical team initially thought.

At the time, the former Bravolebrity explained he was “waiting on the pathology results” to determine whether his “incredible surgeon,” Dr. Michael Ahdoot at Los Angeles’ Cedars-Sinai Medical Center, “got it all.”

“If I hadn’t ended up in the hospital with that kidney stone, I probably wouldn’t have known this tumor was there until it was too late. I like to think my dad had a part in that,” Taylor reflected as he gave his “amazing girlfriend,” Lori Krebs, a shout-out for being “by [his] side” through it all.

Days later, Taylor returned to Instagram to elaborate on the surgery, saying he “underwent a robotic-assisted left partial nephrectomy to remove a 4.1 cm cancerous tumor from [his] left kidney,” which was “classified as clinical stage I (T1b) kidney cancer.”

He added that the pathology results, which he expected to receive in “about a week,” will “confirm the exact type and characteristics of the tumor, its final stage and whether the surgical margins are clear.”

Beneath that post, Krebs, 42, commented, “So strong ❤️.”

Over the weekend, Schroeder — whose tumultuous relationship with Taylor was well-documented on “Vanderpump Rules” — told E! News her response to his cancer reveal was “one of confusion.”

“I’m not here to doubt somebody’s diagnosis. I hope the best for everyone involved,” she insisted, replying with a quick “no” when asked whether she had a message for her former boyfriend.

Soon after, Schroeder, 38, was asked by a paparazzo whether she had any “well wishes” for Taylor, but she laughed off the inquiry and walked away.

Meanwhile, Taylor and Krebs — who previously worked as his and Cartwright’s publicist — were photographed enjoying a casual date at popular shopping center The Grove. Taylor appeared in good spirits as he put his arm around Krebs and kissed her forehead while browsing a store. The couple later grabbed coffee.
