Turkey arrests 5 over alleged past links to al-Qaeda

2026-09-01 14:24

A Turkish court has arrested five people over alleged past involvement with al-Qaeda following a police operation in the northwestern province of Kocaeli, the state-run Anadolu news agency reported on Tuesday.

Al-Qaeda-linked militants have carried out major attacks in Turkey, most notably a series of suicide bombings in İstanbul in November 2003. The attacks targeted two synagogues, the British Consulate General and the headquarters of HSBC, killing dozens of people and injuring hundreds.

In the latest operation police detained eight suspects during simultaneous raids on August 28 as part of an investigation into the militant group’s activities, according to a statement from the Kocaeli Police Department cited by Anadolu.

Police said the suspects were believed to have been involved with al-Qaeda in the past but provided no details about their alleged roles, when they were active or whether they were suspected of planning an attack.

After questioning, the eight suspects appeared in court. Five were arrested pending trial, while three were released under judicial supervision.

The operation followed a larger crackdown on alleged members of al-Qaeda and the Islamic State in Iraq and the Levant (ISIL) in Kocaeli in late December.

Police detained 42 suspects in simultaneous raids on December 29, 2025. Eighteen were subsequently arrested, three were released under judicial supervision and one was released after questioning by prosecutors. The remaining 20 were transferred to immigration authorities for deportation proceedings.
