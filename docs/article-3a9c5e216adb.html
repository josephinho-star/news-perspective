Suspected lioness sighting prompts search in northern İstanbul

2026-11-23 11:33

İstanbul prosecutors opened an investigation Monday after a resident reported seeing a lioness in the city’s northern Sarıyer district, where wildlife officials and security teams were working to capture the animal alive.

The İstanbul Chief Public Prosecutor’s Office is trying to determine who kept the animal and where it may have escaped from. Its owner had yet to be identified, the Cumhuriyet daily reported.

The search began Sunday evening after a resident called emergency services to report seeing a lion in Uskumruköy, a neighborhood about five kilometers (three miles) from the Black Sea coast.

The İstanbul Governor’s Office said the animal had appeared on security cameras at around 6 p.m. Officials who reviewed the footage said it appeared to be a lioness.

The Directorate General of Nature Conservation and National Parks sent in teams equipped with thermal imaging cameras after an initial sweep found no animals, Agence France-Presse reported.

“Following operations in a wooded area, the animal’s location was identified. Efforts to capture it are continuing, with a team that includes specialist veterinarians on site,” the directorate said on X, according to AFP.

The governor’s office said “the necessary care is being taken to capture it alive.” Gendarmes warned nearby residents to be cautious, and authorities restricted access to the area around the woods. Authorities had yet to announce a capture as of Monday.

In a separate incident in July 2025, a male lion escaped from a wildlife park in the southern province of Antalya and seriously injured a farmer before it was shot dead.

Agence France-Presse, with reporting from Turkish Minute
