Hailey Bieber’s beloved Rhode blushes are now available in mini sizes

2026-09-16 14:50

Don’t make us blush.

Rhode just released the cutest minis of its cult-favorite blushes, and they’re sure to sell out quickly (in fact, one shade is already gone after yesterday’s drop).

The collection, which marks Rhode’s first foray into a shimmer finish for its creamy Pocket Blushes, is part of a collaboration with F1 WAG and influencer Alexandra Leclerc. But anyone can get into the cute-as-a-button colors that can be layered over other blush or used alone for a sheer flush.

Oh, and they’re just $16 and only available through Rhode.

The new drop is currently available in two shimmery shades: Monet, a fall-ready apricot hue, and Monaco, a terracotta.

Made with peptides and tamanu oil, the cruelty-free range is known for the modern, natural beauty looks that are practically synonymous with the brand’s founder, Hailey Bieber.

The model and entrepreneur famously sold her company for one billion but remains an active chief creative officer for the beauty label.

The chubby sticks have become legendary for their long wear and dewy finish.

Alas, while there was a cute carabiner to hang the blushes from your bag, that also sold out within 24 hours, as did the matching Peptide Lip Tint. However, there are still plenty of shades to shop that will keep lips hydrated as the seasons change.

If you’re looking to score one of these cutie blushes — which are so perfect to slide in any bag — don’t delay. They might not make it another 24 hours.
