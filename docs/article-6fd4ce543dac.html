Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce ride off in style in vintage Cadillac after Chiefs game

2026-11-08 23:48

Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce made a stylish getaway from Arrowhead Stadium in a vintage Cadillac after the Kansas City Chiefs faced off against the New York Jets on Sunday.

The newlyweds were spotted leaving the stadium in a cream-colored classic car featuring a contrasting brown roof, chrome detailing and whitewall tires, according to video shared on social media.

Kelce, 37, appeared to be behind the wheel of the luxury ride, sporting dark sunglasses as he drove away from the venue.

Swift, who appeared in the passenger seat, was still wearing her red-and-pink striped Tommy Hilfiger sweater polo that she wore during the game.

The couple’s latest joyride was reminiscent of their first public date in September 2023, when the NFL star famously whisked the pop superstar away from Arrowhead Stadium in his burgundy 1970 Chevrolet Chevelle SS convertible.

At the time, Swift, 36, had attended her first Chiefs game to cheer on her then-rumored beau before the pair headed to a Kansas City restaurant Kelce had rented out for his teammates and their partners.

A similar vehicle even made an appearance at the couple’s lavish July wedding at Madison Square Garden.

Page Six exclusively obtained footage of a 1970 Chevrolet Chevelle SS convertible featuring a customized New York license plate reading “JST&T MRD” outside the iconic venue the day after their nuptials.

The burgundy muscle car matched the one Kelce drove during the couple’s first public outing nearly three years earlier, and was later offered as a prize in a reception raffle.

The tight end previously gushed about his collection of “old school” convertibles during a September 2023 episode of his “New Heights” podcast, just days before his romance with Swift became public.

“That thing sounds so mean coming around the corner and it looks sweet,” he told his brother and co-host, Jason Kelce, while discussing his plans to bring his burgundy convertible to the game.

Kelce’s impressive car collection also includes a Rolls-Royce Ghost, an Aston Martin Vanquish and a Mercedes-AMG G63.

The “Opalite” singer watched the action from a private suite alongside her parents, Andrea and Scott Swift, as well as longtime pal Karlie Kloss.

Swift was seen celebrating when Kelce scored a touchdown in the third quarter, helping the Chiefs extend their lead over the Jets.

The Grammy winner also made headlines for her reunion with Kloss, 34, following years of speculation that the former best friends had suffered a falling-out.

The supermodel, who attended Swift and Kelce’s star-studded wedding in July, was spotted smiling and cheering alongside the singer during the game.

Swift and Kelce tied the knot at MSG on July 3 in front of approximately 1,000 family members, friends and celebrity guests.
