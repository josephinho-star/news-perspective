US envoy’s Kashmir ‘part of India’ remark rattles Pakistan ties

2026-08-29 07:00

Whether a slip or a calculated trade tactic, Sergio Gor’s remark upended decades of studied American neutrality on Kashmir

Seven words, spoken almost in passing on a recent afternoon in Srinagar, have set off a diplomatic tremor across South Asia.

“This is an important part of India,” Sergio Gor told reporters on August 19, during the first visit by a US ambassador to Indian-administered Jammu and Kashmir in six years.

India now administers the populous central and southern Kashmir valley, while Pakistan controls a western portion of the region known as Azad Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan.
