The one thing Bob Chesney thinks he needs to do better after UCLA’s dream season

2026-11-17 04:46

Bob Chesney has spearheaded the resurgence of UCLA’s unbeaten football team, is a lock to win every major coach-of-the-year award and might be on the verge of sainthood in Westwood.

But there’s something he thinks he needs to do better: community outreach.

“I’ve got to do a better job in the offseason of making sure we get out there and interact with more people,” Chesney said Monday, “and this [last] offseason was a little tight just because we got there and things started to roll, but that’s all part of our responsibility as a program as well.”

Chesney was being a little hard on himself given everything he’s already done in Year 1. He’s handed out free pizza at fraternities, invited campus flag football teams to practice and been a presence at seemingly every other sporting event on campus, including the championship banner raising for the women’s basketball team on Sunday.

Football attendance is ticking upward, with the 45,800 on hand Friday for the No. 4 Bruins’ 22-14 victory over Illinois at a rainy Rose Bowl representing a season high.

Fans chanted Chesney’s name as he walked off the field with his family.

Three days later, Chesney returned the compliment.

“To stand there and do it in the rain and to be present of it, it takes a village, it takes a whole community,” Chesney said, “and I’m really proud of the students that were there, I’m really proud of everyone that hung out and stayed and the energy they brought to the stadium and we’re just scratching the surface, and that’s ultimately my job is to help get people to these games, and I think the product on the field helps.”

Having the right coach is also a major plus.

UCLA is reported to be on the verge of locking up Chesney with a contract extension that could keep him in place for years to come, allowing him to go about winning over more fans.

The Bruins (10-0 overall, 7-0 Big Ten) can set a single-season school record for victories on Saturday if they beat Michigan (5-5, 2-5) at Michigan Stadium in Ann Arbor.
