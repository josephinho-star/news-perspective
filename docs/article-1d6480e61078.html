Is ‘Saturday Night Live’ New Tonight? Here Are The Next Three Hosts For ‘SNL’ Season 52

2026-10-31 23:00

Happy Halloween!

Looking for a spooky movie to watch with your little ones? Decider’s Anna Menta recently published a list of the best Halloween movies for kids on Netflix! Perhaps you’re looking for an under-the-radar gem? Colin and Cameron Cairnes’ innovative supernatural film Late Night with the Devil is an absolute must-see for horror fans.

The best part? It’s currently streaming for free on Tubi!

But maybe you’re looking to unwind with a laugh after a hectic day of tricking and/or treating? If so, we have good news to report: Saturday Night Live is back with an all-new episode.

Who’s hosting tonight’s special Halloween installment? Is SNL new next week? Here’s everything you need to know.

Is Saturday Night Live New Tonight, October 31?

Yes! SNL returns tonight with an all-new episode hosted by Obsession star Inde Navarrette. Gracie Abrams serves as the musical guest.

Who Are The Next Three SNL Season 52 Hosts?

After tonight’s Inde Navarrette-led episode, SNL will air new installments for the next two weeks:

- November 7: Druski with musical guest The Strokes

- November 14: John Mulaney with musical guest Phoebe Bridgers

How To Watch Saturday Night Live Online:

If you have a valid cable login, you can watch SNL on NBC or the NBC website/app. Already cut the cord? No problem!

The only way to watch shows airing live without cable is by using a live TV streaming service. Our favorite is DIRECTV, which comes with a five-day free trial before payment begins.

DIRECTV offers a number of flexible subscription options, including Genre Packs, so you can best choose which streaming plans fit your needs and budget. Packs start at $19.99/month.

You can also watch NBC live with an active subscription to fuboTV, Hulu + Live TV, YouTube TV, or Sling TV. YouTube TV, fuboTV, and Hulu + Live TV offer free trials for new subscribers.

Can I Watch Saturday Night Live on Peacock?

You can stream new episodes of SNL live or on demand on Peacock. The upcoming Inde Navarrette-hosted episode will be available to watch on demand on Peacock beginning Sunday, November 1.
