The Young Bucks make AEW history with victory in three-way ladder match at All Out

2026-09-27 17:46

The Young Bucks are back on top of the All Elite Wrestling (AEW) tag team division.

Adam Copeland and Christian Cage put the AEW Tag Team Championship on the line against Matt and Nick Jackson and Dax Harwood and Cash Wheeler (known as FTR), who returned to the fray at All In. At All Out, it was a three-way ladder match.

For anyone who has watched Copeland and Cage’s careers, seeing them go into the ladder match would be akin to Michael Jordan playing at the United Center. The match was almost as exciting as watching Jordan cook defenses on the court.

It was all about the spots and the bumps.

Harwood and Copeland met each other at the top of a ladder with one ladder set up perpendicular to the ropes. Harwood suplexed Copeland onto the ladder. Cage and Wheeler got to the top turnbuckles and each hit a big splash on Harwood and Copeland respectively.

In another spot, Wheeler was sent flying through a table from the ring onto the floor. Matt Jackson placed Harwood onto a table next and Nick Jackson hit a 450 splash onto the competitor.

The Young Bucks had another big moment minutes later. Each wrestler climbed to the top of a tall ladder with Harwood and Copeland sprawled across tables on the floor. They each hit an elbow drop and a splash. Wheeler was in the ring watching it all unfold. He saw an opportunity to hit a suicide dive through the middle of the ladder and onto Cage.

Bodies were strewn across the floor. The match came down to who wanted it more. The Young Bucks survived a Shatter Machine from FTR.

Matt and Nick Jackson climbed to the top of the ladder and captured the title belts.

It is the fourth reign for the Young Bucks. They have the most tag team titles in AEW history. Their win also ended Copeland and Cage’s reign at 125 days. It is the Young Bucks’ first title reign since April 2024 when they won the titles at Dynasty.

The new champions will seemingly take on Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher first.
