Oprah can’t stop raving about these viral sweatpant jeans

2026-11-17 15:32

Oprah’s favorite pants aren’t what they seem.

When the media mogul dropped her 2026 Favorite Things list last week, we couldn’t help but notice one brand clinched a spot two years in a row: Rag & Bone, maker of the viral sweatpant jeans.

While Oprah spotlighted the Miramar Sport Stripe Track Pant last year, this time around she picked the brand’s Miramar Mallow Trucker Jacket and Miramar Mallow Pant — which similarly look like denim, but are actually as comfy as sweatpants.

“I liked last year’s version of ‘hard pants that are actually soft pants’ so much that I chose these updates,” she wrote, saying that her friend Gayle King also wears hers “all the time.”

“Each piece is printed to look like denim, but they’re actually a comfy stretch fabric,” she added of the pull-on styles, which have elastic waistbands.

The new Mallow styles boast an extra “marshmallow-like,” pillowy soft texture.

We tried out the Miramar jeans after last year’s Oprah shout-out, and were immediately impressed. So many people have mistaken them for real jeans — and are shocked to hear they’re actually sweatpants.

“They’re easy to machine wash and don’t seem to stretch much, truly justifying the price tag,” commerce reporter Erica Radol wrote at the time, calling the pants “life-changing.”

Should you prefer something a bit more colorful, Oprah’s 2025 pick — the Miramar Sport Stripe Track Pant — comes in a wide range of colors. (“You probably know someone who’d say, ‘Yes, please!’” she wrote of the ultra-giftable style at the time.)
