Jon Gosselin claims he has ‘grounds to sue’ ex-wife Kate over son’s alleged abuse

2026-10-22 18:58

Jon Gosselin said he wants to sue his ex-wife, Kate Gosselin, or pursue criminal charges against her over their son Collin’s allegations of childhood abuse.

Jon made the remarks Wednesday in an interview with Entertainment Tonight, while reacting to Kate’s suggestion that Collin’s allegations stemmed from mental illness.

“She needs to stop. He is not mentally ill. I would definitely have grounds to sue Kate for defamation and libel,” he told the outlet.

“I don’t even think it’s suing. I think it’s just criminal charges. So, that’s up to the state.”

This isn’t the first time the “Jon and Kate Plus 8” alum threatened legal action against his ex-wife, to whom he was married from 1999 to 2009.

Earlier this month, Jon sent a message to Kate via Us Weekly, “If you continue to falsely label my son Collin as ‘mentally ill,’ you will be held accountable.”

Collin also exclusively told Page Six that he’s considering legal action against his mother over his allegations of childhood abuse, including potentially filing a police report.

“I have the grounds to do so,” Collin told us. “And this justice is very important to me for all children to know that they should absolutely fight and they should actually look into what has been done to them and never stop fighting it.”

Among the shocking allegations was that she kept him locked up in “a small holding” cell for two years before sending him away to the mental institution when he was 12 years old, isolating him from the rest of his siblings.

He also claims his now-estranged mom forced her eight children — fellow sextuplets Alexis, Aaden, Leah, Joel and Hannah, as well as twins Cara and Madelyn (now 25) — to act a certain way in front of cameras while filming their hit TLC show.

Kate, however, hit back at her estranged son via TikTok after the book’s release, when she responded to to a supporter who labeled Collin’s claims as “terrible lies” and said it “should be illegal to defame someone” in a memoir.
