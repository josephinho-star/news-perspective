Did Madison Beer ruin Justin Herbert?

Since getting engaged to Beer, Herbert is 0-4 and has the second worst QBR in the league, according to ESPN. Only Kyler Murray is worse. At NFL.com, Herbert's passer rating is 70.6. Of full-time NFL QBs, that's the worst rate. We're talking Jameis Winston territory. Herbert's six interceptions is only surpassed by Drake Maye's pitiful start to the season.

Add it all up and even the Big J blue checkmark journalists are starting to wonder if off-the-field activities are starting to catch up with Herbert. Has there been too much focus on fiancée Madison Beer and not enough on reading defenses?

Remember, I wrote about how the NFL used training camp to push the narrative that Beer was going to be the next Taylor Swift. The league openly admitted to a social media campaign where Herbert had at least two interactions with Beer that were seemingly cooked up for TikTok groupies.

As someone who wishes Jim Harbaugh nothing but the worst with his career, I'm laughing over this 0-4 start, but this should serve as a warning to the other 31 teams. If the NFL starts trying to socially construct TikTok content with members of your team, RUN. Lock your doors. Tell Goodell's social media goons to go away.

Trust me, as a Bengals fan, Joe Burrow got caught up in the same social media content construction and now he's a mess.

Gen Z kids returning home from college early to live with mom and dad

– CF in Kansas writes: Just read an article about Gen Z college freshmen quitting school and returning home to live with mom and dad. I find this more than a little disturbing. While some of the kids made it a point they simply opted to take college classes online, there seems to be a common denominator among them. Several have anxiety issues.

There is a better way to get these kids to stay in college and experience the growth they need to take the next step into the working world. Start with junior college or a 4 yr college campus located in or near the city where you live. Most cities have small colleges where kids can attend as day students without leaving home. I would be remiss not to mention trade schools as an option. The goal is to get a job, move out of the house and start a life of your own.

Joe, since Screencaps Jr will (I assume) someday go to college, what are your thoughts?

Kinsey: My thoughts are that this isn't exactly a new trend. I just talked to a parent this weekend whose freshman son is dealing with the stress of living in a dorm unit with 11 guys and very little space for studying. The son isn't coming home, but there are adjustments to be made. My thoughts are that you tough it out during fall semester, and then, if the son or daughter is absolutely miserable and a change needs to be made, you make it. I came home for a semester my sophomore year in college due to a complete idiot roommate.

After a semester at a community college, I realized I had to get out of there and I went right back to Toledo where my life started to change.

BTW, trade schools are great. Save the emails. I like having plumbers and electricians available to call on short notice.
