Why Did ‘The Great British Baking Show’ Replace Prue Leith With Nigella Lawson?

2026-09-25 18:00

It’s the first week of autumn and there’s a crackling crisp in the air and a brand new episode of The Great British Baking Show on Netflix.

Yes, The Great British Baking Show is back — but not everything in the tent is the same. Sure, Alison Hammond and Noel Fielding continue to charm us with their adorable antics and iconic judge Paul Hollywood is prepping his first Hollywood handshake of the season, but Dame Prue Leith has departed the franchise.

Who replaced Prue on The Great British Baking Show? Why, none other than the “domestic goddess” herself, Nigella Lawson.

Hardcore “Bake Off” fans have known about Prue’s exit and Nigella’s arrival since last January. Prue posted a heartfelt Instagram post explaining why “now feels like the right time to step back” from the super popular cooking competition show.

“(I’m 86 for goodness sake!), there’s so much I’d like to do, not least spend summers enjoying my garden. Whoever joins the team, I’m sure they’ll love it as much as I have. I feel very lucky to have been part of it,” she wrote.

Shortly thereafter, producers closed in on hiring Nigella Lawson as Dame Prue Leith’s replacement. She confirmed the news on her Instagram on January 26, noting that it was “daunting to be following in the footsteps of Prue Leith and Mary Berry before her,” before admitting she was “thrilled to be joining the team.”

Well, now Nigella is officially here! But why did Prue Leith leave The Great British Baking Show? And where have you seen Nigella Lawson before?

Here’s what you need to know about The Great British Baking Show‘s latest personnel shakeup…

Why Did The Great British Baking Show Replace Prue Leith With Nigella Lawson?

Wondering what happened to Dame Prue Leith? She’s okay! Prue Leith decided to leave The Great British Baking Show in 2026 so she could have more time to enjoy her gardens in the summer. (No, really, if you read her statement, quoted above, she cites being 86-years-old and wanting to do other things.)

You can keep up with all things Prue by following her on Instagram. She’s currently promoting her new book, Being Old… and learning to love it!

Nigella Lawson emerged as the show’s top pick to replace Prue Leith as judge on The Great British Baking Show.

The Great British Baking Show: Who is Nigella Lawson?

Nigella Lawson is a 66-year-old British food writer, television presenter, and former journalist. In 1999, she launched the groundbreaking series Nigella Bites, which followed her life as a home cook in London. (I recently fell into a hole on YouTube watching bootlegs of this show and it’s still so good!) Nigella Bites went on to revolutionize the cooking show genre and it established Lawson as an international lifestyle expert.

Over the years, Lawson has appeared on other shows in both the UK and US, including The Taste. However, joining The Great British Baking Show might be the biggest move for Lawson — and The Great British Baking Show — in years. It’s a perfect alchemy of format and star. Lawson’s warm, unfussy approach to cooking is a great match for the cozy charms of the “Bake Off” tent.

The first episode of The Great British Baking Show Collection 14 is now streaming on Netflix. New episodes will premiere on Fridays through the fall.
