Nasty nabe: Two women accused of separate sick sex crimes live just doors from each other in small KY town, court docs

2026-11-18 19:19

Two women accused of sickening sex crimes lived practically on each other’s doorsteps in small-town Kentucky – one a former teacher who admitted to having sex with her underage student, the other an ex-jail employee accused of committing incest with her minor relative, The Post has learned.

Mary “Hanna” Mattingly, 32, was living in a tree-lined, single-story home on Glenview Drive in Bardstown, a rural community of roughly 14,000 residents about 40 miles south of Louisville, when she was arrested in March for sexually abusing and exchanging nude photos with her 16-year-old male student at Bardstown High School.

Just one house down – and less than 500 feet away – lived Lauren Winans, 31, a former Nelson County corrections employee who was arrested just last month on a slew of child sexual abuse and incest charges, according to addresses listed in both women’s criminal indictments.

It wasn’t immediately known if Mattingly and Winans knew each other personally, but their uncanny proximity and on-the-surface similarities — both are wives, appear to have young children and even share the same dirty-blond, shoulder-length and side-parted hairstyle, according to social media pics — make it seem plausible.

Winans “committed the offense of incest when she had sexual intercourse or deviate sexual intercourse with a person whom she knew to be an ancestor who was less than 12 years of age” sometime between Jan. 1, 2022, and Nov. 20, 2022, the court documents allege.

She is also accused of committing two counts of first-degree sexual abuse and one count of sodomy against a victim younger than 12, who is only identified as C.G.W. in the indictment, within the same period.

It was not immediately clear whether Winans’ underage relative was the same alleged victim mentioned in the other three charges she is facing. Authorities have not disclosed her relationship with the family member.

Winans, who has remained in custody on $250,000 bail since her Oct. 22 arrest, tied the knot with Andrew Winans, a current Bardstown cop, in June 2024 – years after her alleged crimes are said to have occurred.

The accused child abuser’s occupation was listed as “deputy jailer” on their marriage certificate.

A Nelson County Sheriff’s Office employee confirmed to The Post on Tuesday that Winans once worked inside the local lockup, but could not provide the dates of her employment.

The ex-staffer was being held inside of LaRue County Detention Center, a facility located about 25 miles southwest of her former workplace, while awaiting her next court date on Thursday.

She has entered a not-guilty plea to the charges, a Nelson County Circuit Court clerk previously told The Post.

Mattingly, meanwhile, broke down in tears on Friday while pleading guilty to first-degree sexual abuse of her 16-year-old student as well as having nude photos of him “portraying a sexual performance of a minor,” according to reports.

The disgraced substitute teacher – who is also married and has at least one child – is expected to be given five years behind bars as part of a plea agreement reportedly struck ahead of her Jan. 21 sentencing.

Mattingly’s twisted tryst with the student came to the surface after school officials reportedly received an anonymous letter about it this spring.

The victim later told investigators that their relationship kicked off with Mattingly asking him to help clean the girls’ locker room during middle school soccer season – where he would “sit down [with Mattingly] and talk about her life and what is going on,” according to the Lexington Herald-Leader.

The pair then began to talk on Snapchat “more and more” — before they started having sex between March 1 and July 31, 2025, the outlet reported, citing the victim’s statement.

Prosecutors recommended that Mattingly be required to serve 85% of her sentence before she is eligible for probation or parole. She will be required to register as a sex offender upon her release.
