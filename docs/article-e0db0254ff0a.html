11 Best New Shows on Netflix: September 2026’s Top Upcoming Series to Watch

2026-09-02 20:35

This September, Netflix programming delivers grade-A content all month long. While it’s always hard to say goodbye to the titles leaving Netflix, you won’t feel down for long once you get a good look at the impressive array of Netflix Original series coming to the platform to take their place. With everything from premiering titles to long-awaited continuations of fan favorites, Netflix’s diverse and delightful September 2026 premieres are sure to have something for everyone.

The streamer is set to help you celebrate the new month with premieres from highly anticipated continuations of established hit series across several different genres (so there’s truly something for everyone), like The Gentlemen: Season 2, STEEL BALL RUN JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure: 2nd-3rd Stage, and Stranger Things: Tales From ’85: Season 2.

There are also plenty of exciting brand-new shows that will begin streaming exclusively on Netflix starting this month. Keep an eye out in particular for A Different World, Crew Girl, and Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story, which are all already looking to be some of the most promising new releases out of September’s entire streaming lineup.

So what makes the cut for our Top 11, and which of them have what it takes to be in the Netflix Top 10? Keep reading to treat yourself to the greatness that is the list of streaming standouts for September 2026.

DESCRIPTION: Two years after it premiered on the platform, Netflix Original action comedy The Gentlemen is back with a new season that looks to raise the stakes like never before. Season 2 picks up with Eddie (Theo James) and Susie (Kaya Scodelario) one year after they teamed up to work for Bobby Glass’s (Ray Winstone) cannabis empire overseas. With Bobby’s increasingly questionable decision-making in mind, the duo is prepared to act without his blessing in order to expand their criminal empire to Italy. Get in on all the action by catching this thrilling addition to the world of The Gentlemen this September, only on Netflix.
