LegalZoom review: Basic legal services starting at $39.95 per month

2026-09-23 14:30

Not everyone can afford a law firm's pricey retainer or hourly rate when a legal issue comes up. Platforms like LegalShield can make it more affordable to consult with a lawyer, have important documents reviewed and get legal assistance with basic issues — all for a fixed monthly fee.

Here's what you need to know about LegalShield, including its pricing, plans and features.

$39.95/month for Basic plan; $49.95/month for Advanced plan; $59.95/month for Premium plan

Family law, consume rights, estate planning, real estate, employee rights, traffic and accidents, business law and more

Each tier gives you a different level of access to notarization services, number of personal legal issues, family legal support, discounts and more

LegalShield is an online platform that offers a wide variety of personal and business legal services. You can get help with estate planning, family, traffic issues, real estate, renter issues, employee rights, debt collection, civil litigation, contracts, business licenses, prenuptial agreements and more.

LegalShield also provides resources like an online blog with educational articles, a lawyer directory to help you find a lawyer near you and AI document review.

The LegalShield platform has different plan tiers, each with a different level of access to the services. Here's a breakdown of what you'd get:

Across the board, higher priced tiers offer additional services or more attention on your personal and business matters. For instance, if you want your package to include prenup services, you'll have to upgrade from Basic to Advanced.

LegalShield doesn't charge an hourly rate. Instead, it offers tiered pricing plans for a fixed monthly fee. For personal use, prices start at $39.95 per month. For business use, prices start at $59.95 per month.

LegalShield also offers a "Young Adult" plan that includes assistance with things like small claims, traffic tickets, help with rental leases, phone consultations and more. This plan costs just $14.95. It's worth noting, though, that it is not available in every state.

- Offers monthly pricing model that's cheaper than paying an hourly rate

- Resources like educational blog articles

- Available for personal and business use

- Offers discounts on additional services

- Notarization included in each package

- Connects users to experienced law firms

- "Young Adult" plan not available in every state

- Page limit on document reviews may be insufficient

- Doesn't cover pre-existing situations

- Standard membership may not be able to help with more complicated or unique matters and you may need to pay for additional legal assistance

- Doesn't assist with International Law, Military Law or Tribal Law

Overall, LegalShield may be most helpful as an affordable way to get legal help and consultations on basic situations, like reviewing a lease or brief document reviews. But if you have a more complicated circumstance or need help in a highly specialized area that isn't covered (like Military Law), you'll have to seek outside assistance. LegalShield can offer a discount you can use with its provider law firms but you'll still wind up paying more than the small monthly subscription fee in this case.
