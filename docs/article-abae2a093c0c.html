First regular-season AP Top 25 poll features expected shuffling after shaky Week 1 of college football

2026-09-08 20:35

The first regular-season AP Top 25 college football poll is here, and you'll never guess what happened.

Some teams fell in the polls, others rose, and a total of five programs remained exactly where they were in the preseason poll. While the Week 1 slate of games around the country didn't necessarily jump off the page, teams were awarded by sending messages (hello, LSU), while others left some voters aggressively scratching their heads (we're looking at you, Michigan).

Virginia is the lone team in the poll that was unranked the week prior, but when it comes to the biggest jumpers and fallers, the aforementioned Tigers and Wolverines earned the two respective titles.

LSU embarrassed Clemson 51-10 to begin the Lane Kiffin era in Baton Rouge on Saturday night, moving up three spots from 11th in the preseason poll. As for Michigan, it needed an additional second added to the clock and a Mulligan Hail Mary attempt from quarterback Bryce Underwood to beat Western Michigan 13-12, and as a result, fell out of the poll after entering the week ranked 16th.

Oregon may have passed the stiff test Boise State delivered to the tune of a 34-27 victory, but the unexpected, four-quarter battle with the Broncos led voters to move Oregon down four spots from the preseason No. 2 spot.

Like Oregon, SMU avoided a close call on the road at Florida State on Monday, but moved up two spots to 17th after escaping Tallahassee with a 27-24 win. Washington, however, suffered a similar fate to its Big Ten counterparts in the polls after a sloppy 24-10 win in the Apple Cup over Washington State on Sunday.

In terms of surprises in the Week 1 poll, well ... there aren't any, which is refreshing in this over-reactionary era despite most teams around the country having played just one game.

Michigan still received 69 votes to be ranked after Week 1, while Western Michigan received 12.
