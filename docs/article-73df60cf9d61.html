Jalen Brunson launching new management group with mom and sister focusing on ‘off-court business’

2026-09-16 12:00

Jalen Brunson conquered basketball this year. Now he and his family are trying to own the business side.

The Brunsons announced Wednesday the launch of Thirty Third Management Group, a brand advisory firm that will manage his various off-court endeavors. The firm’s goal, as outlined in its press release, is to build a wider clientele of athletes and brands while initially prioritizing women’s professional sports.

Jalen’s mother, Sandra, was named President of the new company. His sister, Erica, is the Director of Client Services. His good friend and former high school teammate, Connor Cashaw, is the Director of Business Development.

The company’s name is based on Jalen’s humble 33rd draft position in 2018.
