Russell Brand receives chilling threat from jailed murderer: ‘We’ll be seeing you soon’

2026-11-11 13:03

A jailed murderer appeared to threaten Russell Brand with a chilling Snapchat upload after the actor’s rape conviction.

Terngu Agera, who is serving life for a 2014 slaying, posted a blurry image, seemingly taken from a jail cell, with a message to the disgraced comedian, according to the US Sun.

“Russell Brand you naughty boy, we’ll be seeing you son,” the caption read.

The post went viral on social media, with X users debating whether Agera really shared it under his rap name, Mover — and one claiming, “Everyone can get a phone in jail; it’s not hard.”

Others slammed Agera over being “bored” and allegedly “drawing unnecessary attention to himself.”

One individual wrote, “Imagine publicly announcing you’re waiting for someone who isn’t even in the same prison as you, with no confirmed plans for them to be transferred there. What exactly is he trying to achieve? … Absolutely ridiculous.”

Agera, notably, made headlines in 2019 for reportedly recording a 10-track rap album with a smuggled phone while behind bars, briefly streaming it on Spotify before it was removed.

Brand’s lawyer did not immediately respond to Page Six’s request for comment.

The comedian, 51, was convicted last week of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault.

He was subsequently remanded into custody and has been at HMP Wandsworth where, Page Six hears, he will remain “for a few weeks.”

Solicitor Kally Sahota explained to us that the move will be based on “security, safety and rehabilitation needs.”

A former prisoner who served time at HMP Wandsworth told the US Sun that the “Get Him to the Greek” star is a “dead man walking.”

The ex-inmate dubbed Brand a “marked man,” adding, “If Brand stays in Wandsworth, he’s in real trouble. He needs to survive until he’s got his permanent move.”

Brand, per the Daily Mail, has spent his time imprisoned so far “reading, meditating and praying for hours on end.”
