New York is the No. 1 state with highest dog bite risk — here’s how to keep your canine in check

2026-08-31 17:44

No matter how cute and cuddly your canine might be, sometimes, pet behavior spins out of control.

In March 2025, “Bachelor” alum Sean Lowe made headlines when he revealed his rescue dog viscously attacked him twice. He was rapidly transferred to the hospital, where doctors gave him stitches in “five or six different places” on his arm.

“It was so bizarre,” he said in an Instagram video at the time. “Why is my dog, who I know loves me, attacking me so aggressively?”

The dangers (and price) of dealing with dog bites are not to be ignored. A recent study found serious bites that require a hospital stay cost victims between $23,222 and $26,330 out of pocket.

Delivery drivers are particularly susceptible to dog bites. USPS reported more than 5,200 attacks on postal workers in 2025, and 269 of those incidents were in New York.

Additionally, these injuries charged U.S. insurers $1.862 billion in claims in 2025, at an average payout of $65,450.

Unfortunately, New York is leading the ranks in overall victim risk, with the highest average claim at $110,488.
