Stocks making the biggest moves after hours: ServiceTitan, Mission Produce, Chime Financial and more

2026-09-08 20:56

Check out the companies making headlines in after-hours trading. ServiceTitan — The software provider's stock tumbled 19% after posting current-quarter revenue guidance that came in slightly below analyst forecasts. However, ServiceTitan surpassed Wall Street consensus estimates on both lines in the second quarter. Casey's General Stores — The chain lost 10% despite beating expectations on both lines for the first fiscal quarter. Casey's said it anticipates inside same-store sales will grow by between 2% and 5% in the full year. Mission Produce — Shares of the avocado producer popped 7.5%. Mission Produce beat the expectations of every analyst polled by FactSet for both earnings per share, excluding items, and revenue in its fiscal third quarter. Braze — The customer engagement platform's stock dropped nearly 10% following weaker-than-expected earnings guidance for the current quarter. Braze said to anticipate non-GAAP earnings per share between 13 cents and 14 cents, while analysts surveyed by FactSet penciled in 16 cents a share. Chime Financial — The financial technology stock jumped almost 10% after providing upbeat guidance for the third quarter and full year. Chime also said it entered into an agreement to buy Stride Bank for $590 million in cash. InnovAge — The health care stock rallied 11% on the back of strong full-year guidance. Fourth-quarter revenue also surpassed expectations. On the other hand, the company's fourth-quarter earnings per share was slightly below forecasts.
