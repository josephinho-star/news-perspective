‘Socialist’ AOC spent $12K on glam German digs while pushing for ‘working class’ rights

2026-11-22 10:30

“Squad” Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez ran up $9,000 in lodging expenses alone in February during a posh trip to Germany — where she road-tested her pitch as a global leader and demanded “material gains for the working class,” campaign filings reviewed by The Post reveal.

The five-star pampering during the three-day getaway was enough to make Trier native Karl Marx blush — or pen a fresh manifesto against bourgeois extravagance.

The Bronx-Queens Democrat’s trip to the annual Munich Security Conference included a stay at the Hotel Bayerischer — located in the city’s Old Town just steps from its famous clock tower — and cost $5,251, according to her filings.

The restored 1841 hotel features a two-star Michelin restaurant, a spa with four stories, and a guest book featuring boldface names like Ben Affleck, Gwyneth Paltrow and Brigitte Bardot.

The hotel’s long history as a draw for Hollywood royalty extends to screen legends Kirk Douglas, Buster Keaton, Alfred Hitchcock and Grace Kelly.

The price suggests she had a room to meet her outsized ambitions: a junior suite at the hotel goes for $1,091 a night at New Year’s. The Bronx-born AOC, who famously waited tables after attending Boston University — where she interned in the foreign affairs and immigration office of Sen. Ted Kennedy — attended two days of events at the conference.

The conference focused on transatlantic security, but the hotel features plenty of perks. Its Atelier restaurant includes a tempting seven-course prix fixe meal for $310, and includes smoked eel, Norwegian scallops and duck, while featuring rock lobster and fries with caviar as add-ons.

Its Blue Spa, perched “high above the rooftops of Munich,” offers an 80-minute “indulgence” massage for $278.

There is a “very gentle organic sauna in the ladies area,” a plunge pool, and relaxation areas including a winter garden with an open fire that “leaves nothing to be desired,” it boasts.

New York City Councilman Frank Morano (R-SI) was floored by the size of AOC’s tab. “I’m not suggesting that Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez has to stay at a Motel 6 every time that she travels. But $9,000 on hotels is a pretty remarkable tab for someone whose entire political career is about railing against the excesses of capitalism. Apparently when it comes to accommodations, democratic socialism has a luxury suite exception,” he scoffed.

When the Munich event was over, the 37-year-old comrade headed to the capital city of Berlin by rail, her $748 train expense for Deutsche Bahn suggests. First-class tickets go for less than $200, indicating AOC brought either companions or security with her.

She spoke to 1,100 students at the Technical University Berlin, where she called to throw “sand in the gears” of injustice – before bedding down at the Westin Berlin Grand, with lodging costs totaling $3,730.

The hotel’s superior rooms offer “iconic views of Unter den Linden, with the Brandenburg Gate to the left and Berlin Cathedral to the right,” while garden rooms provide a “peaceful retreat overlooking the hotel’s idyllic garden amid the vibrant city,” it boasts.

The hotel’s 30-meter staircase rises under an eight-story octagonal atrium.

The city’s mayor, Franziska Giffey, turned out to introduce AOC at her speech as “one that connects lived experience, public debate and democratic participation” while calling her a “loud voice fighting for the rule of law, for civic rights, equality and freedom.”

One thing she didn’t have to fight for is a taxi. She spent $2,650 in campaign donor funds on a “premium driving service” during her stay.

That puts the total for the junket over Valentine’s Day weekend at $12,000 – with a price tag that doesn’t even include meals or incidental expenses. AOC’s campaign filed the expenses on March 23, the same day she filed two American Express payments, one for $1,000 and another for $12,024.

AOC’s office did not respond to a request for comment or details on her travel arrangements.
