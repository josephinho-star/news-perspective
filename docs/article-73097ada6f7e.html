Noah Wyle is named People magazine’s ‘Sexiest Man Alive’

2026-10-27 11:13

The outlet named the “Pitt” star Tuesday morning, with the 55-year-old posing in an all-denim ensemble for his cover shoot.

“I thought that sounded like a prank — just not in a million years did I think that this would be an honor bestowed on me,” Wyle gushed.

The “E.R.” alum joked to the outlet that his family is going to “tease” him about the honor “for the next 50 years.”

Wyle predicted, “I am going to take a lot of ribbing … for sure.”

The Emmy winner is a three-time dad, sharing son Owen, 23, and daughter Auden, 21, with his ex-wife, Tracy Warbin, as well as daughter Frances, 11, with wife Sara Wyle.
