Is ‘Saturday Night Live’ New Tonight? Return Date, First Four Hosts For ‘SNL’ Season 52

2026-09-26 23:30

Over the last few months, I’ve rewatched a slew of classic Saturday Night Live sketches as I put together my perfect SNL episode.

From Steve Martin’s memorable “Not Gonna Phone It In Tonight” musical opening to ’90s favorites like “Van Down by the River” and “Unfrozen Caveman Lawyer,” my own personal SNL mixtape is very ’90s/early-2000s centric, but who knows what future classic sketches await as we prepare for Season 52.

Before we get to the return of SNL, we have a few Saturday Night Live-related streaming recommendations. Netflix recently uploaded all six seasons of the iconic Canadian sketch series SCTV, and a documentary on the series, SCTV: The Greatest Show You’ve Never Heard Of, premieres October 30 on the streamer. Netflix also has a Norm Macdonald documentary in the pipeline, Judd Apatow’s Norm: The Tale of Norm Macdonald, that drops on October 16, and HBO is airing an “NFL Short” on Dennis Miller’s time on Monday Night Football, Miller Night Football, on Tuesday, September 29 at 9:00 p.m. ET. But let’s get back to the main event.

When does Saturday Night Live return with new episodes? Is SNL on tonight? Here’s everything you need to know.

Is SNL New Tonight (September 26)?

Yes! The season premiere of SNL airs tonight at 11:30 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock.

Who’s Hosting The Season Premiere of Saturday Night Live?

Knicks legend Jalen Brunson hosts tonight’s season premiere, with musical guest Katseye.

Saturday Night Live Release Schedule/Hosts:

We also know who will host the next three episodes of Saturday Night Live:

- Episode 2 (October 3): Dakota Johnson/Turnstile

- Episode 3 (October 10): Shane Gillis/Rosalía

- Episode 4 (October 31): Inde Navarrette/Gracie Abrams

How To Watch Saturday Night Live Online:

If you have a valid cable login, you can watch SNL on NBC or the NBC website/app. Already cut the cord? No problem!

The only way to watch shows airing live without cable is by using a live TV streaming service. Our favorite is DIRECTV, which comes with a five-day free trial before payment begins.

DIRECTV offers a number of flexible subscription options, including Genre Packs, so you can best choose which streaming plans fit your needs and budget. Packs start at $19.99/month.

You can also watch NBC live with an active subscription to fuboTV, Hulu + Live TV, YouTube TV, or Sling TV. YouTube TV, fuboTV, and Hulu + Live TV offer free trials for new subscribers.

Can I Watch Saturday Night Live on Peacock?

Yes! You can stream new episodes of SNL live or on demand on Peacock. The upcoming Jalen Brunson-hosted episode will be available to watch on demand on Peacock beginning Sunday, September 27.

The season premiere of Saturday Night Live airs September 26 at 11:30 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock.
