Kevin Bacon pleads for help recovering missing FedEx package rerouted multiple times around the globe

2026-10-19 02:19

Kevin Bacon was yearning and burning for somebody to tell him where his lost clothes were hiding.

Bacon, 68, pleaded with his followers to help him figure out where FedEx sent a package of clothing he sent last month to a location across the pond.

"Hey everybody, it's me. I'm over here, I'm working in Ireland," Bacon told his followers. "I wanted to pack light, so I decided to ship some old clothes over here to Ireland. I don't buy a lot of clothes. I've had these clothes probably since the '90s.

"So I used a shipping service that I don't want to say the name but it rhymes with BreadX," he said before explaining that he shipped a box of clothing on Sept. 26 from upstate New York to his filming location.

"It went straight to Memphis, and then from Memphis it to France and by Sept. 28 it arrived in Germany, and by Sept. 29, they shipped it straight to Israel, not Ireland – Israel."

The "Footloose" actor went on to explain that the package traveled from Israel back to France on Oct. 3, only to be sent back to Israel the following day. It traveled to Germany on Oct. 5 before being routed back to Israel on Oct. 6.

"On Oct. 10, that box of old clothes made its way all the way to France, and on Oct. 11 it arrived in the UK, in England," he said. "Pretty close. Guess what? On Oct. 11, it left the UK and came straight to Ireland, where it has been sitting for five days, and they say they're unable to deliver it."

Bacon admitted he was frustrated with the situation and couldn't see a solution to his problem.

"I don't understand why. It's just sitting there," he said. "I can't get a person the phone. I have not been able to talk to anybody. I know what you're thinking: Just go buy some more clothes.

"And you're right. I could buy some clothes, but I want these clothes. I've had them since the '90s. These are like ... they have a certain sentimental value."

The "Tremors" star noted that the package contained "old boots, old jeans, old hats" as he wondered if he was "ever going to see this stuff" again.

"It's starting to get cold here," Bacon said.

FedEx representatives did not immediately respond to Fox News Digital's request for comment. The company has separately apologized for the incident and said it was in direct contact with Bacon's team to deliver the package and was reviewing what went wrong.
