Bunnie Xo, 46, defends age-gap romance with Dylan Wolf, 24, after Jelly Roll divorce

2026-09-22 14:46

Bunnie Xo stood by her age-gap romance with “Calabasas Confidential” star Dylan Wolf in an Instagram comment over the weekend.

The 46-year-old posted a Saturday Instagram video of herself and her new beau, 26, at Waffle House — and clapped back at the slew of comments bashing the couple’s 22-year difference.

The “Dumb Blonde” podcast host did not reply to a specific hater, instead issuing a general PSA to her followers.

“Hey guys, I’m having fun & he gave me my smile back 💞,” Bunnie Xo wrote. “Ya girl is living her best life — let meeeee k thanks byeeeee.”

One of the negative comments read, “If it’s his first time at Waffle House , he’s too young for you sis 😂😂😂😂 small joke. lol.”

Another called the social media upload an example of “cougar season,” with a third quipping, “mamas feeding him breakfast before he catches his school bus.”

She and the reality star made out at the Grammy winner’s Goodnight Nashville bar in Nashville, Tennessee, on the Fourth of July.

The former escort defended doing so at the time, telling podcast listeners that her ex-husband, 41, was aware of it and took “no issue” with it.

“I .. have a floor there,” she pointed out at the time. “I also feel very safe there. I also feel protected there. That’s our home. If I’m gonna land at any bar, I’m gonna do it at Goodnight Nashville.”

Bunnie Xo added, “I had fun. I am healing out loud. So what I had fun under the fireworks? I hope I get kissed under 10 more fireworks.”

However, she clarified that she was “not dating” and didn’t “want to be with anybody,” explaining, “You’re not pinning this bitch down. Catch me if you can. Your girl is just out here having fun, living my best life.”

When Wolf was asked about the makeout by TMZ, he said, “Sometimes a cowboy wants to hunt cougars and rabbits. That’s all that is.”

While the country singer has not debuted a new romance since their divorce was settled in July, Bunnie Xo has claimed that her ex was “raring and ready to go.”

She said, “He’s all hopped up on testosterone, let me tell you. He’s even started dating. … His DMs are open.”
