The playoff issues Yankees hope to iron out — including with possible gamble — over final five games

2026-09-24 00:02

Spring Training II began Wednesday for the Yankees.

They had five meaningless games to try to address their biggest concerns going into a postseason in which:

- Their troubles against lefty pitching will be challenged by the southpaw-heavy Red Sox in the wild-card round.

- Their troubles playing clean defensively will be exacerbated if they get to the division series against the uberaggressive Rays and their funky home stadium.

- Their uncertain chain from the rotation, a team strength, to closer David Bednar can expose them against any postseason opponent.

The Yankees learned — and earned — their fate Tuesday when a doubleheader split against the Rays guaranteed Tampa Bay the AL East title and the league’s No. 1 seed while the Yankees now know they will host the best-of-three wild-card series next week, almost certainly against the Red Sox.
