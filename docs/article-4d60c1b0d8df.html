Taylor Swift celebrates husband Travis Kelce’s touchdown for Chiefs with wedding ring point

2026-09-21 01:55

Taylor Swift made sure everyone knew whose man was hers when Travis Kelce scored his first touchdown of the season.

The pop icon was elated watching Kelce pull in a 13-yard touchdown catch in the first quarter to give the Chiefs a 10-7 lead over the Colts on “Sunday Night Football” from Arrowhead Stadium.

On second-and-7, Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes fired a pass to Kelce, who corralled the ball and dove into the end zone for Kansas City’s first touchdown of the game.

The NBC broadcast quickly cut to a view inside Kelce’s suite, where Swift was ecstatically celebrating the score and appeared to shout, “Let’s go!”

The “Blank Space” singer also proudly showed off her wedding ring and pointed to it as she celebrated the Kelce touchdown.

“That’s your guy!” NBC broadcaster Mike Tirico said with a chuckle as the footage played on screen.

Swift had also been shown on the broadcast sitting in the suite earlier in the game.

Swift was in the Kelce suite with her mother, Andrea Swift, and father, Scott Swift.

It’s the second consecutive game Swift has attended this season, just over two months removed from her July wedding to Kelce at Madison Square Garden.

Kelce and Swift were also on hand for Mahomes’ birthday celebration recently.

The Chiefs quarterback turned 31 on Sept. 17 and the couple was seen attending his birthday party at 1587 Prime, the steakhouse the two NFL players opened last year.

Kelce’s touchdown catch was the Chiefs’ only one through the first quarter and a half of the game as Kansas City looked to start 2-0 after missing the postseason in 2025.
