Jamie Foxx’s Halloween bash turns bloody after guest stabbed in neck: report

2026-11-09 00:12

A partygoer was reportedly stabbed in the neck at a Halloween bash held at Jamie Foxx’s mansion in Thousand Oaks, California, Saturday.

A male suffered a superficial injury and was treated by paramedics at the scene, TMZ reported Sunday. A male suspect was arrested for assault with a deadly weapon, sources told the outlet.

In video footage obtained by TMZ, law enforcement appeared to be breaking up the party as a large crowd of people gathered outside. One man was in handcuffs as two officers led him away.

“I want to press charges,” he said.

Multiple males were also seen in handcuffs and sitting on the ground with officers standing over them.

Ambulances with their lights on were present at the chaotic scene.

Later, security officers were seen yelling at the crowd to get off the road so a truck could pass through.

It is unclear if Foxx was at his mansion at the time of the incident.

Page Six has reached out to the actor’s rep for comment.

The Oscar-winning actor made no mention of the alleged incident on social media, instead, sharing video of rapper OhGeesy performing at the party and praising his 18-year-old daughter, Anelise, in an Instagram post Saturday, The father-daughter duo throw a Halloween bash at his house every year called SKVLK Fest.

“@skvlkfest is on Fuego. @anelisefoxx has done it again!!!! and just getting started @ohgeesy 🎤 🔥,” he wrote.

SKVLK Fest’s official Instagram page also didn’t mention the alleged incident, instead posting happy pics and video of the party.

“BEST NIGHT OF THE YEAR🎡🔥,” one post reads. “Thank you all for coming to SKVLKFEST 2026! CANT WAIT for 2027 to see y’all again!”

This isn’t the first time Foxx’s annual Halloween party had had a dramatic turn of events. At last year’s soiree, the “Django Unchained” star went on an expletive-filled rant after an audience member apparently threw an item at rapper GloRilla while she was performing.

“Why would you throw something at the stage, man? Why? This is for free,” a clearly upset Foxx went on the mic to ask in video captured by a fan. “Y’all don’t deserve this s—t, bro.”

Foxx said he was “so disappointed” at the audience, adding, “You throw some s—t in my house, in my crib? It’s not cool, bro.”

The comedian, 58, recently welcomed his third child — and his first with girlfriend, 31-year-old Alyce Huckstepp — People reported last Wednesday.

He’s also a dad to daughter Corinne, 32, whom he shares with ex-girlfriend Connie Kline.

He shares Anelise with ex-girlfreind Kristin Grannis.
