Jamie Foxx jokes he’s ‘still got it’ as he makes rare comment about expecting 3rd baby at 58

2026-09-23 14:36

The “Ray” actor told the host that he’s “about to be back in the drop-off line,” adding, “I wasn’t on the fence about it, but being 58 … you know what I’m saying?”

“I said, ‘Al, how old are you?’ He said, ‘84.’ I said, ‘Man, you’re gonna be 84 years old back in the drop-off line,’” to which he said Pacino had responded, “’84 years old, back in the drop-off line, every time I get out, they pull me back in.’”

The “Django Unchained” star is welcoming his third child with 31-year-old girlfriend Alyce Huckstepp.

“I even tell my girl, I said, ‘Look, just hang in there for 12 years,’” Foxx playfully told Kimmel.

“If I’m 58, 12 years I’ll be 70 … you’ll just put a blanket over my thighs, give me some tapioca pudding…”

Page Six reported in May that Huckstepp was “several months along” in her pregnancy.
