Draymond Green daughter’s viral appearance has Warriors fans saying same thing

2026-11-10 20:48

Draymond Green holds nothing back on the basketball court, both with how he plays and what he says.

But the Warriors star forward and four-time NBA champion is more reserved regarding his personal life.

This is why it was fun for fans to see his daughter, Hunni, be shown on Chase Center’s center-hung screen during the Warriors’ blowout win against the Pelicans on Monday night.

At a break in the game, the Warriors’ in-arena cameras panned to Hunni Green while wishing her a happy birthday.

Bay Area news station 95.7 The Game made an X post that showed a snippet of Green’s screen appearance with the caption, “Happy Birthday to Draymond’s daughter, Hunni! 🥳.”

It has gone viral, tallying over 240,000 views in about 14 hours. And the most common comment is fans noticing how similar Hunni and Draymond look.

“Daddy’s genes are strong. Happy birthday little girl. 🎉,” one X user wrote in a comment to the original X post.

“The eyebrows and side eye 😂 that’s his baby,” added another fan.

“Got his whole face lol,” wrote a third.

The apple clearly did not fall far from the tree in the Green family, at least in the looks department.
