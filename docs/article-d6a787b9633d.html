'American Idol' star Bo Bice trades signature rocker look for new image as fans sound off

2026-10-31 20:17

Long-haired rocker Bo Bice is a thing of the past — at least for now.

The former "American Idol" runner-up drew mixed reactions from fans after unveiling a dramatic transformation on Instagram.

Bice traded his familiar long-haired rocker look for a shorter hairstyle, pairing it with sunglasses, layered necklaces and an open patterned shirt.

"Very different look but I would suggest to ditch the necklaces and button up the shirt," one Instagram user commented.

Another Instagram user was even less impressed, writing, "I don't care at all for the new look! It's awful. Do, however, like to [hear] him sing. Not onto that look at all! YUK!"

Bice appeared to embrace the change, sharing a split-screen "Then and Now" photo that compared his longtime rocker image with his new look.

"Well, well, Tinkerbell... Who's THAT guy?!" he joked in the Instagram caption. The graphic featured the words "A BRAND NEW MAN · RESURRECTED 2027."

Despite the criticism, other fans praised Bice's new appearance.

"Look good! Glad you are back!" one person wrote.

Another commented, "You looked good then and now. Keep rockin!"

"Let's go Bo!! I was cheering for you then & now as well!!" a third fan added.

Bice also hinted at a musical comeback after a two-year music hiatus.

"Ain't it funny what 2 years'll do in your life," he wrote. "I haven't released new music since Born of Heroes Blood - Hollywood Independent Music Awards for Most Impactful Song of the Year - Vocalist- Bo Bice."

"Here's a blast from my past and a glimpse into my future with a new life, new style, but the same southern soul. I'm here to offer you a #BRANDNEWMAN!"

Bice rose to fame during the fourth season of "American Idol" in 2005, finishing as runner-up to Carrie Underwood.

His gritty vocals, Southern rock sound and signature long hair helped make him one of the season's most recognizable contestants.
