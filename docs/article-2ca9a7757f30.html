Hunter Biden announces he ‘may have to’ run for president during radio interview

2026-09-05 23:40

Hunter Biden may try to follow in his father’s footsteps into the Oval Office, he revealed in an upcoming interview.

The scandal-scorned former first son, 56, said he “may have to” run for president in 2028 during an interview with billionaire supermarket mogul John Catsimatidis on his WABC Cats Roundtable radio show airing Sunday.

“If I get your [Catsimatidis’] endorsement, I may have to [run for president]. The things I care most about is the 50 million Americans that are still suffering with their addictions,” Biden said.

Biden, a self-described “former crackhead” who was convicted on federal gun and tax charges, said that his top qualification for becoming president is “adhering to the rule of law.”

“Here is my qualification for becoming president, if you believe in the Constitution of the United States of America that you swear to uphold and protect. I want the next president to commit to one thing: to adhering to the rule of law,” he said.

Becoming commander in chief would be the first time Biden would enter America’s military structure since he was booted from the Navy in 2014 after testing positive for cocaine when he was 44 years old.

The Delaware-born artist’s previous foray into a presidential campaign came via the “Laptop from Hell” revelations published in The Post — which detailed evidence he allegedly trafficked on his father’s position as vice president to enrich his family.

Biden’s laptop also contained countless explicit photos of him with scantily clad women, performing lurid acts or taking drugs and was used as evidence in his gun and tax offense trials.

Though he has five children with three different women — including a former stripper — Biden recently disclosed that he is “48.6 percent” gay on the Luke Beasley Show podcast.

Ultimately, the former lobbyist didn’t commit to running for president and said that the Democrats have an “incredible bench.”

“I love Gavin Newsom, personally,” he said.
