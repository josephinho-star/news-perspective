Martha Stewart is turning a California casino into her own winter wonderland

2026-11-18 00:05

The holiday season kicks off a bit early this week, at least at Yaamava’ Resort & Casino at San Manuel, with the help of the iconic Martha Stewart.

The 85 year old will kick off Martha’s Forest of Wonder Wednesday, described as “a resort-wide immersive holiday experience,” according to the resort.

Stewart helped design the experience, which features a number of pieces from her personal collection, which culminates in a special tree-lighting ceremony.

There will also be a Legends Dinner, with tickets selling for over $1,000, though they now appear to be sold out.

The dinner “will feature a menu she personally curated, paired with the kind of storytelling and hospitality expertise that has defined her decades-long career,” according to the press release.

There will also be a live Q&A following the dinner, moderated by Jay Leno, with tickets still available for that via AXS ranging from $59 to $113, though only two sections are still available.

“I’m thrilled to partner with Yaamava’ to create a holiday experience that captures the beauty, warmth and wonder of the season,” Stewart said in a statement. “From the festive décor to an evening shared with guests, I hope this celebration inspires cherished holiday memories.”

“The holidays are a time for gathering, creating meaningful memories, and celebrating traditions both old and new,” General Manager of Yaamava’ Resort & Casino Kenji Hall said.

“Partnering with Martha Stewart allows us to bring a truly one-of-a-kind experience to our guests, filled with beauty, wonder, and the spirit of the season in a way only Yaamava’ can,” Hall added.

Martha’s Forest of Wonder “will transform Yaamava’ into a sparkling woodland retreat, immersing guests in a curated color palette of brass, cranberry, forest green, pearl white, sage green and snow.”

The installation will take over the entire property, featuring items such as The Great Woodland Wreath, “an oversized ethereal wreath framed by freestanding bears and trees with a dedicated guest photo opportunity.”

Other items of note are The Mushroom Grove, “a glowing, moss-covered woodland scene at the high-limit table games area” and Guardians of the Woodland, “a pair of sentinel spiraling trees framing the high-limit slots entrance.”

Fans will also be able to partake in Martha’s Sleigh Ride, “an enchanted birch forest scene featuring Stewart’s personal sleigh making its way through a winter landscape.”

“Illuminated woodland creatures” are also set to “appear throughout the property, bringing the forest to life for guests of all ages.”

The tree-lighting ceremony Wednesday will be limited to invited guests only, though public access to the experience will follow immediately after the ceremony.
