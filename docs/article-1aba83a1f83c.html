Drone nearly strikes Zelensky’s plane before takeoff for Norwegian king’s funeral, PM says

2026-09-10 04:06

A plane carrying Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky to Norway for the king’s state funeral was “almost hit” by a drone as it prepared to take off from Moldova on Tuesday, according to Reuters.

Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Støre told public broadcaster NRK about the incident Wednesday, the outlet said, after Zelensky’s aircraft arrived safely in Oslo Tuesday evening.

“His plane was almost hit by a drone as it was about to take off from Moldova. That is the reality he is facing,” Støre said, without specifying how close the drone came to the Ukrainian leader’s aircraft.

Later Wednesday, a Moldovan government spokesperson said two Russian drones were involved in the incident, with Zelensky’s departure to Oslo delayed after authorities closed the country’s airspace.

His aircraft was then cleared for takeoff once officials determined that one Russian drone had landed on Moldovan territory, a spokesperson told Reuters.

One landed in a field north of the capital, sparking a fire, while the other continued into Romanian airspace.

The incursions forced Chișinău International Airport to temporarily suspend arrivals and departures Tuesday, sending incoming flights into holding patterns or diverting them to neighboring countries, according to tracking data.

Zelensky arrived in Oslo Tuesday evening, where he discussed military aid, including air defense support and civilian assistance with Foreign Minister Espen Barth Eide and Finance Minister Jens Stoltenberg, according to Deutsche Welle.

The Institute for the Study of War also said a Russian Shahed drone flew through Moldovan and Romanian airspace for about an hour Tuesday before crashing in Moldova.
