Germany is poised to enter one of its most dangerous moments yet

2026-08-31 02:00

John Kampfner is a British author, broadcaster and commentator. His new book, “Braver New World: The Countries Daring to Do Things Others Won’t,” was published in April. He is a regular POLITICO columnist.

Picking up a recent issue of German weekly Der Spiegel, the cover seemed to scream. “Germany’s Most Dangerous Man,” it shouted, the bold words emblazoned below a photo of the far-right Alternative for Germany party’s (AfD) Ulrich Siegmund.

The intention was clear: A warning for both Germans and the world regarding the consequences of a far-right victory in the small eastern state of Saxony-Anhalt, as Siegmund seems poised to become his party’s first regional leader after the upcoming election there.
