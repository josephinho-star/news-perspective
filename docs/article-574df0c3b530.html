2026-27 Calder Trophy picks, odds: Fade favorites Gavin McKenna, Porter Martone

2026-09-25 15:00

Every year, it seems like the NHL’s rookie class gets better and better.

In 2023, we saw the arrival of Connor Bedard and Leo Carlsson; the next year, we got introduced to Macklin Celebrini, Lane Hutson, and Cutter Gauthier; and last season was the coming-out party for Matthew Schaefer and Ivan Demidov.

The 2026-27 debutantes have big skates to fill, and it’s unlikely any player in this group will match what we saw from Schaefer last season, but there is reason to believe that this year’s crop of rookies will be the deepest we’ve seen in a long time.

That means the race for the Calder Trophy will be wide open.

Calder Trophy favorites, picks

The bookmakers at bet365 Sportsbook believe that there are two clear favorites to win the Calder: Philadelphia’s Porter Martone and Toronto’s Gavin McKenna.

It should surprise nobody that these two wunderkinds top the board.

Martone turned plenty of heads last spring when he posted 10 points (4G, 6A) in nine contests in the regular season and then added five more in 10 playoff games.

The No. 6 overall pick will almost certainly land a top-six role with Philadelphia out of the gate, and he’s already proven he’s up to NHL speed.

With 19 games of high-stakes NHL action already under his belt, it’s sensible that the market has pegged Martone as a +270 favorite.

The price is too short for my liking in an award that is extremely volatile, but I can’t argue with the logic of putting Martone in the catbird seat.

It’s much more of a guessing game with McKenna.

Selected No. 1 overall this June by the Maple Leafs, McKenna certainly has the skill to be an impact rookie, but his size is a concern.

Generously listed at 170 pounds, the Whitehorse, Northwest Territories native will have to prove he can handle the rigors of an 84-game NHL season.

All of that uncertainty makes his +300 price quite unappealing.

The good news about having two players priced this short in a volatile market is that it means you will be able to grab some lofty numbers on other rookies with a real shot at being on the ballot:

2026-27 Calder Trophy picks, best bets

Viggo Bjorck, Winnipeg Jets (40/1, Fanatics)

I’m having a hard time seeing why Viggo Bjorck isn’t listed among the favorites to win this award.

The Jets have made it pretty clear that the 18-year-old Swede will get every chance to be the team’s No. 2 center, which gives him a massive edge over plenty of other rookies who are toss-ups to even make their club’s Opening Night roster.

Getting the opportunity is one thing, but Bjorck also seems poised to take it and run with it.

The No. 8 overall pick from the 2026 Draft has not looked out of place in preseason play, and has the potential to become one of the stories of the league this season.

Albert Smits, New York Rangers (150/1, Caesars)

Like Bjorck, Albert Smits seems like he will get every chance to claim a regular role for the Rangers.

The 18-year-old was touted as the most NHL-ready defenseman in this year’s draft, and he’s backed up that claim with a strong start in camp and in his first preseason game. That shouldn’t be a surprise, as he was a standout for Latvia at the Olympics, so he’s already proven he can hold his own against the best players in the world.

Smits may not produce the offensive numbers needed to make noise in this market, but that’s baked into this generous price.

Sergei Murashov, Pittsburgh Penguins (300/1, Fanatics)

The Penguins are quite high on Murashov and for good reason.

The Russian netminder posted a .919 save percentage in 39 games in the AHL last season, and he didn’t look out of place during a five-game stint with the big club.
