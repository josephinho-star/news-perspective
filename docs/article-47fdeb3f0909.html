Tyreek Hill fires back at ex-wife over alleged witness tampering in divorce mess

2026-09-08 15:06

Tyreek Hill fired back at his estranged wife Keeta Vaccaro as their months-long divorce saga continues.

The former Dolphins wideout is going after Vaccaro for purportedly publicly and falsely accusing him of witness tampering before their divorce trial, TMZ reported.

Vaccaro claimed Hill refused to answer questions about allegedly paying off a woman who previously accused him of abuse.

She also took issue with Hill recently visiting his ex, Crystal Espinal, whom he shares three children with.

Per court docs, the Super Bowl champ is asking the judge to punish Vaccaro and her lawyers for what he believes are baseless claims.

Vaccaro, who had daughter, Capri, with Hill in 2024, alleged last month he did not answer if he offered, promised or paid “any consideration” to Espinal.

She also claimed Hill had not spoken to Espinal for over five years.

However, that came before Hill’s attorney reached out to Espinal one week before her deposition in the divorce, allegedly telling her there “needs to be trust” between them.

Court documents then claimed the eight-time Pro Bowler took a flight to Arkansas to visit Espinal and their children for the first time in two years.

Vaccaro claimed Espinal spoke favorably about Hill in the deposition despite previously accusing him of child abuse in 2019 and assaulting her while pregnant. Hill has denied those allegations.

In his filing, Hill said Vaccaro’s documents identified “no threat, no misleading statement and no offer of any benefit — because there was none.”

He added that she has not come up with evidence that he used intimidation or physical force, initiated misleading conduct or paid someone off to hold them from testifying in the divorce case.

The old flames got married in November 2023 before Vaccaro filed for divorce from in 2025.

It was filed only one day after police were called to a domestic disturbance at the couple’s condominium in Sunny Isles Beach, Fla.

She told authorities she witnessed Hill throw a laptop on the floor and grab the couple’s daughter before walking toward the balcony of the condo.

Hill admitted to police that he grabbed his daughter, but argued he has “the same rights as a father as her as a mother, and I can take my baby whenever I want,” the report stated.

The 32-year-old is a free agent ahead of his potential 11th NFL season and aims to bounce back after suffering a season-ending knee injury during Week 4 in 2025.
