Woman claims she lost almost 30 pounds after making a single change to her routine

2026-09-14 21:01

She quit the crash diets, added a couple of shakes and then finally lost the weight.

It almost sounds too good to be true, but according to a 36-year-old Florida woman named Denise, that’s all it took for her to shed 29.2 pounds.

The change she made was simple. She added two of The Collagen Co.’s protein-packed Glow Shakes into her daily routine.

According to The Collagen Co., a science-backed beauty nutrition company out of Australia, the tasty meal replacement drinks contain a blend of clinically backed, nutrient-packed ingredients, including 29 grams of high-quality protein alongside hydrolyzed collagen peptides, prebiotic fibre, MCT oil and 24 vitamins and minerals.

The shake-and-sip drinks are formulated to support overall health and wellness, and countless reviewers rave that they see results in less than a month. According to the company, 91% of users experienced weight loss taking Glow Shakes — Denise included.

“My experience with the Glow Shakes was truly life-changing,” Denise emphasized. “Before starting the shakes, I was crash dieting, and even though I saw progress, I felt awful. My diet was imbalanced, heavier on carbs and light on protein. I had no energy.”

Denise began incorporating the drinks back in January, swapping her typical breakfast and lunch for a Glow Shake instead.

“Suddenly, I had a simple, protein-forward meal replacement that gave me energy and motivation,” she added.

Denise believes it was the consistency that helped her achieve the astounding weight loss result. Fortunately, the Glow Shakes are offered in a variety of flavors, so it’s easy to change things up if you’re prone to getting bored of eating the same thing over again.

Denise’s favorite flavor is coffee, and she says she “literally cannot get enough” of it. Each serving of Iced Coffee Glow Shakes also contains 120mg of caffeine from natural coffee for an added energy boost.

Glow Shakes are also gluten-free, soy-free and made without artificial flavours or additives — ideal for a variety of dietary needs. Plus, they’re backed by dietitians and thousands of customer reviews.

Each pack contains 14 meal replacements, and a scoop is included for easy preparation.
