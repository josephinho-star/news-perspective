Ryan Garcia vs. Conor Benn odds, prediction: Best bet, full card for Saturday’s main event

2026-09-12 20:00

Two of boxing’s larger-than-life personalities clash at 147 pounds Saturday, as reigning WBC welterweight champion Ryan Garcia (25-2, 20 KOs, 1 NC) defends his belt against Conor Benn (25-1, 14 KOs) on a nine-fight card put together by Zuffa Boxing.

The main event bout will be held at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas, with the preliminary card starting at 5 p.m. ET and the main card slated to begin at 8 p.m. ET.

In the co-main event, Jai Opetaia defends his “The Ring” and Zuffa cruiserweight titles against WBC cruiserweight titleholder Noel Mikaelian in a 12-round showdown.

Garcia vs. Benn prediction, best bet

Garcia is coming off an impressive unanimous decision win over Mario Barrios in February, where his remarkable hand speed and power were clear against the experienced Barrios.

This performance marked a solid comeback following his second career loss to Rolly Romero and a no contest against Devin Haney in 2025.

Garcia weighed in at 145.5 pounds and has a reach advantage, measuring 70 inches compared to Benn’s 68 inches.

The London-born Benn has won his last two bouts, recently defeating Regis Prograis in April and avenging his loss to longtime rival Chris Eubank Jr. last November.

Now, Benn returns to the United States hoping to follow in his father Nigel’s footsteps and pull off a significant world championship upset on North American soil.

However, he faces a couple of challenges. This will be his first fight at 147 pounds since 2022 and Garcia appears to have regained the form he exhibited earlier in his career.

“It’s been four-and-a-half years since I made it, and it feels like I’ve been through the pits of hell and had a fight with Satan, and God brought me back to life. That’s how it feels to make this weight. But ultimately, I’ve done it, and that’s it,” Benn said at Wednesday’s press conference.
