NFL Week 4 player props, picks, best bets

Christian Watson is perhaps my favorite wide receiver to back this week.

Some fantasy football experts, including FantasyLife’s Dwain McFarland, have him ranked among the top 10 receivers of the week, with McFarland ranking him No. 7.

Watson has vowed to put on a show in Tampa Bay this weekend, as it is his homecoming, having been from the area. Watson is fifth in the league in yards per route run, while the Buccaneers are 20th in DVOA against the pass.

TreVeyon Henderson over 49.5 rushing yards (37.6 percent, Kalshi) | 90+ rushing yards (+1040, DraftKings)

It’s befuddling that Rhamondre Stevenson is still the starter in New England, but TreVeyon Henderson has an impressive history against the Buffalo Bills.

Last year, Henderson really put his best foot forward against the Bills, rushing for 148 yards and two touchdowns on 14 carries in a game Stevenson also played in.

I’m buying low on Henderson, as I believe he will put together a big game on Sunday in a must-win game where they need him involved.
