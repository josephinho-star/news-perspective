SpaceX earnings live updates: Starlink growth, AI revenue and merger speculation

2026-10-20 13:33

SpaceX is set to announce third-quarter results after the bell on Tuesday, the second earnings report for Elon Musk's defense tech conglomerate since its record IPO in June.

Here's what analysts are expecting, according to LSEG consensus estimates:

- Revenue: $12.72 billion expected

- Earnings per share: 13 cents expected

While SpaceX is best known for its reusable rockets, most of its revenue and profit in recent quarters came from the company's connectivity segment, which consists of its Starlink satellite internet service.

Buzz is building about Starlink's plans to build a U.S. network after SpaceX, earlier this month, announced that it's buying 800 MHz of low-band spectrum, and after the Federal Communications Commission approved a plan for the company to build out a 15,000-satellite constellation.

The moves strengthen "Starlink Mobile's ability to compete with major carriers by enabling broader, deeper coverage," RBC Capital Markets analysts wrote in a note ahead of earnings. The firm has a $225 price target on SpaceX stock and the equivalent of a buy rating.

In the third quarter, analysts are expecting AI revenue of $6.47 billion, connectivity sales of $4.84 billion, and revenue in the space business of $1.41 billion, according to StreetAccount.

During the third quarter, SpaceX also closed its $60 billion purchase of AI coding startup Cursor.
