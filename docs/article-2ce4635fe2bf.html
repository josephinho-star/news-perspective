Richard Sherman makes bold prediction for Sunday’s NFC West showdown

2026-11-25 03:54

If anyone has an idea of what it’s like to play for the Seahawks and 49ers, it’s Richard Sherman.

For the second time this season, the teams will clash, and Sherman was asked who he believes will win Sunday at Levi’s Stadium.

“I think this is going to be a fantastic, fun game to watch,” Sherman said. “I just think it’s going to be too hard to bet against the San Francisco 49ers right now with how they’re rolling and how well they’re playing offensively and defensively.”

The last time the teams met was in early October; they were nearly even until the fourth quarter, when San Francisco pulled ahead with a 7-yard touchdown pass from Brock Purdy to Brandin Cooks and followed it with a two-point conversion by Mike Evans to make the score, 29-26.

Seattle kicked a field goal to tie the game, but the 49ers kicked a field goal on their ensuing possession and prevented Seattle from scoring on its final offensive drive — holding on for a 32-29 victory.

“[Kyle Shanahan] is looking at that first game and watching things; he could’ve done better. That Brock could have done better. They could’ve called better in terms of offensive line and protections, to protect Brock better,” Sherman said. “I’m looking forward to seeing how it goes.”

Purdy completed 19 of 29 passes for 189 yards, with two touchdowns, one interception and three sacks.

One of the reasons Sherman believes San Francisco can win is if Purdy doesn’t turn the ball over.

Purdy leads the NFL in quarterback rating (84.6) and has thrived protecting the football, having only five interceptions and a completion percentage of 71.

San Francisco is coming off a 35-10 victory against the Vikings. Purdy completed 21 of 26 passes for 255 yards and three touchdowns.

Sherman added another reason why he believes the Niners will win: their revamped defense.

The last time the teams played, Nick Bosa was on injured reserve and DeForest Buckner played for the Colts.

Bosa was activated from the IR before Week 10’s game against the Cowboys, and Buckner was sent over to San Francisco before the trade deadline.

Bosa spent most of Sunday’s game in the Minnesota backfield, harassing quarterback Kirk Cousins and sacking him. Buckner had half a sack and two tackles.

“In the end, I think this is going to be a really close game like the first one was. I think Brock Purdy is going to be better in this game,” Sherman said. “Sam Darnold, I still got some question marks about. Obviously, he’s not having the best season in the world. Jaxon Smith-Njigba, I think Raheem Morris will have a focus on Jaxon Smith-Njigba.”
