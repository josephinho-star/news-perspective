'Brought the star power': 49ers' petty posts aimed at Cowboys top NFL Week 10 trolls

2026-11-16 20:44

The NFL's oldest rivalries bring plenty of history, memories and gut-wrenching wins and losses. It's what makes the games more valuable -- and the trolls follow suit.

The San Francisco 49ers put on an offensive show against the Dallas Cowboys on Sunday in a 46-40 victory.

San Francisco had 584 total yards of offense, the most yards Dallas has allowed in a home game in franchise history, according to ESPN Research. 49ers running back Christian McCaffrey had 262 yards, plus three total touchdowns. He's the first 49ers player with at least 200 yards from scrimmage and three touchdowns in a regular season game since Jerry Rice in 1995.

The 49ers poked fun at the Cowboys multiple times with Texas- and Dallas-specific posts, including cowboy boots outside of AT&T Stadium with a 49ers cowboy hat on top of them. In one post, lyrics from country star Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" were in the caption, while another caption read: "Brought the star power."

Here are the top trolls from Week 10.

The Buffalo Bills have won five in a row after their victory over the New York Jets.

Buffalo kept New York out of the end zone and scored four touchdowns -- three total from quarterback Josh Allen, and running back James Cook III had the other. The Bills' 8-1 record ties their second-best nine-game start in franchise history. Five of Buffalo's wins have come on the road, including Sunday.

The Bills play in the western New York area, not far from Canada, and have embraced that region by including the Canadian national anthem at their home opener this season. Buffalo claimed more than a win after beating New York. It wrote on X after the game: "PSA: the Buffalo Bills are the only football team in New York." with a smiling face at the end.

PSA: the Buffalo Bills are the only football team in New York. 🙂

— Buffalo Bills (@BuffaloBills) November 15, 2026

Divisional matchups tend to bring the best out of each team on and off the field. The Carolina Panthers epitomized that after a last-second win against the New Orleans Saints.

With the score tied and 41 seconds left in the game, Panthers kicker Ryan Fitzgerald nailed it from 54 yards to clinch the victory and snap Carolina's three-game losing streak.

New Orleans is home to the French Quarter, which boasts Mardi Gras annually. The Panthers put their spin on the location with a graphic of their mascot dragging a parade boat through the location and the caption: "The Carolina Quarter."

The Los Angeles Rams took care of business versus the Arizona Cardinals for a commanding road victory.

Los Angeles held Arizona scoreless after the first quarter and outgained Arizona 436-123 in total yards in the win. Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford had 249 passing yards and three touchdowns. Cardinals signal-caller Carson Beck struggled in his debut with 108 passing yards and two interceptions.

Los Angeles utilized an Angry Birds-themed post to poke fun at its opponent.

The Seattle Seahawks won their second straight after a road meeting with the Las Vegas Raiders.

Seattle scored 14 points in the third quarter to go ahead for good after falling behind 10-7 heading into halftime. The Seahawks held the Raiders to just 163 yards and sacked quarterback Fernando Mendoza -- making his debut as starter -- six times.

The city of Las Vegas has its unique quirks, including the "Welcome to Las Vegas" sign. The Seahawks put their spin on various Las Vegas elements to poke fun at the Raiders.

The Indianapolis Colts' victory over the Miami Dolphins ended Indianapolis' five-game losing streak.

Miami received possession with 3:15 left in the game and a chance to take the lead. But Indianapolis forced a turnover on downs, then ran the clock down to clinch it.

The big win came with a simple troll postgame, as the Colts referenced the Dolphins' "fins" nickname. They instead wrote "Phin" in their graphic.
