How To Watch NFL Games on Paramount+ For Free

2026-09-13 14:30

The NFL is everywhere this season. Monday Night Football games air on ESPN and ABC; Thursday Night Football matchups will mostly stream on Prime Video; and Sunday Night Football once again belongs to NBC and Peacock.

Just like previous seasons, the Sunday slate of games will air on CBS and FOX, but you can also watch those two networks live with an active subscription to DIRECTV, Fubo, Hulu + Live TV, YouTube TV, or Sling TV. Plus, let’s not forget that select games will air on Netflix and NFL Network, and there’s also NFL RedZone, Sunday Ticket, and NFL+.

I don’t know about you, but we’re dizzy.

One final option for NFL action is Paramount+. If you’ve cut the cord but are still looking for a CBS live stream, this streaming platform is for you. Here’s how to watch NFL games on Paramount+.

Can I Watch NFL Games on Paramount+?

Yes! You can watch all NFL games airing on CBS live on Paramount+. The NFL website also has a helpful tool that shows you what games you can watch on Paramount+ — as well as other streaming platforms that offer NFL games — each week in your area.

Are NFL Games Available on The Paramount+ Essential Plan?

You bet. While a CBS live stream is only available for Paramount+ Premium subscribers, all NFL on CBS games and UFC fights will also stream live on Paramount+’s ad-supported Essential plan.

How To Watch NFL Games on Paramount+:

Paramount+ offers two subscription plans, Essential and Premium. Right now, you can get started with a seven-day free trial on the Premium plan.

The ad-supported Essential plan typically costs $8.99/month, while the ad-free Premium plan (which comes with Showtime titles and live CBS) costs $13.99/month.

Does Paramount+ Offer a Free Trial?

Not directly, but you can add Paramount+ to your Hulu or Amazon account for $13.99/month. Both streamers offer free trials for new subscribers.

You can also subscribe to Paramount+ through YouTube, which offers a free trial.
