Sicko who bragged he was ‘into underage girls and incest’ handed 30-year sentence for child porn

2026-11-26 01:09

An Oklahoma sicko, who once bragged in a message that he was “into underage girls and incest,” admitted his guilt in a child pornography case and was sentenced to 30 years in prison, officials announced.

Jason Dean Poledouris on Monday copped to seven charges of having and distributing troves of the depraved materials, according to the Oklahoma State Bureau of Investigation.

The 54-year-old Owasso man had hundreds of images and dozens of videos of girls getting raped and sexually assaulted — and one vile video even included bestiality, according to an arrest warrant affidavit from July 29.

One photo showed a partially clothed girl smoking a cigarette, the affidavit alleged.

The disturbing materials were found on his two cell phones and social media accounts, including on at least seven accounts on the messaging app Kik, the affidavit alleged.

Poledouris allegedly messaged others on the app about his perverted desires, sent others the twisted images, and once sent a 13-year-old girl child porn imagery on Jan. 1, the affidavit claimed.

“I’m into underage girls and incest as well,” he disgustingly boasted in a message sent on the platform.

In messages, Poledouris discussed sexually abusing kids, including a child that was expected to be born in May and said he abused children in his family as well, the affidavit claims.

He also said he’d gotten a 13-year-old girl high and had sex with her and taught a 4-year-old how to engage in sex acts, the affidavit alleged.

He also allegedly gave an adult male the identity and phone number of a 13-year-old so that the man could exchange nudes with the preteen, the affidavit said.

And Poledouris allegedly contacted his ex, telling her he asked two minors to have sex in exchange for money.

A probe was opened for Poledouris after multiple tips came into the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children. He was ultimately connected to over 20 such cybertips, investigators said.

After his July 31 arrest, Poledouris told investigators he had over 20 Kik accounts and that he’d been banned from the platform, the affidavit alleged.

He told cops he was transitioning to become a woman and was going by Rebecca or Becca. Some of his usernames included transtweaker25.1., tweakerbarbie1, wowbanned4what now, beccathetweaker, Becca’s_pregnant yup 3 months, the affidavit alleged.

Poledouris told investigators he served 10 years for possession of methamphetamine. Cops discovered he had prior convictions for driving under suspension in 2026 and for drug possession and stalking in California where he previously lived, the affidavit said.

Poledouris’ public defender didn’t immediately return a request for comment Wednesday.
