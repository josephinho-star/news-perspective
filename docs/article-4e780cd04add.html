LeBron James, Jessica Alba and Tarik Skubal lead star-studded crowd for ‘Monday Night Football’

2026-09-22 02:25

The stars were out at SoFi Stadium on Monday night, and not all of them were wearing helmets.

The Los Angeles Rams’ primetime showdown with the New York Giants brought plenty of celebrities to watch the action.

Actress Jessica Alba was spotted in attendance alongside boyfriend and actor Danny Ramirez, while new Los Angeles Dodgers ace Tarik Skubal took in his first Rams game since joining the back-to-back World Series champions.

Skubal’s teammate Mookie Betts was also in attendance and shared a suite with Rams’ defensive end Myles Garrett who underwent knee surgery last Monday and will miss at least the next four weeks on injured reserve.

Then there was LeBron James, the former longtime Lakers superstar and newest member of the Philadelphia 76ers, who apparently couldn’t resist doing a little quarterbacking from his suite.

After Matthew Stafford dropped down and fired a ridiculous sidearm touchdown pass to Davante Adams, cameras caught James mimicking Stafford’s throwing motion.

If the King wasn’t enough, cameras caught the Great One himself, Wayne Gretzky, sharing a suite with ESPN’s Pat McAfee, former Philadelphia Eagles center Jason Kelce, and NBA legends Russell Westbrook and DeMar DeRozan.

Rams owner Stan Kroenke made an appearance on the sideline before kickoff, surveying the scene inside his $5-billion football palace, while former Dallas Cowboys quarterback and current ESPN broadcaster Troy Aikman worked the field before sliding into the “Monday Night Football” booth.

“Breaking Bad” stars Bryan Cranston and Aaron Paul, partners of award-winning mezcal and tequila brand Dos Hombres, were also in attendance.

Seven-time Olympic gold medal winner Allyson Felix enjoyed watching Stafford throw touchdowns as did comedian Trevor Wallace who watched the game from the Bootsy Bellows club on the field.
