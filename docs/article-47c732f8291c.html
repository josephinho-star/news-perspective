No 1 Florida loses to Houston as four of the preseason AP top five fall before Thanksgiving

2026-11-19 00:55

Houston had waited since April 2025 for another shot at Florida. On Wednesday, the Cougars made sure this one would not be decided in the final minute.

No. 8 Houston beat No. 1 Florida 86-76 in Las Vegas, advancing to the Players Era 8 championship game and handing the Gators their first loss of the season. Florida had beaten Houston 65-63 in the 2025 national championship game. Florida’s loss also means four of the preseason AP top five have already been beaten, with Duke losing twice.

Florida, which was the AP preseason No. 1 and entered the game 5-0, led 40-37 at halftime. Houston took the lead early in the second half before Florida briefly regained the lead at 47-46. But Houston scored the next 11 points and never trailed again, building a lead as large as 14 points.

Dedan Thomas Jr. scored 30 for the Cougars, while Joseph Tugler recorded a 12-point, 10-rebound double-double.

Kelvin Sampson’s Houston program entered the game ranked eighth and has been among the sport’s contenders for years. Still, beating the team that denied Houston a national title, and doing it while that team sat at No. 1, gives the Cougars a massive November resume boost.

It also makes the analytics look pretty smart. The AP voters had Houston 10th in the preseason, but KenPom rated the Cougars third. After beating Florida by 10 on a neutral floor, Houston looks a lot closer to No. 3 than No. 10.

Florida joins Duke, Illinois and Arizona as preseason AP top five teams that have already lost less than three weeks into the college basketball season. UConn is the lone unbeaten member of that group, at least heading into its Wednesday night visit to Arizona.

Arizona was the first to fall, losing 84-75 to UCLA on opening night. The Wildcats are ranked No. 12 in this week's poll. A week later, Michigan State beat then-No. 2 Duke 79-73, while Texas Tech knocked off then-No. 3 Illinois 84-78.

Illinois responded Tuesday by going into Duke and winning by 21. The Blue Devils, the preseason No. 2, have now lost twice before Thanksgiving. The Michigan State loss only dropped Duke from No. 2 to No. 5, but expect another fall for the Blue Devils when the Week 4 rankings come out on Nov. 23.

Michigan State’s stay at No. 2 may be short, too. After beating Duke, the Spartans managed just 46 points in a 61-46 loss at No. 14 Tennessee on Tuesday.

And the trouble has reached beyond the preseason favorites. Unranked Memphis opened its season by beating No. 18 Iowa State 80-76. The Tigers followed that by erasing an 18-point deficit against No. 10 Louisville on Sunday and winning 98-78 after outscoring Louisville by 31 points in the second half.

Over the weekend, LSU went to Gonzaga and beat the then-No. 14 Bulldogs 81-80 on a late shot. On Tuesday, Indiana added an 81-76 win over No. 20 Arkansas.

Kentucky can be grateful it is missing from that list. The Wildcats, who entered the game as 30-point favorites, needed a late three-pointer to escape Northern Arizona 78-77 at Rupp Arena last week, a close call that fit the season’s mood even if it didn't count as another upset.

None of this settles who will matter in March because there is still a lot of basketball to play. I'm certainly not ready to write off Florida, Duke, Arizona or Illinois because of an early-season hiccup (or two, in Duke's case).

Still, that’s five losses for the preseason AP top five. The voters put Florida first and Michigan State second on Monday. By Wednesday night, both had lost by double digits. Good luck with next Monday’s ballot (If the AP has a spare one, I’ll take it).
