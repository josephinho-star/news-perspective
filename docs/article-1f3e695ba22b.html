New footage challenges reported lion sighting in Istanbul

2026-11-24 06:32

An animal filmed in Istanbul’s Sarıyer district was neither a lion nor another large cat, the governor’s office said on Nov. 23, following searches prompted by a reported sighting.

Officials reached the assessment after examining new footage taken from different angles. Searches on the ground and from the air also found no concrete evidence of a lion in the area, the statement said.

The operation began after authorities received a report on the evening of Nov. 22 that a lion had been seen in woodland above sports facilities in the Uskumruköy neighborhood.

Teams from the Nature Conservation and National Parks Directorate, the gendarmerie and disaster management agency AFAD joined veterinarians from Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality and Sarıyer Municipality in the search.

They used drones equipped with thermal cameras, placed bait and installed motion-activated camera traps.

Authorities restricted access to woodland and nearby residential areas during the operation. Belgrad Forest was closed to pedestrians and vehicles on Nov. 23 as teams widened their search.

Earlier that day, Sarıyer Mayor Mustafa Oktay Aksu said teams were searching five locations across three neighborhoods after an overnight thermal-camera search failed to detect the animal.

Further security camera footage published late on Nov. 23 showed an unidentified animal emerging from bushes. Its species remained unclear.

The governor’s office said it was assessing reports in coordination with the relevant agencies to protect public safety. Teams remained ready to reassess the situation if new information emerged.
