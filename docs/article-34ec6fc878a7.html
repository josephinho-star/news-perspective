2 NYC public-school paraprofessionals busted — one for punching boy, 6; the other for kissing girl, 7: authorities

2026-11-18 00:13

Two city public-school paraprofessionals were just busted — one for punching a 6-year-old boy and the other for kissing a 7-year-old female special-needs student on the lips, cops and prosecutors said Tuesday.

Miguel Sanchez, 35, is accused of striking the little boy inside the cafeteria of PS 63 Author’s Academy on Franklin Avenue near East 169th Street in Morrisania in The Bronx around noon Friday, according to authorities and sources.

The young student refused medical attention, police said. It was not clear what if anything provoked the attack.

Sanchez was arrested around 9:40 a.m. Tuesday and charged with assault and endangering the welfare of a child, authorities said.

Sanchez’s arrest comes about three weeks after Pasquale Papandrea, 57, employed by PS 25 on Hylan Boulevard near Kenny Road in Staten Island, grabbed a 7-year-old girl’s face and kissed her on the lips, according to police and prosecutors.

The girl’s mother reported the act to police, according to a criminal complaint.

The school enrolls special-needs students, law-enforcement sources said.

Papandrea was charged with sexual abuse and acting in a manner injurious to a child, according to prosecutors.
