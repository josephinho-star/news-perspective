Brad Pitt, Angelina Jolie settle 4-year French winery lawsuit that sparked plane abuse claims

2026-11-25 21:19

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have settled their four-year legal battle over the sale of their French winery, Château Miraval.

"We have settled out of court in the best interests of our family and will not be commenting further. Any comments in media stories attributed to ‘sources’ are in fact not coming from either of us, or anyone close to us," representatives for Pitt and Jolie said in a joint statement Wednesday to Fox News Digital after both sides moved to dismiss claims against each other.

Terms of the settlement were not disclosed.

Pitt first sued Jolie in 2022, after she attempted to sell her company, Nouvel, to Tenute del Mondo, a subsidiary of the Stoli Group, in 2021, effectively transferring her 50% ownership interest in Miraval. The actor has fought back against the sale since 2022, claiming it breached an agreement between the two.

After Pitt sued Jolie, the actress' company accused the actor of running a "vindictive campaign" to "loot" the profitable business since she first filed for divorce in 2016.

Nouvel accused Pitt of "hijacking" Château Miraval and "wasting" the company's assets on unnecessary renovation projects, including spending $1 million on swimming pool renovations, according to court documents previously obtained by Fox News Digital.

Details of their divorce also became known when she filed her cross-complaint, which claimed it was Pitt who "refused to buy her interest unless he received his newly expanded NDA, enforceable by an $8.5 million holdback specifically designed to force her silence about his abuse and cover-up."

The cover-up refers to allegations of an incident that took place on a private plane from France to Los Angeles involving Jolie and her children.

The "Maria" actress also filed a suit against the FBI in April 2022, six years after the bureau launched an investigation into the alleged 2016 altercation. The investigation closed two months after the alleged incident without charges being brought against Pitt.

During the 2016 fight, Pitt allegedly "choked one of the children and struck another in the face" and "grabbed Jolie by the head and shook her."

"Some of the children pleaded with Pitt to stop," according to documents filed in Los Angeles Superior Court. "They were all frightened. Many were crying."

She later dropped the lawsuit against the FBI.

After Jolie filed the cross-complaint, a source close to Pitt told Fox News Digital called the move a "legal attack" which took "a simple business dispute" and turned it into her team's "the latest example of them harming the entire family by making it personal."

Pitt has denied being abusive. He also spoke about that period of his life while on the "Armchair Expert" podcast in June 2025, noting he had gotten sober since then.

"I was pretty much on my back, you know, or on my knees," Pitt said of his decision to get sober. "I was trying anything and everyone. Anything anyone threw at me. It was a particularly difficult time. I needed rebooting. I needed to wake the f--- up in some areas. And it just meant a lot to me."

"I had taken things as far as I could take it, so I removed my drinking privileges," he added later.

In an interview with Esquire earlier this year, he said he was sober for seven years but had started drinking again "in a more restrained manner."

The couple bought a controlling stake in Château Miraval in 2008, and spent time there together throughout their relationship.

The two were expected to face off in court in 2027, with Pitt and his legal team accusing Jolie of dragging out the dispute in April, after she requested to push the trial start date by nine months to late 2027.

"One key witness, Ms. Jolie’s former business manager, Terry Bird, has unfortunately passed away," a joint status report previously obtained by Fox News Digital read. "Plaintiffs understand that another potential key witness, Ms. Jolie’s transactional lawyer, Laurent Schummer, is likely too ill to testify. This is yet another reason that a nine-month delay of the trial date would prejudice Plaintiffs."

Pitt's team added that the ongoing legal issue left him "deprived of the quiet enjoyment of his home in France," and hurt the business, with Jolie's team responding that there is "no evidence anywhere" that Pitt's ability to live in the chateau "has been impacted by this case one iota."

Jolie's team also claimed the request to delay the court proceedings was because of the complexity of the case.

The two actors met in 2004 while filming the movie "Mr. & Mrs. Smith." Pitt and his wife, Jennifer Aniston, later announced they were separating in January 2005, and finalized their divorce later that year. He and Jolie were then spotted in Kenya with her adopted son, Maddox, seemingly confirming their relationship.

Over the course of their relationship, Pitt adopted Maddox, and together went on to adopt Zahara and Pax, and also welcomed three biological children; daughter Shiloh and twins Knox and Vivienne.

They tied the knot in August 2014 at Château Miraval, but Jolie filed for divorce just two years later in September 2016, following the alleged altercation on an airplane.

Fox News Digital's Lauryn Overhultz and The Associated Press contributed to this report.
