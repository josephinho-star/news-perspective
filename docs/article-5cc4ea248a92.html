John Harbaugh endorses Cam Skattebo’s new anti-backflip stance

2026-11-21 00:50

John Harbaugh is celebrating Cam Skattebo’s decision to change how he reacts to scoring a touchdown.

But the Giants head coach is not going to do a backflip to celebrate no more backflips by his running back.

Skattebo told The Post earlier this week that he planned to stop doing his signature backflips unless “we go to the playoffs or something.”

It’s Skattebo’s cautionary response to Lions star Aidan Hutchinson’s season-ending torn ACL, which he suffered last Sunday while jumping in the air to celebrate a sack.

Giants receivers Darnell Mooney and Malachi Fields also have done backflips this season.

“I thought about one guy [Skattebo] maybe to talk to, but then I found out I didn’t have to because he brought it up first,” Harbaugh said. “Yeah, that’s unfortunate [for Hutchinson]. Just knowing Aidan a little bit through Michigan, when my brother coached there, I have great respect for him. Ultimate competitor-type guy and very much of a fluke, but it’s happened before. You have to think about everything.”

The Giants have a clean injury report and dodged a scare Friday when TE Isaiah Likely (groin) did not practice.

Harbaugh called it a “minor thing” and said “he’ll be fine.”

No players on the 53-man roster are listed as out, doubtful or questionable.

DBs Nic Jones (knee) and Eljiah Campbell (knee) — both of whom are key special-teamers — were limited Friday participants.

CB Paulson Adebo was a healthy scratch last game despite carrying the 11th-highest salary cap charge ($24.1 million) for any defensive player in the NFL this season.

Adebo missed five games due to injury, returned to start against the Texans, was a backup against the Eagles and then inactive against the Commanders.

“He had a really good game against the Texans,” Harbaugh said. “But we were healthy again. And so that puts him more in the backup role, and then that backup role ends up being more of a special teams type of a priority for us. And like I told him, ‘In my mind, you’re a starter in this league. And when you started here, you’ve done really well. And you’re going to have to start again.’ So when that time comes, he’s in a good place.”

Adebo didn’t play any special teams snaps as CB1 last season but could be looking at kick coverage as his way back onto the field eventually.

OG Daniel Faalele, whom the Giants traded to the Jaguars in August for a 2027 sixth-round pick, has played 10 snaps on offense and 26 on special teams over four games for his new team.
