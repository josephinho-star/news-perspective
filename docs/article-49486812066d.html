Here’s where to watch Rams vs. Broncos live on Sunday Night Football

2026-09-27 20:20

Week 3 of the NFL season is winding down with a Sunday Night Football matchup between the L.A Rams and the Denver Broncos.

The last time these teams met was on Christmas Day four years ago. The Rams won, 51-14, and the Broncos fired rookie head coach Nathaniel Hackett the next day. It ultimately set in motion the chain of events that led to Denver hiring Sean Payton and drafting quarterback Bo Nix. Now, these teams will face off as two squads who reached their respective conference championships last season.

Coming off a dominant 28-6 victory over the New York Giants in Week 2, the Rams enter Sunday night at 1-1 and looking to stack back-to-back wins. It’s likely they won’t have receiver Puka Nacua back from injury, but they still have Davante Adams, who had 195 yards and two TDs in that MNF win over the Giants.

- When: Sept. 27, 8:20 p.m. ET

- Where: Empower Field at Mile High (Denver, Colorado)

- How to watch: Sling Select and Blue plans

The Broncos also arrive at 1-1 after a hard-fought Week 2 victory over the Jacksonville Jaguars, bouncing back from a tough season opener against Kansas City. Denver will have to lean on its thin-air altitude advantage and rely on Nix and J.K. Dobbins to move the chains against a heavy Rams pass rush.

Here’s what you need to know if you want to tune in to the Rams vs. Broncos game without cable.

Rams vs. Broncos is scheduled to kick off at 8:20 p.m. ET today, Sept. 27, on NBC.

How to watch Rams vs. Broncos

If you don’t have cable, you’ll need a live TV streaming service to stream the Rams vs. Broncos game for free.

Sling TV is an affordable way to watch TV live and stream college football games all season long. Its Select plan includes ABC and starts at $19.99/month; you can also stream NBC live with 40+ other channels on Sling’s Blue plan ($54.99/month).
