Five dead after mine collapse in western Turkey

2026-10-02 08:48

Turkish rescuers on Friday recovered the bodies of three miners trapped in a mine collapse in western Turkey, raising the death toll to five, local authorities said.

“The search and rescue operations carried out after the mine collapse in Soma district have ended and the bodies of the three mine workers who were trapped have been recovered,” the Manisa governor’s office said on X.

The collapse occurred Thursday at an underground coal mine operated by İmbat Madencilik in Soma, in Manisa province, about 100 kilometers (60 miles) northeast of the resort city of İzmir. Turkish media reported that seven workers were caught in the collapse.

Rescue teams initially recovered the bodies of two miners, while two others were rescued and taken to hospital, leaving three workers trapped underground.

Their bodies were recovered early Friday, bringing the death toll to five. One of the two injured workers has since been discharged from hospital, while the other remains under treatment.

Soma was the site of Turkey’s deadliest mining disaster in 2014, when 301 miners died from carbon monoxide poisoning following an underground fire triggered by an explosion.
