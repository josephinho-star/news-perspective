Six people dead and nine injured after fire at Brooklyn home

2026-11-05 19:00

Six people died in a fire that roared through a residential home in New York early on Thursday and nine others were injured, including three firefighters and two police officers, the city authorities said.

The major fire was reported in the early hours in Brooklyn and brought more than 200 first responders rushing to attend to the blaze and those trapped by it, in the city borough’s Sunset Park neighborhood.

It took about three hours to bring the blaze under control after it spread throughout the building and to another home, and the cause was being investigated. Flames tore through a two-storey house after the fire ignited on 58th Street between 6th Avenue and 7th Avenue at about 1.40am on Thursday, witnesses and the authorities said.

A lithium-ion battery, or possibly two, was found at the scene, but it was unknown if that was related to the cause of the fire, the New York fire department (FDNY) said.

However, there has been an increase nationwide of fires sparked by the batteries, which are used in e-bikes, electronic chargers and various household devices.

Zohran Mamdani, the New York mayor, praised firefighters and first responders when he appeared on television early on Thursday.

“We are currently tracking this fire that claimed the lives of six New Yorkers. It is devastating, and it is a reflection of a number of things. One, I want to always acknowledge the courage of our firefighters, who go in every single day, no matter what the level of danger is, looking to save people’s lives,” he said.

Second, the mayor said, he wanted to address fire prevention and safety.

“We are investigating what the cause of this fire is, but I can tell you that e-bike fires as a whole have increased to more than 400 fires this year [in New York]. They are something that can cause people to lose their lives as well as their livelihoods. We are really looking at every avenue, we have to keep them safe,” Mamdani added, as reported by ABC News.

Last week, an e-bike battery fire killed four people in the Bronx.

Four people died at the scene of Thursday’s blaze, the FDNY said, and two people with critical injuries were pronounced dead at the hospital. Four people suffered non-life-threatening injuries.

The nine people who were injured were expected to survive, including the three firefighters and two police officers who had minor injuries, the fire department said. No names or ages of the victims had been released by early Thursday afternoon.

“Upon arrival, we had fire on every floor,” Kevin Woods, FDNY chief of fire operations, said at a news conference near the severely charred wood home later on Thursday. “Fire blowing out every window, meeting us at the front door.”

Firefighters rescued four people, getting through a rear entrance in the two-unit home to get to two people who were trapped behind window bars, he said. A staircase partially collapsed inside the home, he added.

The US Fire Administration said 1,555 people have died in home fires nationwide so far this year.

The Associated Press contributed reporting
