Paraguay’s President Pena impeached over Ueno banking crisis, placed under house arrest

2026-11-06 23:41

The leader had offered to resign, but lawmakers went ahead with proceedings to strip him of immunity granted to former presidents

Paraguayan President Santiago Pena was placed under house arrest on Friday on suspicion of breach of trust, shortly after the Senate removed him from office over the collapse of a major bank tied to his government.

Pena had offered his resignation over the disaster, but lawmakers pressed ahead with impeachment proceedings that would strip him of immunity granted to former presidents.

He did not attend the trial in the Senate despite earlier pledging to defend himself.
