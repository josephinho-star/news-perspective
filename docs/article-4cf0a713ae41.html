Katie Couric shares her daily essentials, from a favorite sunscreen to reading glasses that are actually chic

2026-09-17 17:44

Ever wonder what your favorite celebrities buy when they’re browsing their favorite boutique or shopping online late at night? Welcome to Six Picks, where we ask stars to spill their style and beauty must-haves — so you can shop like you’re famous, too.

When it comes to making it in journalism, Katie Couric literally wrote the book on it.

Over a decade after becoming the first woman to solo anchor a network evening newscast, she’s running her own media company, newsletter and podcast — plus, a book club spotlighting her favorite reads.

So it’s only fitting that her latest project is a limited-edition reading glasses collaboration with Caddis, a brand she’s been shopping for years.

The stylish optical label isn’t the only company she’s currently keeping her eye on, however. Below, Couric shares six of her everyday essentials, from a great SPF to her secret to acing pickleball style.

“I love entertaining and try to sit outside for as many months as we can, and my favorite way to spruce up a tablescape is with these battery-operated candles.”

“And the other thing you need year-round: a good SPF. I have sensitive skin, and this one always does the trick!”

“I love Caddis readers — especially our collab we’ve designed with them for the Katie Couric Book Club. The color is Heirloom (yes, like the tomato) and they’re a fun neutral that will make your eyes pop.”

“Pickleball is still my favorite activity, no matter the season. And lately I’ve been playing in this Lululemon Align Skirt that keeps me stylish and doesn’t get in the way of my game.”

“I am hopping on the Lola blanket train and let me tell you: The influencers are right. These blankets are amazing! And this collab benefits the Morgan Pressel Foundation ahead of Breast Cancer Awareness Month. “

“I just hosted my family for a reunion and having a stash of totes for the beach, the pool or the farmers’ market always makes life easier. I love that Seabags are waterproof and so durable they last for centuries.”
