How to watch Arizona State-Kansas in inaugural Union Jack Classic for free

2026-09-19 12:00

College football is going across the pond.

Arizona State and Kansas will open Big 12 play in the inaugural Union Jack Classic, the first in a series of planned FBS matchups to be played annually in London, England.

The 86,000-seat Wembley Stadium, home of England’s national soccer football team, will host the game.

Watch Arizona State vs. Kansas for free

- What: Union Jack Classic

- When: Sept. 19, 12 p.m. ET

- Where: Wembley Stadium (London, England)

- How to watch for free: DIRECTV (five-day free trial)

The matchup in London marks just the second time these programs have met on the field. Two years ago, Arizona State defeated Kansas, 35-31, thanks to a game-winning touchdown reception by Jordyn Tyson with 16 seconds remaining in the game.

This season, both teams have 1-1 records going into Week 3 and both lost their Week 2 matchups. Kansas hosted rival Missouri on September 11 and fell 38–21, while Arizona State lost 48–20 to No. 10 Texas A&M on the road.
