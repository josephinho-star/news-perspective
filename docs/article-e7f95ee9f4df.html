Nasdaq-100 futures gain as traders gear up for Anthropic’s highly anticipated debut: Live updates

2026-11-19 10:24

U.S. equity futures jumped early Thursday as investors looked ahead to Anthropic's public trading debut.

Futures tied to the Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 0.57%. S&P 500 futures added 1.09%, and Nasdaq-100 futures surged 1.94%

In Wednesday's regular U.S. trading, the blue-chip Dow lost more than 560 points, or 1.1%. The broad market S&P 500 fell 0.2%, marking its fifth consecutive losing session and longest slide since June. The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite rose 0.4%.

Traders are focused on how the company behind Claude performs when it debuts on the Nasdaq Thursday, trading under the ticker "ANT." The artificial intelligence company priced its initial public offering at $83 a share, above the expected range, a person familiar with the matter told CNBC Wednesday.

Anthropic also upsized the offering, selling around 980 million shares, bringing the amount raised to $81.3 billion and surpassing SpaceX, which held a record IPO in June.

The market has become more cautious on AI as of late, with investors focusing on whether heavy spending on the build-out will translate into meaningful profits. The iShares AI Innovation and Tech Active ETF (BAI) has fallen 11% this month, and embattled AI bellwether Nvidia has dropped 26% while the company grapples with the resignation of its CEO Jensen Huang. Shares of neocloud CoreWeave, which had seen its bond yields trade above 16% earlier this month, is also down almost 13% in November alone.

"It'll be an important barometer for investors' AI appetite," Callie Cox, director of investment strategy at Plancorp, said of Anthropic's IPO. "AI stocks desperately need some wind at their backs, too ... Wall Street needs a sign that investors still believe in the potential for AI beyond already-elevated expectations."

"The challenge in that thesis is that hot IPOs rarely perform well right out of the gate, especially these days, when every company seems to be vying for a limited pool of capital to fund their AI strategy," she said. "While I wouldn't be surprised to see the stock market take cues from Anthropic's performance, I'd caution investors about trying to read the tea leaves from one event too much."

On Thursday, traders will keep an eye out for weekly jobless claims. On the earnings front, Walmart is slated to report in the morning, followed by clothing retailer Gap after the market close.
