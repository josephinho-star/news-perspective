Colorado police officer killed, 3 officers wounded after suspect opens fire near bus

2026-11-06 07:07

Michael Sinkewicz, Fox News

Published Nov. 6, 2026, 2:07 a.m. ET

See more of our coverage in your search results.

A Colorado police officer was killed and three more officers were wounded in a shooting Thursday evening near a bus outside Denver, authorities said.

Lakewood police said officers responded to a report of a suspicious person near the intersection of Colfax Avenue and Wadsworth Boulevard around 6:30 p.m., where they encountered a suspect attempting to board a bus.

The suspect opened fire on officers, striking four of them, Lakewood Police Public Information Officer John Romero said during a news conference.

“We are very sorry to report that one of those agents has been pronounced dead of their injuries,” Romero said.

The suspect was shot by police and pronounced dead at the scene, Romero said.

A civilian was also injured and transported to a local hospital.

Police did not immediately provide details about the extent of the injuries or the conditions of the three surviving officers.

Authorities said the suspect was attempting to board a Regional Transportation District (RTD) bus when officers approached.

“Within seconds of agents approaching the suspect, the suspect opened fire on our agents,” Romero said.

Romero also delivered an emotional message to the fallen officer’s family:

“We just want the family to know that we love them, that we’re here for them, and Lakewood Police Department is a family, and we will be here for each other.”
