Pillow talk: This $8 mulberry silk pillowcase is a dream for your hair

2026-10-12 13:44

Great hair days don’t have to start with an expensive salon treatment or a complicated styling routine. Sometimes, they begin with what you rest your head on at night.

When it comes to building a hair-care routine, it’s easy to focus on shampoos, conditioners, leave-in treatments and heat protectants while overlooking one everyday essential: your pillowcase. After all, your hair spends hours rubbing against it while you sleep, making your bedding a surprisingly worthwhile place to consider a little upgrade.

That’s why we’re eyeing the Lyshin Mulberry Silk Pillowcase on Amazon, an affordable find that promises to bring a touch of luxury to your nightly routine without the luxury price tag.

At only $8 right now, this pillowcase is the go-to option for anyone curious about silk bedding but not quite ready to splurge on a more expensive version.

If you regularly wake up with frizz, tangles, or strands that seem determined to go in every direction, your pillowcase could be one factor worth considering. Traditional cotton pillowcases can create friction as you toss and turn throughout the night, potentially contributing to hair breakage and frizz, particularly if your hair is curly, textured, fine or prone to tangling.

Silk pillowcases offer a smoother surface that allows hair to glide more easily as you move in your sleep. By reducing friction, they may help minimize tangles and friction-related breakage, making it easier to maintain your hair between wash days. They won’t magically repair damaged strands or guarantee glossy locks overnight, but they can be a useful addition to a more thoughtful hair-care routine.

The Lyshin Mulberry Silk Pillowcase is the budget-friendly bedding upgrade on our radar. Plus, because it’s breathable and cooling, it offers another potential perk for sleepers who crave more comfortable bedding.

If you’re looking for a simple, budget-conscious way to support your hair-care goals, the Lyshin Mulberry Silk Pillowcase is worth a closer look. Consider it a small step toward less friction at bedtime — and a potentially smoother start to your morning.

How does a Mulberry silk pillowcase benefit facial skin?

Mulberry silk absorbs less moisture than cotton, keeping serums on your skin while its smooth surface reduces friction and sleep creases.

How does sleeping on silk prevent hair damage and frizz?

Silk creates up to 24% less friction against hair fibers than cotton, preventing bedhead, tangles, split ends and breakage overnight.

What’s the difference between genuine Mulberry silk and polyester satin?

Mulberry silk is a natural, breathable protein fiber that regulates temperature, while polyester satin is a synthetic fabric that traps heat.

Does a Mulberry silk pillowcase help hot sleepers stay cool?

Yes, Mulberry silk features natural temperature-regulating and moisture-wicking properties that promote airflow and maintain a cool sleeping surface.

Why is a silk pillowcase a smart choice for budget-conscious shoppers?

Silk prevents fabric from absorbing expensive skincare products and preserves styled hair longer, reducing the need for constant restyling.

How do you properly wash and care for a silk pillowcase?

Machine wash on a delicate cold cycle with a pH-neutral detergent and line dry away from direct sunlight.
