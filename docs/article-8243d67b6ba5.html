Robert Pattinson and Suki Waterhouse share sweet kisses at Venice Film Festival

2026-09-06 00:27

Robert Pattinson leaned in for a kiss from Suki Waterhouse amid thunderous applause for “Primetime” at the 83rd Venice Film Festival.

In a clip circulating on social media, the “Twilight” actor turned to Waterhouse for a quick kiss during a standing ovation that, according to Variety, lasted seven minutes.

Pattinson wore an off-white pinstriped suit with a striped cranberry necktie for the outing, while Waterhouse modeled a mint green lace two-piece skirt ensemble.

The “Back in Love” singer beamed while applauding Pattinson, and the couple leaned in multiple times to chat over the applause.

“In 2006, To Catch a Predator host Chris Hansen sets out to make television history,” an official log line for the A24 crime thriller reads.

In a comments thread on X, however, fans were more interested in the affectionate dynamic between the “The Odyssey” actor and musician.
