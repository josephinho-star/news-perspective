‘Unforgettable’ teen’s death ruled homicide after ‘severe psychological manipulation and extortion’: coroner

2026-11-21 18:00

An “unforgettable” 14-year-old girl’s death was ruled a homicide after she suffered “severe psychological manipulation and extortion by an outside actor,” a coroner determined.

Brynlee Jo Stempa died by hanging Oct. 1, with her official cause of death determined as anoxic brain injury and respiratory failure, the Allen County Coroner’s office said Friday in a release obtained by the Journal Gazette.

But her untimely death was ultimately ruled a homicide after a thorough autopsy and toxicology analysis was conducted, as well as an extensive review of digital and investigative evidence provided by cops, the FBI and international law enforcement partners, the outlet reported.

“Under forensic pathology standards, a manner of death ruling of homicide indicates that a death resulted from the intentional, coercive or culpable actions of another person,” the coroner’s office reportedly found.

“Based on corroborating evidence gathered during the joint investigation, the Allen County Coroner’s Office determined that the decedent’s actions were directly orchestrated and compelled through severe psychological manipulation and extortion by an outside actor.”

Stempa’s family remembered her in a tear-jerking obituary for her “unique spirit, her unforgettable personality and the joy she brought to those around her.”

“She brought so much love, light and laughter to those who knew her,” her family wrote.
