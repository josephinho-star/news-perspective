Not to mention, anti-aging skincare can get *expensive* quickly, so this 40%-off discount is substantial. With your favorite cleanser, a serum (ahem, Medicube’s) and this cream, you’re about to treat your skin to the soothed, nurtured trifecta.

What are the key active ingredients in this cream?

Key actives include PDRN (Sodium DNA), TECA (Centella Asiatica extract), multi-hyaluronic acid, niacinamide, ceramide NP, glutathione and damask rose flower water.

What skin concerns does Centellian 24 Expert Madeca Cream target?

This cream targets fine lines, loss of elasticity, dryness and barrier damage by deeply hydrating and firming the skin.

When in my skincare routine should I apply this cream?

Apply it as the final moisturizing step of your routine, immediately following your cleansing, toning and serum steps.

How often should I use the Expert Madeca Cream?

Use it daily in both your morning and evening routines. You can apply a thicker layer at night as a sleeping mask.

How do I apply this cream for optimal absorption?

Dispense a small amount, spread evenly over your face and neck, and gently pat into the skin using warm palms.
