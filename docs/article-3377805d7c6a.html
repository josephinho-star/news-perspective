Prince William, Kate Middleton dragged into Thomas Markle child porn scandal as more questions arise over 2019 letter to police

2026-10-30 21:31

Prince William and Kate Middleton were reportedly made aware of a 2019 letter sent to police — which was later passed on to Meghan Markle — containing allegations of child sexual abuse against the Duchess of Sussex’s estranged father, Thomas Markle Sr.

The insider also told the outlet that Meghan, 45, and her husband, Prince Harry, 42, would not have received the letter “in isolation.”

“It was the police’s duty to make the Palace aware of the claims, and they will have done so,” the source said.

Page Six reached out to representatives for William and Kate, both 44, for comment but did not immediately hear back.

As previously reported, Thomas Sr., 82, is currently under investigation by the Cebu City Police Office in the Philippines after “hundreds, if not thousands,” of child sexual abuse images were allegedly discovered in his home by his son, Thomas Markle Jr.

Thomas Jr., 60, allegedly found the images inside several boxes stored among family “mementos,” telling the Sun last week, “When I found this material, I was horrified and I had no option but to report my dad.”

In a statement later shared with Page Six, Meghan said she was “deeply distressed” by the news, revealing that she had received a letter in 2018 containing similar “troubling and disturbing” allegations against her father, which she said contributed to their estrangement.

Thomas Sr., for his part, has vehemently denied the allegations against him, dismissing them as “sick lies” and telling the Daily Mail, “I’m shocked, I’m hurt and I’m very upset that my son is making these accusations against me.

“I could never do what I’m being accused of. It’s the worst thing anyone could accuse me of,” he continued. “This is bulls–t. It’s just a bunch of lies. It’s sick. I have no idea why Tom is trying to destroy me with these lies.”

“Despite Harry and Meghan’s return to the UK, relations remain complicated, and I don’t see the Palace wanting to become involved in matters concerning Meghan’s father,” she added.
