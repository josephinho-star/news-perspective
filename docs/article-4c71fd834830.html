US Army veteran killed by pack of dogs after being attacked in yard of abandoned home as daughter calls for justice

2026-11-23 10:33

An Army veteran unaware she was set to become a first-time grandma was mauled to death by a pack of dogs after being attacked in the yard of an abandoned home – as her grief-stricken pregnant daughter calls for justice.

Allison Lynn Anderson, 36, was found with “fatal injuries” on Nov. 16 after cops responded to reports of an unresponsive woman in a field in Cartersville, Georgia, the Georgia Bureau of Investigation revealed, citing preliminary autopsy results.

The circumstances behind the attack remain unclear but several of the dogs involved are now in the custody of Bartow County Animal Control, the GBI said, without revealing the exact number of animals involved in the attack.

Anderson, a mom of three who joined the Army in 2007, was set to become a grandmother – something which she wasn’t yet aware of, according to her 20-year-old daughter, Miley Garner, who is expecting her first child with her fiancé, Jesse Jarrett.

“If my mom knew that I was carrying her first grandchild, she would be over the moon and full of excitement,” Garner told WAFB.

“I am so disgusted and absolutely heartbroken that the owners of these dogs did not have control over them. What my mom went through was torture.

“She deserves justice and even though what happened [on Nov. 16] was terrible, her heart is so pure she would forgive them.”

Tributes have been paid to Anderson – who has been described as someone with a “larger than life personality.”

“She was outgoing, full of personality, and never met a stranger. If someone needed help, Allison was there, whether she knew them or not,” her family said in an obit, announcing her unexpected passing.

“Allison lived life on her own terms. She danced to the beat of her own drum and wasn’t afraid to be exactly who she was.

“She loved being barefoot whenever she could and was happiest outdoors, especially near the water, whether she was swimming or fishing. Allison also loved music and singing, and those who heard her sing know she had a voice that was as beautiful as it was unforgettable.”

Neighbors have been left horrified by what unfolded in the yard, while others recalled how there was a large police presence in the aftermath amid the ongoing death probe.

“I had saw her about last week, I think it was. She had come by and I had talked to her. She was friends with my son and some of his friends. She was a real sweet girl — she was so sweet,” Betty Mathison, a neighbor, told Atlanta News First last Tuesday.

Investigators are combing through surveillance video from the area as part of their probe. No arrests have been made.
