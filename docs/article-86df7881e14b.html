Steelers coach Mike McCarthy injures himself and it was serious enough he never got to halftime locker room

2026-10-18 19:26

It's been a rough day for Pittsburgh Steelers coach Mike McCarthy, and that's only partially because his team is struggling against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

The Steelers trailed the Bucs 13-3 at halftime with their offense obviously not scoring a touchdown and also converting only 1 of 6 third-down opportunities.

So it was time for McCarthy to get his team in the halftime locker room and rethink the game plan. That makes sense because, you know, McCarthy needed to address whatever was not working, make adjustments, and maybe yell some motivating words.

Except McCarthy never made it to the halftime locker room.

While he was walking off the sideline and headed to an interview with a CBS sideline reporter, McCarthy apparently suffered some sort of leg injury. Multiple reports said McCarthy appeared to twist an ankle.

The 62-year-old coach then had another Steelers' staffer help him off the field by serving as a bit of a human crutch.

And then the coach went directly into the team's blue medical tent where he spent the entire halftime period.

McCarthy returned to the sideline at the start of the second half and the club had informed CBS he is going to limp through the end of the game.

There is no word who exactly made whatever adjustments on offense the Steelers might have wanted at halftime.

McCarthy serves as both the team's head coach and offensive play-caller.
