Blac Chyna hit with staggering tax debt years after Rob Kardashian’s $20k-a-month child support payments end

2026-11-04 00:00

Blac Chyna has been hit with a hefty six-figure tax lien years after her ex Rob Kardashian stopped paying her $20,000 a month in child support.

The California Franchise Tax Board filed a $116,732.37 lien against the reality star, whose real name is Angela White, for the 2024 taxable year.

The lien comes years after Kardashian, 39, stopped making the monthly payments. He and Chyna, 38, share a 9-year-old daughter, Dream.

Kardashian initially agreed to pay Chyna $20,000 a month in child support after the former couple reached a custody agreement in 2017.

However, in November 2018, he filed to have the payments lowered, claiming that he could no longer afford the amount and that their messy breakup had damaged his earning potential.

In March 2019, the former couple reached a new agreement that eliminated child support payments altogether.

Under the deal, neither parent would pay the other, and they would each cover Dream’s expenses during their respective parenting time while splitting certain costs, including medical care and school.

Chyna later appeared to embrace the arrangement, calling raising her children without child support her “biggest flex” during a 2020 interview with SiriusXM’s Hip Hop Nation.

“Just honestly taking care of my kids by myself as a single parent, but no child support,” she said at the time.

Kardashian said he paid $37,000 annually for Dream’s school, covered her medical bills and extracurricular activities and kept her Tuesday through Saturday.

Tyga, 36, said he paid $40,000 a year for their 14-year-old son King Cairo’s tuition and that the boy lived with him Monday through Saturday each week.

Tyga’s child support obligations were also addressed in a 2023 custody settlement.

The former couple agreed to joint legal and physical custody, granting Chyna time with King from Friday through Monday, with Tyga taking the remainder of the week.

Under the arrangement, Tyga did not have to pay child support.

Chyna and Kardashian sparked speculation about a possible romantic reunion last fall.

In October 2025, Chyna shared photos of herself in an all-white outfit and tagged Kardashian with the caption, “This love is forever.” He added fuel to the rumors by liking the post.

Days later, Chyna addressed their relationship during an appearance at the Los Angeles Women’s Expo, saying they were “healing,” communicating and “just going with the flow.”

“We’re just taking our time. I’m excited. He’s my person,” she said, while adding that a reunion would depend on God’s will.
