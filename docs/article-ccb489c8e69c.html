Cindy Crawford, husband Rande Gerber hold hands in first public outing since son Presley’s death

2026-10-10 23:58

Cindy Crawford and Rande Gerber were spotted holding hands during a walk on Malibu’s Zuma Beach following the death of their only son, Presley Gerber.

The longtime couple was pictured wearing white tops, with the supermodel donning black yoga pants and Rande wearing black shorts.

Both wore ball caps and sneakers for the somber outing on Saturday, while Crawford carried a smartphone.

The sighting comes a day after longtime family friend — and Rande’s Casamigos tequila brand co-founder — George Clooney called the tragedy “the worst thing that could ever happen with family.”

“Quite honestly, it’s one of the most devastating things that’s happened to our family,” the “Up in the Air” star told Entertainment Tonight at the Albies Friday.

“And their family is just … as you can imagine,” he added.
