Bravo officially severs ties with ‘Below Deck’ star Nathan Gallagher following domestic violence arrest

2026-09-05 23:08

Bravo has officially cut ties with the “Below Deck Mediterranean” star following his domestic violence arrest.

“Bravo does not plan on working with Nathan in the future,” a spokesperson for the network told Page Six Saturday.

Gallagher has been a cast member on the “Below Deck” spinoff for the past three seasons, including its current 11th installment.

Page Six has learned that Bravo has no plans to edit him out of the current season’s footage.

We’re also told production on the season now playing out on television wrapped in December 2025 — prior to Gallagher’s current legal trouble.

Gallagher was arrested in Sydney, Australia, on Dec. 27, 2025, after allegedly assaulting a 29-year-old woman, who was not publicly identified at the time

The woman has since been confirmed to be his fellow “Below Deck Med” castmate Gael Cameron, with whom he shares a 1-year-old son, Kayden.

Cameron had filed an Apprehended Violence Application against Gallagher.

He was charged with assault occasioning actual bodily harm, intentionally choking a person without consent and common assault.

“I’m not claiming I was perfect. I wasn’t. I made mistakes, and I will take responsibility for my own actions,” he said in part, adding that he would not accept “being portrayed solely through one person’s account of a relationship, when there is another side and evidence relating to it.”

Gallagher said that evidence includes “contemporaneous video footage from the night in question, as well as messages and other records.”

The US Sun also reported at the time that Gallagher would not return for Season 12 of “Below Deck Med,” which had already been filmed.

“They’re all on a year-by-year and per-season basis,” a source told the outlet. “If they get picked up for another season, great, but if not, it’s not like this big dramatic firing thing. In Nathan’s case, though, looks like that ship has sailed — pun intended. It seems likely he won’t be back.”

An arrest warrant was issued for Gallagher in Australia on Tuesday after he failed to appear at a hearing at Burwood Local Court, where he was officially convicted, according to documents obtained by Page Six.

New South Wales (NSW) Police Force said on Facebook that he was “believed to be offshore” and asked for “anyone with information into his whereabouts” to let them know “immediately.”

He is facing an assault charge stemming from an alleged incident involving a man on Main Street in Dungloe in August 2022.
