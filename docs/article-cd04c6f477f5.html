Trump says Iran unleashed missile, drone surge after midterms

2026-11-22 23:33

President Donald Trump said Sunday that Iran has launched more missiles and drones over the past week than at any point in more than a year, adding that U. S. forces shot them down without any damage and as tensions escalate following the U.S. midterm elections.

In a post on Truth Social, Trump also tied the surge in Iranian attacks to the Nov. 3 midterm elections, when Democrats won control of both chambers of Congress amid voter frustration over the war with Iran and rising fuel costs.

"As everyone remembers, I predicted two things concerning the November 3rd Midterm Elections," Trump wrote on Truth Social.

"One, that if the Democrats win, the Stock Markets would go down, substantially, which they have, and two, that the Islamic Republic of Iran, which has killed 81,000 protestors as of today, will grow more aggressive in their attacks, which has also occurred. "

Trump also said that Iran launched "more drones and missiles over the past week than at any time for over a year," though he said U.S. defenses neutralized the threat.

"Don't worry, they've all been knocked down, and done no damage," Trump said.

"Iranian leaders consider the Democrats weak, ineffective, and Country destroying, but I won't let that happen!" he added.

The remarks also came days after the Senate rejected a war powers resolution in a 48-51 vote on Nov. 19, leaving Trump's military authority intact.

Speaking to Fox News earlier Sunday, Trump said he is inclined to resume strikes on Iran after the new year if diplomatic talks fail to produce a deal, warning that Iranian vessels testing the U.S. naval blockade would end up "at the bottom of the sea like the rest of the navy."

In his social media post Sunday, Trump accused both Democrats and Iran of trying to keep fuel prices high ahead of the midterm elections and separately questioned the integrity of the vote without providing evidence.

"Both the Dems and Iran fought like hell to keep the Oil price up as long as possible and, since the Election, the Oil price has dropped by a record level," Trump wrote, adding that it was too bad voters "didn't understand this before they pulled the trigger."

"The whole Election was very 'strange,'" he said. "In the end, America will win! "
