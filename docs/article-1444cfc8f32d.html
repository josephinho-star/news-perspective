MMA fighter receives big bonus on OnlyFans after suffering a wardrobe malfunction during loss

2026-10-20 18:16

A wardrobe malfunction turned into what could be as much as a $500,000 OnlyFans bonus for Lucero Acosta.

During the MMA fighter’s PFL debut on Friday night in Chicago, opponent Shannon Clark had her pinned against the canvas and at one point Acosta’s top shifted and briefly exposed her chest on the live broadcast.

The fight continued and Clark went on to secure the technical knockout win at 1:59 of the final round.

Despite the loss, Acosta revealed her added cashed benefit of the weird fight incident on Instagram to her 503K followers.

“💰💰💰Extra payment on @onlyfans because we showed our nipples during the @pflmma live 😅,” she wrote in a post on Sunday. “It’s cute.🤗😋 Official sponsor.”

Acosta, also known as “La Loba,” followed up on her Instagram story by sharing a figure of $500,000.

She has not clarified whether that number represented her total earnings, new subscriber revenue or something else, but she did seem to link the payday to the wardrobe mishap at the PFL event.

Acosta’s loss ended a four-fight winning streak that spanned from Sept. 2022 to April 2025, and dropped her professional record to 6-3.

After Friday night Clark has won four consecutive fights since she suffered an upset on “Contender Series.”

Acosta, who went viral training underwater in a bikini, spent her career competing for Combate Global before joining PFL.

Her popularity seems to have followed her to PFL and she didn’t seem to leave Chicago without some extra weight to her wallet.
