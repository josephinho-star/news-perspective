Jay-Z pauses London show to wish Beyoncé a happy 45th birthday with crowd singalong

2026-09-05 14:48

During his London concert at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on Friday, Jay-Z (born Shawn Corey Carter) took a moment to honor his wife, Beyoncé, getting the crowd to join him in a serenade for her 45th birthday.

The performance marked the first of two London dates on Jay-Z’s limited “Jay-Z 30” run, which celebrates 30 years of his debut album, “Reasonable Doubt,” and 25 years of 2001’s “The Blueprint.”

The “99 Problems” rapper, 56, is set to take the stage again Saturday night for his second and final London show.

In a video posted to Instagram by People, Jay-Z could be seen leading the crowd in a rendition of “Happy Birthday” as fireworks went off around the stadium.

Beyoncé appeared on the Jumbotron in a black hoodie, smiling as she blew kisses and waved to the crowd while they sang to her.

Jay-Z and Beyoncé, who tied the knot in April 2008, share three children: daughter Blue Ivy, 14, who was born in 2012, and 9-year-old twins Rumi and Sir, who were born in 2017.

The price was a nod to her birthday, Sept. 4, 1981, while the release also coincided with the 20th anniversary of her second solo album, “B’Day.”

The “Love on Top” singer, who reached billionaire status last year, faced backlash on social media over the hefty price tag.
