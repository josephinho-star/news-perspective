SC dad left sleeping daughter, 6, outside Target at 1 a.m. to save his spot in line for Pokémon cards: cops

2026-11-08 03:00

A brazen South Carolina father left his 6-year-old daughter sleeping in line outside a Target store in Charleston so he wouldn’t lose his spot overnight to get his hands on a new Pokémon card drop, according to police.

John-Michael Dooley, 35, gave his sleeping daughter a pillow and blanket while waiting alongside numerous other people in line for the trading cards outside the West Ashley neighborhood Target around 1 a.m. Friday, the Charleston Police Department said in a news release.

Dooley, a Johns Island local, then left the big-box store — recklessly abandoning the youngster alone in line, police said.

Over an hour later, concerned line-goers contacted the cops around 2:30 a.m., who busted Dooley when he arrived back at the store sometime later.

The irresponsible father confessed to police that he had gone home but left his daughter outside Target “so he could maintain his place in line and avoid missing the opportunity to purchase the cards,” according to Charleston Police.

His bond was set at $40,000 and is slated to return to court on Dec. 12, according to online court records.

The youngster was unharmed and was placed into emergency protective custody with the South Carolina Department of Social Services, police said.

Pokémon dropped several new trading card releases as part of the “Mega Evolution – Delta Reign” and “30th Celebration” collections on Friday — which saw crowds of dedicated fans line up across the country to get their hands on packs that start from around $5.
