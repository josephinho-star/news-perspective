Howard-era minister Sharman Stone dies via voluntary assisted dying, aged 75

2026-11-19 19:44

Former Liberal MP Sharman Stone has died via voluntary assisted dying after spending much of her political career advocating that people should have autonomy over their bodies.

Dr Stone died aged 75 on Wednesday surrounded by family after a long fight against glioblastoma, an aggressive form of brain cancer.

During her political career, Dr Stone championed contentious medical research and supported access to abortion, and readily - and publicly - disagreed with former leader Tony Abbott on both topics.

Dr Stone was one of the first women superintendents in the Victorian Office of Corrections, the first female director of the Victorian Farmers Federation, and the recipient of an economics PhD.

She was elected to federal parliament in 1996, serving through the Howard years in what was then the seat of Murray in northern Victoria.

Dr Stone was remembered as a strong voice for her local community, right up to her late days.

“She was born and bred in the heart of the electorate she served - a sixth-generation northern Victorian from a farming family,” opposition leader Angus Taylor said after her death.

“She understood the rugged and regenerative nature of the land and what makes regional Australia tick.”

Dr Stone spent two decades in parliament and was characterised by her independence.

She held several roles during the Howard government, including as parliamentary secretary to the environment and finance ministers.

She also held the shadow environment portfolio after the Liberals’ defeat to Kevin Rudd.

Dr Stone retired from politics in 2016, and served as the Australian Ambassador for Women and Girls until 2020.

Her celebration of life is expected to be held after the November 28 Victorian election.
