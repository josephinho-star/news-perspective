Prince Harry finally comes clean about decision to move back to UK with Meghan Markle, kids

2026-10-20 13:54

Prince Harry moved back to the UK with his wife, Meghan Markle, and their two kids to create “opportunities for the family.”

“Being able to put the kids in a British school has meant a lot to me,” the Duke of Sussex shared about his kids, Prince Archie, 7, and Princess Lilibet, 5, in a candid interview with GQ.

“I didn’t know how important it was to me,” he added.

“I always thought it was gonna be something that I would try and do or that we would try and do, but now they’ve been in school for two weeks.”

Harry, 42, said Lilibet and Archie are “loving” their new school and have been able to gain “a whole different experience.”

“Don’t worry, I’m not criticizing American schools,” he clarified. “But I think for kids at 7 and 5 to be able to have that comparison and to have two completely different cultural experiences is so important today.”

“So, it’s nice to be back,” he added.
