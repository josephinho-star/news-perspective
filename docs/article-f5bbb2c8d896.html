Even Jennifer Lopez’s swimsuit is bedazzled and beaded

2026-09-08 20:55

Jennifer Lopez brought her glam style poolside.

The singer is well-known for her embellished bodysuits come performance time, but her one-piece swimsuit is just as bold as her on-stage looks.

Sharing two snaps on Instagram, Lopez wished her followers a happy Labor Day while wearing an embellished Camilla swimsuit and a matching caftan.

With an all-over print and plunging V-neck, the swimsuit is covered in beads and crystals, finished off with a tie-waist.

Holding up her bright orange iPhone to the mirror, she can be seen wearing a chunky gold ring on her right hand as well as two stacked gold and diamond bangles.

Fans were loving the look, with one commenting, “Flawless as per usual girlie” and another saying, “the prettiest woman alive, I said what I said.” A third joked, “happy labor-slay.”

Lopez had quite the European summer, enjoying Paris Couture Week with her sister Lynda, celebrating their birthdays together in the City of Light. She then made a quick stop over to Italy to perform as part of Dolce & Gabbana’s couture weekend and took in Wimbledon in London days later.

Plus, she and her twins had an Italian road trip before sending her children off to college.

We can’t wait to see what Lopez has in store for fall.
