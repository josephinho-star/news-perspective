What are the main active ingredients in Olay Super Serum?

It features five core ingredients: activated niacinamide (vitamin B3), vitamin C, collagen peptide, vitamin E and alpha hydroxy acid (AHA).

How often should I apply Olay Super Serum?

It’s designed for twice-daily use, every morning and night, on clean skin before your moisturizer.

What products should I pair with Olay Super Serum?

Pair with a broad-spectrum daily SPF in the morning to protect against sun sensitivity and follow with a hydrating moisturizer at night.

What ingredients should I avoid pairing with this serum?

Avoid combining with strong exfoliants, high-strength AHA/BHA chemical peels or prescription retinoids in the same step to prevent irritation.

Is Olay Super Serum suitable for sensitive skin?

It’s formulated for all skin types, including sensitive skin, but contains added fragrance and mild AHAs that may require a patch test.

What benefits does Olay Super Serum provide?

Clinically tested to deliver five benefits: smoother texture, more even tone, firmer skin, reduced appearance of fine lines and long-lasting hydration.

What is the warranty and return policy for Olay Super Serum?
