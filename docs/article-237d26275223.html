What Time Does The Next Episode of ‘Lanterns’ Come Out?

2026-10-04 15:00

For the past two months, Sunday nights have belonged to HBO‘s Lanterns. The gritty superhero drama starring Kyle Chandler as original Green Lantern Hal Jordan and Aaron Pierre as his would-be replacement John Stewart has charmed comic book nerds and newbies to the saga with its stellar acting, wild twists, and grounded approach to otherworldly material.

Last week’s penultimate episode of Lanterns ended with the “Halogram” (i.e. Kyle Chandler playing a slightly younger “back up” version of the recently murdered Hal) and John Stewart journeying to a terrifying planet on the other side of the universe. Here, they encounter water and mist that makes you confront your greatest fear — but that’s not all. Sinestro (Ulrich Thomsen) is also on this strange world, but why?

Lanterns Episode 8 is the last episode of the show’s first season. We still don’t know for sure who killed Hal Jordan and why, nor do we know how Sinestro escaped the Guardians’ prison on Oa. Will the Lanterns season finale finally give us answers?

Here’s everything you need to know about when the next episode of Lanterns comes out on HBO and HBO Max…

Is Lanterns on Tonight? (10/4/26)

Yes, a new episode of Lanterns will be on HBO tonight — Sunday, October 4, 2026.

Lanterns Season Finale Release Date:

Lanterns Episode 8 — which doubles as the Lanterns Season 1 finale — will premiere on HBO and HBO Max on Sunday, October 4.

What Time Does Lanterns Episode 8 Come Out?

Lanterns Episode 8 “Dirt and Stars” comes out on HBO and HBO Max at 9 PM ET on Sunday, October 4.

How Many Episodes are in Lanterns? Lanterns Episode Guide:

There are a total of eight episodes in the first season of Lanterns. Here’s your guide to when each and every episode of Lanterns came out:

- Episode 1 “Pilot”: Sunday, August 16

- Episode 2 “Trust Fall”: Sunday, August 23

- Episode 3 “OutKast”: Sunday, August 30

- Episode 4 “The Weenie”: Sunday, September 6

- Episode 5 “Lights Out”: Sunday, September 13

- Episode 6 “Bad Optics”: Sunday, September 20

- Episode 7 “The Jordan Boys’ Legacy”: Sunday, September 27

- Episode 8 “Dirt and Stars”: Sunday, October 4

Lanterns is an HBO original, which means you can only catch it with an HBO or HBO Max subscription. Don’t have HBO Max?

If you’re new to HBO Max, you can sign up for as low as $10.99/month with ads, but an ad-free subscription will cost $18.49/month.

If you want to stream even more and save a few bucks a month while you’re at it, we recommend subscribing to one of the discounted Disney+ Bundles with Hulu and HBO Max. With ads, the bundle costs $19.99/month and without ads, $32.99/month.

Will There Be a Lanterns Season 2?

We don’t yet know if Lanterns will get a second season, but it definitely feels like a distinct possibility.

That said, we do know that Aaron Pierre is set to reprise his role of John Stewart in next summer’s follow up to Superman, Man of Tomorrow. So even if HBO doesn’t bring Lanterns back, we’ll get more of John Stewart and the Green Lantern Corps.

Is There a Lanterns Trailer?

Yes, there is a Lanterns trailer. You can watch the official Lanterns trailer by scrolling to the top of this page.

Lanterns Cast: Who’s Who in HBO’s Green Lantern Show:

HBO’s Lanterns features an incredible ensemble cast of major stars, TV faves, and up-and-coming talent. You might recognize some of the Lanterns cast from projects like Friday Night Lights, Rebel Ridge, Fargo, Deli Boys, Matlock, and more. Here’s who’s in the Lanterns cast:

- Kyle Chandler as Hal Jordan

- Aaron Pierre as John Stewart

- Kelly Macdonald as Sheriff Kerry Kane

- Poorna Jagannathan as Zoe

- Nathan Fillion as Guy Gardner

- Jason Ritter as Billy Macon

- Ulrich Thomsen as Thaal Sinestro

- Shea Pritchard as Noah Macon
