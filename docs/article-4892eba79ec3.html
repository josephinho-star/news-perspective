Beverly Hills plastic surgeon branded ‘magician’ reveals more of his tricks with other stunning facelift transformations

2026-11-26 00:31

Dr. Marc Levin has proven that he is far from a one-trick pony.

Earlier this week, the Beverly Hills plastic surgeon, who specializes in “dramatic yet undetectable facial rejuvenation,” left the internet speechless after showcasing his work.

He was branded a “magician” and “miracle worker” after sharing before and after images of 61-year-old Helen.

The mom, originally from South Africa, underwent a 9.5-hour procedure as she overhauled everything from her eyelids to her earlobes.

She said that the $45,000 surgery had given her confidence back, stating three months post surgery: “I feel like I look like my daughter [now].”

But Helen is not the only patient that Dr. Levin has worked his magic on in recent months — and he has now spoken to the California Post about his craftsmanship.

Aesthetics Sales Consultant Rebecca, who is in her mid-50s, was also among Dr. Levin’s clientele.

Rebecca underwent an extensive series of procedures, including a deep plane face and neck lift, upper and lower blepharoplasty, fat grafting, CO2 laser and a partial submandibular gland trimming — which removes a prominent portion of salivary glands.

Discussing her surgery, Dr. Levin explained: “Also, her earlobes do not look attached anymore. Some patients are born with a ‘pixie ear’ and we can actually reverse that with a well done tension free facelift.”

The expert, who became one of the youngest board-certified surgeons in North America when he qualified, continued: “She is the most sweet, caring and lovely patient who has overcome a major personal health hurdle in the last few years and felt it was time to do something for her.”

He noted that she now looks “more awake, brighter, happier,” adding: “Just wow!”

Elsewhere, Tammy, who has been an emergency department nurse for more than 20 years, is another patient who opted for a Dr. Levin refresh.

“She constantly works overnight shifts and was tired of looking tired,” the doctor explained.

“When she came to me wanting full facial rejuvenation, I felt so honoured to be trusted by another health care worker. She wanted to look like herself, but cleaned, refined and refreshed and awake. She wanted undetectable rejuvenation.”

Tammy, also mid-50s, opted for a deep plane face and neck lift, endoscopic brow reset, upper blepharoplasty and full facial fat grafting.

“She is so happy she did the surgery and I am so thrilled for her,” he shared.

Maria, 55, was another to visit Dr. Levin’s practice to undergo a deep plane face and neck lift, buccal fat removal and full facial fat grafting.

The emergency department nurse was “singing with joy” after checking out her results just seven weeks later.

“She looks incredible! She looks like herself, just cleaner, younger and more refreshed,” the surgeon said.

Speaking about his practice as a whole, Dr. Levin told The Post: “Every face has its own unique beauty and character, and it is a privilege to help patients look and feel like the most refreshed, youthful versions of themselves.

“My goal is to preserve what makes each person’s face distinctive while enhancing their natural features.”

He explained that patients visit him for “modern, comprehensive facial rejuvenation that delivers a dramatic transformation while remaining beautifully undetectable.”
