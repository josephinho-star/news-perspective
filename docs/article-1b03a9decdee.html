Martha Stewart, Gov. Kathy Hochul celebrate The Post’s 225th birthday with Empire State Building tribute

2026-11-16 21:09

Martha Stewart and Governor Kathy Hochul helped celebrate The Post’s 225th birthday on Monday by lighting the Empire State Building red with editor-in-chief Keith Poole.

“This is one of my favorite buildings,” Stewart, 85, exclusively told Page Six at the event. “It always has been. It’s a great honor to illuminate it.”

The famous homemaker also told us that The Post is “one of [her] three daily newspapers.”

“I have various viewpoints and various news cycles I follow,” she added. “And I get my laughs from the New York Post.”

In his speech, Poole called Stewart “the ultimate example of New York grit, character, vision, energy and reinvention.”

He also joked that Hochul, 68, showed “a real love of New York and real courage” by standing in a room full of The Post editors, adding, “I know how much you love being on our front page.”

During Hochul’s speech, she admitted getting the invite to celebrate The Post’s milestone birthday was “unexpected,” and joked that the newspaper “couldn’t get rid of me” during her 2026 gubernatorial race that she won against Republican challenger Bruce Blakeman.

“This is quite an institution,” she said about the Empire State Building. “It is our symbol. It is our motto. And to be here at this moment in The Post’s history is not lost on me how important this is.”

The Democratic politician said she and Stewart “have a lot in common” from both knowing “how to turn bad press to our advantage.”

“She turned hers into a lifestyle empire, and I turned mine into reelection,” Hochul said.

When Stewart took the stage, the entrepreneur — who fittingly wore a bright red shirt — reflected on her history living in New York, recalling that she modeled in the city as a teenager and attended Barnard College.

“I love this city and I love everything that’s going on in the city,” she said. “We should all be very proud that we’re here in this beautiful city and we should all work to make it even better.”

Stewart also revealed that she’s still a daily subscriber of The Post, saying, “I get it delivered every morning, and I get really mad when the paper boy doesn’t show up.”

However, Stewart did call out the paper for occasionally picking on her, joking, “The illustrations are the worst. I do not like those drawings they use. But it gets readership.”

“Thank you again for helping me illuminate this beautiful structure in honor of your birthday,” she concluded.

Stewart exclusively told Page Six that despite being from New Jersey, she considers herself a proud New Yorker — though her least favorite part of the city is “double and triple parkers.”

“Since I live in my car — because I have to, I go to so many places in a single day — it’s annoying,” she expressed.

“I’d take him to the top of the Empire State Building. I’d also take him to several of the museums. He likes going to museums and learning,” she said about the rapper. “I’d like to educate him as much as I can.”

Stewart added that she’d bring her “peculiar” pal to the “iconic and delicious” Italian restaurant Elio’s in the Upper East Side.

When asked to give herself her own Post headline, Stewart joked it would be, “Martha, Don’t Make Any More Headlines.”
