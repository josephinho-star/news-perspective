Wild trail cam footage shows buck protecting a doe by tossing a bobcat giving chase with its antlers

2026-11-22 17:45

Chivalry is alive and well and this trail camera footage from the wilderness in Georgia proves it. The video shows a bobcat pursuing a doe before a buck steps between them.

The buck wasn't about to let the bobcat get close enough to attack the doe. A doe can be seen running out of the trees on one side of a small clearing into the trees on the other side.

After the doe disappears into the woods, a buck emerges out of the trees nearby. It turns and starts heading towards the trail camera. As it does, a bobcat can be seen stepping out from the trees.

The bobcat stops dead in its tracks after noticing the buck was eyeing it. There's a brief standoff as the two animals size each other up. The buck gives a couple of stomps with its front legs then charges.

This is where the video goes full National Geographic. The bobcat attempts to jump on the buck and is tossed through the air. The buck used its head and antlers to send the bobcat flying.

It's at that point that the bobcat decides that it wasn't all that hungry after all and the doe isn't worth the effort or the antlers.

It runs off, the buck gives chase, and at least as far as the matter is concerned in this footage, the doe is safe.

The little pump fake by the bobcat didn't fool the buck at all. This wasn’t going to be a playful exchange.

The buck saw the move from a mile away and charged immediately. The bobcat should have known better once the buck headed its way.

Perhaps it fell for the claim that chivalry was dead and thought it could chase the buck off and force it to leave his lady unprotected. That was not the case.
