Lindsay Clancy’s wheelchair has a curious sticker – what it could mean

2026-09-04 02:44

Lindsay Clancy’s wheelchair appears to have a bizarre nod her lawyer — in the form of a sticker, eagle-eyed trial observers noticed this week.

A pink sticker reading “#sidebah” affixed to a drink holder carrying a Diet Coke on Clancy’s wheelchair was captured in a photo on Wednesday as jury deliberations continued in the Massachusetts mother-of-three’s murder trial.

The sticker’s phrase, which has been the subject of scrutiny on social media, appears to refer to the way her lawyer, Kevin Reddington, says sidebar in his Boston accent.

The passionate criminal defense lawyer, who has held Clancy’s hand through tough moments of the trial, has become a feminist icon among many supporters of the postpartum moms.

It remains unclear whether Clancy placed the sticker on her own wheelchair, but the lighthearted quip puzzled some social media users who have followed the grueling, six-week trial.

“#LindsayClancy has the audacity to put a “joke” sticker “SideBah” on her drink holder?!?!!! Does she think that’s all a joke?!” one user wrote on X.

“The kids are in the ground and she thinks this is a damn joke ?? Tell me that’s not who she is,” another user said.

The former labor and delivery nurse then slit her wrists and throat and threw herself from the second story of the home, leaving her partially paralyzed.

Clancy claims she should be found not criminally responsible for the murders, arguing she was in the throes of severe postpartum psychosis at the time.

The 12-person jury, of nine women and three men, has been deliberating Clancy’s verdict for close to 40 hours over six days.

The judge was not swayed by Reddington’s impassioned plea to dismiss the one stubborn juror on Thursday, so the jury will return to deliberate on Friday.
