Giants survive near-collapse for statement win over Jaguars as defense dominates

2026-11-23 01:06

The Giants’ critics are going to need new material.

In a Sunday spotlight game that was supposed to legitimize the Jaguars as Super Bowl contenders, the upstart Giants instead overcame familiar self-inflicted wounds and showed their surprise season is not just the product of a weak schedule with a 23-18 win at MetLife Stadium.

Credit the defense for a monster game that included forcing two interceptions, a turnover on downs in the red zone and holding tight two other times to force chip-shot field goals, including a strange decision by the Jaguars to kick a 26-yarder on fourth-and-9 with 3:43 to go.

The Jaguars, who could have tied the game with a fourth-down touchdown and two-point conversion, only had one timeout remaining.

Cam Skattebo, who vowed to stop back-flipping earlier this week out of injury concern, broke his promise after rushing for a first down at the two-minute warning that essentially called “Game over!”

You could see Skattebo’s relief after his lost fumble earlier in the game nearly allowed the Jaguars (7-3) room to rally.
