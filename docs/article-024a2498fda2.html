Blake Snell dishes 7-word response to bromance with Tarik Skubal ending amid Mets move

2026-11-26 00:51

The pair had formed a quick friendship once Skubal was traded to the Dodgers at the deadline and evidently Snell hoped to continue to the whirlwind bromance in 2027.

Skubal signed a nine-year, 450 million landmark deal with the Mets and posted a hype video to social media once the move became official Wednesday. Snell expressed his sentiments on the Instagram post.

“What a time we had lil bro,” Snell commented with a sad face emoji.

Earlier this month, Snell said he felt like Los Angeles had a good shot at retaining the two-time Cy Young winner this offseason.

“I’ve done my pitch. I hear the Dodger fans hitting me up all the time to get lil’ bro to stay here. We’ll see what he does, but I think we have a good shot,” Snell said.

While Snell and Skubal were arm-in-arm this season, the lefties will be National League rivals in 2027.

As for the Dodgers’ plan now that Skubal has been taken off the board remains somewhat of a mystery, but it’s clear the team won’t be making the biggest splash of the winter as has been the case in previous offseasons.

It’s the end of the Skubal era in Los Angeles, but fans are anxious to see whether the Dodgers dynasty continues next season and beyond.
