Halle Berry arrives to court for restraining order, custody battle hearing as son Maceo, 13, shows up with dad Olivier Martinez

2026-10-19 15:49

Halle Berry was joined by her fiancé Van Hunt while arriving at her custody battle hearing as her ex-husband Olivier Martinez showed up with their 13-year-old son, Maceo.

The “Catwoman” actress arrived at Los Angeles County Superior Court on Monday afternoon wearing a sleek black oversized blazer, coordinating slacks, and a black blouse, according to photos obtained by Page Six.

Berry, 60, also toted a black woven purse and concealed her face with sunglasses.

Hunt stayed by his fiancée’s side while entering Stanley Mosk Courthouse with her. He was wearing a double-breasted suit.

The couple maintained stoic expressions as paparazzi snapped their photos.

Martinez, also 60, arrived at court with Maceo, who was dressed casually in basketball shorts, an eyewitness exclusively told Page Six. Last week, a judge ruled that Maceo would be testifying in court.

We’re told that once inside the courtroom, Berry glanced at Martinez when he walked in, only to quickly look away to speak with her legal team.

Martinez accused Berry of physically abusing their son, claiming that she jumped on Maceo and choked him in a September incident. He also claimed that Hunt was present during the alleged incident.

Berry denied her ex’s claims, with her attorney calling the accusations “entirely meritless and deliberately misleading.”

“There is voluminous evidence, including numerous judicial rulings, that Olivier Martinez repeatedly violated court orders and interfered with necessary therapeutic and educational interventions to the detriment of his and Halle Berry’s minor son Maceo — misconduct for which Mr. Martinez has already been legally sanctioned,” her attorney, Marina Beck, shared in a statement with Page Six at the time.

The statement continued, “Halle is used to this kind of outrageous conduct from Mr. Martinez and, while she is deeply saddened, she will not allow it to deter her from fighting for Maceo’s best interests and providing him with the love and support he needs,” the statement concluded.
