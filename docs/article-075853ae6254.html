Thomas Markle’s granddaughter says she’s ‘not surprised’ at child-sex allegations because something was always ‘off’ about him

2026-10-28 11:13

“The more and more I think about it… it just doesn’t. I don’t know why it’s not a surprise,” said Rasmussen, whose mother, Samantha Markle, is the duchess’s half-sister.

“Maybe it was a vibe that I subconsciously got… something’s not right about a person, and you don’t actively register it. But your body tells you when something’s off,” she said of her few interactions with Markle.

Rasmussen stressed that she was never molested by her grandpa — but is tormented by the fear that others could have been.

“I’m glad that I had never been victimized or had the chance to be victimized,” she told the Telegraph from her home in New Mexico.

“I don’t think he would attack family, but when you do not know what somebody’s capable of, you can’t assume.”

For this reason, she said, she “100% backs” her aunt Meghan Markle’s decision to keep her young kids away from their now-accused granddad.

“I support her decision to prevent her kids from seeing him,” she said of Archie and Lilibet, Meghan’s kids with Prince Harry.

“I wouldn’t want my kids around somebody who was being accused of child molestation,” she said.

She was equally disturbed by allegations that a letter earlier warning about her grandpa was lost by UK cops — and never forwarded to the FBI to investigate Meghan’s US-based dad.

“It blows my mind,” Rasmussen said of the apparent police failure to handle the written in 2019 by Thomas Markle’s ex-wife, Roslyn Loveless.

“That is a failure on the police’s part…that is such a failure of the system. It’s embarrassing,” she said.
