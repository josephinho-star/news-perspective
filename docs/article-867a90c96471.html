Koe Wetzel and Ella Langley's 'That's Why We Fight' performance at RedWest Festival goes viral

2026-10-20 13:18

Koe Wetzel and Ella Langley linked up for a performance going viral online.

Wetzel and Langley have a long history of working together, and the pair has pumped out some monster country music hits together.

The two released "That’s Why We Fight" in 2023 and "Jaded" this year. Both songs are big hits with country music fans.

Now, the two talented performers are, once again, setting the internet on fire.

Koe Wetzel and Ella Langley link up for a performance in Utah.

The two performed at the RedWest Festival in Utah this past Friday, and they linked up on stage to bust out a performance of "That's Why We Fight" for fans in attendance.

Wetzel shared a video of the performance on his TikTok, and as you'd expect, the duo gave fans exactly what they were expecting.
