Stocks making the biggest moves premarket: Akamai, Synopsys, Nike & more

2026-09-25 11:40

Check out the companies making the biggest moves premarket: Akamai Technologies – The cloud computing company jumped over 21% after announcing a seven-year power contract and $11.6 billion deal with Anthropic on Thursday. Akamai also issued a warrant that gives Anthropic the right to buy up to roughly 5% of the company's shares at an exercise price of $111.33 each. Scholastic – Shares for the publisher of children's books slid over 10% after the company posted a loss in the fiscal first quarter. Scholastic reported an adjusted loss of $3.63 per share, compared to the $2.52 per share loss in the year-ago period. Revenue of $216.8 million reflected a 4% decline from a year earlier. Synopsys – The software company gained over 3% in premarket trading after HSBC upgraded Synopsys to buy from hold. HSBC's Frank Lee noted the company's new business model will make Synopsys a "high-growth AI beneficiary." Nike – Shares for the athletic apparel company slid nearly 2% after Bank of America downgraded the company to underperform from neutral, expecting sales declines from the second quarter through the rest of fiscal 2027. Costco Wholesale — Shares were down slightly even after the company reported better-than-expected results for the fiscal fourth quarter. Costco earned an adjusted $6.60 per share on revenue of $95.72 billion. Analysts polled by LSEG expected a profit of $6.53 per share on revenue of $94.86 billion. — CNBC's Darla Mercado contributed reporting.
