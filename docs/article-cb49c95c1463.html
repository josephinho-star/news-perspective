Say goodbye to summer — Labor Day end-of-season furniture sales are here (save $2,000+)

2026-08-29 14:30

Get ready to refresh your home for less.

Sunsets are creeping up, the days are cooling off (for the most part), and summer is winding down — which might not feel like great news. Unless, of course, you remember what that means for your wallet.

Labor Day is nearly here, which means shoppers are about to score some of the best deals of the season, with markdowns across the board — including on furniture from top retailers including Amazon, Walmart, Wayfair and more.

Labor Day officially kicks off on Sept. 7, but many retailers will be offering Labor Day sales that stretch well beyond the holiday, giving shoppers extra time to take inventory and get ready to spruce up their homes in time for fall.

Whether you’re looking to upgrade your kitchen setup, turn your bedroom into the ultimate sleep oasis, plan a major refresh of your living room and entertainment space, or simply want to add a few end-of-summer touches to your patio setup, Labor Day furniture sales are the perfect time to start shopping and save big on something for every corner of your home.

The Post Wanted team has spent hours sifting through the best markdowns to round up the best places to shop for furniture this Labor Day weekend. Happy savings!

Amazon has something to furnish every room in your home, whether you’re searching for a sweet convertible sofa bed for total relaxation retreat or patio furniture for that end-of-summer BBQ. Plus, Prime members can score free same-day and next-day delivery on many eligible products.

Walmart’s Labor Day sale kicks off early, giving shoppers plenty of time to score serious rollbacks on sofas and sectionals, budget-friendly modular furniture, bed frames, and even dorm-life essentials. Our advice? Stock up before the best finds are gone.

Save up to $1,000 storewide at Ashley Furniture during the Labor Day 2026 Sale! Big-ticket items are the way to go, with major savings across the home. Kitchen furniture is up to 30% off, best-selling mattresses start at just $449, living room furniture is up to 40% off, and outdoor furniture is up to 60% off.

The Povision Labor Day Sale gives shoppers up to $800 off a range of fully assembled furniture. Save time with hassle-free setup while saving your wallet with sofas, chairs, storage solutions and more.

The Home Depot’s Labor Day sale runs Aug. 27 through Sept. 9, 2026, with up to 35% off select furniture and major appliances, plus savings of up to $450 on select kitchen and laundry appliances. Of course, there are plenty of outdoor deals to take advantage of as well, including over $200 off this sweet dining set.

Wayfair doesn’t mess around with its Labor Day sale. Shoppers can save up to 70% on sofas and sectionals across categories, with plenty more to shop, including rugs, bed frames, decor and more. It’s a great time for members to collect some Wayfair rewards, too.

Appliances may not always be the most exciting things to shop for — but they are when the deals are this good; you might change your mind. The hardware giant is offering up to 35% off major appliances (think fridges, dishwashers, dryers and more), plus sales on furniture, outdoor lawn and garden and decor also in the mix.
