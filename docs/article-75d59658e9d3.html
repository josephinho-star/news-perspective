Family first: Le Pen clan tightens its grip as Bardella hits trouble

2026-09-30 02:00

PARIS — In Marine Le Pen’s political world, family has a habit of finding its way back to the center of power.

That could now matter more than ever for Jordan Bardella.

The 31-year-old president of the far-right National Rally is battling allegations that he exchanged antisemitic messages as a young activist — accusations he has dismissed as “blatant falsehoods.” The controversy comes at an awkward moment for a party Le Pen has spent years trying to distance from the antisemitism associated with its past.
