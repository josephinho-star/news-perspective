8-year-old Nepal flood survivor reunited with mother after rescue

2026-08-28 21:01

Eight-year-old Zoyal Ghale survived Nepal’s devastating floods by fleeing into a forest and climbing trees to escape the rising water. Rescued from flood-hit Rasuwa with a broken leg, he was reunited with his mother who had spent days searching for him and his brother.
