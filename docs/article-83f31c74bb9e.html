George Lombard Jr. relishes first champagne celebration after spearheading Yankees rally

2026-09-15 05:11

MINNEAPOLIS — George Lombard Jr. got his first taste of champagne celebrations as a big leaguer Monday night.

The rookie shortstop was a key figure in making sure the Yankees got to do so in style on the back of an 8-3 win over the Twins, a comeback that Lombard helped spearhead with a six-run eighth inning.

“It was really cool,” Lombard said. “Obviously, a big part of the game and doing whatever I can to get on base there.”

The 21-year-old has fit right in after making his MLB debut in early August, even with some occasional defensive hiccups — the part of the game that was his calling card throughout his minor league career.

He had another in the ninth inning Monday, when he stepped off second base too early while trying to catch a throw from second baseman Anthony Volpe.

Throughout all of the ups and downs, Lombard has impressed the Yankees with his maturity, which has a chance to serve him well in his first bite at the postseason when the stakes are elevated.

“Obviously, it’s intense and every single pitch counts and everything matters, but we’re prepared for it,” Lombard said. “It’s going to be the same preparation, same routine. We just got to go out there and do our thing.”
