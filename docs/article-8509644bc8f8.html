Amazon cargo jet pilot miraculously escapes fiery crash that left 5 dead at Miami airport

2026-09-07 21:03

One of the pilots involved in the deadly Amazon cargo jet crash had jumped out of the burning plane just moments after it landed at the Miami International Airport, wild video shows.

Harrowing footage from Sunday’s crash showed the pilot struggling to get out of the downed plane as it lay burning on the airport runway, NBC Miami reported.

The pilot drops to the ground and quickly hobbles away as spectators stare in horror at the violent crash.
