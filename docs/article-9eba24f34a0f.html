‘Dutton Ranch’ Season 2 Release Date Info: When Does The Second Season Of ‘Dutton Ranch’ Come Out?

2026-09-06 14:00

Another week, another search to see when Taylor Sheridan’s hit Yellowstone spin-off, Dutton Ranch, is returning with new episodes.

Dutton Ranch Season 1 finale spoilers ahead.

Nearly two months ago, Dutton Ranch Episode 9, “El Padrino,” ended with Beth (Kelly Reilly) and Rip (Cole Hauser) learning their son Carter (Finn Little) had been kidnapped by the cartel. When we last left Beulah (Annette Bening) and Oreana (Natalie Alyn Lind) they were processing the unexpected death of Rob-Will (Jai Courtney). And Joaquin (Juan Pablo Raba) was dealing with his dad Mariano’s (Raoul Max Trujillo) return to Rio Paloma, Texas.

In addition to crazing more Dutton drama, Dutton Ranch fans have been asking a number of burning questions. Who killed Rob-Will? Is Everett’s (Ed Harris) wife still alive? Is Miguel (Berto Colón) bad news? And of course, will Dutton Ranch and Marshals have a crossover episode to save Carter and Tate (Brecken Merrill). Those are just a few questions fans need answered after “Will there be a Season 2 of Dutton Ranch?” And if so, “When does Dutton Ranch return with new episodes?”

Curious if Dutton Ranch is new this weekend? Is there a Dutton Ranch Season 1, Episode 10? What’s Dutton Ranch Season 2’s renewal status? When is Dutton Ranch Season 2’s release date? Is Rip leaving Dutton Ranch? And where can you watch Dutton Ranch online? We’ve got answers. Here’s what to know about the future of Dutton Ranch.

Is Dutton Ranch New This Weekend?

Sadly, no. There’s no new episode of Dutton Ranch on Paramount+ this weekend.

Dutton Ranch Total Episodes: Is There A Dutton Ranch Season 1, Episode 10?

Season 1 of Dutton Ranch consists of nine episodes. Not eight episodes, not ten episodes, only nine! Dutton Ranch Episode 9, “El Padrino,” served as the Season 1 finale, which means fans will have to wait for Season 2 to learn what the future holds for Beth, Rip, Carter, the Jacksons, and the rest of the Rio Paloma, Texas crew.

Will There Be A Season 2 Of Dutton Ranch? Dutton Ranch Season 2 Renewal Status:

You bet! On Wednesday, June 24, ahead of Dutton Ranch Episode 8, Paramount+ officially renewed the series! And it’s no surprise!

Dutton Ranch has a Certified Fresh 90% Tomatometer rating on Rotten Tomatoes, and according to a Paramount+ press release, “Dutton Ranch cemented itself as a smash-hit by amassing 12.9 million viewers globally in the 7 days following its premiere — marking the biggest Original Series launch in Paramount+ history. The series also delivered 2.9 million total viewers during its two-episode debut on Paramount Network on May 15, making it the biggest series premiere on cable since 2023. With 1.85 million viewers for the premiere telecast alone, Dutton Ranch also ranked as the #1 cable entertainment telecast among both adults 18-49 and total viewers.”

“Dutton Ranch has resonated with audiences in a powerful way, building on the legacy of beloved characters, while establishing its own identity,” Matt Thunell, President of Paramount Television Studios, said in the release. “Thank you to our partners at Paramount+ and 101 Studios for a phenomenal first season. We look forward to bringing fans more stories from these unforgettable characters in Season 2.”

When Does The Second Season Of Dutton Ranch Come Out? Dutton Ranch Season 2 Release Date Info:

There’s no official Dutton Ranch Season 2 premiere date on the books yet, but The Hollywood Reporter confirmed that “production slated to begin early 2027.”

“We’re slated to start early 2027 to film,” Reilly told THR in July. “But the room starts up pretty quick. I think we’re going to be very much involved in the room, so the next sort of six months is all about writing it.”

Assuming production sticks to the predicted timeline, here’s hoping Dutton Ranch Season 2 will premiere in 2027. Stay tuned for more updates!

Is Rip Leaving Dutton Ranch? Is Cole Hauser Leaving Dutton Ranch?

This is a popular question from Dutton Ranch fans — especially after recent feud escalation rumors between Hauser and Sheridan — and though the two have a long history, as far as we know, Rip isn’t leaving Dutton Ranch, nor is Cole Hauser! The Yellowstone spin-off was specifically written to continue Beth, Rip, and Carter’s stories — plus, Hauser is an EP on the series. So we don’t expect to say goodbye to Beth’s partner in crime any time soon.

Who Is Dutton Ranch‘s New Showrunner?

When Dutton Ranch returns, it will have a new showrunner in Benjamin Cavell, who joined the team ahead of Season 2. Season 1 showrunner Chad Feehan departed from the series just three weeks before its premiere.

When Does Marshals Season 2 Start?

While Dutton Ranch doesn’t have a Season 2 premiere date yet, Sheridan’s other Yellowstone spin-off Marshals does! Curious when you can see Luke Grimes/Kayce Dutton back on your screen in new episodes? Marshals Season 2 premieres Sunday, October 4, 20256 on CBS, with streaming on Paramount+.
