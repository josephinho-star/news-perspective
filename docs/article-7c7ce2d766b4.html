Alaska’s Dan Sullivan dupe: Letters to the Editor — Oct. 31, 2026

2026-10-30 21:17

The Issue: Alaskan Democrats allegedly planting a second Dan Sullivan on the ballot to confuse voters.

It’s distressing to learn that members of Senate hopeful Mary Peltola’s campaign were allegedly behind the recruitment of a candidate whose place on Alaska’s ballot was clearly meant to confuse voters (“More ‘Decoy Dan’ probes,” Oct. 30).

But there is irony in the fact that the kings of dirty tricks — the Trump regime — are complaining about receiving a dose of their own medicine. Regardless, two wrongs do not make a right.

Peltola went low, and it is embarrassing to the Democratic Party.

Peters Township, Pa.

Mail-in ballot fraud, recruiting candidates with the same name as your opponent, ranked-choice voting, illegal immigrants voting, not requiring IDs and banning ICE from polling stations: How much of our democracy do Democrats need to defraud in order to “save” it?

There is a lot of chatter right now about the election in Alaska and I agree that recruiting a BS candidate for the ballot should be illegal.

But let’s not lose sight of the bigger picture: President Trump is trying his hardest to disrupt the midterm elections because he’s afraid he’ll lose control of the House and Senate. This election is do or die for our democracy. He needs to be stopped.

Just take a look at your cost of living and vote accordingly.

Peltola’s people campaigning to recruit another Dan Sullivan to run against the senator deserves condemnation as an attempt to confuse voters. But the same standard should be applied to partisan gerrymandering.

Widespread partisan gerrymandering is a more serious threat to self-government. While voter deception seeks to distort an individual choice, gerrymandering seeks to distort the system and preserve that distortion across elections.

Neither practice becomes acceptable because the other exists. The principle should be consistent: Voters should never have their choices manipulated by those seeking to gain or retain power.

Grafton, Wisc.

“Existential threat to democracy” is the phrase Democrats use to describe Trump and his administration. Yet, it’s the Dems who allowed fraud to run rampant in Alaska, refuse to cooperate with ICE to rid our country of illegal migrants and won’t provide voter rolls to prevent noncitizens from voting.

Not one Democrat is in favor of the SAVE Act, likely because Trump is in favor of it. They continue to be the party of fraud and deceit.

It appears The New York Times has broken free of its partisan chains, exposing Alaskan Democrats’ plan to confuse names on the ballot to siphon votes away from the Republican candidate.

Has the Old Gray Lady finally decided it’s time to practice journalism rather than activism?

Before crediting the Times with seeing the light, consider it’s just as likely its editors recognized this hare-brained scheme could not possibly escape scrutiny. So they decided they might as well be the one to break the story, instead of burying it as usual. Congratulations, I guess.

Will the real Alaskan Senate candidate Dan Sullivan please stand up? A non-campaigning, Trump-hating fraud is on the ballot in Alaska. Even The New York Times made it a headline.

Peltola and her team have their fingerprints all over it. Where are Chuck Schumer and the DSCC? Where is the outrage from the liberal media?

Will the real Slim Shady — ahem, Daniel Sullivan — please stand up? We knew politics were dirty, but the Democrats have taken it to new heights with this latest scheme in the Alaska Senate race.

Delray Beach, Fla.

If the Democrats don’t cheat, they know they’ll be beat. Just imagine how dishonest and deceitful Peltola will be if she is elected, considering she’s already trying to trick her constituents.

Clearwater, Fla.
