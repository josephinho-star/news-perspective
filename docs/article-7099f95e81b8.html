How to watch Colts vs. Chiefs, Taylor Swift intel, time

2026-09-20 20:20

The Kansas City Chiefs are starting to look like their Super Bowl-winning selves again.

After a lowly 6-11 2025 season, Patrick Mahomes, Travis Kelce, Kenneth Walker III and co. were back in fine form in Week 1 roughing up their AFC West rivals, the Denver Broncos 31-10 at home while Taylor Swift watched from a suite with her parents, brother, Tom Cruise and Donna Kelce.

Today, they look to do it all over again — possibly with Swift in attendance — when Daniel Jones’ 0-1 Indianapolis Colts visit Kansas City’s Arrowhead Stadium for a big Week 2 Sunday Night Football matchup.

Oddsmakers give Andy Reid’s competitive club a cushy 6.5-point advantage with Sports Illustrated speculating that “there’s a real chance that this line moves to Chiefs -7 by the end of the week.”

Even with the cards stacked against them, Colts head coach Shane Steichen still has faith in his team.

- When: Sunday, Sept. 20, 8:20 p.m. EST

- Where: Arrowhead Stadium (Kansas City, Missouri)

“We got a lot to clean up,” he said in a press conference. “Things we gotta improve on [include] communication, tackling, we gotta limit the explosive plays, special teams penalties and we gotta be better offensively…we gotta move on.”

Should things go their way, this could be a down and dirty showdown.

If you want to tune in to this elite Week 2 AFC matchup, here’s what you need to know.
