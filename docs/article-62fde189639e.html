Inside Hailey Bieber’s tropical 30th birthday getaway with Justin, Kendall and Kylie Jenner

2026-11-25 16:31

The model rang in her 30th birthday with a tropical getaway to the Bahamas, joined by husband Justin Bieber and friends Kendall and Kylie Jenner, according to Deuxmoi.

The group was spotted enjoying the festivities at Nippers Beach Bar & Grill, though the Jenner sisters’ respective boyfriends were notably absent.

Jacob Elordi was reportedly on Long Island filming his upcoming movie, “Scapegoat,” while Timothée Chalamet was courtside at Madison Square Garden for the New York Knicks’ game against the Indiana Pacers.

Hailey also gave fans a closer look at her milestone celebration, taking to Instagram on Tuesday to share a series of snaps from her island escape.

In one photo, the birthday girl lounged aboard a boat in a metallic gold bikini, a cheetah-print cover-up and a straw hat, simply captioning the post, “30 ❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️.”

Elsewhere in the carousel, the media personality shared two sweet snaps of herself and Justin, 32, cuddling up and sharing a kiss on a dock as they watched fireworks light up the sky.

She wrapped up the post with a look at her birthday cake, which was covered in red sprinkles and featured the number 30 written out in white sprinkles.

Hailey also took to her Instagram Stories to reveal that she and her friends had a cake contest, sharing three different options and asking fans to vote for their favorite.
