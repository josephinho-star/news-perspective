Von der Leyen set to deliver major speech on Europe’s space policy

2026-08-31 17:25

BRUSSELS — European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen will deliver a major speech dedicated solely to space at the International Space Summit in Paris on Sept. 10, two officials working on preparations for the event told POLITICO.

The address comes as the Commission prepares to unveil its European Space Shield, an action plan aimed at protecting the bloc's satellites and other space assets in what officials increasingly describe as an increasingly hostile security environment.

French President Emmanuel Macron is convening a two-day International Space Summit at Paris' Grand Palais venue on Sept. 9-10. It is expected to bring together delegations from more than 120 countries alongside space agencies, industry leaders, scientists and military officials.
