Rock legend son’s haunting note before jumping off celeb-packed LA tower: ‘Don’t stand a chance’

2026-11-28 00:11

The son of a rock legend penned an apparent suicide message online two days before he plunged off a tower in West Hollywood, the California Post can reveal.

Chris Kacher, 58, wrote an ominous passage Sunday saying he had become a “person of interest” in the US and the UK and claimed he “almost jumped” to his death after being poisoned.

Kacher was the son of guitarist and music inventor Del Casher.

The UC Berkeley graduate, finance industry expert, and frequent blogger penned an updated entry in his online book that detailed a new fifth “mega-crisis” he had experienced.

He suggested that his book had cost him “everything” and that Americans and the British had it out for him.

“Sad to say but once you become a ‘person of interest’ with UK and US, you don’t stand a chance,” he said.“My marriage and my book has cost me everything.”

Kacher said the book made him subject to attacks from his ex-wife’s family and the mainstream media. He even believed there had been multiple attempts on his life.

In some of his final written words, Kacher spoke in deeply reflective terms, warning people not to revive him if gravely injured, and hinted at how he would take his life.

“While I’m deeply imperfect in many ways, I know that when I get my life review, I will be most proud of the things for which I was persecuted/prosecuted.

“The younger gen will always be our future innovators. I am grateful for the many bonds created over the years, some lifelong,” he wrote.

“In case I am badly injured, DNR (do not resuscitate). They tried to poison me with psychotics/PCP/nerve agent/etc. (makes you think you can fly) a number of times. I almost jumped to my death.

“To those who destroyed me, forgive them for they know not what they do,” he continued.

He then added a brief note, “I posted this brief part above on Facebook. If you don’t see it, it was removed by authorities.”

After finishing the note, Kacher went to check out an 18th-floor condo unit at the celeb-filled Sierra Towers on Monday.

He had been looking for a home for his mother and himself as he sold the family home to settle financial struggles kicked off by his famous father’s death in October.

He told real estate agents he liked it enough to return for another look Tuesday, according to WeHOonline. A close family friend previously told The Post that he felt Kacher had a “plan in mind.”

But the two agents lost track of him after he went into a bedroom and made the horrifying discovery that he had fallen off the building.

The Los Angeles County Medical Examiner ruled his death a suicide, meaning he had intentionally plunged from the tall building.

It was unclear why he committed suicide exactly, but the family friend said he admired his father and had been distressed about his death.

Kacher lived a lavish life — he was spotted partying with supermodels, tech moguls like Elon Musk, and traveling to exotic destinations.

His suicide marked the second fatal fall during a condo showing at the building in six years.
