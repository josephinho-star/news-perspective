Gypsy Rose Blanchard detailed Ken Urker relationship ‘challenges,’ couples therapy before his death

2026-10-02 16:53

Gypsy Rose Blanchard revealed she and fiancé Ken Urker “had challenges that would break many couples” about a year before his heartbreaking death.

The pair, notably, attended couples therapy during Season 2 of “Gypsy Rose: Life After Lock Up” and later discussed that experience in a March 2025 interview with People.

“I like it because it gives an unbiased opinion,” she explained at the time. “Because you don’t want to go to your family all the time to talk about the bad stuff. You never want to have anybody going, ‘Oh, I think…’ You don’t want their opinions to be changed or affected or a bias or anything.”

The reality star said it was “nice for [them] to have someone in the middle” to hear their opinions and sides, noting they definitely had aspects of their relationship they needed to “improve.”

“We have been through more than most couples have and have had challenges that would break many couples,” she admitted. “But we still stand strong, and I don’t ever see that changing.”

Blanchard also told the outlet that her biggest goal she hoped to achieve with couples therapy was “to have open communication.”

“I hope that we can get even more knowledge and let down our walls, because I’ve built walls over many years, and I think everybody has a set of walls that they put up. So I think it would be wonderful for us to bring those down and be the most vulnerable that we’ve ever been with each other. That would be my goal.”

As Page Six previously reported, Urker was found dead in his bed at his Raceland, Louisiana, home by a family member Thursday — on his 34th birthday.

The Lafourche Parish Sheriff’s Office is investigating Urker’s death. However, Blanchard suspects he died of an overdose, noting he was being severely cyberbullied prior to his passing.
