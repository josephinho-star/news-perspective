Army pulls off wild prank on Navy weeks before annual rivalry game

2026-11-22 00:27

Three weeks before the annual Army-Navy football game, the rivalry has already begun to heat up.

Before Navy hosted No. 24 Memphis on Saturday, Army cadets snuck into Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium and edited the large letters in the upper seating level of the stadium.

Seats that typically read “GO NAVY” became “GO ARMY.”

According to The Athletic, the Navy staff discovered the prank — or “spirit mission” as the academies call them — and began working on the seats at 6 a.m. Saturday, finishing the job by the time gates opened at 10 a.m.

It reportedly took 20 staff members to restore the stadium to order in time for Navy’s thrilling, 38-34 upset victory in which the Midshipmen scored with 15 seconds left to steal the game.

The latest prank in the historic rivalry builds some momentum for the Dec. 12 matchup at MetLife Stadium. Navy has won the past two years and leads the series 64-55-7 all time, though the prank battle advantage is more up for debate.

The two service academies have stolen each other’s mascots several times, most recently and somewhat embarrassingly in 2021, when Army tried to swipe Navy’s goat and goat-napped the wrong one.
