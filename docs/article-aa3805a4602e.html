Democratic Rep. Stevens endorses Rogers over El-Sayed in Michigan Senate election

2026-11-01 18:16

Michigan Democratic Rep. Haley Stevens, in a stunning move, endorsed Republican Senate nominee Mike Rogers over Democratic nominee Abdul El-Sayed on Sunday.

Stevens had lost the primary for Democratic nomination to El-Sayed earlier this year.

Her endorsement of Rogers came after days of rising tension with El-Sayed and his campaign over an ad by a political action committee.

"Hey Michigan, as you may have seen, our Democratic nominee for U.S. Senate, Abdul, said I'm no longer welcome in his campaign, and I should stay away, and he's right," Stevens said in a video statement on X.

"And that's why I am endorsing Mike Rogers for the United States Senate today," she said.

"I don't belong in Abdul's campaign. I do not stand with Hasan Piker, who gets trafficked on our college campuses," said Stevens.

Piker is a controversial political commentator who has campaigned with El-Sayed.

"I'm sorry people are upset," Stevens told reporters at a press conference in Detroit as she stood with Rogers, with whom she had attended church Sunday morning.

"I understand that," she said.

Rogers and El-Sayed are vying for a seat that is set to become vacant due to Democratic Sen. Gary Peters' decision not to seek re-election.
