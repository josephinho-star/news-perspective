Reddit users debate the best movie quotes of all time and the answers are truly iconic

2026-11-11 13:02

What's the greatest movie quote of all-time?

We've been on a Reddit kick lately here at OutKick, and as long as it keeps providing, then the flow of content won't stop.

As I often say, it's one of the last remaining unfiltered bastions of entertainment on the internet.

Speaking of entertainment, we have a bit of a crossover topic today.

Reddit users debate the best movie quotes.

I was scrolling through Reddit when I came across a thread titled, "What is the greatest movie quote of all time?"

Immediate click.

Check out some of the answers below, and then we'll dive in further:

- Kung fu panda . Yesterday is history tomorrow is mystery but today is valuable that's why we call it present.

- I’m gonna make him an offer he can’t refuse

- They may take our lives, but they’ll never take our freedom.

- "What you've just said is one of the most insanely idiotic things I have ever heard. At no point in your rambling, incoherent response were you even close to anything that could be considered a rational thought. Everyone in this room is now dumber for having listened to it. I award you no points, and may God have mercy on your soul."

- "Leave the gun, take the cannoli" Clemenza, The Godfather

- Literally every Doc Holliday line from tombstone. From beginning to end everything Val Kilmer said were the coolest words ever spoken

- "I am serious. And don’t call me ‘Shirley’."

- You’re gonna need a bigger boat.

- Alright, alright, alright.

- The entire Quint Indianapolis monologue

- "You see, in this world there's two kinds of people, my friend: those with loaded gun and those who dig. You dig."

- You can't handle the truth!

- We’ve all got it coming kid.

- I love you, I know - princess lea and Han Solo

- "As far back as I can remember, I always wanted to be a gangster." -Goodfellas

- The Terminator. " I'll be back".....

- Juuuuuuust a bit outside

- So you're telling me there's a chance!

- You either die a hero or you live long enough to see yourself become the villain.

Lots of great and iconic lines there. Almost too many great ones to choose from. However, there's one that always sticks out to me above all else.

The locker room speech from the 2004 film "Miracle" with Kurt Russell playing Herb Brooks:

Great moments are born from great opportunity. And that's what you have here tonight, boys. That's what you've earned here tonight. One game. If we played 'em ten times, they might win nine. But not this game. Not tonight. Tonight, we skate with them. Tonight, we stay with them. And we shut them down because we can! Tonight, WE are the greatest hockey team in the world. You were born to be hockey players. Every one of you. And you were meant to be here tonight. This is your time. Their time is done. It's over. I'm sick and tired of hearing about what a great hockey team the Soviets have. Screw 'em. This is your time. Now go out there and take it.

Another golden gem that is often overlooked is the following line from the 2015 film "Sicario" delivered by Benicio del Toro:

You should move to a small town, where the rule of law still exists. You will not survive here. You are not a wolf, and this is a land of wolves now.
