Turkish Airlines to become Liverpool shirt sponsor in reported £300 million deal

2026-09-08 12:55

Turkish Airlines will become Liverpool’s new shirt sponsor from the start of the 2027-28 season, the English Premier League club announced on Tuesday.

Liverpool did not disclose the financial terms, but the BBC reported that the five-year agreement was worth £300 million ($406 million).

The airline said its partnership with the English Premier League club would begin June 1, 2027, with its logo appearing on the men’s, women’s and academy shirts.

Turkey’s national flag carrier is controlled by the country’s wealth fund, which owns 49.1 percent of its shares. The airline says it flies to 356 destinations in 132 countries, with its network centered on İstanbul Airport.

The reported value of the agreement amounts to £60 million a season, up from Liverpool’s current £50 million-per-season deal with Standard Chartered, which has sponsored the front of the club’s shirts since 2010.

The new deal is believed to be the most lucrative agreement solely for sponsorship of the front of a shirt in Premier League history. Arsenal’s agreement with Emirates and Manchester City’s partnership with Etihad Airways also include stadium naming rights.

Liverpool’s home ground retains its original name, Anfield.

Standard Chartered will remain the club’s shirt sponsor this season before becoming a “global partner” from the 2027-28 season.

In February, Liverpool announced record revenue of more than £700 million for its most recent financial year. The club was also the highest-ranked Premier League team in the Deloitte Football Money League.

Liverpool became the first major English professional football club to have a shirt sponsor in 1979, when Japanese electronics company Hitachi’s name appeared on its shirts.

Talks with Turkish Airlines are understood to have begun before 1892 Holdings, a consortium led by Liverpool vice-chairman Amit Bhatia, bought a stake of around 38 percent in the club last month.

Liverpool chief commercial officer Ben Latty said the front of the shirt “holds a special place in the history” of the club. He added that the partnership would build on “strong foundations” from Liverpool’s Champions League final victory in İstanbul in 2005.

Turkish Airlines chief executive Ahmet Olmuştur said: “This partnership brings together two global brands united by their international reach, commitment to excellence and ability to inspire millions of people around the world.”
