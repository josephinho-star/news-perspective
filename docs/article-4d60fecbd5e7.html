Your whole household can get a lifetime VPN for just $30

2026-09-07 14:50

TL;DR: Protect up to 15 devices with FastestVPN Pro, featuring 900+ servers, unlimited bandwidth, and a suite of privacy tools for $29.97 (reg. $600).

Phones, laptops, tablets, TVs, game consoles, and whatever else is fighting for your Wi-Fi can add up fast. FastestVPN Pro covers up to 15 devices simultaneously under one account, so you don’t have to start playing favorites with your tech. For a limited time, you can grab a lifetime subscription to the service for only $29.97 (reg. $600).

This subscription nets you access to more than 900 servers across 100+ countries, with unlimited bandwidth and server switching. The 15-device allowance also means you can keep a whole household’s worth of tech connected at the same time without constantly signing devices in and out.

FastestVPN Pro goes beyond simply disguising your IP address, too. Its security and privacy toolkit includes:

- 256-bit AES encryption to protect your internet traffic

- Double VPN to route your connection through two VPN servers when you want an additional layer of privacy

- Kill switch to cut your internet connection if the VPN unexpectedly disconnects

- Split tunneling to choose which traffic travels through the VPN

- NAT firewall, ad blocker, anti-malware, and IPv6 leak protection

- P2P-optimized servers for downloads

- Dedicated streaming servers designed to work with services including Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, BBC iPlayer, and Amazon Prime.

FastestVPN also says it maintains a zero-logs policy, meaning it doesn’t store or track your browsing activity. According to the company, that policy has been independently audited.

It works with a wide range of devices, including Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, smart TVs, routers, Apple TV, Roku, Xbox, PlayStation, Kindle, Kodi, Raspberry Pi, and more. There’s also 24/7 technical support via in-app chat if you run into trouble connecting one of them.

As a bonus, the Pro plan includes one year of Passhulk Password Manager, which can store passwords, credit card and banking details, licenses, and secure attachments in an encrypted vault.

Usually $600, you can grab a lifetime subscription to FastestVPN Pro for only $29.97.
