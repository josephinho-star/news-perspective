US denies it tried to influence Malaysia’s political leadership in 2014

2026-09-06 05:40

The response came after PAS leader Hadi said a US envoy once asked the party whom it would propose as prime minister if it took power

“The United States has never sought to decide Malaysia’s political leadership,” US embassy in Malaysia Charge d’Affaires David H. Gamble Jnr said in a statement on social media on Sunday. “Any account to the contrary is false.”

According to Hadi, the ambassador asked: “Who does PAS propose as prime minister?”

The cleric said he responded: “Who does America agree with?” and it left the ambassador “stunned”. The remark drew laughter from delegates at PAS’ gathering in Kelantan state, which the party has ruled for decades.
