New Movies on Streaming: ‘Moana,’ ‘One Night Only,’ and More

2026-09-11 15:00

This week’s new movies on VOD include some fun hits that were in theaters this summer, including the live-action version of Moana and the dystopian rom-com One Night Only.

Also out this week is Mutiny, the latest Jason Statham film where he’s a one-man butt-kicking machine. Statham stars in the film as former Royal Marine commando Cole Reed, a man framed for killing his boss. When he boards a ship as a stowaway to find answers that will help clear his name, he uncovers a conspiracy bigger than he ever realized. Peaky Blinders‘ Annabelle Wallis co-stars.

These are just a few of the films that are available to watch on Amazon Prime Video, iTunes, YouTube, and through your cable service this week. Check out what movies are available to buy or rent on demand now.

Despite being panned by critics, Disney’s live-action version of Moana, starring Dwayne “The Rock” Johnson as Maui and Catherine Laga’aia as Moana, took in over $300 million at the box office this summer. The film arrives to digital platforms this week, so if you missed it in theaters, you can buy or rent it at home. (No word on when Moana will hit Disney+, but based on other films’ rollout schedules, you could expect to see it as early as October.)

One Night Only is that rare sci-fi rom-com, but even though it’s got a twinge of dystopia, it’s still ultimately just a way to see hot people flirting and hooking up. In the universe of the film, premarital sex is banned, except for one night a year when single people are legally allowed to have sex. Which means that on that one night, everyone’s looking to get some tail. Callum Turner stars as Owen, a man who gets ditched by his girlfriend so she can hook up with someone else, and keeps running into Allie (Monica Barbaro). It takes a while but eventually they realize they should probably just boink.
