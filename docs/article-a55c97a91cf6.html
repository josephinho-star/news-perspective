Apple AirTags just dropped to $24 on Amazon — one of the best times to stock up

Keys buried in the couch? Luggage nowhere in sight? An AirTag can help you track down easy-to-lose belongings through Apple's Find My network. Right now, both single AirTags and four-packs are discounted at Amazon, making now a good time to put one on your keys, slip one into your luggage or stock up for the whole household.

If there's one important item you constantly misplace, attach an AirTag and use your iPhone to track it down. The Find My app can trigger a chime from the AirTag that's 50% louder than the previous generation, making nearby belongings easier to find.

If your item is farther away, you can securely share its location with friends and family. More than 50 airlines also support AirTag location sharing, which can help them track down misplaced luggage. Only authorized users can access the location, and AirTag doesn't store location history.

AirTags run for about a year on a replaceable coin cell battery, and your iPhone lets you know when it's running low. Keep a pack on hand so you're ready when it's time for a swap.

Do you constantly lose your keys, the remote or other daily essentials? An AirTag four-pack can save you from tearing the house apart every time something goes missing. Precision Finding provides directions on your iPhone or Apple Watch to help lead you to nearby items.

Setup takes just a tap: Connect each AirTag to your iPhone or iPad and give it a name based on what you're tracking. If something goes missing far away, mark it as lost to receive updates when it's detected through the Find My network.

AirTags are also built to handle everyday wear and tear. They're dust-, splash- and water-resistant, so you don't have to worry about the occasional spill or rainy day.
