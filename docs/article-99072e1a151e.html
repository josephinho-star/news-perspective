Margot Robbie turns heads in blue bikini on set of new 'Ocean's Eleven' prequel

2026-09-18 20:52

Margot Robbie was spotted sporting a blue bikini while filming her latest project.

The 36-year-old actress was photographed in a 1960s-style blue bikini top and matching high-waisted bottoms while filming her new movie in Saint-Tropez, France, with Bradley Cooper.

Some of the photos feature the actress standing knee-deep in the ocean and walking along the shore, while others showcase behind-the-scenes moments in which she was hanging out with Cooper and reading the script.

Robbie and Cooper are currently filming the prequel to the 2001 comedy heist movie "Ocean's Eleven," starring Brad Pitt, George Clooney and Julia Roberts.

The prequel follows the story of Danny Ocean’s parents, played by Robbie and Cooper, as they attempt to pull off a heist at the 1962 Monaco Grand Prix.

"Before Danny Ocean ever stepped foot in Vegas, two masterminds taught him everything he knows — his parents," Robbie said at CinemaCon in July, per Variety. "You’ll see them in their prime, and in our new movie, pulling off an epic heist at the 1962 Monaco Grand Prix."

In addition to Cooper and Robbie, the film will feature Academy Award-nominated actor Wagner Moura, Josh Gad and Academy Award-nominated actress Monica Barbaro.

Cooper is also acting as a writer and director of the film, which is being produced by Robbie's production company, LuckyChap Entertainment, which she owns with her husband, Tom Ackerley.

The "Wuthering Heights" star got her start on the Australian TV show "Neighbors," but had her big breakthrough in the 2013 film "The Wolf of Wall Street," alongside Leonardo DiCaprio.

She has worked steadily in Hollywood since, but explained to Variety for its January 2026 issue that things have changed for her the longer she has been in the industry.

"Earlier in my career, I’d speak more freely in interviews. I’ve just been burnt so many times, when people have taken what I’ve said out of context. And I read stuff all the time where people put me in quotation marks saying things I’ve never said."

Robbie recalled not being able to "get my head around it" the first time that happened to her, and said she "just had to accept that."

After becoming a mother, however, Robbie explained there are certain things she will not tolerate anymore, noting, "I’m trying to keep that side of things private and protect him."

"When you shift from your 20s to your 30s and beyond, you think, ‘I am going to do things differently. Here are my new boundaries,'" the actress said.

She and Ackerley welcomed their son in October 2024. The couple has chosen to keep details about their son private, and have not shared his name, public photos or any additional details.
