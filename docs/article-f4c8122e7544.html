Bethenny Frankel wears this $50 cashmere sweater ‘on repeat,’ and it’s got 35,000 glowing reviews

2026-09-25 17:22

Autumn’s calling, and Bethenny Frankel has answered.

The “RHONY” legend recently took to Instagram to share her fall go-tos, and one stand-out essential she name-checked is a $50 cool-weather hero: Quince’s Mongolian Cashmere Crewneck Sweater.

She captioned the Story, “Wearing on repeat … a staple” — and shoppers couldn’t agree more that it’s a wardrobe must-have. The cozy top has racked up almost 36,000 reviews on the direct-to-consumer website and has a near-perfect rating.

The “Just B” podcast host featured the camel shade, but it also comes in over a half dozen other options. It’s made from a pill-resistant, sustainably sourced cashmere and is sold in sizes XS-XL, plus extended sizes. (Some shoppers say they size up for a more relaxed fit.)

Part of the brand’s massive cashmere line, which ranges from V-necks to plush cardigans, the crewneck is a classic cut that never goes out of style.

“This cashmere sweater is to die for! It is so soft, and the blue color brings out the blue in my eyes,” reads one of the thousands of reviews. “I’ll be wearing it a lot this winter to keep cozy and cute.”

“This is at least my fourth one of the Mongolian Cashmere crewneck,” wrote another fan. “All the colors are beautiful; the yarn is soft, luxurious. You can’t go wrong. I hand wash them. Sometimes I get a larger size for a more casual look or layering.”

It’s not the only Quince style in Frankel’s closet, as she also shouted out an Italian leather shoe from the brand a few weeks ago.

Sarah Michelle Gellar, Chelsea Handler and Jessica Alba have also been spotted in the label, with the latter wearing the Mongolian Cashmere Oversized Boyfriend Cardigan last year.
