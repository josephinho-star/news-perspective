Ivy League student slashed in neck, suspect accused was arrested just weeks before

2026-11-17 15:29

One man is facing charges after police say a college student was slashed in the neck by a suspect in a brazen attack that happened outside a dorm hall.

The New York Police Department says the victim, only identified as a 21-year-old woman, was stabbed in the back of her neck next to her ear.

It happened Thursday evening outside the dormitory on 600 W. 116th Street. The young woman was a student at Columbia University's Barnard College, the campus newspaper reports.

"I originally thought that someone had robbed Morton Williams and I was like, ‘Wow, they have a lot of police officers for that.’ But then I saw a girl, and she had blood on her neck," Eva Kates a student at the school told the newspaper.

NYPD confirmed to Fox News Digital the victim was walking on on the street when the the suspect lunged at her and cut her with an unknown object behind her right ear.

"Victim was transported to St Lukes Hospital for a laceration. The perpetrator was arrested on scene," NYPD said.

Earl Baker, a 44-year-old man from Brooklyn was arrested. He pleaded not guilty to a charge of assault with intent to cause injury with a weapon.

According to online court records Baker also has a previous criminal case listed from Oct. 16 , where he is due on court on Dec. 21. No charge information is available for that case.

Police are still looking into a possible motive for the attack.

Fox News Digital reached out to Barnard College , which chose not to comment on the incident and instead directed the inquiry back to NYPD.
