Britain’s Liberal Democrats get a new think tank

2026-09-01 02:00

LONDON — Britain’s Liberal Democrats are dreaming of a return to government. A new think tank wants to help.

The Britain Project — a newly formed group offering liberal policy ideas — will officially launch at the party’s conference in Brighton on September 21.

It comes as divisions on the right of British politics make the idea of a progressive pact involving the Liberal Democrats at the next election more likely, even as their poll ratings stagnate.
