Kim Kardashian transforms into 13-year-old daughter North West for Halloween — including blue hair and piercings

2026-10-20 18:25

A mini-me makeover.

Kim Kardashian transformed into her eldest daughter, North West, for Halloween this year.

The reality star showed off her unrecognizable look via Instagram Tuesday, rocking West’s signature waist-length turquoise blue hair, spiky lashes, facial piercings and black grills.

Kardashian, wore an oversized black graphic tee with black-and-white striped sleeves, a chunky silver chain necklace with a skull-and-crossbones pendant and tons of spiked jeweled accessories.

She completed the look with a set of extra-long, pointed stiletto nails featuring a black-and-silver gothic designs, very reminiscent of her daughter’s manicure.

“Halloween 2026🧟♀️,” she captioned the carousel of snaps in which she posed just like her 13-year-old daughter.

“Twins!!!!!!” Kim’s sister Kourtney commented on the upload. Her mom, Kris Jenner added, “This is so fun!!!!! .”

Many fans admitted that they were fooled at first.

“Girl I thought this was northhh😂😂,” one wrote. “Wait y’all look like twins!!!!!” another added.

Kim’s costume comes after months of backlash she’s faced for the teen’s dramatic look.

Earlier this month, the mother-daughter duo addressed claims that West dresses “demonic.”

“Oh no, I’m not demonic, guys. Stop saying I’m demonic. No, I’m not,” the aspiring rapper said on a recent TikTok Live.

“Just because I like to dress this way, don’t judge a book by its cover,” West, who sported bright blue hair, added.

Kim said she feels like people call her daughter demonic because of the black grill and piercings she wears. However, during that same TikTok live, they revealed that her piercings are not real.

Zooming in one a few of them, she poked fun at those who haven’t noticed. “I change them so much that you guys think it’s real.”

She has previously expressed her wishes to get real piercings, but her mom has shut that down.

Kim — who shares North, Saint, Chicago and Psalm with ex-husband Kanye West — has previously shared her struggles with criticism over North’s look with Alex Cooper on “Call Her Daddy.”

“It’s really hard and it’s really interesting because all the kids are wearing the same things,” she explained, adding, “But then my daughter tries to wear it and then I’m like, ‘okay, we’re never wearing that again.’”

She added, “Unfortunately, we made that mistake in front of the whole world.”
