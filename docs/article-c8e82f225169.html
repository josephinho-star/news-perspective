Everything To Know About ‘Marshals’ Season 2

2026-09-13 14:30

It’s been almost four months since Marshals — Taylor Sheridan’s first Yellowstone sequel spin-off — aired its Season 1 finale and left viewers craving more Dutton drama. But Season 2 is already right around the corner!

For those who need a refresher, after Kayce (Luke Grimes), Mo (Mo Brings Plenty), Cal (Logan Marshall-Green), Belle (Arielle Kebbel), Cruz (Ash Santos), Miles (Tatanka Means), and even Tate (Brecken Merrill) came together to fight an armed team hired to assassinate Rainwater (Gil Birmingham), Marshals Episode 13 saw the core characters part ways. Cruz was headed to D.C., Belle and Cal teamed up on an investigation ahead of Cal’s oncology appointment, Kayce got closer to Dolly (Ellyn Jameson), and Tate went to Texas to fish with Tom Weaver (Chris Mulkey), who will likely be Season 2’s main antagonist.

Since Marshals has some major unfinished storylines, drama, romance, and lots more storytelling potential (including a potential crossover with Kelly Reilly and Cole Hauser’s Yellowstone sequel spin-off Dutton Ranch), fans are eager to learn more about what’s to come.

Wondering if there will be a Marshals Season 2 on CBS and Paramount+? When does Season 2 of Marshals come out? What time will new episodes of Marshals air? Is there a Marshals Season 2 trailer? And how can you stream Marshals Season 1? Here’s everything to know about Season 2 of Marshals, including Season 2 premiere date info, a trailer breakdown, and beyond.

Will There Be A Season 2 Of Marshals? Marshals Season 2 Renewal Status:

You bet! In March 2026, ahead of its third episode, CBS announced a very early Marshals Season 2 renewal after the series premiere became the “best network TV launch since 2017” and “most streamed CBS episode ever.”

“MARSHALS delivered a breakout performance, capturing a massive audience across platforms and quickly establishing itself as one of TV’s most powerful new series,” said Amy Reisenbach, president of CBS Entertainment. “The overwhelming viewer response speaks to the strength of the YELLOWSTONE world, the bold character-driven storytelling from the creative team and the dynamic cast performances led by Luke Grimes.”

When Does Marshals Return On CBS? Marshals Season 2 Release Date:

Marshals Season 2 premieres Sunday, October 4, 2026 on CBS, with streaming on Paramount+. The official synopsis for Episode 201, “All Hat No Cattle,” is as follows: “When Kayce realizes Tate has been taken hostage, he goes rogue in a desperate race to save his son as the Marshals uncover a powerful criminal operation tied to the East Camp land grab. A violent showdown ensues, putting everything Kayce cares about at risk.”

When Does Season 2 Of Marshals Come Out? What Time Does Marshals Come Out?

While Marshals will return to its typical Sunday 8:00 p.m. ET/PT time slot starting October 11, whene Season 2 premieres on Sunday, October 4 on CBS it will air at 8:30 p.m. ET due to an NBC doubleheader.

What Will Marshals Season 2 Be About?

Marshals Season 2 will undoubtedly follow the team on fresh cases, but crucially, the sophomore season has several major storylines to continue. Season 1’s finale ended with Cruz planning to leave Montana behind to head to D.C., so we’ll have to see how she returns to the team. When we last left Belle and Cal they were getting shot at, so we’ll see how their high-stakes investigation unfolds. We’ll have to see if Kayce takes the next step in his relationship with Dolly, how Tate gets home safely from his bass fishing trip with Tom Weaver, and what Weaver’s next moves will be. Here’s a sneak peek of Marshals Season 2 to get you hyped!

Is There A Marshals Season 2 Trailer?

You bet! On September 2, we got an eventful Marshals Season 2 trailer that teased the battle ahead. Kayce is ready to uphold the Dutton family legacy of “always having to fight” — for his son, his team, his chosen family, and his land.

When Does Dutton Ranch Season 2 Return?

While Kelly Reilly and Cole Hauser’s Yellowstone spinoff Dutton Ranch was also officially renewed for Season 2, there’s no official Dutton Ranch Season 2 premiere date on the books yet. That said, The Hollywood Reporter confirmed that “production [is] slated to begin early 2027.”

“We’re slated to start early 2027 to film,” Reilly told THR in July. “But the room starts up pretty quick. I think we’re going to be very much involved in the room, so the next sort of six months is all about writing it.”

Assuming production sticks to the predicted timeline, here’s hoping Dutton Ranch Season 2 will premiere in 2027. Stay tuned for more updates!

Will There Be A Marshals And Dutton Ranch Crossover?

Based on the Season 2 trailer, we get the sense that Tate’s rescue will happen fairly quickly and perhaps not require any help from Kayce’s Texas fam, but we’ll ultimately have to wait and see what Sheridan has up his sleeve. And since Marshals is returning much sooner than Dutton Ranch, there’s still hope for Kayce to appear in Dutton Ranch Season 2 to try to help rescue Carter. Until we know more, here’s our case for a crossover.

Where Can I Watch Marshals? Paramount+ Streaming Info:

Paramount+ offers two subscription plans, Essential and Premium. Right now, you can get started with a seven-day free trial on the Premium plan.

The ad-supported Essential plan typically costs $8.99/month, while the ad-free Premium plan (which comes with Showtime titles and live CBS) costs $13.99/month.
