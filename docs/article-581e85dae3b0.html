How Alix Earle helped make this self-tanner a ‘DWTS’ staple

2026-09-29 17:09

The mirrorball isn’t the only thing glowing on “Dancing With the Stars.”

Between celebs and their pro partners, nearly half the cast get bronzed pre-performance using Dolce Glow, a self-tanning brand that “really blew up” on set when Alix Earle competed on the show last season.

“I already had a relationship with Alix, so when she joined the show, she was getting tanned every week,” founder Isabel Alysa told Page Six Style.

“Once everyone started seeing her tan, it became this domino effect, which I’m so grateful for.”

Now, with Season 35 currently airing, the brand’s worked with everyone from pros like Derek Hough, Val Chmerkovskiy and Jenna Johnson to competitors including Julia Stiles, Maura Higgins, Jenna Dewan and Sarah Jane Nader.

But you needn’t have Alysa on speed dial to get glowing at home. She recommends using her Express 2-Hour Self-Tanning Mousse to achieve the same “smooth, longer-lasting natural tan” in no time.

If you really want the ballroom treatment, follow it up with the brand’s Post Tan Drying Powder and Mia Instant Body Glow — the latter of which she calls her “favorite finishing touch, especially on the legs.”

“These dancers are sweating, moving and changing costumes constantly, so they need a tan that isn’t going to drip or rub off,” she explained.

“It also has to look just as good under those stage lights and on camera as it does in person. That’s always been really important to me with Dolce Glow — the tan should look like your skin, just better.”

It’s been a big year for Alysa, as her brand also just completed an $11 million series A funding round, with investors including famous clients like Olivia Culpo, Sofia Richie Grainge and Yris Palmer.

But at the end of the day, the self-esteem boost that comes from a camera-ready glow is priceless.

“There’s a real family feeling behind the scenes,” Alysa said. “I love that they love the tan, but even more than that, I love hearing how it makes them feel when they step onto the dance floor. That’s really what Dolce Glow has always been about for me — confidence.”
