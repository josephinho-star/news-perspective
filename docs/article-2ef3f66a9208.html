Hugh Jackman, Sutton Foster enjoy romantic beach day in Australia

2026-11-14 19:00

Hugh Jackman and Sutton Foster enjoyed a recent trip to the beach.

The couple was photographed at Bondi Beach in Sydney, Australia, after the actor recently celebrated his 58th birthday.

In the photos, Jackman and Foster are seen walking along the shore and relaxing on the sand.

One of the photos shows Foster looking casual in a white T-shirt and black workout shorts, which she paired with sunglasses and a black bucket hat, with her hair styled in braids underneath.

The two were all smiles as they played Frisbee together. Jackman wore a blue long-sleeve shirt, sunglasses, pink-and-white patterned swim trunks and a green baseball cap. Bondi Beach is a familiar destination for Jackman, who previously spent time there with his ex-wife and their kids.

Other photos show Foster and Jackman them leaning toward each other and sharing a kiss.

Jackman and Foster were first photographed together as a couple in January 2025 after months of relationship rumors. The photographs showed them holding hands while out in Santa Monica, publicly revealing their romance, though it is unclear when they began dating.

Jackman and Foster previously worked together in the Broadway production of "The Music Man," which ran from 2021 to 2023.

"He has an impeccable reputation of being the hardest working man, incredibly kind, and generous—and all of that is true. He disarms everyone, and he doesn’t make anything about him," Foster told Vogue in June 2022 about Jackman. "And he’s now become one of my best friends, which was a surprise, because you usually go into these things thinking, ‘Well, I hope we get along.’"

In September 2023, Jackman announced that he and his wife, Deborra-Lee Furness, were separating after 27 years of marriage. Foster filed for divorce from her husband, screenwriter Ted Griffin, in October 2024, after 10 years of marriage.

They made their red carpet debut as a couple a year later at the premiere of Jackman's film, "Song Sung Blue," in October 2025.

Foster and Griffin share a daughter, Emily, while Jackman and Furness share two children: Oscar and Ava. Jackman and Furness finalized their divorce in June 2025.

"We have been blessed to share almost 3 decades together as husband and wife in a wonderful, loving marriage," Furness and Jackman said in a statement shared with People magazine about their separation. "Our journey now is shifting, and we have decided to separate to pursue our individual growth."

The "Wolverine" star and the "Younger" actress were previously spotted packing on the PDA during a romantic vacation in Costa Rica in January.
