Oprah’s Favorite Things 2026 list is here, featuring everything from beauty products to Birkenstocks

2026-11-12 14:12

Oprah’s most famous tradition is turning 30.

The media mogul just celebrated the anniversary of her beloved “Favorite Things” list with a 79-product 2026 installment, which she says includes “something for just about everybody.”

For the first time in 15 years, she’ll also be hosting a special holiday episode of “The Oprah Podcast” on Prime Video on Nov. 12, which sees her gifting her favorites to an unsuspecting group of caregivers.

While the full list will appear in Oprah Daily’s special print 2026 Holiday Edition, on newsstands Nov. 17, we’ve curated a few of the can’t-miss highlights below — from cozy treats to beauty buys.

Oprah declared this Vuori design her “new favorite workout pants.” It features thoughtful details like peachy soft fabric, a drawstring tie and a “no-slip” fit. Kaia Gerber and Gwyneth Paltrow are also fans of the brand’s leggings.

With loyal clients including Kendall Jenner and Hailey Bieber — the latter of whom uses her M.ph lip gloss, too — Mary Phillips is one of the most well-known makeup artists in Hollywood.

Available in eight pretty shades, the formula is non-sticky and buildable — and you can easily see the color through the clear tubes, making it a little easier to pick. (Though why not try a few?)

Over a decade after Oprah praised Kiehl’s Créme de Corps Body Butter, the apothecary-inspired brand is back on her radar thanks to this hand cream she called “surprisingly good.” (Our Style & Shopping Director, Elana Fishman, swears by it too!)

It’s rare to see an Amazon clothing item under $30 on Oprah’s list, but she delivered just that with this effortlessly chic pick. It comes in a variety of colors, from white and black to multiple striped options.

“I’ve searched far and wide for the perfect white shirt—and this is it,” she declared on the list, saying she purchased it herself.

Who said you have to ditch your sandals come fall? These shearling-lined Birkenstocks can be worn around the house as slippers. We’ve also spotted Meghan Markle, Oprah’s friend and Montecito neighbor, wearing the same pair.

Oprah’s in good company when it comes to loving Gap’s viral denim tote. Everyone from Gayle King to Jordyn Woods owns one, while Martha Stewart said on Instagram that she’s “in love” with the pocket-covered carryall — despite not being a “handbag girl.”

Perfect for travel, this poncho can also be repurposed as a scarf or a blanket at a moment’s notice.

“We looked high and low before we found this,” Oprah wrote. “It looks great, and the price can’t be beat.”

“If you like a matte finish, Anastasia’s lip mousse is perfect,” the media mogul wrote. “You’d find one in my makeup bag right now.”

In a pickle searching for a gift for a racquet sports enthusiast? These Oprah-approved shoes were co-created by tennis great Roger Federer — though they’re designed for pickleball or padel, too.

“These are my sneakers — and I wear them a lot,” she wrote.

There’s no place like home — or Oprah’s “Favorite Things” list. These sequined slippers come in both kids and adult sizes, and the box even contains a foam apple like the one that falls from the ceiling during the Sphere’s “Wizard of Oz” show.

“I think this is really great,” Oprah said in a teaser video for the list, while holding the cooling plate attachment up to her neck. It also comes with two other modes: a fan and a mister.

“It’s great for heat waves, but I’m also giving it to all my ‘hot flashing’ friends,” she added on the list, saying she packed it for a trip to France over the summer.

Oprah’s list often includes a luxe bath product and this year’s was no exception. She picked this mineral-rich, waterless bath powder, which features ingredients like pink Himalayan salt and coconut milk powder.
