Khloé Kardashian bought these K-beauty lipsticks in ‘every color’

2026-09-23 23:37

Even Khloé Kardashian isn’t immune to a mid-scroll impulse buy.

While speaking with the Zoe Report about her favorite beauty trends this week, the Good American co-founder organically name-dropped a surprising favorite product from K-beauty brand Colorgram.

“TikTok is dangerous, the ads are just right there,” she quipped. “I buy these Colorgram lipsticks in every color because they have every color. For some reason I need it.”

While Kardashian didn’t name-check an exact product, she was likely referring to the company’s Nude Blur Tint, which is available in a wide range of shades, from pretty pinks to burnt maroon. And since it’s rare for her to shout out a beauty buy that’s not from a brand owned by her own famous family, we were immediately intrigued.

The lightweight, non-sticky matte formula can also be used as a cheek tint. (One Amazon reviewer even called it “so blurring, it’s insane.”)

Better yet, the product’s currently 25% off on Amazon; just be sure to check “apply” on the digital coupon before checkout.

Since expanding to the US, Colorgram has gained traction — and now, Kardashian’s attention — on TikTok shop.

The Khloud founder’s other beauty non-negotiables include mascara — “I wear [it] to the gym, and my gym is in my basement,” she quipped — and moisturizer.

“I cannot not put a moisturizer on, it just feels wrong,” she said. “I love to be lubed up when I’m going to sleep … my face, obviously.”

Kardashian’s routine these days also includes her new fragrance, XO Blue, which she describes as a “vacation in a bottle.”

“It’s coconut and transports you to a beach, maybe to a hot man,” she joked to the Hollywood Reporter of the fresh scent.
