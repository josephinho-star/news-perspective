New balloons will fly in the postponed 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade

2026-11-27 09:45

Some new balloons are set to fly in the weather-delayed Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade.

The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade is set for tomorrow after weather delays in New York.

WILL COSS: You only have one chance to celebrate one hundred parades, and we're not going to let that opportunity slip through our fingers.

MARTÍNEZ: Will Coss is the parade's executive producer. The postponement meant figuring out logistics for star performers, volunteers and young musicians, some of whom traveled hundreds of miles.

COSS: These kids, high school collegiate musicians have been practicing for months and months and months leading in, so we want to do everything that we can to ensure that everyone can remain here in New York City and show up, represent their school and have a wonderful parade.

MARTÍNEZ: Not everyone can stay, though, and the performance by the full Great American Marching Band of high school students was canceled. One high school band from the Houston area raised $350,000 to pay for two more days of hotel rooms, meals and new trips home for the 500-person group. Now, the early Macy's parades were a bit different.

COSS: Our first parade in 1924 had live animals. But the animals unfortunately scared the children a bit more than our early parade producers had anticipated.

MARTÍNEZ: So they brought in the balloons. For the 100th parade on Saturday, there will be a hundred balloons. It takes a lot of planning to get them up in the air.

COSS: Each balloon has its own level of complexity, so a balloon from beginning to end can take anywhere from four to six months.

MARTÍNEZ: This year, there will be eight new giant balloons, including Bluey, Hello Kitty and Mickey Mouse from "The Sorcerer's Apprentice." Returning favorites include SpongeBob SquarePants, Spider-Man and the balloon that made its debut in 1927 as the parade's first inflated character, Felix the Cat. Each one begins with a simple drawing.

COSS: Every balloon that you see in the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade is hand-sketched and hand-fabricated down to the pupil on an eye, the detail on the sneaker. Every element across the entire balloon is fabricated by hand by our talented team at the studio.

MARTÍNEZ: The balloons are made of waterproof polyurethane fabric. The pieces are then heat-sealed together and painted. How giant are these giant inflatables? Well, they're about three stories tall.

COSS: If you've ever been to New York City, it's a hustle and bustle, and there's just an energy on the ground. The street just calms for a bit. And then you just look up, and there goes Snoopy.

MARTÍNEZ: We asked Coss which balloon he's most excited to see overhead.

COSS: That's a tough question. The balloons are like my children.

MARTÍNEZ: At the end of the day, he says, it's all about the memories.

COSS: We have a great opportunity to not only create some excitement, create some surprise, create some delight. We have an opportunity to create lifelong memories.

MARTÍNEZ: The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade takes place tomorrow. It airs on NBC and streams on Peacock.
