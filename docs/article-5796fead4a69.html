Miley Cyrus marries Maxx Morando in intimate Palm Springs wedding

2026-11-22 00:46

Cue the flowers… Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are married!

The singer and her rocker beau tied the knot in a lavish wedding ceremony held at a private residence in Palm Springs, California, on Saturday, according to the Daily Mail.

Cyrus’ parents, dad Billy Ray and mom Tish, were present for the nuptials alongside their respective partners, Elizabeth Hurley and Dominic Purcell.

During her Oct. 18 show at the iconic venue in Los Angeles, the “Flowers” singer, 33, brought out Morando, 28, as a special guest. He played drums while she belted out her song “Let’s Get Married” (from her new “Bass Persuades” album) during the encore.

Page Six was at the show and witnessed the two appearing head over heels while performing the romantic track together. As the song wrapped, Cyrus adorably kissed her fiancé before walking off stage.

As previously reported, Cyrus and Morando got engaged in Kyoto, Japan, in late 2025, after four years of dating.

“We went to Tokyo first for a couple days, and then on to Kyoto. I think she had a sense that it was coming, so I was waiting until the very last moment of the very last day [of our trip] to throw her off the scent as much as I could,” Morando told Wonderland magazine in July, while reflecting on his proposal.

“We took a walk around the hotel grounds. There was this little outer garden … we walked in, and it was a little dead end, this beautiful Japanese forest-y situation. She was taking videos and was turned away. That’s when I got on one knee and tapped her on the shoulder.”

The musician popped the question to the former “Hannah Montana” star with a 14-karat gold creation by celebrity-loved jewelry designer Jacquie Aiche.

The sparkler was front and center as Cyrus walked the red carpet for the “Avatar: Fire and Ash” premiere in Los Angeles last December with Morando by her side.

“Me and Max have been together for four years, and it’s very obvious the drastic amount of growth that I’ve had in those four years … a lot of that is because I have a great partner,” she later gushed during a guest appearance on “Good Morning America,” after Page Six exclusively confirmed the engagement.

“You just want to dream the biggest you can, and you want to have someone by your side that always tells you you can do it.”

“We got put on a blind date. Well, it was blind for me and not really for him,” she recalled, revealing they started dating “a couple of summers” ago at the time. “I thought, ‘The worst that can happen is I leave.'”

In the years since then, the two have supported each other at several high-profile events, where they haven’t been shy about packing on the PDA. They’ve also gushed over their love in various interviews and on social media.

“I ended up with a person who means a lot to me and treats me really well and respects me,” Cyrus said in a September 2025 interview with the Cut. “My man’s hot as hell.”

As for how Cyrus knew Morando was “the one” for her?

“There’s a whole list of things. I can’t read you my vows,” she joked to Apple Music’s Zane Lowe in September 2025. “But the No. 1 thing I think that I noticed from the beginning was we talked about the kind of wisdom of a child and just, like, their unknowing is what makes them so brilliant. That’s what I love about Maxx the most.”

“This part of my life is something I’ve always wanted,” she added of their relationship. “And this is what I hope other people get to have, what I have. I don’t know if everybody gets to find their Maxx.”

Miley — who is the daughter of country singer Billy Ray Cyrus and Tish Cyrus — was previously married to actor Liam Hemsworth.

The two tied the knot in 2018 but called it quits less than a year later.

The “Hunger Games” star has since moved on with model Gabriella Brooks. The two have been engaged since September 2025.
