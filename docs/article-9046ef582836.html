‘Mean Girls’ star reveals ‘secrets’ of the White House she discovered childhood hangouts with first daughter Amy Carter

2026-11-27 15:16

Ana Gasteyer said that hanging out at the White House as a young girl was like being Eloise at the Plaza.

The “Mean Girls” star — who played Betsy Heron, Cady Heron’s (Lindsay Lohan) mother — became friends with President Jimmy Carter’s daughter, Amy Lynn, because they both played the violin and liked to read.

“I just thought it was the most glamorous thing on Earth,” she told Page Six in an exclusive interview.

“There were all these secret … like dumbwaiters and stuff. There’s a single-lane bowling alley; it’s amazing, we used to bowl.”

“When you’re a kid, you kind of just wander around,” she explained. “I remember those dumbwaiters. I thought it was just a magic trick.”

The “SNL” alum, who was known for her wicked impersonation of Martha Stewart, is currently on a nationwide tour of “Ana Gasteyer’s Sugar & Booze: A Holiday Spectacular,” in which she sings holiday favorites from “Sugar & Booze,” her 2019 album of seasonal favorites and holiday originals.

“I am not a religious person,” she explained, “But I love the holidays. I love to light; I love the parties; I love this sentiment. I loved that people, you know, even like office parties.

“You know, it’s like the one time of year people are like, you know what, it’s cold outside. We’re tired. It’s been a long one. Let’s, like, let it rip for a second and also, like, kick our shoes off and that’s what I love about it.”

She also threw her hat in the ring for the role of Duchess of Christmas after acknowledging Mariah Carey is the Queen of Christmas.

“Mariah’s the Queen, but I’m really happy to be the Duchess!” she said.

Additionally, it’s been a big year for Gasteyer. She was nominated for a Tony for her role in “Schmigadoon” and snatched the Emmy for Outstanding Voice Performer in a Preschool Animated Program for her role in “RoboGobo.”

She will take her “Sugar & Booze” Holiday Spectacular to New York City’s Town Hall on Dec. 4.
