NFL legends, Hall of Famers put Aaron Rodgers on blast for Steelers rant

2026-11-26 20:45

This time of year, everyone typically expresses what they are grateful for.

Not Aaron Rodgers.

The now former Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback went on to the Pat McAfee Show on Wednesday, lighting up the organization and head coach Mike McCarthy. That prompted the Steelers to swiftly cut the future Hall of Fame quarterback.

As the day progressed, reactions surfaced all over the internet. Many people came to the defense of Rodgers, pointing out that everything he stated during his interview was accurate and true. While others had a very different perspective, none louder than Hall of Fame tight end Shannon Sharpe.

Sharpe, while on Wednesday night’s episode of the Night Cap podcast, absolutely eviscerated Rodgers’ character.

“Every time I critiqued Aaron Rodgers, go back and look at CBS. I said he was arrogant. I said he was aloof, he was flippant, he was condescending.”

“I said he lacked leadership.

“That is why he is a four-time MVP and only went to one Super Bowl, because he is not a leader of men. He’s always been selfish. If you notice, he’s never accepted responsibility.”

Sharpe very nearly made it personal at that point, before backtracking.

“Everybody that he’s fell out with from his own family. But I ain’t gonna talk about his family. But I’m not gonna talk about his family because it seems like he’s trying to reconnect and I commend him for that.”

“But what’d they say at the Jets, they got his ass up out the building.”

The three-time Super Bowl champion tight end then made eyebrow-raising comparisons to other legendary quarterbacks.

“I’ve been calling him a terrible leader, but y’all didn’t want to listen… This is why he couldn’t go to another team and do what Tom Brady did, which was lead a team to the Super Bowl. Go to a team and lead the team to the Super Bowl like Peyton Manning did.

“Does he have more God-given talent, one thousand, two hundred, and 12 percent. But does he have half the leader they ever was?”

Given Rodgers’ history throughout the league, it is incredibly difficult to argue with anything Sharpe said.

But he is far from alone among legendary and elite players to put ARod on blast.

Russell Wilson, a potential future Hall of Fame quarterback, joined the Thanksgiving Day pregame show on CBS and ripped Rodgers as well.

“The reality is Aaron Rodgers wanted to call the plays, right? And that’s the difference here…. The reality is the ultimatum was the ultimatum…. If not, I want my release, essentially. And so they made that decision…. It’s about Aaron Rodgers’s legacy.

“If I’m his family members, if I’m somebody close to him, I’m his significant other, whatever it may be: ‘Is this how you want to go out?’ After all the success he’s had, is this how you want to go out?… This is a disappointment.”

Rodgers will turn 43 years old next week. He is without a team, and likely staring at an unceremonious end to an illustrious career.

But that was how this story seemed destined to end.
