Chris ‘CT’ Tamburello’s estranged wife issues scathing response to reconciling as Phaedra Parks romance heats up

2026-09-07 18:06

Days after Chris “CT” Tamburello and Phaedra Parks announced their relationship, the “Challenge” alum’s estranged wife posted a pointed message that she has no interest in reconciling with him.

Catalina White clarified to her Instagram followers Sunday whether she’d rather “jump off a cliff or get back with [her] ex.”

The OnlyFans creator posted this hypothetical on her Story alongside a video of a woman bungee jumping and an “OK” emoji.

White, 40, notably, went public with her and Tamburello’s romance in 2024 — and married the reality star that October.

She filed to divorce him in January 2026.

While the duo were “negotiating a marital settlement agreement” in March and expected a “settlement [to be] imminent,” per court docs obtained by People, they have yet to do so.

When Tamburello, 46, debuted a new romance with his “Traitors” co-star Phaedra Parks last week, White urged her ex to “sign the papers” in an Instagram comment.

She went on to tell TMZ on Monday that he has allegedly been delaying their divorce.

“What I would like is for him to put the same energy into ending our marriage that he’s putting into publicly promoting his new relationship,” White told the outlet.

“I’ve been trying to finalize this divorce,” she claimed. “My counsel has made repeated efforts to move things forward as quickly as possible, and we continue to wait for his cooperation.

“I do not — and never did — need or want money from this man,” the former WWE star continued. “I’m not asking for anything from him other than his cooperation in finalizing our divorce. If Chris is ready to publicly move on, he should be ready to legally move on too.”

White clarified she is “not interested in commenting on whether his new relationship is real, for publicity, or anything in between.”

She “genuinely wish[es] Chris well in whatever relationship or career he chooses to pursue.”

Tamburello’s rep did not immediately respond to Page Six’s request for comment.

The “Real World” alum and Parks, 52, made headlines Friday for confirming their romance, three years after they filmed “Traitors” together.

“He made it clear to her he really, really liked her and he fell for her when they were on ‘Traitors,’” a source told Page Six. “He makes her really happy. They really do laugh together a lot and their relationship is really sweet.”
