Why is ‘The View’ Showing Reruns? When Will ‘The View’ Return Live?

2026-09-02 15:00

It’s almost time for new episodes of The View!

It’s been a while since we’ve gotten new installments of the hit ABC daytime talk show, which is co-hosted by none other than Whoopi Goldberg, Joy Behar, Sara Haines, Ana Navarro, Sunny Hostin, and Alyssa Farah Griffin.

Since Season 29 of The View wrapped on July 24, reruns of The View have been airing in its usual time slot. However, fans shouldn’t fret. Like many other talk shows, The View has historically taken a summer hiatus in between seasons.

So, when is The View back? When will The View return with new shows? Here is what we know.

When Does The New Season of The View Start? The View Season 30 Start Date:

The View Season 30 premieres on premieres on Tuesday, Sept. 8 on ABC. People unveiled The View‘s promotional video for the new season, which teases that viewers are “about to be surprised” by who will be stopping by during The View‘s Season 30 premiere week.

The View Guests This Week:

Like we mentioned before, The View is currently airing reruns. For those still tuning in every day, here is the episode lineup for the remainder of the week:

Wednesday, Sept. 2 (OAD: 6/9/2026) — Sheryl Underwood guest co-hosts; Asif Ali, Saagar Shaikh and Poorna Jagannathan (actors, “Deli Boys”); Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis (songwriting and record production team, “Nothing But Hits” Las Vegas Residency at Voltaire in the Venetian)

Thursday, Sept. 3 (OAD: 6/11/26 ) — Sophia Bush Guest co-hosts; Emily Blunt (actor, “Disclosure Day”)

Friday, Sept. 4 (OAD: 6/19/26) — Anthony Anderson and Cedric The Entertainer (authors, “AC Barbeque: The Husky and Handsome Guide to Grilling”)

The View airs on weekdays at 11/10c on ABC, with streaming available on Hulu and Disney+.
