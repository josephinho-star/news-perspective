Lakers’ Ziaire Williams proposes to girlfriend at SoFi Stadium during Bruno Mars residency

2026-10-03 19:59

Ziarie Williams has yet to play a single minute in a Los Angeles Lakers uniform but has already started to make memories he’ll never forget.

While Mars was playing his hit song, “Marry You,” during the second night of his five-night residency at SoFi Stadium, Williams went for a buzzer-beater shot and hit nothing but net.

With the confetti falling from the ceiling of the stadium, Williams picked the perfect moment to get down on one knee and propose to his then-girlfriend, now fiancée, Paris Timan.

The moment was shared across the jumbotron at the stadium, while the two were seen jumping up and down, screaming in excitement.

After the engagement happened, SoFi Stadium shared a video and tagged both of them, with the caption, “It really was a beautiful night 🥹.”

Timan who is known for her work as a fashion and commercial model, has been with Williams for at least a few years now.

The newly engaged couple are about to embark on a new journey together, as Williams signed a one-year $15 million deal with the Lakers over the offseason.

Williams was once a Top 10 pick in the NBA out of Stanford, after he was drafted by the New Orleans Pelicans and had his draft rights traded to the Memphis Grizzlies.

Williams spent three years in Memphis until he was traded to the Brooklyn Nets in 2024.

Last season, he only appeared in 13 games after missing most of the season due to a small back fracture and lower back contusion from a hard fall in October.
