Blue Jays to sign former Yankees All-Star Jazz Chisholm Jr to $20 million contract: report

2026-11-27 23:30

The Toronto Blue Jays will reportedly be hearing some sweet tunes next season.

The Blue Jays agreed to a deal with two-time All-Star second baseman Jazz Chisholm Jr., according to multiple reports. The contract is a one-year, $20 million deal with a 2028 player option.

The 28-year-old has a $6 million bonus, a $12.5 million base salary and a $1.5 million buyout on the player option in 2028. If Chisholm declines his $16 million player option for 2028, the Blue Jays are then given a $40 million club option on the second baseman.

If Chisholm has a fantastic 2027 season and decides to decline his player option and the Blue Jays pick up the club option to prevent him from hitting free agency in 2028, the Bahamas native will earn $58.5 million over two seasons.

Chisholm is coming off an uneven 2½-year stint with the New York Yankees. The Bronx Bombers acquired him at the 2024 trade deadline from the Miami Marlins, and Chisholm was a key part of propelling the Yankees to an American League pennant.

In his first 46 games with the Yankees in 2024, he played third base and hit .273 with 11 home runs, 23 RBIs and 18 stolen bases. Chisholm continued his strong play in 2025, this time playing second base, hitting .242 with 31 home runs, 80 RBIs and 31 stolen bases. He made his second All-Star team, won a Silver Slugger and became just the third player in Yankees history to have 30 home runs and 30 stolen bases in a season.

However, in 2026, Chisholm regressed, hitting .231 with 19 home runs, 51 RBIs and 41 stolen bases, with his OPS dipping over 100 points from 2025. He also made headlines for eating a lollipop during a game.

Following the down season, the Yankees declined to to give Chisholm the qualifying offer.

Chisholm's blend of power and speed is rare, and the Blue Jays are hoping to tap into that to add a dynamic bat to their offense. With Ernie Clement at second base, it isn't clear where manager John Schneider will deploy Chisholm.
