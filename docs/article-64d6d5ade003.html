‘Chad Powers’ Season 2 Release Date: When Does ‘Chad Powers’ Season 2 Come Out?

2026-08-31 14:45

It’s been nearly a year since Hulu‘s hit football series, Chad Powers, aired its eventful Season 1 finale. And fans are finally about to learn the fate of the South Georgia Catfish and star quarterback Russ Holliday/Chad Powers (Glen Powell).

Inspired by one of Eli Manning’s Eli’s Places segments on ESPN, Chad Powers Season 1 saw disgraced quarterback Russ Holliday pull a Mrs. Doubtfire, slap on a wig and facial prosthetics, and attempt to reclaim a college football career as wholesome player Chad Powers.

The Season 1 finale left viewers on an agonizing cliffhanger, with Chad and his crush, Coach Ricky (Perry Mattfeld), at odds after she learned his big secret. The Catfish had a make-or-break game to play. So do they win at Sanford Stadium? Do Russ/Chad and Ricky sort things out? Do Coach Hudson (Steve Zahn) and the rest of the world learn the truth about South Georgia’s star QB? And can Russ redeem himself in the end? Season 2 has answers!

Was Chad Powers renewed for Season 2? And if so, when does Chad Powers Season 2 come out? How many episodes are in Chad Powers Season 2? What will Chad Powers Season 2 be about? And where can you watch the sports comedy? Here’s what to know about the return of Chad Powers.

Will There Be A Season 2 of Chad Powers on Hulu? Chad Powers Season 2 Renewal Status:

You bet! On December 3, 2025, Hulu officially renewed Chad Powers for a second season.

When Does Chad Powers Season 2 Come Out? Chad Powers Season 2 Release Date Info:

Chad Powers Season 2 comes out Thursday, September 3 on Hulu.

How Many Episodes Are In Chad Powers Season 2?

Like Season 1, Chad Powers Season 2 has six episodes. But this time, all six episodes drop on Hulu at once — a shift from Season 1’s weekly release schedule.

What Will Chad Powers Season 2 Be About?

Naturally, Chad Powers Season 2 will continue to follow Russ/Chad’s football career as he maintains his elaborate lie. We’ll learn the result of the big Season 1 finale game, see how Coach Hudson recovers from his medical scare, and crucially, see how Ricky and Chad’s dynamic shifts following the Season 1 finale epiphany.

“…What’s clear is that whether she likes it or not, Ricky has been pulled into this conspiracy,” co-creator Michael Waldron told Decider after Season 1. “So it’s her, Russ, and Danny pulling off Chad Powers. And that’s a whole new, really fun dynamic. It’s gonna be a blast.”

Want more Chad Powers Season 2 info? Hulu’s official synopsis is as follows: “Chad Powers is a superstar on the field, but his odd behavior off the field has everyone suspicious — especially Coach Hudson and Gerry. Ricky knows the truth, but if Russ and Danny want Chad to survive the regular season and lead the Catfish to the college football playoff, the only person who can help them is the last person who wants to: Ricky.”
