What Channel Is The Patriots-Seahawks Game on Tonight? Where To Watch For Free

2026-09-09 15:00

Here we go! After weeks of preseason action, it’s finally time for the start of the 2026-27 NFL regular season. Live from Lumen Field in Seattle, the Seahawks host the New England Patriots in a Super Bowl LX rematch!

Week 1 is going to be a ton of fun. After the Pats/Hawks game, the Los Angeles Rams and San Francisco 49ers collide Thursday night on Netflix in the first-ever regular-season NFL game from Australia. Sunday’s action is headlined by the Houston Texans hosting the Buffalo Bills, with Sunday Night Football featuring an NFC East showdown as the New York Giants host the Dallas Cowboys.

The first week of games concludes with the Denver Broncos going on the road to battle the Kansas City Chiefs on Monday Night Football (8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN and ABC).

From start time to channel to complete streaming info, here’s how to watch the Patriots/Seahawks game for free.

What Time/Channel Is The Patriots-Seahawks Game on Tonight?

The Patriots/Seahawks game starts at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock. Pregame coverage begins at 7:00 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock.

Patriots vs. Seahawks Live Stream Options:

If you have a valid cable login, you can watch the Seahawks/Pats game live on NBC or the NBC website/app.

The only way to watch shows airing live without cable is by using a live TV streaming service. Our favorite is DIRECTV, which comes with a five-day free trial before payment begins.

DIRECTV offers a number of flexible subscription options, including Genre Packs, so you can best choose which streaming plans fit your needs and budget. Packs start at $19.99/month.

You can also watch the game live with an active subscription to fuboTV, Hulu + Live TV, YouTube TV, NFL+ (via your phone or tablet), or Sling TV. YouTube TV, fuboTV, DIRECTV, and Hulu + Live TV offer free trials for new subscribers.

How To Watch Patriots vs. Seahawks For Free on Peacock:

Tonight’s game is also streaming on Peacock.

Peacock currently offers two subscription types: Premium with ads and Premium Plus ad-free. Peacock Premium costs $12.99/month, while Premium Plus costs $19.99/month.

You can save a bit by subscribing to one of Peacock’s annual plans, which give you 12 months for the price of 10. These cost either $129.99 with ads or $199.99 without ads.

Peacock Premium Plus is also available to subscribe to via Prime Video with a seven-day free trial that you can’t get by subscribing directly on Peacock.

You can also bundle Peacock and Apple TV, with plans starting at $14.99, or purchase Peacock through YouTube for $19.99/month.

Does Peacock Offer a Free Trial?

Peacock doesn’t directly offer a free trial, but as we mentioned above, you can score a free trial of the streamer through Prime Video. Finally, you can also get a free subscription to Peacock through Instacart+, which offers a free trial.
