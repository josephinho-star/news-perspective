TCU's most famous cheerleader on the sideline as 'Landman' filmed during Horned Frogs win

2026-11-15 17:36

When the TCU Horned Frogs took the field at home on Saturday to face the Kansas State Wildcats, they did so with some extra support in attendance at Amon G. Carter Stadium. Including their most famous cheerleader, Ainsley Norris.

Ainsley, a character played by actress Michelle Randolph, was on the sideline during TCU's 20-10 win as the hit show "Landman" filmed for its upcoming third season. Ali Larter, who plays her mom Angela Norris on the show, was also spotted at the game.

During season two of the Paramount+ series, Ainsley enrolled at TCU and became a member of the cheerleading squad.

There was a hint a few weeks ago that her cheerleading was going to remain part of the upcoming season's storyline. And why wouldn’t it?

It was reported that "Landman" was filming at TCU in Fort Worth and that around 30 TCU cheerleaders were expected to be involved in the production in some way.

Who knew they were going to show up at an actual TCU football game? Well, they did and Randolph and Larter weren't the only ones who were in attendance.

Stefania Spampinato, who plays Bella Morrell on the show, was seen filming a scene alongside Larter during the game as well. There was no chance the Horned Frogs were going to lose this game with that much firepower in attendance.

Another of TCU's special guests on Saturday was actor Glen Powell. He was there on the sideline apparently supporting his girlfriend Michelle Randolph and stacking the odds even more against Kansas State.

A tough way to spend a few hours on a Saturday.

Paramount+ has not yet announced the premiere date for Season 3 of "Landman." However, the earliest it's expected to return is the middle of 2027.
