The AI frenzy has pushed SK Hynix’s U.S. shares to a premium. One hedge fund is betting on its unravelling

2026-11-20 10:01

SK Hynix 's U.S.-listed shares trade at a hefty premium to their equivalent securities in Seoul and Frankfurt — and one hedge fund manager says the price difference between the two offers investors a "highly tradable" alpha opportunity. Tej Johar, portfolio manager at Trium Capital, has built what he sees as a "simple" bet on a sharp narrowing of that premium. SKHY 1M mountain SK Hynix U.S.-listed shares. Speaking at this year's Sohn London investment conference on Wednesday, Johar said the idea — dubbed a relative value arbitrage trade in hedge fund parlance — is "very timely" as the artificial intelligence trade continues to shape markets globally. "It's a really simple trade — buy the cheap one, short the expensive one," he said. SK Hynix, which is valued at about $650 billion, was listed on the Nasdaq in July , via ADRs, or American Depositary Receipts. Since then, the U.S. shares' premium over Seoul-traded shares and Frankfurt-traded global depositary receipts has widened from 13% to about 48%, Johar said. Soaring appetite among U.S. investors for exposure to AI companies has driven the premium higher, according to Johar, while investors elsewhere have not paid the same markup. He said the gap could narrow sharply, partly because of the "shaky" AI trade and partly if large institutional shareholders opt to switch to the cheaper securities. "I think this is the biggest one — Holders can switch, and arguably, we think they have a fiduciary duty to do so. Paying 48% more for the same share is incredibly hard to justify to clients, to risk committees, frankly, to anyone who's ever been in the investment business." He said Seoul- and Frankfurt-listed securities in the South Korean chipmaker closed at about $90 on Nov. 13, while comparable shares traded at $132 in the U.S. HY9H-FF 1M mountain SK Hynix Frankfurt-listed shares. He added that the typical ADR premium over the last 25 years is "about zero." SK Hynix is an "extra special case" at 48%. "This is going to catch up. In the past, ADR gaps like this have closed very quickly." "The best trades are very simple trades. They have the fewest moving parts... and we think that trade today is very scalable. So it's about an $8 billion alpha opportunity. We think it's highly tradable," he added. "We run a lot of statistical analysis going back 20-30 years. We think there's a very high likelihood that you're going to close this gap. As a hedge fund, you always want to take these trades on, size correctly, and, if the past is any guide, you're going to see that three shares will trade at one price in the near future."
