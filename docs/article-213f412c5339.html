Christian McCaffrey’s wife Olivia Culpo shows daughter’s unimpressed reaction to historic touchdown

2026-09-21 02:15

San Francisco 49ers superstar running back Christian McCaffrey had a historic day Sunday — but one member of his family didn’t appear too impressed.

McCaffrey had 10 carries for 23 rushing yards and two touchdowns to go along with four catches for 43 receiving yards in the 49ers’ 35–13 win against the Miami Dolphins.

Those two rushing touchdowns were the 99th and 100th of McCaffrey’s career, which is a massive milestone for the 30-year-old.

McCaffrey’s wife, Olivia Culpo, is currently pregnant with the couple’s second child, and therefore watched Sunday’s game at home with their first daughter, Colette.

Culpo posted an Instagram video of the TV replay when McCaffrey scored his historic second touchdown. She then panned the camera to Colette, who didn’t look a bit impressed, and ran the other way.

“Well one of us cares about a touchdown @christianmccaffrey 😝,” Culpo captioned the video.

Naturally, Colette doesn’t know what she’s watching, what with being only a little over a year old at this point. But she’ll be celebrating McCaffrey’s touchdowns soon enough.

And she’ll have a sibling to do so with in the near future, as Culpo is due to have a second child this fall.
