Minnesota State Fairgoer savagely beaten, stomped in teen mob attack

2026-09-11 05:11

A Minnesota State Fairgoer was savagely attacked by a group of “young men” who fractured his jaw in multiple places — turning his night out with friends into a “hellish nightmare,” his fianceé claimed.

Sammy Kimbrough, 45, was left in critical condition and needed emergency reconstructive surgery Saturday night after the brutal assault that knocked him unconscious and left him bloodied at the annual event in St. Paul, Minn.

“Last night, Sammy was brutally assaulted by a group of young men in a completely senseless act of violence,” Kimbrough’s fianceé Heather Hoffman wrote on Facebook Sunday.

“Anyone who knows Sammy knows that he is one of the most laid-back, kind-hearted, and non-confrontational men you could ever meet. This has been a lot to wrap our heads around,” Hoffman added.

Kimbrough attended the closing weekend of the state fair, which is billed as the largest in Minnesota, with friends and his soon-to-be-bride when he was attacked.

Kimbrough and two others split from the main group and were exploring the 322-acre fairground when he accidentally bumped into another person who was with a group of eight to 12 people.

“This young man ended up getting into a verbal conversation with Sammy, becoming aggressive,” Hoffman told St. Paul Pioneer Press. “Sammy had tried to say, ‘Excuse me,’ ‘Back up,’ when he then got punched.”

Kimbrough was knocked down by another person who threw a punch before more people in the group began “stomping his face into the pavement,” Hoffman said.

“What should have been an insignificant moment escalated into something unimaginable. The group attacked him, knocking him unconscious and kicking him in the face after he had fallen,” family friend Kira Roland wrote on GoFundMe.

Kimbrough was knocked out and bleeding from the mouth, suffering a “significant fracture” to the right side of his jaw and several smaller fractures to the front left side, Roland added.

He was rushed to Regions Hospital in St. Paul and underwent emergency surgery, during which doctors placed plates and screws into his jaw.

Kimbrough was hospitalized for two days and discharged on Tuesday.

The injuries were so severe, surgeons had to bandage his mouth shut and add bands to hold the jaw in place.

“Once that fracture heals enough for him to open his mouth, he then will have very extensive dental surgery as he has a whole lot of broken, knocked out and loose teeth in his mouth because of this,” Hoffman told the outlet.

Kimbrough said the surgery went well and was ready for the next step.

“It’ll be a long recovery!” he wrote on Facebook. “I’ll be back to normal in no time!”

State Fair police confirmed the alleged assault took place Saturday night, but no arrests have been made, CBS News Minnesota reported.

“As of right now, the people responsible have not been caught. When they are identified, we will absolutely be pursuing charges to the fullest extent of the law,” Hoffman said. “There is no excuse for this kind of violence, those responsible will be held accountable for what they have done.”

The fair ran from Aug. 27 to Labor Day and was open daily until 11 p.m.
