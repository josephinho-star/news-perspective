Kimberly Guilfoyle shared X-rated photos, ‘cavalierly’ talked about sex with Fox News friends, ex-host claims

2026-10-16 01:52

Ousted ambassador Kimberly Guilfoyle openly bragged about her sex life while at Fox News and proudly flashed her collection of X-rated photos paired with her conquest’s credit card, a former colleague claims.

Lisa “Kennedy” Montgomery initially bonded with the embattled ex-diplomat over their “shared disdain” for California Gov. Gavin Newsom — Guilfoyle’s ex-husband — but became “less comfortable” the more they became acquainted, the Fox News contributor wrote in the Daily Mail Thursday.

At one point, Guilfoyle flaunted her relationship with a man she referred to as “Lex Luthor” and described as a “lovesick cash cow,” according to Kennedy, who overlapped with her at the network.

“Kimberly showed me and several of our friends pictures of his Amex black card – front and back,” the former MTV VJ wrote. “The images were matched with a corresponding picture of the card holder’s private parts.

“There were at least three sets of these credit card-penis pairings.”

Kennedy revealed that Guilfoyle’s alleged sexual escapades earned her cryptic nicknames – “Seabiscuit” and “USO” – inside the network.

“In fact, Kimberly talked about sex so cavalierly that she made Samantha from Sex and the City seem like Greta Thunberg,” she quipped.

The former Fox Business host described Guilfoyle as both “insanely charming” and a “status-chasing monster” afflicted by an “unrelenting narcissism.”

In 2020, the New Yorker reported that Guilfoyle engaged in “graphic sexual talk” and “often shared lewd images,” including “photographs of male genitalia” while at Fox News.

“I have never engaged in any workplace misconduct of any kind,” Guilfoyle told the publication at the time, denying the allegations.

Guilfoyle and a lawyer for the former US ambassador to Greece did not immediately respond to The Post’s request for comment.

Earlier this week, Guilfoyle resigned from her posi in the wake of an allegation that she pressed a Trump donor to pay off her personal credit card.

The Wall Street Journal reported last week that Guilfoyle asked former GOP congressional candidate Eric Deters for $100,000 to pay off her American Express card ahead of her confirmation hearing for the ambassadorship.

Guilfoyle denied wrongdoing in her farewell statement.

“These allegations are false, and I intend to vigorously defend my name, my reputation, and my integrity,” Guilfoyle wrote. “I have dedicated my professional life to fighting for justice, standing up for what is right, and defending the values that make America exceptional.

“I will not allow false accusations to define my decades of public service.”
