Jeff Bezos says he was 'heavily involved' in picking Robert Pattinson as James Bond

2026-11-19 01:30

When it came to naming Robert Pattinson the next James Bond, Jeff Bezos says he was "heavily involved" in the selection process.

During an appearance at Semafor’s Silicon Valley & The World conference Tuesday, the Amazon founder opened up about the casting process and said the team initially considered choosing a lesser-known actor.

"Yes, I was heavily involved. We ran a really tight process, and we made the final decision on Friday. We down selected. There were a big group initially. We down selected to five finalists," Bezos said, according to The Associated Press.

"And then we did real, old-fashioned screen tests of those five finalists, and it was — it was really interesting, you know? Full costume, real sets, other actors with them. We did three different scenes with each of the five finalists. And then we got together on Friday to spend a couple of hours looking at all those screen tests and reviewing all that."

While Bezos said all the other actors were "extraordinarily talented," there was something about Pattinson.

"By the time we got to this list of five, they were all amazing. But there was something special about Rob and his performances," Bezos said.

"And it was kind of immediately undebated. Denis, the director, we all sort of gravitated. ... By the way, initially, I would say we were slightly biased a little against him just because he’s already so famous. We sort of thought maybe we should have somebody a little more unknown. But in the screening test, it was so obvious. He is James Bond."

The news of Pattinson's casting was announced Monday.

"James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime," Pattinson said in a statement. "I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team."

"I think we are going to be blown away," Brosnan recently told Vanity Fair.
