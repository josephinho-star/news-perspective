Darius Rucker reveals A-lister who saved him from drowning on vacation: ‘Not on my watch’

2026-11-17 15:25

Darius Rucker revealed longtime pal Woody Harrelson saved him from drowning when they were on vacation together in Hawaii in the 1990s.

“I called Woody and he was going to Hawaii to his place and he says, ‘Man, come spend a couple of weeks with me in Hawaii,’” the “Wagon Wheel” singer recalled in his new Hulu documentary, “Darius Rucker: They Called Me Hootie.”

“I was like, ‘Oh, absolutely.’”

Rucker, 60, called the trip “one of the best two weeks of my life,” saying that he “had a blast” with the “Cheers” actor — until they decided to walk down a cliff to a nearby island one day.

“As we’re walking down, [Harrelson] says, ‘Can you swim?’ And I’m like, ‘Yeah,’” Rucker explained. “He tells me, ‘Jump in there, and when you dive out, don’t stay in the water long, come straight up. If you wait at all, you’re gonna get caught in this current and you’re not gonna get out.’”

Rucker jumped into the water, but realized there was no beach and got “caught in the current.”

“The water was a little rugged that day,” Harrelson, 65, remembered. “Probably shouldn’t have gone in.”

Rucker recalled the “Hunger Games” actor swimming toward him as he was drowning, saying, “It seemed like forever that we were out there trying to get out.”

Rucker admitted he felt “really exhausted” in the moment and thought he was going to die, adding, “I had gotten myself to a peace. I accepted that God had given me this amazing life, exactly what I’d asked for, and if it had to end right then, when I got to Heaven I was just gonna say thank you.”

However, Harrelson “giggled” at Rucker and told him, “Not on my watch.”

“I wasn’t going to let that happen,” the “True Detective” star said with a smile in the doc.

Calling the moment Harrelson got him out of the water “surreal,” Rucker added, “Woody Harrelson had really, literally just saved my life.”

Harrelson said Rucker was “very humble” after the singer’s “evolutionary” near-death experience.

“That would’ve been the end of my career, at least, if I’d just been the guy who killed Darius Rucker,” Harrelson reflected with a laugh. “My life would’ve gone on, but I… ‘Oh, there’s the guy! He’s the one, you know?’ That wouldn’t have been fun.”

During a 2024 appearance on “Today,” Rucker revealed that while he’s seen Harrelson “a million times” since the Hawaii trip, they’ve “never talked about” the incident.

“He’s so humble,” Rucker stated of Harrelson. “He wouldn’t want to talk about it.”

“Darius Rucker: They Called Me Hootie” is streaming on Hulu and Disney+.
