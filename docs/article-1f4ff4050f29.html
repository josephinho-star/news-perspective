’90 Day Fiancé’: Mido gives Debby a brutal rating after they have sex for the first time

2026-09-07 02:00

Still, Debby is glowing after her night with Mido — whom she hadn’t slept with after six years of dating due to his Muslim faith — telling cameras that he “did good” and was “a natural” at having sex.

While Mido, 41, smiles, he’s also not nearly as happy as Debby, 55. When she kisses him all over his face as they lie in bed together, he tells her, “Enough kisses.”

Debby laughs it off and the two giggle as they talk about sleeping together for the first time.

“I’m a professional, of course I know my way around,” Mido brags to the cameras after Debby notes he “knows where to go.”

He does tell his new bride that it was worth the wait, but then when asked by a producer how he would rate the experience overall, he brutally replies, “so-so.”

Later, Debby pressures Mido to finally post her on his social media now that they’re married, which he obviously doesn’t want to do.

“Mido has never posted a picture of me on social media because he says that his social media is for his his acting work,” Debby tells cameras. “I think that’s kind of baloney because it’s not like he hasn’t posted other women on his pages.”

She adds, “It bothers me, I don’t like it.”

While Debby says it’s not her job to tell him what to post on social media, she points out that he did tell her that he would post a picture of them on his social media once they were married.

But Mido still attempts to stall — even claiming that the WiFi signal isn’t working — until Debby demands that he give her his phone. Eventually, he does post a pic of the two from their wedding day and Debby says she’s “happy.”

“I’ve waited six long years,” she notes.
