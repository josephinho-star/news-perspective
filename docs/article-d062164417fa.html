XVII club owner seeks to recapture the magic of 1OAK after taking over Chelsea space

2026-11-27 15:31

Lucciano Grey has been operating XVII, a pop-up club in the old 1OAK space, and sources tell Page Six he’s aiming to recreate “the old-school vibe” of the original 1OAK.

“The goal is to use the venue and the history behind it to reactivate Chelsea and the Meatpacking District with that old-school vibe of 1OAK under completely new rebranding,” an insider told Page Six.

The club started hosting select pop-up parties in September, including a recent album release party for rapper Offset, which brought out some of Akiva’s “minions,” according to the source.

“Richie wasn’t in attendance, but many of his friends were, and a lot of familiar faces from the old 1OAK era,” the source said.

“They weren’t partying. They weren’t having a good time. They were just there to see what’s going on,” the insider claimed.

We hear the club is planning a big post-Thanksgiving weekend to celebrate the NBA Cup. Grey told us he and his partners are anxious to fill the void on West 17th Street after COVID forced 1OAK to shutter in 2022. “We’re giving downtown something it’s been asking for for a long time,” he said.

“This room has energy you can’t just build from scratch. So many great nights happened here, and so much came out of it. Bringing it back to life is something truly special,” he added.

Grey further said, “XVII isn’t about recreating the past. It’s about giving this room its next chapter. We’re just getting started.”

XVII has even got a co-sign from Akiva, who recently hosted his birthday party and a 1OAK pop-up at the venue. Still, nothing can compete with the original — even with the founder’s stamp of approval. “Everyone will realize it’s not 1OAK when I open 1OAK,” Akiva teased.

In the interim, Akiva is happy to inspire. “Imitation is a form of flattery,” he said.
