As a beauty reporter, I’ve never seen Medicube 67% off — but it is ahead of Prime Day

2026-09-21 14:30

I’ve covered K-beauty for years, and I’ve never seen Medicube’s One Day Exosome Shot 2000 67% off.

I’ve reviewed the product — and nearly all of Medicube — in both South Korea and the U.S., and the brand reigns supreme as one of the most effective K-beauty names for achieving a refined, dull-reversing glow. So, seeing one of its most talked-about treatment products at such a steep discount ahead of Prime Day (which is returning on Oct. 6, BTW) immediately caught my attention.

The Medicube One Day Exosome Shot 2000 targets the skin concerns I hear about constantly: visible pores, uneven texture and a complexion that has lost its smoothness and brightness. The formula uses Medicube’s spicule technology, which creates the prickly, tingling sensation that has become one of the product’s defining features.

I’ve tested plenty of products that promise smoother, brighter skin, and Medicube consistently stands out for the visible difference it can make in how polished my complexion looks. The brand has built a following around products that target texture, pores and dullness without making skincare feel overly complicated.

The One Day Exosome Shot 2000 is designed as an at-home treatment for improving the look of skin texture and pores. It’s not a substitute for professional microneedling, despite the comparisons that sometimes surround spicule-based skincare. Instead, it offers a more intensive step than a typical serum or essence, with the spicules creating a noticeable sensation as you apply it.

The formula also contains ingredients including niacinamide, panthenol and adenosine, along with lactobacillus extracellular vesicles. Together, the ingredients are aimed at supporting a smoother, brighter-looking complexion while the spicule technology gives the product its treatment-focused edge.

The sensation is the biggest consideration. This is not a barely-there serum that disappears onto the skin without you noticing. The spicules can feel prickly, so I would be more cautious about using it on particularly sensitive or already irritated skin. However, it’s great on my sensitive skin, and I use it about once a week at most for a facial-esque treat-myself moment.

At 67% off, the One Day Exosome Shot 2000 is now considerably more tempting to try, particularly because this is one of the best early Prime Day beauty deals I’ve seen.

Medicube has earned its reputation in my skincare routine through products that deliver the refined, luminous finish I associate with the best of K-beauty. With the One Day Exosome Shot 2000 currently marked down this heavily, it is a standout opportunity to try one of the brand’s more intensive approaches to smoother, brighter-looking skin.

Your Prime Big Deal Days FAQs, answered

What is Prime Big Deal Days and when is it?

Prime Big Deal Days — fondly known as October Prime Day — is Amazon’s biggest fall sale, offering massive discounts before the holiday rush of Black Friday and Cyber Monday. October Prime Day runs from Tuesday, Oct. 6 to Wednesday, Oct. 7, 2026.

Prime Day vs. Prime Big Deal Days

Prime Day and Prime Big Deal Days are both Prime member-exclusive seasonal sales with massive discounts on rotating bestsellers. Unlike July’s Prime Day, Prime Big Deal Days runs every October as a precursor to the busy holiday season, allowing shoppers to start saving on gift shopping early.

Do you have to be an Amazon Prime member to shop?

October Prime Day is exclusively available to Prime members. However interested shoppers can grab a 30-day free trial to try Prime, and its many benefits with no strings attached, and gain access to exclusive deals and the convenience of fast, free shipping.
