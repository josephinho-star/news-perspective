Brittany Mahomes announces baby No. 4 as Patrick Mahomes, Chiefs gear up for AFC push

2026-11-11 02:51

The Mahomes household is growing again, and Chiefs Kingdom is officially back on baby watch.

Brittany hit Instagram on Tuesday to drop the news with beachside photos and a short caption: "Another blessing from God & then there were 6."

Baby No. 4 joins an established crew at home with Sterling Skye, Bronze and Golden Raye.

Sterling arrived in February 2021, followed by Patrick "Bronze" Lavon III in November 2022, and Golden Raye in January 2025 during the Chiefs' playoff bye week.

Speculation is already mounting over whether the fourth child keeps the precious-metals naming streak alive.

While most comments congratulated the couple, some couldn't help but chime in from a football perspective.

Some of those reactions from fans read:

Meanwhile, Chiefs general manager Brett Veach beat the 4 p.m. trade deadline cutoff to hand Patrick a fresh target in slot receiver Josh Downs. The Chiefs enter the second half of the 2026 season firmly in the AFC hunt.

In Patrick's 10th NFL season, Kansas City has had to navigate a tight divisional race alongside a stubborn Denver Broncos squad.

After a brief road skid following their early bye, Patrick answered in Week 9 with a 35-12 rout of the Jets at Arrowhead, lifting Kansas City to 6-2. Patrick gave Chiefs Kingdom a legitimate scare when he hit the turf holding his right elbow before halftime, but disaster was averted when tests showed no structural damage.

With the offense adding pass-catching depth and the family officially on the clock for baby No. 4, Patrick has a busy stretch run ahead.
