Five earthquakes rattle southern Turkey, no injuries reported

2026-10-04 20:16

Five shallow earthquakes rattled southern Turkey on Sunday, with tremors also felt in northern Syria, but there were no reports of injuries or damage, authorities on both sides said.

Three of the earthquakes struck in the early afternoon and two more in the evening, Turkey’s Disaster and Emergency Management Authority (AFAD) said on X, adding that they had caused “no adverse situation.”

The area is about 150 kilometers (90 miles) from the epicenter of the massive February 2023 earthquakes, which killed at least 53,000 people in Turkey and around 6,000 in Syria.

Four of Sunday’s earthquakes struck the Saimbeyli district of Adana province: a magnitude 4.8 quake at 2:19 p.m. (1119 GMT), followed by a 4.9-magnitude quake at 2:36 p.m., a 4.4-magnitude quake at 7 p.m. and a 4.3-magnitude quake at 8:28 p.m., AFAD said.

A magnitude 5.2 earthquake also struck the Sumbas district, about 80 kilometers farther south, at 2:43 p.m.

Syria’s National Earthquake Center said on Telegram that all five earthquakes had been felt in parts of northern Syria, without reporting any injuries or damage.

Turkey is crossed by several major fault lines and has suffered a number of devastating earthquakes.
