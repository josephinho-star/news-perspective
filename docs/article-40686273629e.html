Millie Bobby Brown shares rare glimpse of her and Jake Bongiovi’s baby girl on family trip to Italy

2026-09-07 17:17

Millie Bobby Brown shared a rare glimpse of family life while recapping her and husband Jake Bongiovi’s Italian getaway with their baby daughter.

The “Stranger Things” star shared a carousel of photos and videos via Instagram Monday. The first slide was an adorable video of Brown carrying their little one as she and Bongiovi walked alongside each other with llamas.

Neither Brown or Bongiovi have yet to show their daughter’s face, but in the video fans can see her wearing a white onesie with hair styles in pigtails.

In other snaps, the actress showed off her bikini body and the other excursions they went on.

Last month, Page Six obtained photos of the family of three boarding a water taxi with their 1-year-old in Lake Como.

In the snaps, Brown — sporting a red two-piece set — held her baby, who wore a white floral dress.

“22. grateful for my husband and daughter. for my family and friends. all of my animals. I am so blessed. thank you for the birthday wishes. xoxo,” she captioned a sweet photo of her and her daughter spending quality time on the beach.

In the snap, her daughter bundled up in an adorable fuzzy onesie with a hood.

Fans have speculated, however, that she could be named Ruth after Brown’s grandmother, after the Emmy nominee wore a necklace bearing that nameplate in May.

The couple have also received backlash over starting a family at their age, though the “Enola Holmes” star has repeatedly defended their decision.

“This has been my thing since before I met Jake. … I wanted to be a [young] mom just like the way my mom was to me,” she told “SmartLess” podcast listeners in March 2025. “It’s so important to start a family for me personally.”

She also teased that she and the model plan to expand their family even more.

“I really want a big family. I’m one of four. He’s one of four. So, it is definitely in our future.”
