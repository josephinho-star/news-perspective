Taylor Swift’s ex Harry Styles makes cheeky comment about singer’s MSG wedding to Travis Kelce

2026-08-30 17:24

Harry Styles made a cheeky comment about his ex Taylor Swift’s wedding to Travis Kelce while taking the stage for his Madison Square Garden residency show.

Gigi Hadid, Selena Gomez, Bradley Cooper and Benny Blanco were among the A-list attendees spotted making their way to the iconic venue to support their friends.

While the couple had curtains set up to block onlookers from peeking inside the venue, they made sure to let fans know when the ceremony ended with digital signs outside MSG that read, “Just T&T Married!”

Comedian Adam Sandler officiated the ceremony, while Swift’s brother, Austin, served as her man of honor and Kelce’s brother, Jason, as his best man.

Styles’ fiancée, Zoë Kravitz, was also in attendance and appeared to have attended the festivities solo.

The “Watermelon Sugar” crooner, 32, and Swift first publicly dated in late 2012 and split a few months later in January 2013. A few years later, the “Cruel Summer” singer befriended Kravitz in 2016, and the besties quarantined together in 2020 in the UK.
