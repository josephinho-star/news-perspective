Mass layoffs rock California gaming giant as boss’ internal memo leaks

2026-10-21 19:03

A famed Silicon Valley gaming company is laying off hundreds of employees and shuttering prominent video game studio BioWare, according to an employee memo leaked Wednesday.

Electronic Arts, amid a business restructure, is laying off 3% of its global workforce, or about 400 jobs, according to Variety.

BioWare’s closing ends the gaming studio’s 31-year-run after it developed prominent gaming franchises in Mass Effect and Dragon Age.
