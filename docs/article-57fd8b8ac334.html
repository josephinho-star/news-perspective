Stephen A. Smith says Black NBA players have thanked him over white Lakers comments

2026-11-25 02:45

Stephen A. Smith raised some eyebrows last Wednesday when he called out the NBA for potentially letting the Lakers win a championship. A team that’s led by a trio of white stars in Luka Dončić, Austin Reaves and Walker Kessler.

The talk show personality faced a lot of scrutiny and criticism for his comments. He questioned Black NBA players for letting a group of “white brothers” “win a championship.”

But even with the scrutiny, he’s also received support.

The television personality revealed on his show, “Straight Shooter,” on Tuesday night that Black NBA players have reached out to him to thank him for his comments about the Lakers trio.

“You know how many brothers in the NBA reached out to me and thanked me for saying it?” Smith said.

When the Lakers trio was finished being assembled in July when Los Angeles acquired Kessler via a sign-and-trade deal with the Jazz, Smith questioned the team’s decision to form the trio and said, “Your three best players are white dudes. This ain’t golf. And we got a whole bunch of brothers on Team USA. This is basketball, what y’all think this is?”

The Lakers entered Tuesday night tied for the NBA’s best record with the Spurs (15-3) and have been led by Dončić, who’s an early MVP candidate.

Smith expressed his admiration for the trio by calling Dončić a “superstar,” Reaves “a baller,” and Kessler “no scrub,” but he doubled down on his comments and said, “The Lakers got a chance. I get it. Do I expect them to win? No.”

“I’m just saying to you. When I think of cats in the Eastern Conference and the Western Conference. If those are the three players you are riding with, when it really counts, I don’t think you’re winning a best-of-seven series and [you’re] winning a [championship],” Smith said. “I don’t think it’s happening.”

While Smith doesn’t believe the Lakers stand a chance to win an NBA title, former Celtics champion Kevin Garnett called out Smith on an episode of the “KG and Stephen A.” show and said, “They making you look crazy. You talking all that s–t about them white boys, saying they can’t win, and s–t.”
