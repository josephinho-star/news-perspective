‘Worth every penny’ — Key reason shoppers upgrade to $179 Apple Watch SE 3 for Black Friday

2026-11-28 00:26

If there’s one thing New York Post readers seem to agree on, it’s that the Apple Watch SE is a seriously smart buy during Black Friday.

The sleek, feature-packed Apple Watch is flying off the shelves at Amazon, and proving to be a hit with our savviest shoppers. It’s easy to see why: a stylish design, impressive functionality and, just in time for Black Friday 2026, a nearly 30% discount that brings the price to just under $180.

The Apple Watch SE also punches well above its price tag, offering advanced health and fitness tracking for everything from heart health insights to sleep apnea detection and real-time workout metrics. You’ll also get up to 18 hours of battery life, so you can bring it along for longer days running errands or traveling.

The appeal goes beyond fitness. The SE basically doubles as a personal assistant right on your wrist. Siri AI lets you stay on top of all of your tasks, ask everyday questions and receive notifications, so it’s easy to stay connected.

Shoppers say it’s “worth every penny” — and we can see why. It packs all the features you want into a bright, easy-to-use, simple package.

The SE 3 is an easy tech upgrade for folks with various goals — health or otherwise.

It’s lightweight, comfortable to wear and pre-loaded with plenty of health and fitness tracking features, making it a smart pick for fitness enthusiasts who want to optimize their routines without splurging on a bulkier, high-end watch.

You can also think of the SE 3 as an extension of your phone. You can send texts, take calls, stream music and podcasts, or ask Siri for help without ever reaching for your phone.

“The Apple Watch SE 3 delivers flagship features at an accessible price,” one impressed reviewer shared. “Whether you’re focused on health, fitness, staying connected, or safety, this watch does it all beautifully. It’s the perfect smartwatch for anyone who wants premium functionality without the premium price tag.”

That’s a whole lot of smartwatch for less — and a deal worth jumping on before Black Friday 2026 comes to an end.
