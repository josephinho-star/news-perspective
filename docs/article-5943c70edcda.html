You can’t miss cozy V-neck sweaters for winter: Shop affordable styles before Christmas outings

2026-11-02 20:37

V-neck sweaters are the perfect transitional piece to take you from fall to winter. Many are made out of cozy knit fabrics that keep you warm, but the open neckline is breathable enough so that you don’t break a sweat.

Personally, I prefer a V over a crewneck to let my décolletage peek through. The silhouette is stylish, universally flattering and comfortable amid colder temps.

“V-neck sweaters are trending for fall and winter because they can be layered for a preppy look with a dress shirt underneath, or under a sleek pea coat,” celebrity stylist Jazmin Whitley said. “They are very versatile and can create so many different fall looks. They’re all the rage.”

Below, shop three top V-neck sweaters for fall, ideal for your office holiday party, a cozy Christmas tree lighting, Secret Santa night and more seasonal activities.

It’s almost snow season. Get Winter White ready with this stretchy cable knit. The pullover slips right over your head and makes for comfortable all-day wear. Although the white version is on sale, you can also shop in 12 other colors, including light blue, khaki and coffee.

Already Christmas tree shopping? Same. This Hunter Green hue is perfect for casual winter outings. It’s made out of a breathable premium viscose-nylon-polyester blend, with ribbed cuffs and hem. I can smell the woodsy pine from here.

The leaves are changing, meaning there’s no better time than now to usher in a brick red sweater. This lightweight, slim-fit pullover features an eye-catching, cable knit pattern that screams winter. Wear it with jeans during a scenic walk through the park this month, or layer under a coat to get in the Christmas spirit in December.
