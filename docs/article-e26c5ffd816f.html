2026-11-13 13:11

"We are upgrading Autodesk shares to Buy from Hold and establishing a $310 price target, reflecting improving fundamentals and an increasingly attractive risk/reward profile." Stephens initiates Korn Ferry as overweight Stephens said the company is well positioned for earnings growth. "We are initiating coverage on Korn Ferry (KFY) with an Overweight rating and a $94 price target." StoneX initiates Birkenstock as buy StoneX said the shoe and sandal company has several "growth levers." "We are bullish on BIRK, given the brand's increasing relevance, multitude of growth levers, and best-in-class margins that should continue to benefit from greater scale." Roth initiates Allegro MicroSystems as buy Roth said Allegro has underappreciated growth. "We initiate coverage of Allegro MicroSystems (ALGM) with a Buy rating and $40 12-month price target.
