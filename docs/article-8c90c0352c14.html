Welfare wars: review, rinse, repeat

2026-11-27 03:00

MPs in Westminster have spent the week chewing over yet another review of how to reform the U.K.’s welfare system.

But with questions over whether the Milburn or Timms reviews will ever lead to meaningful change, Westminster Insider went on a mission to find out why so many government reviews end up kicked into the long grass or, worse still, completely ignored.

Carol Black, who has chaired four separate reviews, spills her secrets on how to actually get politicians to act on your recommendations. But she admits it’s “pretty awful when … nothing happens.”

Sascha also speaks to Jill Rutter, senior fellow at the Institute for Government, who explains how to avoid the dreaded “graveyard of landmark reviews.”

Labour MP Rachael Maskell, who lost the whip in July 2025 after taking part in a rebellion against Keir Starmer’s welfare proposals, says she is “Team Timms” and warns of the potential tensions between the two welfare reviews that could be coming down the track.
