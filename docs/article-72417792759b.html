Jaroslav Chmelar tells The Post how he’s making sure his Rangers dream stays on track

2026-10-06 23:34

Every NHL team plays the waiver game in some way to start the season, often making roster decisions partially — or entirely — based on which players must be exposed to a potential claim on their way to an AHL assignment.

During training camp, Jaroslav Chmelar was one of the few skaters legitimately competing for a spot in the Rangers lineup who is waivers exempt.

“The question is: Do we just put guys in the lineup and send guys down just because they’re waivers exempt, or do we put the guys in the lineup that we think are going to give us the best chance to win? We chose to make a decision based on the latter,” head coach Mike Sullivan said Tuesday before the Rangers took on the Islanders at Madison Square Garden. “We think the guys that are in the lineup are deserving to be in the lineup through their performance and their play. Jaro is one of those guys. The expectations that we have, and how we’ve defined it with Jaro, is his intentions are in the right place — and we think he’s had an impact on the game.”
