How Phaedra Parks’ ex-husband feels about her steamy new romance with ‘Traitors’ co-star CT Tamburello

2026-09-07 23:01

He told the outlet that as long as the “Challenge” veteran treats his ex and their two sons well, he’s “got no worries.”

The construction worker also confessed that like many fans, he saw a romance “brewing” between Parks and Tamburello while they were filming “The Traitors” Season 2.

Nida noted that he is not too concerned with the “Real Housewives of Atlanta” alum’s dating life and the two have a cordial co-parenting relationship.

He said that Parks “has always handled herself appropriately” when it comes to their children.

Parks and Nida wed in 2009 and share two sons: Ayden, 16, and Dylan, 13.

As the couple were already facing marital problems, Nida pleaded guilty to conspiracy to commit mail, wire and bank fraud in May 2014 and was sentenced to eight years in prison.

As Page Six previously reported, Parks confirmed her relationship with “The Real World” alum during her Thursday appearance on SiriusXM’s “Jeff Lewis Live.”

She told listeners that their relationship is “not platonic,” three years after they filmed “Traitors” together.

“He made it clear to her he really, really liked her and he fell for her when they were on ‘Traitors,’” a source told Page Six. “He makes her really happy. They really do laugh together a lot and their relationship is really sweet.”

In an E! News Instagram post Saturday about Tamburello and Parks’ relationship, White commented, “Sign the papers” alongside three clapping hands emojis.

Last month, she told TMZ that their divorce had still not been finalized and that it was “taking a while.”

She also noted that the two are not on speaking terms.
