Iran plotting new Oct. 7-style attack against Israel with its terrorist proxies: report

2026-09-06 00:08

Iran is reportedly gearing up for a new Oct. 7-style attack on Israel as part of a multi-front assault alongside its terrorist proxies in the region, according to Israeli intelligence assessments.

The Islamic Republic is allegedly plotting to repeat Hamas’ barbaric Oct. 7 attacks — which saw more Jews killed in a single day than any time since the Holocaust — but this time with full coordination of their sprawling terrorist network, which includes Hezbollah in Lebanon and the Houthis in Yemen, according to the Jerusalem Post.

Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps officials have sought to revitalize its “Axis of Resistance” terrorist network and have held multiple planning meetings with leaders from Hezbollah, the Houthis and Iraqi militias about an escalation against Israel, the outlet reported.

The IRGC is also conducting joint training exercises with its terrorist proxies and is increasing drone and missile production to replenish their stockpiles that have been exhausted in its wars against Israel and the US ahead of the next campaign, the sources told the publication.

Hezbollah and Hamas have also increased coordination to pressure Israel from multiple fronts, the intelligence indicates.

Iran’s alleged plot to launch a coordinated military strike against Israel from across the region would be the realization of Oct. 7 mastermind Yahya Sinwar’s ultimate dream, the Jerusalem Post reported.

Israeli intelligence found that Sinwar had hoped that the wider Arab world would join in after he launched his Oct. 7 rampage — which killed 1,200 Israelis, kidnapped 250, and left thousands of others wounded or raped.

But Iran and Hezbollah were not given prior notice of the date of the attacks and did not immediately join in the attacks as part of a full ground invasion, the intelligence stated.

Iran is hoping to join forces in its recreation of the horrific attack on Israel.
