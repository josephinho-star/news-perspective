Alabama vs. Kentucky prediction: College football Week 2 picks, odds, best bet Saturday

2026-09-12 15:00

At any other school in any other situation, Kalen DeBoer’s first two seasons would have been more than acceptable.

Unfortunately for DeBoer, he’s at Alabama and replacing Nick Saban, so a 20-8 record and one trip to the College Football Playoff in his first two seasons is enough for his seat to be simmering ahead of his third campaign.

Adding to the drama in Tuscaloosa is that DeBoer is putting his job security on the shoulders of a redshirt freshman quarterback, Keelon Russell.

Russell passed his first test in a Week 1 thrashing of East Carolina, but he’s now set to make his first road SEC start Saturday against Kentucky.

Alabama is a 10.5-point favorite at Kroger Field.

The Wildcats are not expected to make much noise in the SEC in Will Stein’s first season, but overlook this roster at your own peril.

Stein brought quarterback Kenny Minchey in from Notre Dame and he beefed up the offensive line and the backfield in the portal.

The Wildcats won’t be easily pushed around when they have the ball, which wasn’t the case in 2025.

If Kentucky can find a way to move the chains, the defense should hold up its end of the bargain.

The Wildcats ranked 35th in SP+ on the defensive side of the ball in 2025, and they return plenty of experience this season. A step forward is a fair expectation of this unit.

Alabama fans were encouraged by what they saw from Russell against East Carolina in Week 1.
