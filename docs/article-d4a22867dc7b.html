Pavel Dorofeyev misses game after all as Rangers have another injury to navigate

2026-11-17 05:48

The Rangers have another injury concern to navigate after all.

tRY IT NOWPavel Dorofeyev missed the game against the Canadiens on Monday with a lower-body injury despite head coach Mike Sullivan saying the past two days that he wasn’t concerned about Dorofeyev — who exited their practice Sunday for precautionary reasons — missing time. Sullivan said Monday morning that Dorofeyev participated in their optional morning skate.

Will Cuylle took Dorofeyev’s spot on the second line and scored a pair of goals, while Matt Rempe drew into the lineup for just the sixth time this season and skated on the fourth line. Eeli Tolvanen moved up to the third line, joining Noah Laba and Oliver Bjorkstrand.

When asked what went into ultimately keeping Dorofeyev out, Sullivan just said he was being evaluated for the injury.

In a season already filled with injuries, Dorofeyev’s added another one to their list. Their campaign was altered in the sixth game, when franchise goaltender Igor Shesterkin sustained a season-ending knee injury. Joe Veleno is on long-term injured reserve.

Alberts Smits and Sean Durzi are on injured reserve too. Joonas Korpisalo didn’t make his season debut until Nov. 7 — and didn’t make his MSG debut with the Blueshirts until Monday.

Dorofeyev, for all the posts he’s hit and the goals he hasn’t scored through his opening 21 games with the Rangers, remained at the center of their campaign. With his one-timer and left-handed shot, Dorofeyev, who scored 72 goals across the last two seasons and managed 20 on the power play last year, was added to help anchor their man-advantage unit and inject scoring into the Rangers lineup.

And for at least one night, they needed to operate without him.

Monday night was the Rangers Centennial Capstone Game, marking 100 years to the day since the franchise’s first game against the Montreal Maroons. They held a pregame ceremony — led by former play-by-play announcer Sam Rosen — that featured former players, or representatives from their families, who had their numbers retired by the organization. Mike Richter, Adam Graves, Brian Leetch, Mark Messier and Henrik Lundqvist were the former players in attendance on the ice.

The daughter of Harry Howell, the wife of Rod Gilbert, the daughter of Eddie Giacomin, the granddaughter of Jean Ratelle and the granddaughter of Vic Hadfield were also present on the ice. Billy Dea, the oldest living Rangers alumnus at 93 years old, sent a video that played on the videoboard during a commercial break in the third period.

Scott Morrow’s goal marked his first since March 2025 and first with the Rangers since the trade that sent K’Andre Miller to the Hurricanes. He said he didn’t see it go in from his vantage point at the blue line, but he said, “It was cool to score at Madison Square Garden. Felt good.” … Miller picked up an assist on Cuylle’s first goal and moved within two of 500 for his career. … Defenseman Sean Durzi participated in the Rangers’ optional morning skate in a regular blue jersey, shedding the red non-contact one, and skated with the extras afterward. He hasn’t played since Oct. 26. … Defenseman Marc Del Gaizo was assigned to AHL Hartford postgame. … Joonas Korpisalo became the first goaltender to win his first three starts with the Rangers since Antti Raanta in 2015-16 and just the seventh in franchise history.
