Hot property: Big banks, fashion stores seek ‘cheaper’, eye-catching Hong Kong retail

2026-08-31 09:00

HSBC to open its first flagship operation at the Capitol Centre, where market sources say the rent is much lower than its previous tenancy

The bank’s new Causeway Bay flagship branch and Premier centre will open on October 20, as its existing Premier centre at the Causeway Bay Plaza 2 on Lockhart Road, and the nearby Park Lane branch on Gloucester Road will be closed at the end of business on October 17 “due to lease expiry”, a spokesman said.

The spokesman, who declined to share more leasing details by citing a confidentiality agreement, added that renovation was in progress for the new location in Capitol Centre.

The bank did not comment on the rent.
