‘Yellowstone’ spinoff ‘Marshals’ is back for Season 2—How to watch for free

All hat, no cattle.

Season 1 wrapped up with many of the show’s main characters going in different directions. Cruz was D.C.-bound, Belle and Cal were getting shot at and Tate was kidnapped, likely to be used as leverage.
