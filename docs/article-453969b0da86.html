French first lady Brigitte Macron’s daughter, 42, follows in mom’s footsteps — and bags a beau half her age

2026-09-01 17:37

French First Lady Brigitte Macron’s daughter is following in her mother’s footsteps by dating a much younger man, but in a stunning role reversal — her boy toy beau is her teacher (sort of).

Tiphaine Auziere, 42, posted a picture of herself with Antoine Lecomte, 22, on vacation in Amsterdam on her Instagram.

The pair reportedly met in the glamorous northwestern French coastal town of Le Touquet, where Lecomte was working as a kitesurfing instructor.

“It was immediately obvious between them,” a close friend of Auziere told Gala.

“They have a lot in common: a love of Le Touquet and thrills, but also travel and entrepreneurship,” the friend added.

Brigitte is said to be “delighted” that her daughter has found love, just months after breaking up with right-wing French TV host Cyril Hanouna.

“She reacted just like any mother would. All that matters to her is her daughter’s happiness, so she’s very happy to see her thriving,” the source told Gala.

“She’ll be delighted to meet Antoine,” they added.

Antoine has reportedly already met Tiphaine’s two children, Aurele, 10, and Elise, 12.

The two-decade age gap is five years smaller than that of her 73-year-old mother and her second husband, French President Emmanuel Macron, 48.
