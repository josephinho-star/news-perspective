When Will Season 3 of ‘Landman’ Come Out? Here’s The Latest on The New Season of ‘Landman’

2026-11-07 17:00

If you find yourself missing Landman, you can head on over to the show’s official YouTube page to watch a plethora of must-see recap videos.

There’s a ton to choose from, including the “Best of Season 2,” “Landman’s Most Unlikely Partnerships,” “9 Moments That Took a Turn,” and “Landman’s Messiest Romances.” It’s not quite the same as a new episode (obviously), but it’s an enjoyable trip down memory lane, nonetheless.

What exactly does the future hold for Landman? Well, the show’s been renewed for a third season, but unlike the first two installments, the new season won’t be following the same November release schedule. Production on Season 3 is underway, so when can we expect new episodes of Landman to drop on Paramount+?

Here’s everything we know.

Is There a New Episode of Landman This Week (November 7)?

Nope. No new episode this week. The Season 2 finale of Landman aired on January 18, 2026.

Has Landman Been Renewed For Season 3?

You know it. By any metric, Landman has been one of Paramount+’s most successful shows. Unsurprisingly, the network renewed the series for Season 3 back in December 2025.

Why Is Season 3 of Landman Delayed?

We understand the confusion. The first two seasons of Landman premiered in November of 2024 and 2025, respectively, so many fans are like… umm… where are the new episodes?!

Sadly, Season 3 won’t follow the same schedule. Production on the upcoming season was delayed, and the show didn’t start shooting in Fort Worth, Texas, until October. No official reason for the delay is known, but it’s certainly possible that Taylor Sheridan’s busy schedule was a factor.

When Will Season 3 of Landman Come Out on Paramount+?

We still have a ways to go. With production likely continuing through early 2027, our best guess is that Season 3 of Landman will drop in mid-to-late 2027 on Paramount+.

Landman on Paramount+ Streaming Options:

Seasons 1 and 2 of Landman are streaming on Paramount+.

Paramount+ offers two subscription plans, Essential and Premium. Right now, you can get started with a seven-day free trial on the Premium plan.

The ad-supported Essential plan typically costs $8.99/month, while the ad-free Premium plan (which comes with Showtime titles and live CBS) costs $13.99/month.

Paramount+ doesn’t directly offer a free trial, but you can sample the streamer by adding Paramount+ to your Prime Video or Hulu accounts for an additional $13.99/month. You can also purchase Paramount+ through YouTube, which offers a free trial.

Landman Season 3 Casting Update:

Not only has Lioness scene-stealer Michael Kelly joined the series for Season 3 — playing a “titan of industry who leads the largest oil management firm in the world” — but Colm Feore (Nathan), Mustafa Speaks (Boss), Bobbi Salvor Menuez (Paigyn Meester), and Francesca Xuereb (Cheyenne) have all been upgraded to series regulars.

The first two seasons of Landman are available to stream on Paramount+.
