Florida vs. Missouri predictions, best bets: Don’t sell high on Gators just yet

2026-10-03 15:00

The Florida Gators are the story of college football this season.

Jon Sumrall’s first four games in charge couldn’t have gone better, and last week’s rout of Ole Miss has made the 44-year-old the toast of the town in Gainesville.

The AP has Florida (4-0) ranked No. 8, SP+ has the Gators as the 12th-best team in the country and Sagarin has them even higher at No. 9.

Florida is really good, but the schedule doesn’t relent. The Gators travel to Faurot Field as 5.5-point favorites over No. 25 Missouri on Saturday.

Florida vs. Missouri odds, prediction

This is a classic sell-high spot, but the Gators haven’t given us any reason to believe that a stumble is coming. Offensive coordinator Buster Faulkner has Florida in carnival mode with the ball, and the defense has done its bit, too.

Quarterback Aaron Philo, a first-year starter who transferred from Faulkner’s old team, Georgia Tech, has hardly put a foot wrong, silencing skeptics who were wondering whether or not the lack of experience would prove too much in the SEC.

He will face another good defense Saturday, but the Gators may not need to light up the scoreboard to win this game in Columbia.

It is hard to trust Mizzou’s offense right now.

Part of its struggles is not of its doing, with star running back Ahmad Hardy is still recovering from a gunshot wound he suffered in May, but the Tigers are not making life easy on a stalwart defense.

Missouri gained just 4.7 yards per play in a lackluster win over Troy in Week 3, and it couldn’t close the gap against Kamario Taylor and Mississippi State in Week 4.
