‘The Office’ star has the ultimate LA solution to last-minute Thanksgiving dinner rushes

2026-11-23 23:05

Now, the Sherman Oaks store is doing something no other Honey Baked Ham location in Southern California is offering: white-glove delivery service of a Thanksgiving meal.

Customers can order their Thanksgiving meal from the store and have it delivered directly to their door, with a dedicated “Hambassador” personally handling the delivery. Orders can be placed by phone or online.

The helpful measure is designed to ease the long lines that slow shoppers down during the holiday rush.

Though they’re known for their ham, the chain sells complete Thanksgiving meals complete with sides and desserts.

On Monday, Nov. 23, Baker hosted a meet-and-greet for shoppers at the Sherman Oaks store, posing for photos and shaking hands.

Located at 14427 Ventura Blvd, Baker and business partner Tony Vennie first opened the location in June.

“Growing up, Honey Baked was always part of our holiday traditions,” Vennie told Patch at the time.

“It’s a brand that brings people together around great food and meaningful moments.”

Honey Baked Ham was founded in Detroit, Michigan in 1957 by Henry Hoenselaar. In 1924, Hoenselaar invented the bone-in spiral-slicer that cooked and smoked ham, but the invention attracted no interest from meat companies for the first 24 years of its existence, prompting Hoenselaar to open his own store.

Today, the company has nearly 500 locations across the United States. In addition to its signature spiral-sliced, slow-smoked bone-in ham, the store also sells turkeys and side dishes including mac-and-cheese, vegetables, and potatoes.
