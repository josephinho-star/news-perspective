Will losing US midterms see Trump double down or signal beginning of the end?

2026-11-02 00:00

Trump will almost surely retain the ability to block legislation challenging his plans, but voters’ frustrations over his policies grow

On the one hand, an expected strong showing by opposition Democrats could bring more Congressional scrutiny over some of the most unconventional, maximalist and disruptive policies generated by an American president in the nation’s 250-year history.

At the same time, he is not on the ballot and little is likely to change structurally. No matter who wins or how reinvigorated lawmakers become, Trump will almost certainly retain his ability to block legislation that challenges his agenda.
