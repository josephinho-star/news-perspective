Top politician caught on hot mic discussing dangers of keeping cellphone near his ‘d–k’

2026-11-13 21:01

Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese was caught in an embarrassing hot mic gaffe yukking it up with another politician about the dangers of keeping his cell phone near his “d–k.”

The 63-year-old commander-in-chief was talking to his climate change minister, Chris Bowen, while on a break at a summit in Turkey Friday, when he first warned the fellow pol against putting the gadget in the breast pocket of his blazer, footage shows.

“It can interfere with your rhythm,” Albanese said, referring to his heart — before the convo turned to his, well, land down under.

“I’m more conscious of — I stopped putting it in my pants pocket,” Bowen replied, according to news.com.au

“Because of your d–k?” the prime minister asked — seemingly clueless he was being captured on a microphone.

“Yeah, because of my d–k, that’s rather important, arguably more important than the heart,” Bowen responded, according to The Telegraph.

The package-fixated politicians then burst into laughter while apparently unaware they were being filmed at the COP31 climate conference.

“They were having a joke in between meetings,” a spokesman for the prime minister later said.

Electromagnetic waves released by cellphones can decrease the quality of man’s sperm by zapping their mobility, according to a study published in the journal Environmental Research in 2022.

There is, however, no conclusive evidence that keeping a cellphone near your groin causes erectile dysfunction, according to the National Institutes of Health.
