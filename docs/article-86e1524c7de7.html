Sean Payton preps Broncos for highly anticipated 49ers matchup in strange way

2026-10-01 19:43

The 49ers and Broncos square off in a highly anticipated Week 4 matchup.

To prepare for the showdown, Denver coach Sean Payton had the 49ers’ logo painted on the Broncos’ practice field as the team gets ready for Sunday’s game at Levi’s Stadium.

The 49ers enter the matchup as one of few remaining undefeated NFL teams at 3-0. Meanwhile, the Broncos pulled off an insane comeback against the Super Bowl favorite Rams last week, scoring all 30 of their points in the second half of the matchup.

Niners coach Kyle Shanahan has put together an impressive offense despite numerous injuries to start the season. Much of the 49ers’ wide receiver room has been decimated by injury, but San Francisco has escaped unscathed to start the season.

The Broncos (2-1) will look to score quickly this time after an improbable comeback against another NFC West team.
