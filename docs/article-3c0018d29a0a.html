Steve Young sees one reason the 49ers can avoid peaking too early

2026-11-23 22:56

The 49ers are a season removed from a blowout 41-6 loss to the Seahawks in the divisional round of the playoffs.

With a fresh start, San Francisco has become viewed as the top team in the NFC, holding a 9-1 record, and former three-time 49ers Super Bowl-winning quarterback Steve Young believes the team has yet to peak and views them as similar to last year’s Seahawks team.

“And then I remember when the 49ers—when the Seahawks showed up again in Levi’s Stadium in the beginning of January for that final game, and I was watching that game like, who are these guys? What in the world is happening? This is—I mean, they were—they had peaked in that way, what you just described, at just the perfect time. And then they had five weeks of just domination,” Young said, on “The Herd,” hosted by Colin Cowherd.

When Week 11 started in the NFL season last year, it was the beginning of a seven-game regular season win streak by Seattle that lasted all the way through the Super Bowl to give them their second trophy in franchise history.

San Francisco just wrapped up Week 11 on Sunday night, when they blew the Vikings out of the water with a 35-10 victory, where quarterback Brock Purdy threw three touchdowns while completing 21 of 26 passes for 255 yards.

Young told Cowherd that he believes Purdy has played a large part in the team’s success this year, as well as “Hall of Fame” coaching from Kyle Shanahan, that’s been mixed with the “grinding personalities” of George Kittle, Christian McCaffrey, Mike Evans and Fred Warner.

“Those two combining together with the talent around them. Four Hall of Famers, Colin,” Young said. “You think about George, you think about Trent Williams, you think about Christian McCaffrey, you think about Mike Evans. There’s four, not prime. …They’re still in the thick of their careers. Four Hall of Famers that jump into that mix.”

“They’re not gonna give an inch,” Young said. “So in that way, as long as no one gets hurt.”

Young added that he does believe the Bears and Seahawks are a legitimate threat to the 49ers once the playoffs begin in January.
