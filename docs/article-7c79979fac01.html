Amy Schumer debuts bangs, hilariously channels Sabrina Carpenter

2026-11-26 19:49

Just call her Sabrina Schumer.

Amy Schumer made a major change to her look this Thanksgiving, showing off new bangs in a video on Instagram Thursday.

“Now that I have bangs, a lot of people are thinking that I am someone I’m not, like Sabrina Carpenter,” she said before adding, “well, a carpenter.”

One friend got in on the joke, commenting, “I’m confused I thought this was a Sabrina carpenter fan account. My bad 🔥☕️”

It’s unclear what sparked the “Trainwreck” star, 45, to chop her hair as she’s never had bangs — but she did try out the idea in a 2017 sketch for “Inside Amy Schumer.”

When a version of herself from the future with bangs comes to visit, the real-time Schumer exclaims, “I look so good with bangs!”

Meanwhile, Carpenter is known for her signature style, with the “Short n’ Sweet” singer rocking bombshell blond waves for the past few years.

And, just in case Schumer needs any tips, perhaps she can call Carpenter’s hairstylist, Laurie Heaps, who divulged all of her secrets to Page Six Style in 2024. The maestro’s hot tip? She uses the Eleven Australia Medium Round Brush ($41) to “add volume and shape.”

While Sabrina has yet to respond, we’re hoping these two blonds with bangs can link up soon.
