Turkish military helicopter crash kills 5 during training flight

2026-11-24 20:13

Five crew members were killed Tuesday when a Turkish military helicopter crashed during a training flight in central Turkey’s Eskişehir province, Turkey’s defense ministry said.

The Turkish Air Force’s AS-532 Cougar, a French-made troop transport helicopter, had taken off from a base in Eskişehir, between Ankara and İstanbul.

Television footage showed emergency vehicles in an open field near the province’s Tepebaşı district, Agence France-Presse reported.

Tuesday’s crash was the second fatal military helicopter crash in Turkey in 12 days. On November 12, four military personnel were killed when an S-70 helicopter crashed in the eastern province of Malatya. The cause of that crash remains unknown.
