Former AKP, MHP mayors arrested in Turkey corruption probe

2026-09-29 12:12

A Turkish court has ordered the arrest of 15 people, including two former mayors from President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan’s ruling Justice and Development Party (AKP) and its nationalist ally, in a corruption investigation into a district municipality in central Turkey, the DHA news agency reported.

The arrests are part of an investigation into the Yahşihan Municipality in Kırıkkale province.

Former mayors Ahmet Sungur, who was elected for the AKP, and Osman Türkyılmaz, who served as mayor for the Nationalist Movement Party (MHP), were among those jailed pending trial, DHA reported.

Thirty-five suspects had been detained in coordinated operations in four provinces after prosecutors issued warrants for former deputy mayors, municipal council members, municipal employees and businesspeople as well as Sungur and Türkyılmaz.

Two suspects were placed under house arrest and 18 were released under judicial supervision. Another suspect who was already in prison in connection with a separate case was also placed under judicial supervision, while one suspect remains at large.

Prosecutors are investigating allegations of forming and belonging to a criminal organization, bribery, extortion by public officials, bid rigging and fraud against public institutions.

The case was reopened after new evidence prompted prosecutors to reverse earlier decisions not to pursue charges.

The investigation is separate from an earlier corruption case involving Sungur.

Sungur was detained in September 2025 along with municipal officials and businesspeople and later jailed pending trial in a separate corruption case. The Interior Ministry subsequently suspended him from office, while the AKP referred him to its disciplinary board with a request for his expulsion.

In April, the Kırıkkale 1st High Criminal Court sentenced Sungur, a former deputy mayor, the municipality’s zoning director and a businessman to six years and eight months in prison each for extortion by a public official. A municipal employee was sentenced to three years and four months, while six other defendants were acquitted.

Sungur served as Yahşihan mayor from 2009 to 2019 and won the office again in the March 2024 local elections with 41.58 percent of the vote. Türkyılmaz, who defeated him in 2019, governed the district from 2019 to 2024 before losing to Sungur.

The operation stands out because it targets former mayors from both parties in Erdoğan’s governing alliance. Corruption investigations involving AKP- and MHP-run municipalities have been less common than the series of probes targeting municipalities controlled by the main opposition Republican People’s Party (CHP) since its gains in the 2024 local elections.

More than 20 CHP mayors have been jailed pending trial, while others have been suspended or removed from office. The opposition says the investigations amount to a politically motivated campaign, while the government denies interfering in judicial proceedings.
