Merz has no good answers as the far right targets his downfall

2026-09-08 02:00

BERLIN — Germany’s far right is moving to turn its landslide election victory into a knockout blow against Friedrich Merz and his beleaguered conservatives — and the chancellor appears at a loss over how to respond.

Merz seemed to have no clear plan for saving himself and his party as his government confronted its biggest political crisis yet, beyond ambiguous promises to become a better, more empathetic politician 16 months into his chancellorship.

“We are all deeply shocked,” Merz said Monday, after the far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) reached a new high in Saxony-Anhalt’s weekend election while support for his Christian Democratic Union fell by more than half. “This is the worst election defeat the CDU has suffered in years — or even decades,” the chancellor added. “Of course, there will have to be consequences.”
