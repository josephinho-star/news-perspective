Vanessa Trump 'finally cancer-free,' she says after health battle in emotional video with daughter Kai

2026-10-23 23:54

Vanessa Trump revealed she is now cancer-free after battling breast cancer, sharing the emotional health update in a new video with her daughter, Kai Trump, months after publicly announcing her diagnosis.

The 19-year-old granddaughter of President Donald Trump shared a preview of the conversation on Instagram during Breast Cancer Awareness Month, revealing that her mother, who's the ex-wife of Donald Trump Jr., was diagnosed in April.

"And my mom was diagnosed with breast cancer in April, so we thought we'd share her story and how she was diagnosed, how she overcame it," Kai said in the video.

To finish the social clip, Vanessa shared the news that she is now cancer-free.

"And it came back clear, so I'm finally cancer-free," Vanessa said.

"Love you!" Kai responded.

The video also features former Fox News medical contributor Dr. Nicole Saphier, who discusses the importance of breast cancer awareness and early detection.

"It feels different for everyone," Saphier said. "The reason I like social media talking about breast cancer is because it's reminding people that it is common."

In her Instagram caption, Kai said she sat down with her mother and Saphier to discuss Vanessa's cancer journey, prevention and early detection. She also announced that the full conversation would be released on YouTube the following day.

Vanessa, the mother of five, first revealed her breast cancer diagnosis in May.

"I've recently been diagnosed with breast cancer," Vanessa wrote at the time. "While this isn't news anyone expects, I'm working closely with my medical team on a treatment plan."

She revealed that doctors had performed a procedure earlier that week and thanked her family for their support.

"I am staying focused and hopeful while surrounded by the love and support of my family, my kids, and those closest to me," she wrote.

The announcement prompted support from Ivanka Trump, who wrote, "Praying for your continued strength and a swift recovery. Love you mama."

In June, Vanessa said she was recovering from surgery and preparing to begin the second stage of treatment, Fox News Digital reported.

Kai Trump later opened up about the emotional experience in an August interview with Fox News Digital, shortly after beginning her freshman year at the University of Miami.

"There was definitely, there was a lot of crying in, like, the week leading up," she said of the days before leaving for college. "We would just be driving, and then we'd just start crying together."

At the time, she said her mother was improving after treatment.

"My mom, she's doing a lot better," she said. "Kind of after treatment, she wasn't feeling very good. Just like how treatment will make you feel … but she's getting a lot better now."

Kai described the extra time she spent with her mother over the summer as a "blessing in disguise."

"I was able to spend extra time with her that I would normally probably spend on the golf course," Kai said. "It was nice to spend those extra hours with her throughout the day before going to college."

Now, months after Vanessa first revealed her diagnosis and Kai described their emotional summer together, the mother and daughter are sharing a hopeful new chapter.

Fox News Digital's Sophia Compton and Paulina Dedaj contributed reporting.
