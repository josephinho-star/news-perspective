Top 10 things to watch in the stock market on Black Friday

2026-11-27 15:08

Our 10 things to watch Friday, Nov. 27, 2026 1. U.S. stock futures look to open higher on this holiday-shortened trading day, with technology stocks leading the early charge. The three major averages are on pace for strong weekly gains, with the Dow up 4.2%, the S & P 500 up 3.9%, and the Nasdaq up 4.1%. As a reminder, equity markets close at 1 p.m. ET; the bond market closes at 2 p.m. ET. 2. The S & P 500 is on track for one of its best Black Friday sessions on record , buoyed by a strong rally in Chinese technology and chip stocks. The CSI Semiconductor index, which tracks 40 stocks, rose 17.6% in its biggest single-day gain on record. 3. Consumers spent roughly $7.1 billion online on Thanksgiving Day, 10.4% more than last year, "fueled by big discounts on electronics, toys and apparel," Reuters reported , citing Adobe data. The total beat Adobe's $6.9 billion forecast. 4. Shares of Anthropic are up 3% in premarket trading. As we discussed Wednesday , the stock's performance matters both for the AI frontier lab's ability to raise funds and for the prospect of an OpenAI IPO. The stock traded as high as $115 on Nov. 19, minutes after its IPO, but has since struggled to surpass its debut price, last trading just below $83. 5. Palo Alto Networks is down nearly 3% following a Wednesday Bloomberg report that a former executive is suing the cybersecurity company, alleging he was fired after raising concerns about accounting fraud. Palo Alto reported a beat-and-raise quarter on Nov. 18 and announced an acquisition that should further embed the company in customers' operations. 6. GE Vernova CEO Scott Strazik moved $3.23 million of existing 401(k) balances into the company's stock fund, adding 4,275 share equivalents valued at $756.71 each, according to a securities filing this week. It wasn't an open-market purchase, but we view the decision as a vote of confidence in the company's long-term growth prospects. 7. Two Chinese companies installed Nvidia's high-end Blackwell chips in a Shanghai data center linked to Megaspeed and Alice Huang, the suspected Chinese spy, Bloomberg reported . It is illegal to ship these powerful chips to China without an export license. Nvidia shares rose 3% in the premarket amid a broader rally in tech names. 8. Chip sellers have told neoclouds — companies that rent compute power for AI — they won't supply Nvidia chips if any of the computing capacity will be loaned to Chinese companies, two people familiar with the situation told CNBC. 9. OPEC+ is expected to keep its first-quarter 2027 oil production targets unchanged when ministers meet on Sunday. 10. Shares of Vertex are pointing to a lower open after the FDA delayed its decision on povetacicept, the company's potential treatment for IgA nephropathy, a chronic kidney ailment also known as Berger's disease, until February 2027.
