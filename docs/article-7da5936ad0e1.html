Kandi Burruss’ ex Todd Tucker reveals shocking sex confession about their 11-year marriage

2026-09-16 02:05

Kandi Burruss’ ex-husband Todd Tucker is making a bold claim that he and the “Real Housewives of Atlanta” star had threesomes during “most” of their decade-long-plus marriage.

“We did. I would say most of our marriage we did,” Tucker tells “RHOA” personality Peter Thomas during a revealing trailer for his “Til Divorce Do Us Part” film.

The clip — which dropped on Instagram on Monday — shows Thomas riding in the passenger side of the vehicle when he directly questions Tucker, 53, about threesomes during his marriage to Burruss, 50.

“For real?” Thomas, 65, asks. When Tucker responds in the affirmative, Thomas — who was previously married to “RHOA” star Cynthia Bailey — exclaims, “I’m a saint!”

The trailer promises to pull back the curtain on the post-divorce lives of the men behind the drama on the hit reality show.

In addition to Thomas and Tucker, the project also features Apollo Nida, who was married to Phaedra Parks; Dennis McKinley, who was engaged to Porsha Williams; and Ralph Pittman Jr., who was married to Drew Sidora.

Fans in the comments thread were thrilled by the trailer, with one person writing, “Well the scheme has finally arisen 😂😂 I’m not mad at the concept.”

Another remarked, “You know what 🤔… yall might be on to something… a show about the ex husbands and their new journey from the man’s perspective. We don’t see that often or hardly ever.”

A third chimed in, “I want this as a reality show 🙌 yeah I’m a woman but I will most definitely watch.”

A rep for Burruss did not immediately respond to Page Six’s request for comment on Tuesday afternoon.

The former couple married back in April 2014 in a lavish ceremony in Atlanta. The big day was chronicled in Bravo spinoff series “Kandi’s Wedding.”

The singer revealed in a November 2025 statement to People that the pair — who share kids Ace, 10, and Blaze, 6 — would be splitting.

Burruss also shares daughter Riley Burruss, 24, with ex Russell Spencer, and Tucker shares daughter Kaela Tucker, 30, with former partner Keema Parker.

“After deep thought and a lot of prayer, I’ve made the decision to move forward with a divorce,” Burruss told the outlet.

The reality TV personality added, “This is a difficult and emotional time, but my focus remains on protecting my peace, being the best mother I can be, and co-parenting with love and respect.”
