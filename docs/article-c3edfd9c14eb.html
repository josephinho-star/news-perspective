Connor Wood channels Tom Holland on ‘Dancing With the Stars’ with epic drag-filled freestyle inspired by ‘Lip Sync Battle’

2026-11-25 03:18

Ahead of taking to the ballroom during Tuesday night’s episode, Wood said it was the perfect choice because “so many people say [he] look[s] like him,” in reference to the “Spider-Man” actor.

“That’s what you call give the people what they want freestyle,” Derek Hough said during judges’ feedback, while Carrie Ann Inaba pointed out a “mistake” during the routine.

He and Arnold earned a 29/30 for the freestyle, bringing his total for the night to 82/90.

Holland and now-wife Zendaya’s “Lip Sync Battle” episode aired in May 2017 and immediately broke the internet.

“Like, I met him and I was like, ‘Tom Holland.’ Like, ‘You are exactly him,'” she said of her first impression of Wood. “He is down for anything.”

While Arnold initially said they’d “recreate” the viral television moment, she later clarified, “So, basically, my idea is, like, we’re going to make our own version of it.”

The pro dancer added that their routine is “not going to be the same choreography” and that it’ll be set to a “fun remix” of the two songs.

“Connor’s going to be showing some skin at the end,” she also shared. “I’m, like, so excited.”

Prior to the big finale, Arnold broke down in tears when she opened what she deemed “the most thoughtful and meaningful” gift from Wood.

“Stop it!” she exclaimed as she lifted the lid to reveal the floral purple Rapunzel dress she wore during “Disney Night” when they performed a waltz to “I See the Light” from “Tangled.”

“You guys, he bought my Rapunzel dress. We don’t get to keep our costumes — ever,” Arnold, who joined “DWTS” in Season 32, which aired in 2023, said. “Wow, he won. This is literally perfect.”

Wood — who had initially not wanted to be in the room when his partner opened her gift — walked in and confirmed he “begged” for the show’s costume department to sell him the dress, though it’s unclear exactly what he ultimately paid.

On Friday, Arnold revealed in a playful Instagram video that her big sister, Lindsay, a former Mirrorball champ, will join her and Wood for their highly anticipated freestyle.

Wood fought his way to the “DWTS” Season 35 finale all season long and is facing off against “Fresh Prince of Bel-Air” alum Tatyana Ali, Paralympian Ezra Frech, Olympic figure skater Amber Glenn and reality star Maura Higgins.
