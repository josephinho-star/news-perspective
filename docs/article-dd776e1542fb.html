Charissa Thompson mocks ex-husbands on TNF: ‘They’re all bad’

2026-10-23 18:42

Charissa Thompson threw some shade at her ex-husbands on “Thursday Night Football” during Amazon Prime Video’s coverage of the Bears’ 30-24 win over the Patriots.

A clip went viral of Thompson, 44, cracking a joke on the topic during a panel segment.

“I thought you were going to make me rank my ex-husbands — and they’re all bad,” Thompson quipped.

Thompson has been married twice before, tying the knot with her first husband at age 25.

The details of that marriage have largely been kept private, but Thompson did briefly speak about him in a 2016 Facebook post.

“When your ex-husband helps you through sadness and makes you smile. He’s a hot mess and I appreciate him so much. Ladies, by the way he’s single! I’ll hook you up,” she wrote.

Thompson then remarried in 2020 to agent and former baseball player Kyle Thousand.

The couple divorced in April 2022, with Thompson citing “irreconcilable differences.”

Thompson has since said she doesn’t plan on marrying again.

“No, I will never legally get married again. I won’t sign any paperwork,” Thompson wrote on an Instagram Q&A. “But happy to have a party and celebrate how much I love Steve with friends and family.”

“Steve” is in reference to Thompson’s current boyfriend, marketing executive Steve Cundari, with whom she attended Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce’s wedding over the summer at Madison Square Garden.

Thompson and Cundari were set up by Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford and his wife, Kelly, as Thompson revealed on her podcast.

“Matthew and Kelly were sick of me dating losers and they were like, ‘We have a great guy for you.’ So, they were the matchmakers,” Thompson explained.

Thompson joined Prime Video in 2022 as the host of their “Thursday Night Football coverage.” She previously worked for ESPN and Fox Sports.
