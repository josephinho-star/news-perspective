Lane Kiffin hits Death Valley with Shaq, Paul Skenes, Angel Reese and, naturally, Kevin Costner

2026-11-07 20:17

LSU is gearing up for a huge game against Alabama, and head coach Lane Kiffin — who I think we can comfortably say is the PT Barnum of college football — called in some serious firepower to join him as he walked off the bus and into Tiger Stadium.

On Saturday, he was joined by LSU royalty in NBA great Shaquille O'Neal, Pittsburgh Pirates ace Paul Skenes and WNBA star Angel Reese.
