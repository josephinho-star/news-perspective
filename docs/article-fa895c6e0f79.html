Kylie Jenner gives tour of ‘dream house’ in the making 6 years after buying $15M property

2026-09-07 12:03

“I’m going to go to my construction site,” the makeup mogul, 29, said over the weekend. “I have to do some approvals and you’re coming with me. Let’s go.”

The reality star kicked off her vlog by showing off her landscaping, from lavender bushes and “old, ancient” olive trees to a planned rose garden because she “just want[s] to be a princess.”

After gushing over her “unreal” reclaimed cobblestone driveway, she approached the “beautiful dream Italian home.”

She showed “what [her] floors look like” — “reclaimed gorgeous wood” — before selecting the plaster color she wants “for the main areas” of the residence.

Jenner subsequently filmed the “old fireplace … in pieces” that will go in the entryway, alongside old brick.

She then ventured to her kitchen to display the “beautiful” cabinetry pre-staining treatment.

The “Keeping Up With the Kardashians” alum’s tour included another fireplace, a gym basement, a spa area and guest bathrooms with “stunning stone” and “a vintage tile … moment.”

Jenner wrapped up with a glimpse of her gravel “sports court,” fountain and pizza oven for future dinner parties and birthdays.

Concluding the social media upload, the businesswoman said, “That is it for all the approvals today and all the newness. I’m so excited. I’ve been working on this for so long, and I’m just ready for it to be done. And to move in.”

Jenner made headlines for purchasing the five-acre property in April 2020, the same month she bought a $36.5 million Holmby Hills, California, compound.

While she initially didn’t plan to document the building experience, she explained to fans, “It’s kind of all over the Internet anyway.”

She has previously shown her Instagram followers the “yummy laundry room” and massive walk-in closet.

“When I was younger, I got really excited, and I got the craziest house I could in Beverly Hills, and I just kind of went for it,” she explained in the August interview. “I’ve realized that my life got too big, and I said, ‘Yeah, never mind.'”

Jenner noted, “I think I lost control of my life a little bit for a while there.”
