Women's AP Top 25 poll breakdown: What to know about each team

2026-11-23 23:04

It was a steady week in the AP poll, with every team in the top 10 keeping its place. Oklahoma State dropped out of the 25th spot and was replaced by Baylor. After a solid week, Kentucky leapfrogged Vanderbilt and Ole Miss dropped two spots after a tight loss to Ohio State.

There will be fireworks this week with No. 1 South Carolina facing off against No. 7 UConn on Tuesday night.

Information from The Associated Press and ESPN Research contributed to this report. All times ET.

Stat to know: South Carolina's win over Providence was its 104th straight win against an unranked opponent, which is the longest active Division I streak. South Carolina is 6-0 for the 12th time under Dawn Staley.

What's next: Tuesday vs. UConn, 8 p.m., FS1; Saturday vs. Tennessee State, 2:30 p.m., SECN+

Stat to know: Michigan's 81-point win over Niagara is its largest in program history. The Wolverines previous largest win also came against Niagara in 2016, which it won by 72.

What's next: Monday vs. Miami, 4 p.m.; Wednesday vs. Toledo, 1:30 p.m., TBD

Stat to know: Louisville's nine-game winning streak is its longest to start out the season since the 2020-21 season.

What's next: Friday vs. Akron, 1 p.m., ACCNX; Sunday vs. ETSU, Noon, ACC Network

Stat to know: JuJu Watkins recorded her second career game with at least 35 points, 10 rebounds and five steals in a win against Baylor. That was Watkins' fifth straight 20-point game to start the season, which is the longest streak by a USC player to start a season this century.

What's next: Monday vs. Northern Arizona, 10 p.m., B1G+; Friday vs. Saint Joseph's, 1:30 p.m.

Stat to know: Duke's 111 points against Fordham ties for its most in a game over the past 10 seasons. The win was also its 15th straight home win, which is the longest active streak in the ACC.

What's next: Saturday vs. UConn, 7 p.m., FOX

Stat to know: Breya Cunningham's 19 points against Ohio State was her most against an AP Top 25 opponent in her career. It was also her first career double-double.

What's next: Wednesday vs. SE Louisiana, 7 p.m., SEC Network; Sunday at SE Missouri, 3 p.m., ESPN+

Stat to know: UConn won its 83rd straight game against an unranked team in a win over Seton Hall. Jovana Popovic recorded her second game with 20+ points and five-plus rebounds this season. All other Big East freshmen have combined for zero such games.

What's next: Tuesday vs. South Carolina, 8 p.m., FS1; Saturday vs. Duke, 7 p.m., FOX

Stat to know: Both Ava Heiden and Journey Houston recorded double-doubles in Iowa's win over Montana State. Houston had a career-high 13 rebounds, while Heiden put up a second consecutive double-double.

What's next: Monday vs. Utah, 1:30 p.m.; Wednesday vs. Navy, 11 a.m.

Stat to know: LSU had its sixth straight win by at least 45 points, the longest streak by any Division I team over the past 22 seasons.

What's next: Monday vs. Blemont, 8 p.m., SECN+; Friday vs. Villanova, 4:30 p.m.

Stat to know: Sienna Betts recorded 12 points, eight rebounds, nine assists, four steals and five blocks against Cal Poly. She's the only Division I player over the past 25 seasons to hit all those marks in a single game.

What's next: Tuesday vs. St. John's, 5 p.m., Peacock; Sunday vs. Penn, 5 p.m., B1G+

Stat to know: Me'Arah O'Neal scored 16 points, while Clara Strack added 10 points, and they both had nine rebounds in Kentucky's win over Marshall.

What's next: Friday vs. Maine, 4:45 p.m.; Saturday vs. South Dakota State, 3:30 p.m.

Stat to know: Mikayla Blakes is on a 58-game streak of scoring in double figures.

What's next: Monday vs. Arizona, 5 p.m.; Wednesday vs. Oregon State, 6:30 p.m.; Sunday vs. Georgia State, 1 p.m., SEC Network

Stat to know: Hannah Hidalgo surpassed Duke star Alana Beard for the most-made career free throws in ACC history with 587.

What's next: Tuesday vs. Davidson, 1 p.m., ESPN+; Thursday vs. West Virginia, 11:30 a.m., ESPN2

Stat to know: LeeLee Bell, Sa'Myah Smith and Brooklyn Stewart all recorded double-doubles in Oklahoma's win over Northwestern State. Aaliyah Chavez also added to that with scoring 22 points.

What's next: Monday vs. Virginia, 2:30 p.m.; Tuesday vs. Montana State, 4 p.m.

Stat to know: TCU is 8-0 for the fourth consecutive time under coach Mark Campbell. It had never started better than 7-0 in the NCAA era prior to Campbell's arrival.

What's next: Friday vs. Georgia Tech, 1:30 p.m.; Saturday vs. Mississippi State, 11 a.m.

Stat to know: Washington allowed just five points in the third quarter of its win against Cal Poly.

What's next: Friday vs. Oklahoma State, 1:30 p.m.

Stat to know: Dawn Plitzuweit became the second-fastest Minnesota coach to 75 wins after beating Central Michigan.

What's next: Thursday vs. Missouri State, 6:30 p.m.

Stat to know: Maryland held Morgan State scoreless in the second quarter of their recent matchup. That's the first time the Terps have ever allowed zero points in a quarter.

What's next: Tuesday vs. Monmouth, 7 p.m., Big Ten Plus

Stat to know: Jaloni Cambridge, Kennedy Cambridge and Ava Watson each have at least one steal in every game this season.

What's next: Saturday vs. VCU, noon, ESPN+

Stat to know: Illinois forced 20 turnovers in its win over Ball State.

What's next: Friday vs. Marquette, 11 a.m.

Stat to know: Jada Richard scored her 500th career point in the win over Alabama A&M.

What's next: Sunday vs. Memphis, 3 p.m., SEC Network

Stat to know: West Virginia had 10 blocks and 10 steals against Akron. That's the first time the Mountaineers have hit those marks since 2022.

What's next: Tuesday vs. Florida, 3:30 p.m., ESPN+

Stat to know: Nebraska's 113-37 win over Mount St. Mary's contained its largest-ever margin of victory over a Division I opponent.

What's next: Tuesday vs. Kansas City, 8 p.m., Big Ten Plus

Stat to know: There were 16 lead changes in North Carolina's win over Alabama.

What's next: Tuesday vs. Gardner-Webb, 7 p.m., ACC Extra

Stat to know: Baylor outscored Northern Kentucky 23-6 in the fourth quarter of its 96-42 win.

What's next: Friday vs. Penn State, 5:30 p.m., ESPN+
