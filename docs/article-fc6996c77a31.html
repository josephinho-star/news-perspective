Pavel Dorofeyev unfazed by slow start to Rangers tenure, temporary demotion thanks to important trend

2026-10-22 02:25

The opening act of Pavel Dorofeyev’s Rangers tenure had already featured a slow start, with just two goals through 11 games. In the latest sample Monday, he’d strung together unsuccessful high-danger scoring chance after unsuccessful high-danger scoring chance.

But then head coach Mike Sullivan added another wrinkle to it, revealing that he temporarily removed Dorofeyev from their first power-play unit for staying on the ice too long and not changing smartly.
