Power out? This Jackery station can keep essentials running and it’s under $500

2026-09-26 10:30

You might not have been prepared for the storm this time around — but you can be for the next one.

A good portable power station can get you through unexpected blackouts and keep essential devices running for longer. Jackery makes a ton of great options, including the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 Portable Power Station, which is on sale right now for under $500 — the lowest price it’s been at in 30 days, thanks to early Prime Big Deal Days deals.

It has a 1,500W AC output and a 3,000W surge peak, with enough power for household essentials like your refrigerator and smaller appliances. You can also power multiple devices at once.

The portable power station has two USB-C ports, one USB-A port, one DC car port and three AC outlets, so it can charge a range of devices. At 24 pounds and with a carry handle, it’s easy to take on the go — perfect for car camping or keeping in the trunk for emergencies.

What’s more, you can use the Jackery app to monitor and manage the power station. There are multiple charging modes, including Quiet Charging Mode for times when you want to keep noise to a minimum and Emergency Super Charging Mode, which can fully recharge the power station in about an hour under the brand’s test conditions.

If you want to be well-equipped for the next emergency or just need a portable power station for your fall camping trips, Jackery’s Explorer 1000 v2 Portable Power Station is a great buy, especially now while it’s at its 30-day low price.

How fast does the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 charge?

It charges in 1.7 hours via a standard wall outlet, 1 hour using Emergency Super Charge, or via 400W solar input.

What ports are available on the Explorer 1000 v2?

It features six outlets: three AC outlets, two 100W USB-C ports, one 18W USB-A port, and one 12V car port.

What warranty and return policy cover this product?

It includes a 3-year warranty (extendable to 5 years upon online registration) and is backed by a 30-day return policy.

Your Prime Big Deal Days FAQs, answered

What is Prime Big Deal Days and when is it?

Prime Big Deal Days — fondly known as October Prime Day — is Amazon’s biggest fall sale, offering massive discounts before the holiday rush of Black Friday and Cyber Monday. October Prime Day runs from Tuesday, Oct. 6 to Wednesday, Oct. 7, 2026.

Prime Day vs. Prime Big Deal Days

Prime Day and Prime Big Deal Days are both Prime member-exclusive seasonal sales with massive discounts on rotating bestsellers. Unlike July’s Prime Day, Prime Big Deal Days runs every October as a precursor to the busy holiday season, allowing shoppers to start saving on gift shopping early.

Do you have to be an Amazon Prime member to shop?

October Prime Day is exclusively available to Prime members. However interested shoppers can grab a 30-day free trial to try Prime, and its many benefits with no strings attached, and gain access to exclusive deals and the convenience of fast, free shipping.
