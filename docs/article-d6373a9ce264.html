Lionel Richie remains hospitalized as new details about his health emerge

2026-09-01 23:40

The singer, 77, is undergoing testing for cardiac-related issues, TMZ reported Tuesday.

He’s expected to be released from the hospital Wednesday, according to the outlet.

Page Six has reached out to Richie’s rep for comment.

The “American Idol” judge checked himself into a local hospital in St. Louis, Missouri, over the weekend after he struggled to finish his concert at the Muny Amphitheater Saturday.

Doctors were reportedly looking into “slight dehydration” as the cause.

During the concert, the Grammy-winning musician had to ask for a chair while performing his final song, “All Night Long,” so that he could sit down.

“Some things have been happening on this tour that kind of shook me up a little bit, because I couldn’t figure out what the hell was going on,” he told the audience, adding, “I went back to the doctor, and he said, ‘You are suffering from dehydration.’”

He was previously hospitalized in June after he fell ill onstage and abruptly ended his concert early at the Grand Casino Arena in St. Paul, Minnesota.

Videos shared online at the time showed Richie — who is co-headlining his 26-city North American tour with Earth, Wind & Fire — being forced to sit down several times while singing “Dancing on the Ceiling.”

“When you’re feeling dizzy, sit your ass down,” he told the crowd, before he left the concert altogether.
