‘RHONY’ star Daisy Toye talks makeup must-haves and getting skin care tips from Martha Stewart

2026-10-20 18:35

As Martha Stewart’s longtime makeup artist, Daisy Toye has had plenty of (literal) brushes with fame.

But now that she’s forging her own path on “Real Housewives of New York,” she told Page Six Style she’s been wearing “a lot” more makeup herself — and regretting it when she goes barefaced.

“Watching the shows back I’m like, “Sh—t ,why didn’t I do my makeup that day?” she quipped. “It’s definitely more fun being a ‘Housewife’ with makeup.”

She told us she’s picked up “a ton” of tips from the 85-year-old icon over the years — in addition to helping test out her Elm Biosciences brand’s “amazing” A30 Elemental Serum before it launched.

“[Martha] takes really great care of her skin,” she raved.

“She gets facials all the time. She’s really diligent about washing her face [and using] her serums. She’s my influencer.”
