Ciara pregnant with baby No. 5, her fourth with husband Russell Wilson

2026-09-06 23:51

The “One, Two Step” singer is pregnant with her fifth child, her fourth with her husband, Russell Wilson.

The couple announced the happy news on Instagram Sunday. They posted a video of her hanging up white t-shirts on a clothesline representing each member of their family, ending with her hanging up a onesie representing their incoming bundle of joy.

A visibly pregnant Ciara smiles, showing off her baby bump in a tank top with her hair up in rollers.

In another video, both she and the former NFL athlete playfully hold up five fingers.

“I guess,” Ciara cracked.

The two captioned the post, “One more person to Love 🖐🏽🥺🖤.”

The athlete asked Ciara about expanding their family further “shortly after” baby No. 4 joined the family.

“He was like … ‘So, cinco?’” Ciara recently explained to Us Weekly. “And I [was] like, ‘Wait a minute now, like, slow it down, sir. We just had a baby here. Let’s be in this moment here, sir.'”

She said — “in a laughing way” — that her husband’s ask was “disrespectful.”

As for her answer, Ciara told the outlet, “Listen, I will say this. I love being a mom. It’s my favorite job of all. And I couldn’t stand here and tell you, like, that I don’t see that happening one day in our lives to have cinco.”

“One of my all time favorite videos of you is ‘Ride,'” the NFL star, who retired in June, said at last year’s Grammy Museum event. “Speaking of riding, when is cinco coming?”

At the time, Ciara chided Wilson for what should have been “a private conversation.”

She told “Today” show viewers earlier that same year that she needed “a little time” before conceiving again, explaining, “My youngest is 1, so listen, there’s a trend with all of our babies –– they’re all three years apart. If it’s up to Russ, ‘cinco’ is coming next month.”

Sure enough, Wilson wrote via Instagram eight months after Amora’s birth that his wife looked “so crazy [good] she [was] ready to have another one.”
