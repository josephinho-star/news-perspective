Crimean Bridge attacked again, Russia says

2026-11-19 07:32

The Crimean Bridge has been attacked by Ukraine for the third time this week, Russia has said.

The Moscow-installed head of Crimea, Sergei Aksyonov, said that rail traffic was temporarily suspended following another strike on the bridge connecting Russia to occupied Crimea.

On Tuesday, Ukrainian missiles and drones destroyed the central road section of the critical bridge, collapsing part of it into the water.

The important infrastructure has long been targeted by Ukraine because of its significance as a logistical and symbolic asset to Moscow's war effort.

It comes as Russian President Vladimir Putin meets US President Donald Trump and China's President Xi Jinping on Thursday for landmark talks in China.

Aksyonov confirmed the strike on the bridge in the early hours of Thursday via Telegram.

Russia's transport ministry said nobody had been hurt in the attack, and that specialists were assessing damage to the bridge.

"As a result of a nighttime drone attack, train services on the railway section of the Crimean Bridge have been temporarily suspended," the ministry said in a statement on Telegram.

Meanwhile, Ukrainian sources have continued to issue air raid alert warnings overnight for districts just outside of the capital Kyiv.

At least four people were killed in a Russian drone attack on the southern city of Odesa on Wednesday, according to Odesa Governor Oleh Kiper. Officials described the attack as "brutal and deliberate... directly targeting people and their lives".

President Volodymyr Zelensky called the initial strikes on the Crimean Bridge an "unprecedented operation" weeks in the making.

Ukraine used drones and missiles against it on 17 November which brought down a key section of the road.

That section of the double-arched, 12-mile bridge now lies on the seabed, brought down by an arsenal of Ukrainian long-range weaponry in a seven-stage operation culminating in a dramatic collapse.

In response, Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov has said the bridge "will be rebuilt, and it will be even better than before".

While Ukrainian forces have struggled to close the bridge for good, recent attempts have brought them closer to destroying the bridge's crucial road and rail route for Russian soldiers heading towards the front line.

Russian forces have also targeted key bridge crossings over the Dnipro River in Kyiv in recent days.

Russian strikes hit the Pivnichnyi (Northern) and Pivdennyi (Southern) bridges causing traffic closures, partial damage to the metro system and some power and water outages.
