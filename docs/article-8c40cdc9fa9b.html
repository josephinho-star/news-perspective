Dua Lipa’s Chanel couture suit and statement hat divides fans: ‘Giving grandma’

2026-10-30 17:11

The “Houdini” singer’s cream Chanel skirt suit and frayed black hat got mixed reviews online after she stepped out at the WSJ. Magazine Innovator Awards on Thursday.

“Giving grandma,” one fan wrote, while another applauded, “Mary Poppins ate here.”

Chanel creative director Matthieu Blazy first showed the suit in July as part of his fall 2026 couture collection, where it was one of three scarecrow-inspired looks.

Lipa’s hat, made from black raffia crafted to look like straw, commanded the most attention. “Bird nest 🪺 🐣,” one commenter wrote, while another said it reminded them of the plastic grass tucked inside Easter baskets.

Vining embroidery and woolen botanical details covered the skirt and jacket, which fastened with purple pansy buttons. A safety pin decked out in pearls and chain was affixed to the front, and ornate black embellishments trimmed the collar and cuffs.

Stylist Lorenzo Posocco wrapped a Chanel metal and glass pearl necklace around the 31-year-old’s ankle, then added black satin pumps and a black handbag from the house.

Bulgari supplied the shine with diamond earrings from its high jewelry collection and a two-coil Serpenti Viper ring set with full pavé diamonds.

Social media users were quick with the comparisons. “Serving Emily Gilmore 🙌,” one fan wrote, as another called her “queen elizabeth if she was albanian and served.”

Others saw shades of Audrey Hepburn’s Eliza Doolittle in “My Fair Lady.”

Lipa has been a Chanel ambassador since starring in the house’s handbag campaign in 2025. For her June wedding to Callum Turner in Sicily, she wore a custom Blazy-designed couture gown embroidered with 480,000 beads.
