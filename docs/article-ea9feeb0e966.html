Citi launched a refreshed AAdvantage Executive Mastercard with $600+ in new benefits. Here’s how it compares to other options

2026-09-03 20:37

Citi is an advertising partner.

The card formerly known as the Citi® / AAdvantage® Executive World Elite Mastercard® has new benefits, a new name and an increased annual fee of $695.

Now known as the Citi® / AAdvantage® Executive World Legend Mastercard®, the card's core value remains the same despite the changes: Admirals Club lounge access. If you're interested in visiting American Airlines Admirals Club lounges, this card remains the most affordable option as the price of a paid full membership recently increased.

Let's take a look at what's changed and what's staying the same.

The Citi® / AAdvantage® Executive World Legend Mastercard® is one of the best ways to get access to American Airlines' network of Admirals Club® airport lounges.

- Access to nearly 100 Admirals Club® and partner airport lounges

- Global Entry or TSA PreCheck® application fee credit

- Additional fee for authorized users

- No bonus rewards outside of American Airlines purchases

Highlights shown here are provided by the issuer and have not been reviewed by CNBC Select's editorial staff.

- For a limited time, earn 125,000 American Airlines AAdvantage® bonus miles after $15,000 in purchases in the first 5 months of account opening.

- The only card with Admirals Club® membership. Membership includes access for you and up to two guests or immediate family members traveling with you.

- Global Entry® or TSA PreCheck® application fee credit of up to $120 every 4 years.

- Earn a 10,000 Loyalty Point bonus after reaching 50,000, 90,000, 165,000 and 240,000 Loyalty Points in the same status qualification year.

- First checked bag is free on domestic American Airlines itineraries for you and up to 8 companions traveling with you on the same reservation.

5% of each balance transfer; $5 minimum

While this card is only now adding World Legend to its name, it has been a World Legend Mastercard since last year, a Citi spokesperson told CNBC Select. You'll continue to have access to perks such as priority dining reservations and premium event tickets.

What is changing is the card's annual fee, which has increased by $100. Cardholders also now have access to over $600 in new annual credits, including:

- American Airlines Vacations credits: Up to $500 in statement credits every calendar year on eligible American Airlines Vacations travel packages or experiences

- Inflight and Admirals Club credits: Up to $100 in statement credits for inflight and eligible Admirals Club purchases every calendar year

- Lyft credits: A $15 Lyft credit after taking three eligible rides in that calendar month, for a total of up to $180 per year

Previously, you could earn up to $120 in Lyft credit every year; now you can earn an additional $60 in new Lyft credits annually.

You can also earn more AAdvantage miles and elite status credit (Loyalty Points). Eligible AAdvantage Hotels and AAdvantage Cars bookings now earn 12X AAdvantage miles instead of the previous 10X miles, and the card has new Loyalty Point milestones.

The refreshed Citi / AAdvantage Executive World Legend Mastercard now offers up to 40,000 bonus Loyalty Points per year, with the addition of 10,000-point bonuses for reaching 165,000 and 240,000 Loyalty Points in a status qualification year. Previously, you could earn up to 20,000 bonus Loyalty Points each status qualification year: 10,000 Loyalty Points once you reached 50,000 Loyalty Points and another 10,000 after reaching 90,000 Loyalty Points.

New rental car and hotel perks

If you're the primary cardholder of the Citi Executive Mastercard, you can enroll to receive complimentary Avis President's Club status. This is Avis' highest tier of status and includes perks such as double upgrades on weekends (when available) and guaranteed car availability for eligible bookings.

Primary cardholders can also enroll to receive complimentary Omni Hotels & Resorts Champion Status. This grants perks such as early check-in, late check-out and a complimentary upgrade when available. In addition, once per calendar year, you can receive an Omni Free Night Reward after completing a qualifying stay booked directly with Omni Hotels & Resorts (two-night minimum required).

The Citi / AAdvantage Executive World Legend Mastercard continues to earn AAdvantage miles at the following rates:

- 4X miles on eligible American Airlines purchases

- 5X miles on eligible American Airlines purchases for the remainder of the year after you spend $150,000 or more on your card in a calendar year

- 1X miles on all other purchases

Cardholders can also take advantage of over $400 in existing credits and continue to receive benefits such as Admirals Club access and free checked bags:

- Rental car credit: Up to $120 in statement credits for prepaid rental cars booked directly with Avis and Budget per calendar year

- Grubhub credit: Up to $120 in Grubhub credit ($10 per billing cycle) every 12 billing statements

- Trusted traveler program credit: Up to $120 in Global Entry or TSA PreCheck application fee credits every four years

- Admirals Club membership: Access to nearly 50 Admirals Club airport lounges (as well as partner lounges for the primary cardholder) for you and your immediate family or two guests (a value of up to $850); a same-day boarding pass for an eligible American Airlines flight is required

- First checked bag free: You and up to eight companions on the same reservation will receive your first checked bag free on domestic American Airlines itineraries (normally $45 to $55 per first checked bag)

- Priority privileges: Enjoy priority check-in, screening and boarding (Group 4) when traveling with American Airlines

- 25% savings on eligible inflight purchases

- No foreign transaction fees

The Citi Executive Mastercard makes the most sense for anyone who would otherwise pay cash for an Admiral Club airport lounge membership or those who are extremely loyal to American Airlines.

Even with the $100 annual fee increase, paying for this card is still much cheaper than shelling out for a lounge membership. On Aug. 23, 2026, American Airlines revamped its Admirals Club memberships, which now cost:

- Admirals Club membership (up to two complimentary guests and to access partner lounges): $1,200 to $1,400, or 120,000 to 140,000 miles, based on your elite status level

- Single membership (no complimentary guests): $750 or 75,000 miles

Primary cardholders receive a full Admirals Club membership with complimentary access to partner lounges for themselves and eligible family members or up to two guests. You can also add up to three authorized users for $175 a year (additional authorized users cost $175 a year each). Every authorized user receives Admirals Club access (no partner lounge access) for themselves and eligible family members or up to two guests.

The cost of adding authorized users is staying the same, which makes adding cardmembers an even better deal than before.

If the Citi AAdvantage Executive card isn't a great fit for you, there are alternatives that offer airport lounge access, AAdvantage miles and American Airlines elite status credit.

The Citi® / AAdvantage® Platinum Select® World Elite Mastercard® has a much smaller $99 annual fee, which is waived for the first year. It also provides a free first checked bag on domestic American Airlines flights for the primary cardholder and up to four companions on the same reservation. You'll also earn AAdvantage miles on every purchase and 1 Loyalty Point per dollar spent on purchases.

The Citi® / AAdvantage® Platinum Select® World Elite Mastercard® is worth considering if you fly with American Airlines once or twice a year because you'll get your first checked bag free on domestic American Airlines itineraries.

- First checked bag free on eligible American Airlines flights for the cardholder and up to four travel companions on the same reservation

- No foreign transaction fees

- American Airlines Flight Discount has a high spending requirement

The Citi Strata Elite℠ Card has fewer American Airlines-specific perks, but it earns flexible Citi ThankYou® Points, which can be transferred to American Airlines and many other airlines and hotels. You'll also get access to Priority Pass airport lounges and four Admirals Club passes every calendar year.

The Citi Strata Elite℠ Card offers luxury travel benefits and heightened rewards on travel and restaurant purchases — while still earning 1.5X points on all other purchases.

- Exceptional rewards rate on travel booked through cititravel.com

- Earns flexible points that transfer to popular airline and hotel partners, like American Airlines

- Features roughly $1,500 in value through statement credits, airport lounge access and more

- $75 annual fee for each authorized user

- Limited rewards categories

Highlights shown here are provided by the issuer and have not been reviewed by CNBC Select's editorial staff.

- Earn 75,000 bonus Points after spending $6,000 in the first 3 months of account opening.

- Earn 12 Points per $1 spent on Hotels, Car Rentals, and Attractions booked on cititravel.com and 6 Points per $1 spent on Air Travel booked on cititravel.com

- Earn 6 Points per $1 spent at Restaurants including Restaurant Delivery Services on CitiNights℠ purchases, every Friday and Saturday from 6 PM to 6 AM ET. Earn 3 Points per $1 spent any other time

- Earn 1.5 Points per $1 spent on All Other Purchases

- Up to $300 Annual Hotel Benefit: Each calendar year, enjoy up to $300 off a hotel stay of two nights or more when booked through cititravel.com.

- No Foreign Transaction Fees
