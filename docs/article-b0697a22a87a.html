Taylor Red country singer Nicole Taylor dead at 35, her triplet sisters reveal

2026-11-03 12:58

In a heartbreaking YouTube video, Nika and Natalie explained that their big sister passed away and is “at peace and no longer suffering.”

“We are still processing everything. This honestly feels really hard to talk about, but we’re going to do our best in sharing some of her story,” Natalie said in the clip.

“She was diagnosed with breast cancer back in 2022,” she added, saying they found out the shocking news while they were out in a “public place.”

Nika said Nicole, both 35, later found out she had Stage 3 breast cancer that was “very aggressive.”

“She pushed us all to have fun and not live in fear and hopelessness and she even did CrossFit during the time she was getting chemo treatments,” Natalie shared.

“And I remember one day specifically at church; it was soon after the diagnosis. She was prayed over and God gave her so much peace after that, she said. She said that peace stayed with her so long. I think that’s what kept her so fierce during this time,” she added.

The sisters also created a page in Nicole’s memory, on which they revealed she died on Sept. 28.

“Nicole, the oldest of us three sisters, will be greatly missed. She was our leader and our best friend,” Nika and Natalie wrote in a statement.

“We are heartbroken, but we know she is in Heaven and will see her again,” the influencers added.

Nika and Natalie also revealed that they have already held a private funeral for Nicole.

The red-haired identical triplets grew up playing instruments like the guitar, banjo, fiddle and mandolin from childhood.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, the trio — who was originally named Red Roots — began posting highly synchronized comedy skits, transition videos and musical challenges on TikTok and YouTube.

They grew their online presence, garnering more than 13 million TikTok followers and 16 million YouTube subscribers.

The Taylor Red singers also built brand partnerships with several brands such as Sonic Drive-In, Crocs, Lipton and Skittles.
