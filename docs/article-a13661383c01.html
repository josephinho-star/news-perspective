Kelly Osbourne ‘couldn’t eat,’ relapsed after dad Ozzy’s death: ‘Broken in every way’

2026-09-15 20:35

“I lost my mind. I was a shell of myself,” Kelly, 41, told host Paul C. Brunson. “I couldn’t speak. I couldn’t eat. I couldn’t do anything.”

“I was just empty, broken in every way a human being could possibly be broken, and completely unable to take care of myself.”

The “Osbournes” alum said she hit “ground zero” after her father’s death and ultimately began drinking again, admitting she “couldn’t handle” the loss.

“I can honestly say, today I’m eight months sober,” Kelly added. “I have the most incredible people around me. I closed my circle because I lost a lot of friends. People don’t like sadness, and they expected me to be over it.”

“And I will never be over it. I will never, ever be over it,” she concluded.

“He sat me down and said, ‘Tots, I need you to know something. No matter what happens in this life, you can live the rest of your life knowing that there was one man on this planet [who] truly loved you, [who] truly loved you for every single thing you are. And that man is me,” she said.

Kelly said she began crying and begged her father not to “talk like this.”

She recalled that he responded, “You know, I’m not going to be here forever, and I know you struggle with people … believing that you are loved, but I love you more than anyone.”

The former “Fashion Police” co-host previously spoke about her weight loss following her father’s death, telling people who commented, “Get off Ozempic” to “f–k off.”

Ozzy also shared daughter Jessica with Riley, and adopted her son, Elliot Kingsley.

Kelly broke her silence in the days following her father’s death, writing on her Instagram Stories, “I feel unhappy. I am so sad. I lost the best friend I ever had 💔.”

Her message seemingly alluded to “Changes,” the 1972 Black Sabbath song Ozzy co-wrote and performed with the band.
