Jax Taylor posts ‘graphic’ photo of his tumor after undergoing cancer surgery

2026-11-20 01:43

Jax Taylor shared a “graphic” photo of his tumor after undergoing surgery on his kidney amid his cancer battle.

“I know this is a bit graphic, but I can’t tell you how much I am relieved this is out of me,” he wrote via his Instagram Stories Thursday.

“Hoping they have gotten it all.”

Taylor, 47, candidly posted his tumor after he went public with his cancer diagnosis hours prior.

“This is something I’ve kept private for the past few months because it’s been one of the scariest times of my life,” the “Vanderpump Rules” alum wrote alongside a photo of himself in a hospital bed.

The reality star reflected on initially going to the hospital in July, believing he had kidney stones.

However, the visit “turned into something [he] never imagined.”

“My CT scan also showed a tumor on my left kidney,” Taylor added. “After many tests and appointments, I found out it was cancer.”

The Bravo star explained the tumor, which was “a lot larger than [doctors] thought,” had been removed after a five-hour surgery, and they were “waiting on the pathology results.”

Taylor reflected on how life can change in an instant, saying that he “probably wouldn’t have known about [the] tumor” if he didn’t get checked out for kidney stones.

Additionally, the Bravolebrity stated that his father, Ronald Cauchi, died from cancer in 2017 at the age of 61.

He also gave a special shout-out to Lori Krebs, his “amazing girlfriend,” for standing by his side as his “rock,” saying how he was “so insanely lucky to have her in my life.”

Taylor started dating Krebs after his split from his ex-wife, Brittany Cartwright. The exes parted ways in January 2024 and have yet to finalize their contentious divorce.

However, they reached a custody agreement for their son, Cruz, 5, agreeing that they are responsible for his expenses while in their individual care.

Taylor also has custody of his son on Wednesday nights and every other weekend, while Cruz is with Cartwright for the remainder of the time.
