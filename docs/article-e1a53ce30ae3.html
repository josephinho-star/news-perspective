Under $20 for Anua’s toner pads? We’re switching from Medicube for a hot second

2026-10-20 13:41

I love me a good toner pad, and Anua’s Heartleaf 77 Toner Pads might just be the affordable K-beauty find that has me switching things up from Medicube for a hot second.

If there’s one step in my skincare routine that makes me feel like I have my life together, it’s swiping on a refreshing toner pad after cleansing. And if you know me, you know I’m always down to try the latest and greatest in Korean beauty. So when a product promises smoother-looking skin, a refreshing boost, and an easy addition to my routine, I’m listening. Especially when it’s under $20.

Enter the Anua Heartleaf 77 Toner Pads, a K-beauty staple worth adding to your skincare radar.

First things first: the formula. These pads feature 77% heartleaf extract, an ingredient loved in Korean skincare for its soothing properties. If my skin is feeling a little stressed after cleansing, I can see why a calming toner step would be appealing.

The formula also contains polyhydroxy acid (PHA), a gentle exfoliating ingredient that helps remove dead skin cells and improve the appearance of skin texture. Translation? These aren’t just about giving my face a quick refresh. They’re designed to help skin look smoother, too.

And let’s talk about the dual-textured pads, because I love a product that does a little extra. One side features an embossed texture for gently sweeping across the skin, while the smoother side can be used to deliver an extra dose of hydration. It’s a simple two-in-one approach that makes my post-cleanse routine feel a little more elevated without adding five new steps.

Don’t get me wrong: I’m not breaking up with Medicube. But skincare is all about finding what works for my skin, and I’m always interested in exploring alternatives that don’t require a major investment. That’s where Anua comes in.

With 70 pads per jar, the Heartleaf 77 formula offers plenty of opportunities to work this step into my morning or evening routine. At less than $20 when discounted, it’s also a more budget-friendly way to explore another K-beauty favorite.

If you’re as obsessed with toner pads as I am, Anua’s Heartleaf 77 Toner Pads deserve a spot on your shortlist. Between the heartleaf-focused formula, gentle exfoliation and convenient dual-sided design, there’s plenty to like about this K-beauty find.

I’m always here for a skincare upgrade that feels refreshing without wrecking my budget. And if I can save a few dollars while keeping my routine firmly in its K-beauty era? Even better.

What are the key active ingredients in Anua Heartleaf 77 Clear Pads?

They feature 77% Heartleaf extract to soothe irritation, mild PHA (Gluconolactone) for gentle exfoliation and Centella Asiatica. They are free of alcohol and artificial fragrance.

How do I properly use the dual-textured pad?

Wipe the embossed side across your cleansed face to clear dead skin. Flip to the smooth side to hydrate or leave it on dry areas for three to five minutes as a mask.

How often should I use these clear pads on dry skin?

Start by using them two to three times per week. Monitor your skin comfort before gradually increasing usage to daily AM and PM routines.

Will these exfoliating pads dry out or strip my skin?

No, gentle PHAs have humectant properties that draw moisture into the skin while exfoliating, making them ideal for hydrating dry skin without stripping.

Do these pads replace my regular serum and moisturizer?

No, these pads function as an exfoliating toner step. Always follow up with a rich moisturizer to properly lock in hydration.

Can I use these pads with retinoids or vitamin C?

Combining PHA with strong active ingredients in the same routine may cause redness. Alternate usage between morning and evening or use them on separate days.
