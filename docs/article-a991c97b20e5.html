Far right’s rise in rapidly re-arming Germany gives Poland cause for concern

2026-09-08 17:32

Polish Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski warned that the Alternative for Germany party's electoral gains could turn Berlin’s military buildup into a security risk for Europe, even as Warsaw backs a stronger German army.

The far-right party won a record 43.8 percent in Sunday’s Saxony-Anhalt election, more than doubling its 2021 result. Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s center-right Christian Democratic Union crashed to 17.2 percent, its worst-ever result in the eastern state.

“We want Germany to play a strong role in securing our common security under democratic leadership and as Europe’s largest economy,” Sikorski told German outlet Handelsblatt in an interview published Tuesday.
