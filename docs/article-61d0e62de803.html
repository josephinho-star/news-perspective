Nolte: Judge Says Trump’s $15B Libel Suit Against New York Times Can Proceed

2026-11-20 20:31

There’s nothing like heading into the weekend with the wind of a libel suit against the fake media at your back.

“A federal judge is allowing President Donald Trump to move forward with part of a $15 billion libel lawsuit he filed last year against [the far-left] New York Times, several of its [activist] reporters and a major publishing house over reporting on his business and financial dealings,” reports the also far-left Politico.

The judge ruled that “some of the statements suggesting Trump had likely committed ‘complex’ tax crimes appeared to have been based on insufficient input from those with expertise to make such assessments.”

No shit.

Two New York Times reporters made these false allegations in a book called Lucky Loser. The sleazy Times then used the book as a source to republish the allegations on its “””“news”“”” pages.

“Both sides are likely to ask [U.S. District Judge Steven] Merryday to rule in their favor after discovery is complete. If neither side prevails, a jury trial is possible.”

Tee hee.
