49ers’ Kaelon Black manifested NFL future with Kyle Shanahan

2026-11-21 23:53

Kaelon Black is making a name for himself by putting together a strong rookie year for the 49ers, proving to be a perfect supporting piece to Christian McCaffrey.

Black was the lead back for the national champion Indiana Hoosiers last year, but he always had his mind on one thing.

Playing in the NFL and playing for a Shanahan.

On Thursday, he appeared on the NFL Network show “Good Morning Football” and told host Jamie Erdahl that he wrote in a book as a kid that he would play in the NFL under a Shanahan.

“It’s funny because I did,” Black said. “I was probably in like fourth grade in Washington at the time, and at the time, Mike Shanahan was the coach and I was saying, like, ‘I’m going to be the first overall pick drafted by Mike Shanahan. I’m going to play with [Robert Griffin III]’ and all this other stuff. I was one of those kids who just dreamed big, and I knew one day that I was going to be in the NFL.”

While Black had to watch former Hoosiers teammate Fernando Mendoza be selected with the No. 1 overall pick, the 25-year-old running back was taken by San Francisco in the third round and has found himself a role in the NFL.

This year, Black has 79 carries for 356 yards and two touchdowns — both happened over the 49ers’ last three games.

In last week’s 46-40 win against the Cowboys, Black had one of his most impressive runs of his young NFL career, when he ran a 25-yard touchdown down the field and started it by running through Caleb Downs, the No. 11 overall pick in the 2026 draft, when he lowered his shoulder on the Ohio State product.

Black then got himself back into position and immediately shoved Cowboys cornerback Cobie Durant to the ground to finish the play.

Black’s championship-caliber DNA has transitioned to the NFL. The 49ers enter Week 11 with an 8-1 record — the NFC’s best — and appear to be true Super Bowl contenders.
