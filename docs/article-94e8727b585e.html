Beau Lamarre-Condon ‘executed’ Jesse Baird and Luke Davies then lied about it, prosecution argues

2026-11-23 00:54

Beau Lamarre-Condon is a “proven and persistent liar” and the evidence in his double murder trial shows the deaths of Jesse Baird and Luke Davies could only have been an “execution”, a court has been told.

Lamarre-Condon is accused of fatally shooting the Sydney couple with his police service weapon in Baird’s Paddington home on 19 February 2024.

The former officer has pleaded not guilty to two counts of murder and one charge of aggravated break and enter, claiming the two men died while he acted in “self-defence”.

Crown prosecutor Brett Hatfield SC urged the jury to reject Lamarre-Condon’s account and convict him on all three counts, as he wrapped up his closing statement on Monday.

Driven by his jealous obsession, the accused drove to Paddington in a hired car, waited for his opportunity, entered the house with a gun loaded with stolen bullets, and shot Baird in the back and temple, Hatfield alleged.

“It was not self-defence or an accident,” he told the New South Wales supreme court.

“The evidence speaks only to an execution.”

Lamarre-Condon had testified he shot Baird after he lunged at him with a knife.

During this struggle, his gun was then flung across the room and picked up by Davies before the officer grabbed his arm and pushed the gun towards Davies.

Davies then pulled the trigger and shot himself, Lamarre-Condon told the court.

But this defence rests entirely on his own account and even if Baird had picked up a knife, it would not have assisted him against a glock, Hatfield said.

“You might think he is a proven and persistent liar and such a scenario is completely contrary to the weight of the evidence,” he told the court.

“The accused’s account is not the truth. It is a story made up after the fact in an attempt to meet the evidence.”

The trial before Justice Natalie Adams continues.
