What Time Does ‘Lanterns’ Episode 5 Come Out?

2026-09-13 15:00

HBO‘s Lanterns is a gritty new take on the Green Lantern comic books. Long-time Green Lantern Hal Jordan (Kyle Chandler) has been tasked with training his “backup,” John Stewart (Aaron Pierre), whom he naturally intuits is his replacement. When Hal gets a mysterious message from his “Halogram,” a holographic version of himself, to investigate Rushville, he heads there and jumps into the middle of a murder mystery. While at first it appears strange that aliens were gunned down during a high school football game, things get weirder when John learns that a Manhunter is hiding in the rural American town.

Lanterns dropped a doozy of a twist last week, revealing that the Manhunter lurking in Rushville was none other than the beguiling Zoe (Poorna Jagannathan)! You know, the hot married lady that John Stewart has been hooking up with this whole time! So now that Hal Jordan and John Stewart know what they’re up against, how will they neutralize the threat? Will they be able to finally work together? And why does Hal keep setting John up to magically survive car crashes?

Here’s everything you need to know about when the next episode of Lanterns comes out on HBO and HBO Max…

Is Lanterns on Tonight? (9/13/26)

Yes, a new episode of Lanterns will be on HBO tonight — Sunday, September 13.

Lanterns Episode 5 Release Date:

Lanterns Episode 5 will premiere on HBO and HBO Max on Sunday, September 13.

What Time Does Lanterns Episode 5 Come Out?

Lanterns Episode 5 comes out on HBO and HBO Max at 9 PM ET on Sunday, September 13.

How Many Episodes are in Lanterns? Lanterns Episode Guide:

There are a total of eight episodes in the first season of Lanterns. New episodes of the superhero drama will premiere on Sundays between now and October 4, 2026.

Here’s your guide to when each and every episode of Lanterns comes out:

- Episode 1 “Pilot”: Sunday, August 16

- Episode 2 “Trust Fall”: Sunday, August 23

- Episode 3 “OutKast”: Sunday, August 30

- Episode 4 “The Weenie”: Sunday, September 6

- Episode 5: Sunday, September 13

- Episode 6: Sunday, September 20

- Episode 7: Sunday, September 27

- Episode 8: Sunday, October 4

Will There Be a Lanterns Season 2?

We don’t know yet! There might not be a second season of Lanterns, but we do know that Aaron Pierre’s John Stewart is scheduled to appear in next summer’s Superman sequel, Man of Tomorrow.
