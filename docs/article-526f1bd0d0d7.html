Momcozy invention solves one of the ickiest parts of parenting — It’s on sale for Prime Day

2026-10-06 12:30

New parents face all sorts of challenges — from spit-up and crying spells to sleepless nights and tricky feeding positions — but if there’s one of the most widely accepted (and, let’s be honest, ickiest) parts of parenting, it’s probably diaper duty.

According to a review published in the National Library of Medicine, one child is estimated to use between 4,600 and 4,800 disposable diapers during the first three years of life. That’s a whole lot of diapers, and a whole lot of waste.

And, that’s exactly where companies like Momcozy come in. The premium baby-care brand has designed a smart diaper pail meant to make the disposal process much easier. And for October Prime Day, running Oct. 6 through Oct. 7, the brand is offering 20% off this genius contraption, so new parents can snag the popular pail for just $72.

The Momcozy Triple-Seal Diaper Pail features patented triple-seal odor control to help lock in diaper odors from drop-off through bag removal. Many reviewers agree the popular pail is thoughtfully designed, stylish and truly odor-free.

“I’ve been using the Momcozy Diaper Pail for a while now, and honestly, it’s one of those products that makes everyday parenting noticeably easier,” one user shared. “From its design to its performance, it feels like every detail was created with real parents in mind.”

Below, find out what sets this pail apart from some other designs — and browse more Momcozy deals that support every stage of the parenting journey.

The Momcozy Triple-Seal Diaper Pail is designed to make every diaper disposal easier and more hygienic. It fits standard 13-gallon trash bags for simple use, and the rotating handle and extra-wide opening allow for easy one-handed disposal (no bending over required).

Bag removal is just as simple. The bag opening stays clamped and lifts with the lid, keeping messes and odors contained during removal. Plus, a child-safety lock helps to keep everything secure — even if little hands try to take a look at what’s inside.

With a 22-liter capacity, the pail can hold up to 60 newborn diapers at a time, so you won’t have to empty it throughout the day. A transparent viewing panel also lets parents quickly check the fill level.

The sleek and simple design also helps the pail blend in with other nursery furniture, making it an easy addition to your little one’s set-up. Bag refills and deodorizer can also be purchased separately.

What is Prime Big Deal Days and when is it?

Prime Big Deal Days — fondly known as October Prime Day — is Amazon’s biggest fall sale, offering massive discounts before the holiday rush of Black Friday and Cyber Monday. October Prime Day runs from Tuesday, Oct. 6 to Wednesday, Oct. 7, 2026.

Prime Day vs. Prime Big Deal Days

Prime Day and Prime Big Deal Days are both Prime member-exclusive seasonal sales with massive discounts on rotating bestsellers. Unlike July’s Prime Day, Prime Big Deal Days runs every October as a precursor to the busy holiday season, allowing shoppers to start saving on gift shopping early.

Do you have to be an Amazon Prime member to shop?

October Prime Day is exclusively available to Prime members. However interested shoppers can grab a 30-day free trial to try Prime, and its many benefits with no strings attached, and gain access to exclusive deals and the convenience of fast, free shipping.
