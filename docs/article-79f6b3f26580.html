Horrifying moment shooting breaks out at Airbnb rented for music video in California

2026-10-27 03:04

A wild rap video shoot at a California Airbnb exploded into a chaotic shooting battle early Sunday after uninvited guests were denied entry.

The shooting happened at 2:24 a.m. near Fulton Street and Dennett Avenue, in the Tower District of Fresno, where at least 10 rounds were fired.

“Officers determined a studio in the area was used to film a rap video. Several individuals who were denied entry left the location and fired multiple rounds, striking the exterior of a business on Fulton Ave. It appears there was an exchange of gunfire,” the Fresno Police Department said.

“No injuries have been reported, and the investigation remains ongoing. Our investigation will include looking into any prior calls for service at that location,” the department added on.

The surveillance video shows two groups arguing in front of a home before one person opened fire, resulting in two businesses being damaged.

One business shop owner arrived at her nail studio on Sunday and found a bullet on the floor and a bullet hole in the wall. Another business also had its window boarded up according to ABC 30.

People were celebrating a “zombie crawl” in the area, a Halloween-themed bar crawl, when police dispatched in the area heard nine gunshots being fire outside MIA Nightclub.

It also comes after Fresno Mayor Jerry Dyer announced new safety measures on October 21 by increasing police patrols and having brighter street lighting.

“We don’t want any violence… we’re all in this together, and we have zero tolerance for violence in the Tower,” the mayor said.

Just last year at this time, there were 128 shooting incidents in Fresno, but there have already been 132 incidents so far this year.
