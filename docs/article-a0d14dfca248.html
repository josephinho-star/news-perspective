Scott Morrow making long-awaited Rangers impression — and he’s not done yet

2026-11-26 01:02

BUFFALO — The last time Scott Morrow faced the Hurricanes, the organization that drafted him No. 40 overall in 2021 and traded him to New York in July 2025, the young defenseman had already been up and down from the minors and in and out of the Rangers lineup multiple times through the first three months of last season.

He registered just one hit through 13:55 of ice time in the 3-2 loss Dec. 29, 2025, one of many games where Morrow struggled to make any sort of impact in his first season with the Blueshirts.
