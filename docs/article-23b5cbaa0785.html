Where to watch the postponed Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade 2026 for free

2026-11-28 09:30

Thanksgiving isn’t over just yet.

At 8:30 a.m. ET, the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will begin its long-awaited march across Midtown New York City.

The Parade was postponed from its usual Thursday holiday slot due to severe weather conditions.

Watch the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade for free

- When: Nov. 28, 8:30 a.m. ET

- Where: Midtown (New York, New York)

- How to watch for free: DIRECTV (five-day free trial)

“I am still shocked at it, to be honest with you,” said participating Pearland High School band’s director Ryan Demkovich.

Big names joining Pearland in the televised stroll include household name pop stars RAYE, Megan Moroney and Teddy Swims along with NBC regulars Jimmy Fallon and The Roots.

This year’s Broadway musical medley features performers from “The Lost Boys,” “The Rocky Horror Show,” “Wicked,” “Schmigadoon!,” “Aladdin” and more.

Notable floats on display include fan favorites Bugs Bunny, Scooby-Doo, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles as well as a number of others.

Mayor Zohran Mamdani will not be in attendance. The Post wrote he’ll miss the Parade “because of upcoming surgery over his torn Achilles tendon.”

Weather-wise, the lack of rain should be the real star of the morning. AccuWeather says “[it will be] breezy; considerable cloudiness in the morning… dry for the parade.”

Prior to this year’s postponement, the last time the Parade was altered was in 2020. Although the event went off without a hitch, no audience was allowed because of COVID-19.

Who is performing at the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade?

A number of big-time talents.

In addition to the artists and Broadway shows listed above, Natasha Bedingfield, Queen Latifah, Natalie Merchant, DEVO, Leslie Odom Jr. and many, many more will be on hand.

What will the weather be like at the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade?

Significantly better than it was on Thursday.

CBS reports “the highs will be in the low 50s and the lows will be in the mid-40s, with partly cloudy skies. With the threshold being 34 mph, and current forecasted gusts between 20 and 25 mph, the balloons should be able to fly without a hitch.”

What will be different about this year’s Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade?

Well, for starters, it’s taking place on a Saturday for the first time in its centennial year.

- 100 balloons will set a record for the most in the Parade’s 100-year history.

- Joe Cool Snoopy, Bluey, Hello Kitty, and Sorcerer’s Apprentice Mickey balloons will all make their debut.

- Underdog, who was part of the Parade from 1965-84, returns for the first time in over 40 years.

- The long-lost Blue Hippopotamus balloon who last appeared in 1931 before disappearing is back as well.

What route is the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade taking?

After kicking off the festivities at 76th Street and Central Park West, The Parade will head southbound to Columbus Circle before going east to 6th Avenue and finally down to 34th Street.

For a clearer picture, we recommend taking a look at Macy’s official Parade route map.

Which floats and balloons will be at the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade?

The full lineup of balloons for the 100th parade has yet to be revealed by Macy’s but they did tease that a record-breaking 100 (!) inflatables will float down Manhattan for the event.

A few of the most iconic set to appear include Parade newbies Bluey, Hello Kitty, “Joe Cool” Snoopy and Sorcerer’s Apprentice Mickey, all of whom are making their debut.

Yes, Snoopy and Mickey have appeared before but never as Joe Cool or the Sorcerer’s Apprentice.

According to NPR, “there’s an All Star float carrying beloved inflatable characters like Bugs Bunny, Popeye, Bullwinkle, Sonic the Hedgehog and the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles.”

And, don’t forget that a Harry Potter-themed float topped with the Sorting Hat will be making the rounds as well.
