Will There Be A ‘Chad Powers’ Season 3 On Hulu?

2026-09-04 20:30

No spoilers, but Chad Powers Season 2, Episode 6, “12th Quarter,” leaves off on another delicious cliffhanger (and in this case, facehanger — IYKYK) that will have fans of Hulu‘s hit football comedy begging for more.

The question is, are more new episodes of Chad Powers coming? Is this the end of our time with Chad/Russ (Glen Powell), Ricky (Perry Mattfeld), Danny (Frankie A. Rodriguez), Coach Hudson (Steve Zahn), Tricia (Wynn Everett), Gerry (Colton Ryan) and the South Georgia Catfish? What does the future hold for Chad and Ricky? What did Coach Hudson see in that final scene? And can the team win a national championship?

Curious how many episodes are in Chad Powers Season 2? Will there be an Episode 7 of Chad Powers Season 2, or is Episode 6 the Chad Powers Season 2 finale? And if so, will there be a Chad Powers Season 3? We’ve got answers!

Read on to learn more about the future of Chad Powers, including Season 2’s episode count, Season 3 info, and more.

How Many Episodes Are In Chad Powers Season 2?

Like Season 1, Chad Powers Season 2 has six episodes. But this time, all six episodes dropped on Hulu at once — a shift from Season 1’s weekly release schedule.

Will There Be A Chad Powers Season 2, Episode 7?

Nope! We know it’s not ideal for a half-hour comedy to have such a short season, but Chad Powers Season 2 only has six episodes, not seven, not eight, not 10. That means Season 2, Episode 6, “12TH QUARTER,” was the Season 2 finale. Read on for Season 3 info!

Will There Be A Season 3 of Chad Powers on Hulu? Chad Powers Season 3 Renewal Status:

As of Season 2’s premiere date, Hulu had yet to officially renew Chad Powers for a third season, but that’s not to say it won’t happen. Co-creator Michael Waldron has said he and Powell have ideas for three seasons, so if given the opportunity to make more episodes, Waldron, Powell, and the team would love to continue!

While Season 1 got a 60% Tomatometer rating on Rotten Tomatoes, Season 2 got a perfect, Certified Fresh 100%. So it’s clear that critics (including Decider) loved the shift in tone, the twists and turns of Season 2, and those major cliffhangers that brilliantly tee up a third season. Season 1’s finale aired in October 2025, and Season 2’s renewal wasn’t announced until December 2025. So Season 3’s status may take a few months, but we have faith that Chad Powers will make another comeback. Stay tuned for updates!

When Does Chad Powers Season 3 Come Out? Chad Powers Season 3 Release Date Info:

Since Chad Powers hasn’t been officially renewed yet, there’s no Chad Powers Season 3 premiere date on the books right now. But after Season 1, Waldron told DECIDER that he and Powell would never want to make fans wait multiple years for another six-episode season.

“We want to make it on time, on schedule,” Waldron explained. “I don’t want this to be one of the shows that people have to wait forever for. There’s only it’s only six half-hour episodes. It’s not a long thing. We don’t want to withhold any longer than we have to.”

So if Chad Powers Season 2 is greenlit promptly, it sounds like fans could expect new episodes in 2027.

Where To Watch Chad Powers: Hulu Streaming Info

While we wait for new episodes of Chad Powers, you can stream Season 1 on Hulu.

If you’re new to Hulu, the basic (with ads) price is $11.99/month. If you want to upgrade to Hulu ad-free, it costs $18.99/month.

If you want to stream even more and save a few bucks a month while you’re at it, we recommend subscribing to one of the Disney+ Bundles, all of which include Hulu. These bundles start at $12.99/month for ad-supported Disney+ and Hulu and goes up to $32.99/month for Disney+, Hulu, and Max, all ad-free.
