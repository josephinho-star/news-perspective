Five killed as Amazon cargo plane crashes at Miami airport

2026-09-07 05:37

Video footage from Miami shows an Amazon Air cargo plane overshoot the runway while attempting to land at the city's international airport, killing five people and seriously injuring five more.

The Boeing 767-300 cargo aircraft crashed just before 14:00 local time (19:00 GMT) after departing from Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport in San Juan, Puerto Rico. The flight also hit several vehicles on the ground.
