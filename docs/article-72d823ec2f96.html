Kylie Minogue, 58, wows in yellow string bikini on European vacation

2026-09-05 06:34

Kylie Minogue is farewelling the Euro summer in the most appropriate of ways: in a yellow bikini.

The 58-year-old singer soaked up the sun as she wrapped up her holiday before heading back home to Australia to perform at the AFL Grand Final later this month.

Sharing a clip on Instagram, the Aussie actress looked incredible in her yellow string bikini as she reclined on a sun lounger with a straw hat and silky sarong.

“Bye Euro Summer,” she captioned the clip as she peeked from beneath her hat. “️Hello Aussie football szn.”

In a major coup for the AFL, Minogue will lead headline the pre-game entertainment at the Melbourne Cricket Ground on September 26.

The two-time Grammy winner will be the first Australian act to perform at the sporting event since Eskimo Joe in 2021, when the grand final was played in Perth.

“The AFL Grand Final at Melbourne’s one and only, forever iconic MCG? Yes please!” Minogue said back in March when the announcement was made.

“As a Melburnian, I’m so excited to be coming home for the biggest day in the Aussie sporting calendar.

“I’ll not only be performing, but I’ll be one of 100,000 fans at the ’G, watching the wonder that is footy.”

Musical acts who have performed at the grand final in previous years have included Robbie Williams, Kiss, Katy Perry and, most recently, Snoop Dogg.

Minogue’s video caps off her recent Euro getaway during which she shared videos and photos of herself poolside or seeing the sights.

It appears her sister, Dannii Minogue, even joined her for the Paris leg of her travels with the pair hilariously recreating a photo they took under the Eiffel Tower almost 40 years ago.

“OK, we’re here in Paris by the iconic Eiffel Tower and we’re gonna attempt to recreate an iconic photograph from 1988. Can you do it?” she asked her young sister in the video shared on Instagram.

“My goodness, I don’t even know if I can pull this face anymore but let’s try,” Dannii, 54, replied.

The siblings then got into their positions and got their shot, 40 years in the making.

“Sisters in Paris and just HAD to recreate this vintage family pic. One frame then. One frame now!!! Nailed it!! LOVE THIS SO MUCH!!” Kylie gushed.
