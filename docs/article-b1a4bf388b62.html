‘Crew Girl’ Cast: Meet The Stars of Netflix’s New YA Romance Series

2026-09-11 14:30

Crew Girl has finally arrived on Netflix, and the cast of the new YA romance is ready to row their way into your heart.

Warning! Spoilers ahead for Crew Girl, now streaming on Netflix.

The eight-episode series follows Teagan Tao (Miku Martineau), a 16-year-old rower in Los Angeles, whose life is completely shaken up when her hotelier father (Byron Lawson) is exposed for defrauding his investors. When Teagan and her mother Ella (Jessica Paré) move back to Ella’s hometown of Eagle’s Cove, Massachusetts, adjustments await for the both of them—especially when Teagan discovers that her new school, Easton Prep, does not have a women’s crew team.

Fortunately, Teagan impresses Coach Matt Hayden (Thomas Cadrot), and ultimately bests the team’s current coxswain in a race to land the spot at the head of the boat. However, she must also win over her teammates.

Wonder who else stars in Crew Girl, and where you might have seen them before? Check out our Crew Girl cast guide, and head over to Netflix to watch Crew Girl now.

Miku Martineau as Teagan Tao

Miku Martineau stars as 16-year-old Teagan Tao, who after moving to Eagle’s Cove, Massachusetts from Los Angeles, California due to her father’s fraud scandal, lands a spot as the coxswain on the men’s crew team at her new school, Easton Prep. In this role at the head of the boat, she must win over her teammates’ respect.

Martineau can also be seen in the Netflix series Bet, which is set to return for a sophomore season that wrapped production in August, per What’s On Netflix.

Want to learn more? Follow Martineau on Instagram at @miku.martineau.

Kyle Clark as Cam Dillinger

Cam Dillinger (played by Kyle Clark) is a kindhearted, hardworking Eagle’s Cove native, who when not in class or at crew practice, is helping his father at his auto repair shop. Despite his teammates calling him FAID (referring to the fact that he is on financial aid at Easton Prep), he is a core member of the boat, and is one of the first of Teagan’s teammates to back her when she becomes coxswain.

Clark has appeared in Riverdale and The Baby-Sitters Club, in addition to the 2024 Lifetime movie Mommy Meanest starring Lisa Rinna.

Want to learn more? Follow Clark on Instagram at @itskyleclark.

Sam Braun plays Josh Regis, a sixth-generation Easton Prep student who appears to have it all: money, looks, a relationship with Class President Britney Garner, and the coveted stroke seat in the boat for the school’s men’s crew team. But hidden health struggles of his, in addition to parental pressure from his father, make things more complicated for him than they might seem.

Outside of Crew Girl, Braun most recently appeared in Netflix’s Voicemails for Isabelle as Equinox Ethan. He’s also earned credits in Percy Jackson: Sea of Monsters, Superman & Lois, and The Way Home .

Want to learn more? Follow Braun on Instagram at @samhbraun.

Jessica Paré as Ella Emerson-Tao

Ella Emerson-Tao (Jessica Paré) is Teagan’s mother, who must navigate their sudden move back to her hometown after her husband is exposed for defrauding investors in his hotels. Though it’s not easy—and she certainly might not always be on Teagan’s good side throughout the process—Ella ‘s heart is always in the right place throughout this transition. Plus, heading back to Eagle’s Cove might even reunite her with a former flame…

Paré’s TV work spans series like Jack & Bobby, Mad Men, and SEAL Team. On the film front, she has worked on Wicker Park, Hot Tub Time Machine, and Brooklyn.

Thomas Cadrot as Coach Matt Hayden

Thomas Cadrot plays Coach Matt Hayden, an Easton Prep men’s crew team alum, who is determined to land his team a victory—especially because his contract might soon be up. He gives Teagan a shot to make her mark as coxswain, and though tough, he ultimately has the players’ best interests in mind.

Among Cadrot’s TV credits are Arrow, Once Upon a Time, Firefly Lane, and So Help Me Todd. Plus, he appeared in 2023’s Scream VI.

Want to learn more? Follow Cadrot on Instagram at @thomas.cadrot.

Sage Linder as Britney Garner, Alaska Leigh as Amber, and Olga Petsa as Juliet Morny

Now it’s time for the ladies of Easton Prep! First off, Britney Garner (Sage Linder), Easton Prep’s Class President—who happens to be the daughter of a senator, the “twibling” of Teagan’s teammate Bronson “Baloo” Garner, and Josh’s girlfriend—who feels threatened by Teagan once she makes her way onto the crew team. Linder’s TV credits include The Next Step, Harlan Coben’s Shelter, and Percy Jackson and the Olympians.

Next up, we have Amber (Alaska Leigh), the chair of the dance committee and Britney’s sidekick. Leigh has previously appeared in Blockbuster, Fire Country, and Stick.

And then last but not least, Juliet Morny (Olga Petsa), who becomes a friend and confidant for Teagan as she navigates the school. However, Juliet is dealing with her own struggles at home; specifically, absent parents.

Want to learn more? Follow them on Instagram at @sagelinder, @alaskaleigh, and @olgapetsa.

Jude Wilson as Bronson “Baloo” Garner and Connor Paton as Ryder Regis

Let’s give it up for the men of the Easton crew team, starting off with Bronson “Baloo” Garner, played by Jude Wilson. Baloo is a sweetheart who has so much love for the crew team, though his own pursuit of love may get him in some hot water with his teammates. Wilson has previously worked on shows such as Fellow Travelers, A Million Little Things, and Law & Order Toronto: Criminal Intent, and is set to star in Netflix’s This Summer Will Be Different.

Ryder Regis (Connor Paton) is Josh’s cousin, though their familial relationship is anything but loving, as the two boys are constantly competing. Josh’s grandfather had an affair with their family’s dogwalker, leading to a secret daughter, who is Ryder’s mother. Outside of Crew Girl, Paton could recently be seen in Netflix’s Little House on the Prairie and Paramount+‘s Happy Face.

Want to learn more? Follow them at @juderwilson and @connnorpaton.

Connor Wong as Perry Chou

Connor Wong plays Perry Chou, another Easton Prep men’s crew teammate who also has an eye for fashion, which leads to some spats (or maybe a spark?) with Juliet.

Wong recently appeared in Hallmark Channel‘s Absolutely Devoted to You, and also can be seen in Cruel Summer, Monster High 2, and Elle.

Want to learn more? Follow Wong at @connor.wong.

Riley Davis as Noah Bainbridge

Noah Bainbridge (Riley Davis) is another member of the Easton Prep men’s crew team, and is definitely the brains of the group, going so far as to craft a murder mystery night for his teammates when they would much rather drink and party.

You might recognize Davis from Season 1 of Off Campus, in which he played Sean McCall. He has also worked on shows like Murdoch Mysteries and The Copenhagen Test.

Want to learn more? Follow Davis at @rileydavisfilm

Vincent Muller as Damon Quaid and Jarrett Carlington as Chris “Crab Catcher” Padmore

Vincent Muller plays Damon Quaid, a fun-loving crew team member who is not above pulling pranks to get revenge—even if said pranks are pulled on the principal’s grandson. Muller recently appeared in an episode of Hallmark+‘s When Calls the Heart spinoff, Hope Valley 1874. He also starred in the 2025 horror film Clown in a Cornfield.

Meanwhile, the kindhearted Chris “Crab Catcher” Padmore, played by Jarrett Carlington, earned his nickname by catching crabs, or getting his oar stuck in the water while rowing. Carlington’s TV credits include Zoey’s Extraordinary Playlist, Upload, and Resident Alien.

Want to learn more? Follow them at @_vincentmuller_ and @jarrett.carlington.

Niall Matter as Richard Regis

Richard Regis (Niall Matter) is Josh’s father, who wants his son to follow in his footsteps when it comes to both rowing and working for the family’s airline. However, the pressure he puts on Josh—which has been passed down from Richard’s own father—might be too much to bear.

Matter is a familiar face for Hallmark Channel fans, having appeared in over 25 films for the network. He most recently starred alongside Ashley Williams in Much About Love, and will lead A Grand Biltmore Christmas with Holland Roden. He also appeared in 2018’s The Predator.

Want to learn more? Follow Matter at @officiallyniallmatter.

Byron Lawson as David Tao

Byron Lawson plays Teagan’s father and boutique hotelier David Tao, who at the start of the series, goes on the run after learning the FBI is onto hi for defrauding his investors.

Lawson appeared in 2006’s Snakes on a Plane, and has worked on TV shows such as Smallville and Murder in a Small Town.

Want to learn more? Follow Matter at @officiallyniallmatter.
