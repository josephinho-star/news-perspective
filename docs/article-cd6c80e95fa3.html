Paige Spiranac leaves it all on the field, and little to the imagination, while asking NFL fans tough question

2026-11-18 00:15

There's a lot going on when Paige Spiranac drops new social media content. It's often written off as one-dimensional, but nothing could be further from the truth.

Sometimes her content is educational. Sometimes she's asking a tough question. Sometimes she does both. Most of the time her content is conversational.

There's a back-and-forth taking place that requires, at times, several views to fully grasp. Tuesday she asked NFL fans a tough question.

I admit, this doesn’t appear to be a tough question at all. She's showcasing a new NFL collection for Swag Golf while asking, "Which team is your favorite?"

All you have to do is state the name of your favorite team and off you go about your day. That is until about the third time you watch it.

What if your team isn't one of the handful Paige goes through in the video? Does it now become a question about which team of the ones she has shown is your favorite?

By the time you figure that out, you're a half a dozen or more views in. Your mind is in a pretzel, and you try to straighten yourself back out by watching it a couple more times.

You then realize you're in too deep to turn around. You need to decide which way to go. Another time through to help you decide what you should do and, ultimately, you go with your gut.

And for everyone, that answer is going to be different. That's how Paige Spiranac asks the tough questions. She left it all out on the field on Tuesday.

As for Paige, she's supported multiple teams. Though, she has her favorites.

"Both my parents are from Pittsburgh so I was born a Steelers fan," she told her followers. "Can’t forget about my love for the Bills tho."
