Meghan Markle’s estranged father hit with child sex abuse material charges in Philippines as US case unfolds

2026-11-17 23:24

The criminal investigation into Meghan Markle's estranged father has taken another troubling turn.

Philippine police filed three charges tied to alleged child sexual abuse material as he faces a separate criminal case in the United States.

Two of those charges involve the alleged distribution or trafficking of child sexual abuse material and one involving possession, iTV reported. Prosecutors are reviewing those charges before deciding whether to proceed.

Fox News Digital reached out to the Philippine National Police for additional information.

The Sun previously reported that Meghan's half-brother, Thomas Markle Jr., recently found "hundreds, if not thousands" of child sex abuse images in boxes at his father’s former home in the Philippine city of Cebu when he was clearing out the property. Markle Jr. told the newspaper in October that he reported the images to police in the Philippines and contacted the Federal Bureau of Investigation in the U.S.

Meghan condemned the alleged abuse and shed new light on her decision to cut ties with her father.

"I am deeply distressed at the news that has come to light in the last 24 hours, and my thoughts are with any children who may have been harmed," she said in a statement previously shared with Fox News Digital at the time.

"In 2018, a letter was sent to the Metropolitan Police and Royalty and Specialist Protection. That letter was subsequently shared with me. It made troubling and disturbing allegations against my father, and I was given assurances that these were being investigated."

"I hope it can now be understood that, despite certain media publications’ distortion over the years, any distance from my father and my choice to not allow him to have contact with our children, was for one reason: to protect them."

"I trusted that the authorities were doing the same for other families," the statement continued.

Federal prosecutors in America also charged Markle Sr. with possession of child pornography in a criminal complaint filed Nov. 5 before he was arrested in Florida.

According to the 14-page complaint obtained by Fox News Digital, FBI agents searched two storage units registered to Markle and seized approximately 78 items, including film negatives, slides and books containing suspected child sexual abuse material. Investigators say Markle had rented the units since 2008 and continued paying for them through October 2026.

Agents identified approximately 18 suspected illegal images among film negatives recovered from one unit. A witness, who had known Markle since the 1970s, allegedly identified him as the adult man appearing with a prepubescent girl in at least two photographs. The complaint did not name the witness.

American investigators also traced details in photographs recovered from Markle's California storage units to Anahaw Lodge, a hotel in the Philippines. The U.S. complaint, however, does not establish when the photographs were taken, whether Markle traveled to the Philippines or whether the images are connected to any alleged offenses there.

Markle was initially released on bond and placed under house arrest. Days later, Markle allegedly cut off his ankle monitor and was ordered by the court to remain in custody.
