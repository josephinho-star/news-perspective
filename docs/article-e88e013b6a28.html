Israel condemns Green party passing ‘Zionism is racism’ as a ‘stain on history of UK’ – UK politics live

2026-10-04 15:51

Israeli foreign ministry condemns Green party vote as 'stain on history of UK'

The Israeli foreign ministry has issued a statement condemning the Green party in the light of today’s vote, Tom Scotson from the Sun reports.

In the statement, the foreign ministry said:

The shocking resolution adopted today legitimises armed struggle, calls for a Palestinian state “from the river to the sea” - meaning the destruction of the State of Israel - and labels as “racist” the aspiration of the Jewish people for self-determination.

This goes far beyond the boundaries of legitimate political debate. As long as this resolution remains in place, it will stand as a stain on the history of the United Kingdom.

Adrian Ramsay, the former co-leader of the Greens, has issued this statement about today’s vote.

I am deeply saddened the ‘Zionism is Racism’ motion has passed. It doesn’t reflect Green principles or my own view.

The Green Party has long supported the principle of self-determination, and I stand by our 2024 manifesto commitment of equal rights of Israelis and Palestinians.

The Board of Deputies of British Jews, the Community Security Trust and Jewish Leadership Council wrote a joint letter to the Green party before today’s vote saying that, if the ‘Zionism is racism’ motion were to pass, the party would place itself “on the extreme margins of democratic politics”.

The Lib Dem MP Tim Farronsays Green party members unhappy about the ‘Zionism is racism’ vote are welcome to join his party.

That the antisemitic entryists who have captured the Green Party, are being antisemitic, is no surprise. That the Party Leader skipped this vote and failed to use his authority to argue against them, is an astonishing act of weakness. Real Greens are welcome in the Lib Dems…

Tories claim Green party's 'Zionism is racism' vote shows it's 'toxic to its core'

Chris Philp, the shadow home secretary, has issued this statement on behalf of the Conservative party about the Greens’ ‘Zionism is racism’ vote. He said:

British Jews should not have to disclaim a belief in Jewish self-determination and the existence of Israel in order to be accepted by a British political party.

One look beneath the surface of Zack Polanski’s Green party shows a party toxic to its core. It is clear the Green party cannot bring itself to stand unambiguously against those who hate Jews.

It’s hard to see how the Green party’s motion today will avoid an investigation by the EHRC, given Labour was previously found in breach.

From Adam Ramsay, the writer, journalist and Green party member

My brother just rang with a basic point: if you take the subjectivity of marginalised people seriously, then you have to accept the right of Palestinians to name the oppression they experience. Once we do that, it’s clear that Zionism is racism.

Senior party sources now admitted the Greens have been a plunged into chaos. Polanski and MPs way too late and weak in trying to oppose Zionism motion. New policy puts GPEW totally out of step with policy on ME elsewhere in Europe

This is fromPaul Ovenden, the former director of political strategy for Keir Starmer in No 10, on the Green party’s vote.

Last week, the Green Party were offered the biggest opportunity they’ve ever had, in the shape of electoral reform. Today, they’ve made it impossible for them to be accommodated in any administration.

The Green party has sent out an updated news release saying that the statement issued by the five MPs earlier saying is there “no conflict between supporting the rights of Palestinians and the rights of Jewish people” has now also been endorsed by Zack Polanski, the Green leader, and Rachel Millward and Mothin Ali, the two co-deputy leaders.

Earlier I described the statement as the five MPs implicitly disassociating themselves from the motion. (See 3.53pm.) Given that Ali has now backed it, it would be fair to conclude any implict criticism is quite well hidden; Ali was in favour of the motion.
