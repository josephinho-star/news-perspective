Mother of murdered 8-year-old excoriates Gavin Newsom for commuting death row sentences of 548 inmates

2026-11-23 22:41

WASHINGTON — President Trump went for Gavin Newsom’s jugular when he had families describe the horror they endured as the California governor commuted the death row sentences of their loved ones’ killers.

Their “lives were forever changed by the vicious behavior of the death row inmates, whose sentences have now been commuted by Governor Newsom,” Trump said at a heartbreaking event Monday at the Justice Department.

The family members joined him to describe horrific crimes with one mother demanding Newsom face her to explain why her son’s killer received a commutation.

Sandy Friend choked up as she described how her 8-year-old son Michael Lyons was raped, sodomized, and tortured. “Sorry this is hard,” she said as she struggled to regain her composure.

She ended her remarks with a plea: “Face me, Governor Newsom. Tell me Michael’s life doesn’t matter. Michael deserved a governor who would stand with the victims, honor their justice, and respect the will of the people.”

In May 1996, Michael was abducted while walking home from school. He was tortured and murdered by serial killer Robert Boyd Rhoades. Newsom commuted Rhoades’s 1999 death sentence in November as part of a broader group of death row commutations.

Newsom’s office expressed sympathy for the families, telling The Post in a statement: “To the families standing with him today: your grief is real. Every person on California’s former death row will die in prison. Not one walks out. Donald Trump’s criminals walk out.”

In all, Newsom commuted the sentences of 548 death row inmates in his final weeks in office.
