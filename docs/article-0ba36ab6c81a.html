Stunning twist as parents charged in son’s deadly e-bike crash

2026-10-22 02:00

The heartbroken parents of a 13-year-old Orange County boy who died in an e-bike crash are facing criminal charges for allowing their son to ride the powerful bike illegally — even after he suffered a serious injury in an earlier wreck.

The teen was rushed to UCI Medical Center, where he died. Before the fatal crash, Nguyen had been running red lights, speeding and popping wheelies on the high-powered E-Ride Pro SS 2.0, prosecutors said.

Now, his father, Vu Thanh Nguyen, 56, and mother, Hong Cuc Thi Nguyen, 49, both of Santa Ana, are facing charges over their alleged failure to stop their son from riding the motorcycle illegally.

The boy’s father had even bought him an even faster, more powerful bike despite the teen breaking his leg in a separate accident just six months earlier, according to authorities.

“When parents refuse to hold their children accountable and that refusal to parent results in unspeakable tragedy, then I have no choice but to hold parents accountable,” Orange County District Attorney Todd Spitzer wrote in a release.
