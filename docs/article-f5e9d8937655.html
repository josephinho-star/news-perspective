Prince William ‘will be watching’ estranged brother Harry ‘closely’ following unexpected UK return

2026-09-09 18:00

Prince William will reportedly keep a close eye on his estranged brother, Prince Harry, after the latter unexpectedly moved back to the UK.

“William will be watching his brother very closely and trying to work out what his game plan is,” Grant Harrold, a former royal butler, told Us Weekly in an interview published Wednesday.

A source told the outlet that the brothers’ “relationship was already broken,” explaining, “The move doesn’t break it again [but] it does put the break in the same country.”

The insider shared that the Duke of Sussex and his wife, Meghan Markle, returning to the UK is a “shock” to William and Princess Kate Middleton, both 44.

“They’re still processing it, but their initial surprise has evolved, and they’re taking a wait-and-see approach,” they said.

As for a potential reunion between William and Harry, the source isn’t betting on whether it will happen anytime soon.

“No one’s booking a table for two for William and Harry,” the insider claimed. “Everyone wants a reunion story, but it’s not something that’s in the cards.”

If a reunion occurs in the future, the insider believes it “will be a slow and private process,” and on the Prince of Wales’ terms.

A rep for Harry and William wasn’t immediately available to Page Six for comment.
