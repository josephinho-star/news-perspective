Bars expect 50% jump in sales on final night of Hong Kong’s ‘Halloween Month’

2026-10-31 10:30

Operators also point to surge in visitors from mainland Chinese cities in Greater Bay Area, who are taking advantage of easier border crossing

Hong Kong’s inaugural “Halloween Month” has been a boon for businesses, with bar and restaurant operators in Lan Kwai Fong expecting sales on Saturday to rise by more than 50 per cent compared with last year.

With Halloween falling on a weekend this year, businesses were expecting a bumper turnout as the Tourism Board’s inaugural campaign for the celebrations wrapped up. Operators noted a surge in visitors from mainland Chinese cities in the Greater Bay Area.

“It’s been very well received by everybody,” said Anthea Cheung So-may, director of the Lan Kwai Fong Association, on Saturday. “People are really happy to see this celebration.”
