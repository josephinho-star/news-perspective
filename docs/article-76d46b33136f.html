Long Beach’s ICE debacle offers chilling warning of what ‘sanctuary’ really means in California

2026-11-25 03:36

Video footage reveals that police in Long Beach simply abandoned Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agents to face a violent mob in the city last month.

“Good luck,” one of the local cops is heard saying on the bodycam tape.

In a “sanctuary state,” criminal illegal aliens are safe — but law enforcement officers are fair game.

That’s the mob rule that the Democratic Party has brought to California.

At every level of government, Democrats have tried to expose ICE agents to greater physical danger.

Outgoing Gov. Gavin Newsom and Rep.-elect Scott Wiener pushed laws through Sacramento that would prevent ICE agents from wearing masks, and force them to wear badges.

They are quite happy to let activist mobs “dox” ICE officers, follow them to their homes, and target them.

Luckily, a federal judge stopped them cold last week, throwing out Newsom and Wiener’s dangerous laws.

But the rot goes straight down to the roots, at the local level.

Anti-ICE activists want LAPD Chief Jim McDonnell fired because he dares to allow LAPD officers to shield ICE agents from rioters.

McDonnell has made clear that the LAPD is not involved in immigration enforcement. But he has also made clear that it is his sworn duty to protect everyone in the city, including ICE agents.

No one is above the law — but no one is beneath its protections, either.

The DSA-Long Beach chapter has vowed to “bring the fight to” LA Mayor-elect Nithya Raman if she does not keep a promise to fire McDonnell.

The radicals of Los Angeles want the LAPD to behave just as the Long Beach police did in that bodycam footage, and to leave ICE agents defenseless.

The socialists want to be able to attack federal law enforcement at will. And in Long Beach, they can.

That twisted policy is why we saw deadly confrontations between ICE agents and anti-ICE activists in Minnesota last winter — not because the agents were spoiling for a fight, but because “sanctuary” governors and mayors refused to allow police to cooperate with ICE.

Is that the kind of violence we want in the streets of California? Evidently, some do.

But the rest of us remember — with great bitterness — the Black Lives Matter riots of 2020.

The mobs ransacked mom-and-pop shops in the Fairfax district of LA. They vandalized the public library and the courthouse in Santa Monica.

And all because Democrats told police to “take a knee” and leave “peaceful protests” alone.

The anti-ICE riots of last summer, which rocked downtown LA, would have spread nationwide if President Donald Trump had not called out the National Guard.

Enough.

All that is needed is for state and local leaders to do the right thing, and stand with law enforcement — at every level.

But they lack the courage to confront the mob. And some of them are part of the mob.

It’s tragic: So many problems in California, and in our nation, could be avoided if leaders simply did their jobs.

The chaos on our college campuses after the Hamas attack on Israel on Oct. 7, 2023, for example, could have been avoided if administrators had the courage to condemn terrorism.

They could have said: We will not support any groups that advocate the intentional murder of civilians. Period.

Few of them said anything like that. The result was “encampments” that blocked campuses for weeks, and violated the rights of Jewish students who simply wanted to go to class.

If you don’t like immigration laws, change them. The Democrats just won both houses of Congress.

But violence? Never. Long Beach: What a disgrace!

Joel Pollak is the Opinion editor of The California Post.
