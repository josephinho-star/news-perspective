Beau Lamarre-Condon trial live updates: former police officer tells court of fatal confrontation with Jesse Baird and Luke Davies

2026-11-06 01:08

Lamarre-Condon says he thought he would be ‘signing my own deathwish’ if he went to police

The accused says he was “guilty of manslaughter” over the death of Jesse Baird and knew the maximum sentence was 25 years in prison and was worried about what would happen to him in jail.

In my mind I was fearful that if I went to police, if I did the right thing I was signing my own death wish by potentially getting stabbed or killed in jail.

I genuinely believed if I was going to jail, I would be killed.

Not because of who I was as a person, but because of the uniform I wear.

Lamarre-Condon tells court about buying second surfboard cover

The former police officer said he was intending to buy suitcases or a sleeping bag but decided to buy another surfboard cover after the alleged murders because he knew he already had one.

‘I killed somebody’: Lamarre-Condon told friend he shot Jesse Baird

Lamarre-Condon arrived at a friend’s work and told him about the shootings, the jury heard.

“I said ‘I killed somebody’, referring to a singular person and I continued the conversation with him.

“I said the first shot got Jesse … went through the shoulder and out the door and the second shot went through the door … I never said anything about shooting Luke ... I only told him about two shots going off related to Jesse Baird and Jesse Baird only.”

‘It got Luke’: Lamarre-Condon tells court about moment Davies was shot

The former officer said police are trained to redirect gun muzzles when firearms are pointed at him.

I saw the gun, he was holding it with both hands I thought fuck he’s going to fire the gun and that’s where my police training kicked in.

My left hand would have grabbed Luke’s left wrist and turned it away … my other hand pushed against the foreman and then I pushed the pivot point of the left wrist away from me.

Lamarre-Condon claims he told Davies ‘I’m not here to hurt you’

He tells the court that as he dialled the emergency hotline to try get help for Baird, Lamarre-Condon looked up and saw Luke Davies, who he didn’t recognise at the time.

The accused claims Davies had picked up his gun:

“It was pointed towards me at my chest and he said ‘I’m going to fucking shoot you’,” he tells the court.

I had both my hands up in the air and was calling triple zero …

I started lowering my body towards the ground: I said, ‘Please don’t shoot me I’m not here to shoot you.’

I’m not a threat I’m not here to hurt you, that’s when he raised the gun towards my head and that’s when he said ‘Fuck off’.”

Lamarre-Condon says Baird had ‘too much adrenaline’ and lunged at him with the knife

As police officers we are trained to shoot people at the centre of body mass … I had a split-second moment where I said drop the knife … It was just police mode now.

If I didn’t do that I was 110% going to have that knife driven into my throat.

And Jesse Baird was going to be sitting in my position today.
