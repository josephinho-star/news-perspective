‘Lanterns’ Episode 6 Finally Brings Guy Gardner — And Carol Ferris — to the HBO Green Lantern Show

2026-09-21 02:00

Lanterns Episode 6 brings us back to 2026 Rushville. It’s ten years after the horrific events of Episode 5 and most of the town doesn’t see what happened as a miracle — you know, that they all survived — but a catastrophe orchestrated by the Green Lanterns. This means that not only is John Stewart (Aaron Pierre) not welcome in the small town, but neither is current Green Lantern Guy Gardner (Nathan Fillion).

But that doesn’t stop either man for stopping by the latest episode of the HBO hit…

**Spoilers for Lanterns Episode 6, now streaming on HBO Max**

Lanterns Episode 6 follows John Stewart as he works with Sheriff Kerry Kane (Kelly Macdonald) to investigate the murder of Hal Jordan (Kyle Chandler). It turns out that after John walked away from being the Green Lantern, Hal was thrown in jail, while the Guardians settled on giving a ring to gym teacher Guy Gardner. Hence, why it’s Guy (and not John or Hal) who is the Green Lantern in recent DC live action hits like Superman and Peacemaker. It’s also why John feels Guy owes him one big favor in this week’s episode. (And it’s a favor that has very unexpected consequences…)

Besides looking into who killed Hal Jordan this week, John and Kerry also have to attend his funeral. It becomes clear when John reads Hal’s eulogy — written by Hal himself — that Earth’s first Green Lantern assumed his passing would be a monumental affair. Instead, it’s a small occasion only marked by John, Kerry, and a woman named Carol Ferris (Amie Mackenzie). The only sign that Hal was Green Lantern? John pushes Guy to sneak to Oa to retrieve Hal’s old suit and ring so he can be buried in it.

While John swears he wants Hal to be buried in his suit and ring as a sign of respect, as soon as the other mourners leave, he gets to work, digging up Hal Jordan’s grave so he can finally take his ring. However, it’s not that John suddenly wants to be Green Lantern. Rather, in order to gain access to Hal’s safe, he needs Hal’s voice. He needs Hal’s Halogram.

Confused by what just went down? Don’t understand why that woman at Hal’s funeral was such a big deal? Need more Guy Gardner? Here’s what you need to know about the end of Lanterns Episode 6 on HBO and HBO Max…

Lanterns Episode 6 Ending Explained: Why Did John Stewart Steal Hal Jordan’s Ring?

So why did John Stewart go to all the trouble of summoning Guy Gardner to Rushville to convince him to bring back Hal’s suit and ring if he doesn’t still want to be Green Lantern after all of these years? Well, it’s because John really is more interested in finding out what’s going on in Rushville and to discover that, he needs access to Hal’s vault. And Hal’s vault can only be opened with Hal’s real voice; not a recording.

We know that this is why John dug up Hal’s grave because the very first thing he uses the ring for isn’t to fly or bring back his bird. Rather, he summons the Halogram, who promptly sums up the situation: “God damn it.”

Now the question is which version of Hal is backed up in this Halogram. Will he be a helpful Halogram or an echo of the combative Hal we met in 2016?

Who is Carol Ferris? All About Hal’s Ex, Played By Amie Mackenzie on Lanterns:

Lanterns snuck in a poignant Easter egg at Hal Jordan’s funeral, by having none other than Carol Ferris show up.

Even though Sheriff Kane is Hal’s love interest in the HBO show, Carol Ferris is his original squeeze from the comics. Before he was Green Lantern, Hal was a test pilot for Ferris Aircraft, a company owned by Carol’s father that she eventually takes over. While Hal kept his superhero status a secret from Carol at first, she is soon pulled into his cosmic adventures. Sometimes she’s been a superhero and sometimes a supervillain.

In the past, Carol Ferris has been played by Blake Lively in the 2011 Ryan Reynolds Green Lantern film and voiced by actresses Kari Wahlgren, Jennifer Hale, and Olivia d’Abo in animation. In Lanterns, Carol is played by Denver-based actress Amie Mackenzie. The 60-something Mackenzie has an incredible personal story, having been a fixture of Colorado theater for decades. After surviving cancer, she booked her first onscreen part in Better Call Saul at the tender age of 53. Since then, she’s appeared in everything from The Madison to Mountainhead.

When DECIDER spoke to Lanterns star Kelly Macdonald, we asked about the missed opportunity to see Carol Ferris and Kerry Kane interact at Hal’s funeral.

“Would have been lovely, wouldn’t it?” Macdonald said. “I wanted a moment with Guy Gardner as well. Like, who wouldn’t?”

How Did Guy Gardner Become Green Lantern?

Speaking of Guy Gardner… If you watched last summer’s Superman and were confused about how a guy like Guy Gardner became Green Lantern, we now know he was the unplanned backup to both Hal and John. Had John assumed the mantle, per the Guardians wishes, Guy Gardner never would have donned the ring. (And he knows it!)

“That guy is hilarious. That character and that actor and that person,” Kyle Chandler told DECIDER. “I’ve got a love for that guy. He is fantastic.”

“He is fantastic and he is hilarious on and off screen,” Aaron Pierre said. “Yeah, that’s my guy! No pun intended.”

“Jesus,” Chandler said.

“Come on!” Pierre said. “That was good, that was good.”
