From Luka Doncic bobblehead to 2020 Championship rings, Lakers celebrating present and past

2026-09-10 20:38

From bobbleheads celebrating current stars to ones honoring key figures of the past, to Hello Kitty – yes, Hello Kitty – the Lakers are slated to have a well-rounded promotional nights schedule for 2026-27.

The team announced Thursday their full slate of promotional nights and special recognitions for the upcoming season.

They’ll celebrate superstar guard Luka Dončić as the NBA’s 2025-26 scoring champion with a bobblehead giveaway during the Nov. 1 home game against the Raptors.

Lakers star guard Austin Reaves will also have his own bobblehead giveaway night during the Dec. 27 home game against the Grizzlies.

The franchise will also honor legends through its new “League of Stars” program, which will include throwback bobbleheads of two-time All-Star and NBA champion Norm Nixon (Dec. 14 vs. Pelicans), NBA champion coach Paul Westhead (Jan. 14 vs. Magic) and four-time NBA champion Kurt Rambis (March 17 vs. Suns).

The Lakers will also have a Phil Jackson bobblehead night during the April 9 home game against the Timberwolves after Jackson’s statue outside of Crypto.com Arena is unveiled earlier that day.

Keeping up with celebrating their past, the Lakers will have a 2020 Championship ring replica giveaway on Jan. 24, when they’re slated to host Anthony Davis and the Wizards.

A few other promotional nights include a Lakers x Dodgers baseball-style jersey giveaway on Nov. 18 vs. the Jazz, the franchise’s first Hello Kitty night on March 7 vs. the Nets, in addition to giveaways during Opening Night against the Warriors on Oct. 21 and the 76ers on Christmas Day.

Lakers’ 2026-27 promotional nights

- Oct. 8: LGBTQ+ Pride Night with Pride t-shirt

- Oct. 21: Opening Night vs. Warriors

- Nov. 1: Luka Dončić scoring champion bobblehead

- Nov. 17: Veterans Appreciation Night

- Nov. 18: Lakers x Dodgers baseball-style jersey giveaway

- Dec. 1: League of Stars honors Norm Nixon with bobblehead

- Dec. 25: Christmas Day vs. 76ers

- Dec. 27: Austin Reaves bobblehead night

- Jan. 14: League of Stars honors Paul Westhead bobblehead

- Jan. 24: Lakers 2020 Championship ring replica giveaway

- Feb. 10: Mental Health Awareness Night

- March 7: Hello Kitty Night

- March 17: League of Stars honors Kurt Rambis with bobblehead

- April 9: Phil Jackson statue unveiling and Phil Jackson bobblehead
