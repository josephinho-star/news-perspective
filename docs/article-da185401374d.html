Travis Kelce breaks down why MSG was perfect venue for ‘magical’ Taylor Swift wedding

2026-09-02 11:38

“Thank you [to] everyone at MSG that allowed us to do this,” the NFL star, 36, said while chatting with brother and co-host Jason Kelce. “We wanted to do it somewhere where we could have a very intimate and genuine setting without a lot of distractions.”

The Kansas City Chiefs player noted, “We needed it to be a private feel. And MSG went above and beyond to give us that.

“I can’t thank everybody enough that showed up but also that helped put the show on, and it was a very magical, magical night.”

The arena, notably, was transformed into a garden for the July 3 ceremony, one month after Page Six broke the news of the wedding location.

The tight end said Wednesday that he wished the star-studded event — which was “so much fun” — “could’ve lasted even longer than it did [because] it felt like it just happened in the blink of an eye.”

Travis told listeners, “The night was a night we’ll never forget. Me and Taylor are so thankful for everybody that showed up, you know, from the vows to just seeing everyone to how much fun we had throughout the entire evening.”

He specifically shouted out Adam Sandler for being the “f–king best person” and getting ordained in the state of New York to officiate the wedding.

“It was a fun offseason, man,” Travis said at the time before diving into the “best night of [his] life.”

He raved, “I appreciate everyone who came out and celebrated and had fun with us. That’s about all I really got from that night. It was a crazy night. It was full of a lot of celebration.”

Travis subsequently quipped it was time to get back to “football, baby.”
