WNBA playoffs preview: why the Fever at +145 could be the best bet against defending champion Aces

2026-09-25 17:42

We had one play in the WNBA last night, and it went the exact opposite of how I expected it to go. The Fever played like they had nothing to gain, and the Lynx played like they were fighting for playoff spots. Now we move into the postseason. Before we start the actual games, which tip off on Sunday, we should look at each series and see if there are any future bets that are worth putting into our accounts. All eight teams have a legitimate case for the championship, so this could get interesting fast.

When the season first started, the Liberty were the favorite to win the WNBA Title. I'm not sure if New York was just the popular pick or if it was due to Breanna Stewart, Sabrina Ionescu, and Jonquel Jones on the roster. Either way, the season didn't go quite as planned for the Liberty but they still ended up winning almost 60% of their games. The Lynx were also expected to have a different season. Without MVP Naphesa Collier for much of the year, it was expected that Minnesota would struggle this year. Olivia Miles, this year's Rookie of the Year and potential MVP, led the way, and they earned the best record in the WNBA. I think there is some good value on taking the Liberty to win at least one game in this series. I'm going to take this series to go three games at +100.

One team is built on star power, and the other is built on being a complete team. The Wings have the star power with Paige Bueckers, and she will be getting her first taste of the postseason. The team lost the #1 overall pick, Azzi Fudd, earlier this year to injury, so they aren't quite as deep as they used to be. The Valkyries are the #2 seed overall and have the best defense in basketball. They have the personnel to deal with Bueckers and everyone else on the Wings. I don't see the Wings winning a game here; I like this one to go just two games at -130.

The Atlanta Dream are one of the better teams in the game right now. They went 30-14 and have a good balance between inside and outside offense. The Dream have had good health this season, and all five starters are averaging nine or more points per game. The Mystics are led by Sonia Citron, but there are still some other solid players. Shakira Austin led the team in scoring and could cause some problems for the Dream. Washington beat the Dream 2-1 in the regular season. They were blown out in the loss and then won two home games, both by five points. I don't love the game for the Dream, and I think it will be just a two-game series, but I won't put an official bet on this one.

This is the marquee matchup of the first round. The Aces are the defending champions, and they've won three of the past four titles. A'ja Wilson is still the elite player who can be the difference-maker in any series. The Fever have been rather inconsistent this year, but they seemed to lock in for the postseason last year, and I think there is reason to believe they can do it again. I'm going to back Indiana to win this one at +145. Indiana gave Las Vegas the toughest battle last year in the playoffs; I think they make this go the full three games, even if the Fever don't win.
