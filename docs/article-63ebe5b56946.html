US intelligence witnesses to 2025 UFO orb incident could testify before Congress

2026-10-10 23:45

US intelligence officials who witnessed a bizarre parade of orange orbs — including smaller objects that allegedly emerged from a giant “mothership” orb — could be called to testify before Congress, Rep. Eric Burlison (R-Mo.) told The Post.

The officials witnessed the strange encounter near an undisclosed West Coast military base during a mission in late 2025.

They claimed that multiple orange orbs flew alongside their helicopter as “countless” smaller objects swarmed in all directions against a mountain backdrop, according to an FBI report released by the Trump administration in May.

One of the smaller “oval-shaped” orange, white, and yellow orbs approached the helicopter and split in two, according to the report.

Burlison (R-Mo.) said he would explore whether the witnesses could testify publicly, but warned that bringing intelligence personnel into the spotlight could expose sensitive information.

“There’s some people that are working for the agency that having them come forward publicly may expose other things,” he said. “And so that’s a good place to explore and I’m happy to look into that to see if that’s a possibility.”

One of the witnesses, from the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, is a career bureaucrat who is not known to the general public, Burlison said.

Some of the witnesses have met with congressional members behind closed doors, but have not shared all of the video footage of the encounter, according to the congressman.

Loeb said he and other members of the scientific advisory council also want to visit the site of the alleged encounter to investigate the objects themselves.

“If we are informed of the location and other details of this unusual encounter, we can go there and obtain high quality data. This could help us figure out the nature of the mysterious orbs,” he said.
