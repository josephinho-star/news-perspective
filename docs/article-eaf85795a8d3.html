Dolphins vs. Vikings odds, picks: Brian Flores to overwhelm Malik Willis in Week 4 blowout

2026-10-04 16:00

Post sports gambling editor/producer and digital sports editor Matt Ehalt is back for his third season in the NFL Bettor’s Guide.

MINNESOTA VIKINGS (-10.5) over Miami Dolphins

Get ready to hear these words come April 29: “With the first pick in the 2027 NFL Draft, the Miami Dolphins select…”

The Dolphins are a miserable excuse for a football team, and things are not going to get any easier now that do-it-all running back De’Von Achane is lost for the season with an ACL tear.

Miami will be the team you pick against each week in your Survivor Pool and for good reason.

This team lacks talent on both sides, and Miami’s front office is clearly looking ahead to the 2027 season.

Now, we normally don’t like laying double-digit points since the NFL can be so wonky. We’ve seen some crazy upsets this year.

We’re willing to do this week, though, since Miami has lost each game this year by at least 14 points and now faces a blitz-heavy Minnesota team that is firing on all cylinders defensively.

Brian Flores is going to blitz, blitz, blitz Malik Willis all game long, and Miami does not have the receivers that can exploit Minnesota’s secondary.

Without Achane as the lead back and a dump-off option, there will be even fewer ways for the Dolphins to move the ball.

Minnesota does give some reason for concern with Kyle Murray looking terrible last week against a Tampa Bay defense, but Miami has allowed an average of 28.67 points per game, the fifth-worst mark in the sport.
