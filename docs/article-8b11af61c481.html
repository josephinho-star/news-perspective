Eagles coaches had ‘intense’ sideline fight during Steelers win: ‘Punched in the neck’

2026-11-25 16:07

This Eagles coach apparently saw the Sylvester Stallone biopic before Week 11.

Philadelphia associate head coach Clint Hurtt punched head strength and conditioning coach Fernando Noriega “in the neck” on the sidelines during Sunday’s win over the Steelers, according to PHLY Sports reporter EJ Smith.

“I saw Clint Hurtt kind of just punch Fernando in the neck and they had to be separated,” said Smith, who added that players confirmed the tiff. “It was an intense scene on the sideline.”

Smith said the altercation happened roughly midway during the third quarter of the Eagles’ 19-16 win — which may be the final game of Aaron Rodgers’ career after his shock benching Tuesday — and it transpired after Pittsburgh receiver DK Metcalf’s ejection.

His understanding is that Noriega attempted to have several players back away from the sideline, seemingly in the aftermath of the ejection, and Hurtt “turned around and let him have it.”

Hurtt, who also oversees the defensive line, “was very frustrated with the insistent reminder that they needed to stay on the sideline,” per Smith.

One unnamed player told Smith it showed “how angry the defense played in this game. How everybody was on edge.”

Philadelphia entered the game on a seven-game losing streak and a once-proud defense has not lived up to expectations this season.

The Eagles allowed at least 25 points in their three losses from Weeks 6-8 before holding the Giants to 19 points in Week 9, and they returned from their Week 10 bye with a strong effort.

The Eagles held the Steelers to 255 total yards and a 5-of-17 showing on third down.

This season marks Hurtt’s third with the team and he became the associate coach this offseason, while Noriega is in his sixth season with the franchise.

Noriega also holds the title of director of player performance and sports science.

“Both guys just doing their job,” Smith said. “But it was definitely an interesting window into kind of the mindset of the defense overall. But hey, Clint Hurtt got those guys ready to play.”
