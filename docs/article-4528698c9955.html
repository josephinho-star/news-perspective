Jon Gosselin speaks out in defense of son Collin’s bombshell memoir, threatens legal action

2026-10-16 02:08

“First and foremost, I couldn’t be more proud of my son’s heartbreaking memoir and his mission to help other victims of abuse,” Jon told Us Weekly in a statement Thursday.

“Also, let me be very clear: If you continue to falsely label my son Collin as ‘mentally ill,’ you will be held accountable,” he continued. “As you know, Collin has been evaluated by multiple independent psychiatrists, and that narrative is proven categorically untrue.”

The former reality star, 49, noted that repeating false claims about Collin’s mental health is “damaging to his reputation, his credibility and his future.”

He concluded, “Consider this fair warning: The days of using mental health to discredit and silence my son are over.”

Among the shocking allegations was that she kept him locked up in “a small holding” cell for two years before sending him away to the mental institution when he was 12 years old, isolating him from the rest of his siblings.
