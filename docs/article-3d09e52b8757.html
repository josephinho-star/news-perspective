Woman arrested for threatening to shoot Trump at Florida rally

2026-11-01 00:59

Allison Albro was charged with written threat to kill and unlawful use of a two-way communications device to facilitate a felony

A 21-year-old woman who posted on social media that she was planning to shoot US President Donald Trump at his rally in Florida’s Daytona Beach has been arrested, authorities said.

Trump visited Daytona on Friday to help drum up the vote for Republican gubernatorial candidate Byron Donalds.

But the night before, investigators said Allison Albro shared an online post showing a photo of herself captioned, “Going to the Trump rally in my city tmr so I can shoot him with my glock! Wish me luck guys!!”
