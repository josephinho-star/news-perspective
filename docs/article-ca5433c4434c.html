Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show: Where To Watch, Start Time, Free Streaming Info

2026-10-18 17:00

Who’s ready for Supermodel Sunday? Live from Los Angeles, it’s the 2026 Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show!

This year’s event features the return of the Fantasy Bra, which was co-designed by Jacob & Co.

“When we decided to bring back the Fantasy Bra this year, I couldn’t think of a more perfect person to design it than Jacob,” Victoria’s Secret Chief Creative Officer Adam Selman said in a statement. “I have been a huge fan of his work for a very long time, and he has played a big role in shaping pop culture. I love his artistry, his craft, and his fearlessness. Jacob understands that a Fantasy Bra has to be spectacular, but what made him the right partner is that he designs for the person wearing it. That’s what Victoria’s Secret is all about.”

You can tune in for the fashion and the music as this year’s runway show includes performances by Katsye, Megan Thee Stallion, Tate McRae, and Megan Moroney. Where can you watch all the fun? From start time to free streaming information, here’s how to watch the 2026 Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show live online.

When Is The Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show?

This year’s fashion show takes place on Sunday, October 18.

What Time Does The Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show Start?

All the fun begins at 7:30 p.m. ET (4:30 p.m. PT) on YouTube.

You can register for the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show After Party, which streams on TikTok and starts at 8:45 p.m. ET, on the brand’s TikTok page.

How To Watch The Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show 2026 Live For Free:

This year’s show is streaming for free on the Victoria’s Secret YouTube page (embedded below).

Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show Performers:

As we mentioned above, this year’s show features performances by Katsye, Megan Thee Stallion, Tate McRae, and Megan Moroney.

The Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show airs Sunday, October 18 at 7:30 p.m. ET on YouTube.
