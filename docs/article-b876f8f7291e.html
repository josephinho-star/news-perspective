‘Call The Midwife’ Season 15 Netflix Release Date: Here’s When New Episodes Drop on The Streamer

2026-08-29 11:30

Call the Midwife fans, the wait for Season 15 on Netflix is finally coming to an end.

The latest season of the beloved BBC period drama has already aired on PBS, but Netflix subscribers in the U.S. have been waiting for their turn to catch up with the residents of Nonnatus House. Fortunately, that wait is almost over.

Call the Midwife Season 15 is coming to Netflix in September, and there’s good news for anyone who prefers to binge their favorite shows: You won’t have to wait around for new episodes each week. All eight episodes will be available to stream at once.

Even better, Netflix’s Season 15 drop will also include the two-part 2025 Christmas special. So, if you’ve been holding off on the latest season, you’ll have plenty to watch when it arrives.

Here’s everything you need to know about when Call the Midwife Season 15 comes to Netflix, how many episodes to expect and what else will be included in the new-season drop.

When Does Call the Midwife Season 15 Come Out on Netflix?

Call the Midwife Season 15 premieres on Netflix on Monday, September 7, 2026.

All eight episodes will be released at once, so Netflix subscribers can watch the season at their own pace rather than waiting for each episode to arrive.

The September release also isn’t exactly a surprise for longtime Netflix viewers. Seasons 11, 12, 13 and 14 all made their way to the U.S. streamer in September, with Season 14 arriving on September 15, 2025.

In other words, Call the Midwife is right on schedule.

What Time Does Call the Midwife Season 15 Come Out on Netflix?

Netflix typically releases its new movies and TV shows at 12 a.m. PT / 3 a.m. ET on the scheduled release date.

That means Call the Midwife Season 15 should be available to stream at 3 a.m. ET on Monday, September 7.

And yes, there’s more than just the eight episodes waiting for you.

How Many Episodes Are In Call the Midwife Season 15?

Season 15 consists of eight episodes.

The new season moves the story into 1971, with Nonnatus House facing a changing social and political landscape. The Women’s Liberation movement is gathering momentum, while the midwives continue dealing with the medical and personal challenges that come through their doors.

The season tackles storylines involving premature birth, placenta previa, kidney cancer, tuberculosis and slavery.

Here’s a look at what’s coming:

- Episode 1: The Nonnatus team grows increasingly concerned about the welfare of four young children.

- Episode 2: A new medical technique threatens to change obstetrics forever.

- Episode 3: Rosalind gets involved in an unusual domestic case, while Joyce is seconded to the hospital.

- Episode 4: Sister Catherine is devastated when a newborn baby is taken from the maternity home.

- Episode 5: A tragedy strikes a local family, while Dr. Turner comes under scrutiny.

- Episode 6: The Nonnatus team helps a young father after he receives a life-threatening diagnosis.

- Episode 7: Timothy Turner, now newly qualified, joins the midwives on their district rounds.

- Episode 8: Sister Veronica is forced to make a major decision about her future with the order.

What About the Call the Midwife Christmas Specials?

There’s another reason Netflix’s Season 15 release is worth circling on the calendar.

The streamer will also add the two-part 2025 Christmas special on September 7, giving viewers a chance to catch up on the holiday episodes before diving into the new season.

The specials take the Nonnatus team beyond Poplar, with part of the story unfolding in Hong Kong. Members of the team travel there to deal with a crisis involving the order’s branch house, while the second installment follows a rescue effort that doesn’t go quite according to plan.

The Christmas specials originally aired ahead of Season 15, setting the stage for what’s to come.

Can You Watch Call the Midwife Season 15 Before It Comes To Netflix?

You can — and, in fact, U.S. viewers have already had the opportunity to watch Season 15.

The season premiered on PBS on March 22, 2026, with new episodes airing weekly. PBS Passport members were also able to stream episodes ahead of their broadcast premieres.

But if you haven’t been following along on PBS, Netflix’s September 7 release gives you an easy way to catch up. All eight episodes will be waiting for you on the same day.

And for anyone who needs to do a little more catching up first, Seasons 1 through 14 are currently streaming on Netflix in the U.S.

Will There Be A Call the Midwife Season 16?

Yes. There’s more Call the Midwife on the way.

Season 16 has already been confirmed, with production expected to begin in 2027. The series will continue moving further into the 1970s.

And that’s not all. The BBC and Neal Street Productions are also developing a World War II-era prequel set in Poplar, along with a feature film set overseas in 1972 that will bring back characters from the series.

For now, though, Season 15 is next.

Call the Midwife Season 15 premieres Monday, September 7, 2026, on Netflix.
