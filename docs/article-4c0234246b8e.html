Chilling clue connected to missing NorCal woman washes ashore in king tides

2026-11-27 02:00

A hiker discovered a Prada purse belonging to Se Nah Park, a 24-year-old Cal Poly graduate, Wednesday evening at Four Mile Beach, just south of Panther State Beach, where she disappeared.

The Santa Cruz County Sheriff’s Office notified Park’s father Thursday morning, according to The San Francisco Chronicle.

Park vanished around 8:30 p.m. Nov. 8 while attending a bonfire with five friends at Panther State Beach.

The group was attempting to leave through a narrow rocky passage known as the Keyhole when an incoming wave knocked four of them off their feet.

Three managed to escape to safety, but Park was swept out into the Pacific.

Searchers recovered a pair of sneakers matching Park’s size and style two days later, along with a bunny keychain that had detached from the same Prada purse.

The Coast Guard suspended its 95-square-mile search Nov. 10 after battling cold water and 12-to-16-foot swells, offering the family its “deepest condolences.”

Park’s body has not been found.

She remains officially listed as missing by the California Department of Justice, although her family’s efforts have increasingly focused on bringing her home.

More than 40 friends and relatives gathered at San Jose Christian Alliance Church to remember Park. Her family has also shared messages expressing comfort in knowing she is “in heaven.”

Volunteer diver Juan Heredia and his team spent hours searching accessible sea caves, rocky crevices and openings along the coastline before dangerous Thanksgiving-week weather forced a pause.

The family has temporarily suspended organized shoreline searches and closed its makeshift headquarters at the Panther Beach parking lot.

Relatives say they are taking private time to “process this tragedy” while continuing to coordinate with local agencies.

The family expects to issue another public update Friday, and is urging surfers, boaters and coastal hikers between Half Moon Bay and Monterey to remain vigilant.

Anyone with information or possible sightings is asked to contact the Santa Cruz County Sheriff’s Office at 831-471-1121.
