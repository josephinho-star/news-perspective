Lisa Barlow says people ‘grossly underestimate’ the power of lip liner — and this one’s her favorite

2026-10-29 19:56

Lisa Barlow’s love for this brand is anything but hypocrisy and bulls—t.

While reality star just went viral for reapplying her MAC lip liner during an argument on “The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City,” she’s actually been loyal to its products since the ’90s. (“I walked block upon block to get the first ever-collab with RuPaul,” she recalled in a press release.)

Now, Barlow’s teaming up with the brand on special TikTok Shop bundles featuring her two favorite lip combos: Barely Barlow (Dervish Lip Pencil with Honeylove Lipstick) and the OG Lisa Lip (Whirl Lip Pencil and Velvet Teddy Lipstick).

“People grossly underestimate the importance of a killer lip line, you have to use a combo,” she said, quipping that MAC stands for “Most Amazing Cosmetics brand.”

She also starred in a campaign for the company — even posing with a larger-than-life version of her beloved lip pencil, in an image exclusively shared with Page Six Style.

“The heavy pigments, the beautiful colors, the consistency, the way the brand formulates each one of the products, there’s so many reasons to go back to MAC,” she added.

But the Bravoleb-turned-pop star was singing MAC’s praises long before she was a brand partner; she told E! in 2022 that Whirl was her favorite, and recently shared with us that the brand’s Subculture shade is also in her arsenal.

“I love a pink-brown lip liner that’s close to my natural lip color,” she explained of the latter. “It’s my go-to for defining my lips while still keeping the look soft.”

She’s in good company when it comes to scoring a MAC campaign, as everyone from Kris Jenner to Doja Cat to Martha Stewart has appeared in its ads over the years. (In terms of lip colors, latter is partial to Fleshpot — which her makeup artist, Daisy Toye, said she goes through “so quickly.”)

Now that’s “Baby Gorgeous.”
