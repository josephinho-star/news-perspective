China-Nepal floods: rescuers pull 2 survivors out of rubble near Tibet border crossing

2026-08-29 04:47

Chinese emergency response teams continued searching after reaching the Gyirong border checkpoint that was destroyed by a mudslide

But at the border crossing itself, there was no sign of any survivors. Liu Maolin, the rescue team leader, said the checkpoint building had been completely destroyed by a mudslide.

Seven people have been confirmed dead, with 544 still missing on the Tibetan side. In Nepal, more than 610 have died with over 2,400 missing.

The rescue teams had to battle through harsh terrain and challenging conditions, with China’s Meteorological Administration warning that Gyirong county in Tibet, one of the worst affected areas, would see light rainfall and temperatures as low as 8 degrees Celsius (46 degrees Fahrenheit) over the next three days.
