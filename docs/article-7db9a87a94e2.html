New Shows & Movies To Watch This Weekend: Netflix’s ‘Crew Girl,’ ‘Colin From Accounts’ on Paramount+ and More

2026-09-11 12:00

This week, tons of great shows both new and returning favorites, arrive on streaming. You can catch the final season of the romantic comedy series Colin From Accounts on Paramount+, satisfy your true-crime craving with A Killer Story on HBO Max, or watch the teen sports drama Crew Girl on Netflix. These are just a few of the big shows arriving on streaming this week, and they’re just the beginning of the list.

Not sure which new releases to check out? Let us here at Decider help you figure out what to watch this weekend and where to stream it.

New Movies & Shows To Stream This Weekend: Crew Girl, A Killer Story, Colin From Accounts and More

Looking for a new teen romantic drama to immerse yourself in? Netflix’s Crew Girl, out now, is the perfect weekend binge. Also arriving this week is the third and final season of Colin From Accounts on Paramount+, you can also binge every episode of that show. If you like your shows to drop weekly, check out A Killer Story on HBO Max, a weekly docu-series about a woman tried for murder and the citizen sleuths trying to prove her innocence. Episode one is out now.

New on Netflix September 10: Crew Girl

The new Netflix show Crew Girl tells the story of a 16-year-old competitive rower, played by Miku Martineau, whose family is forced to relocate to a new town after a scandal. When she arrives at her new school, she discovers that there’s no women’s rowing team, so she’s forced to join the men’s team. Drama, romance and competition ensue. All eight episodes of the show are available now.

New on HBO Max September 10: A Killer Story

A Killer Story is a new three-part documentary series on HBO Max that follows the trials of Dana Chandler, who was accused of killing her ex-husband and his girlfriend in 2002. The series follows a group of investigative journalists and citizen detectives who are convinced of Chandler’s innocence. The show is both a true crime documentary and a fascinating look at real-life true crime obsessives.

New on Paramount+ September 10: Colin From Accounts (Season 3)

The Australian sitcom Colin From Accounts, created by husband and wife team Patrick Brammall and Harriet Dyer, comes to an end this week. The show began with the chance encounter between Ashley (Dyer) and Gordon (Brammall), a couple who meet as the result of a car accident and get together to adopt an injured dog they name Colin from Accounts. Over the course of three seasons, the couple’s relationship has been complicated to say the least, and now we’ll finally see how things wrap up for the pair.
