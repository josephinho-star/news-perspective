Why Islanders likely sent Calum Ritchie down to minors just after activating him

2026-11-18 19:11

WINNIPEG, Manitoba — The Islanders activated Calum Ritchie on Wednesday before sending the 22-year-old centerman on loan to AHL Hamilton.

While the thinking behind the move wasn’t explicitly laid out, the educated guess is that the Islanders want Ritchie — who practiced Monday in a non-contact jersey — to take some hits before he comes back to the NHL, and without any practices scheduled on the two-game trip that begins Thursday against the Jets, calculated that a short AHL stint is the best way to make that happen.

Hamilton faces the Belleville Senators at home on Friday, so all eyes will be on that game.

Ritchie does need to get into at least one AHL game before being recalled to the NHL as part of the league’s rule to prevent paper transactions.

It would be quite a surprise, though, if he wasn’t back in the NHL quickly. There’s no reason to believe a player who was centering the Islanders’ second line before suffering a lower-body injury last month has fallen that far down the organization’s depth chart.

More likely, this is a precursor to his return to the Islanders, and allows the team to delay a roster move that will be necessary once Ritchie is added back onto the 23-man roster.
