‘Last victim’ claims fake 49ers player Daejon Love was still wooing her on day of arrest

2026-08-29 20:36

For an alleged scheme built on promises of a better future, Daejon Love may have been selling that fantasy right up until his own came crashing down.

A woman who describes herself as Love’s “last victim” says she remained romantically involved with the 35-year-old through Aug. 24 — the same day federal agents arrested him and his alleged accomplice Taylor Jamie Chan at Boise Airport in Idaho.

Love is accused of posing as a San Francisco 49ers player while defrauding 26 women of roughly $1.3 million through a sprawling romance and investment scheme.

The woman’s TikTok account now offers a different window into the case: not the fake contracts, football helmets or investment statements prosecutors have described, but what an alleged relationship with Love looked like from the inside.

Her video stitches together fishing trips, travel, shopping excursions and private messages with a contact saved as “Jordan,” whom she identifies as Love.

The exchanges include romantic language and conversations about “building for the future,” while the montage also features a Corvette and a diamond ring that appears to have been presented as a gift.

She does not say whether she gave Love money or suffered a financial loss.

What stands out is the timing.

Federal prosecutors say Love’s alleged operation continued until his arrest. If the woman’s account is accurate, he was still maintaining at least one romantic relationship as authorities closed in.

That fits the broader picture investigators have painted. Love allegedly used dating apps to meet women before steering some toward bogus investments, while Chan posed as his financial adviser.

The football persona was equally elaborate. The Post previously detailed how Love allegedly built an entire fake 49ers career with customized gear, staged workout videos and a purported contract signing. He also allegedly name-dropped Jordan Love, Tyreek Hill and other NFL players to reinforce the illusion.

If her account is correct, Love’s alleged scheme did not simply stretch across four years and multiple states. It appears to have continued until the very day the fantasy finally collapsed.
