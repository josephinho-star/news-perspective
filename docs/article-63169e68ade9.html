CrowdStrike Said in Talks to Buy Cybersecurity Firm Zscaler

2026-11-20 21:53

CrowdStrike Holdings Inc. is in talks to acquire Zscaler Inc., according to people familiar with the matter, in a deal that would be among the largest-ever in the cybersecurity sector. Zscaler, which had a market value of $32 billion at the end of regular trading Friday, rose about 16% after the close. The two companies are discussing a potential combination that could come together soon, the people said, asking not to be identified because the information is confidential. Bloomberg News Deals Reporter Ryan Gould reports on the deal. (Source: Bloomberg)
