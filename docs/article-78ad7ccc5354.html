I’m stuck in the Bahamas with my toddler due to the FAA shutdown — This item is saving my sanity

2026-11-16 21:29

Did I ever think I’d become the mom defending the iPad kid allegations? Nope.

I try to avoid handing my daughter a screen, but sometimes circumstances have other plans. Case in point: I’m currently stuck at the airport in the Bahamas for the second time, waiting to board a flight home after disruptions caused by the FAA shutdown.

And as anyone who’s traveled internationally solo with a toddler knows, keeping a little one entertained during an unexpected airport delay is not so easy. I came prepared with toys, snacks, books and enough surprises to fill an entire carry-on. But when you’re facing hours of waiting at the gate, even the best-planned toddler travel activities will inevitably hit their limits.

Enter the Amazon Kids Tablet: the one travel essential I can genuinely count on to keep my toddler occupied while I attempt to maintain a semblance of sanity. The newest model is currently under $230 on Amazon (and you can save 20% when you trade it in before Black Friday).

This tablet isn’t just for watching shows. She’s drawing sharks, giving her favorite Disney characters makeovers, learning new songs and playing counting games — and yes, that does make me feel a little less guilty about it. More importantly, it gives me a much-needed moment to write an article, check on our flight or simply make it through airport security without completely losing my sanity.

When traveling solo with a toddler, I’ll take the help wherever I can get it. This little tablet included.

The tablet comes with a one-year Amazon Kids+ subscription and a kid-proof bumper case that, in my experience, has successfully survived more than its fair share of drops, bumps and rough handling. It’s designed to last for years, and so far, it’s lived up to that promise.

The battery lasts up to 14 hours, which is particularly important when you’re facing long travel days or unexpected delays. It also comes with 64GB of storage, giving me plenty of room to download everything we might need ahead of time. And once she’s finally asleep, I can catch up on a show or do a little reading myself.

The educational content is another plus: There are Blippi learning games, interactive books featuring Peppa Pig and Bluey, and more to keep her entertained while sneaking some learning along the way. Alexa is also built in, allowing the tablet to read stories aloud, which is a nice bonus.

Does this make parenting a little easier? Absolutely. When you’re navigating an unexpected travel disruption or alone in an airport with a toddler, this little gadget is an absolute lifesaver.
