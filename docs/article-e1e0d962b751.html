LSU's Kiffin apologizes for using profanity during halftime interview

2026-11-23 23:08

LSU coach Lane Kiffin apologized Monday for using foul language during his halftime interview with the school's radio network in Saturday's 27-13 loss at No. 9 Tennessee.

Kiffin was upset after Tigers star safety Ty Benefield was ejected for targeting while tackling Volunteers tight end DaSaahn Brame with 26 seconds left in the first half. Three plays later, Brame caught a 2-yard touchdown to give the Vols a 10-6 lead at the half.

"Obviously, we played really well defensively," Kiffin told the LSU Sports Radio Network at halftime. "We started well offensively. Bad punt, really poor in the red zone. So, we have to come back, have to move the ball better on offense, and not get a f‐‐‐ing bulls‐‐‐ targeting call."

Video of the interview shows LSU play-by-play announcer Chris Blair removing his headset and putting his hands over his head following Kiffin's comments.

"I want to apologize for my language at halftime in the interview," Kiffin told reporters Monday in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. "No excuses. I was frustrated with the first half and really couldn't even hear the question, kind of like our center down in the student section. But there's no reason for that. And so, to people who were offended, I apologize for that."

It was LSU's third straight loss, which dropped its record to 6-5 in Kiffin's first season. The Tigers close the regular season at Arkansas on Saturday.

The Volunteers scored two touchdowns in the fourth quarter to pull away from LSU. The Tigers were also tied or led in the fourth quarter of losses to Ole Miss, Kentucky and Texas.

"People say, 'What's the problem with this season, and are you really concerned about the program?'" Kiffin said. "And I keep saying, 'I'm not concerned about the future of the program. I'm excited about the future of the program, but we have one core issue to fix, and that's how we finish games. We're in these games against really, really good teams, and we don't play well late, and that can be part of a mindset that we don't have right."

Kiffin, who signed a seven-year, $91 million contract with LSU after he was lured away from Ole Miss in November 2025, is the first Tigers coach to lose five games in his first season since Curley Hallman went 5-6 in 1991.
