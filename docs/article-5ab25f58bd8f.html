Lenovo’s 2-in-1 Yoga 7i is a great Labor Day deal at $750

2026-09-01 20:54

Best Buy is celebrating Labor Day all week long with big deals, including a deep discount on the Lenovo Yoga 7i 2-in-1, taking its original $1,1199.99 price down to $749.99. It’s a capable configuration for the price, with a Core 7 Ultra 256V processor, 16GB of LPDDR5X memory, and a 512GB SSD. The IPS touchscreen has a 1920 x 1200 resolution with a 60Hz refresh rate, good enough for scrolling, taking notes, or catching up on YouTube videos. It can be tough to find a laptop at this price that doesn’t cut corners, and this model fits well within our criteria for a laptop suited for school and casual use.

Save almost $500 on a great laptop for note-taking, studying, or procrastinating on your homework.

Notably, this model is a 2-in-1 and features a 360-degree hinge, letting you fold the device all the way around to use it like a tablet, or prop it up on a surface for watching content. With an advertised battery life of up to 12.5 hours per charge, you shouldn’t have any issues getting through a full day of classes and studying, or just mucking about on Reddit.
