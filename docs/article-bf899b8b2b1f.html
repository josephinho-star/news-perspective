Meghan Markle spotted in UK for first time since move as she mingles with fellow A-list transplant

2026-09-21 17:36

Meghan Markle broke cover in the United Kingdom on Monday, mingling with fellow A-list expat Ellen DeGeneres at a pub.

The duo were spotted chatting at The Three Horshoes near Burford, Oxfordshire, on Monday, along with DeGenere’s wife, Portia de Rossi, in photos obtained by Page Six.

On Monday, Markle cut a casual figure in a white pants and a light blue top, with her sleeves rolled up to her elbows.

The Duchess of Sussex wore her hair down, completing her look with white sneakers and a pair of sunglasses.

Markle’s rep did not immediately respond to Page Six’s request for comment.

The “Deal or No Deal” alum, notably, has remained out of the spotlight in recent weeks while Harry, meanwhile, stepped out for back-to-back events.

The Duke of Sussex’s first official outing was at the Invictus Spirit Gala Dinner & Awards on Thursday.

The 42-year-old, suited up in a tuxedo, told People at the event that it felt “good to be back on British soil,” where he and Markle landed with their two kids — Prince Archie, 7, and Princess Lilibet, 5 — last month.

Harry was all smiles at the launch of the INSPIRE Education Programme the following day, and he went on to attend the WellChild Awards on Monday.

At last year’s charity event, Harry dropped a major hint at his and Markle’s relocation to England while chatting with singer Joss Stone about her move back to their homeland.

“He was saying how wonderful the schools are here and how important community is for children,” she revealed in September 2025, saying the prince felt “drawn to” return.
