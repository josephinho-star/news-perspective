United Airlines says system outage resolved

2026-11-16 23:18

United Airlines on Monday said a system outage has been resolved, an issue that comes days after the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) issued a ground stop that heavily impacted the airline’s services.

United told The Hill that it had “resolved a technology issue that delayed flight departures” but that there was “no impact on flights in progress, or on United Express flights.”

“There is no indication that this was a cybersecurity-related issue,” the airline continued.

When questioned about the outage on social media, United said it was aware of a “widespread system outage” and it was “working hard to get things back up and running as soon as possible.”

This comes after Friday’s nationwide ground stop caused flights across the country to be cancelled or delayed for several hours on Friday. It was only the third such stoppage in U.S. history, and its effects still were being felt days later.

United wrote on Sunday that most of its flights “this weekend are operating normally following” the ground stop but “some flights will continue to be delayed or canceled as we work to restore our aircraft and crew schedules.”

“Safety is our highest priority, and we’re grateful for the extraordinary work of our employees and your continued patience,” it added.

FAA officials indicated that preliminary findings pointed to a router at an Arkansas radio facility and a radar site in Amarillo, Texas as reasons for the excessive data flooding the systems, which was behind the nationwide ground stop.

Issues at both sites prompted the FAA to take them both offline, but a soaring rate of connections caused widespread failures, leading to the ground stop, The New York Times reported.

More than 10,000 flights within, into or out of the United States were delayed Sunday, and more than 2,300 were canceled, according to the flight-tracking website FlightAware. The incident marked the third nationwide grund stop, following a 90-minute outage in 2023 and on Sept. 11, 2001.
