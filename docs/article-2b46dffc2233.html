Who Got Eliminated On ‘Dancing With The Stars’ Night 2?

2026-09-17 02:15

It’s a sad night in the Dancing with the Stars ballroom once again.

In the end, it was Sarah Jane Nader and partner Hailey Bills who were eliminated after hitting the dance floor and giving it their all with their Week 1 dance, which landed them a score of 14/30. The final moments showed the duo as they were embraced by their fellow dancers and contestants, who now go on to Week 2, which will eliminate at least one more couple from contention for the Mirrorball Trophy.

The elimination comes after a tough first night, which also featured an elimination, marking the first time the show has sent competitors packing in Week 1 since Season 32, when Veep star and comedian Matt Walsh was booted immediately.

Online, fans of the long-running reality program expressed mixed emotions over the double elimination in the first week of Season 35, with some protesting and calling it unfair and others saying that it gives the show an exciting twist, especially with more couples dancing overall this season.

As for where things stand going into Week 2 and who all is left in the show after Dancing with the Stars Week 1, scroll on as we’ve got answers to all of your most pressing DWTS Season 35 questions…

Who Went Home On Dancing with the Stars Night 2?

The second night of competition ended with Sarah Jane Nader and partner Hailey Bills leaving the ballroom following their first dance of the series. As the credits rolled, the pair embraced and held back tears as their fellow dancers rushed to console them. Clearly, this short-lived experience was meaningful to Nader, who will no doubt pop up in the ballroom again later this season.

Who Went Home On Dancing with the Stars Night 1?

It was Secret Lives of Mormon Wives star Conner Leavitt and Adele Zaikman, the winner of Dancing with the Stars: The Next Pro, who were eliminated first in the competition after receiving a 12/30 for their cha-cha.

Will Dancing with the Stars Week 2 Be Two Nights As Well?

Nope, just Week 1 of Dancing with the Stars Season 35 is a two-night affair! Things will be back to regularly scheduled programming, a.k.a. one night a week, starting on September 22.
