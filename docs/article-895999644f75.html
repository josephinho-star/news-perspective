Pete Davidson reveals ‘brutal’ consequence of removing all his tattoos in $200K saga

2026-11-11 21:00

“You don’t know what’s going on under there,” he quipped, referring to the distinct stages your skin undergoes in between procedures.

While Davidson’s skin is nearly free of tattoos after he started the multi-year process in 2020, he joked that “they might come back.”

In fact, the 32-year-old actor recently debuted his daughter Scottie’s name inked on the side of his ear during an appearance at Las Vegas CinemaCon in April.

At the time, he took the stage to show off his nearly bare arms, wearing a red T-shirt with small patches of his ink that were hardly visible.

Davidson welcomed his daughter with his ex-girlfriend Elsie Hewitt in December 2025.

During a January 2025 appearance on “The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon,” Davidson told the TV personality that he was “trying to clean-slate it” and his ink was “almost gone” now.

“I’ve been burning them off,” he explained, sharing that the removal process felt “worse” than getting the tattoos.

Davidson warned the audience to really consider the tattoos they want, rather than making impulse decisions, referring to himself as a “sad boy” when he got inked.
