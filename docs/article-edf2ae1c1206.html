When Does The Next Season of ‘Mayor of Kingstown’ Premiere on Netflix and Paramount+? ‘Mayor of Kingstown’ Season 5 Release Date

2026-11-01 18:00

Life is good, Paramount+ subscribers.

We’re in the midst of another intriguing season of the Tom Hardy-led action thriller MobLand; Sylvester Stallone’s popular crime drama Tulsa King recently returned for Season 4; and Paramount+ Premium subscribers are welcoming back their favorite fictional vigilante because Dexter: Resurrection is back for a second season of serial killer chaos.

There’s been some news in the wild world of Mayor of Kingstown, too.

Not only did Paramount recently drop a bevy of first-look images for the upcoming final season, but the first four seasons of Taylor Sheridan and Hugh Dillon’s beloved series are now on Netflix. But what about new episodes of Mayor of Kingstown?

We thought you’d never ask. From release date to complete streaming info, here’s when the next season of Mayor of Kingstown debuts on Paramount+.

How Many Seasons Are There of Mayor of Kingstown?

There are currently four seasons streaming on Netflix and Paramount+, with one final installment on the way.

When Does The Next Season of Mayor of Kingstown Come Out on Paramount+?

We now have an official release date! Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 debuts Friday, January 15, 2027, on Paramount+.

When Will Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 Come Out on Netflix?

As we mentioned above, the first four seasons of Mayor of Kingstown are now available on Netflix. The fifth season premieres on Paramount+ in January, and since new episodes are exclusive to Paramount, Mayor of Kingstown won’t be on Netflix anytime soon.

It’s possible that the fifth season eventually finds its way to Netflix, but that wouldn’t be until later in 2027 at the earliest.

How To Watch Mayor of Kingstown For Free:

Seasons 1-4 of Mayor of Kingstown are now streaming on Netflix and Paramount+.

Paramount+ offers two subscription plans, Essential and Premium. Right now, you can get started with a seven-day free trial on the Premium plan.

The ad-supported Essential plan typically costs $8.99/month, while the ad-free Premium plan (which comes with Showtime titles and live CBS) costs $13.99/month.

You can also add Paramount+ to your Hulu or Amazon accounts for $13.99/month. Both streamers offer free trials for new subscribers.

Is Paramount+ on YouTube?

Yes! You can currently purchase Paramount+ on YouTube for $10.99/month for the first three months. YouTube also offers a free trial.

How Many Episodes Will There Be of Mayor of Kingstown Season 5?

The final season consists of eight episodes.

Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 Cast Info:

The final season of Mayor of Kingstown will feature a few new faces. David Morse has joined the cast as seasoned FBI agent Russell Hardy, and Nestor Carbonell will portray Enrique Molina, a “boss who comes to Kingstown to avenge a relative who has gone missing.”

Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 debuts Friday, January 15, 2027, on Paramount+.
