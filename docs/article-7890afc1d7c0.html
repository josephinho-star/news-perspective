Fever's Sophie Cunningham wears sensational cheetah-print outfit ahead of playoff game vs Aces

2026-09-27 19:57

Sophie Cunningham has owned the pregame runway for the Indiana Fever throughout the 2026 regular season.

She showed up to Game 1 of the WNBA playoffs against the Las Vegas Aces ready for the spotlight.

The Fever guard was seen wearing a long cheetah-print coat with cheetah-print shorts before tip-off between Indiana and Las Vegas. The fantastic fit came days after a sweater she wore before the penultimate game of the regular season against the Minnesota Lynx, which read "Kick Rocks."

Indiana will certainly need the precision of a cheetah to help them get past the Aces in their three-game series in the first round of the playoffs. Las Vegas has built a dynasty in the WNBA, winning three of the last four championships.

The Aces finished the 2026 season with a record of 31-13, and were just short of getting the No. 1 seed in the playoffs by two games. The Lynx finished 33-11 and the Golden State Valkyries finished second in the WNBA with a 32-12 record.

Indiana is looking for a massive upset with its team getting healthy. Fever head coach Stephanie White said Aliyah Boston will play in Game 1 after missing some time with a leg injury. Caitlin Clark has put together her best season in the WNBA so far and Kelsey Mitchell set the single-season record for most points scored in a season.

Cunningham will need to be a sparkplug off the bench.

Amid her own wild season, Cunningham averaged 7.9 points and 1.2 assists in 43 games for the Fever. She shot 46% from the field and 41.9% from 3-point range in 2026.
