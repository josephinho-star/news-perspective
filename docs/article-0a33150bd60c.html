Mater Dei’s coaching carousel continues with surprising decision with Raul Lara

2026-11-17 23:54

Mater Dei announced on Tuesday that two-year head coach Raul Lara will no longer coach the school’s football program.

According to Steve Fryer of The Orange County Register, the school “described the parting as a mutual agreement” between the two sides.

“Mater Dei High School announces the departure of Raul Lara as head football coach. Lara joined Mater Dei in 2024 and led the Monarchs to an undefeated 13-0 season, capturing Trinity League, CIF Southern Section Division 1, CIF State Open Division and National Championships in his first season,” the school said in a statement.

“We thank Coach Lara for his service to Mater Dei High School and his contributions to our football program. We wish him the best in future endeavors,” said President Michael Brennan in the statement.

In Lara’s place, Randy Wright has been appointed as the school’s interim head coach while they begin searching for a new leader of the historic football program.

Mater Dei, the only high school to produce three Heisman Trophy winners in the country, came crashing to Earth two years after the undefeated season. Nevertheless, it appears the expectations are sky high.

Lara’s departure comes after the school finished with an 8-3 record that included a 25-8 loss to Bishop Gorman from Nevada, while going 4-1 in the Trinity League with a 14-7 loss to reigning CIF Division 1 Champion, Santa Margarita.

Last Friday, the Monarchs were shockingly blown out of the water by Corona Centennial, losing 45-15 in a quarterfinals matchup of the CIF Southern Section Division 1 playoffs.

Fryer reported that Lara told Monarchs players of his departure on Tuesday morning.

Lara’s tenure with the Monarchs comes after he replaced Frank McManus, who coached there for only one year in 2023.

McManus came on the scene after they parted ways with Bruce Rollinson, who coached at the school for 33 years from 1989 to 2022.

Through their trying year, Mater Dei rotated between three different quarterbacks last season and eventually stuck with freshman Paytyn Guess.

According to MaxPreps, Guess played in 11 games this season and completed 73 of 125 passes for 1,108 yards with 10 touchdowns and four interceptions.

According to Fryer, Guess was replaced in Friday’s game against Centennial with senior Cameron Polley.

The Monarchs struggled to get their running game going, as they ran for just 20 yards on 34 attempts.
