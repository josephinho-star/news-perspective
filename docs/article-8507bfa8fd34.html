‘The Body’s Marcel Ruiz on the viral petition to cancel the show: ‘That is such a flex’

2026-11-20 01:14

Netflix’s The Body may have audiences around the world begging for more after word of the series spread like wildfire online, but it also has some begging for the streaming service to cancel the series.

Just one week after debuting at No. 3 on the charts and reaching 5.1 million viewers in its first week — the show has since taken the No. 1 spot and become a global phenomenon — pushback to the series, which follows an all-girls dance team at a Catholic school experiencing religious hysteria, has been swift. One viral petition has been signed more than 120,000 times, which star Marcel Ruiz thinks is actually pretty impressive.

Chatting with DECIDER about the success of the show and his role as Tyler Flores, the young actor shared his candid and hilarious response to those who want to see the show ended (or fully removed) by the platform for being a “blasphemous anti-Christian series featuring Satanic ritual.”

“I wonder if all of those people that have signed the petition have seen the show? That’s like, something I wonder. And also, in a way, it doesn’t surprise me, not because the show is disrespectful, or because the show deserves to have a petition like that, but that’s sometimes art,” Ruiz explains, adding that it’s certainly made waves around him, as well. “I did hear a funny story; my dad told me that this friend of his told him that in his daughter’s private Catholic school, they were telling the kids not to watch it. And I thought, ‘That’s such a flex.’ I loved hearing that.”

For the One Day At a Time alum, it actually makes him feel like the Quinn Shepard project might be doing something right or talking about things that people really care about if so many are up in arms. And hey, even if there are viewers who are hate-watching or tuning in out of morbid curiosity, one way or another, it’s making their little show that could go up the charts.

“Sometimes, things that are moving the ball forward are also going to have some pushback in some areas. You can’t please everyone at the end of the day, I think. It’s good enough that the show is number one on Netflix. Can’t ask for much else,” he shares.

Although the cast is asking for one more thing: a Season 2 renewal swiftly. After ending Season 1 (spoiler alert) with Grace (Sofia Wylie) waking up from her coma and proclaiming “I am God,” Ruiz and his co-stars are desperate to explore what comes next for the St. Mark’s Lions.

“We’re waiting by the phone,” the actor shares. “What was so cool is when we were shooting Season 1, you can’t help yourself but think of a future for these characters and ideas. And I remember the day that I wrapped, I went home and I was so emotional that it was over that I just went on my phone and started writing ideas for Tyler for Season 2. Just like as a way to cope and like have some closure. Definitely, I’m excited to, when the time comes, be able to pick that back up.”

Netflix, don’t make us wait any longer!
