Heroes, zeros from Yankees’ Subway Series win: Cody Bellinger mashes key home run

2026-09-12 04:34

Heroes, zeros and the inside pitch from the Yankees’ 6-4 win over the Mets on Friday night in the Subway Series in The Bronx:

Cody Bellinger, whose father Clay was a member of the 2001 Yankees, hit a three-run homer in the second inning that gave the Yankees a lead they didn’t give up.

After allowing just five runs — four earned — over his previous five starts, Nolan McLean had an uncharacteristic poor start, as the Yankees tagged him for five runs in five innings. McLean also gave up a career-worst 10 hits.

Carlos Rodón had his longest outing of the season, tossing 6 ¹/₃ ⅓ innings, despite Aaron Boone saying he’s not fully built up.

5: With Paul Blackburn and Brent Headrick struggling, David Bednar was called on with one out in the eighth inning and the bases loaded. He got out of that inning and despite giving up a leadoff homer to A.J. Ewing in the ninth, picked up his second five-out save of the year.

“The energy in the crowd was special. I’m glad it felt like … you want a ballgame to feel like. I thought a lot of people were here having a great time on what, for many, is a difficult day.”

— Aaron Boone on the atmosphere at the Stadium.
