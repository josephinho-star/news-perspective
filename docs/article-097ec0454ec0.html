Sean ‘Diddy’ Combs’ twin daughters match in minis at NYFW as rapper remains in prison

2026-09-15 21:05

Sean “Diddy” Combs’ twin daughters, D’Lila and Jessie Combs, hit up New York Fashion Week in matching looks.

The 19-year-old sisters were photographed at the LaQuan Smith spring/summer 2027 fashion show at Brookfield Place on Monday night.

In true twin fashion, they wore similarly designed dresses, with D’Lila choosing red and Jessie sporting black. They accessorized with gold Alexis Bittar purses and drop earrings.

The teenagers finished off their plunging looks with strappy heels and styled their hair in the same low ponytail style, both adding chandelier earrings.

D’Lila and Jessie put on a united front by holding hands as they made their way into the Big Apple venue.

The duo’s outing comes after Page Six exclusively reported that they were launching their own fashion brand, 12TWINTY1, as their famous father remains behind bars on charges of transportation to engage in prostitution.

D’Lila told us that she believes she and Jessie can separate themselves from Combs’ image, saying, “His press is his press and our press is our press.”

“Our journey is separate,” she continued. “We try to stay positive, and we’re focused on what we’re building.”

The forthcoming business has been in the works for three years.

“Our dad has always had incredible style, and our mom [Kim Porter] did too, so they’ve both inspired us,” she added, referring to the late model who died in 2018 from natural causes due to pneumoniaat the age of 47

The twins shared that Combs has been giving them advice on building their company from prison, with Jessie adding, “He’s very proud of us. We’ve gone to him for guidance many times, and he’s been incredibly helpful.”

In October 2025, Combs was sentenced to four years and two months after he was held at the Metropolitan Detention Center in Brooklyn following his September 2024 arrest.

The Bad Boys Records founder was transferred to Fort Dix, a low-security prison, in New Jersey, in November 2025, with a release date set for May, 8, 2028.
