Tom Brady rips into Aaron Rodgers for breaking ‘the sanctity of an NFL locker room’

2026-11-27 02:42

Another Hall of Fame quarterback is coming out against Aaron Rodgers’ recent comments.

This time it was Tom Brady, who kept it professional but made clear that Rodgers’ takedown of the Steelers after his benching needed to stay in house.

“The sanctity of an NFL locker room, it’s a very sacred place,” Brady said on the Fox broadcast of the Eagles’ 39-38 win over the Cowboys on Thanksgiving in Dallas. “We shouldn’t ever lose track of that as players. It’s a privilege to be in that locker room. You’re fighting with your teammates, you’re fighting with your coaches, you’re always acting as a team together. Shouldn’t always be the place for private things to become public, that’s what I’ll say.”

The Steelers announced on Tuesday night that they were benching Rodgers against the Broncos in favor of Mason Rudolph, and Rodgers responded on Wednesday by going scorched earth on head coach Mike McCarthy during an appearance on “The Pat McAfee Show.”

Among Rodgers’ gripes was the 19-year coach falling asleep in team meetings and leading a “1, 2, 3, Packers” chant after a huddle. The Steelers cut Rodgers soon after the appearance on “McAfee,” and he cleared waivers Thursday to become a free agent.

Brady, who knows a thing or two about working with longtime coaches, acknowledged that feuds can happen in the locker room, but that doesn’t mean things need to boil over like they did in Pittsburgh.

“I wasn’t a perfect player, [Bill Belichick] wasn’t a perfect coach, but we did some things in the game that we’re so proud of now,” Brady said. “And even me being four years removed from the game, things that matter more than winning a Super Bowl or trophy is my relationship with my teammates, my coaches. That’s going to transcend anything you do on the field.

“It’s just unfortunate. I hate it, I hate to be even talking about it. It’s a privilege to be in the NFL. You want to inspire the next generation of athletes, and you always want to do it the right way.”

Brady wasn’t the only NFL legend who had something to say Thursday on Fox. Michael Strahan, Howie Long, and former Steelers quarterback Terry Bradshaw took time out of the pregame show to discuss Rodgers’ comments.

While Rodgers now has the ability to sign with any team, his release by the Steelers may spell the end of his NFL career. ESPN insider Adam Schefter reported Thursday that it’s unlikely Rodgers finds a landing spot to finish out the season.
