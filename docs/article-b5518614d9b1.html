Kyle Richards’ daughter Farrah gives birth to first baby after ‘RHOBH’ star made frantic dash to the hospital mid-event

2026-11-17 15:16

While the “Buying Beverly Hills” alum has yet to officially announce the arrival of her son, her mom spoke to us on the red carpet at the Maison D’Or cocktail launch in Los Angeles Saturday night, sharing her excitement about becoming a grandmother.

“I’m on baby watch right now,” Richards gushed. “I can’t believe [I’m gonna be a grandma]. I hope it’s not tonight, because am I gonna go to the hospital like this?”

A Page Six spy then spotted Richards excitedly saying goodbye to her “RHOBH” co-stars about an hour into the event, which was being filmed for the show’s next season, before rushing to the elevator in a hurry.

Our source overheard someone ask Richards if her daughter was in labor and she replied, “I think so!”

Richards’ older sister, Kathy Hilton, also told us on the carpet that she was late to the party because she was on the phone with the hospital.

“I’ve been calling to make sure this is not a false alarm because I’m gonna call the hospital to make sure that she gets right in and has a room because, you know, we’re very close to the hospital,” Hilton told us.
