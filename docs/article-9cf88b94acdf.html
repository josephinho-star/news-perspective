Is ‘SNL’ New Tonight? Here’s When ‘Saturday Night Live’ Returns With New Episodes

2026-09-19 23:30

If you find yourself missing Saturday Night Live, you’ll be happy to know that the season premiere of SNL UK is now streaming on Peacock! The first episode of Season 2 features host Jeff Goldblum and musical guest CMAT, with Jamie Demetriou and Amelia Dimoldenberg set to host the next two installments.

New episodes of Saturday Night Live UK debut Sundays at 1:01 p.m. ET on Peacock.

In original SNL news, the classic late-night institution added two new featured players to the cast — Saidah Belo-Osagie and Grace Reiter — while Ashley Padilla and Jane Wickline have been promoted to repertory players.

When exactly does Saturday Night Live return with new episodes? Who are the first four hosts for Season 52? Here’s everything you need to know.

Is SNL New Tonight (September 19)?

Nope. Sadly, tonight’s episode of Saturday Night Live is a repeat (Season 51’s Will Ferrell/Paul McCartney episode).

When Does Saturday Night Live Return With New Episodes?

Next week! Jalen Brunson hosts the Season 52 premiere of SNL on Saturday, September 26. Katseye will be the musical guest.

Saturday Night Live Release Schedule:

- Episode 2 (October 3): Dakota Johnson/Turnstile

- Episode 3 (October 10): Shane Gillis/Rosalía

- Episode 4 (October 31): Inde Navarrette/Gracie Abrams

How To Watch Saturday Night Live Online:

If you have a valid cable login, you can watch Saturday Night Live on NBC or the NBC website/app. Already cut the cord? No problem!

You can also watch NBC live with an active subscription to DIRECTV, fuboTV, Hulu + Live TV, YouTube TV, or Sling TV. YouTube TV, fuboTV, and Hulu + Live TV offer free trials for new subscribers.

Can I Watch Saturday Night Live on Peacock?

You can stream new episodes of SNL live or on demand on Peacock. The upcoming Jalen Brunson-hosted episode will be available to watch on demand on Peacock beginning Sunday, September 27.

The season premiere of Saturday Night Live airs September 26 on NBC and Peacock.
