Doctors warned Ozzy Osbourne not to perform final concert just 17 days before his death, son Jack reveals

2026-10-23 07:49

Christina Dugan Ramirez, Fox News

Published Oct. 23, 2026, 3:49 a.m. ET

See more of our coverage in your search results.

Jack Osbourne is reflecting on the weeks leading up to his father Ozzy Osbourne’s death, revealing the stark warning doctors gave the rock legend before his final performance.

“They basically were just saying like you’ve got so many things going on,” Jack explained. “We recommend you don’t do this because the energy expenditure.”

Jack revealed that his father had been dealing with multiple medical complications leading up to the show.

“There was like something with his heart and he had some like artery stuff going on,” he said, adding that Ozzy had experienced “a ton of medical kind of mishaps leading up to the show.”

“They were just like energetically, this is going to … this is not good. This is not good. Like you don’t have it in you,” Jack recalled of the doctors’ concerns.

Although Jack admitted he was heartbroken over his father’s death, he said he wasn’t angry about the rock legend’s decision to perform despite the medical warnings.

“But he wanted his journey. And so I can’t … I’m not angry at that,” Jack explained.

“I think everyone wants to at least have some say in how they go,” he continued. “Obviously, deeply heartbroken that he’s gone but also he went the way he wanted to.”

The 10-hour show, hosted by Jason Momoa, featured performances from Metallica, Guns N’ Roses, Jack Black, Steven Tyler and more.

For the closing set, according to a photo posted on X and various videos, the legendary rocker was lifted onto the stage while seated on a black throne adorned with skulls and topped with a black bat. He performed hits including “Crazy Train” and “Mama I’m Coming Home” while remaining seated throughout the set.

“It’s so good to be on this stage… You have no idea how I feel,” Ozzy told the crowd, according to Planet Rock Radio. “Thank you from the bottom of my heart.”

The Black Sabbath frontman died on July 22, 2025. He was 76.

“It is with more sadness than mere words can convey that we have to report that our beloved Ozzy Osbourne has passed away this morning,” his family said in a statement provided to Fox News Digital at the time. “He was with his family and surrounded by love. We ask everyone to respect our family privacy at this time.”

Jack’s emotional reflections come as his mother, Sharon Osbourne, has also opened up about the final moments she shared with her husband of 43 years.

During an appearance on “Piers Morgan Uncensored,” Sharon, 73, broke down in tears as she recalled her last conversation with the rock legend.

Sharon explained that Ozzy had been “up and down” throughout the night before waking her around 4:30 a.m.

“He said, ‘Wake up’ and [I said] ‘I’m already bloody awake, you’ve woken me up,'” Sharon remembered. “And he said, ‘Kiss me.’ And then he said, ‘Hug me tight.'”

Sharon emotionally confirmed that those were the last words her husband said to her before he died later that morning.

“He went downstairs and worked out for 20 minutes and passed away,” she said.

Sharon recalled hearing “screaming” inside their home before rushing downstairs and discovering that attempts were being made to revive her husband.

“They were trying to resuscitate him and I’m like ‘Don’t. Leave him. Leave him. You can’t. He’s gone.’ I knew instantly he’s gone,” she recalled.

“And they tried and tried and then they took him by helicopter to the hospital and they tried and it’s like, ‘He’s gone. Just leave him.'”

Sharon admitted that her grief following Ozzy’s death was so overwhelming that she “couldn’t function.” She also struggled with regrets about their final exchange.

“If only I’d have told him I loved him more. If only I’d have held him tighter,” she said through tears.
