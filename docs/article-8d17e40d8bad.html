New Movies on Streaming: ‘The End of Oak Street,’ ‘Tony,’ and More

2026-09-18 15:00

Anne Hathaway fighting dinosaurs? Yes, please! The actress stars alongside Ewan McGregor, Maisy Stella and Christian Convery in the sci-fi survival story of a family who unexpectedly find themselves living in the age of T-Rex in The End of Oak Street, one of this week’s new movies on VOD. Also out this week is the Mubi slasher-satire Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma, plus the critically acclaimed Anthony Bourdain biopic Tony, and the rom-com Finding Emily.

Angourie Rice stars as a college student named Emily Raine trying to help a fellow student, Owen (Spike Fearn), locate a woman he had a chance encounter with, also named Emily, in Finding Emily. In Owen’s quest to find Emily, he decides to try to contact all the Emilys on his campus, at Emily Raine’s urging. As you might imagine, it turns out the Emily he was looking for might be the one right in front of him all along.

These are just a few of the films that are available to watch on Amazon Prime Video, iTunes, YouTube, and through your cable service this week. Check out what movies are available to buy or rent on demand now.

In The End of Oak Street, Anne Hathaway and Ewan McGregor star as a couple whose house, and all the houses in their neighborhood, are transported back in time to the age of dinosaurs. As they search for a way to reverse the unexpected time travel, they also need to contend with the carnivorous beasts who want to eat them for lunch. The film arrived on digital earlier this week.

Matt Johnson, who previously directed BlackBerry and starred in Nirvanna The Band The Show The Movie, directs the new A24 biopic Tony, about chef Anthony Bourdain’s early years working as a cook in Provincetown, MA. Based on some of the stories that appeared in Bourdain’s book Kitchen Confidential, Dominic Sessa plays the young Bourdain and Antonio Banderas plays his boss, chef José Vatel. The cast also includes Leo Woodall, Emilia Jones and Stavros Halkias.
