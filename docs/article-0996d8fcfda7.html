Trade 20 minutes of doomscrolling for 3 minutes with Socrates for $30

2026-08-29 17:50

TL;DR: Roots turns 2,500 years of philosophy into 217 bite-sized lessons from 22 thinkers, with guided reflections, audio narration, and more. Lifetime access is $29.99 (reg. $59) for a limited time.

Before you spend another 20 minutes doomscrolling, you could spend three with Socrates, Marcus Aurelius, or Buddha. Roots Daily Reflections & Philosophy App turns centuries of philosophy into bite-sized daily lessons on everything from anxiety and anger to purpose and difficult decisions. For a limited time, you can get a lifetime subscription for only $29.99 (reg. $59).

You don’t necessarily need to work your way through a shelf of philosophy books or enroll in a Philosophy class to borrow a little wisdom from some of history’s greatest thinkers. Roots distills 2,500 years of philosophy into lessons that take just 2 to 3 minutes each.

The app includes 217 handwritten lessons from 22 thinkers, spanning Stoic, Buddhist, Taoist, existentialist, and modern traditions. You’ll encounter ideas from names like Marcus Aurelius, Socrates, Buddha, Aristotle, Spinoza, Laozi, and Simone Weil, without having to wade through academic jargon or pretend you understood it the first time.

These aren’t just condensed history lessons, either. Roots focuses on applying philosophical ideas to problems that still feel pretty familiar today, including anxiety, anger, loss, purpose, difficult decisions, and mental clarity. Each lesson pairs an explanation with a real-life example and guided reflection.

If you’d rather have some structure, there are 27 guided paths that group lessons around specific challenges and themes, allowing ideas to build on one another rather than serving up a stream of inspirational quotes that could have come from your aunt’s Facebook feed.

And on days when even 3 minutes of reading feels like too much, every lesson comes with audio narration that runs in the background or with your screen off. You can listen during your commute, on a walk, or while doing something around the house. Aristotle probably didn’t envision himself accompanying you while you fold laundry, but here we are.

Normally $59, grab a lifetime subscription to the Roots Daily Reflections & Philosophy App for only $29.99.
