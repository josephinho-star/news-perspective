Martha Stewart ‘dined and dashed’ at swanky NYC restaurant Polo Bar, ‘RHONY’ newbie Hailey Glassman claims

2026-09-30 16:57

Hailey Glassman claims Martha Stewart “dined and dashed” when she met up with the “Real Housewives of New York City” cast at the Polo Bar in New York City.

The “RHONY” newbie spoke exclusively to Page Six’s “Virtual Reali-Tea” about her night out with the lifestyle icon ahead of filming for Season 16 earlier this year.

“We went to the Polo Bar before filming,” Glassman, 39, recalled. “Martha stopped by, and she dined and dashed, actually. She ordered some food and we all, like, covered it.”

In addition to Glassman and Stewart, “RHONY” stars Erin Lichy, Jessel Taank, Daisy Toye, Erika Hammond and Sai De Silva were also in attendance at the group dinner.

A rep for Stewart didn’t immediately respond to Page Six’s request for comment.

While Glassman said she never filmed with Stewart for the show, the media mogul has made a few cameos in the new season.

That’s because Toye — who joined “RHONY” alongside Glassman and Hammond in Season 16 — works as Stewart’s makeup artist.

In fact, her first scene in the show was with Stewart, who came to her rent-controlled apartment to get her makeup done and, of course, gab about Ryan Murphy’s “Love Story.”

Stewart revealed in the scene that she spoke to “Love Story” subject John F. Kennedy Jr. a few days before he and his wife, Carolyn Bessette, as well as her sister, Lauren, perished in a plane crash.

“I was with John-John the Monday before the Friday when they died. He called me,” Stewart said, sharing that Kennedy wanted advice about his political-lifestyle magazine, George.

Stewart said they met up for dinner that week, and when she asked him about his weekend plans, he said he was flying his wife and sister-in-law to a wedding in Martha’s Vineyard.

Stewart has popped into “RHONY” several times since then, with Toye calling her for cooking advice and the two women grabbing a bite to eat together in the latest episode.
