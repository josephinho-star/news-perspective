Illegal immigrant suspected of distributing ‘Shoot Trump’ stickers arrested by ICE

2026-11-24 06:15

A Venezuelan national was arrested by federal authorities after an investigation linked the illegal immigrant to the distribution of stickers “intended to encourage the assassination of President Trump,” the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) announced Monday.

Hector Alejandro Mujica-Hernandez was arrested in Provo, Utah, by Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) and Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) agents on Oct. 16, after the FBI shared information about his alleged assassination threat with the two federal law enforcement agencies.

Mujica-Hernandez allegedly distributed stickers reading, “Shoot Trump, be a hero, become unforgetable [sic]” during a “People of Provo Against ICE” meeting.

DHS noted the letter “A” sticker was stylized as an upside-down anarchist symbol.

Mujica-Hernandez entered the US legally in July 2016, but overstayed his visa, according to the department.

“This illegal alien not only broke our laws by illegally overstaying his visa, but threatened the life of the President of the United States,” DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin said in a statement.

“The Department of Homeland Security will not tolerate death threats of any kind,” Mullin continued. “Under the leadership of President Trump, our brave ICE officers are working tirelessly to make America safe again.”

The DHS secretary further urged the media and the far left to “stop vilifying ICE law enforcement officers and President Trump.”

People of Provo Against ICE appears to host weekly meetings and claims it is working to stop immigration enforcement activity in the city “through any legal means,” according to the group’s Instagram account.

The group also shared a link to a fundraiser organized to pay Mujica-Hernandez’s legal fees, describing his detainment as “unjust.”

“On October 16th, DHS entrapped and abducted Hector from our local coffee shop,” the organizer, Kyler Jensen, wrote. “He is a pillar to our community, and we just want him home and safe.”
