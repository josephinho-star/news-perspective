Tommy Paul has 1-word reaction to wife Paige’s US Open crop top look before Alcaraz clash

2026-09-06 12:00

The biggest day of American tennis star Tommy Paul’s summer was when he got married to his longtime girlfriend, Paige Lorenze.

The second biggest day of Paul’s summer might be when he faces Carlos Alcaraz in the U.S. Open Round of 16 on Sunday. As good as Paul has been playing lately, no match of his will get more attention than when he’s facing the 23-year-old Spanish superstar.

Then again, it’s Paige Paul who is getting attention before her husband’s match against Alcaraz, given the Instagram post she made showing off the crop top outfit she wore for Paul’s third-round match against Alexander Bublik.

The post showed Paige wearing a white cropped blouse with puffed sleeves, along with a high-waisted navy skirt.

“🙂↔️🙂↔️🙂↔️🙂↔️🙂↔️,” the post was captioned.

It didn’t take Tommy Paul long to comment, as he simply wrote, “Woah” in the Instagram post’s comments a few minutes after it was posted.

Tommy and Paige Paul have been together since 2022, and Paige has spent the past four years becoming one of the most prominent and popular tennis WAGs in the sport.

She supports Paul at nearly all of his tournaments and has built an impressive Instagram following of over 1.3 million.

Perhaps being a married man will inspire a sensational performance from Paul against Alcaraz.
