Turkish Airlines to buy up to 150 Boeing 737 MAX jets

2026-09-23 21:49

Turkish Airlines said Wednesday it plans to buy up to 150 Boeing 737 MAX aircraft, with deliveries scheduled between 2033 and 2037.

The flag carrier said it had “decided to purchase a total of 150 aircraft from Boeing,” including a firm order for 100 Boeing 737 MAX jets and options for another 50.

The announcement came as President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan was in New York for the UN General Assembly, a day after meeting US President Donald Trump alongside Middle Eastern leaders.

Trump hailed the deal in a post on his Truth Social platform, calling it a “big day for Boeing and American manufacturing” and also pointing to plans by Bangladesh to purchase more Boeing aircraft.

He said the deals would amount to tens of billions of dollars in sales and support tens of thousands of US jobs.

The latest order comes almost exactly a year after Turkish Airlines announced plans to buy another 225 Boeing aircraft following talks between Erdoğan and Trump at the White House.

Based at İstanbul Airport, Turkish Airlines operates a fleet of 514 aircraft and flies to more than 130 countries. The airline carried 92.6 million passengers in 2025, according to figures on its website.
