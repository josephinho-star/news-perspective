‘MobLand’ Season 2: How Many Episodes, Season Finale Release Date, Season 3 Info

2026-11-13 20:30

The penultimate offering of MobLand concluded with the Idles song “MTT 420 RR” asking if we’re “ready for the storm.” After watching the aptly titled episode “Nervous Breakdown,” I can confidently say… um… hell no.

The highlight of the episode was once again the tense yet almost comical showdown between Harry (Tom Hardy) and Frankie (Johnny Flynn).

“Feel those muscles. I mean, I’m tasty, but if we got in an actual ruck, I wouldn’t stand a snowflake’s,” Frankie says as he greets his adversary. “I bet you could kill me with your bare hands. Good job, I’ve got an army outside.”

Love it. For more on all the craziness from this week’s episode, make sure to read Sean T. Collins’ excellent recap on Decider.

“Nervous Breakdown” was the perfect appetizer for what’s sure to be a bloody, unpredictable season finale. How many episodes are there of MobLand Season 2? When is the MobLand Season 2 finale? What’s the latest on Season 3? Here’s everything you need to know.

MobLand Season 2: How Many Episodes?

The second season of MobLand consists of ten episodes. The penultimate episode (Season 2, Episode 9: “Nervous Breakdown”) is now streaming on Paramount+.

When Does The MobLand Season 2 Finale Drop on Paramount+?

The Season 2 finale of MobLand debuts Friday, November 20 at 3:01 a.m. ET on Paramount+.

Season 2, Episode 10 Synopsis: “Coordinated raids set the Harrigans’ empire on fire. With Conrad’s blessing, Harry cuts a savage deal. Meanwhile, Maeve turns the voltage up as Frankie takes a massive gamble and Eddie bottles it again.”

Will There Be a MobLand Season 3?

Thankfully, Tom Hardy, Pierce Brosnan, and Helen Mirren are all set to return for “more bloody chaos.” Ahead of the Season 2 premiere, Paramount+ renewed MobLand for a third season.

How To Watch MobLand on Paramount+ For Free:

MobLand streams exclusively on Paramount+.

Paramount+ offers two subscription plans, Essential and Premium. Right now, you can get started with a seven-day free trial on the Premium plan.

The ad-supported Essential plan typically costs $8.99/month, while the ad-free Premium plan (which comes with Showtime titles and live CBS) costs $13.99/month.

More Paramount+ Streaming Options:

Paramount+ doesn’t directly offer a free trial, but you can sample the streamer by adding Paramount+ to your Prime Video or Hulu accounts for an additional $13.99/month. You can also purchase Paramount+ through YouTube, which offers a free trial.
