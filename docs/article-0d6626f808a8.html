Candace Cameron Bure claims she was ‘duped’ into joining ‘The View’: ‘I didn’t sign up for this’

2026-11-27 15:39

Candace Cameron Bure claimed she was “duped” into joining “The View” when the talk show appeared to be moving away from political discussions in 2015.

The “Full House” actress said on Tuesday’s episode of “The McBride Rewind” podcast that she and former co-host Raven Symoné “were both told that the show was going to be pop culture and evergreen topics and not politics.”

Bure explained that she had guest co-hosted the ABC program multiple times, but was “naive” to the fact that she was “actually auditioning” for a permanent seat at the table.

When the official offer came in, Bure said she was “shocked,” and told producers her hesitations about joining the panel.

“I was like, ‘I’m not comfortable talking about politics — I actually don’t even know if I know enough, but to actually verbalize all of the opinions,’” she recalled. “They go, ‘Actually we’re steering away from politics. We’re going a new direction in the show and it’s going to be much more about social issues, pop culture.'”

“They said, ‘We might talk about politics here and there, but that’s not the focus of the show,'” Bure added.

But as Bure feared, the show “just turned into politics,” and then “all hell broke loose” once Donald Trump announced he was running for president that same year.

“Even with social topics, you can be morally conservative or not, but I know me,” she explained. “I’m me all the time. I’ve never changed who I am over the years. I’ve been very consistent my whole life and in my Christian faith and my beliefs. So, I’m always going to represent that point of view, no matter what topic we’re talking about.

But once it turned very political, I was like, ‘I didn’t sign up for this.'”

Bure noted that when she was on the show, producers encouraged her to “jump in” on the political conversations more, but Bure didn’t want to fight with the other women, so she quit in 2016 after two seasons.

However, Bure insisted she’s “grateful” for the experience.

“They still have great producers over there and, off camera, everyone was lovely,” she shared. “I loved Joy [Behar] so much, and I think she’s always the name that people are like, ‘How’s Joy?’ Joy loved me and I loved her. There was respect behind the cameras, even though there was a lot of disagreeing on camera.”

Bure returned to “The View” as a guest panelist on Nov. 13, after turning down a prior invite to be back at the table.

Speaking to People in 2021, Bure said “The View” was “one of the toughest jobs” that she had ever had, but noted she was “incredibly grateful for the opportunity.”
