Smokin' Charley Hull has heard enough criticism of Sydney Sweeney.

"They're making a big deal out of nothing. It's an actress slash model modeling for an advert. And I think she owns the company as well," Hull added. Sweeney holds an undisclosed equity stake in Novig.

Even as we pivoted back to talking about golf and this upcoming indoor tournament, Charley was still fired up.
