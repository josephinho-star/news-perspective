Eagles pull off miracle Thanksgiving rally as Cowboys stunningly collapse after disastrous decision

2026-11-27 01:46

ARLINGTON, Texas — Jake Elliott kicked a 59-yard field with 2 seconds remaining, and the Philadelphia Eagles stunned the Dallas Cowboys by rallying for a 39-38 Thanksgiving victory on Thursday.

The Eagles (4-7) got a chance for the win when Cowboys coach Brian Schottenheimer decided to go for it with a two-point lead on fourth-and-1 from the Philadelphia 39-yard line with 55 seconds remaining. Dak Prescott was sacked at the 48 of the Eagles, who needed just 11 yards to give Elliott a shot at the long field goal with 7 seconds to go.

The shocking loss is sure to turn up the pressure on Schottenheimer after the Cowboys (5-7) again failed to win consecutive games and made any path to the playoffs difficult at best.

Dallas’ only winning streak in two seasons with Schottenheimer was a three-game run last November capped by a Thanksgiving win over Patrick Mahomes and Kansas City.

The Cowboys led by 21 points in the third quarter and were still up 14 in the fourth before the Eagles started their improbable comeback by blocking a punt and returning it for a touchdown.

Joe Milton had touchdown passes of 50 and 45 yards to DeVonta Smith and a 14-yard scoring run against his former team as the Eagles won when they were already missing quarterback Jalen Hurts and lost star running back Saquon Barkley (stinger) and cornerback Cooper DeJean (concussion) in the first quarter.

The defending NFC East champions have won back-to-back games since a seven-game losing streak.

Prescott threw for two touchdowns and ran for a score while Javonte Williams accounted for three TDs in a game the Cowboys didn’t trail until Elliott’s winning kick.

The Cowboys were up 14 early in the fourth when Ty Robinson blocked Bryan Anger’s punt, and Kenny Moore II picked up the bouncing ball at the Dallas 3 for the easy score.

Prescott answered by running through and vaulting over Moore on a 14-yard run that restored the two-touchdown lead.

Dallas had another special teams breakdown on Britain Covey’s 55-yard kickoff return. Smith, who finished with a career-high 200 yards receiving, had a 23-yard catch after having gone to the sideline earlier with an apparent back injury when he landed hard on an incompletion. Milton ran 15 yards for a score on the next play.

The Eagles went for a 2-point conversion and didn’t get it, so they had to try again after Makai Lemon’s 24-yard TD catch pulled them within 38-36 with 2:10 remaining. After a holding penalty, Milton’s pass was broken up at the goal line.

Before Lemon’s score, Brandon Aubrey missed a 40-yard field goal that would have put Dallas up 11.

The biggest mistake for Dallas belonged to Schottenheimer. The Eagles were out of timeouts when Schottenheimer tried to put the game away instead of opting for a short punt that could have pinned the Eagles deep in their end of the field.

Milton, who was on Dallas’ practice squad a little more than a month ago, was 21 of 31 for 331 yards with three touchdowns in his third career start. The 50-yarder to Smith marked the fifth consecutive week the Dallas defense has given up a touchdown at least that long.

Prescott’s scoring passes were to KaVontae Tuprin and Williams, who also had scoring runs of 1 and 12 yards. CeeDee Lamb had seven catches for 134 yards for Dallas.
