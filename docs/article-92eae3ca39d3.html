Blue state governor candidate claims he ‘met Satan in person’ in resurfaced podcast episode

2026-09-03 17:29

Colorado's Republican gubernatorial nominee claimed in a resurfaced podcast episode that he "met Satan in person" through another person.

Talking Points Memo first reported that Colorado GOP gubernatorial nominee Victor Marx, a Marine veteran and Christian ministry leader, claimed in a 2020 episode of his podcast, "I’ve met the devil" and "I’ve met Satan in person, through a person."

According to Marx’s account on his podcast, the encounter occurred when he was praying for a girl in a high-security youth prison in Texas. He said, "This girl started manifesting a demon" and emphasized, "it was so real that all the other locked up girls, all the other gang members, man, they all kind of huddled in a corner."

Marx described the encounter as "horrible," saying, "This demon… just really manifested pretty nasty." He said that when he asked the demon its name, it responded, "I am Satan." He added, "If you think I’m joking, I’m not."

Though Marx described the little girl as "just adorable," he said he could tell "she had a demon in her."

"All of a sudden, this demon goes, ‘I guess you’re going to say I’m possessed.’ And I just — I just started laughing," said Marx, adding, "I was like, ‘I don’t think I have to. It’s kind of obvious.’ I mean, really, that’s — I just snickered, which makes the devil mad."

Marx is the founder of All Things Possible Ministries, a Christian ministry whose website says it is devoted to "setting captives free spiritually, emotionally and physically."

After winning the GOP gubernatorial nomination earlier this summer, Marx made headlines for assembling an all-Marine ticket that he said can bring the "steely spines" needed to combat a democratic socialist movement gaining ground in races around the country. He is pitching himself and his running mate, retired Marine Col. George Markert, as political outsiders, arguing that they have the discipline and accountability needed to take on the movement.

He has built his long-shot campaign around public safety, social conservatism and the cost of living. The campaign comes as the Democratic Party's left flank scored a series of primary wins nationally, including in Colorado, where democratic socialist Melat Kiros upset longtime incumbent Rep. Diana DeGette.

Marx is running against Democrat Phil Weiser, the attorney general of Colorado who is favored to win the race to succeed Gov. Jared Polis, another Democrat who is term-limited.

Fox News Digital's Ashley J. DiMella contributed to this report.
