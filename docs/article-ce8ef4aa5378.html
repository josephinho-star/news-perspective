Trump & Xi to Meet Amidst Friction on Wars, Tech, Trade

2026-09-21 00:50

Ahead of an approaching trade truce deadline, discussions are likely to center on artificial intelligence competition, semiconductor export policy, and potential Chinese EV manufacturing in the US. Against the backdrop of ongoing tariff debates and geopolitical tensions over Taiwan and Iran, Bloomberg’s Stephen Engle previews what is at stake for both economies. (Source: Bloomberg)
