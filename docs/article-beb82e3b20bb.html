PWHL’s Sirens have a date for Madison Square Garden return

2026-11-17 15:34

The world’s best women’s hockey league is headed back to the World’s Most Famous Arena this season.

The New York Sirens of the Professional Women’s Hockey League are set to make their return to Madison Square Garden on Jan. 23.

The game, against the expansion Detroit Clutch, will mark the second-ever PWHL game at Madison Square Garden.

Last season, the Sirens made history when they beat the Seattle Torrent 2-1 on April 4 in front of a record-setting, sold-out crowd of 18,006 at the Garden.

“What we experienced at Madison Square Garden last April was truly special, a memorable moment for our organization, our league and women’s hockey,” Sirens general manager Pascal Daoust said in a statement. “For those who were there, the atmosphere, the energy, the emotion and the level of play made it an experience of a lifetime. Having the privilege of returning to The World’s Most Famous Arena gives us another opportunity to showcase the excellence of our game, bring people together, and create another unforgettable moment on January 23.”

The Sirens, who open training camp Wednesday, regularly play their home games at Newark’s Prudential Center, which they share with the NHL’s New Jersey Devils.

The next MSG game is part of the PWHL’s continued efforts to bring women’s hockey to new venues and markets as part of the league’s “Takeover Tour.”

This year’s initiative is expected to be even bigger than it was a season ago, with the league slated to play at 24 neutral sites — up from 16 last season.

The PWHL is also expected to return to Capital One Arena in Washington D.C. for the second consecutive season, with the Boston Fleet set to take on the Ottawa Charge Jan. 2.

Other tour dates haven’t been announced.

Sources told The Post that the PWHL has discussed playing a second game at the Garden this season.

The PWHL expanded to 12 teams for its fourth season. The four new franchises are the Clutch, Hamilton Havoc, San Jose Sea Lions and Vegas Vanguards.

The season starts Dec. 5.

The Sirens are scheduled to open their season Dec. 6 against the Boston Fleet.
