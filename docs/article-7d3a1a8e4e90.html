Will Takaichi visit Kyiv? Zelensky’s invite puts Japan PM in diplomatic bind

2026-09-17 00:00

Analysts say a Ukraine trip will be an ‘excellent opportunity’ for Takaichi to show her mettle, despite concerns about chilly Russia ties

Zelensky extended the invitation through Eisuke Mori, speaker of Japan’s House of Representatives, during his visit to the Ukrainian capital earlier this week.

Mori, who helped establish the cross-party Japan-Ukraine parliamentary friendship group in 1995, is widely regarded as one of Japan’s more experienced parliamentary voices on Ukraine and did not hold back during his visit.

In a speech to the Supreme Council in Kyiv on Tuesday, Mori condemned the Russian invasion as “a barbaric act that shakes the international order and the values that the international community has built over decades”.
