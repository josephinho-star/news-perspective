Costco members can get $5 off the reimagined Ocarina of Time

2026-11-07 16:00

While some retailers were offering preorder discounts on the remastered Legend of Zelda: The Ocarina of Time, it’s not too late to save if you missed out. Costco members can currently get $5 off the digital version online, bringing the price down to $54.99. Considering an annual Costco membership runs $65, that discount almost offsets one month of your plan — a prime example of “gamer math.”
