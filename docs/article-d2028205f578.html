The Best Halloween Movies You Can Watch For Free on Pluto TV

2026-09-15 12:00

Even though we still have a few more weeks before October—and even though the temperature outside is still way too hot—we know plenty of you are eager to start watching Halloween movies on streaming. Hey, life is short. Spooky season can be as long as you want it to be!

That said, maybe you don’t currently have a subscription to a streaming service like Netflix or HBO Max. We don’t blame you. Those subscription fees really add up. Lucky for you, you can still watch Halloween movies on streaming, without paying a dime. How? With Pluto TV!

If you have internet access, you can watch hundreds of movies and TV shows either live or on demand, for free right, now on Pluto TV. You don’t even need an account! (Though you might want to make one, so you can save your progress while you watch.) Pluto TV is a legitimate, free, ad-supported streamer, which means there will be a few commercials while you watch—but it’s nowhere near as many commercials as you’d see watching a movie on cable. You can watch on the Pluto TV app, or on the Pluto TV website.

There are a lot of Halloween movies streaming on Pluto TV right now. To help you cut through the noise, we’ve assembled a list of the best Halloween movies on Pluto TV in September 2026.

CAST: Amanda Seyfried, Megan Fox, Adam Brody

RATING: RDespite a criminally inaccurate marketing campaign—which painted it as the sexy, sleazy, “Megan Fox nudity” movie—Jennifer’s Body has, in recent years, finally found its audience as a feminist cult classic. Written by Diablo Cody and directed by Karyn Kusama, it’s a tonally perfect horror comedy about a toxic teen girl friendship between Amanda Seyfried and Megan Fox. It just so happens that one of those teens is also a murderous succubus. From 9/11 allegories to the not-so-subtle sapphic undertones, Cody and Kusama captured exactly what it felt like to be a teen in the early 2000s, and made a kick-ass horror film in the process.

CAST: Jennifer Love Hewitt, Sarah Michelle Gellar, Ryan Phillippe, and Freddie Prinze Jr.

RATING: RThis classic ’90s teen horror flick—written by the same writer as the Scream franchise, Kevin Williamson— tells the story of four friends who, after a drunken night of partying, accidentally kill a pedestrian by hitting him with their car. They decide to dump the body, and vow never to speak of what happened on that fateful day, to anyone. But while they attempt to move on with their lives, a hooked-hand killer begins to stalk them, in order to remind them that, well, someone knows what they did last summer. From Jennifer Love Hewitt to Sarah Michelle Gellar to Freddie Prinze Jr., this movie features all your ’90s faves.

CAST: Sosie Bacon, Jessie T. Usher

RATING: RThis terrifying supernatural thriller follows a psychiatrist (played by Sosie Bacon) who witnesses a patient taking her own life. Following that traumatic incident, she becomes haunted by a terrifying image—everyone in her life starts smiling. But it’s not like, a nice smile. It’s the creepiest smile ever. Writer/director Parker Finn really knows just how scary a forced smile can be.

DIRECTOR: Barry Sonnenfeld

CAST: Anjelica Huston, Raul Julia, Christropher Lloyd, Christina Ricci

RATING: PG-13If you’re looking for spooky vibes without the scares, The Addams Family movie a perfect choice. Based on the comic book strip characters created by Charles Addams, and subsequent TV show, this campy supernatural comedy tells the story of a strange, macabre family who dabble in the occult and delight in their bizarre, gothic aesthetic. If you love Jenna Ortega as Wednesday Adams in the Netflix spin-off, this is a must-watch. And after you watch the first film, you can watch the sequel, Addams Family Values, also streaming free on Pluto TV.

The Texas Chainsaw Massacre (1974)

CAST: Marilyn Burns, Paul A. Partain, Edwin Neal, Jim Siedow, and Gunnar Hansen.

RATING: RIn the mood for some good old-fashioned slasher gore? Then look no further than the original Texas Chainsaw Massacre film. After a group of teens make the mistake of picking up a hitchhiker, they find themselves being brutally slaughtered by a chainsaw-wielding, masked maniac, aka Leatherface. You can thank this movie for originating many of the slasher tropes that still permeate the genre over 50 years, from the masked killer to the final girl. For horror film aficionados, it’s an essential watch.

CAST: Anya Taylor-Joy, Ralph Ineson, Kate Dickie

RATING: RIf you really feel like getting scared this spooky season, one of the scariest horror films of the 2010s, The Witch, is currently streaming free on Pluto. The film stars Anya Taylor-Joy, in her feature film debut, as Thomasin, the eldest daughter of a Puritan family living in 1630s New England. When an evil force descends on the family, Thomasin is accused of witchcraft. And honestly? They might be right.

CAST: Michael J. Fox, James Hampton, Scot Paulin

RATING: PGBefore the MTV show starring Tyler Posey and Dylan O’Brien, there was the bizarre, campy, coming-of-age ’80s comedy starring Michael J. Fox. Fox stars as popular teen boy, star of his high school’s basketball team, who wakes up one morning to discover he’s inherited a family curse: He’s a werewolf! But he’s not going to let a little fur and some claws keep him from the court. This movie is pure silly fun, perfect for a non-scary Halloween watch.

CAST: Johnny Depp, Christina Ricci

RATING: RNo one does Halloween vibes like the goth king Tim Burton, and one of Burton’s most Halloween-y movies ever—his retelling of Washington Irving’s 1820 short story “The Legend of Sleepy Hollow”—is streaming for free on Pluto TV. Depps stars as Ichabod Crane, a police constable sent to investigate the village of Sleepy Hollow, where residents are being decapitated.

DIRECTOR: Luca Guadagnino

CAST: Taylor Russell, Timothée Chalamet, Andre Holland, Mark Rylance

RATING: RCannibalism is definitely a creepy horror movie staple, but it’s also strangely romantic in this critically-acclaimed film adaptation of the 2015 novel of the same name, by Camille DeAngelis. Taylor Russell and Timothée Chalamet stars as two teens who fall in love and roadtrip across the country in 1988. They just so happen to also share a craving for human flesh. Though the film failed to make a splash at the box office, it was beloved by critics, and considered by some to be one of the best horror flicks of the century so far.

CAST: Andrew Robinson, Clare Higgins, Ashley Laurence, and Doug Bradley

RATING: RYou can watch the original Hellraiser movie that launched a 11-film franchise streaming for free, right now, on Pluto TV. The franchise, based on a 1986 novella from the film’s writer/director Clive Barker, centers on a puzzle box that, when used correctly, opens a gateway to a realm of demonic creatures called Cenobites, and their malevolent leader, Pinhead. The first film was known for its graphic, bloody, and disturbing depictions of horror. In fact, many of the most graphic scenes were cut by Barker, in order to get an R-rating for the film.

DIRECTOR: Keenen Ivory Wayans

CAST: Marlon Wayans, Shawn Wayans, Regina Hall, Anna Faris

RATING: RAs much as we love horror movies, we also know that they can be pretty silly sometimes. This parody franchise mercilessly mocked all the slasher movie tropes, with a particular focus on Scream and I Know What You Did Last Summer. If you’re looking to take a break from the scares with some stupid dumb fun, the Wayans brothers always know how to deliver on that.

DIRECTOR: Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg

CAST: James Franco, Jonah Hill, Rogen, Jay Baruchel, Danny McBride, Craig Robinson, Michael Cera and Emma Watson

RATING: RKeep the comedy horror train going with this meta comedy from Superbad writers Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg, starring Rogen and a bunch of his Hollywood pals as themselves, in the face of an LA-based biblical apocalypse. The pop culture references are dated, but (some of) the jokes hold up.

Bram Stokers Dracula (1992)

DIRECTOR: Francis Ford Coppola

CAST: Gary Oldman, Winona Ryder, Anthony Hopkins, Keanu Reeves

RATING: RBram Stoker’s 1897 novel Dracula has been adapted countless times, but Francis Ford Coppola’s version is surely one of the staples for vampire fans. The inimitable Gary Oldman stars as the pointy-toothed count, while Keanu Reeves plays the protagonist Jonathan Harker, who traverses to Transylvania and finds himself in the creatures home. Anthony Hopkins plays Van Helsing, and Winona Ryder plays Mina Harker. That’s a stacked cast for a spooky classic.

Vampire in Brooklyn (1995)

CAST: Eddie Murphy, Angela Bassett, Allen Payne

RATING: RAfter you watch Coppola dark, serious take on vampires, then you can check out Eddie Murphy’s not-so-serious version—starring Murphy as a Brooklyn-based, millennia-old vampire named Max—as a palette cleanser. Though critics panned the film at the time, Decider’s Jesse Hassenger argues the movie is “actually pretty cool.”

DIRECTOR: Christophe Gans

CAST: Radha Mitchell, Sean Bean, Laurie Holden

RATING: RBased on the popular Japanese horror video games, the Silent Hill movies center on a mysterious town where people can check in any time they like, but can never leave. When the protagonist daughter goes missing in the town, she’s determined to fight off the supernatural entities lurking in the ghost town in order to get her back.
