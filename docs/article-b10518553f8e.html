Beanie Feldstein reveals she was hospitalized again after giving birth, ‘could barely move for weeks’

2026-11-21 18:03

The actress opened up about her recent health scare on her Instagram Story Friday, sharing the news alongside a photo from the UK premiere of her upcoming film, “Focker-In-Law,” where she posed with co-stars Ben Stiller, Robert De Niro, Ariana Grande, Skyler Gisondo and Eduardo Franco.

“okay but to be real for a second i had a c-section four months ago and ended up rehospitalized with complications and literally could barely move for weeks so to get to this point is truly surreal in every sense of the word,” Feldstein, 33, wrote.

“im so in love with my focker family and so glad i could be there to celebrate our movie!!!” she added, while noting that “all postpartum people are superheroes.”

The “Booksmart” actress shared similar sentiments in an Instagram post Friday, which featured several photos from the film’s premiere, as well as snaps from her time in London.

“the fockers go to england!! the land of MY in laws!!” she wrote in the caption. “no but for real i had a c-section four months ago and could barely walk so this is all deeply surreal!!!”

The UK premiere of “Focker-In-Law” took place Wednesday, a week after the film’s New York City premiere, which Feldstein also attended. The movie is set to hit theaters Nov. 25.

Earlier this month, the “Lady Bird” actress revealed that she and her wife, Bonnie-Chance Roberts, had welcomed their first child together, a baby girl whose name has not been publicly disclosed.

“She is genuinely beyond my wildest dreams. I really did dream her,” Feldstein told People at the time. “I always wanted to be a mom, and I always wanted a daughter. I look at her every day, and I’m like, ‘You’re just beyond anything I could have even tried to imagine.'”

She also told the outlet that she experienced complications after giving birth, adding, “I am learning my strength every day.

“I feel this new kind of depth of my strength from being a mom that I think is so empowering and beautiful and very new.”

Feldstein and Roberts announced they were expecting their first child together in May with a joint Instagram post featuring photos of the actress showing off her baby bump alongside a cake that read, “B+B are having a baby!”

The couple, who met on the set of the 2019 film “How to Build a Girl,” captioned the post, “Limited Edition Scouse Beanie Baby coming soon!! 🎀🍼”

Feldstein announced her engagement to Roberts in June 2022, sharing photos of the proposal on Instagram after Roberts got down on one knee. “i do, bon,” she captioned the post.

They tied the knot in May 2023 during a summer camp-themed ceremony at Cedar Lakes Estate in Hudson Valley, New York.
