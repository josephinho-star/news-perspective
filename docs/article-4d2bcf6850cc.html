Ella Langley dominates CMA Awards with major wins as Taylor Swift makes stunning return to stage

2026-11-19 08:26

Ella Langley dominated the awards ceremony, as the winner of entertainer of the year, the biggest award of the night.

While accepting the award, she joked that the crowd must be sick of her already, getting a laugh from those in the room.

“This isn’t real life,” she said while accepting the award. “Thank you Jesus, I’m at a loss for words,” she added, before saying, “This has been the most special night.

As she swept all the categories she was nominated in, Lainey Wilson brought out a bedazzled broom for her, telling her she knew she had it prepared as she knew Langley was going to sweep the show.

Langley also won music video of the year before the broadcast.

“Choosin’ Texas” was named as the music video of the year winner during Good Morning America Wednesday morning.

Later in the show, Langley got teary-eyed as she accepted the award for album of the year, for her album, “Dandelion,” noting it took two years to make. She was speechless while accepting the award and received a standing ovation from the audience.

“I’m at a loss for words. I don’t know what to say,” she said in her speech. “I love country music and I love what I do.”

Her wins also resulted in two wins for Miranda Lambert, who is credited as a producer on the song.

Langley continued her winning streak later on in the show when she took home the award for female vocalist of the year, beating out Lambert, Musgraves, Moroney and Wilson for the honor.

“I have so much love and respect for every single person and every single woman in this category,” she said while accepting the award. “This is what I have lived and breathed not only just the past seven years that I moved to Nashville, but genuinely my whole entire life. I haven’t wanted to do another thing. There wasn’t anything on my heart. I never had a plan B. I was never gonna have a plan b. If I was still playing cover gigs with a tip jar out in front of me for four hours I’d still be doing this.”

Rising star Langley led the pack with 9 nominations, tying Lainey Wilson and Lambert’s all-time record for the most nods by a female artist in a single year. Her win for single of the year left Taylor Swift going home empty-handed tonight, which didn’t stop Taylor Swift from cheering for her as she went up to accept the award.

Swift made her big return to the CMA Awards – after last attending in 2016. Her single, “I Knew It, I Knew You,” is nominated for single of the year.

The singer took the stage to perform at the CMA Awards for the first time in 13 years, starting her performance with her nominated song, before transitioning into one of her classic hits, “Our Song.”

Lambert also had a moment on stage with Kacey Musgraves, when the two sang their hit duet, “Horses and Divorces,” from Musgraves’ latest album, “Middle of Nowhere.”

The song references the rumored feud between the two singers, with them patching things up earlier this year.

Zach Top honored veteran and military families, bringing one on stage and surprising them with the return of their son from service.

In addition to her performance with Musgraves, Lambert took the stage once again with Langley to sing their song, “Snakeskin Boots” for the first time on television. The two matched in their versions of brown dresses with snakeskin print and fringe as they commanded the stage during their performance.

Brooks and Dunn won vocal duo of the year for the 17th time in a row at this year’s award show. In addition, the duo also received the Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award, which was presented to them by Swift.

Prior to Swift handing them the award, Luke Bryan took to the stage alongside Brothers Osborne, HARDY, Marcus King, and Halestorm to pay tribute to the iconic duo.

Little Big Town announced Chris Stapleton as the winner of male vocalist of the year. The musician took the stage earlier in the evening with both Mumford & Sons to sing “Here” and Stephen Wilson Jr. to sing, “I’m a Song.”

During his speech he encouraged fans to follow their dreams, and dedicated the award to his mother, who died last year around this time.

“She always told me that I could be whatever I wanted to be, and turns out that was true,” he said.

Lady A introduced Tim McGraw to the stage to perform, “Don’t Take the Girl” for the first time on the CMA stage, as he is one of the Country Music Hall of Fame inductees this year. It was an intimate performance with the majority of the stage lights turned off save for a few spotlights behind him.

Wilson returned to host the event for the third consecutive time, her second solo. Wilson is also nominated for entertainer of the year, which she won in 2023 and 2025.

She also took to the stage during the show to perform her hit song, “Just A Woman.”

Megan Moroney blew fans away while strumming on her pink guitar as she sang “Beautiful Things” into her pink microphone. During her performance, fans surrounding the stage held up pink roses, while everyone in the arena swayed to the beat as with their flashlights lighting up the crowd.

Tucker Wetmore got the crowd pumped with his performance of “Who Told You That,” encouraging the crowd to get up and dance. While singing he enthusiastically played the piano which was surrounded by a circle of fire on the stage.

Parton was one of the most decorated figures in CMA Awards history, earning 45 nominations and winning 10 trophies over her career, including entertainer of the year in 1978, two female vocalist of the year awards, and the Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award in 2016.

Beyond her wins, she also helped define the telecast itself, famously co-hosting the ceremony in 1988 and returning in 2019 to co-host alongside Carrie Underwood and Reba McEntire.

At the start of the show, Wilson made lighthearted jokes about Swift and Travis Kelce’s mega Madison Square Garden wedding.

While Swift, Wilson and Langley have already taken the stage for solo performances, Luke Combs, Megan Moroney, The Red Clay Strays, Tucker Wetmore, Vince Gill, and Zach Top are still set to perform.

Cody Johnson was initially slated to perform at the 2026 CMA Awards, but the four-time nominee and entertainer of the year contender pulled out of attending and performing at tonight’s ceremony, announcing a day prior that he had to step away due to mental and physical exhaustion.
