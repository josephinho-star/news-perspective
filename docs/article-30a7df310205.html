Robert Saleh assures Cam Ward is in 'a good headspace' amid Titans embarrassing start to season

2026-10-14 18:38

The Tennessee Titans are 0-5 to start the season, they have the lowest-scoring offense in the NFL and second-year quarterback Cam Ward has not taken anything even resembling a positive step in his development. This is also a franchise that has not beaten a team in its own AFC South division since November 2024.

The Titans are objectively an awful football team, but their fans shouldn't have to wait too much longer for something, anything positive to happen, according to the message head coach Robert Saleh is relaying this week.

Tennessee is coming off a 26-3 home loss to what was a winless Houston Texans team that managed to sack Ward four times. During his Wednesday press conference, Saleh was asked how his young quarterback could not let pressures and sacks affect him moving forward, which is when the coach offered up some questionable comments.

"Really cool conversation with Cam this week," Saleh said. "He's in a good headspace, and he's got a lot of confidence in the o-line."

Saleh claiming that he had a "cool conversation" with Ward and presenting that as some sort of selling point for his team's fanbase would be one thing if the Titans were winning football games. You add that on top of his comment that Ward is in "a good headspace," and it's hard to come up with a more disingenuous message that will assuredly fall on deaf ears.

Ward is 3-19 as a starting quarterback, which includes a 0-7 mark against AFC South foes, and is staring another loss to a divisional rival in the face with the Titans traveling to Indianapolis to take on the Colts on Sunday.

While Saleh must do everything in his power to instill confidence in a quarterback that the Titans' front office views as the face of the franchise for years to come, flipping the narrative and sharing that Ward is angry yet motivated wouldn't make the situation worse.

The reality is the situation in Nashville couldn't possibly be any worse, with the franchise firmly sitting at rock bottom.

Since firing Mike Vrabel as head coach in January 2024, the Titans have gone 6-33 and are on their second general manager and second head coach across that time frame.

With Saleh beginning his Tennessee tenure at 0-5, his head coaching record in the NFL sits at 20-41 overall after a multi-season stint with the New York Jets.
