Marlowe Sturridge borrowed from mom Sienna Miller’s closet for the ‘War’ premiere

2026-09-17 17:40

Sienna Miller’s 14-year-old daughter, Marlowe Sturridge, just swiped part of her look from her fashionable mom.

Miller looked glamorous in a black feathered strapless gown by Dilara Findikoglu, originally shown during her fall/winter 2023 collection.

Supporting her mom on the red carpet for the London premiere of “War,” the teenager dressed down in a black midi skirt, vintage Pittsburgh Pirates 1979 tee and carrying a black Chloé Paddington bag ($2,990).

Sturridge’s bag is a reissue from 2005, brought back last year to much buzz for it’s 20th anniversary.

The Paddington was one of the first major ‘It’ bags, often seen on the shoulder of actresses like Lindsay Lohan, Kate Moss, Mischa Barton and — of course — Sienna Miller.

And, as Miller’s style was emblematic of the early-aughts, it’s only natural that her daughter follows in her footsteps.

The “Alfie” actress, 44, said in 2024 that her teen is “a little fashion monster,” calling her “a young Anna Wintour in her total disdain for some of the things I wear.”

Sturridge isn’t the only celebrity daughter to snag some iconic pieces from their stylish predecessor’s wardrobes.

Delilah Belle Hamlin wore her mom’s vintage Gucci gown to the 2025 GQ Men of the Year Awards, Apple Martin swiped Gwyneth Paltrow’s Calvin Klein dress for the “Marty Supreme” NYC premiere, and both Brooke Shields as well as Kelly Ripa have both publicly shared the decades-old looks their own children have re-worn in honor.

Miller shares Sturridge with ex-fiancé Tom Sturridge and recently welcomed her third child, second with fiancé Oli Green.
