Bank earnings live updates: JPMorgan’s earnings call is underway after rare earnings miss

2026-10-13 13:06

Four of the largest U.S. banks reported third-quarter results on Tuesday: JPMorgan Chase, Wells Fargo, Goldman Sachs and Citigroup.

Bank of America and Morgan Stanley will post earnings on Wednesday.

The quarter was dominated by headlines around artificial intelligence, IPO uncertainty, high fuel prices and rising borrowing costs.

The banks that reported Tuesday put up mixed results. JPMorgan, the biggest U.S. bank by assets and the largest in the world by market capitalization, posted a rare miss. Goldman Sachs, meanwhile, blew past revenue estimates, led by strong equities trading.

Earnings calls with company executives, starting with JPMorgan, are underway.
