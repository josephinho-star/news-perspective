Netflix boss gives cryptic response to Meghan Markle’s ‘The Gentlemen’ casting rumors

2026-08-28 21:20

“There have been inquiries in the entertainment space recently and things she is considering but nothing is confirmed right now,” a source exclusively told Page Six on Aug. 21.

Our source added, “Something is being discussed but it’s not solid.”

Fittingly, Netflix’s “The Gentlemen” is set in England and filmed in the UK.

“Her show did not go on so it did not make sense to continue the partnership,” an industry source exclusively told Page Six in March.

Markle, best known for her breakout role as Rachel Zane in the legal drama “Suits” from 2011 to 2018, announced her decision to step back acting to focus on royal life in 2017 following her engagement to Harry.

In November 2025, eight years after she stepped back from the big screen, Page Six reported that Markle was making her big return acting.

She is set to make a cameo as herself in the upcoming Amazon MGM comedy “Close Personal Friends” with Brie Larson and Lily Collins. A release date has yet to be announced.

At the time, a source told the Sun that Harry was “really supportive” of her decision and “quite simply wants Meghan to do whatever brings her joy.”

Markle and Harry made their move back to Harry’s homeland with their two young children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet, on Wednesday.

The younglings reportedly start school in England next week.
