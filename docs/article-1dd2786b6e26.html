Notorious Greenwich Village homeless man Tiger knocks man out, bizarrely helps him up: video

2026-10-31 15:39

The deranged homeless man known as Tiger violently attacked a man near his encampment on Bleecker Street — and then bizarrely helped the knocked-out man to his feet.

Wild video posted to social media on Oct. 17 showed Tiger, real name Noel-Teru Gibson, 44, and an unknown man breaking out into a fight outside of bar The Red Lion.

The tail-wearing hobo was taped following a man across the street and then jumping in front of him and standing in his way, arms akimbo.

The unidentified man then shoves Tiger sharply in the back.

The notorious vagrant then winds up and punches the man from behind — packing several wallops to the side of his face before tossing him to the ground where the man hit his head against a scaffolding pole.

Tiger then runs the pockets of the unconscious man — pulling out what appears to be an ID badge and his cell phone, video showed.

The vagrant then bizarrely hands the man his belongings, pats him on the shoulder, and tries to pick him up and drag him off the street.

The unidentified man is seen later in the video walking around under his own power with a developing bruise on his right jawline.

The NYPD did not have a record of Tiger being arrested on Oct 17.

In another incident, Tiger was arrested on Oct. 14 for dancing and gyrating while completely nude on Bleecker Street, according to the NYPD.

Video of the arrest shared with The Post showed only in underwear and wearing his tail while arguing with two police officers.

He faced charges of public lewdness and a harassment for that unsavory display, the NYPD said.

Greenwich Village neighbors previously told The Post that Tiger’s presence in the neighborhood is increasingly volatile — with one anonymous father claiming Tiger chased his son earlier this year and that his young daughter saw him naked on the street while on the way to school.
