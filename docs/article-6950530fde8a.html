Brenda Song pitches goal line play ‘Senda Brenda’ to Manning brothers

2026-10-13 03:47

It’s no secret that Brenda Song is a massive fan of the Los Angeles Rams. But could she find her way on the team’s coaching staff?

As Song made her highly-anticipated cameo appearance on the “Manning Cast,” hosted by Peyton and Eli Manning, during the Rams Monday night matchup against the Bills, she broke down her key goal line play, the “Senda Brenda.”

As Peyton went to a whiteboard to draw out the play, she told him that she couldn’t take credit for it, as it was originally named by Rams defensive end Kobie Turner.

“This is the Rams version of a tush push. We’re lined up; you have the running back. You pass him the ball. He runs up, and the whole line just tosses him over, and you call it, ‘A senda Brenda,” Song said. “You toss him over both lines into the endzone.”

As Peyton was putting the final touches on the play, his brother, Eli, told him that this play is “The old Mike Leach play,” referring to when Eli had the former Texas Tech head coach on the ESPN show, “Eli’s Places.”

Eli went on to tell Song that Leach always wanted to run this play, and that his teams had practiced it in the past, but with a kicker at the running back position.

When Song found out that her play had been thought of before, she couldn’t help but drop her jaw in enthusiasm.

The show went on to show the clip of Eli running the play on “Eli’s Places” and Song calling it amazing, but also joking with him that they “stole our play.”

The segment ended with Eli telling the former star of “The Suite Life of Zach and Cody” that they need to put her in a Rams jersey and run the play with her.
