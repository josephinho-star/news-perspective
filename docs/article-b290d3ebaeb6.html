NYC’s Biggest Food & Wine Festival Returns This Fall

2026-09-14 04:02

New York City’s most anticipated foodie weekend is back this October 15-18th! The Food Network New York City Wine & Food Festival presented by Invesco QQQ (NYCWFF) returns for its 19th year bringing its signature blend of acclaimed chefs, NYC’s best restaurants, celebrities, innovative spirits, and city-wide flavor to over 50 food and wine events. This year, the festival returns to the downtown Seaport, with the addition of the Rooftop of Pier 17 serving as an exciting new venue for 2026’s return. Between the waterfront location and a variety of events scattered throughout the city, attendees are promised an elevated experience with skyline views to match the world-class bites.

From intimate dining experiences to large-scale, high-energy tastings, the lineup is designed to satisfy every palate. Here are a few of the essential experiences you won’t want to miss:

Grand Tasting featuring Culinary Demonstrations

This cornerstone showcase presented by Bosch Home Appliances features the city’s top restaurants and brands. This year’s celebration is anchored by a powerhouse hosting team: Marcus Samuelsson, the acclaimed chef behind Red Rooster; Andrew Rea of @BingingwithBabish, whose popular digital platform has revolutionized food media; Ayesha Nurdjaja, the Food Network chef known for her masterful Middle Eastern cuisine at Shuka and Shukette; and Food Network veteran Michael Symon known for his bold, meat-forward culinary style. It will also feature live, interactive, culinary demonstrations from chefs and celebrities including mgk, Alex Guarnaschelli, Bryan and Michael Voltaggio, Geoffrey Zakarian, Aarón Sánchez, and more. Grab your tickets to see these stars in action.

It wouldn’t be NYCWFF without this fan-favorite, high-energy throwdown, presented by Pat LaFrieda Meats part of the Norwegian Cruise Line Rooftop Series. The Rooftop at Pier 17 will be the new stage for chefs to go head-to-head to claim the title of the ultimate burger. Daytime host and icon Rachael Ray takes the helm as host, alongside the high-octane energy of comedian and podcast host Bert Kreischer. Expect fierce competition and some of the best patties you will eat all year. Ready to judge for yourself? Secure your spot at the grill to see which chef claims the title this year.

For those who prefer their foodie adventures after sunset, this nocturnal exploration of taste is a must. Kwame Onwuachi, the Best Chef in America Award-winning chef and author known for his inventive, globally-inspired cooking, takes the reins as host. The event will be amplified by the rhythmic beats of special guest DJ, D-Nice, creating a unique atmosphere that bridges fine dining and nightlife. Let the rhythm take over when you dive into Caribbean flavors and globally inspired dishes.

Presented by Mike’s Amazing, this celebration turns up the heat with an incredible lineup of stars. Hosted by influential chef JJ Johnson, radio personality Angela Yee, and the incomparable EGOT winner Whoopi Goldberg, this celebration blends the city’s best black cuisine with unmatched energy. Party with the icons and grab your tickets to The Cookout, featuring a special live performance by hip-hop legend Method Man.

Looking for a unique way to kickstart your day? Join mgk (formerly Machine Gun Kelly) and his coffee brand, 27 Club Coffee, for NYCWFF’s first-ever non-alcoholic gathering with a nod to New York City’s bustling café culture. This experience offers a twist on the morning coffee (or tea) ritual, blending rock-star energy with high-quality caffeine and delicious breakfast bites for a morning you won’t forget.

Spice up your schedule and take a sensory journey through the rich, bold and complex flavors of Indian cuisine. This vibrant event is hosted by two heavyweights in the industry: Maneet Chauhan, the James Beard Award-winning chef and television personality, and Vijay Kumar, the celebrated chef bringing authentic South Indian cuisine to the forefront.

Bank of America’s FoodieCon®

Presented by Sunset, this is the definitive gathering for digital food culture. As the intersection of tech, content creation and culinary art continues to evolve, this year’s edition serves as the hub for the biggest creators, influencers and brand innovators. See where the next trends are born alongside the biggest names in food media with Olivia Tiedmann @oliviated, Bobby Pombo @bobbypombo, Holly Barnes @savourygirll and more.

The Food Network New York City Wine & Food Festival presented by Invesco QQQ is the city’s largest celebration of the food industry’s best, with a menu of mouth-watering events. Featuring an exciting lineup this October, tickets are expected to move fast, so grab yours now before they’re gone!
