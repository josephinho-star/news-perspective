Meet Ultra’s new Energy Pouches: Your go-to caffeine boost to power up your day

2026-09-21 14:25

Meet your new pocket-sized energy boost: Ultra’s new Energy Pouches are here to put a little more power behind your day.

Forget waiting around for the kettle to boil or making yet another trip to the coffee machine. Ultra Energy Pouches are designed for people who want energy, focus and flavor in a simple, convenient form.

Each pouch delivers 180mg of caffeine, paired with L-Theanine and a full B-vitamin stack featuring B3, B6, and B12. The formula also includes Vitamins C and E, bringing together a selection of ingredients designed to complement your daily routine.

The big idea? Fast, focused energy without having to carry a giant coffee cup everywhere you go.

Ultra Energy Pouches are designed to kick in after five to 10 minutes, making them an easy option when you need to get switched on and get moving. Whether you’re powering through a busy morning, tackling a long afternoon, heading into a workout or simply need a little extra momentum, just pop in a pouch and carry on with your day.

So why would a coffee drinker make the switch? It comes down to convenience. Ultra Energy Pouches give you 180mg of caffeine in a compact, ready-to-use format, without the coffee run, the cup, the brewing or the spill. They’re easy to keep in your pocket or bag and use when you need a boost, whether you’re heading to work, travelling, hitting the gym or powering through a busy afternoon.

With zero sugar and zero calories, plus L-Theanine and B vitamins, Ultra offers a simple alternative to coffee so you can enjoy the long-lasting flavor without adding sugar to your routine.

And there’s no nicotine and no tobacco here. Ultra Energy Pouches are completely nicotine-free and tobacco-free, offering a different take on the traditional oral pouch.

Speaking of flavor, Ultra is keeping things fun. Choose from three options: Black Cherry for a bold, fruity hit, Wintergreen for a crisp and refreshing kick or Sweet Mint for a cool, smooth finish. Pick your favorite, or rotate through all three depending on your mood.

With 180mg of caffeine, L-Theanine, B3, B6, B12, vitamins C and E, plus zero sugar and zero calories, Ultra Energy Pouches are built for convenience and a powerful energy experience.

Coffee has had its moment. Now, there’s a new pouch in town.
