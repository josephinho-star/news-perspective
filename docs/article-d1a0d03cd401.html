Anne Hathaway wears Rothy’s clogs on repeat — and her exact pair is under $130 for Black Friday

2026-11-23 22:43

Anne Hathaway is stepping into a new chapter as a mom of three.

The A-list actress was spotted on a stroll with her newborn in New York City last week, keeping it casual in a long trench coat, flowing pants and a lavender cardigan.

Her brown baby carrier nearly matched her shoes — which we couldn’t help but notice are a celeb-loved Rothy’s style, the Teddy Buckle Clog in Russet ReVelvet.

The comfortable design is even on sale ahead of Black Friday, but it’s selling out quickly.

The machine-washable shoes feature a contoured footbed and fluffy vegan shearling that has shoppers “obsessed.” In addition to an olive recycled velvet material, the clogs also come in wool options.

“The most comfortable, fuzzy, supportive shoe ever!” raved one shopper of the style. “Run don’t walk to grab these,” wrote another.

It’s not the first time the “Devil Wears Prada” star has worn Rothy’s shoes recently, either. In a hilarious Reel with her glam squad last month, she sported the brand’s Casual Clog, which is also on sale.

The star joins Jennifer Lawrence, Bethenny Frankel and Rachel Bilson in wearing the label’s clogs, with the latter once writing on Instagram, “My feet living their best lives in these.”

The Casual Clog also comes in ReVelvet (a soft velvet made from recycled water bottles) or a heathered knit with merino wool, in over a dozen color options.

Both styles are easy to slip into, but difficult to take off, according to fans who say they don’t want to wear any other shoes.

Whichever style suits you, don’t snooze on these deals while they last!

Black Friday & Cyber Monday FAQs for savvy shoppers

When is Black Friday 2026?

While Black Friday may feel like its own season, the official sale date is Friday, November 27.

Early deals run throughout November, with many discounts just as good as the main event. Track our best deals and make a plan so you don’t miss out on the goods you really want.

When is Cyber Monday 2026?

The biggest online shopping holiday of the year is on Monday, November 30, 2026.

Which is better, Black Friday or Cyber Monday?

TLDR: it depends. Black Friday sales may carry higher inventory of popular items that may sell out, like UGGs and the Dyson Airwrap. However, Cyber Monday sales sometimes offer deeper discounts, depending on the retailer.

Is it worth it?

Of course! Black Friday and Cyber Monday still offer some of the biggest sales of the year on must-have tech like AirPods, designer clothing, shoes and accessories, beauty buys and top-rated gifts.
