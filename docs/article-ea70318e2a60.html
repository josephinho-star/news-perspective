Amazon delivery drone crashes into garage: ‘It just went crazy’

2026-11-24 19:28

Wild video shows an Amazon drone crashing into a garage in Michigan, damaging the building and triggering a federal investigation.

The drone crashed into Logan Coomes’ garage in Huntington Woods on Friday afternoon, moments after it dropped shampoo off in his neighbors’ yard — their very first delivery by Prime Air, they told local outlet WXYZ.

The neighbor, Jim Brennan, said his wife “ordered shampoo, and we just expected a truck to pull up, and then we get the notifications that it’s coming by drone in seven minutes.”

The drone successfully dropped the package in the Brennans’ backyard — but it then hit a fiber optic cable, knocking off a propeller.

“When it caught that, it just, it went crazy,” Brennan recalled. “Like a sick bird — it tried to get out of here, but it couldn’t.”

Photos show that the drone crashed right through the roof of Coomes’ garage. Amazon crews were on the scene within about 10 minutes to retrieve the downed drone, Coomes said.

“There’s a little corner on the tip of the peak of the garage where I think if it did rain, water would get through right now. Some shingles were flapped up from their original positions,” Coomes said.

Amazon confirmed that Brennan’s delivery had come from Prime Air’s two facilities in Hazel Park — which is just a 5-mile drive by car from Huntington Woods in the Detroit suburbs.

The Federal Aviation Administration is now investigating, a spokesperson confirmed to The Post.

Amazon is also investigating the mishap.

“There were no injuries, the drone has been removed, and we are in contact with the resident,” a spokesperson told WXYZ. “Safety is our top priority and incidents are extremely rare. Anytime a delivery does not go as planned we investigate what happened.”

Billed as ultrafast and convenient, Prime Air drones have delivered hundreds of thousands of packages this year and nearly 6,000 are flown a day, Amazon said.

Amazon flies MK30 drones, which weigh around 83 pounds and have a 5-pound payload capacity for products like groceries, cosmetics or other smaller items, Amazon told CNN.

The MK30 has the “best-in-class perception system to detect and avoid obstacles,” according to the online giant, who said they can avoid common outdoor objects like trampolines or clotheslines that may not have been seen in satellite imagery.

However, last year, Amazon temporarily halted all of its commercial drone flights after two MK30s crashed into a crane outside of Phoenix. No injuries were reported, but the drones were severely damaged.

Amazon already announced plans in August to have autonomous deliveries in 500 municipalities by the end of 2026 — and reach half a billion customers within a decade.
