Sean ‘Diddy’ Combs’ twin daughters party with Hollywood A-listers after rapper’s prison transfer

2026-10-17 18:51

Sean “Diddy” Combs’ twin daughters, Jessie and D’Lila Combs, stepped out for a star-studded bash on Friday following their father’s prison transfer.

In photos from the event, the 19-year-old siblings wore matching fitted pink dresses and denim jackets while stepping out for a party thrown by Cade Hudson in Hollywood on Friday evening.

The twins accessorized with delicate necklaces and wore their hair in soft curls for the outing. The embattled music exec welcomed Jessie and D’Lila with the late Kim Porter, who died in 2018.

Their father’s ex-girlfriend Jennifer Lopez was also on the invite list, along with Heather Graham, Jessica Alba and Seth MacFarlane.

Friday night’s private soirée was also attended by Paris Hilton and her mother, Kathy Hilton, as well as Molly Shannon, Demi Moore, Rachel Zoe and Issa Rae.

The high-profile event took place on the same day Page Six confirmed their father, disgraced rapper Sean “Diddy” Combs, was transferred to a new prison after sustaining a head injury.

He was moved from New Jersey’s FCI Fort Dix to the Federal Correctional Institution in Allenwood, Pennsylvania — both institutions are low-security facilities.

“We can confirm that Sean Combs transferred to FCI Allenwood Low on Friday, October 16, 2026,” the Federal Bureau of Prisons told us on Friday.
