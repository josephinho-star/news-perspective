Eric Ripert’s Le Bernardin named best of the best in US for fifth straight year on La Liste survey

2026-11-23 22:45

Midtown seafood palace Le Bernardin topped La Liste’s ranking of the best US restaurants for the fifth year in a row — and was rated among the roster’s “Magnificent 13” lineup of the best in the world, to boot.

Le Bernardin, owned by chef Eric Ripert and partner Maguy LeCoze, remains a favorite of culinary sophisticates and ordinary food lovers alike after 40 years at 155 W. 51st St.

Paris-based, algorithm-driven La Liste calls itself “the world’s most selective hotel and restaurant guide.” Unlike the Michelin guide and others that rely on reviews by anonymous “inspectors,” La Liste bases its scores on many factors — among them, critical reviews, guidebooks and multiple kinds of media exposure, including social-media postings.

Le Bernardin has dominated the top tier since 2022. Other Big Apple restaurants with high ratings include Restaurant Daniel, Jungsik, Atomix, Sushi Sho and Jean-Georges.

But Le Bernardin was alone among US establishments in La Liste’s “lucky 13” world’s best rankings. Others honored included Guy Savoy in Paris, Matsukawa in Tokyo, Cheval Blanc in Basel, Switzerland, and Da Vittorio in Brusaporto, Italy.

Ripert told The Post he was “honored and elated” by the honor but was traveling and unable to comment further.

An industry insider said, “La Liste was considered an upstart when it was launched in 2015, but it has had a growing influence, especially among travelers from Europe and Asia.”
