HKU raises alarm over suicide rate among under-15s, warns of social media impact

2026-09-10 07:51

Centre at University of Hong Kong uses World Suicide Prevention Day to call for more student support after spate of attempts

“As we pursue academic excellence, we should also look after the mental health of young people,” said Paul Yip Siu-fai, the centre’s founding director.

“It’s been just a while since the school year started, and we have already seen a few student suicides. Here I would like to call on schools to give pupils more time and space to build rapport with their teachers and schoolmates, strengthening their support system.”

Using data provided by the Coroner’s Court, the centre estimated that there were 976 suicide cases, or 13 per 100,000 residents, last year – a slight decline from 2024.

But while levels declined across nearly all age groups, the rate rose among under-15s to 2.9 per 100,000, the highest since 2016.
