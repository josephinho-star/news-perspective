Married Florida deputy fired for sexting multiple women on work phone while on duty

2026-11-22 09:28

A married Florida deputy was fired after he was caught allegedly using his work phone to send and receive explicit photos while on duty — to avoid his wife’s wrath.

Matthew Pippin was sacked from his role with the Osceola County Sheriff’s Office in October after sexually explicit messages were discovered on his work-issued device, WESH reported on Wednesday.

Pippin’s uniformed exchanges were first discovered during an investigation into allegations he had sexually battered a woman three years earlier.

The unidentified woman made the accusations against the 22-year law enforcement veteran in May, when detectives interviewed her for an unrelated case.

An investigation into Pippin was conducted but found no evidence to back the claim that he had forcefully engaged the woman in sexual activity. Prosecutors declined to press any criminal charges.

However, internal affairs officials at the sheriff’s office uncovered Pippin’s lewd interactions on his work phone.

The X-rated exchanges included images of women both nude, bathing and wearing only underwear, WKMG reported.

Detectives determined Pippin sent a majority of the messages while he was on call with the Osceola County Sheriff’s Office.

Screenshots of the messages included a photo of the hard-balling deputy in uniform while flirting with the unidentified recipient.

“Did it do it for you, baby?” Pippin asked in one exchange, according to the outlet.

“It did, but I want you,” the woman replied.

In a separate exchange, Pippin exclaimed, “Holy wow sexy I might hurt myself on that one lol,” after receiving a photo from a woman.

Pippin admitted to the dirty messages when confronted by investigators, saying he did it to protect his marriage.

“Um, the obvious, my wife,” Pippin allegedly said when asked why he used his work phone instead of a personal device.

“And just the fact that I was talking the way that I was talking on the text messages, I shouldn’t have been doing it, one because I’m married and it’s the wrong thing to do. And two, it’s just the wrong thing to do. Period,” the unfaithful deputy told investigators.

Pippin had attempted to resign after the investigation into him was completed, but was terminated for violating agency policy.

He had most recently served for the Sheriff’s Office’s agricultural and marine unit.

Osceola County Sheriff Chris Blackmon blasted Pippin for violating the public’s trust.

“As law enforcement officers, we are held to the highest standard of integrity, accountability, and professionalism,” Blackmon said in a statement obtained by WESH. “When a member of this agency fails to uphold those standards and violates the public trust, decisive action must be taken.

“Following an internal investigation into improper conduct, Deputy Matt Pippin has been terminated from the Osceola County Sheriff’s Office. Our mission is to serve and protect the residents of Osceola County with honor and transparency. No one who wears our badge is above the law or exempt from standards our community rightfully expects,” Blackmon said.

Pippin’s antics took place while disgraced Sheriff Marcos Lopez was still in office.

Lopez was arrested and charged with felony racketeering and conspiracy charges in connection to an illegal gambling operation in June 2025.

He was suspended by Florida Gov. Ron DeSantis, who replaced the disgraced sheriff with Blackmon.
