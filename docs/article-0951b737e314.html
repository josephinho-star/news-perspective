Taylor Lautner and wife Tay welcome their first baby

2026-09-23 20:26

The “Twilight” star, 34, and his partner, 29, broke the news in March that they had a little one on the way.

“What’s better than two Taylor Lautners?” the actor captioned an Instagram photo of himself kissing Tay’s baby bump.

The following month, the duo shared footage of the moment they found out they had a daughter on the way.

While Tay “didn’t really care” about the sex either way, she told “Call Her Daddy” listeners that her husband “really want[ed]” a baby girl.

“He’s, like, girl dad,” she said during the August podcast episode. “He wants all girls.”

Elsewhere in the conversation, she confessed to host Alex Cooper that Taylor was on their list of baby names.

“It may be in the mix,” she teased at the time. “We will find out when the baby comes.”

Tay added, “I feel like if I don’t name my baby Taylor, the Internet’s literally gonna cancel me. I’m gonna get canceled. But I’m like, ‘Guys, are you really gonna want that name?’

“We used to always joke that if we’re having a boy, we’d name him Taylor and call him T.J.,” she continued.

Tay and Taylor started dating in 2018 after being introduced by the latter’s sister the previous year.

Taylor proposed to the nurse in November 2021, gushing via Instagram her saying “yes” made “all [his] wishes come true.”

The “Squeeze” podcast co-hosts tied the knot in Templeton, California, one year later.

While celebrating their anniversary in November 2025, the groom told his Instagram followers, “Three years down. Still touching heaven.”
