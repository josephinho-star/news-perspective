Where to watch the Las Vegas Grand Prix 2026 for free: Time, livestream

2026-11-22 00:00

There are just three rounds remaining in the 2026 F1 season and tonight, they’ll race under the Las Vegas lights in a rare Saturday night race.

Drivers will race around the iconic strip, passing landmarks like the Bellagio fountain, the Sphere and many of the other resorts on Las Vegas Blvd.

Both the constructors’ champion and drivers’ champion have already been crowned this season, with Mercedes taking the constructors crown and their driver, Kimi Antonelli, becoming a first-time world champion.

Watch F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix 2026

- When: Nov. 21, 11 p.m. ET

- Where: Las Vegas Strip Circuit (Las Vegas, Nevada)

- How to watch for free: Apple TV (Black Friday offer: 50% off)

- TV Channel: Streaming exclusive

Due to a grid penalty, Antonelli will start in sixth today. Red Bull’s Max Verstappen and Mercedes’ George Russell make up the front row.

The Las Vegas Grand Prix is raced on a 3.853-mile circuit with 17 turns. The race is 50 laps, for a total of 192.599 miles.

Here’s everything you need to know to tune in to today’s Formula 1 race.

Las Vegas Grand Prix 2026 start time:

The 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix is scheduled to begin today, Nov. 1, at 3 p.m. ET.

How to watch the Las Vegas Grand Prix for free:

All things F1, including today’s Las Vegas GP, stream exclusively on Apple TV in the United States. Apple TV subscribers will get access to the entire Formula 1 season and extras, like Race in 30, at no additional cost.

Apple TV is currently offering a Black Friday offer for new and returning subscribers. You’ll get six months for 50% off ($6.99/month).

You can also subscribe to Apple TV via Prime Video for the same $6.99/month price and stream all of its Formula 1 coverage.
