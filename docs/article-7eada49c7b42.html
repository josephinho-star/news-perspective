Prince William reveals glimpse at Prince George’s jaw-dropping artistic talents with never-before-seen drawing

2026-11-11 12:40

Prince William proved his son, Prince George, is a picture of royal talent by displaying one of the 13-year-old’s drawings in the latest issue of Country Life magazine.

The proud dad chose to showcase his son’s detailed drawing of a kingfisher while guest-editing a special issue of the publication.

The colorful illustration, which George completed when he was just 12, features an incredibly detailed depiction of a bird with dark green and yellow feathers, perched on top of a piece of wood.

“This beautiful picture of a kingfisher was drawn by Prince George, aged 12 at the time, and offers a glimpse into the young royal’s artistic talents and growing curiosity about the natural world,” Country Life captioned an Instagram post of the never-before-seen image.

The drawing was completed at the family’s home during the spring months, per the Daily Mail.

It appears the young royal has inherited his mother’s love of art, with the Princess of Wales previously majoring in art history at the University of St. Andrews in Scotland, where she met her husband.

Meanwhile, George’s grandfather, King Charles III, is also known as a talented amateur painter and has drawn many landscapes of royal properties such as Windsor Castle and his country home Highgrove.

In October 2022, a print of a painting of Balmoral by Charles sold at auction for an impressive $6,500, despite being valued at approximately $675.

The painting, which came with a certificate, was signed in pencil by Charles and dated 2001.

Earlier in the year, 79 of the royal’s watercolor paintings were shown at The Garrison Chapel in London, marking the first full exhibition of his artwork. It included outdoor scenes created in Scotland, France, and Africa.

Time will tell if Prince George has had time to further develop his love of art during his first term at Eton College.

William attended the boarding school from 1995 to 2000. His estranged brother, Prince Harry, also attended the prestigious school from 1998 to 2003.

As for William and Middleton’s youngest children, Princess Charlotte, 11, and Prince Louis, 8, they currently attend day school at the Lambrook School in Berkshire, near Ascot.
