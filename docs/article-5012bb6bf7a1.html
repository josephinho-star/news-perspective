The two critical areas Giants are scouring the league for upgrades at NFL trade deadline

2026-11-10 05:05

The Giants are in it, and by in it, we mean really, really in the thick of the NFL playoff chase as November moves along.

There are all sorts of “short-term vs. long-term’’ discussions to be had as the trade deadline nears (Tuesday, 4 p.m.), and the desire is there to improve right now without setting up for failure by giving away too much down the road. The Giants are hot and heavily involved in scouring the league for potential upgrades, focusing on two areas: stopping the run on defense and finding a running back to help the rushing attack on offense.
