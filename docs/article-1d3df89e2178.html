A good robot vacuum and mop can make cleaning your whole house easy — and save you the hassle of picking up a mop yourself.

This smart robot vacuum has the brand’s OZMO Roller 3.0, which can tackle tough kitchen messes with ease. It’s equipped to handle any kind of mess, even offering efficient carpet dust removal that is a 224% improvement over previous models.

This Ecovacs option is also certified to remove 97.44% of dust allergens from the air, making this a great fit for those who constantly suffer from allergies. It has impressive cleaning power, too, up 69% from the previous model.
