Xi lands in US for high-stakes summit with Trump amid deep tensions

2026-09-23 21:46

Chinese leader begins 48-hour visit as Trump seeks progress on AI, Iran, rare earths and trade, with expectations for deals low

Chinese President Xi Jinping landed at Joint Base Andrews late Wednesday afternoon after a 15-hour flight where he was met on the tarmac by US President Donald Trump in an unusual move for a top American leader as the two sides extended a trade truce.

Xi, accompanied by first lady Peng Liyuan, senior Chinese official Cai Qi, Foreign Minister Wang Yi and several other Chinese dignitaries who were met with a modest arrival ceremony.
