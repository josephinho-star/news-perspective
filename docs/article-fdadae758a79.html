Steve Cohen’s massive Mets spending must finally get payoff to match after Tarik Skubal deal

2026-11-23 00:37

If it needed to be proven again that the Steve Cohen Mets are financial superheavyweights, then yet another monetary seismic boom was delivered Sunday when it was revealed that they had reached agreement on a pitching-record nine-year, $450 million contract with Tarik Skubal.

It was Juan Soto for 15 years at $765 million — the most money ever guaranteed a player. The Skubal deal destroyed the previous highs for total dollars given to a pitcher ($325 million for Yoshinobu Yamamoto) and for annual value (the $43.33 million that Cohen agreed to with Max Scherzer and Justin Verlander).
