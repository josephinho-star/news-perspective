Delta flight turns back 4 minutes after takeoff over pilot incapacitation

2026-09-03 05:16

A Delta Air Lines flight returned to an airport within minutes of takeoff after the crew reported a medical emergency involving a pilot.

Delta Flight 1208 returned to Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport (MSP) shortly after departing for Orlando, Florida, because of a reported medical issue involving a pilot, the airline told Fox News Digital.

“The crew followed established procedures to return to MSP where the aircraft landed safely,” Delta said in a statement shared with Fox News Digital.

“As safety comes first at Delta, we are focused on supporting the pilot and our customers to ensure onward travel.”

The Boeing 757-300 was carrying 178 passengers and seven crew members on the Aug. 31 flight, according to a Delta spokesperson.

The plane took off at about 11:29 a.m., and the crew requested a return roughly four minutes later, according to FlightAware data.

“We need to return to the field,” a crew member said in an air traffic control recording, according to reports. “Nature of the emergency: We have a potential pilot incapacitation. … We would like to return immediately.”

The crew declared an emergency to receive priority handling from air traffic control, the Delta spokesperson said.

After landing safely in Minneapolis, Delta arranged for a new flight crew so that the flight could continue to Orlando.

The flight arrived in Orlando later that evening, according to the airline.

Fox News Digital previously reported on another in-flight medical emergency involving multiple crew members.

A Frontier Airlines flight from Cleveland to Fort Lauderdale declared a medical emergency a couple of weeks ago after four flight attendants developed headaches and nausea shortly before landing.

The pilots asked for emergency medical personnel to meet the Airbus A321 at the gate at Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport, according to air traffic control communications.

The aircraft landed without incident and emergency responders waiting at the airport.

The cause of the flight attendants’ illnesses was not immediately known.

Michael Sinkewicz of Fox News Digital contributed reporting.
