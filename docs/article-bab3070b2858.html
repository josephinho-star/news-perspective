The 42 thoughtful gift ideas for women who already have it all

2026-09-16 20:20

Finding the perfect gifts for women can be tricky, especially when it feels like they already have everything.

Sure, a floral bouquet works in a pinch. I love flower delivery myself. But for those truly special moments, I want to give something thoughtful, maybe even a luxury gift for a milestone occasion — Christmas 2026 included.

Whether I’m shopping for my mom or a female friend, I’ve explored the full spectrum of ideas. From top picks at NAADAM to Cozy Earth, I’ve hunted down the best gifts for women — practically ever.

- Best Luxury Pajamas: Cozy Earth Bamboo Stretch-Knit Pajama Set

- Best Everyday Designer Bag: Coach Tabby Shoulder Bag with Pillow Quilting

- Best Plush Throw Blanket: Ugg Bliss Fleece Plush Throw Blanket

- Best 2-in-1 Hair Styler: Dyson Airstrait Dryer & Straightener

- Best Everyday Pearl Bracelet: Monica Vinader Mini Nugget Pearl Beaded Bracelet

I might be a little biased, but I’m excited about options from lululemon, Our Place, and even stunning jewelry from Kendra Scott and Monica Vinader.

My list is filled with practical, everyday items that she’ll come to love, in addition to some of the most sought-after items I’ve seen. Any of these picks will be adored, cherished, and appreciated whichever way you go.

For even more options, be sure to check out our edit of the best gifts for your wife, mom, girlfriend or sister to find the perfect thing. Few things are more awkward than giving a romantic gift to your mother-in-law, so get it right the first time with something that truly fits the category.

The Cozy Earth Bamboo Stretch-Knit Pajama Set is the Oprah-loved sleepwear set that makes an ordinary Tuesday night feel like a tiny luxury. Made for lounging, sleeping and generally refusing to put on “real clothes,” the set pairs a soft feel with a relaxed, polished look.
