‘Dutton Ranch’ Season 2 Release Date Info: When Does The Second Season Of ‘Dutton Ranch’ Come Out?

2026-08-30 14:00

It’s been nearly two months since Taylor Sheridan’s hit Yellowstone spin-off Dutton Ranch aired its intense Season 1 finale, and every week since, fans have been begging for more Dutton drama.

Dutton Ranch Season 1 finale spoilers ahead.

For those who need a refresher, when we last left Beth (Kelly Reilly) and Rip (Cole Hauser) in Episode 9, “El Padrino,” they were fearing for the safety of their son Carter (Finn Little), who was kidnapped by the cartel. Meanwhile, Beulah (Annette Bening) and Oreana (Natalie Alyn Lind) were processing the immense loss of Rob-Will (Jai Courtney). And Joaquin (Juan Pablo Raba) was reckoning with the consequences of his dad Mariano’s (Raoul Max Trujillo) return to Rio Paloma, Texas.

Dutton Ranch Season 1 left fans with a number of burning questions, from everything about Sheriff Wade’s (Josh Stewart) next move to a possible Dutton Ranch and Marshals crossover extravaganza. But at the top of every fan’s list of questions is “Will there be a Season 2 of Dutton Ranch?” And if so, “When does Dutton Ranch return with new episodes?”

Curious if Dutton Ranch is new this weekend? What’s Dutton Ranch Season 2’s renewal status? When is Dutton Ranch Season 2’s release date? And where can you watch Dutton Ranch online? We’ve got answers. Here’s what to know about the future of Dutton Ranch.

Is Dutton Ranch New This Weekend?

Sadly, no. There’s no new episode of Dutton Ranch on Paramount+ this weekend.

Will There Be A Season 2 Of Dutton Ranch? Dutton Ranch Season 2 Renewal Status:

You bet! On Wednesday, June 24, ahead of Dutton Ranch Episode 8, Paramount+ officially renewed the series! And it’s no surprise!

The series has a Certified Fresh 89% Tomatometer rating on Rotten Tomatoes, and according to Paramount+ press release, “Dutton Ranch cemented itself as a smash-hit by amassing 12.9 million viewers globally in the 7 days following its premiere — marking the biggest Original Series launch in Paramount+ history. The series also delivered 2.9 million total viewers during its two-episode debut on Paramount Network on May 15, making it the biggest series premiere on cable since 2023. With 1.85 million viewers for the premiere telecast alone, Dutton Ranch also ranked as the #1 cable entertainment telecast among both adults 18-49 and total viewers.”

“Dutton Ranch has resonated with audiences in a powerful way, building on the legacy of beloved characters, while establishing its own identity,” said Matt Thunell, President of Paramount Television Studios. “Thank you to our partners at Paramount+ and 101 Studios for a phenomenal first season. We look forward to bringing fans more stories from these unforgettable characters in Season 2.”

When Does The Second Season Of Dutton Ranch Come Out? Dutton Ranch Season 2 Release Date Info:

There’s no official Dutton Ranch Season 2 premiere date on the books yet, but The Hollywood Reporter confirmed that “production slated to begin early 2027.”

“We’re slated to start early 2027 to film,” Reilly told THR in July. “But the room starts up pretty quick. I think we’re going to be very much involved in the room, so the next sort of six months is all about writing it.”

Assuming production sticks to the predicted timeline, here’s hoping Dutton Ranch Season 2 will premiere in 2027. Stay tuned for more updates!

Is Cole Hauser Leaving Dutton Ranch? Who Is Dutton Ranch‘s New Showrunner?

This is still a popular question from Dutton Ranch fans, and we’re once again here to say no! The Yellowstone spin-off was specifically written to continue Beth, Rip, and Carter’s stories — plus, Hauser is an EP on the series. So don’t expect to say goodbye to Beth’s partner in crime any time soon.

When Dutton Ranch returns it will have a new showrunner, though. Benjamin Cavell joined the team in the wake of Chad Feehan departing from the series three weeks before its premiere.

What Is Dutton Ranch Streaming On? Paramount+ Streaming Info:

While we wait for Season 2 news, if you’re looking to rewatch Dutton Ranch Season 1 it will be available to stream on Paramount+.

Paramount+ offers two subscription plans, Essential and Premium. Right now, you can get started with a seven-day free trial on the Premium plan.

The ad-supported Essential plan typically costs $8.99/month, while the ad-free Premium plan (which comes with Showtime titles and live CBS) costs $13.99/month.

Is Dutton Ranch on Hulu? Is Dutton Ranch On Prime Video? Dutton Ranch Free Streaming Info:

While Dutton Ranch is a Paramount+ original series, you can Dutton Ranch on other streaming platforms like Hulu and Prime Video by adding Paramount+ for $13.99/month. Both streamers offer free trials for new subscribers.
