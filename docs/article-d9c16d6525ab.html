Influencer Jack Doherty, 22, arrested for domestic battery in Florida

2026-09-05 02:06

Internet personality Jack Doherty was arrested after an alleged domestic dispute in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, Page Six can confirm.

Online records show the popular streamer and YouTuber, 22, was booked in Broward County on a misdemeanor domestic battery charge on Friday.

Officers responded to a residence in the Riverside Park neighborhood shortly before noon and determined Doherty and a woman were involved in a “physical dispute” inside a home, according to Local 10 News.

Officers noted the woman was uninjured, according to the outlet’s report.

Doherty posed for a mug shot, and he appeared to have a cut on his cheek.

Doherty is being held in Broward County Main Jail with no listed bond as of Friday evening while he waits a pending trial, online records show.

The content creator, who has more than 15 million subscribers on YouTube and nearly 11 million followers on TikTok, was also arrested in Florida in November 2025 for drug possession after allegedly disrupting traffic while recording video for his social media channels.

After he refused to comply with officers at the scene, he was taken into custody, and during a search of his person, officers claimed he had amphetamine and marijuana on him.

He was also charged with resisting arrest.

Videos of the arrest show Doherty challenging cops to a duel while standing in the middle of a Miami street.

State Attorney Katherine Fernandez Rundle’s office dropped the cannabis charge completely, and Doherty was accepted into the Miami-Dade County Felony Drug Pre-Trial Diversion Program in March.

To enter this program, Doherty waived his right to a speedy trial and agreed to fulfill all mandatory community program conditions.

As a result of his acceptance, the State Attorney’s Office directed the Clerk of Court to cancel the upcoming May trial and remove the case from the active court calendar.

Following the trial cancellation, Doherty successfully completed the program conditions, prompting the court to issue a final discharge certificate in May, which effectively cleared the path for his charges to be officially dropped without a conviction.

Doherty’s latest arrest comes less than a year after his previous run-in with Florida law enforcement, though that case ultimately ended without a trial or conviction.

The streamer has also made headlines for his personal life.

In October 2025, he and his then-wife, OnlyFans star McKinley Richardson, split after less than a year of marriage, with the breakup becoming public after their relationship and wedding made waves online.

A court date for Doherty regarding his domestic battery charges has not been set.
