Trump envoys Witkoff and Kushner to travel to Moscow and Kyiv this weekend

2026-09-04 20:23

President Trump's envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner will travel this weekend to Moscow and Kyiv for talks on ending the war, U.S. officials said. Why it matters: This will be the first visit by Witkoff and Kushner to Kyiv since President Trump returned to office. "They're bringing with them a plan to end the war," Trump told reporters in the Oval Office on Friday. State of play: Witkoff and Kushner will travel on Friday, hold talks with Russian President Vladimir Putin at the Kremlin on Saturday and then meet with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky in Kyiv on Sunday.
