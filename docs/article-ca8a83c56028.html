Julianne Hough says she saw ‘insane’ results from this eyelash serum

2026-09-25 23:12

With a new season of “Dancing With the Stars” upon us, all eyes are on Julianne Hough.

But the booked-and-busy star took a break from hosting and rehearsing this week to spill her beauty must-haves to Marie Claire — including the Rodan + Fields Lash Boost Serum she swears by.

“My eyelashes are insane now,” she raved of the results.

“I’ve tried serums in the past and they never worked. This didn’t change the color of my eyes either, or anything like that.”

The conditioning formula is designed to help enhance lashes’ length, volume and definition — all while keeping them properly hydrated and better protected against breakage.

It’s earned a 4.6-star average on the brand’s website, out of over 12,000 (!) reviews.

“I have recommended this to many people who comment on my lashes,” one shopper wrote, while another said they “can’t live without it.”

Hough finishes her eyelash routine with a swipe of Thrive Causemetics Liquid Lash Extensions Mascara, a clean formula that’s a favorite of fellow stars including Meghan Markle and Paige DeSorbo. (“I always come back to this mascara,” the latter explained on Amazon Live earlier this year.)
