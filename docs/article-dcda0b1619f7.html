Bloodied ‘Pretty Little Liars’ star Keegan Allen robbed at gunpoint, knifepoint in attack outside luxury Hollywood grocery store

2026-10-09 08:54

“Pretty Little Liars” star Keegan Allen was robbed at gunpoint and knifepoint on Thursday night as he declared that “West Hollywood is not safe anymore.”

Allen, 37, was left bloodied after two masked men threatened to kill the actor and attacked him for his watch outside of the high-end Laurel Supply grocery store.

“These two dudes hit me in the head and pointed a gun at me with a knife and then took off,” Allen said on Instagram moments after the ambush.“They had masks on and took my watch. They took my watch. Dammit.”

Security footage of the robbery shows the two masked men approach Allen, who was seemingly putting items into his truck’s rear passenger side in the store’s parking lot.

One suspected robber struck Allen in the head multiple times with a gun while the other held the “Walker” actor at knifepoint.

The brazen duo fled once they snatched only Allen’s watch and allowed him to keep a bracelet and a ring as he showed off his bloodied arm.

Allen said in Instagram comments that he went to the hospital after the attack but vowed he would be “OK.” He added that he suspected he had a concussion, saying he is “def concussed.”

Page Six has reached out to Allen’s rep and the LAPD for comment on the incident.

Allen also seemingly called out Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass and California Gov. Gavin Newsom over crime in the city.

“Will update more soon. Let’s hope all these flock cameras that (Karen Bass), (Gavin Newsom) and everyone thinks will stop crime can find these very dangerous criminals. God bless,” Allen wrote.

West Hollywood has 39 Flock Safety license plate readers and six installed live-video cameras, out of 54 approved, according to a city report. The cameras are designed to help cops track suspects and solve crimes.

Allen is best known for playing Toby Cavanaugh on “Pretty Little Liars” from 2010 to 2017. He later starred as Liam Walker on the drama “Walker” from 2021 to 2024.

He most recently appeared in an episode of “The Rookie” this year.
