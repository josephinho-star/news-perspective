Long Island high schoolers taunt football rivals with sickening signs with girlfriend’s pictures, names

2026-09-21 17:37

Disturbing photos show Long Island high school boys taunting a rival football team with nasty, sexually suggestive signs that include pictures and names of the players’ girlfriends.

The sickening signs were flashed in the crowd by Sachem North kids during Friday’s game against William Floyd, with one poster showing a player and his girlfriend with the question who is “easier to score on.”

A second sign asks if they could “touchdown” on another girl after the game.

Fuming parents now want something done about the behavior.

“There were numerous signs being held by students about doing things to their girlfriends — they even had names and faces on their signs — these poor girls had to be victimized by this,” said Paul Horst, who has relatives that play football at William Floyd.

The bullies, according to people at the game, were not reprimanded nor told to put the signs away by administrators.

“Nothing was being done by anyone,” Horst said about the school’s response and allowing the students to display the disgusting signs.

When one of the girls featured on a sign approached the group of disruptive students to confront them and ask them to put the signs away, she was spit on by one of the boys in the group, a relative of the girlclaimed to the The Post.

A small intersex scuffle then broke out between the two, according to a video of the incident shared by a relative and viewed by The Post.
