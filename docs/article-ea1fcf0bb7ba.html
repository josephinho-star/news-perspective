Mets face important question after two pitchers turn seasons around on new teams

2026-09-02 23:48

ST. PETERSBURG, Fla. — Did the Mets miss something?

Freddy Peralta and David Peterson began this season as 40 percent of the team’s starting rotation, floundered and were traded, for one reason or another. Both have resurrected their seasons in new locales, begging the question of whether the Mets somehow overlooked a potential remedy for the pitchers’ struggles.
