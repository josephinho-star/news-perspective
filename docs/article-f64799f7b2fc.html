Still in bed? This 48% off Amazon gadget can already have your coffee brewing

2026-09-24 15:00

Whether or not you own a smart device, a smart plug can help turn your mundane everyday gadgets into something that can be voice-controlled and operated without ever leaving the comfort of your couch or bed.

Imagine telling your Keurig to start brewing your French Roast before even opening your eyes. Or, imagine turning on your lights without flipping a switch.

The Amazon Smart Plug can do just that, and now it’s on sale for 48% off, for a limited time only.

The Amazon Smart Plug is compatible with coffee machines, lights and basically anything with an on/off switch.

The best part is that you don’t require a smart hub to use this device — just the Alexa app on your phone. The brand says you can get it set up within minutes, too, making it very user-friendly.

From there, you can automate schedules, use your voice to start and stop devices and even set up daily routines.

The plug itself has a small form factor and will blend easily into your home without sticking out. Plus, over 500,000 Amazon shoppers have rated it on Amazon, with many loving the ease of setup.

“The Amazon Smart Plug is one of those rare tech purchases that actually delivers on the promise of being simple. No headaches, no complicated setup — just plug it in and you’re basically halfway done,” says one shopper.

If you want to automate more devices in your home without having to blow your budget on new smart appliances, the Amazon Smart Plug is a simple solution, and it’s on sale for Prime Big Deal Days.

Does the Amazon Smart Plug work with Google Assistant or Apple HomeKit?

No. The Amazon Smart Plug works exclusively with Alexa.

Does the plug support 5 GHz Wi-Fi networks?

The device operates exclusively on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks.

Is there a smart hub required?

No smart home hub is required. You can set up and control the plug entirely through the free Alexa app on your mobile device.

Can the Amazon Smart Plug be used outdoors?

No. The Amazon Smart Plug is rated for indoor use only and features no weatherproofing.

What is the warranty and return policy for the Amazon Smart Plug?

The plug features a 1-year limited warranty and is eligible for Amazon’s standard 30-day return policy.

Your Prime Big Deal Days FAQs, answered

What is Prime Big Deal Days and when is it?

Prime Big Deal Days — fondly known as October Prime Day — is Amazon’s biggest fall sale, offering massive discounts before the holiday rush of Black Friday and Cyber Monday. October Prime Day runs from Tuesday, Oct. 6 to Wednesday, Oct. 7, 2026.

Prime Day vs. Prime Big Deal Days

Prime Day and Prime Big Deal Days are both Prime member-exclusive seasonal sales with massive discounts on rotating bestsellers. Unlike July’s Prime Day, Prime Big Deal Days runs every October as a precursor to the busy holiday season, allowing shoppers to start saving on gift shopping early.

Do you have to be an Amazon Prime member to shop?

October Prime Day is exclusively available to Prime members. However interested shoppers can grab a 30-day free trial to try Prime, and its many benefits with no strings attached, and gain access to exclusive deals and the convenience of fast, free shipping.
