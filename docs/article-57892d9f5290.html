Hong Kong must show real remorse for the Tai Po fire tragedy

2026-11-08 01:30

A bow and a resignation are not enough. The government must deliver long-overdue reforms, support survivors and take genuine responsibility

A political apology should acknowledge failure, accept responsibility and express remorse. It should also commit the government to preventing a recurrence. The purpose of a political apology is to bring closure. While the chief executive would like closure, he cannot yet declare that it has been achieved.
