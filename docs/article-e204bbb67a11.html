Kim Kardashian wears baby pink leotard, see-through skirt to support Lewis Hamilton at F1 Singapore

2026-10-11 20:47

The reality star made a show-stopping appearance at F1 Singapore Saturday, wearing a baby pink halter leotard under a sheer maxi skirt.

She accessorized with a matching pink purse and tied her brunette locks back in a slicked-back bun.

Kardashian appeared in good spirits, waving to fans as she walked.

The Skims founder, 45, and the British Formula One racer, 41, have been linked since February, when they were spotted spending a romantic weekend together at Estelle Manor, a luxury hotel and country club, in the Cotswolds.

They’re also comfortable posting one another on social media. In August, Hamilton shared never-before-seen pics with Kardashian on his Instagram.

A month prior, he posted snaps of him spending time with her four kids with ex-husband Kanye West — North, 13, Saint, 10, Chicago, 8, and Psalm, 7 — during a wholesome family getaway.

“Hold your people close 🫂,” he captioned the post.

“I wasn’t ready to open up,” she explained to the Times’ Style Magazine. “I really wasn’t even thinking about it.”

She added, “The universe works in your favor when you just allow it to.”

She has no regrets about her long break from dating, calling it “great,” and explaining that she “could focus on [her] kids and projects that [she] really wanted to be involved with.”

“I really felt no rush,” she said. “Like, I really didn’t feel empty. I felt like I needed my time.”

In April, a source close to West, 49, told Page Six that he approved of his ex-wife’s latest romance. Hamilton and Kardashian had been longtime friends before their relationship turned romantic.

“This is a good thing. Kanye [isn’t] questioning ‘Is Lewis a good person?’ He’s a sweet, smart person, a good person and an incredible athlete,” the source told us, adding that Hamilton is an “acquaintance” of the rapper’s inner circle.

The source noted that West’s feelings toward the popular athlete are definitely more positive than his attitude towards Davidson, 32, whom he “did hate.”

“He feels at peace right now with Lewis being around his kids,” the source said of the “Famous” rapper. “He was trustful about that. He loves [Kardashian] in a way that he wants [the best for] her wellbeing.”
