My late friend Peter Max filled my home with art — and my life with memories

2026-09-22 22:57

Peter Max, my longtime forever friend. We dinner’d together. I visited his studio, he visited my home. I have more Maxes than furniture. When a magazine printed a full-page glossy black-and-white photo of me, he Peter Max’d the magazine’s full page. He did my friend Our Town columnist Arlene Kayatt’s face on a paper napkin. She framed it.

Comedian Gary Willner remembered this. “I was MC’ing a place in Jersey. In those days Peter was praying to Our Lady of the Tax Shelter. He’d even painted a whole airplane for the Clintons. I remember him telling Cindy: ‘Rembrandt was not really a bad painter.’ ”

Willner recounts that I sent him word that I was there in the club with Peter Max and would he introduce Max to the audience. But Willner didn’t know what Peter looked like.

“The spotlight came on, the audience waited,” Willner said. “I didn’t know what to do so I pointed to some guy in the front who shook his head no. Cindy jumped up and said, ‘He’s right here!’ The spotlight hit him and I said, ‘Of course, you look so young I didn’t recognize you!’ Oy, a killer moment.” Willner’s next move was to go through the floor.

Peter’s smiling somewhere now.
