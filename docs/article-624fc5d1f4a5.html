Wembanyama scores 50 points, bags 19 rebounds in Spurs’ 14th win of season

2026-11-22 07:44

Victor Wembanyama has scored 50 points and grabbed 19 rebounds to power San Antonio to a 114-91 victory over New Orleans as the Spurs matched the National Basketball Association’s best record at 14-3.

The 22-year-old, 7-foot-4-inch (2.24m) Frenchman made 20 of 29 shots from the floor, 5 of 11 from three-point range, and added five blocked shots, four steals and an assist in 32 minutes on the court on Saturday.

San Antonio won for the 14th time in 15 games, and Wembanyama became only the third NBA player since the 1973-74 season with at least 50 points, 15 rebounds, four steals and five blocks in a game.

“It is very special, but unlike many of my peers, I’m not very good at stat-padding,” Wembanyama said.

Wembanyama was just shy of his career-high score of 52 points, set in a victory over Dallas on November 4.

“Wemby” scored 17 points in the first quarter as the Spurs jumped to a 32-29 lead over the visiting Pelicans and had 32 points and 13 rebounds at half-time with San Antonio ahead 61-45.

“I thought he started the game off aggressive, and I thought he was trying to play with a lot of force offensively, specifically, just in terms of taking up real estate, trying to get to the rim,” Spurs coach Mitch Johnson said.

“I thought he did a really good job of playing with force.”

Johnson did not relish taking Wemby to the bench but wanted to avoid any chance of an injury in a game well in hand.

“Was it tough to pull him out after he got 50? No, I was just hoping he got to 50 when he did,” Johnson said. “That would have been hard to take him out that close to 50.”

The Spurs levelled with the Los Angeles Lakers and Philadelphia 76ers for the best record in the league, the Sixers having matched the mark earlier in a 132-129 victory over Milwaukee.

Joel Embiid led the Philadelphia 76ers to a fifth consecutive victory despite Tyler Herro’s career-high 50 points for the Bucks.

Embiid delivered 26 points, a season-high 17 rebounds and nine assists for the host Sixers as they dropped the league-worst Bucks to 1-16, stretching Milwaukee’s losing skid to 13 games.

Herro added 11 rebounds and seven assists, but he missed a chance to send the game into overtime.

He sank a three-point shot at the finish, but officials ruled after consulting video replay that the shot left his hand after the final buzzer.

Four-time NBA Most Valuable Player LeBron James scored 21 points, grabbed six rebounds, recorded three steals and provided six assists for the 76ers.

“For us to be a super complete team, I think the power of the pass is very infectious,” James said. “It’s key for our other guys as well to make sure they feel in a rhythm and they feel confident.”

Anfernee Simons added 18 points off the Philadelphia bench while Tyrese Maxey added 17 and VJ Edgecombe scored 16 as the 76ers withstood a late Bucks rally.

“Soon we’re going to have to put together a 48-minute game,” Maxey said.

At Madison Square Garden, Mikal Bridges sank a three-pointer at the final buzzer to give the reigning champions New York Knicks a 97-96 victory over Portland.

The Trail Blazers rallied from a 26-point deficit to seize a 96-94 lead on two free throws by Deni Avdija with 3.2 seconds remaining.

The Knicks tried to inbound the ball to star Jalen Brunson for the last shot but went to Bridges when Brunson was covered.

“They played harder than us in that second half,” Bridges said. “That’s on us, but we just finished the game. It goes 48 minutes, and we just kept persevering through the time and ended up winning.”

The Knicks, without injured starters OG Anunoby and Karl-Anthony Towns, improved to 10-6 with their fourth consecutive victory while Portland fell to 10-6.

Brunson led New York with 30 points, 19 of them in the first quarter, while Landry Shamet added 20 and Bridges had 12 points.

In other games, Jaden McDaniels scored 25 points to lead Minnesota’s 104-95 home win over Memphis, while Kyrie Irving had 25 points and Cooper Flagg added 22 in a 110-100 Dallas home win over the Los Angeles Clippers.
