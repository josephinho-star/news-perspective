GLP-1 costs can spiral fast, but this telehealth plan locks in your price

2026-09-14 14:49

There are a lot of GLP-1 services out there, but many command a high price and are rarely worth the money or the hassle.

There is one brand that wants to make the process easier for all, and that’s Arbor, a telehealth program that helps connect people with licensed U.S. clinicians who can prescribe compounded GLP-1 weight-loss medication online.

What sets Arbor apart, though, is that the price you start your medication at is the price you keep. Normally, you’ll be charged extra for any medication changes. Arbor takes that stress away by locking in your price for life.

Starting is simple, too. You’ll do a quick 10-minute assessment online. Then, a licensed U.S. clinician will review it, and once approved, medication is dispensed by a licensed pharmacy and shipped free via FedEx Express.

There are two plans available on Arbor: compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide. The former is the more affordable alternative and helps curb appetite and reduce cravings. The latter helps curb appetite, reduce cravings and support metabolism.

Apart from making it more seamless to get a GLP-1 prescription, Arbor also offers 24/7 physician access, dietitian-led support and a 12-month money-back guarantee if you’re not satisfied or don’t reach the goals you desire.
