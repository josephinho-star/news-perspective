14-year-old NYC Jewish boy slashed by another congregant, 22, inside Brooklyn synagogue: cops

2026-09-15 01:36

A 14-year-old boy was slashed by an unhinged fellow congregant inside a Brooklyn schul on Monday evening, police said.

The 22-year-old attacker slashed the teen in the hand inside a synagogue on 54th Street near 17th Avenue in Borough Park around 7:20 p.m., the NYPD said.

The slasher was then taken into custody without incident.

The teen victim refused medical attention at the scene.

Sources said that the assailant is well-known in the neighborhood and frequents houses of worship.
