When Does The Next Season of ‘Will Trent’ Premiere on Netflix and ABC? ‘Will Trent’ Season 5 Delay Explained

2026-11-06 18:30

It’s a big week for the ever-increasing Will Trent fandom. Why is that, you ask? Because the first four seasons of the ABC favorite are now streaming on Netflix.

Created by Liz Heldens and Daniel T. Thomsen and based on Karin Slaughter’s novels of the same name, the series has been an ABC staple since its debut in January 2023. Now the show is set to reach a whole new audience thanks to the vaunted “Netflix Bump.” But questions abound.

ABC’s fall schedule feels a little incomplete without Will Trent (and High Potential). So what’s the reason for the delay? Has Will Trent been canceled, or will the series be back for a fifth season of intrigue?

Here’s everything you need to know.

Will There Be a Season 5 of Will Trent? Is Will Trent Cancelled?

Don’t worry. Will Trent’s not going anywhere. ABC renewed both The Rookie and Will Trent for new seasons back in April 2026.

Why Is Will Trent Season 5 Delayed?

Unfortunately, a few of your ABC favorites — High Potential, The Rookie, and Will Trent — are being held for midseason premieres, which means all three shows aren’t on the network’s fall schedule.

It was just announced that the season premieres of Will Trent and High Potential will air Tuesday, January 5, 2027, on ABC. The Rookie and the new spinoff The Rookie North will move to Wednesdays, with their season/series premieres set for January 6, 2027.

Is Will Trent on Netflix?

Yes! The first four seasons of Will Trent premiered Tuesday, November 3 on Netflix.

Will Season 5 of Will Trent be on Netflix?

There’s no official news on if/when Will Trent Season 5 will find its way to Netflix. Either way, it won’t be on the platform anytime soon. If the upcoming season were to drop on Netflix, it would have to be after the entire season airs on ABC.

Again, there’s no official news, but if Season 5 hits Netflix, it would most likely be in the fall of 2027.

Where To Watch Will Trent Seasons 1-4 Online:

You have a lot of options. Seasons 1-4 of Will Trent are streaming on Netflix, Hulu, and Disney+.

Disney+ offers a number of subscription options, so you can find the one that works for you. With ads, a subscription costs $12.49/month; without ads, it’s $21.49/month or $214.99/year.

There are also Disney+ bundles with Hulu, HBO Max, and ESPN+, so you can subscribe to up to three services at once and save over 40% every month. The bundles are available in a few different configurations, starting at $12.99/month for Disney+ and Hulu with ads, and going up to $34.99/month for Disney+, Hulu, and Max ad-free.

Will Trent returns in early 2027 on ABC.
