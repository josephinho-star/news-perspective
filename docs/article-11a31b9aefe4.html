Score up to $800 off the best mattress and bedding sales of the year this Labor Day

You know how it goes.

The days are getting shorter, summer is winding down and things are starting to smell like fall, which means it’s almost time to cozy up in bed and hunker down.

But if there’s one little slice of good news, it’s that the end of summer also marks Labor Day weekend — and with it, some major shopping deals across a variety of big-ticket items, including mattresses, bed frames and luxurious bedding. In translation: major savings for your wallet and serious upgrades for your new sleep oasis.

While Labor Day officially falls on Sept. 7, Labor Day mattress sales are already kicking off during the weekend, giving shoppers a little extra time to take advantage of deals of up to $800 off sleep essentials that make the end of summer feel a little less grim.

The good news? Many of our Post Wanted writers and editors have personally tested mattresses and best-selling bedding from top retailers like Saatva, Cozy Earth, Amazon and more, so we can speak to exactly what’s worth buying for every kind of sleeper — and what’s not.
