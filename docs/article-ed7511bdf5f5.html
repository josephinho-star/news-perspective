Indonesia factory fire kills three, 36 missing

2026-11-22 07:46

A fire at a sweets factory in Indonesia has killed at least three people and 36 others are missing.

Photos and videos circulating online on Sunday showed flames and thick smoke coming from the Fonusa Agung Mulia factory in Banten province, which borders the capital Jakarta. Footage from inside the three-storey appeared to show the fire caused extensive damage, with equipment blackened and destroyed.

A company representative said 300 workers were inside when the blaze started. He added 54 were initially missing, but 15 have since been rescued.

Four of those rescued were taken to hospital, said the National Search and Rescue Agency in a statement.

“The fire is still burning in several locations and firefighting teams are continuing extinguishing and cooling operations,” rescue official Rizky Dwianto told The Associated Press news agency.

Firefighters from Serang district and Banten province were battling flames in several parts of the facility more than 12 hours after the fire began, Dwianto said.

Rescue workers along with fire department personnel and the Indonesian Red Cross searched different sections of the factory, focusing on areas where victims might be trapped, he added.

The disaster agency said the cause of the fire was under investigation.

Local broadcasters and social media accounts shared footage of families gathering outside the factory.

The factory produces milk-based snacks and sweets, known as Milkita.

Indonesia has suffered a number of deadly fires in recent years. A fire in an office building in Jakarta killed at least 22 people last December. An explosion at an Indonesian nickel-processing plant killed at least 13 people in 2023.
