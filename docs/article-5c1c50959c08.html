Here’s how to watch ‘The Shards’ finale: Episode 9 start time, streaming

2026-09-08 21:00

“The Shards” Season 1 is coming to a dramatic conclusion tonight.

With the penultimate episode ending with more questions than answers, fans won’t want to miss the Ryan Murphy-directed FX show’s season finale tonight.

The headline-making series is based on the novel of the same name by Bret Easton Ellis, which follows rich prep school teens whose lives are upended by a serial killer in 1981 Los Angeles.

Read on for everything you need to know to catch the episode as soon as it drops.

When is ‘The Shards’ season finale?

The ninth and final episode of “The Shards” Season 1 hits Hulu on Sept. 8, 2026 at 9 p.m. ET.

How to watch ‘The Shards’ season finale

“The Shards” season finale begins streaming on Hulu in just a few hours, so you’ll need a subscription to tune in. If you’re new to Hulu, the basic (with ads) price is $11.99/month. If you want to upgrade to an ad-free plan, it costs $18.99/month.

Igby Rigney stars as 17-year-old Bret Easton Ellis, while Homer Gere, Graham Campbell, Hayes Warner, Wes Bentley and Kaia Gerber round out the rest of the main cast.

Gerber and Gere have been giving fans flashbacks to another era in more ways than one throughout the retro series’ press tour, given Homer’s dad Richard Gere and Kaia’s mom Cindy Crawford were married from 1991 to 1995.

“He’s such a wonderful actor. I mean, my god. This was his second thing that he ever did,” Gerber told the Associated Press of Homer. “He has this confidence and this charisma. He’s so magnetic to watch … He’s also just a really great guy.”
