Gypsy Rose Blanchard’s fiancé Ken Urker’s autopsy complete: coroner

2026-10-02 17:07

Gypsy Rose Blanchard’s fiancé Ken Urker’s autopsy has been completed after he was found dead in his Louisiana home Thursday.

“As you all know an autopsy was conducted this morning on Mr. Urker. At this time, there is no definitive cause of death,” the Lafourche Parish Coroner’s Office confirmed in a statement to Page Six. “The case remains pending completion of toxicology testing.”

The office will provide no further updates until results are received and the investigation is complete, according to the statement.

Urker was found dead on what would’ve been his 34th birthday.

The Public Information Officer from Lafourche Parish told Page Six officers responded to his Louisiana home on Oct. 1 after receiving a call at 6:15pm.

“Kenan Urker, 34, was found dead upon arrival,” the statement read. “We are continuing the death investigation. To this point in the investigation, we have found no evidence of foul play.”
