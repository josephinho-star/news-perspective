Russian drones hit Turkish-owned civilian ship in Black Sea, Zelenskyy says

2026-10-05 13:08

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Monday that two Russian drones struck a Turkish-owned civilian vessel carrying corn in neutral waters in the Black Sea, killing the ship’s captain.

Zelenskyy said Azerbaijani and Indian nationals were aboard the vessel at the time of the attack.

Eleven crew members were rescued, including one who was injured, while the fate of another person was initially unknown, he said in a statement on social media platform X.

The Ukrainian leader described the vessel as a Turkish-owned civilian ship carrying corn and said it was sailing in neutral waters when it was hit by two Russian drones.

Ukraine had proposed measures aimed at reducing tensions and improving security in the Black Sea, and that proposals had also been put forward by third countries, Zelenskyy said.

He accused Russia of choosing attacks rather than ensuring the safe passage of civilian vessels and called for international pressure to restore security in the Black Sea and protect global food supplies.

The incident comes amid heightened security concerns over commercial shipping in the Black Sea as the Russia-Ukraine war continues.
