Major drug kingpin ‘The Kraken’ caught living secret life in posh California city

2026-11-17 04:17

Federal agents arrested an alleged Guatemalan drug kingpin notoriously known as “Kraken” in San Diego and slapped millions in rewards on him and a fugitive cartel boss.

40-year-old Axel Bladimir Montejo Saenz –– who goes by “Mosh” and “Kraken” –– was caught after being accused of running a massive cocaine ring as head of a transnational criminal organization.

The “Kraken” had ties to narcoterrorist cartels and was previously indicted on November 1, 2018 for international cocaine trafficking in multiple South American countries.

“According to publicly filed documents, during the multi-year investigation targeting Montejo Saenz, agents seized over 5,500 kilograms of cocaine and $3.7 million USD linked to Montejo Saenz,” the release added.

The US Department of State and Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) also offered a $10 million reward for information that leads to Saenz’s conviction.

Along with Saenz, a $10 million prize for information leading to the arrest or conviction of 44-year-old Walfre Donaldo Calderon-Calderon –– known as “El Teniente Jr.” and “Teniente” –– was posted.

The 44-year-old was charged in 2019 on cocaine-related charges and now “leads a drug trafficking operation with links to narcoterrorist cartels in northwest Guatemala near the Guatemala-Mexico border.”

In June 2025, “Teniente’s” father was killed during a shootout with Mexican law enforcement. His father, Baldemar Calderon-Carrillo, was a lead defendant in the 2019 indictment.

Monday’s announcement comes as another alleged Guatemalan drug kingpin was arrested back in April. Eugenio Dario Molina-Lopez, 61, was accused of being the leader of a transnational criminal organization called Los Huistas.

“Los Huistas is a drug trafficking organization primarily based in the Huehuetenango region of Northwest Guatemala that borders Mexico and is engaged in a massive cocaine trafficking operation,” an April press release said.
