I thought ‘brain gum’ was bizarre — until I chewed away my sky-high work stress before Black Friday

2026-11-25 16:29

Full disclosure: When I first started seeing social media users rave about so-called “brain gum,” I thought the whole thing was pretty bizarre.

The idea of chewing gum to boost your brainpower felt a bit far-fetched. But one brand kept popping up on my feed: Neuro Gum. TikTokers have raved about its mood-boosting and calming benefits; even Joe Rogan endorsed the brand on his podcast.

The gum is beautifully packaged in little yellow packets and comes in different formulations, but the Calm & Focus option was the one I was most intrigued by. Unlike traditional energy-boosting gums, this formula is entirely caffeine-free, aspartame-free and sugar-free. A 54-piece pack typically retails for $28 on Amazon, but it’s currently marked down by 20% for Black Friday.

The Calm & Focus formula combines KSM-66 ashwagandha, an ingredient that has been studied for its potential to help manage stress and support healthy cortisol levels, alongside vitamins B6 and B12, which play key roles in normal nervous system function and neurotransmitter production.

Even considering this impressive list of ingredients, I wasn’t entirely convinced that popping a piece of gum could do much to help me relax. My stress levels tend to skyrocket ahead of major shopping events like Cyber Week, when we’re spending hours hunting for deals, testing products and writing around the clock.

So, when my first pack of Neuro Gum landed on my desk a couple of weeks before the big event (we consider it our Super Bowl), I decided it was time to give it a chance — and it ended up seriously surprising me.

I’m not typically a fan of fruity flavors, but I found this one surprisingly tasty — sweet without being so overpowering that I felt like I was chewing candy. It was also easy to chew, without making that slightly annoying smacking sound.

It also had a pleasant smell, which probably helped with the overall experience. After about 10 minutes, the flavor started to mellow out a bit, which I didn’t mind. That’s also around when I began noticing the effects.

It wasn’t as if I was suddenly hit with an unstoppable urge to power through my workload or run laps around the office. Instead, I noticed a slight sense of calm that helped me keep going without feeling frazzled, crashing or reaching for caffeine to get me through it.

The focus benefits were where I noticed the biggest difference; I felt slightly less distracted by the usual workday interruptions and better able to stay on task. And judging by the reviews, I’m not the only one who has felt this way. Many have noticed that it’s helped calm them down and keeps them feeling “a bit more settled.”

“The calming effects take a little while to realize, but I’m very happy with my focus,” one please Neuro user shared. “And for anyone on the fence, especially if you’re distracted through the day, you’ll most likely see a huge benefit.”

The brand recommends chewing one piece two to four times daily to support calm and focus. The formula is vegan, gluten-free and third-party tested for safety and purity. It’s also manufactured in a GMP-certified facility.

As with any supplement, it’s a good idea to consult your physician before use, especially if you have any preexisting health conditions or take medication..
