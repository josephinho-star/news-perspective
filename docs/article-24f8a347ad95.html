Taylor Swift drops raunchiest lyric since ‘Wood’ in Travis Kelce-inspired ‘Cleveland!’

2026-09-25 14:24

Taylor Swift sent fans wild over her “dirty” lyrics in her new track “Cleveland!” inspired by her husband, Travis Kelce.

In the brand-new song from “The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore,” Swift sings, “The taste of me is acquired, but it’s his favorite dish.”

Many listeners interpreted the lyric as having a double meaning.

“THE TASTE OF ME IS ACQUIRED BUT ITS HIS FAVORITE DISH????????????? OHHH TAYLOR I LOVE IT WHEN YOUR DIRTY,” one fan wrote on X.

“The taste of me is acquired, but it’s his favorite dish” oh my god Taylor lol,” another added.

A third quipped, “THE TASTE OF ME IS ACQUIRED BUT ITS HIS FAVORITE DISH @taylorswift13 YOU ARE INSANE BABE.

“The taste of me is acquired but it’s his favorite dish oh they’re FREAKYYYYYYYYYY,” a fourth posted.

This isn’t the first time Swift has hinted at her and Kelce’s intimacy in her songs.

Her song “Wood,” included in the original October 2025 release of “TLOAS,” featured raunchy lyrics about the Kansas City Chiefs tight end.

“Forgive me, it sounds cocky / He (ah!)matized [d–kmatized] me / And opened my eyes / Redwood tree / It ain’t hard to see / His love was the key / That opened my thighs,” Swift sang.

She ramped up the innuendos further by singing, “New Heights of manhood / I ain’t gotta knock on wood,” fully calling out Kelce’s sports podcast.

“You only need to find one person in this entire world / Who doesn’t find you completely insufferable / And I have an important announcement to make / I found mine!” she sings toward the end of the track.

The song ends with Swift dropping her first-ever mention of her and Kelce’s Madison Square Garden nuptials when she sings, “Now I’m wearing white!”

Her four newest tracks — which Swift wrote and produced in Sweden alongside longtime collaborators Max Martin and Shellback — include “Patient Zero,” “Cleveland!,” “Pink Clouding” and “Babylon.”

The tunes dropped Friday at midnight after Swift, 36, sent fans into a frenzy with a countdown on her website on Tuesday before revealing the imminent drop of “Patient Zero.”
