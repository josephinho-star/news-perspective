Alix Earle spotted dating Madison Beer’s ex on Justin Herbert’s home turf

2026-10-01 23:56

Los Angeles Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert can only hope to have his name in the same conversation with Tom Brady’s once his football career ends.

For right now, he’ll have to content with being linked to Brady through their respective romantic lives.

Chargers fans are well aware that their starting quarterback is engaged to singer Madison Beer. They might not be as familiar with Beer’s dating history.

The 27-year-old Beer was in a relationship with Zack Bia for about two years, between 2017 and 2019. Bia is an American DJ and record executive who lives in Los Angeles.

On Thursday, photos surfaced of Bia on a dinner date in Beverly Hills with Alix Earle.

Sports fans are familiar with Earle because of the multiple athletes she has dated over the years. These include recently retired baseball player Tyler Wade and New York Giants wide receiver Braxton Berrios.

Earle sparked dating rumors with Tom Brady earlier this year after the two were spotted at Super Bowl parties together. They never directly confirmed there was any romance between them, but Earle — who now has a reality television series titled “Earle Meets World” on Netflix — has hinted at it several times.

In other words, Herbert is engaged to Beer, who once dated Bia, who is now dating Earle, who was (allegedly) dating Brady.

Both Herbert and Brady would probably prefer to keep the conversation centered on football.
