Rangers acquire Max Sasson in trade with Canucks to bolster forward depth

2026-11-21 00:26

The Rangers have made a move for a reinforcement just as the injuries have started to accumulate along their forward lines.

The Blueshirts acquired penalty-killing center Max Sasson from the Canucks for a 2029 fourth-round pick. The 26-year-old has netted five goals among eight points for Vancouver this season after scoring 13 times in 66 games in 2025-26.

tRY IT NOWThe move came a few hours after veteran forward Oliver Bjorkstrand missed practice Thursday with an upper-body injury.

Coach Mike Sullivan said it’s “a fair statement” that Bjorkstrand may not be available to play in Sunday’s game against the Hurricanes at MSG.

“He’s being evaluated for an upper-body injury. It was just something that he’s been dealing with,” Sullivan said. “I don’t know specifically when it occurred, but obviously, just from his discussion with our medical team, I think prompted more evaluation.”

Pavel Dorofeyev (lower-body injury) and Joseph Veleno (also lower body) skated on their own ahead of the session in Tarrytown.

Dorofeyev sat out Monday’s win over the Canadiens, while Veleno has been sidelined since getting injured Oct. 4 against the Mammoth.

Defenseman Sean Durzi (lower body) practiced for the second consecutive day and might be in line to make his first appearance Sunday since Oct. 26 against the Kings, according to Sullivan.

“We’re encouraged with Durzi’s progress,” the coach said. “I think he’s had two good practices. I think he looks way more mobile out there. He’s moving stronger, he’s more assertive. So we’re really encouraged … I would anticipate him being a game-time decision.”

Sullivan on Sunday’s matchup against the Hurricanes: “Well, they’re the Stanley Cup champions. They’re a deep team. They can really skate. They’re the ultimate test. Right now, Carolina is the standard of the league with how they performed and what they built there, and so it’ll be a big test for us.”

Sullivan had a bandage on his face, which he revealed was the result of a sun spot a dermatologist removed this week. He initially joked that he had been “sparring” with Matt Rempe after Thursday’s practice “and he got the best of me.”
