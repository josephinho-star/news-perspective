Adam Schein reveals his favorite Week 2 NFL parlay: Trust the Jets and angry Los Angeles teams

2026-09-20 14:00

Adam Schein is back for another bite at the apple.

The host of The Post’s “Schein Time” will put together an NFL parlay every Sunday using his favorite picks from the upcoming slate.

Last week’s parlay fell short, but shockingly, it wasn’t because of the Jets.

Gang Green easily covered the 1.5-point spread and Garrett Wilson surpassed the 50-yard receiving plateau in a dominant 23-10 road win vs. the Titans, but Justin Herbert and Omarion Hampton each fell short for the Chargers in their tough-look loss to the Cardinals.

This week’s four-leg parlay features three moneylines and one pick against the spread for a payout of +766 ($20 wins $173.38) at DraftKings Sportsbook.

The parlay will get started in the 1 p.m. ET window with the Jets hosting the Packers.

Just like last week, Adam is backing Gang Green on the moneyline (+164).

The Jets treated us right last week, but the Chargers fell flat in a shocking loss to Arizona.

Adam is hoping the Bolts rectify that with a win as a -305 moneyline favorite over the Raiders.

We then shift our focus to the biggest mismatch of Week 2, as Adam is laying the 12.5 points with the 49ers against the Dolphins in San Francisco.
