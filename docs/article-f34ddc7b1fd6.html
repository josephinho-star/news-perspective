Prime Big Deal Days FAQs, answered

When is Prime Big Deal Days?

Prime Big Deal Days — better known as October Prime Day — runs from Tuesday, Oct. 6 to Wednesday, Oct. 7, 2026.

Do you have to be a Prime member to shop?

Like its July counterpart, October Prime Day is a member-exclusive sale. You can get a 30-day free trial to enjoy Prime Day savings (plus all the benefits of the subscription) with no strings attached.

Prime Day vs. Prime Big Deal Days

Prime Day and Prime Big Deal Days are both Prime-exclusive seasonal sales featuring deep discounts on tech, beauty, clothing, essentials and more. October Prime Day occurs in the fall, kicking off the holiday shopping season, making it the perfect time to start buying gifts while inventory is up and prices are still down.

What deals are actually worth shopping?

From deal-hunting on Amazon sales for years, we know the sale is the perfect time to save on top-rated tech like Apple AirPods, restock your favorite beauty and skincare, peruse gift-worthy accessories like jewelry and handbags, and comb through all of Amazon’s best-sellers for steep discounts.
