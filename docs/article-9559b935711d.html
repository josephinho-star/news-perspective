Swimmer dies after being bitten by shark off popular Perth beach

2026-09-18 08:27

A man has died after being bitten by a shark off Sorrento Beach in Perth’s north.

Emergency services were called to the popular Sorrento Beach about 10.30am on Friday after reports of a shark-related incident.

Western Australia police said a man had been bitten by a shark while swimming along the coast. He died as a result of his injuries, police said.

Emergency services, including water and air personnel, conducted an extensive search of the area.

The Alkimos Surf Life Saving Club said Sorrento Beach will remain closed until 12pm on Saturday. Locals have been urged to stay out of the water until the beach is declared safe for swimming.

The City of Wanneroo has closed all other beaches along its coastline until 12pm on Saturday.

Local MP Liam Staltari extended his condolences to the man’s family, friends and loved ones.

“Like all locals and people from across WA, I’m deeply saddened by the tragic death of a man following today’s shark attack at Sorrento Beach,” he said.

“Our entire community is thinking of you at this incredibly difficult time.”

The fatality follows a shark attack on a surfer at a remote WA beach on Monday.

The man was seriously injured at Glenfield Beach, north of Geraldton.

He was taken to the nearby Geraldton Health Campus and is in a stable condition.
