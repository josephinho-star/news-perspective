Cam Skattebo is not a John Harbaugh guy: Rex Ryan

2026-11-23 22:21

Cam Skattebo is having a hard time selling Rex Ryan.

The Giants rusher celebrated his game-clinching first-down run during Sunday’s 23-18 win over the Jaguars with a backflip, days after saying he was done with the move. It didn’t sit well with Ryan — and he doesn’t think it endears him to Big Blue coach John Harbaugh, either.

“Truth be known, this is not a John Harbaugh-type guy,” Ryan told ESPN New York’s “DiPietro & Rothenberg” on Monday. “I’m telling you. Now, his toughness, that’s a John Harbaugh-type player. But all the other stuff that comes with this kid, the staring in the camera, the backflipping, all this kind of stuff. He says that energizes the team, I don’t think so. I think it just says, ‘Look at me, look at me!’ — how about you look at me while I fumble the ball?”

The ex-Jets coach also referenced Skattebo’s third-quarter fumble — his fifth of the season — that put Jacksonville on offense at the Giants’ 31-yard line.

Ryan invoked the legendary Barry Sanders, who fumbled 41 total times over 3,062 rushing attempts during his 10 NFL seasons — nearly half the rate of Skattebo’s six over 231 carries in 17 games.

“I mean, golly, for a big, powerful back, I like this kid, I love the way he plays: physical, downhill type guy, all those type of things, is impressive,” Ryan said. “If you’re going to run like a big powerful man, the No. 1 thing is, I should be able to give you the ball 30 times and never have to worry about you fumbling. This is an issue, this is an absolute issue.”

“They got me and it sucks to say,” Skattebo said following the win, which saw the Giants improve their NFC East-leading record to 6-3-1. “You fumble and nobody really says s–t to you other than, ‘Next play.’ I had a couple guys come up to me and say, ‘You’re good.’ … I think they know I’m gonna screw my head back on and be ready to go when I get the ball the next time.”

Harbaugh sent his top rusher a message after he accrued four fumbles in the first five games of the season, giving him zero touches in a 34-27 loss to the Saints in Week 6. The coach hasn’t hidden his want to upgrade the running back room, hoping to land Jeremiyah Love in the draft and then engaging in trade talks for Jonathan Taylor. Neither came to fruition, and for now, Skattebo remains atop the depth chart.

“The fact that they came back to me and trusted me at the end of the game to close it out shows you how much they trust me,” he said Sunday. “And I give love and props and support and I appreciate them for that.”
