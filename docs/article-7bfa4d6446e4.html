Runaway turkey leads NJ troopers on chaotic pre-Thanksgiving highway chase

2026-11-27 03:11

A runaway gobbler led Garden State troopers on a hilarious highway car chase on Wednesday — just one day before Thanksgiving.

The at-large turkey was spotted running afoul along the Garden State Parkway, zigzagging between lanes, trotting along to the song “Bad Boys” from the show “Cops,” according to footage shared by the New Jersey State Police.

“Yesterday, one turkey decided he wasn’t going down as the main course and led a trooper on a wild chase along the Garden State Parkway,” state police wrote on X.

“He’s running, he’s running… he’s running in the right lane right now,” a trooper trailing the turkey in a cop car with sirens says on the police radio.
