Legal battle over Hong Kong tycoon Lim Por-yen’s HK$2.9b estate set to rumble on

2026-08-30 04:00

Lim’s estate appeals ruling voiding his 2004 will, which found the late tycoon lacked testamentary capacity and allowed his third family to inherit

Lim’s estate lodged an appeal after losing the legal battle last year, while Pearl Ling Meng-chu, daughter of Lim’s third wife, told the South China Morning Post that she had not ruled out taking further action.

“She is reviewing all options available to her and the estate and is not ruling anything out,” Ling’s spokesman said.

Ling, also known as Pearl Lam, is the daughter of the late tycoon, founder of the conglomerate Lai Sun Group, and his third wife, Koo Siu-ying.

Lim’s estate asked the court to validate a will purportedly signed by the tycoon in December 2004. Ling and Koo were named as defendants in a probate lawsuit that began in 2011, after each lodged a caveat asserting an interest in the tycoon’s estate.

Lawyers for Lim’s estate had assessed the value to be about HK$2.9 billion.
