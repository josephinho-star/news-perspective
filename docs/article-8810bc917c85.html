Patrick Clancy reveals what Lindsay told him about killing their three children: ‘It was like a dream’

2026-09-21 11:58

Patrick gave his first interview to CBS’ “60 Minutes” since the murder trial of his former spouse, Lindsay Clancy, ended in an 11-1 mistrial, revealing new grim details about their conversations in the immediate aftermath of the January 2023 killings of 5-year-old Cora, 3-year-old Dawson and 8-month-old Callan.

“She said it was like a dream. And — she remembered it much in the way you would remember a dream after waking up,” he said.

Patrick Clancy, who has been the target of threats and what his lawyers called “a relentless, escalating and destructive defamation campaign” for months leading up to the shocking non-verdict, remarried in April.
