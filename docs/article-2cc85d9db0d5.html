Fall soup season is here, and this under $30 Dutch oven is the early Prime deal you need

2026-09-16 14:51

There’s nothing like the lure of a hot, steamy soup come fall.

From chicken noodle to pumpkin, there’s a recipe for every palate, and the right Dutch oven can help make it happen. The Newbulig 6 Quart Cast Iron Dutch Oven is on sale right now for under $30, at a massive 49% discount as part of early Prime Big Deal Days sales.

Built to provide even heat distribution, this pot comes with a snug-fitting lid, which the brand says traps moisture, making this a great fit for slow-cooked meals like a braised short rib with just the right amount of jus.

This Dutch oven is also built to perform six cooking functions, including searing, sautéing, braising, simmering, roasting and even baking bread. It’s oven-safe, too, up to 500 degrees Fahrenheit.

It’s also easy to clean: The brand says just hand-wash it with hot, soapy water and your pot will come out looking as good as new. The enamel coating is nontoxic, so you don’t have to worry about any PFOAs leaking into your cooked meals.

If you’ve been wanting to upgrade your cookware with something that is both aesthetic and durable, the Newbulig 6 Quart Cast Iron Dutch Oven is a great buy, especially at its current under-$30 price point.
