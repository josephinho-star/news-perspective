Alina Habba’s wedding to Turkish billionaire estimated to cost $10M — and guests included Mike Tyson

2026-09-27 20:11

MAGA star Alina Habba was all smiles with her new hubby, Turkish billionaire Turhan Mildon, during their extravagant, star-studded wedding at the ritzy Waldorf Astoria in New York City, pictures obtained by The Post show.

Habba, 42, tied the knot with Mildon, 29, CEO of Miller Holding, a Turkish industrial conglomerate, on Saturday in a top-dollar soiree that one source pegged at around $10 million.

The glittering reception took place in the fourth-floor ballroom of the hotel, which was adorned with some 900,000 fresh blooms from all over the world in a dazzling spectacle with numerous Trump-world figures and international guests in attendance.

Habba, who previously served as President Trump’s personal lawyer and briefly as the acting New Jersey US attorney, wore three different dresses during the lavish wedding, the source said.

Her two young kids, Chloe and Luke, were seen being escorted through the lobby just ahead of the extravagant ceremony. Details about the wedding were first reported by the Daily Mail and the Correspondent.

The newlyweds had tapped YSD Events, which worked with some of Habba’s friends in the past, to organize the opulent wedding, which included a 30-piece band to perform, The Post was told.

A singer from France was flown in specifically to perform their first dance song, Heir Encore. Their first course during dinner was individual tins of caviar for all of the guests.

Guests included heavyweight boxing champion Mike Tyson, as well as Trump administration officials FBI Director Kash Patel, US Attorney General Todd Blanche, White House aide Margo Martin, Assistant Attorney General for Civil Rights Harmeet Dhillon.

During an afterparty, the couple enjoyed a performance by three-time Grammy Award winner, rapper Nelly.

This is the MAGA darling’s third wedding.

Habba and Mildon met during an event at the Kennedy Center earlier this year. They were seen together at the White House Correspondents’ Dinner in April, which was upended when a gunman attempted to storm the premises.

Later, they were seen living it up at the Executive Branch, a private club in Washington, DC, during the Fourth of July.
