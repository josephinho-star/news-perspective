35 Christmas gift ideas girlfriends will love—recommended by a girlfriend

2026-09-09 17:41

I’ve been a girlfriend for over three years now, and the small, thoughtful gestures are still the ones that matter most.

Don’t get me wrong: luxury jewelry and experiences are always appreciated (I’m just a girl, after all!). But what makes me light up the most are presents with a personal touch, or those where it’s obvious a lot of effort was put into. Like when my boyfriend gifted me a small book of all the reasons why he loves me, or when he surprised me with a pair of slippers I’d been eyeing for months.

The holidays are coming up, and my man is already bugging me about giving him gift ideas. I can imagine a lot of fellow girlfriends are out there in a similar boat and considering what they’re interested in purchasing.

Below, we assembled the best, romantic and affordable Christmas gift ideas for girlfriends. Ladies, if you’re reading, don’t be afraid to send this to your man. Guys, if you’re already here…you’re welcome.

- Best Cozy Gift for Girlfriend: UGG Women’s Disquette Slippers

- Best for Coffee Lover Girlfriend: Nespresso Vertuo Pop+ Coffee Machine

- Best Skincare Gift for Girlfriend: Rhode Peptide Eye Prep Depuffing Eye Patches

- Best for Pilates-Obsessed Girlfriend: Pvolve Precision Mat

- Most Thoughtful Gift for Girlfriend: Camp Snap Camera 2

Best Sweater for Girlfriend: Quince Lightweight Cotton Cashmere Link-Stitch Dolman Sweater

It’s cozy season, and have you ever seen cashmere prices this low? I live for football Sundays with my boyfriend in the fall, and this buttery-soft sweater is the perfect companion. It comes in eight colors, including heather oatmeal, atlas navy and sugar stone.

Best Cozy Gift for Girlfriend: UGG Women’s Disquette Slippers

UGGs never go out of style. Keep your girlfriend’s feet warm this winter thanks to the brand’s signature slides in sheepskin and suede. Shop the popular chestnut hue or go bold with dark peony.

Best for Sleepy Girlfriend: Lemme Sleep Gummies

Your girlfriend deserves to wake up and fall asleep feeling like a queen. If she struggles to get shut-eye, she’ll love these Kourtney Kardashian-curated gummies. A combination of melatonin, chamomile, lavender and elderberry gently helps one drift off.

Best for Coffee Lover Girlfriend: Nespresso Vertuo Pop+ Coffee Machine with Milk Frother, Candy Pink

Imagine your girlfriend’s delight when she sees her daily dose of caffeine in a baby pink machine. The Vertuo Pop+ brews coffee in different sizes and strengths. It comes with the Aeroccino3 milk frother, which stirs up hot or cold milk froth in just seconds.

Best Skincare Gift for Girlfriend: Rhode Peptide Eye Prep Depuffing Eye Patches

Anything Hailey Bieber puts her stamp of approval on immediately has my attention. It’s no secret her beauty brand, Rhode, is a massive success. I’m obsessed with their hydrogel eye patches, which depuff and brighten under the eyes — ideal when applied first thing in the morning.

Best Gift for the Girl Who Has Everything: Diptyque Baies Candle

Some girls insist they don’t need anything for the holidays. An aromatic candle is just one of those gifts you can never turn down. This one features an irresistible blend of fruits that instantly brings cozy vibes into your home.

Best Self-Care Gift for Girlfriend: Boll & Branch Signature Hemmed Pillowcase Set

Sleep is the most sacred form of self-care. Your girlfriend will appreciate the opportunity to melt into these breathable sateen weave pillowcases during cold winter months.

Best Affordable Gift for Girlfriend: Nodpod Patented Gentle Pressure Sleep Mask

Want to show your girlfriend you care without breaking the bank? She’ll be raving over this weighted, fleece sleep mask that helps alleviate anxiety and boost overall calmness.

Best for Fashionista Girlfriend: Lululemon Everywhere Belt Bag 1L

This belt bag is one of those trends that seems to never die — and we don’t want it to. My small purse goes just about everywhere with me. It’s water-repellent, has pockets and comes in 20 fun colors, including cranberry charm/silver, seafoam/silver and pink pearl/rose gold.

Best Fitness Gift Idea for Girlfriend: Bala Bangles

Give your pilates princess her flowers — or in this case, ankle weights. They add resistance to a variety of workouts, including walking, yoga and core strength training. For an extra $25, you can even personalize them with your girlfriend’s name or initials.

Best for Pilates-Obsessed Girlfriend: Pvolve Precision Mat

Jennifer Aniston’s go-to sculpting workout is the perfect way to get your sweat in at home. The celebrity is a longtime partner and member of the brand, known for its low-impact exercises performed on a Twister-like mat.

Best Gift for Book Lover: BookCast Projector

Instead of flipping through pages close-up, project your holiday reading on your wall. This creative gift makes reading an immersive experience that’s perfect to share with a loved one.

Best Customizable Gift for Girlfriend: Pluto Pillow

If you’re girlfriend wants to be in the driver’s seat, this present is perfect. Surprise her with the opportunity to design her very own pillow. All she has to do is take a short quiz to fill out her sleep preferences, and a personalized plush will be delivered in the mail.

Best Makeup Gift for Girlfriend: Sephora Collection The Total Brush Set

I’m very picky about the type of makeup I apply to my sensitive skin, as well as the brands I use. For girls like me, a set of brand-new brushes is perfect to paint on the beloved products that are already present in my drawer.

Best for Glam Girlfriend: Laifen Swift Special High-Speed Hair Dryer

Don’t know what to get your girlfriend for the holidays? A hair dryer that achieves a salon-worthy blowout is practically fool-proof. This set includes a diffuser, concentrator and smooth nozzle for a quick blow-dry and glam strands.

Best for SPF-Obsessed Girlfriend: Freaks of Nature Freaks Full Coverage Bundle

Any skincare junkie knows the importance of SPF — even during colder, shady months. This set includes two sprays and a stick that shield from UV rays. Each product is 100% non-nano mineral and fragrance-free, and they don’t leave over a chalky white cast.

Best Spa-Inspired Gift for Girlfriend: Humanrace Recovery Kit

Give your girlfriend the benefits of a spa treatment without the hassle of leaving your home. This set includes a Smoothing Body Scrub and Recovery Body Lotion to give your skin some much-needed TLC. Fun fact: the brand was founded in 2020 by music superstar Pharrell Williams!

Best for Runner Girlfriend: Brooks Adrenaline GTS Running Shoes

Support your girl’s fall fitness goals by gifting her a brand-new pair of running sneakers. These have become my go-to pair for marathons because of their high-quality cushioning and shock absorption, ideal for longer distances.

Best Accessory for Girlfriend: Quay Hit Single Sunglasses

Designed by the Nader sisters, these trendy shades have star-quality. Shop the geometric metal frames in gold, smoke or digi pink to channel the reality stars’ signature style.

Best for New Girlfriend: Levain Cookie Tin Gift Set

Cookies are always a crowd pleaser. Your new girlfriend will be licking her lips after digging into the brand’s viral chocolate chip walnut, dark chocolate chocolate chip, oatmeal raisin and dark chocolate peanut butter chip cookies.

Most Thoughtful Gift for Girlfriend: Camp Snap Camera 2

The holidays are all about making memories with your loved ones. Capture special moments this season with a digital camera, which comes with several built-in filters: standard, vintage 1–3, analog and black and white.

Best for Health-Conscious Girlfriend: Ballerina Farm Farmer Protein Powder

Protein is an integral part of my diet, especially while marathon training. If your girlfriend is also in tune with her health and wellness route, she’ll love this delicious protein powder, made with 24g protein per serving and collagen peptides. The maple cinnamon flavor is my favorite, but strawberry crème and creamy chocolate get honorable mentions.

Best Gift for Salon-Worthy Hair: Nick Stenson Beauty Smoothing Créme

Getting my hair looking sleek and shiny is basically my life’s journey at this point. This product helps: it enhances my natural curl pattern when applied to dry strands, and it helps my hair look healthy and smooth when I blow dry.

Best for Glowy Skin: First Aid Beauty Facial Radiance Pads

Enhance your girl’s natural glow with these radiance pads, formulated with glycolic and lactic acids to gently exfoliate and awaken the complexion. The product can be used solo to smooth and hydrate, or to prep for makeup application.

Most Romantic Gift for Girlfriend: Magnificent Roses Preserved Red Roses

A bouquet of red roses gets me every time. Flowers that last an entire year? Even more special. This box, adorned with red satin bow, will remind your girlfriend just how much you love her.

Best Loungewear for Girlfriend: Cozy Earth Women’s First Layer Pajama Short Sleeve Tee & Pajama Pant Set

Cuddle up on the couch with your girl during colder temps. This loungewear set — made out of premium Supima cotton and bamboo viscose — is perfect for your next binge-watch session.

Best Candle for Girlfriend: Bath & Body Works Marshmallow Fireside Single Wick Candle

It’s starting to smell like Christmas! This holiday-inspired aroma has notes of toasted marshmallow, smoldering woods, fire-roasted vanilla and crystalized amber.

Best Gift Your Girlfriend Didn’t Know She Needed: Badesofa Back Path Pillow

Bathtime just got a lot more comfortable. Instead of laying on the hard ledge of your tub, give your head some buffer with these plush cushion. CleanDry+ Technology prevents waterlogging, and the fabric won’t get ruined by bath salts or oils.

Best Gift for Stylish Girlfriend: CHI Steam Handheld Garment Steamer for Clothes

Sometimes, your clothes need some extra love. Steam stubborn wrinkles with this handheld steamer, compact enough to stash in your suitcase during upcoming travels. It heats up in just 35 seconds and comes with a travel bag to take on the go.

Best Jewelry Gift Idea: Mill Rock East Petite Name Bar Necklace

No need to drop thousands of dollars on diamonds for the holidays. This nameplate necklace is a special, personalized way to show your girlfriend how much you care. Choose a gold, rose gold or silver bar. You can even get the jewelry gift wrapped for an extra $8.95.

Best Gift for Girlfriend Who Loves to Cook: Blue Apron Subscription

Holiday eats await. This subscription grants your girl easy access to delicious meal kits. Some recipes can be cooked in 20 minutes or less, perfect for when you’re hungry and get home late from the office.

Best Slippers for Girlfriend: Bombas Women’s Plush Sunday Slipper

You can’t go wrong with a cozy slipper. This plush pair has a squishy footbed and sherpa lining that makes it feel like you’re walking on clouds. They come in leopard, fawn taupe, lavender gray and alpine cream.

Best Clothing Gift for Girlfriend: Skims Cami Bodysuit

Kim Kardashian has shapewear down to a science. Shop from the reality star’s best-selling fashion brand, known for their figure-hugging pieces. This cami bodysuit features a universally-flattering wide scoop neckline and low back.

Best Gift for Dog Lover Girlfriend: All About Vibe Custom Dog Pillow

For the girls who love their boyfriend almost as much as they love their dog. Customize a mini, small or medium-sized pillow using an actual photo of your canine. You’ll do a double take every time you look at it on the couch!

Most Relaxing Gift for Girlfriend: Lush All the Best Gift Set

Your girl deserves all the best. Show your love and appreciation with a set of Lush’s best-selling, spa-grade bath products: Honey I Washed The Kids Soap, Rub Rub Rub Shower Scrub, The Olive Branch Shower Gel and Dream Cream Body Cream.
