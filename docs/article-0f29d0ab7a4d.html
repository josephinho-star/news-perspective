Dak Prescott's ex Sarah Jane Ramos blasts Craig Carton's cheating take: '$350 million doesn't give you a pass'

2026-10-07 17:44

Dak Prescott and ex-fiancée Sarah Jane Ramos' messy split has spiraled into an all-out media war of words with Ramos now taking aim at OutKick host Craig Carton over his defense of the Cowboys quarterback.

Things heated up this week when Ramos caught wind of a recent episode of "The Craig Carton Show" where Craig went off on the messy split, addressing Sarah Jane's claims of infidelity as well as her viral post shutting down pregnancy rumors by declaring she's simply been eating well.

"Like, sweetheart, I think it sucks that you fell in love and didn't see the writing on the wall. You're dating the starting quarterback of the Dallas Cowboys. It's his job to cheat on you," Carton observed. "You're not dating a firefighter. You're not dating a plumber. You're dating the most popular guy in the state of Texas."

Some may say that's harsh, but, you have to give Craig credit for saying what we're all thinking. Needless to say, that didn't sit well with Ms. Ramos who emailed the Carton show Wednesday.

"Craig please!" Sarah Jane, who had two kids with the Cowboys QB, wrote. "Your take was so mean and ridiculous. $350 million [Dak's contract value] doesn't give you a pass on cheating. You're either a weak man or you're not. Get the heck out of here with that nonsense."

"Ugly and weak people cheat because they don't know what to do with the attention," she continued. "Period. The end."

As for any chance of Sarah Jane coming on the show to discuss this matter further, she didn't give a full "no" answer. "You really want me on your show roasting you? I think not," Ramos concluded.

Actually, it would do massive YouTube numbers. Craig, we're told, is totally down with hopping on a Zoom to get roasted. Bring it, Ramos.
