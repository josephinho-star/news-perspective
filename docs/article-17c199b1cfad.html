Helicopter crashes in fireball near site of deadly LA bus crash

2026-09-16 02:24

A news helicopter crashed in Los Angeles Tuesday evening near the scene of a deadly Metro bus wreck, with at least one person killed in the crash, according to reports.

The Los Angeles Fire Department said the person killed was likely a pedestrian who was not in the helicopter.

“We are still searching the helicopter right now to determine how many people were on board,” LAFD spokesman Branden Silverman told reporters.

There did not appear to be any survivors, according to authorities.

It was not immediately clear who was involved but early reports indicate the helicopter belonged to KNBC.

“We have lost contact with our people,” Colleen Williams, longtime anchor at NBC4, said while crying on air as she covered the story.

The helicopter crashed between two commercial buildings on the 9100 block of Mason Avenue, setting several nearby cars on fire.

When firefighters arrived at the scene, they found both the helicopter and the cars burning close to where emergency crews were already helping people from an earlier bus accident.

Flames had since been extinguished at the helicopter crash scene, but firefighters remained in the area, ABC 7 reported.

The helicopter crash unfolded as first responders were dealing with a separate deadly collision nearby involving a Metro bus and another vehicle.

At least two people were killed in the bus crash, emergency personnel said.

Six others were injured and taken to hospitals in unknown condition.

The bus collision was reported shortly after 5 p.m. near Nordhoff Street and De Soto Avenue, according to the Los Angeles Police Department.
