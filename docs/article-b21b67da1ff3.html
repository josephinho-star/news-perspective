Mossad abducted Iranian general in İstanbul, then killed him: report

2026-11-17 15:25

Israel’s Mossad intelligence agency abducted former Iranian general Alireza Asgari in İstanbul in December 2006 and later killed him after weeks of interrogation, The New York Times (NYT) reported on Sunday, citing three former US officials.

Asgari had served in the Quds Force, the overseas operations arm of Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), and as Iran’s deputy defense minister. His wife, Ziba Ahmadi, told the NYT that he disappeared in December 2006 after checking into the Ceylan Hotel in İstanbul. She said Iranian investigators later told her that Israeli agents had entered his room, drugged him and abducted him. Investigators also found a vehicle believed to have been used in the operation abandoned near the Black Sea, she said.

Three former US officials who had worked closely with Israel told the newspaper that Mossad agents interrogated Asgari for weeks before killing him. One of the officials said he was questioned aboard a boat and that his body was dumped into the Black Sea.

Israel had targeted Asgari because he had been on Mossad’s radar for years, had close ties to Hezbollah leaders and had arrived in Lebanon shortly after Israeli air force officer Ron Arad was captured there in 1986, the officials told the newspaper. The interrogators found no evidence linking Asgari to Arad’s disappearance, they said.

Asked why Israel would want to question her husband, Ahmadi said to the NYT, “Have you ever heard of Ron Arad?”

Yitzhak Ilan, an investigator with Israel’s Shin Bet domestic security agency who died in 2020, had told the NYT that he was briefed on an intelligence mission related to Arad before Asgari disappeared. He did not name Asgari but recalled believing the operation would not solve the case.

Iran notified INTERPOL about Asgari’s disappearance on January 25, 2007, and informed Turkey on February 4, according to a Turkish government source cited by the London-based broadcaster Iran International in an April 2024 investigation. The outlet reported that Asgari had arrived in İstanbul on December 7, 2006, and disappeared two days later.

Asgari was alive in the United States under a new identity with protection from the CIA, Iran International reported in April 2024, citing three US intelligence officials and a senior European diplomatic source. That account conflicts with the NYT’s reporting that Israeli agents killed him after questioning him.

Israel has denied involvement in Asgari’s disappearance. During subsequent negotiations between Washington and Tehran over Iran’s nuclear program and prisoner exchanges, Iranian officials asked their US counterparts about the missing general. Washington denied involvement, but Israeli officials later informed American negotiators that Asgari was dead, a former US official who participated in the negotiations told the NYT.

The NYT reported that Mossad declined to comment on its intelligence about Arad or the operations to find him. An Israeli military spokeswoman said Arad was regarded by the military as alive and missing in action. The Israeli air force officer disappeared on October 16, 1986, after ejecting from his damaged F-4 Phantom fighter jet over southern Lebanon.
