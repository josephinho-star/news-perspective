Russian national accused of surveilling US tech company

2026-11-18 19:44

Robert McGreevy , David Spunt, Fox News

Published Nov. 18, 2026, 2:44 p.m. ET

See more of our coverage in your search results.

A grand jury indicted Russian national Igor D’iu on Wednesday on charges of conspiring to act as an agent of a foreign government, acting as an agent of a foreign government without notice to the Attorney General, interstate stalking, and fraud in connection with identification documents in connection to alleged espionage of a U.S. company in Silicon Valley.

“Igor D’iu was allegedly recruited, directed, and funded by the Russian government to stalk employees of a major U.S. technology company, using physical surveillance, electronic tracking devices, and fraudulent identities to gather intelligence,” Roman Rozhavsky, Assistant Director of the FBI’s Counterintelligence and Espionage Division, said in a statement.

D’iu was arrested in October after allegedly being recruited by co-conspirators through the messaging platform Telegram to clandestinely target employees of a Silicon Valley defense and technology company.

D’iu’s co-conspirators, who provided him with instructions, equipment, and financial support, told him his espionage was for “the Motherland,” explaining that the company he was spying on was supplying weapons used against Russia in Ukraine, according to an arrest affidavit.

“Russia may rely on covert operatives and criminal intermediaries as it attempts to gain an edge in its war against Ukraine, but those tactics do not place its government or its collaborators beyond the reach of the law. This arrest should serve as a warning to anyone looking to undermine U.S. interests or threaten our national security: No matter where you are or who you’re working for, justice will find you,” Rozhavsky added.

D’iu is accused of entering the U.S. illegally, traveling from Istanbul, Turkey, to Mexico City, Mexico, in June, at which point his co-conspirators connected him with an individual who was able to smuggle him across the border.
