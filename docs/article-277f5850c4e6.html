Raiders vs. Broncos Week 11 prediction, best bets: Jonah Coleman goes wild in AFC West clash

2026-11-22 17:25

Fernando Mendoza and the Raiders will head to the Mile High City to take on the division rival Broncos on Sunday.

The 2026 No. 1 pick’s first start had about as many ups as downs, but it’s clear that he’s going to be a step down from Kirk Cousins as he gets through his growing pains, setting the Broncos up for what could be an easy win as they look to keep up in the AFC West.

That presents a tasty opportunity for rookie running back Jonah Coleman, who has taken over the Denver backfield in recent weeks since coming from injured reserve.

The Washington product had a season-high 17 rushing attempts against the Panthers in Week 9, seemingly taking over the RB1 job from veteran J.K. Dobbins, who ran the ball just four times.

Coleman hasn’t had a rush of longer than 15 yards, but he’s shown some explosive play ability, reeling off a 51-yard catch against the Chiefs earlier this month.

He’ll face a Raiders run defense that has been perhaps the worst in the NFL this season.

Las Vegas has surrendered at least 150 rushing yards in four straight games, including 199 to the Rams and 190 to the 49ers.
