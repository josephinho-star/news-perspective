How Hong Kong is remapping the future of higher education

2026-09-25 01:30

A planned university town offers a chance to re-examine the very logic and model of Hong Kong’s higher education development

Hong Kong’s first five-year plan marks an unprecedented milestone not only for its economic and social development, but also for how it governs itself. The plan, which drew on 16,000 public submissions – roughly 80 per cent from ordinary citizens – is a testament to the government’s commitment to long-term development, which shows an unusual convergence of public consensus and political will.

Hong Kong’s reorientation away from a market-led approach indicates not only an upgrade of policy tools, but also a recalibration of governance philosophy.
