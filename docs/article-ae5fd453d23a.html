Lakers must keep fill-in starting guard in rhythm regardless of role

2026-11-21 00:41

The Lakers have unlocked a higher level of play from Quentin Grimes over the last couple of weeks.

The challenge for the team — and the player — is finding ways to ensure Grimes maintains his offensive rhythm even if his designation as a starter or a reserve changes.

Grimes’ career night in the Lakers’ home win over the Hornets on Tuesday, in which the sixth-year guard scored a career-best 47 points, underscored a theme that’s been present through his first month with his new team: He’s been more productive and effective in games when either Luka Dončić or Austin Reaves doesn’t play.

And more specifically, Grimes has been significantly more impactful for the Lakers as a starter compared to coming off the bench.

Grimes entered Friday’s home game against the Kings averaging 29.7 points and 6.3 rebounds in the team’s previous three games. He had at least 20 points in all three games, marking the first time since March 2025 he’s reached that scoring figure in three consecutive games.

And all three of those games came with Reaves sidelined because of a bruised left heel that also kept him out of Friday’s matchup against the Kings.

But Grimes stepping up when Reaves or Dončić isn’t available happened before this week.

He was instrumental in the Nov. 8 road win over the Pistons, which Dončić sat out, scoring 10 of his 12 points for the game in the first quarter before missing the fourth with the sprained finger injury that forced him to miss the Lakers’ next two games.

And before this past week, Grimes’ best game of the season came when Dončić didn’t play in the Oct. 28 road win over the Clippers, with the former Houston Cougar having 23 points, five assists, four steals and three rebounds in the victory.

In five games as a starter, Grimes entered Friday with averages of 24.8 points, 4.6 rebounds and 3.4 assists compared with averaging 5.9 points, 2.7 rebounds and 2.1 assists when used as a reserve.

“Clearly, what we’ve seen for the last few seasons with him is when needed, he can scale up,” coach JJ Redick said. “And as he’s done pretty much every game that he’s been in the starting lineup.”

The increased production as a starter isn’t surprising, despite how stark it has been for the 6-foot-5 guard.

An increase in minutes and usage should lead to an increase in production, too. Grimes entered Friday averaging 36.8 minutes and a 23.4% usage rate as a starter compared with 24.1 minutes and 11.1% usage rate when he came off the bench.

But the difference in efficiency suggests Grimes has been in a flow as a starter that he hasn’t been when he’s used as a reserve. He entered Friday shooting 51.9% from the field and 55.3% on 3-pointers, in games he’s started vs. 44.7% shooting (24% on 3s) in games he came off the bench.

“The biggest thing is just finding that rhythm, regardless,” Redick said. “We want him to, the same way I talk about with Collin [Sexton] and with [Sandro Mamukelashvili] is just we want him to feel that belief from his teammates and his coaches. But also just having that sense of freedom within our system to go be himself.”

When discussing ways Grimes could be more involved offensively when the team is healthier with Dončić and Reaves both available, Redick mentioned finding stretches in games where they don’t have the star guards on the floor and allow the role players, such as Grimes, to have more control of the offense.

“That’s probably what we’re focused on once we’re fully healthy, rather than long-term starting lineup,” Redick said. “Because again, with this group and the way this group [is], it’s just an egoless group. We’re going to be able to be flexible with the starting lineup and flexible with matchups.”

There’s also getting Grimes more touches even when he has to play off the ball more.

“This is a new team, and we’re feeling out a bunch of stuff for each individual guy,” Redick said. “Our job as coaches is to try to maximize each player and maximize the group. And there’s a learning curve to that. I talked about patience — hopefully these guys are being patient with me, just like I’m trying to be patient with them.”
