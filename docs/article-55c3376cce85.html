Denmark accuses Russia of plotting to assassinate US-Ukrainian rapper

2026-11-21 10:26

Denmark has accused Russian intelligence services of plotting to assassinate a Ukrainian-American near Copenhagen, saying they had "planned" and "initiated" the attempt.

Denmark's security and intelligence agency (PET) said it had stopped individuals who were intending to kill the person, named by Danish media as rapper Kyivstoner, at a concert in September. Two people were charged with attempted murder and assisting a foreign intelligence service, and pleaded not guilty.

Denmark's foreign minister said Russia's defence attaché will now be expelled.

Russia's embassy in Denmark called the allegations "unsubstantiated" and said Copenhagen failed to provide evidence of Moscow's involvement.

In a statement, the embassy claimed a "Danish model" had emerged, which it described as a "Russian trace" allegedly being discovered and "exploited for a propaganda spectacle". It accused Denmark of "provocations" intending to "frighten the Danish population in order to justify... a prolonged confrontation with Russia".

Russian state media cite the foreign ministry in Moscow as saying it will respond to the expulsion of its official "accordingly".

In a statement on Saturday, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said: "I strongly condemn the assassination attempt against a US-Ukrainian citizen in Denmark, orchestrated by Russian intelligence services.

"This was an attack on EU soil. And it is unacceptable," she said, adding that "Russia's hybrid threats are becoming less and less hybrid, and more and more real."

The rapper's real name is Albert Vasiliev.

PET said the individuals who allegedly tried to assassinate him were associated with a banned Danish gang named Loyal to Familia. The group is "known for extensive, serious and violent criminal activity", according to the agency.

PET, which worked with police on the case, said three individuals were stopped while travelling by car near the music venue where Kyivstoner was set to perform on 25 September.

Firearms and knives were found in the vehicle, PET said.

Since then, two people linked to the case have been charged with attempted murder and assisting a foreign intelligence service.

Both individuals pleaded not guilty to the charges, local media reported.

In July, Russia's FSB security service accused Vasiliev and the Ukrainian authorities of organising a "terrorist attack" on military facilities and personnel in Russia. The rapper denied this allegation on social media.

In a statement on Friday, Danish Foreign Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen said the alleged assassination attempt was Russian "intimidation" and an attempt to make Denmark "waver" in its support for Ukraine.

Russia's defence attaché must leave the country within seven days, Rasmussen said.

He also said Denmark wants the EU to restrict the ability of Russians to travel throughout Europe, saying even though Copenhagen was already very reluctant to issue visas, Russians could still get a Schengen visa in another country and travel to Denmark.

Copenhagen wants these visas to apply only in the country that has issued them, he added.
