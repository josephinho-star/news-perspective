Porsha Williams lifts lid on Erin Lichy and Dorinda Medley’s unseen ‘RHUGT’ fight

2026-10-17 12:00

Porsha Williams confirmed Erin Lichy was “acting up” at Dorinda Medley’s party featured in the “Real Housewives Ultimate Girls Trip: Roaring 20th” finale.

The “Real Housewives of Atlanta” star spoke exclusively to Page Six’s “Virtual Reali-Tea” about a heated argument between the “RHONY” women that was ultimately cut from the episode.

“They were acting up, and [Medley] was just running around the party checking everybody,” Williams, 45, told us while promoting her partnership with Charmin. “I can’t say that that isn’t true. I think that is probably very well true.”

“I didn’t see it, but Dorinda was gathering whoever was out of pocket at her party.”

The “RHUGT” season finale was hosted at Medley’s Bluestone Manor in the Berkshires, where a slew of “Real Housewives” icons from each franchise — and Andy Cohen — appeared for a Gilded Age-themed party.

Medley, known to make things nice in her New England estate, was activated when the “Real Housewives of Rhode Island” newcomers in attendance began to squabble mid-party and tried to put a stop to the fighting.
