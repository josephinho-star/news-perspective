Watch live: Cao faces Senate confirmation hearing to lead Navy

2026-11-17 13:43

Acting Navy Secretary Hung Cao will appear before the Senate Armed Services Committee on Tuesday as he seeks confirmation to permanently lead the branch.

Cao, a retired Navy captain, took over in April following the ouster of former Navy Secretary John Phelan, who reportedly clashed with Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth. He is expected to face questions about the war with Iran, military readiness and the USS Abraham Lincoln controversy.

His nomination also requires a congressional waiver because he has not been out of active-duty service for the seven years required by federal law.

The hearing is scheduled to begin at 9:30 a.m. EST.
