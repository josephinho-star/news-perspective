Even my dad complimented my skin after I used this skin care duo

2026-11-24 19:30

I thought my skin had reached its glow-up potential, but this Medicube duo proved there was still a little more radiance waiting to shine through.

I’ve been a Medicube user for years, so I’m no stranger to the brand’s skincare and beauty gadgets. I’ve tried my fair share of its viral products, but it wasn’t until this week that I paired the Medicube Booster Pro X2 with the PDRN Pink Collagen Glow Jelly Serum — and, let me tell you, it was the best thing I’ve done for my skin in a while.

The dynamic duo is a match made in glow heaven. First, there’s the Booster Pro X2, a cute pink beauty tool that takes my skincare routine up a notch. Designed to help skincare products absorb into the skin, it gives me an excuse to slow down and turn my usual serum application into a little at-home beauty treatment.

And let’s be real: who doesn’t love a gadget that looks as good on the bathroom counter as it feels in your skincare routine?

Then comes the PDRN Pink Collagen Glow Jelly Serum. This adorable pink formula has a bouncy, jelly-like texture that feels refreshing on my skin and gives it that coveted hydrated, dewy look. It’s packed with PDRN and collagen-focused skincare ingredients and is lightweight.

Together, the serum and Booster Pro X2 are my new favorite pink power couple.

But the real test? Other people’s reactions.

I went over to my parents’ house for dinner this week, and my dad — yes, my dad — literally looked at me and asked, “Did you do anything different to your skin?”

I was like, “Yes, Dad, I’m using this cute pink tool with this pink glowy serum.”

And his response? “I can tell, your skin looks so glowy.”

Obviously, a compliment from your dad is the best compliment of all. He’s not exactly someone who goes around handing out unsolicited skincare reviews, so I knew this little duo was doing something right.

Now, here’s another reason why I’m obsessed with these two products right now: they’re both on sale during Amazon’s Black Friday sale, which officially kicked off on Nov. 19. The retailer is one of the several with early Black Friday deals.

After years of using Medicube, I can confidently say this is one pairing I’ll be keeping in my rotation. Pink, pretty, and apparently dad-approved? That’s what I call a glow-up worth talking about.
