Mötley Crüe frontman Vince Neil’s girlfriend arrested for domestic assault against rocker

2026-11-06 01:05

Mötley Crüe frontman Vince Neil’s girlfriend has been arrested for allegedly attacking the musician in Tennessee.

Rain Hannah Andreani was booked for alleged domestic assault and placed behind bars by the Williamson County Sheriff’s Office in Franklin — roughly 21 miles south of Nashville — on Sunday, per police records obtained by Page Six.

The makeup artist was released Monday after posting a $5,000 bond.

Andreani is expected to appear in court on Dec. 3.

An insider told TMZ that Andreani was arrested for allegedly hitting Neil, 65, following a heated argument.

While it’s unclear what led to the dispute, the source alleged that Andreani started wrecking the rocker’s home.

Andreani’s arrest comes after she and the “Can’t Change Me” crooner called it quits in May 2025 following 15 years of dating, the outlet previously reported.

An insider alleged that the couple separated because Neil suspected Andreani was cheating.

Shortly after the split, Neil was photographed holding hands with two women while walking around Nashville, per TMZ. However, sources told the outlet that Neil and Andreani were back on the following month.

Andreani was also severely injured after Neil’s private jet crashed into a parked plane at Arizona’s Scottsdale Airport in February 2025.

At the time, Andreani was on board with her friend Ashley and their dogs. The pilot tragically died, and Andreani was transported to the hospital with several broken bones.
