European Commission hit by cyberattack: Report

2026-11-19 09:24

The European Commission has been hit by a major cyberattack that might have compromised large amounts of personal data, a Politico report indicated on Thursday.

The commission believes it has contained most of the attack, but the full extent of any damage remains unclear, Politico said, citing an EU official.

The European Commission did not immediately reply to a request for comment on Thursday morning.

Politico said several systems, including HR systems for pay statements and time management and a system for medical reimbursements, were taken offline during the disruption, which lasted around 10 days.

"The European Commission has taken the necessary steps in response to a detected cyber campaign," commission spokesperson Thomas Regnier told the news outlet.

"Individual systems were targeted and, in line with our standard practice, we have informed those concerned."

The Commission convened its highest-level internal crisis management body for an urgent meeting about the cyber incidents, Politico said.

Tech Commissioner Henna Virkkunen briefed other commissioners on Wednesday, it added.
