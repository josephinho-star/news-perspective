What Elizabeth Holmes, Lindsay Clancy, Christa Pike, Ghislaine Maxwell are eating for Thanksgiving behind bars

2026-11-27 01:29

Famous inmates Lindsay Clancy, Elizabeth Holmes, Christa Pike and Ghislaine Maxwell are all being offered festive options to dine on this Thanksgiving while in custody at facilities around the country, according to reports.

Clancy, the 36-year-old mom accused of strangling her three kids, is slated to be offered turkey, mashed potatoes, stuffing, vegetables, and a dinner roll at Tewksbury Hospital in Massachusetts where she is being held as she awaits a retrial, the Sun reported.

Clancy, who is now paraplegic and uses a wheelchair, has been at the medical facility since her arrest for killing Cora, 5, Dawson, 3, and Callan, 8 months, on Jan. 24, 2023 — after which she paralyzed herself in a suicide attempt.

She is slated to be tried a second time on March 29, after her first trial this summer ended with a hung jury that was 11-1 in favor of finding her not guilty by reason of insanity.

In Tennessee, Pike, 50, is back on death row at the Debra K. Johnson Rehabilitation Center in Nashville after she stunningly survived a botched execution Sept. 30.

Pike, who was convicted of torturing and murdering 19-year-old classmate Colleen Slemmer in 1995, would have the option to eat turkey breast and gravy, bread dressing, sweet potatoes, cranberry sauce and seasoned green beans for the main course, the outlet reported.

For dessert, the rehab center is offering a wheat roll with margarine, tea and a slice of pumpkin spice cake.

It’s unclear if Pike would be able to eat the entire meal though, given a slew of health issues her lawyers have said she now suffers from after living through the failed lethal injection.

They claimed she had problems swallowing, pneumonia, a blood clot and swollen arms and hands.

Pike isn’t likely to enjoy any meals this holiday with other inmates though since she is in isolation — a condition which her lawyers will be pushing to change at a hearing on Dec. 1.

In Texas, Theranos fraudster Elizabeth Holmes and convicted Jeffrey Epstein madame Ghislaine Maxwell are both cooling their heels inside the Federal Prison Camp Bryan after their unrelated convictions.

The federal lockup, infamously dubbed “Club Fed” for its cushy conditions, has a holiday menu, including turkey, green bean casserole, cornbread stuffing, cranberry sauce and dinner rolls, People reported. The prison camp is also offering chocolate pecan pie for dessert.

Holmes, 42, is currently in the fourth year of her 11-year sentence after she was convicted in 2022 of lying to investors when she claimed her company’s blood-testing medical device could detect multiple diseases.

She is slated to move to a halfway house to finish out her sentence in August 2027.

Holmes has been back in the headlines over the past month after the release of Nathan Fielder’s documentary “You Can See Everything,” featuring extensive and unsettling interviews with Holmes and her partner, Billy Evans.

Maxwell — a 64-year-old fallen British socialite — was convicted in 2021 of grooming girls and women for Epstein to sexually abuse. She is serving a 20-year sentence. She lost a bid to overturn her conviction and be freed in August.
