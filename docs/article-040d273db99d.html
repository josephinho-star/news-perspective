‘Wheel of Fortune’ announcer Jim Thornton fired after in-flight pedophilia chatroom incident

2026-09-04 21:05

Suspended “Wheel of Fortune” announcer Jim Thornton has been fired following an in-flight pedophilia chatroom incident.

“Following a thorough investigation, Jim Thornton’s employment with Wheel of Fortune has been terminated, effective immediately,” a Sony Pictures Television Studios spokesperson confirmed in a statement to TMZ.

“We have recently been made aware of allegations concerning Jim Thornton,” a rep for Sony Pictures Television Studios told Page Six at the time.

“He has been suspended from ‘Wheel of Fortune,’ and we are recasting while we conduct a thorough investigation.”

While Sony did not elaborate on the nature of the allegations, it was later reported that the suspension stemmed from an incident that arose while Thornton was traveling aboard an American Airlines flight from Charlotte, North Carolina, to Los Angeles on May 14.

“On May 14, American Airlines Flight 1276 was met by law enforcement upon arrival at Los Angeles International Airport following concerns reported by a customer onboard,” a rep for the airline told Page Six at the time.

Later that night, Thornton’s attorney, Allison Hart, said in a statement, that her client “did not engage in any illegal conduct, was not arrested and has not been charged with any crime.”

She explained, “When questioned about the complaint made by a fellow passenger on the flight, law enforcement spoke to [Thornton] for five minutes or less, immediately determined that no wrongdoing had occurred and told my client he was free to go.”

Per the outlet, producers began re-editing those episodes with a new announcer, an effort one source called a “major undertaking.”

On Sept. 3, Hart elaborated on the details of the plane incident, exclusively telling Page Six that her client “has become active in suicide-prevention advocacy since losing a close family member to suicide two years ago, and he has spent time visiting chat rooms offering support to individuals suffering from suicidal ideation.”

According to Hart, Thornton has visited 988lifeline.org and its chat forum, chat.988lifeline.org, in the past, noting that they “offer real-time support for individuals struggling with mental health issues.”

Hart claimed Thornton had been “misdirected to a forum with a similar url, the forum lifeline.chat,” by an “acquaintance in the suicide prevention space.”

Thornton allegedly “understood” that the forum offered suicide prevention support similar to 988lifeline.org and chat.988lifeline.org and “did not know that the website lifeline.chat claims to offer mental health support to individuals with pedophilic thoughts.”

When Thornton realized what the website was about, he allegedly “left the page and has not visited since.”

Hart insisted that the website lifeline.chat itself “is not unlawful, there is no unlawful child pornography or other pedophilic content,” adding that her client “has never viewed, downloaded or consumed any child pornography whether on that commercial flight in May or at any other time.”

Hart made sure to stress that Thornton “was never arrested or charged with a crime,” claiming law enforcement “immediately confirmed that no crime had occurred” and told her client he was “free to go.”

The images showed Thornton — who was wearing a “Wheel of Fortune” jacket — looking at, reading and appearing to engage in a disturbing conversations.

In two of the pictures, messages — presumedly from Thornton — were written in the chat bar. These messages were later sent to the chatroom by username NicholasB77, per additional photos.

According to displayed timestamps, the chatroom conversation spanned for close to an hour.

The passenger who notified a flight attendant about Thornton told the outlet she allegedly saw images and texts suggesting he was in a chatroom for pedophiles, prompting her to share the photos with the crew.

Law enforcement officers were notified about the incident and met Thornton at the gate upon his arrival. Officers questioned the TV personality, who was then released.

To date, Thornton has not been arrested or charged in connection with the incident.
