Fed meeting live updates: No hike expected, but all eyes on Warsh for what's next

2026-10-28 11:43

The Federal Reserve's interest rate decision Wednesday likely will be short on suspense, with markets expecting the central bank to stay on hold even with inflation tracking well above target.

Markets are pricing in less than a 10% chance that the rate-setting Federal Open Market Committee follows up its quarter-percentage-point hike in September with another increase, according to the CME Group's FedWatch tool.

Assuming that sentiment is correct, it will leave the Fed's benchmark interest rate in a range between 3.75% and 4% — and shift investor attention to Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh and whatever clues he chooses to provide in his post-meeting news conference.

Traders expect the Fed to follow up the October skip with a hike at its next meeting in December.

With no updates to the Fed's "dot plot" of rate forecasts or the Summary of Economic Projections on tap for this meeting, there will be less detail about where officials see current conditions and the future path of monetary policy.

Some observers do think there could be dissents from the decision to hold. Regional Presidents Beth Hammack of Cleveland and Lorie Logan of Dallas are potential no votes, as is Neel Kashkari of Minneapolis. Each chose to vote against a hold in July.

The Fed decision comes less than a week before a pivotal midterm election in which the balance of power in Congress is up for grabs. While Fed officials insist that political considerations play no part in their decisions, some economists have speculated that officials would prefer to steer away from increasing rates so close to an election.
