West Coast NFL fans could score stadium-adjacent homes for just $339K — skipping traffic forever

2026-10-12 22:02

Home field advantage just got real. That is, primo real estate for all you football fanatics.

Football season is in full swing, and these West Coast homes currently listed for sale let you tailgate in your own driveway, sizzle a sausage with your friends, then stroll to your seat — without ever risking a DUI.

For die-hard NFL fans and season ticket holders who have too much common sense (and a little cash to splash) to battle that game-day traffic, overpriced parking and the eternal risk of a traffic ticket, a handful of homes put the biggest stadiums in the West practically in their backyard.

So, if you’d rather strut to kickoff than sit in a highway parking lot or gamble on an Uber surge, these listings are for you.

Seattle — See the Seahawks at Lumen Field

The least expensive entry point to living a life in football heaven is in Seattle and sits right across from Lumen Field and is a seven-minute stroll to T-Mobile Park.

A 529-square-foot junior one-bedroom condo at the aptly named Gridiron (590 1st Ave. S, Unit 702) is asking just $339,000, which is a steal in Seattle, a city of tech bros where the average home price reportedly falls around $833,192.

Gridiron’s listing boasts floor-to-ceiling windows, in-unit laundry, rooftop deck with stadium and Elliott Bay views, and you’re literally steps from Seahawks and Mariners games — perfect for walking over after a parking-lot tailgate (or just taking the elevator downstairs). No rental cap, either.

Live in Inglewood to watch the Rams and Chargers at SoFi Stadium

Inglewood is fast becoming a cultural and entertainment hub in Greater Los Angeles. A bright two-bedroom, two-bath condo at 3859 Thorncroft Lane Unit E is listed at a steal — at least, by LA standards — for just $575,000.

Not only does the gated Carlton Square community give residents access to a pool, spa and tennis courts, it’s situated just moments from the biggest and most recognizable stadiums in California: SoFi Stadium, Intuit Dome and the Kia Forum. Jocks and wannabe WAGs rejoice!

Las Vegas Raiders — Allegiant Stadium

Sin City fans can snag an 11th-floor two-bedroom condo at Panorama Towers. 4575 Dean Martin Drive Unit 1103 could be yours for $475,000. True to its name, the 1,054-square-foot space delivers floor-to-ceiling panorama views of the Vegas Strip, Allegiant Stadium, Aria and Vdara, plus a private terrace perfect for pounding the pre-game brews.

Lucky residents also get access to 24-hour concierge, valet, resort-style pools, a fitness center and secure gated access — all just minutes from kickoff.

Glendale, Ariz. — State Farm Stadium for the Cardinals

In Glendale, Ariz., a fully upgraded three-bedroom, two-bath house at 8307 W McLellan Road is pending at $454,900. It’s listed as for sale by owner, and with no pesky HOA, new everything and minutes from State Farm Stadium and the Westgate entertainment district, Arizona has never been cooler.

Santa Clara — Levi’s Stadium for the 49ers

At the other end of the price spectrum and higher up the real estate food chain, a comfy three-bedroom, single-family home at 2199 Avenida De Las Flores is listed at $1.599million. Close enough to Levi’s Stadium that game days become a neighborhood stroll instead of a fraught freeway battle.

Denver Broncos — Empire Field at Mile High

Lastly, a tastefully restored 1886-built Victorian at 2021 Eliot Street is asking for a hair over a mill, at $1.1 million.

Tucked on a quiet cul-de-sac in Jefferson Park, it’s just blocks from the stadium and packed with character: original architectural details, a cozy fireplace, renovated kitchen with oversized island, two primary suites (one with a private deck and skyline views), finished basement and front porch.

Plus, if you love to splash and float, there are multiple options for bathing — think private spa bath, clawfoot tub and sexy outdoor hot tub.

Fully paid-off solar panels and plans for a future garage with ADU add extra upside. Pre-game hangs here would feel special — and the walk to kickoff is short enough to skip the parking chaos entirely.
