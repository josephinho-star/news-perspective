Emily Ratajkowski and boyfriend Romain Gavras share passionate kiss on vacation in Rome

2026-09-04 20:57

Emily Ratajkowski and her boyfriend showed major PDA while on vacation in Italy.

The 35-year-old model and her boyfriend, Romain Gavras, 45, were photographed sharing a passionate kiss while walking through the streets of Rome.

For the outing, Ratajkowski wore a black minidress with a plunging halter neckline, which she paired with black sunglasses, a black purse and white sneakers.

Gavras kept it casual in a pair of white baggy jeans and a green button-up shirt, which he paired with sunglasses and a white baseball cap.

Other photos from their Italian getaway showed the two keeping close to each other while walking around with Gavras keeping his arm around Ratajkowski's waist.

The French filmmaker previously dated pop singers Dua Lipa and Rita Ora in 2023 and 2020 respectively.

Both Lipa and Ora have also since moved on. The "Don't Start Now" singer is now married to actor Callum Turner, while Ora married director and actor, Taika Waititi, in 2022.

Ratajkowski and Gavras first sparked romance rumors in November 2025, when they were spotted out together in New York City, and were photographed with his arm around her and kissing multiple times.

They later went Instagram official in February 2026, when she included photos of him in an Instagram slideshow, including one of him giving her a close embrace while out at a restaurant. They were later photographed together in Paris during Fashion Week.

"❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️ YES to finding love again," one fan wrote in the comments section of the post. Another added, "We love love❤️❤️❤️❤️."

Prior to her relationship with Gavras, Ratajkowski was previously married to Sebastian Bear-McClard. During their marriage, the two welcomed their now 5-year-old son, Sylvester "Sly."

"I had rules: Never miss bedtime and basically no sleepovers. These were natural and easy to abide by," she wrote in The Cut about dating as a single mom. "There was nowhere I’d rather be than when I woke up with my toddler, so no matter the number of martinis, I’d be fully dressed and out the door to relieve the sitter and be ready for my son’s 6 a.m. 'Maamaaaa!'"

Gavras also recently joined Ratajkowski and her son on a trip to Mallorca, Spain. The model shared a series of photos from the trip, which included pictures of her son playing on the beach, as well as one of her and Gavras kissing.
