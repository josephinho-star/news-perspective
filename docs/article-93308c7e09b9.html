Xi Jinping marks second Mid-Autumn Festival in US during state visit

2026-09-25 11:20

On the holiday symbolic of unity, China’s leader is expected to attend a private presidential tea and tour America’s founding documents

Chinese President Xi Jinping has not only made history as the first Chinese leader to undertake two formal state visits to the United States, he is also the only Chinese leader to be spending two Mid-Autumn Festivals on American soil.

Xi offered festive greetings during his toast at Thursday’s state banquet.
