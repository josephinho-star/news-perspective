Sony’s 1000XM4C headphones are a Black Friday bargain at $198

2026-11-28 00:30

Instead of purely looking forward with its headphone designs, Sony went back to its popular WH-1000XM4 design and made some improvements to make it even better. The 2026 version, called the WH-1000XM4C, sounds nearly as good as the pricier XM6 model (currently $348), but is down to $198 during Black Friday at Amazon, Best Buy, and Walmart. That’s $100 off their October launch price.

All three colors (including the stunning lavender) are down to their lowest price yet just a few months after launch.

All three colors (including the stunning lavender) are down to their lowest price yet just a few months after launch.

There’s a reason why Sony went back to refine the XM4. Our former reviewer Chris Welch praised them for being super comfortable and for having close to perfect features for the price, especially compared to its successor, the XM3 (still my daily driver, for what it’s worth). The XM4s had better sound quality and noise cancellation, plus improved mics, multipoint connectivity, and a sensor that can detect when you remove them to automatically pause audio. Now, with the XM4C, there are further improvements, most notably a 10-band equalizer in the app (compared to the original XM4’s five-band) that my colleague John Higgins says really helped the headphones’ midrange frequencies open up.

These are a great buy, but they aren’t your only Sony headphones deal to consider right now. Sony’s WH-1000XM5 are available during Black Friday at Best Buy for $169, their lowest price yet from a major retailer. There are upsides and downsides to this model: They feature improved noise cancellation thanks to more mics, despite using the same ANC chip as the XM4C, but they can’t fold up as compactly, making them a little bigger to pack in your bag. Either one you choose, you’ll have some great headphones to listen to going into 2027.
