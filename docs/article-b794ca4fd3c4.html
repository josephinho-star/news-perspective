‘The End of Oak Street’ hits streaming—Where to watch the Anne Hathaway movie

2026-09-15 09:00

Oak Street is going back in time.

“The End of Oak Street,” which hit theaters on Aug. 14, comes to streaming today, Sept. 15.

The new sci-fi thriller from “It Follows” director David Robert Mitchell follows the Platt family and their fight to survive when their quiet suburban neighborhood is mysteriously transported from 1982 to a prehistoric time when dinosaurs roamed the earth.

‘The End of Oak Street’ streaming release date

Where to watch ‘The End of Oak Street’

If you choose the rental option, you’ll have 30 days to begin watching the movie and 48 hours of access once you’ve pressed play.

‘The End of Oak Street’ DVD, Blu-ray and 4K release date

“The End of Oak Street” will be released on Blu-ray, 4K and regular DVD on Nov. 3, but you can preorder today.

‘The End of Oak Street’ cast guide

- Anne Hathaway as Denise Platt, an aspiring novelist

- Ewan McGregor as Greg Platt, Denise’s husband

- Maisy Stella as Audrey Platt, Denise and Greg’s daughter

- Christian Convery as Brian Platt, Denise and Greg’s son

- Jordan Alexa Davis as Jeannette Christiansen, a neighborhood girl

- P. J. Byrne as Mel Jacobs, the Platts’ irate neighbor

- Emily Kuroda as Mrs. Valcourt, a librarian and one of the Platts’ neighbors

- Anne Gee Byrd as Mrs. Huddleston, a kind, elderly neighbor to the Platts

- Hudson Meek as Kaden Tucker, a neighborhood kid who hates Brian

- David Alexander Kaplan as Charles Tucker, Kaden’s brother
