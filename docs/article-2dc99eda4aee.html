Carmella Garcia Reveals Which ‘Selling Sunset’ Star Should Join ‘Real Housewives Of Orange County’ Season 21: “I Just Love Her Vibe”

2026-09-25 00:00

It’s hard being the new girl on The Real Housewives of Orange County. Just ask Carmella Garcia! A former Playboy Playmate of the Year and WWE Diva, Garcia knew her past and unconventional lifestyle—as well as her relationship with now-ex-boyfriend, Danny Hampel—would be a topic of conversation among the women. So, she was not surprised to learn that Heather Dubrow had called her “trashy” behind her back. “I was very aware coming into this that they would give me a little bit of a hard time,” said Garcia. However, Dubrow was quick to apologize for her insult, and Garcia was quick to forgive her. “I extended grace to Heather because I do think Heather is a nice person,” explained Garcia. “I don’t think that she intended to hurt me.”

Midway through Season 20, the women have clearly warmed up to Garcia. All the women except series OG Vicki Gunvalson. Gunvalson has outwardly questioned Garcia’s generous gifts to their friend group, scrutinized Garcia’s parenting style, and even speculated that Garcia doesn’t truly live with her family in Rancho Santa Fe. “It’s tough when I hear Vicki on different podcasts and on the show say I’m not authentic,” said Garcia. “I’m like, well, that’s a bit of projection.” Garcia also teased a “standoff” between her and Gunvalson at the upcoming series reunion. “There hasn’t been a whole lot of resolution there,” said Garcia. “I don’t know if we’ll get there, but I’m hopeful.”

Could some of Gunvalson’s feelings about Garcia really be about jealousy? Over the years, Garcia has transformed herself into one of the top earners of the Oppenheim Group —the luxury real estate brokerage featured on Selling Sunset and Selling The OC. While the women have questions about Garcia’s real estate developments, it’s clear to audiences that Garcia is thriving in her field. Luckily, she had the support of her boss, Jason Oppenheim, as she embarked on her RHOC journey. “He really eased my mind about things,” said Garcia. “I’m really grateful to him.”

Garcia stopped by the Decider studio to discuss the series, dish on the group dynamics, and tease the “chaos” resulting from the ladies’ upcoming cast trip to Tokyo.

DECIDER: Carmella, surely you felt pressure as a rookie coming into the 20th season of The Real Housewives of Orange County. Did you take a beat before you said “yes” to this opportunity? Especially knowing how tough the new girls usually have it?

CARMELLA GARCIA: I didn’t really know the history of the franchise, so I didn’t really know what I signed up for. It’s been an experience, for sure [laughs]. It wasn’t easy, but I am super grateful. Some of these women have given me a run for my money, but it’s all good.

It’s clear to viewers—though maybe it took Vicki Gunvalson a minute to agree—that you are a fantastic mother. What did your kids think about the show, and were they excited to film themselves a bit?

My girls were really excited. They love being on TV, and they think it’s super cool. My oldest watches the show. The boys could take it or leave it [laughs]. They’re busy teenagers, but they’re happy for me. One of my sons likes to check the ratings. He’ll tell me each week if the numbers go down. It’s like, “Thanks, honey.”

It’s been tough to watch you struggle with Danny’s previous betrayal because you are clearly are still very hurt by it. Has discussing this on the show allowed you to work through some of those feelings?

There were several times that it felt cathartic for me to let feelings out that I have held inside for so many years. However, it was not just one incident of infidelity that happened years ago. There were little betrayals that continued along the way. There was a lot of dishonesty on his part, to be honest. Ultimately, that behavior is what ended the relationship.

I’m sorry to hear that. How are you doing today?

I’m good. I mean, I feel sad. I still feel sad, but it was necessary for my own peace.

You are obviously a kind person, the epitome of nice girls who finished last this season. You gifted Emily a nice bag, you offered to mentor Jen, and the girls jumped down your throat. Why do you think they were so suspicious of your friendly gestures?

It was a bit of a bumpy ride. They were probably suspicious because I came in very hard and fast [laughs]. It was too much too soon, but that’s my personality. I didn’t feel like I should change that. I wanted to come in and be the truest version of myself. It’s not always pretty, but this is me. I’m here, and I don’t know that it matters how I got here.

You are 100% yourself, which is a good thing!

Thank you. It’s tough when I hear Vicki on different podcasts and on the show say I’m not authentic. I’m like, well, that’s a bit of projection. However, I do say too much at times. There are things that I could hold back. Let’s just say that [laughs].

You forgave Heather pretty quickly after she confessed that she called you “trashy” behind your back. Why did you choose to extend her grace?

I extended grace to Heather because I do think Heather is a nice person. I don’t think that she intended to hurt me, but I also was very aware, coming into this, that they would give me a little bit of a hard time. I have a past with Playboy and the WWE, so they could have their pick of what to harp on [laughs]. It’s not like I was naive about what they might say. I just knew that once they got to know me for me, the opinions would change, but it took a while.

You and Heather moved on, but it took the rest of the group a minute. Did you think they were standing up for you, or do you think it was just another item to add to the Heather Dubrow grievance pile?

I don’t know. That’s a hard question because I’d love to say they were sticking up for me, but it was pretty unpleasant early on. I think they were just irritated with things that they were hearing from Heather off camera and then things that she was saying on camera. There were things that just didn’t align.

As a Selling the OC fan, I was so excited to see you and Heather Dubrow in the Oppenheim Group office. What did your O Group colleagues think of you joining The Real Housewives of Orange County?

The Oppenheim Group has been amazing. They’re so supportive. I had a great conversation with Jason [Oppenheim] because I was really nervous. I hadn’t been in the reality TV space in a very long time. He really eased my mind about things. He was like: “Just have fun with this. Don’t let anxiety take over.” I’m really grateful to him. I’ve got a great boss. He’s built an amazing brand.

Which of your Oppenheim Group colleagues might make a good addition to Season 21 of The Real Housewives of Orange County?

Bre [Tiesi]. She’s my pick. I just love her vibe.

What about Alex Hall from Selling The OC?

I like her too.

Me too! I think it made a lot of sense for Heather to ask you to help her daughter find a rental in San Diego. Unfortunately, Gina was really upset by her decision. Could you see Gina’s perspective, or were you as baffled as Heather by her take on the situation?

I could honestly see both perspectives. I understand why Gina’s feelings were hurt, but I also understood Heather’s logic of coming to me instead of Gina. It was hard to be the new person in the middle of that conversation, especially over dinner. They were just going back and forth.

One thing I don’t like about the group dynamic this season is that people tend to talk about you and for you while you are sitting right there. Are we going to see you find your voice more in these upcoming episodes?

I do get a little more vocal as the season goes on. In the beginning, it was hard. I was so nervous during the first scene that I filmed. It took a while to get into a groove and naturally build those connections. Our conversations were very true to what was really going on within the group.

I know it’s a way off, but who do you think will have the most to answer for at the upcoming reunion?

There’s definitely going to be a standoff between Vicki and me because there hasn’t been a whole lot of resolution there. I don’t know if we’ll get there, but I’m hopeful. If we don’t make any progress, then I’ll save that for another time. There are some things with Heather that have just come up that people will have something to say about.

Which of the women’s friendships surprised you the most? Is there anyone you didn’t expect to bond with?

I love Tamra. I feel like I connect with her. I watched a little bit of Season 18 and then some of Season 19, so I don’t have the history with her that some of the other ladies have. However, I had heard things. I did come into the group with some preconceived judgments in my mind about her. However, Tamra and I have a great relationship. I’m really grateful for her.

Glad to hear it! Lastly, what can you tease about the upcoming cast trip to Tokyo?

That’s a little tricky to talk about without spoiling anything. There’s a lot of chaos and drama with Tokyo, so you’ll have to see how it all plays out.
