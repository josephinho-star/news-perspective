What Yankees secrecy is telling us about Aaron Judge’s injury

2026-09-19 23:38

PHOENIX — As is their only way, the Yankees are saying as little as possible about Aaron Judge’s latest ailment. In keeping with the traditions of another well-known group of New Yorkers famed for the strict code of secrecy, nobody knows nuttin’.

“I don’t have a timeline on it,” manager Aaron Boone said. “I can’t predict anything.”
