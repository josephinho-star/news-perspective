Liz Phair is going on tour with Sleater-Kinney. How much are tickets?

2026-08-28 21:08

The dream of the ’90s is alive and well.

Starting Sept. 6, Liz Phair and Sleater-Kinney are hitting the road and dropping into theaters, sheds, bandshells, music halls, festivals, auditoriums and amphitheaters all over North America on their exquisitely-named ‘The Flannel and the Fury Tour.’

That includes a stop at Brooklyn’s Prospect Park Bandshell on Saturday, Sept. 19.

Opening acts on the brief jaunt include exciting indie acts Soccer Mommy, Snail Mail and Frankie Cosmos.

In the event you’d like to get down with the rockin’ co-headliners, tickets are available for all their upcoming concerts.

At the time of publication, the lowest price we could find on tickets for any one show was $27 including fees on SeatGeek (that’s for their Sept. 14 night in Denver).

Prices start at $90 including fees for the Prospect Park gig.

Make sure to use promo code NYPOST10 for $10 off purchases over $250 at checkout (Editor’s Note: this discount is only valid for users’ first purchase on SeatGeek).

“[Our tour] is a nod to 1990’s alt-rock culture and the female-fronted bands who staked their claim to center stage in a male-dominated music industry,” Phair shared via Instagram.

“Sleater-Kinney and I both cut our teeth in the early Riot Girl scene. It’s been a dream of mine ever since to bring our music and our fans together for one night of reckless hope and musical splendor.”

Carrie Brownstein and Corin Tucker added ” Liz Phair has always been a leader and a truth-sayer for the female experience. She’s a legend and a stone cold classic; what a thrill to share the stage with one of our biggest inspirations – we can’t wait to tear it up with her.”

To wrap the run, Phair is slated to headline at Ocean City, MD’s annual Ocean’s Calling Festival on Sept. 25. She’ll be joined by other pre-Millennium icons Gwen Stefani, Dave Matthews Band, Matchbox Twenty, Goo Goo Dolls and Third Eye Blind among others.

For more information, our team has everything you need to know and more about Liz Phair and Sleater-Kinney’s September 2026 ‘The Flannel and the Fury Tour’ below.

Most recently, Phair dropped “Soberish” in June 2021.

Her seventh studio album is 13 tracks of chilled-out yet trenchant rock ‘n roll.

After a few spins, we’d argue that the hypnotic throwback “Hey Lou,” garage rock-esque “Good Side,” quirked-out yet intoxicating “Ba Ba Ba,” raw, vulnerable “Soberish” and haunting, slinky “Soul Sucker” are the five most essential tracks here.

Want to dig a little deeper? Click here to check out “Soberish.”

On Jan. 19, 2024, Sleater-Kinney released their 11th studio album “Little Rope.”

While we love every little nook and cranny of this expansive, fuzzed-out beast of a record, we’re most smitten with the raucous rocker “Hell,” nasty, funky “Needlessly Wild,” slick headbanger “Hunt You Down,” creepy yet peppy “Small Finds” and spaced-out jam “Don’t Feel Right.”

Stick around for the effortlessly groovy “Crusader” as well and thank us later.
