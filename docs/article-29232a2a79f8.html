Liverpool vs. Ipswich Town prediction: Premier League soccer picks, best bets, odds

2026-09-04 15:00

The Andoni Iraola Era at Liverpool is off to a rocky start.

The Reds have posted consecutive 2-2 draws to start the new campaign, and they’ve already put themselves behind the 8-ball compared to Arsenal, Manchester City, and Chelsea in the Premier League race.

Furthermore, Liverpool’s opening fixtures were more than manageable. They were road chalk in their trip to Newcastle in Matchweek 1, and then were prohibitive favorites at home against Nottingham Forest. It’s an opportunity squandered, and anything short of three points against Ipswich Town on Friday afternoon will be a big blow.

Liverpool vs. Ipswich Town prediction

It doesn’t take a rocket scientist to figure out what’s plaguing Liverpool this early in the campaign. The Reds are struggling to keep things tidy in their own half.

Andoni Iraola wants his teams to be active and pressing high up the pitch, but the Reds just don’t seem to have the speed in their ranks to pull off the Spaniard’s system.

Only four teams have allowed more expected goals than Liverpool through two matches this season, and the Reds rank in the middle of the table in shots allowed. Those are concerning numbers considering the quality of competition faced.

Ipswich’s defense grades out even worse, but that should be expected from a newly promoted team. The Tractor Boys were superb in a victory over Sunderland in Matchweek 1, but the defense came unglued on the road against Manchester United.
