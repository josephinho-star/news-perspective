Famed Karen Read attorney Alan Jackson hobbles into court after nasty sports injury

2026-11-21 01:11

The attorney was a no-show at his client Fraser Michael Bohm’s previous pre-trial hearing Nov. 4.

Bohm is charged with four counts of murder and four counts of vehicular manslaughter for his October 2023 car crash on Pacific Coast Highway that resulted in the deaths of four Pepperdine University students.

He also did not appear in court the day of Jackson’s pickleball-related absence.

During that proceeding, someone in the courtroom asked fellow defense attorney Jacqueline Sparagna whether her legal partner was OK.

He was “just in a cast,” she replied.

The hearing had been scheduled to address a bombshell piece of evidence disclosed by prosecutors in September: a 10-to-12-pound tank of nitrous oxide discovered inside Bohm’s vehicle on the night of the deadly crash.

But Jackson’s absence pushed the discussion back by more than two weeks.

And his return Friday didn’t get the case moving much faster.

Jackson and his defense team requested another continuance, which Superior Court Judge Thomas Rubinson granted, pushing further discussion of the nitrous oxide evidence to mid-December.

Jackson is among Hollywood’s most sought-after defense attorneys, having helped Karen Read beat murder charges in two separate trials.

Read was ultimately found guilty of the lesser charge of drunk driving and sentenced to one year of probation after initially facing accusations that she killed her former boyfriend, Boston police Officer John O’Keefe, in 2022.

Read insisted on her innocence from the beginning and spared no expense in hiring an eclectic group of attorneys — one of whom was Jackson.

Bohm’s family hired Jackson last June, ditching criminal defense and DUI attorney Michael Kraut. Months later, the defense team requested slashing Bohm’s $4 million bail in half, stating the family had sold their home and “drained all their retirement” to pay for Jackson’s defense.

Legal expert Royal Oakes previously described Jackson as one of a select group of attorneys Hollywood’s biggest stars would call when facing serious legal trouble.

“You can count the number of those lawyers on one hand, Alan is on the list,” Oakes told The Post in January.

Despite his pickleball setback, Jackson will continue his defense on the Bohm case, with a trial start date set for Jan. 25, 2027.
