The Giancarlo Stanton issue at the center of Yankees’ positional puzzle

2026-11-11 21:17

LAS VEGAS – Brian Cashman and the Yankees face a math problem when it comes to assembling their positional group for the 2027 season – likely a defensive one as well.

Cashman spoke to reporters for 90 minutes Tuesday while at the GM meetings and dropped bread crumbs about how he views roster building this offseason. Most important when it comes to shaping the positional group, Cashman said he views Giancarlo Stanton as a player who finished the 2026 season healthy and would enter 2027 spring training as the DH. In other words, the Yankees are not considering eating the $20 million he is owed to release him and perhaps make the roster more functional.
