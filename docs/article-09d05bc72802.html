Parents of murdered Loyola student Sheridan Gorman fume over accused killer’s request in jail

2026-11-27 23:40

Published Nov. 27, 2026, 6:40 p.m. ET

See more of our coverage in your search results.

The parents of Sheridan Gorman are outraged after learning that the Venezuelan illegal immigrant accused of killing their daughter in March requested a “special pillow” or “sleep accommodations” while in jail.

Jose Medina-Medina, 25, faces charges stemming from the alleged killing of Gorman, an 18-year-old Loyola University Chicago student, on March 19.

The Department of Homeland Security said he entered the US illegally from Venezuela in 2023, at which point he was apprehended and then released into the country by the Biden administration.

He has entered a not guilty plea to charges of murder, attempted murder, aggravated assault, aggravated discharge of a firearm and illegal possession of a weapon.

According to a court order obtained by Fox News Digital, Medina-Medina claimed he is suffering from “head pain due to bullet fragments & skull missing,” adding that he requested to “see a doctor to get a special pillow and/or sleep accommodation” in jail.

Judge Alfredo Maldonado ordered Medina-Medina to be medically evaluated in relation to the health claims.

Sheridan Gorman’s mother blasted the request in an interview with Fox News Digital, contrasting the suspect’s reported sleeping difficulties with the sleepless nights she has endured since her daughter’s death.

“We had to hear our daughter’s alleged killer, his attorneys asking the judge, get this, asking for an orthopedic sleep pillow, because this alleged murderer is having difficulty sleeping in jail,” Jessica Gorman said. “That’s the kind of stuff we’re dealing with. I wish I had an orthopedic sleep pillow. I haven’t slept one night still, the whole night since March 19.

“But I’m so sorry he’s having a hard time sleeping in jail. There’s no one to speak for the victims,” she said.

At an earlier detention hearing for Medina-Medina on April 3, prosecutors said Gorman and her friends were at a Rogers Park pier when she peered around a lighthouse and spotted Medina-Medina. She turned back toward her friends and silently mouthed, “There’s a man behind the lighthouse,” after which Medina-Medina began pursuing them.

Medina-Medina’s attorney said he suffers from epilepsy and still has bullet fragments lodged in his brain. The murder suspect turned himself in at the Texas border in 2023, where he was held in detention before being released. His attorney said that Medina-Medina had requested to be sent back to Colombia, where his mother had moved, but he was transported by bus to Chicago.

Since being held at the Cook County Jail, Medina-Medina in May was caught with a six-inch shank in his possession, according to the sheriff’s office. When officers searched him, they allegedly found a sharpened piece of metal with a handle fashioned out of medical tape from his pants pocket.

Medina-Medina was charged with felony possession of contraband in a penal institution.

He was arrested in 2023 on charges of shoplifting after he allegedly stole just over $130 in merchandise from a Macy’s in downtown Chicago. He failed to appear for court hearings related to that case, and an arrest warrant remained active until the alleged murder.

Court documents earlier obtained by Fox News Digital show that Medina-Medina told officials he was living at Leone Beach Park fieldhouse in Rogers Park in 2023, which was being used as a city-sponsored shelter for migrants.

Despite failing to appear for court hearings and living in city-funded housing, Medina-Medina wasn’t arrested until after he was accused of fatally shooting Gorman. Thomas Gorman, Sheridan’s father, said “multiple” opportunities were missed to take Medina-Medina into custody before he allegedly killed his daughter.

Fox News Digital reached out to Medina-Medina’s attorney for comment.
