Lions owner/chair emeritus Martha Firestone Ford dead at 101

2026-11-25 02:29

Martha Firestone Ford, the owner and chair emeritus of the Detroit Lions, has died, the team announced. She was 101.

The team said Ford died surrounded by her family and loved ones.

Ford and her husband, William Clay Ford, received full control of the team in 1963 and she assumed full control of the Lions after he died in 2014. Ford transitioned to emeritus in 2020.

"In her most recent role as Owner/Chair Emeritus, Mrs. Ford supported her four children – Principal Owner/Chair Sheila Ford Hamp, Owner/Vice Chair Martha Ford Morse, Owner/Vice Chair William Clay Ford Jr., and Owner/Vice Chair Elizabeth Ford Kontulis – as they assumed leadership responsibilities within the family franchise," the team said in its obituary.

"Today the Ford family proudly carries forward the legacy established by Mr. and Mrs. Ford, preserving the values, love, and passion that have shaped a family tradition spanning multiple generations."

Ford Motor Company also shared a tribute honoring her ties to the company and the region.

"All of us at Ford are deeply saddened by the passing of Martha Firestone Ford. Throughout her life, she set an example of grace, generosity of spirit and service to others. Her legacy lives on in the city she cherished, the people she uplifted and the values she embodied. She will be deeply missed, and her example will continue to guide the company and the community she loved. Ford Motor Company extends its deepest sympathies to the Ford family and the Detroit Lions organization, and joins Detroit and all of Michigan in honoring her remarkable life."

The Lions highlighted Ford’s charity work in the community. She backed the Detroit Lions Inspire Change initiative and held top positions in Merrill Palmer Skillman Institute, University Liggett School, Children's Home of Detroit and Edsel and Eleanor Ford House. She was also the founding member of the Henry Ford Maplegrove Center.

"It is with heavy hearts that we acknowledge the passing of Martha Firestone Ford," the team said in a statement. "Mrs. Ford inspired us all as a pillar of the Detroit community and the passionate matriarch of the Detroit Lions football team.

"She will always be remembered for her fierce loyalty to her family, her community and the city of Detroit."

Ford was a part of an exclusive club of pioneering businesswomen in the NFL as well.

She was among the group of NFL matriarchs that included the Chicago Bears’ Virginia Halas McCaskey, Pittsburgh Steelers’ Patricia Rooney and Kansas City Chiefs’ Norma Hunt.

"Her presence on our sideline, in our building, and in our community will be greatly missed. We wish the entire Ford and Firestone families peace in this time of mourning."

Ford is survived by her four children, 14 grandchildren and 17 great-grandchildren, according to the Lions.
