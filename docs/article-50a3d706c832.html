Model Sahara Ray’s cause of death revealed: report

2026-10-20 02:00

The Australian model overdosed on the synthetic opioid Friday and was rushed to a Los Angeles hospital where she was put on life support, the Daily Mail reported.

But her family ultimately made the devastating decision to take her off life support Sunday, an insider told the outlet.

An official cause of death has not been released, and it is unknown if the reported overdose was accidental or deliberate.

Ray’s mother, Gretchen Bach, told the Daily Mail in a statement that “Sahara was an incredibly passionate and kind young woman who loved her family, fashion, her daughter Snow, and surfing.”

The statement continued, “We’re feeling the love that is pouring in from her cherished friends, fans and the many communities that she was part of around the world.”

Ray — who was linked romantically to Justin Bieber in 2016 — was just 34 years old.

Her tragic death comes just months after she gave birth to her daughter, Snow Ray Dow, in January. Ray officially introduced her newborn in April alongside a sweet video of them cozied up together.
