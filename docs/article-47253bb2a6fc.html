Shailene Woodley’s pals alarmed over Aaron Rodgers slamming actress: report

2026-09-12 14:47

Shailene Woodley’s inner circle is reportedly concerned after Aaron Rodgers made a series of pointed remarks about his former fiancée during a podcast appearance this week.

Woodley and Rodgers, whose engagement was confirmed in February 2021, have not spoken in more than two years, sources close to the “Divergent” actress told TMZ Friday.

“We’re questioning why a married dude is bringing up an ex he hasn’t spoken with in over two years,” one source told the outlet.

Woodley, 34, reportedly began dating Rodgers in July 2020, but the pair called it quits in February 2022 in what was described as an “amicable” breakup — not long after he popped the question.

During their relationship, Rodgers apparently understood Woodley valued her privacy and seemed to honor that, making his decision to revisit their past publicly this week appear “out of character,” according to TMZ.

The sources added that Woodley’s friends don’t believe she would be comfortable with Rodgers airing details of their relationship, while noting that she has “no ill will” toward him and has never spoken negatively about him to her circle.

Page Six previously reached out to reps for Woodley for comment, but did not immediately hear back.

During Thursday’s episode of the “Not Just Football” podcast, Rodgers reflected on his engagement to Woodley and claimed that she felt pressured to make a decision, though she “could have said no.”
