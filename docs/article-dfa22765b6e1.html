Jim Cramer's top 10 things to watch in the stock market Monday

2026-10-05 12:59

My top 10 things to watch Monday, Oct. 5 1. Stocks are flat after Friday's rally on a soft jobs report that gave the Federal Reserve more ammo to hold interest rates steady at this month's meeting. Tame oil and bond yields this morning are not helping. While stocks at the index level look good, many names are struggling. It really has been an ugly market lately. I laid out the Investing Club's action plan in my Sunday column . 2. To make matters worse, it's a quiet week for scheduled corporate news and economic data. Left to its own devices, the market will surely find something to latch on to. Fed officials are giving speeches this week. The minutes from the central bank's September rate hike meeting will be out. Marvell holds an investor day tomorrow, and what management says could move our chip stocks. 3. U.S. oil benchmark WTI crude is down about 1% this morning to roughly $90 a barrel, while international standard Brent is slightly higher at $102.50. Interesting report from Reuters : Saudi Arabia unexpectedly cut its price of oil by $5 for Asian buyers. Seen as a way to compensate for higher freight rates. 4. Melius Research's Ben Reitzes upgraded Microsoft to buy from hold. Reitzes has been worried about "AI eating software" and the competitiveness of Microsoft's Copilot, but now he's arguing the doomsday AI warnings from Anthropic and others are changing the narrative. "Satya Nadella and his team are increasingly going to be considered the 'adults in charge,'" Reitzes wrote. Price target of $665 implies over 28% upside for this Club name. 5. Merger Monday: Schneider Electric is buying product design software maker PTC for $22.6 billion . Schneider is listed in France, and the market over there is skeptical of the deal, with shares down almost 10% . Schneider is a rival of Club stock Eaton in data center power gear. Also, logistics provider C.H. Robinson is buying truck brokerage RXO for $5.8 billion . Good to see some M & A activity despite this move in rates. 6. It's especially good to see that Club name Goldman Sachs served as financial advisor to RXO on the sale. Been a rough few months for Goldman's stock, down almost 22% since July 15, the day after reporting a blowout second quarter . Ahead of third-quarter earnings next week, UBS lowered its price target on Goldman to $1,010 from $1,150 and kept its hold rating. This is a wholesale revaluation of the stock's multiple. 7. Wells Fargo , another Club financial, was upgraded to buy from hold at Morgan Stanley. Also named a top pick. Morgan Stanley's thesis: Wells has a clearer path to profitability improvements next year, as the 2025 asset-cap removal moves further into the rearview mirror. This stock has tested my patience at times, but we've held on because of my belief in CEO Charlie Scharf's vision to transform the bank. 8. Bank of America initiated coverage of Vylor with a buy rating and $78 price target, representing 16% upside. This is the Corteva spin-off that began trading last week. Vylor develops seeds for soybean and other crops. The remaining Corteva is crop protection. Vylor is the one to own . Genetically modified seeds are a higher-tech, higher-margin business. 9. Johnson & Johnson has come off its early September record highs. With the AI trade back on, defensive stocks have fallen a bit out of favor. Citi did, however, increase its price target on the Club stock to $303 from $298. The analysts tweaked their estimates around the edges ahead of earnings. I still like J & J, which reports next Tuesday alongside the big banks. 10. Barclays lowered its Nike price target to $37 from $48. But for some reason, it kept a buy rating. The analysts called Nike's latest disappointing quarter and outlook the "final reset" in CEO Elliott Hill's struggles to turn things around. The stock is trading at 13-year lows. Glad we got out of Nike this summer. But, as I said Friday on "Mad Money," I am still rooting for Hill, who was dealt a tough hand. Maybe too tough.
