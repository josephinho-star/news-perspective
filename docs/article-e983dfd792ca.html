49ers veteran receiver’s recent departure hints at Mike Evans’ imminent return

2026-11-21 23:32

It’s unknown if Mike Evans will play in the 49ers’ highly anticipated matchup against the Vikings on Sunday, but the team’s latest move indicates a potential return.

NFL insider Adam Schefter revealed on Saturday that the 49ers waived veteran wide receiver Brandin Cooks, which would create a spot for Evans.

Cooks was signed to the 49ers’ practice squad as insurance to help what was an already injury-depleted wide receiver room as the team was without Evans at the time due to a hip injury.

On Oct. 1, Cooks was promoted to the team’s active roster, a few weeks before Evans went down with an Achilles injury in Week 6 against the Commanders.

This past week, the 49ers have been hinting that Evans could be active for Sunday’s game as he flew to Colorado Springs, Colo., to practice with the team as it prepared to adjust to the altitude in Estadio Azteca — sitting 7,350 feet above sea level.

Cooks, 33, primarily struggled with San Francisco this season, having only five receptions for 58 yards in five games.

Before Evans was injured, he was the team’s leading receiver and was establishing a promising connection with quarterback Brock Purdy, having 27 receptions for 420 yards and three touchdowns through six games.

Evans’ looming return to the 49ers’ roster this week comes a week after Deebo Samuel returned from a hamstring injury and had a two-touchdown performance in last week’s 46-40 win against the Cowboys.

It’s unknown what the future holds for Cooks. San Francisco might welcome him back to the practice squad after wide receiver Christian Kirk revealed on Wednesday that he had knee surgery on his meniscus.

The team is also without De’Zhaun Stribling and third-year wide receiver Ricky Pearsall — who will be out for the rest of the season.
