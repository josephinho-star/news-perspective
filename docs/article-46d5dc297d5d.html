Max Verstappen played an obscene amount of 'GTA VI' and still won the Las Vegas Grand Prix

2026-11-25 16:25

Even casual Formula 1 fans know Red Bull driver Max Verstappen is a freak of nature, but it turns out he also has pretty impressive time-management skills.

The four-time world champ is known as a big gamer and sim racer, often hopping on livestreams.

He was on one such livestream where he revealed that over the Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend he logged an insane 40 hours playing "Grand Theft Auto VI," a game that only came out on the Thursday before the race.

Even wilder, Verstappen still went on to take pole and win the race.

"Forty hours?" someone asked the Dutchman on the stream.

"Yeah," he replied, before being asked if it was the fastest plane trip home given all the gaming he was doing. "No, no, I was sleeping on the plane. I played a lot in the evening and the morning in Vegas... I mean, I can't go out anyway."

It makes sense that with throngs of F1 fans in town, Verstappen would rather lay low than blow some money in a "Wizard of Oz" slot machine, but this is another chapter in the legend of Max Verstappen.

Sure, the race is only a couple of hours, while qualifying and the three practice sessions are an hour each. But Verstappen still has meetings, debriefs, sponsor and team obligations, and still found the time to wreak havoc in Vice City.

While all races require a massive amount of focus, this year’s Las Vegas Grand Prix was one of the biggest chess matches of the season. That was thanks to a combination of the massive tow drivers were getting off of each other and some close racing, plus the strategy involved with managing and deploying electrical energy, something Verstappen mastered to fend off the Mercedes of both George Russell and champion Kimi Antonelli.

Max is built different.

But what do you expect from a guy with a sim rig in his private plane who once got a talking-to about not doing late-night endurance races on race weekends?
