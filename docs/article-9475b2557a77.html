G20 live updates: OpenAI's Altman says the use of AI is 'non-negotiable'

2026-09-02 18:06

Artificial intelligence is taking center stage at the G20 gatherings this week.

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, Anthropic Co-Founder Tom Brown and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman highlight the tech-focused guests on Wednesday for day two of the G20 Innovation Ministerial in Chapel Hill, North Carolina.
