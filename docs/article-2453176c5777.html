Adam22 confirms split from OnlyFans’ Lena the Plug after hoax divorce filing

2026-09-07 20:34

“I would like to formally announce that my wife and I are officially separated,” Adam said on Monday’s episode of his “No Jumper” podcast, explaining that the couple “have ran into some issues” and decided to put their “relationship on ice” for a period of time.

“Will we end up back together? It’s certainly a possibility,” the 42-year-old YouTuber added.

Adam, who married Lena, 35, in May 2023 after they began dating in 2016, explained that their relationship changed after they welcomed daughter Parker, now 5, in 2020.

“Over the last couple of years, in particular, since we had our child, that, I think my extremely one-sided lifestyle of doing podcasts all day every day and not really making myself a terribly active participant in the family life is probably like the biggest factor,” he said of what led to the pair’s split.

“We had a child, and then she kind of just took on like all of the responsibilities of being the parent.”

Adam said Lena would handle everything from household chores to buying Parker’s school clothes, which resulted in her making a “cutting” comment to her husband.

“At one point, she said, ‘You know, you feel like your responsibility in this relationship is to go to work and come back,’ and I couldn’t even say anything,” Adam recalled.

The dad of one admitted that he’s “realized” he needs “more balance” in his life if he and Lena “are ever” going to get back together.

“But we’ve been together for 10, 11 years. I felt like I was doing enough because I wasn’t cheating, doing drinks, drinking,” he noted.

“Lena and I have officially separated,” he wrote Monday. “I hope we are able to work it out but for the time being we are taking a break.”

Adam said the pair “will continue to shoot adult content together for the time being,” adding, “Thank you for your respect for our privacy during this difficult time.”

The duo’s relationship made headlines in June when Lena claimed that a mystery man filed paperwork in her name to try to end her marriage to Adam, alleging financial and physical abuse.

According to the June 1 petition previously obtained by Page Six, a person going by Lena’s name filed for divorce from Adam — born Adam Grandmaison — and requested primary legal and physical custody of their daughter.

The individual also listed the pair’s date of separation as April 15 and disclosed their alleged assets, including $1.15 million they hold in real estate.
