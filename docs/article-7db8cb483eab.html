Jamie Lynn Spears’ daughter Maddie pokes fun at her mom’s ‘high school pregnancy’ in cheeky TikTok video

2026-11-14 23:02

In the comments thread, fans joked that the teen was the reason Jamie Lynn’s hit Nickelodeon series “Zoey 101” was canceled after she became pregnant in 2007 at the age of 16.

“SO UR THE ONE WHO CANCELLED MY CHILDHOOD,” one person exclaimed, while another wrote, “we lost Zoey 101 this isn’t a laughing matter.”

Others branded the video “hilarious,” with one person writing, “I have been having the worst week, and this just made my ENTIRE night. I’m crying. Thank you queens.”

Though speculation about the canceling of “Zoey 101” continues, in reality the show finished filming the fourth season in August 2007, months prior to Jamie Lynn announcing her pregnancy that December.

The show aired its series finale in May 2008, and Jamie Lynn welcomed Maddie with ex-fiancé Casey Aldridge the following month on June 19.

In an August interview on the “Momarchy” podcast, the estranged younger sister of Britney Spears pushed back on the longtime rumors that “Zoey 101” was canceled due to Maddie’s impending arrival.

“If we’re talking about a concrete misconception, it would be that everybody thinks ‘Zoey’ ended because I got pregnant,” she said. “And that is not true.”

“That’s how it is in entertainment,” she went on. “A lot of people who aren’t in the business don’t understand. They think we shoot it, it comes out. No, you shoot it and it comes out maybe the next year or something.”

She added that fans had become “confused” about the process, explaining, “But we were wrapped, we were always wrapped, and there was never going to be another season.”

Jamie Lynn and Aldridge split for good in 2010, and she went on to marry Jamie Watson, with whom she shares 8-year-old daughter Ivey Watson, in 2014.

Jamie Lynn took to Instagram back in 2024 to share where her daughter would be attending college with an emotional upload.

“All in my feels, bc my baby girl has committed to furthering her athletic & academic career at the University of Southern Miss!” she wrote at the time.

“I can remember it like it was just yesterday, as the whole world watched and judged as I made the choice to have my baby at 16 years old, fast fwd to today, and that same little baby girl is now 16 years old herself announcing that she will be going to play D1 ball for an amazing college.”

“I feel like I can’t take much credit for anything,” she continued, “bc I have witnessed that kid go through, face, & overcome some impossible things by her own strong will, convictions, determination, & the sheer grace of God.”
