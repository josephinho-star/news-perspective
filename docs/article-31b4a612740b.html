Get in Best Medicine fans! We’re going to Port Wenn!

When we last left FOX’s medical dramedy — based on the hit UK series, Doc Martin — Port Wenn’s local physician Dr. Martin Best (Josh Charles) learned his medical license was in jeopardy for having his administrative assistant Elaine (Cree) take blood to help him manage his cripling hemophobia. On top of professional setbacks, Martin was also in the midst of navigating the return of his ex, Eden (Eliza Coupe), and his feelings for Louisa (Abigail Spencer), who was considering having a baby with her ex, Sheriff Mark (Josh Segarra).
