Giants beef up defensive line by trading for Ravens’ C.J. Okoye in John Harbaugh reunion

2026-08-29 14:08

John Harbaugh dipped into his Ravens ties to get the Giants some defensive line help.

The Giants acquired C.J. Okoye from the Ravens for a 2028 seventh-round pick, The Post confirmed.

The Giants essentially flipped offensive guard Josh Ezeudu for Okoye, as Ezeudu was traded earlier this week to the Chiefs for a 2028 seventh-rounder.

Okoye came to the NFL through the International Player Pathway Program and played in 13 games for the Ravens last season.

He totaled 15 tackles, a quarterback hit and a pass deflection.

The Giants should still be in the market for defensive tackle help behind starters D.J. Reader and Shelby Harris.

Chauncey Golston plays inside and outside, and 2025 third-round pick Darius Alexander had a disappointing summer.
