Does Trump’s new AI Force signal tech race escalation ahead of Xi Jinping summit?

2026-09-20 12:00

Move may either aid AI risk talks or become containment tool targeting China, one analyst says, as others expect more intense tech rivalry

Experts said the proposed AI Force – whose exact structure remains unclear – could give Beijing a clear counterpart for AI safety talks, but warned it risked becoming an instrument of further technological containment and export controls targeting China.

In a social media post on Saturday, Trump said that “the decimation, or destruction” of artificial intelligence (AI) was among the many hoaxes generated by the “Radical Left Democrats”.

“I, as President of the United States, will not stand by and let this happen,” he said.
