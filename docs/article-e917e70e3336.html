Travelzoo’s November Member Days are like Black Friday for travel deals

2026-11-06 18:15

Consider this a 48-hour event for travel lovers.

Travelzoo’s November Member Days offer travelers a limited-time opportunity to book exceptional vacations around the world at prices they won’t find anywhere else.

Running from Nov. 8 at 12 a.m. PT through Nov. 9 at 11:59 p.m. PT, Travelzoo’s November Member Days are exclusive to Travelzoo Club Members.

Think of Member Days as Black Friday or Amazon Prime Day for travel, with a curated collection of limited-time offers designed to inspire your next getaway.

Held at least four times a year, Member Days are among Travelzoo’s signature membership benefits. Each event brings together a selection of exclusive deals on hotels, vacation packages, cruises, tours and more, giving members the chance to explore destinations around the world at exceptional prices.

You ask, What destinations? How about a Costa Rica road trip with flights for only $589? Or, perhaps you’d like to travel to Prague for only$499, hotel and flights included.

Members and those who join Travelzoo Club began previewing the November offers on Nov. 1, giving travelers time to browse the available deals and start planning before the event officially begins.

Here is a preview of a few of the planned offers*:

- $719 5-star private beach week for 2

- $589 peak-season Costa Rica road trip with flights

- 50% off New St. Lucia resort all-inclusive suite for 2

- $499 Prague trip, including hotel and flights

- $255 Santorini in summer trip — 55% off jacuzzi suite

What are Travelzoo Member Days?

Travelzoo offers exclusive deals to its members, featuring limited-time deals on vacations, hotels, cruises, flights, dining and other travel experiences. During these events, members may get access to particularly attractive prices or deals that aren’t available to the general public.

The offers typically have specific booking periods, travel dates and availability restrictions, so members should check the details carefully and act quickly if they find a deal they like.

Is Travelzoo worth it?

Yes, Travelzoo is legitimate and can be worth it if you travel and enjoy hunting for deals on hotels, vacations and cruises. Membership costs $50 per year in the U.S., so compare its discounts with regular prices to ensure you save more than the fee. It’s best for flexible travelers;

Exclusive offers, expertly vetted

What sets Member Days apart is the quality and exclusivity of the offers. Travelzoo’s deal experts rigorously vet each offer and negotiate directly with travel providers to secure outstanding value and quality. The result is a collection of opportunities that cannot be found anywhere else.

Past Member Days events have featured remarkable trips at prices that make far-flung destinations feel more attainable. Examples include a Northern Lights tour in Iceland, with flights, from $499 per person; a Bali vacation for two featuring a private pool villa, breakfast and massages, from $599 per person; and a getaway to Portugal’s Algarve region, home to some of Europe’s best beaches, with flights from $599 per person.

These past offers provide a taste of the kind of travel experiences members may discover during the November event. With deals spanning destinations, travel styles and budgets, Member Days can be an opportunity to turn a long-imagined trip into a real booking.

Travelers should act quickly, however. Seats, rooms and bookings are limited for every offer, and popular deals often sell out before the 48-hour event ends. Previewing the offers in advance and being ready to book when Member Days opens can make all the difference.

More than just travel deals

Member Days are just one part of the value of a Travelzoo Club membership. Members also gain access to exclusive travel deals throughout the year, airport lounge access when flight delays disrupt their plans, and a Travel Enthusiast Hotline for assistance with unexpected travel problems.

Whether a traveler has lost a suitcase, forgotten essential medication or needs help navigating an unforeseen situation, the hotline offers support regardless of where the trip was booked.

How to join before Nov. 8

For travelers hoping to take advantage of the November offers, joining ahead of the event is a smart first step. A Travelzoo Club membership is available with a 30-day trial for just $1, and trial members can participate in Member Days just like other Club Members.

To get started, click the membership link, sign up for the $1 trial to preview the November offers on Nov. 1.

With exclusive pricing, carefully vetted experiences and limited availability, Travelzoo’s November Member Days give members a compelling reason to start planning their next adventure. The event lasts just 48 hours, but the right deal could inspire a trip to remember for years to come.
