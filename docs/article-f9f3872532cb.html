Brad Pitt and girlfriend Ines de Ramon hit red carpet for premiere of ‘The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth’

2026-11-17 04:19

The movie is written by the two-time Academy Awards winner Quentin Tarantino and is a 1977-set dark comedy drama that shows Cliff Booth’s backstory as he starts a new career as a Hollywood fixer.

Pitt, who starred in Tarantino’s last film “Once Upon a Time in Hollywood” as Cliff Booth, is once again taking center stage and playing the role of the main character.

“The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth” is a spin-off sequel of Tarantino’s previous movie, but one that focuses more on Booth’s new life in Hollywood.

In “Once Upon a Time in Hollywood,” Booth is the longtime stunt double and best friend to Rick Dalton –– a character played by Leonardo DiCaprio.

The movie came out in 2019 and this upcoming film is set to be in theaters on November 25.

Pitt, 61, and de Ramon, 32, have been dating since 2022 and made their red carpet debut in 2024. According to PEOPLE, the couple are “fully living together” and planning for the future.

When the actor’s mom passed away in August 2025, a source shared with PEOPLE that de Ramon was “supporting Brad as he grieved.” “She wants to be there for him, and Brad is very much letting her be that person,” the source added on.

The 32-year-old has been working as the Vice President for a Los Angeles luxury jewelry brand called Anita Ko Jewelry. She graduated from the University of Geneva and started working in the jewelry industry.

Pitt was previously married to Jennifer Aniston in 2000 before filing for divorce in 2005. He then tied the knot with Angelina Jolie in 2014 before the pair separated just two years later.
