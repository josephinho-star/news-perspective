Hollywood legend’s strange stash going up for auction in LA with iconic props, costumes and more up for grabs

2026-10-01 01:59

The auction will take place online and at The Groundlings Theatre (7307 Melrose Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90046) on Wednesday, October 28th at 10 a.m., with tickets going on sale Friday, October 2nd at Groundlings.com.

After creating his Pee-Wee Herman character during an improvisation exercise at The Groundlings Theatre in 1978, Reubens created the stage show dubbed “The Pee-Wee Herman Show” in the early 80s.

That paved the way for Reubens to write the 1985 feature film “Pee-Wee’s Big Adventure,” which served as the directorial debut of Tim Burton and grossed over $40 million from a $7 million budget.

The success of the film quickly led to the 1986 TV series Pee-Wee’s Playhouse, which ran from 1986 to 1990 and featured rising stars such as Phil Hartman, Laurence Fishburne and S. Epatha Merkerson.

The auction features dozens of items from both the film and TV series, with possibly the most coveted item being the full grey suit and red bowtie Reubens wore in Netflix’s 2015 film “Pee-Wee’s Big Holiday.”

The slate grey suit was worn by Reubens throughout the film, with an opening bid of $4,000 already placed, with the auction house estimating a sale between $8,000 and $10,000.

Another big-ticket item is a replica bike similar to the one Reubens rode in “Pee-Wee’s Big Adventure,” though not the exact bike from the film.

This bike is a replica that was ridden by Reubens on stage at the 2011 Spike TV Scream Awards to accept the Visionary Award, which is expected to fetch between $5,000 and $7,000.

There are also a number of more economical items, including a collection of 13 bowties, which had an estimate value between $100 and $200 but has a current bid of $500.

There are also a set of six Pee-Wee Herman matchbox ventriloquist dolls, which has a current bid of $500, right at the low end of the $500-$700 range.

Also on the auction block is a signed photo from “Pee-Wee’s Big Adventure,” which has a current bid of $350.

Other items include various scripts from “Pee-Wee’s Playhouse” and several other projects, clothing items, toys, merchandise and much more.
