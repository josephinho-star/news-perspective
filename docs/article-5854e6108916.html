Twice-divorced Ben Affleck confesses his children savagely mock his dating history

2026-09-25 14:58

The “Animals” star, who has been married and divorced twice, recalled a time he tried to share his “lived experience” at the dinner table.

“They’ll talk about their friends and the drama and their relationships,” he explained of his and first wife Jennifer Garner’s three kids — Violet, 20, Seraphina, 17, and Samuel, 14.

“I said, ‘You know, well, the thing about that relationship there with that person…’” he remembered of one particular conversation. “The 17-year-old just turns to me and goes, ‘Oh, like you’re the expert on relationships?'”

The Oscar winner, notably, wed Garner, 54, in 2005, with the former couple separating a decade later.

The Golden Globe winner quipped to host Jimmy Kimmel that he is a “caricature” to his kids now that they’re approaching adulthood.

“I, like, give advice or try to talk to them like a parent, as you do … but what I’ve found is that now it’s not only one teenager rolling their eyes,” he shared. “I catch them giving each other looks.”

The “Gone Girl” star shouted out his middle child again to divulge an epic diss dished out during a bookstore trip.

“We’re just browsing around and there’s some book by a French guy and it’s called ‘I’m Sorry for Everything,’ and my kid immediately turns to me, deadpan, and goes, ‘Oh, look, Dad, it’s your autobiography,'” he told viewers.

Affleck laughed telling the story, admitting he was “proud” of his offspring’s comedic timing.
