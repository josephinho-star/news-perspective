French man accused of flying to US to murder woman he went on one date with 3 years earlier

2026-11-28 11:00

A French man is suspected of traveling to the United States to murder a woman he had gone on a date with three years earlier, according to police.

Lauren Becker, 22, was stabbed to death on Tuesday morning as she was walking into her job at an Omaha, Nebraska, daycare center, where her alleged killer waited for 45 minutes before stabbing her, police said during a news conference. The suspect, identified as 21-year-old French national Terry Kanmacher, had been living in France before he arrived in Omaha and allegedly killed Becker.

Becker and Kanmacher developed an online relationship in 2023, and the French national traveled to Omaha for two weeks that summer. The two met in person once before Becker ended the relationship in September, according to police.

Becker had reported concerns about Kanmacher to police in Omaha in October 2023 after learning that he allegedly made threats against her online, authorities said.

Omaha Police Chief Todd Schmaderer said Becker's concerns about Kanmacher were "very detail-oriented," adding that many of her concerns "mirrored what took place."

"It is clear that Miss Becker had ended the hopes of a relationship and was afraid of Kanmacher," Schmaderer said.

Police said Kanmacher arrived in Omaha on Nov. 22 and stayed at an Airbnb before going to the daycare center, Kids Can Community Center, and allegedly killing Becker just two days later.

Following Becker's death, detectives said Kanmacher changed his travel plans and tried to fly out of Minneapolis-Saint Paul International Airport, where he was arrested by Homeland Security Investigations agents on Tuesday. He initially was supposed to fly out of Kansas City, Missouri, on November 30.

Schmaderer described the stabbing as a "vicious" and "gruesome" attack, noting that it unfolded outside classroom windows where children were present.

Schmaderer praised Kids Can as a "model" for protecting children during such incidents, noting that a teacher quickly closed the classroom blinds to shield students from witnessing the attack.

Kanmacher, who authorities say entered the U.S. on a travel passport, is being held in Minnesota while he awaits extradition to Omaha.

Retired Omaha police officer Robert Butler, who documented Becker's concerns about Kanmacher in an October 2023 police report, died Nov. 17 after a yearslong battle with cancer, just one week before her murder. Police said Butler's report provided investigators with "critical leads" in the case.

Kids Can Community Center wrote on Facebook that Becker made a "lasting impact" on the kids and staff members.

"We are heartbroken by this loss and extend our condolences to her family, friends, and all who had the privilege of knowing and working alongside her," the childcare center wrote.
