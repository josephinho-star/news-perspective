Britney Spears reuniting with estranged family for Thanksgiving following surprise apology after years of feuding

2026-11-26 01:01

Page Six has learned the singer is in Louisiana for Thanksgiving. She’s currently back in her hometow where she’s spending the holiday with her mom, Lynne, her dad, Jamie, her younger sister, Jamie Lynn Spears, and her two sons, Sean Preston and Jayden James, TMZ also reported Wednesday.

Britney went back to her roots following a trip to London, which she documented on her Instagram, and has already been there for a couple of days, according to the outlet.

Page Six reached out to Britney’s rep for comment, but didn’t receive an immediate response back.

The reunion with her formerly estranged family came after the pop star, 44, said she missed them in an Instagram post over the weekend that she’s since deleted.

“I’m so blessed to have such a beautiful family…I’m so very sorry for bringing up past issues, but I think I did because I realized I needed to protect my heart…it’s the way I love and when I love you, I REALLY love you,” Britney wrote.

“My stubbornness is childish and embarrassing,” she continued.

Britney concluded: “Thoughts on the past, I’m obviously human and there actually might be something to those things I said [psychology] wise…either way I’m sorry to my family bringing up those memories, but I’ve never actually spoke to them face to face on it…it’s frankly bc I just miss them like crazy!!! And I’m very sorry!!!”

Earlier this month, she publicly blasted Jamie Lynn on Instagram along with ex-husband Kevin Federline. She accused the “Zoey 101” star of prohibiting her from holding her 8-year-old daughter, Ivey, although Britney misspelled her niece’s name.

“Jamie Lynn never let me hold Ivy one time when I would visit in Louisiana during covid in two week trips,” she wrote via Instagram alongside a photo of Dakota Fanning sticking up her middle finger in the 2003 film “Uptown Girls.”

“They made me feel like a slave and have all their secret things they did behind my back and all I ever did was work for my dad!!!” she added. “I will find a new family!!!”

Last month, she admitted she was still “bitter” over the 13-year conservatorship her parents subjected her to.

“They say the crazy ones are usually the best…I will admit it, I wasn’t crazy I used to be f—-g literally insane, but the ironic part is there’s a madness and a constant rush of just sitting still with the way my heart used to work,” she wrote in another since-deleted Instagram post.

“Yes, I’m bitter, all the hours, seconds, time, and secretly, subconsciously knowing my capabilities as a woman,” she continued.
