See Timothée Chalamet’s reaction to girlfriend Kylie Jenner’s surprise Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show debut

2026-10-19 01:58

A screenshot from the event’s livestream showed Chalamet — looking as suave as ever in a pair of sunglasses — smiling and clapping for the “Kardashians” star.

He was seated alongside Jenner’s mom, Kris, who looked on with a smile of her own as she filmed her youngest daughter’s big moment on her iPhone while seated next to beau Corey Gamble.

The evening marked Jenner’s first-ever time walking in the iconic lingerie brand’s famous runway — and she even got a pair of wings to mark the occasion.

Jenner’s Fantasy Bra is the first for Victoria’s Secret since 2018 and the most expensive one ever.

The piece of fashionable art features a center yellow diamond worth an estimated $22 million, per the jeweler, and features “every cut to exist.”

Chalamet and Jenner quietly started dating in 2023 and have rarely addressed their relationship despite often showing up to support each other at important events in their respective careers.

Jenner most recently joining Chalamet at the 2026 Academy Awards in March, where he was up for Best Actor and Best Picture for the film “Marty Supreme.”

It was the last stop on an awards season run that began with the film’s premiere in December 2025 and included stops at the Critics Choice Awards, Golden Globes and BAFTA Film Awards throughout early 2026 — off of which Jenner showed up to for Chalamet.
