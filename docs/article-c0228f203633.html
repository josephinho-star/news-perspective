Stock futures decline as Huang ouster sends Nvidia reeling, oil prices rise: Live updates

2026-11-12 14:25

Stock futures fell on Thursday as Nvidia shares slumped on the ousting of CEO Jensen Huang and investors' hopes for a resolution in the Middle East soured.

Nasdaq-100 futures lost 1.2%, while S&P 500 futures fell 0.6%. Dow Jones Industrial Average futures dropped 47 points, or 0.1%.

Selling pressure on Nvidia resumed in extended U.S. trading after the company announced that Huang has resigned and named CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO. This comes after Huang reportedly had an affair with an alleged Chinese spy. Shares were down more than 12% following the announcement.

Investors moved into fellow chipmakers Advanced Micro Devices and Broadcom, with both stocks up around 2%.

However, other key stocks in the AI trade were still down alongside Nvidia. Shares of software company Oracle lost 4%. Neocloud Nebius moved 20% lower in premarket trading Thursday after the company posted better-than-expected third-quarter revenue but offered underwhelming guidance.

In addition to some AI-related pressure, oil prices moved higher after President Donald Trump said in a Truth Social post, "We don't need a deal with Iran. Those days have long since passed." U.S. West Texas Intermediate futures rose more than 1% to above $87 per barrel. International benchmark Brent crude futures also advanced more than 1% to above $94.

Energy stocks Chevron and ExxonMobil were roughly 1% higher as well. Meanwhile, healthcare stocks like Eli Lilly gained more than 1%.

The moves come as macro risks are brewing for the market.

"We would be a little circumspect about the bounce-back as there are some things changing at the margin [like] stubborn inflation, more Fed rate hikes, higher long-term interest rates, change in configuration in Washington DC [and] AI/data center regulation demands," said Sameer Samana, head of global equities and real assets at Wells Fargo Investment Institute.
