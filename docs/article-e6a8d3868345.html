Melissa Gilbert makes emotional Thanksgiving post after Timothy Busfield’s child sex abuse charges dropped

2026-11-26 20:03

Melissa Gilbert expressed her gratitude in an emotional Thanksgiving message to those who stood by her and husband Timothy Busfield throughout his child sex abuse case.

“This Thanksgiving I’m incredibly grateful for all those who came running toward us this year,” the actress wrote via Instagram Thursday alongside a vintage Thanksgiving illustration.

“To those who checked in, took care of our home and animals when we were away, came to hearings, fed us, housed us, spoke up, testified, wrote letters, listened, comforted us, held our hands and checked in regularly, we love you,” she continued.

The “Little House on the Prairie” alum, 62, also thanked the team who helped the couple navigate the legal ordeal.

“Our undying gratitude also, to our extraordinary team who bravely fought and continue to fight for so hard for what is right and true [sic],” she added.

“On this day, as we all acknowledge the blessings in our lives, we send our love and eternal gratitude to each and every one of you,” Gilbert concluded.

Gilbert’s post came more than a month after a New Mexico judge dismissed Busfield’s indictment on four felony counts of criminal sexual contact with a minor.

On Oct. 16, Judge Joseph Montano granted the actor’s motion to dismiss the indictment, citing prosecutorial misconduct during grand jury proceedings.

However, Busfield’s legal battle may not be over, as the judge left the door open for prosecutors to bring new criminal charges against him.

The Bernalillo County District Attorney’s Office said at the time that it was reviewing the ruling to determine its next steps.

Busfield, 69, surrendered to authorities on Jan. 13 after the Albuquerque Police Department issued a warrant for his arrest in connection with allegations of sexual abuse involving 11-year-old twin boys.

The “West Wing” star was initially charged with two counts of criminal sexual contact with a minor under 13 and one count of child abuse.

According to the criminal complaint, Busfield met the children while working as a director on the television series “The Cleaning Lady,” which filmed in New Mexico.

One of the twins alleged the abuse began when he was just 7 years old, while his brother also accused Busfield of inappropriate touching.

Prosecutors alleged the abuse occurred between November 2022 and spring 2024.

An investigation was launched in late 2024 after a doctor at the University of New Mexico Hospital reported suspected abuse to authorities.

In a statement released after turning himself in, Busfield vehemently denied the allegations, calling them “lies” and vowing to prove his innocence.

A representative for Warner Bros. Television previously told People in a statement, “The health and safety of our cast and crew is always our top priority, especially the safety of minors on our productions.”

“We take all allegations of misconduct very seriously and have systems in place to promptly and thoroughly investigate, and when needed, take appropriate action. [We] have been and will continue to cooperate with law enforcement,” the statement continued.

The actress and the “Field of Dreams” star tied the knot in 2013, nearly a year after meeting at a bar.

Both stars had been married twice before and welcomed children with their previous partners.
