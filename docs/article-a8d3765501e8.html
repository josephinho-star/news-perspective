Is ‘Saturday Night Live’ New Tonight? Here’s When The Inde Navarrette-Hosted Episode of ‘SNL’ Airs on NBC

2026-10-17 21:45

The first three episodes of Saturday Night Live Season 52 are in the books.

What are some of your favorite moments from the new season? I personally enjoyed Jalen Brunson’s “Office MVP,” the return of Jeremy Culhane’s hilarious Tucker Carlson impression, and Mikey Day and Marcello Hernandez’s bickering video game characters from Weekend Update.

If you want to do a deep dive on some of the funniest sketches in SNL history, check out Decider’s recent article that asks, “What’s Your Perfect Saturday Night Live Episode?” From Steve Martin’s memorable “Not Gonna Phone It In Tonight” musical opening to classic favorites like “Van Down by the River” and “Unfrozen Caveman Lawyer,” it’s an enjoyable trip down memory lane.

Obsession star Inde Navarrette is set to host the next new SNL. When exactly does that episode air on NBC? Is Saturday Night Live new tonight (October 17)? Here’s everything you need to know.

Is Saturday Night Live New Tonight (October 17)?

Nope. SNL is a rerun tonight. But don’t worry; a new episode is just around the corner!

When Does SNL Return With New Episodes?

The next new installment of SNL airs Saturday, October 31 on NBC and Peacock.

Who’s Hosting The Next New Episode of Saturday Night Live?

Obsession standout Inde Navarrette will make her SNL hosting debut during the Halloween episode. If you’re looking to watch (or rewatch) Obsession, the hit film is now streaming on Peacock.

Content creator Druski is reportedly set to host the November 7 episode, although that has yet to be officially confirmed by SNL.

How To Watch Saturday Night Live Online:

If you have a valid cable login, you can watch SNL on NBC or the NBC website/app. Already cut the cord? No problem!

The only way to watch shows airing live without cable is by using a live TV streaming service. Our favorite is DIRECTV, which comes with a five-day free trial before payment begins.

DIRECTV offers a number of flexible subscription options, including Genre Packs, so you can best choose which streaming plans fit your needs and budget. Packs start at $19.99/month.

Can I Watch Saturday Night Live on Peacock?

You can stream new episodes of SNL live or on demand on Peacock. The upcoming Inde Navarrette-hosted episode will be available to watch on demand on Peacock beginning Sunday, November 1.

Saturday Night Live returns on October 31.
