Will There Be A Season 4 Of ‘Silo’ On Apple TV? ‘Silo’ Season 4 Release Date Info

2026-09-04 15:00

When the end credits roll on Silo Season 3’s jaw-dropping finale, fans of the hit post-apocalyptic sci-fi drama will be left begging for more.

Viewers will be left with a long list of burning questions about both past and present timelines, but the question at the top of everyone’s lists is sure to be, “Will there be a Season 4 of Silo on Apple TV?”

Curious about Silo‘s Season 4 renewal status? And if Silo was renewed, when is Silo Season 4’s release date? Wondering if and when the series returns with new episodes? If Season 4 filming has started? And what Silo Season 4 will be about? You’ve come to the right place!

Here’s everything to know about the future of Silo, including Silo Season 4 renewal information, Silo Season 3’s ending explained, a Silo Season 4 trailer breakdown, and more.

Will There Be A Season 4 Of Silo On Apple TV?

You bet! Halfway through Season 2, Apple TV gave Silo an early Season 3 and Season 4 renewal, writing in a press release, “The fourth season of Silo will bring the series to its final, thrilling chapter, telling the complete story of Hugh Howey’s New York Times bestselling trilogy of dystopian novels.”

“I’ve loved every minute of bringing Juliette to the screen and am immensely proud of what we’ve all created with Silo since the first episode,” added star and executive producer Rebecca Ferguson. “I have always felt passionately about telling the entire story contained within Hugh Howey’s books, so I couldn’t be happier that audiences around the world have enthusiastically embraced the show. Alongside our partners at Apple, Graham, and the entire cast and crew, I cannot wait to dive into these final two thought-provoking seasons that will beautifully conclude this dystopian tale.”

So when is Silo Season 4 coming to Apple TV?

Silo Season 4 Release Date, Filming Updates: When Does Silo Return With New Episodes On Apple TV?

Though an official Silo Season 4 premiere date has yet to be announced, Season 3 and Season 4 filmed back to back, and production on the fourth and final season has concluded. Based on past gaps in between seasons, fans are anticipating at least a year-long wait.

Season 1 aired from May to June 2023. Then, following the 2023 strikes, Season 2 aired from November 2024 to January 2025. And Season 3 ran from July to September 2026. So Silo should return in 2027!

Silo Season 3 Ending Explained: Silo Season 4 Preview

If you’re in need of a detailed Silo Season 3, Episode 10 recap, Decider’s got you covered. But for the purposes of Season 4 storylines, here are some key events to keep in mind. While viewers likely hoped that Silo‘s Season 3 finale would show the immediate aftermath of the suspicious, conveniently timed end-of-world event that forced parents-to-be Daniel and Helen to seek shelter in different places — Silo 1 and Silo 18, respectively — the series took a major leap into the future. So heading into Season 4, more flashbacks that shed additional light on that fateful day and beyond are definitely possible.

Instead of picking up after the blast, Silo leaps three centuries ahead and reveals that Daniel, who goes by the name Troy, was preserved using cryo technology, had his memory wiped, and exists in Silo 1 to help manage the rest of the silos. After Juliette and Silo 18’s residents successfully avoid a Safeguard after capping their delivery pipe, Troy and Juliette make contact.

Disguised as the algorithm, Troy tells Juliette that if she stops trying to make contact with other silos, Silo 1 will stop trying to kill everyone in Silo 18. Though Juliette agrees to the deal, she and her friends plan to take out Silo 1. So that will be a driving storyline in Season 4. And after Victor died by suicide, he left a video for Troy and revealed he once knew a woman named Helen Drew, which flooded Troy’s brain with memories of his life as Daniel. We’re not sure exactly how much he remembers, but we can bet Season 4 will see him racing to put the pieces together, make sense of his life in Silo 1, and reconnect with his sister.

The third book in Howey’s series is Dust, and though Yost has altered and added some major storylines, if you want potential spoilers, that’s the book to read or research. And read on for a first look at what’s to come!

After Silo‘s Season 3 finale dropped, Apple TV shared a sneak preview of Silo Season 4 in a teaser trailer that shows us checking back in with Helen in Silo 18’s past, Sheriff Billings clueing Silo 18’s residents in on the truth about Silo 1 in the present, and Daniel and Juliette coming face to face!
