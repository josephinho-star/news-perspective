LA’s outrageously pricey grocery chain Erewhon with $20 smoothies lands in NYC: ‘I’m gonna cry’

2026-11-18 19:17

Today, the cult Los Angeles grocery store Erewhon announced its first-ever public New York Tonic Bar is setting up shop in NoHo’s Kith Women’s flagship store, with a grand opening scheduled for this Friday from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. EST.

The organic NYC locale will be open seven days a week and offer a curated menu that includes signature tonics, superfood smoothies, bowls, juices, cold-pressed shots, coffee and ceremonial-grade matcha.

“I’m gonna cry,” commented one emotional customer-to-be.

“I manifested this,” said another.

“This line gonna wrap around the whole ass earth,” quipped a more skeptical commenter.

Named after an 1872 utopian novel and doubling as an anagram for “nowhere,” Erewhon initially opened back in 1966. However, it only blossomed into a bona fide status symbol around 2020, paralleling the rise of luxury influencers and social media-fueled wellness culture.

The chic chain is now beloved by loyal fans for its healthy bites and celeb-backed drinks like Hailey Bieber’s Strawberry Glaze Skin Smoothie (sold at most L.A. locales for an eye-watering $21), and currently has 13 full-fledged grocery stores in California.

Though NYC technically already has a drinks-only Erewhon Tonic Bar at the private Kith Ivy luxury padel, wellness and social club in the West Village — opened in late 2025 by the streetwear brand’s founder and CEO Ronnie Fieg — this locale’s menu is just available in-person to club members, who pay dearly with $45,000 initiation fees and $10,000 in annual dues.

While determined New York non-members can still snag one of the drinks from Kith Ivy through Postmates and Uber Eats, at this location, drinks are available in limited quantities and must be ordered within a one-mile radius of the bar.

But at the New York Tonic Bar at 644 Broadway’s Kith Women’s store, deluxe health drinks will be easily accessible to anyone willing to splurge — including a store-exclusive “Erewhon State of Mind” Smoothie, a red-apple-inspired blend featuring organic cold-pressed apple juice, warming winter spices and goji berries.

Sales for the Empire-inspired smoothie will support The Bowery Mission, a longstanding city nonprofit that offers help for New Yorkers experiencing homelessness and other crises.

In a recent statement, Erewhon CEO Tony Antoci shared that “New York has always felt like a natural place for Erewhon to grow.”

“We’ve always believed that access to thoughtfully sourced, high-quality ingredients should extend beyond our stores in Los Angeles,” added company vice president Josephine Antoci. “This new concept allows us to share a part of the Erewhon experience with a broader community, while remaining true to the values and standards that have guided us from the beginning.”

Expansion is set to continue for Erewhon in the new year, with additional California locales opening in Palisades, DTLA and Costa Mesa in 2027, according to the store site.
