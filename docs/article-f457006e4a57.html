AEW All In 2026 match card, preview and predictions

2026-08-29 13:18

All Elite Wrestling (AEW) will hold its biggest event of the year at Wembley Stadium in London on Sunday.

All In has proven to be a showcase of some of the pro wrestling’s best. This year will be no different with championship matches up and down the card, including the AEW World Championship being on the line between Kenny Omega and Will Ospreay.

All In has also featured some surprises in the past with massive returns and debuts that have shaped the AEW roster.

Read below for a list of the match card, a preview and predictions for how All In will shake out.

Adam Page, Bandido & Brody King (c) vs. Jay White, Austin Gunn & Colten Gunn vs. Orange Cassidy, Kyle O’Reilly and Roderick Strong vs. Claudio Castagnoli, Pac & Wheeler Yuta vs. Ricochet, Bishop Kaun & Toa Liona vs. David Finlay, Clark Connors & Gabe Kidd vs. Wildcard Team for the AEW World Trios Championship

A major trios match will kick off the two-hour All In Buy In and bleed into the first few minutes of All In. Adam Page and Brodido, the Bang Bang Gang, The Conglomeration, the Death Riders, The Demand and The Dogs will compete for the AEW World Trios Championship. But there is a wildcard entrant in this match. It could be anyone or it could be who we’re all expecting it to be. All In has been full of surprises in recent years. Let’s get another one in to start the show off with a bang.

Prediction: Adam Page and Brodido retain the trios title despite the chaos that will ensue.

Kyle Fletcher (c) vs. Konosuke Takeshita vs. Kazuchika Okada for the AEW International Championship

Kyle Fletcher, Konosuke Takeshita and Kazuchika Okada may be three of the best professional wrestlers in the world. Their rivalry began after Takeshita defeated Okada for the AEW International Championship at Double or Nothing. Fletcher returned and turned on Takeshita. Weeks later, Fletcher won the title at Dynamite: Spring Breakin’ and turned the title picture on its head. Now, the three men will meet at All In. And while it may seem like Fletcher and Okada are on the same page, with the title on the line, it’ll be every man for himself.

Prediction: Kyle Fletcher, somehow, pulls it out and retains the title.

Kevin Knight (c) vs. Darby Allin for the AEW TNT Championship

Kevin Knight has been the TNT champion for more than 135 days after winning a 10-man Casino Gauntlet match in Vancouver back in April. He’s gone from a fan favorite to the most hated after he turned on "Speedball" Mike Bailey and aligned himself, loosely, with the Don Callis Family. He’s been one of the top stars on the AEW roster since then but may have feuded with the wrong guy in Darby Allin. The challenger has proven that he’s willing to go above and beyond to win any match – even without gold on the line. Allin has been back in the fray for some gold since losing his AEW world title at Double or Nothing to MJF. As he’s dealt with Knight’s mind games, Allin has still shown he’s not willing to go down easy. This will be a big matchup.

Prediction: Darby Allin ends Knight’s title reign.

Megan Bayne and Lena Kross (c) vs. Alex Windsor and Jamie Hayter for the AEW Women’s World Tag Team Championship

Megan Bayne and Lena Kross, the Divine Dominion have been at the top of the AEW women’s tag team division since March and have dispatched every opponent who have come their way. The Brawling Birds, Alex Windsor and Jamie Hayter, have been two women who have not backed down from challenging Bayne and Kross. The Brawling Birds have their home country behind them in this match and the momentum may carry over for them to get the win.

Prediction: The Brawling Birds take home the gold.

Jon Moxley (c) vs. Nigel McGuiness for the AEW Continental Championship

It has been a long and grueling Continental Challenge Cup and the finals will see continental champion Jon Moxley defend his title against Nigel McGuiness. Moxley is back on the All In card after losing the AEW World Championship to Adam Page last year. Moxley is looking for his own personal redemption and it may come at the expense of Nigel McGuiness, who is already battling an injury. McGuiness’ in-ring appearances have been few and far between but getting back in shape to make it through to the end is an accomplishment in and of itself. I don’t think Moxley can afford to lose this match.

Prediction: Moxley retains the AEW Continental Championship.

Men’s Casino Gauntlet match

The men’s Casino Gauntlet match will have a title shot waiting for the winner. The match has seen its own share of surprises in the past and this one may not be different. We already know who the initial entrants are: Andrade El Idolo, MJF, Tommaso Ciampa, Mark Davis and Chris Jericho. Nobody knows who else is going to be in this match, which could make for some wild in-ring action. MJF has the edge after winning this match last year at All In. Will he be able to pull it off again or is his focus too much on AEW national champion Andrade?

Prediction: It’s a wild one. Somehow, Chris Jericho sneaks away with the victory.

Willow Nightingale (c) vs. Mercedes Moné for the AEW Women’s World Championship

Mercedes Moné has been lusting to get back on top of the AEW women’s division since her return to the ring earlier this year. She won the Owen Hart Foundation Women’s Tournament to earn a shot at the AEW Women’s World Championship. After Willow Nightingale defeated Thekla at Redemption, the match at All In became more about the history between Moné and Nightingale than just the championship. Nightingale was the fourth-ever AEW TBS champion and held the belt for 35 days before Moné ended her first reign. Moné held that title for 584 days before Nightingale finally got her revenge. Nightingale was forced to give up her title due to an injury but made her way back to capture the AEW Women’s World Championship. Now, Moné is on her radar again and apparently delivered an on-screen hand injury to Nightingale on the final "Dynamite" before All In.

Prediction: Mercedes Moné picks up the title win.

Christian Cage and Adam Copeland (c) vs. Matt Jackson and Nick Jackson for the AEW World Tag Team Championship

The temps between the teams of Christian Cage and Adam Copeland and Matt and Nick Jackson have turned up ever since they teamed up at Grand Slam Mexico. Things between the teams got even more chaotic on "Dynamite" as Copeland was the last one standing when they butted heads. The AEW world tag titles have meant the world to have for Cope and Cage. But the Young Bucks have been a dominant force in tag team wrestling for years. It’s a fairly important matchup for the wrestling industry and it will be a treat to see these four men in the ring, going up against each other, at the same time.

Prediction: Cope and Cage retain in a great tag match.

Kenny Omega (c) vs. Will Ospreay for the AEW World Championship

It’s the match everyone is waiting for: Kenny Omega vs. Will Ospreay for the AEW World Championship. I don’t have to write too much on their history. Anyone who has ever watched pro wrestling knows the epic matches they put on in New Japan – some of the best matches in wrestling history. Both competitors are a little older now, and a little wiser. Omega is on top of the division as the champion after beating MJF at Spring Breakin’. Ospreay won the Owen Hart Foundation Men’s Tournament to earn the title shot, which everyone initially thought was going to be against MJF. But now Ospreay has a chance to dethrone his "hero" in Omega and do it in the main event at Wembley Stadium in his home country. Ospreay has been clear that it has always been his dream to be the focal point at Wembley and now he can be that and more.

Prediction: It is Will Ospreay’s time. Ospreay wins in a massive main event.

AEW All In begins at 1 p.m. ET and can be purchased in the United States on HBO Max, Amazon Prime, DAZN, PPV.com, YouTube, DirecTV, Sling and Dish Network. The Buy In begins at 11 a.m. ET.
