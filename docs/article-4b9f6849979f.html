Fate of Thanksgiving Day Parade talent revealed after historic weather postponement

2026-11-26 00:24

The Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade was postponed at the last minute due to weather, but talent is still expected to participate, Page Six has learned.

Holly Robinson Peete and her husband Rodney were on their way from Los Angeles to New York City when they were notified the festivities were pushed back to Saturday.

“On the plane to NYC!” Robinson Peete, who is on the star-studded parade roster, told Page Six exclusively. “No matter when the parade takes place, I’ll be there!”

“It’s such an honor to be part of the 100th [anniversary] and I’m excited to be able to promote my upcoming Hallmark movie, ‘Hearts All Aglow,'” she continued. “Mostly, I want to acknowledge how difficult this decision had to be for Macy’s and am happy they made this choice to keep people safe … but baby, the show WILL go on!”

“Due to the extreme weather conditions, the city of New York, Macy’s and NBC have decided, in the interest of safety for both the participants and spectators, to move the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday, Nov. 28,” the spokesperson said in a statement.

“The safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority, and after close consultation with our City and agency partners, we have made the decision to move this year’s celebration,” Macy’s added. “Our team is working through the operational logistics for this change and will share details as they are finalized. Thank you to the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who create the magic millions enjoy each year. We look forward to celebrating together on Saturday.”
