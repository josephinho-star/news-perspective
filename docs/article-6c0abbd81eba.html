Melania Trump attends world premiere for her new docuseries at NYC’s Lincoln Center

2026-11-20 01:44

First lady Melania Trump dazzled in an elegant Dolce & Gabbana black dress as she premiered her new two-part docuseries on the world stage in Manhattan Thursday night — close on the heels of her chart-topping self-titled documentary.

“Melania, The Series” — comprised of two episodes — made its silver screen debut at Lincoln Center’s Alice Tully Hall, a day before it drops in more than 240 countries on Prime Video.

Melania took to the red carpet along with former US Attorney General Pam Bondi, her partner John Wakefield, ex-Trump White House senior adviser Kellyanne Conway and others.

The first lady told reporters that the series “was very exciting to do” but noted it “was a lot of work” and “not an easy task.”

“It was the first time I was involved from the idea till the end,” Melania said. “Everything what you see, it’s my idea. From the advertising, from the music, editing”

Conway praised the first lady and pointed out how frequently she offers advice to President Trump.

“She is a very trusted adviser to him, as well as, obviously, his wife and his partner,” Conway said of Melania. “She’s been my friend for many years.”

The first lady’s new series reveals never-told-before stories of President Trump’s 56-year-old wife and pulls back the curtain on the immediate aftermath of the 47th presidential inauguration in January 2025.

Melania told “Fox & Friends” earlier this year that the series will be “very different” to “Melania, The Documentary,” which followed the first lady throughout the 20 days leading up to her husband’s second inauguration.

“It’s more conversation one-on-one with me,” she said in September. “There’s some private questions that I will answer that people never heard before, so it’s kind of exciting.”

The first episode, titled “Family & Trust” focuses on Melania preparing to once again become America’s first lady but inside the walls of the White House, trust is tested and Melania “must protect those closest to her while navigating the demands of power.”

The second, and final episode, “Pride & Honor,” follows Melania moments after the inauguration as the first lady “sets her mission in motion for the American people.”

Directed by Brett Ratner, Melania serves as executive producer alongside her senior adviser and agent Marc Beckman.
