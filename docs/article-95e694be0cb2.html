England forward Ivan Toney pleads not guilty to Soho nightclub assault

2026-10-01 11:50

Ivan Toney has pleaded not guilty to assault after being accused of headbutting and punching a member of the public at a Soho nightclub.

The England striker appeared at Westminster magistrates court, standing in the dock to confirm his name and date of birth before entering a plea of not guilty to assault occasioning actual bodily harm.

The court heard that on 6 December last year, words were exchanged between the 30-year-old striker and Saber Da Silva at 100 Wardour Street, a live music venue. Prosecutor Tom Heslop said it was not known what was said between the two men, but the exchange resulted in them “squaring up” to one another before Toney “lunged forward and headbutted the complainant, followed by a few punches”. The altercation left Da Silva with injuries to his nose and fingers, it was alleged.

Toney has won 10 caps for England and was part of the squad that reached the Euro 2024 final. He plays for Saudi Pro League side Al-Ahli and was also included in Thomas Tuchel’s squad for the World Cup during the summer, coming on as a substitute in the semi-final defeat to Argentina and starting the third-place play-off win against France.

In a hearing that lasted only 12 minutes, Toney was asked by District Judge Briony Clarke if he wanted the case to be heard at magistrates or crown court. Wearing a shirt, tie and black half-zip jumper, he stood and replied: “Crown court.” His trial will take place before a judge and jury.

Toney was released on unconditional bail but warned by the judge that if he does not attend Southwark crown court at 9.30am on the appointed date, a warrant could be issued for his arrest.

Toney was charged on 31 July following a Metropolitan police investigation. After the charge was announced, a spokesperson for Toney said: “Ivan acknowledges the charge and although he is naturally shocked, he looks forward to being given the opportunity to clear his name in court.”

Toney started his career at his hometown club Northampton. He moved to Newcastle, where he made only two league appearances before moving to Peterborough United. After signing for Brentford, Toney’s goals helped the west London club win promotion to the Premier League in 2021, where he established himself as one of the top flight’s leading forwards. He moved to Al-Ahli in 2024 for £40m.
