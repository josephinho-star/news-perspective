Mina Kimes shades Jensen Huang with ‘MNF’ outfit

2026-11-17 15:16

Mina Kimes had the jokes on “Monday Night Football.”

The ESPN NFL analyst showed off her game day fit while on the “MNF” scene at the Ravens’ M&T Bank Stadium, wearing an all-black outfit complete with a leather jacket.

Kimes went to X to share a photo while simultaneously shading Nvidia co-founder and former CEO Jensen Huang.

“Asians still allowed to wear leather jackets or is it too soon?” she wrote.

Huang, who stepped down from his position last week, is a known leather jacket enthusiast, wearing one practically everywhere.

He left the company following internal investigations over multiple affairs, including one with a Chinese-born executive, who U.S. officials suspect as a Chinese spy.

Kimes pulled off a cheeky joke as she was in Baltimore ahead of the Ravens-Chargers clash for “NFL Live.”

She appears on the desk alongside Marcus Spears, Dan Orlovsky and host Laura Rutledge, along with occasional guest analysts and insiders including Adam Schefter.

Kimes, 41, has been with the Worldwide Leader for over 12 years. She also hosts “The Mina Kimes Show featuring Lenny” on a weekly basis during the NFL season.

A late-game fumble by Zay Flowers just before the two-minute warning cost the Ravens the 23-20 loss to the Chargers, who earned their first victory of the season.

The Chargers were on an 11-game losing streak dating back to last season.

Justin Herbert and Omarion Hampton each scored first-half rushing touchdowns and the Chargers defense held Derrick Henry to 10 carries for 10 yards, despite two scores.

The 1-8 Chargers host the Jets next week, while the Ravens travel to Carolina in Week 11.
