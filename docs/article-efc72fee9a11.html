Dan Aykroyd reveals he and Bill Murray stole police motorcycles during ‘Ghostbusters’ joyride in NYC

2026-09-26 05:57

Dan Aykroyd revealed that he and his “Ghostbusters” co-star Bill Murray once stole two “fully equipped” police motorcycles for their impromptu Central Park joyride.

Aykroyd, who starred alongside Murray in the hit franchise, said that the pair “appropriated” the bikes from the “Ghostbusters II” set during its 1988-89 shoot and took them for a late-night ride in New York City.

“So many adventures over the years,” Aykroyd wrote on Instagram as he celebrated Murray’s 76th birthday Wednesday, sharing two throwback photos of the longtime friends and co-stars.

“One night shooting GB2, we appropriated two fully equipped police motorcycles from the movie’s motor pool and spent an hour chasing each other around a closed Central Park with full lights and sirens.”

Aykroyd clarified that the joyride didn’t get the two actors into any trouble.

“No consequences. After all, we were Ghostbusters,” he quipped.

Aykroyd, 74, also praised Murray in the touching birthday tribute as “the greatest comedy leading man of our time.”

The two comedians first met in the 1970s as they were beginning their comedy careers.

Their most famous collaboration came in 1984, when they starred in “Ghostbusters” alongside Harold Ramis and Ernie Hudson.

The four original Ghostbusters reunited for “Ghostbusters II,” released in 1989.

After Ramis’ death in 2014 at age 69, Aykroyd, Murray and Hudson made cameos in the 2016 reboot before reprising their original roles in “Ghostbusters: Afterlife” in 2021 and “Ghostbusters: Frozen Empire” in 2024.

Aykroyd said he drives “very carefully” in Los Angeles and stays inside during what he calls “prison hours,” referring to the late-night hours he spends in his gated community, he told Adam Carolla in 2023.
