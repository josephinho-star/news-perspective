Want the iPhone 18 Pro? T-Mobile has a deal for new and existing customers

2026-09-12 14:16

The new iPhone 18 Pro and the iPhone 18 Pro Max are available for preorder starting Saturday, Sept. 12. T-Mobile is offering the iPhone 18 Pro on us with an eligible trade-in and an Experience Beyond plan. You can also save $1,200 on the iPhone 18 Pro Max with an eligible trade-in plan.

To celebrate the iPhone 18 Pro's release, T-Mobile is offering a special deal. The new iPhone 18 Pro is reportedly 50% faster and offers up to 13 more hours of video playback than previous generations.

New customers can get an iPhone 18 Pro when they switch to T-Mobile, put in their existing phone number and enroll in the Experience Beyond plan.

READ MORE: Passing on the iPhone 18? Switch to a Google Pixel for free with these T-Mobile offers

Existing customers may also qualify by trading in an eligible device in any condition and enrolling in the Experience Beyond plan. Current Experience Beyond customers may qualify, too.

The Experience Beyond plan costs $100 per month for one line, $85 per line for two lines and about $57 per line for three lines. It includes unlimited talk, text and data, along with Netflix and Hulu.

For more deals, visit www.foxnews.com/deals

If you prefer a larger phone, the iPhone 18 Pro Max offers a slightly bigger screen than the iPhone 18 Pro. It also provides up to 15 additional hours of video playback, for up to 45 hours on a full charge. The phone comes in the same four colors as the iPhone 18 Pro: Burgundy, Silver and Black.

T-Mobile is also offering up to $1,200 off the iPhone 18 Pro Max when you enroll in the Experience Beyond plan and trade in an eligible device in any condition. With the base model priced at $1,299, the offer brings the cost to $99 before taxes, fees and plan costs.
