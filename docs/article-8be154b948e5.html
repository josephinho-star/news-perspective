Prince Harry, Meghan Markle’s UK move making locals ‘wary’: ‘Don’t bring the circus here!’

2026-08-31 20:57

Prince Harry and Meghan Markle’s return to the UK is already making some locals “more wary than excited” about the prospect of their arrival.

Royal commentator Kinsey Schofield exclusively told Page Six that the “chattering class” of the North Cotswolds — where the Duke and Duchess of Sussex are reportedly house hunting — are “not overly excited.”

“The Cotswolds can be incredibly welcoming to high-profile people because there’s an unwritten agreement: ‘You don’t make a spectacle of yourself, and neither will we,” Schofield explained.

And while they are used to celebrities living in the posh countryside enclave, this situation feels “strange” for them.

“The irony is that Harry and Meghan are moving to a place that is accustomed to famous people… but completely unimpressed by fame,” Schofield said. “You can bump into Liam Gallagher at the local pub and nobody cares.”

The “Kinsey Schofield Unfiltered” podcast host was told by an insider that celebrities who live in the area — including David Beckham and Ellen DeGeneres — enjoy the neighborhood because locals don’t “bat an eyelid” at their presence.

“But my sources also describe the area as strongly royalist, which makes Harry and Meghan’s arrival somewhat different from that of a typical celebrity,” Schofield explained.

She heard from one neighbor that the community doesn’t have time for the Sussexes.

“The advice from locals is essentially that Harry should keep a low profile and allow the family to settle quietly into the community,” Schofield said, adding, “One source put it rather colorfully: ‘Shut up for at least eight months!’ and ‘Harry needs to respect the community.'”

Schofield urged Harry and Markle to be “incredibly boring” as they settle into their new surroundings.

“There’s a sense of, ‘Please don’t bring the circus here.'” Schofield said. “These people are used to celebrities. Harry and Meghan are different because wherever they go, the headlines tend to follow.”

However, the royal expert believes there is an opportunity for Harry and Markle to “change the temperature” around them.

“But that requires restraint,” she said, adding, “You cannot ask a community to protect your privacy while simultaneously generating headlines that bring attention to their doorstep.”

“I was told by a friend of Harry‘s earlier this year that he was going to go house hunting and potentially in the Cotswolds near Highgrove,” royal commentator Tom Sykes told Page Six last week, referring to King Charles III and Queen Camilla’s Highgrove House residence.
