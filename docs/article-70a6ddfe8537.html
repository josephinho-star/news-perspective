Dove Cameron marries Damiano David in Italian wedding

2026-09-24 20:48

Dove Cameron exchanged vows with Damiano David in Italy on Thursday.

The “56 Days” actress was pictured outside Montalcino town hall with David, wearing what appeared to be a white suit-inspired bridal look with a cravat and belted, caped jacket.

She wore her dark locks parted down the middle in a sleek, simple look, and carried a bouquet of white calla lilies.

The groom, meanwhile, wore a coordinating off-white suit with an olive green scarf draped around his neck.

In one photo, the couple laughed alongside family and friends, and in another, David held a vehicle door open for the “Shamless” star as she stepped in.

In another, he swept the “Descendants” alum into his arms for a romantic wedding day kiss.

Eventually, they made their exit in an off-white vehicle.

Cameron, 30, and the Måneskin musician, 27, were first romantically linked back in 2023 after the “Boyfriend” singer was seen at one of his Madison Square Garden concerts in New York City.

They made their red carpet debut at Clive Davis’ iconic pre-Grammys gala in Beverly Hills, California, in 2024, packing on the PDA for photographers at the annual event.

Cameron wore a Valentine-coded red corset look for the occasion, while David rocked a vest with slacks.

Last October, the actress was spotted out in Sydney, Australia, modeling a massive diamond ring on her ring finger, with TMZ reporting they’d become engaged.

Cameron subsequently took to social media in January to show off the sparkler and confirm the engagement in a social media post.

Cameron — who previously dated Thomas Doherty — gushed over David in a 2024 interview with Cosmopolitan.

“He’s the best person I’ve met in my life,” she told the outlet. “You spend two minutes around him and you realize he is the kindest, most generous, most innocent, most giving person. He’s just a beautiful, sensitive soul.”

In 2025 interview on SiriusXM Hits 1, David — who previously dated influencer Giorgia Soleri — returned the sentiment, gushing, “I’m kind of in one of the happiest moments of my personal life.”

He added, “I have the healthiest relationship I’ve ever had, and I manage to see my family, see my friends, take care of myself … The victory for me now is actually to make my job and my personal life live together and not constantly fight.”
