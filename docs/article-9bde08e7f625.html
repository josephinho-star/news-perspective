Paige DeSorbo says this viral alarm clock makes her wake up feeling ‘like Snow White’

2026-08-28 22:42

Paige DeSorbo feels “like Snow White” every morning.

The social media star admitted on a recent Amazon Live with Nicky Hilton that one particular product makes her wake up feeling like a princess: a Hatch Alarm Clock.

“It’s really more like a sound machine, but it also has a light, so in the morning, if you set your alarm, the light will slowly turn on and you can wake up to birds chirping,” she explained. “You literally feel like you’re Snow White…I can’t wake up to my phone alarm because it literally induces a panic attack.”

Below, shop DeSorbo’s favorite morning ritual — plus more beauty and home essentials she’s loving at the moment.

Instead of a loud, jarring morning alarm, the Hatch Alarm Clock features customizable light and audio settings that wake you up gently and in a state of complete relaxation. It’s also outfitted with 80-plus science-backed sleep sounds for optimal shut-eye.

“I literally live in the bed, and these are so good. They’re from Squishmallows, everyone’s favorite plush brand,” DeSorbo gushed. “The best part: they come in three different densities. So whether you’re a side sleeper or a back sleeper or a stomach sleeper (I’m all three), there’s one for you. Every single one comes with the softest white pillowcase. I’m obsessed with the whole thing.”

DeSorbo admitted that before a long flight, she puts Aquaphor on her nostrils to protect against germs.

“I think it works!” she exclaimed, then confirmed she’s an “Aquaphor person.”

“I love Vicks VapoRub for anything. I truly feel like it has healing powers. If I’m getting a little cough, I’ll put some Vicks VapoRub [on],” she said. “I’m a 95-year-old woman. My grandma and I were best friends. I’m an old lady. I love Vicks. I have it by my bed, and I also have Aquaphor, because you literally never know.”

DeSorbo — who didn’t specify the exact LED mask she uses — said that the device is a “vibe,” and even if it doesn’t work, “the placebo effect” makes it worth it.

“If I like it, I’m doing it,” she insisted. “I have a mask that is covered, but it almost looks like it has goggles…I do it more when I’m watching TV, but it does come down to the neck. I love it.”
