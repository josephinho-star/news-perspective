LeBron’s 76ers, Grizzlies continue to impress in Post’s NBA Power Rankings

2026-10-31 23:27

Each week, the California Post will power rank the NBA’s 30 teams. Records are as of Saturday morning:

The Rockets take the top spot despite suffering their first loss of the season Friday to the Mavericks. Statistically, they have the league’s best offense and are getting contributions from across the board. Seven Rockets are averaging double-digit scoring figures. (Last week, 5th)

2. Memphis Grizzlies (5-0)

The league’s last undefeated team continues to climb up the rankings after another perfect week. The Grizzlies were the fun preseason pick when asked which team would exceed expectations. And with Ty Jerome and Cameron Boozer leading the way, they’ve done that so far. (Last week, 13th)

3. Philadelphia 76ers (5-1)

Two wins in 10 days over the defending NBA champions? It’s fair to say LeBron James, Tyrese Maxey, Jaylen Brown and Joel Embiid are headed in the right direction and could lead the 76ers to being legitimate NBA title contenders. (Last week, 2nd)

The Pistons have hardly missed a beat since All-NBA center Jalen Duren suffered a right hamstring strain that’ll sideline him for at least another week. Superstar guard Cade Cunningham leads a Pistons roster that’s getting contributions from all over as they look to build off a 60-win season last year. (Last week, 9th)

The Celtics have bounced back after dropping their season opener to the Pistons, rattling off four straight wins. Paul George has been a great fit, and Jayson Tatum is getting back to looking like his pre-Achilles injury self. (Last week, 10th)

6. Oklahoma City Thunder (4-1)

The week went from bad to worse for the 2025 NBA champions. Three starters (Jalen Williams, Chet Holmgren and Cason Wallace) suffered injuries that’ll sideline them for various periods. And the Thunder lost their first game after opening the season with four straight victories. (Last week, 1st)

7. San Antonio Spurs (3-2)

The Spurs have bounced back from a slow start to the season with three straight wins. Victor Wembanyama is back to playing like the two-way force the Spurs need him to be to build off their 2026 NBA Finals appearance. (Last week, 4th)

8. Portland Trail Blazers (3-1)

The Trail Blazers started off hot, winning their first three games of the season before losing to the Grizzlies. But now, they face an uncertain immediate future with Ja Morant sidelined for at least the next month because of a strained left hamstring. (Last week, 12th)

9. Los Angeles Lakers (4-2)

The Lakers enter the weekend with the taste of their blown 25-point lead during the loss to the Warriors still fresh in their mouths. Luka Doncic (league-leading 36.2 points per game) is having an MVP-caliber start to the season, but this past week exposed some flaws. (Last week, 6th)

10. Minnesota Timberwolves (4-2)

The Timberwolves were as hot as any team to start the season, but Anthony Edwards having a procedure on his right knee that could sideline him for a couple of months cooled them off. (Last week, 7th)

11. Dallas Mavericks (4-2)

The Mavericks rattled off three straight wins this past week, and 2025-26 Rookie of the Year Cooper Flagg is looking like he’s taken a leap. Flagg is averaging 26.8 points, 6.6 assists, 6 rebounds, 2.6 steals and 1.2 blocked shots. (Last week, 15th)

12. Toronto Raptors (3-2)

The Raptors only got two games from Kawhi Leonard before swelling in his left knee sidelined the star forward. Scottie Barnes is poised to lead the Raptors while Leonard is out. (Last week, 8th)

13. Cleveland Cavaliers (3-2)

The Cavaliers’ losses came against teams expected to be in the playoff race. They’re performing at their expected level but need more from James Harden to be a true contender in the East. (Last week, 14th)

14. Golden State Warriors (3-3)

Three straight wins over potential playoff teams, including the epic comeback against the Lakers, have the Warriors climbing back up the rankings after a slow start. Steph Curry’s third in the league in scoring (29.2 points). (Last week, 26th)

You can only play the teams in front of you, but the Nuggets’ victories have come against the lowly Pelicans and a Suns team that came out of the gates slowly. They need Nikola Jokic to get back to his MVP form. (Last week, 11th)

Nickeil Alexander-Walker is proving that last season’s breakout year wasn’t a fluke as the Hawks ended the week with a victory over the Cavaliers. (Last week, 18th)

17. New York Knicks (2-4)

The defending champions haven’t looked like it, with an 0-3 start turning into a disappointing October. There will be several opportunities to get back on track and stack wins throughout November. (Last week, 3rd)

18. Washington Wizards (3-3)

Anthony Davis (22.3 points, 9.2 rebounds) and Trae Young (20.7 points, 10.2 assists) have led the Wizards to a respectable record and standing throughout the league so far. (Last week, 23rd)

19. Charlotte Hornets (3-3)

It may not be the most efficient brand of basketball, but Brandon Miller is keeping the Hornets afloat as they wait for Coby White and Kon Knueppel to make their season debuts. (Last week, 19th)

Giannis Antetokounmpo continued to stuff the stat sheet (28.2 points, 12.3 rebounds, 5.5 assists), and the Heat finally picked up their first wins of the season against the Hawks and Pacers to close out the week ahead of an early West Coast swing. (Last week, 17th)

Road wins over the Knicks and Raptors this past week put the Magic in as good of a spot as possible despite an early-season ankle injury for Paolo Banchero. (Last week, 20th)

Tyrese Haliburton’s hamstring injury, which is expected to sideline him for two weeks, took the energy out of the start of the season. The Pacers entered the weekend having lost their previous three games. (Last week, 16th)

The Suns went from a gritty, tough style of play last season to having the league’s worst defense this year. They have to address the less glamorous end of the floor to get back on track. (Last week, 22nd)

24. Los Angeles Clippers (1-4)

The injuries continue to stack up for the Clippers, who signed Blake Wesley on a 10-day hardship deal in light of who they don’t have available. (Last week, 25th)

25. New Orleans Pelicans (1-4)

The Pelicans picked up their first win with their victory over the Kings on Thursday, but they still remain without a clear direction. (Last week, 27th)

Injuries that’ve zapped their frontcourt depth (Jaren Jackson Jr., Jusuf Nurkić) have slowed down what could’ve been a promising start to the season. (Last week, 21st)

Prioritizing the development of young players typically comes at a cost, even when it’s the right direction for a franchise. The Bulls continue to learn this lesson. (Last week, 24th)

28. Milwaukee Bucks (1-4)

The Bucks are feeling the growing pains after trading Antetokounmpo. (Last week, 30th)

29. New Jersey Nets (0-5)

At least the Nets have been competitive in a couple of their games so far. (Last week, 29th)

30. Sacramento Kings (0-5)

Meanwhile, the Kings are getting blown out regularly. (Last week, 28th)
