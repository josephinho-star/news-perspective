Steelers star says international games 'freak me out' after Aidan Hutchinson's injury in Germany

2026-11-18 00:08

If Pittsburgh Steelers star Cameron Heyward never played a game outside the United States again, he would be OK with it.

Heyward was asked about Detroit Lions star Aidan Hutchinson’s brutal season-ending injury he suffered during a sack celebration in Germany on Sunday. The four-time All-Pro referenced how New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye got hurt on the same play.

"I don't like to, you know, those international games freak me out just because of that. It's just, those fields are a little bit different. It's wild to see," Heyward said during an appearance on "The Rich Eisen Show."

Hutchinson sacked Drake Maye, who may have suffered a season-ending ankle injury on the play, and jumped into the air to celebrate. When he landed, he grabbed his right knee and crumpled to the ground at Allianz Arena, home to FC Bayern Munich.

The seven-time Pro Bowler said he feels horrible for Hutchinson.

"Oh man, that was, as a defender, you're like, I get the sack, and then you go to celebrate, and I'm always wary about jumping in there 'cause there's freaky stuff that happens. But you know, I, I feel horrible for the kid. I got a chance to meet Aiden years ago; we were doing a Super Bowl commercial, and he's one of the nicest dudes, and a lot of respect for his family," Heyward said.

Losing Hutchinson is a major blow to the Lions' defense , which is currently without star linebacker Jack Campbell after he suffered a terrifying neck injury last month.

In nine games, Hutchinson has recorded 24 tackles and six sacks. While the Lions are 7-2 and sit atop the NFC North, the Minnesota Vikings and Chicago Bears are also 7-2. The fourth-place Green Bay Packers are 6-4.

In such a competitive division, the Lions were relying on Hutchinson to lead their defense and help them keep pace.

Heyward would know that playing surfaces are different internationally, as the Steelers played in the NFL’s first-ever game in Paris in October, which they won 16-10 over the New Orleans Saints. The Lions-Patriots game was the final European game of the season.

The San Francisco 49ers and Minnesota Vikings will play in Mexico City on Sunday night, marking the final international game of the season.
