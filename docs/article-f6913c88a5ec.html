How Kate Middleton honored Princess Diana for Remembrance Sunday

2026-11-08 19:01

She was photographed on a balcony at The Cenotaph in London, wearing a brimmed hat and an all-black outfit with three poppy pins to commemorate fallen soldiers.

Middleton, 44, is assumed to wear the pins in honor of her great-grandmother’s brothers, who died in World War I.

She was joined by Queen Camilla, 79, as their husbands, Prince William and King Charles III, took part in the ceremony.

William, also 44, and Charles, 77, laid wreaths during the service.

Prince Harry — who returned to live in the UK with his wife, Meghan Markle, in late August — did not attend the ceremony with his estranged family.

Instead, Diana’s youngest son, who sported his medals and a poppy pin, kept a low profile while standing on a side street with locals in Brackley, Northamptonshire.

The Duke of Sussex, 42, was not joined by Markle, 45, or their two children: Prince Archie, 7, and Princess Lilibet, 5.
