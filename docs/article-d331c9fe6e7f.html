Fans outraged at Harry Styles over ‘insane’ NYC concert ticket with eerie nod to 9/11 — 2 days before 25th anniversary: ‘Who approved this?’

2026-09-10 03:00

Harry Styles is under fire for artwork on a ticket for his Together, Together Tour that contains an eerie nod to Sept. 11 — just two days before the 25th anniversary on Friday.

A social media user shared the image of the ticket, which depicts a yellow car — seemingly with Styles in the vehicle — flying over the Empire State Building and leaving a trail across the iconic New York City landmark’s spire.

The ticket in question is for Wednesday, Sept. 9, the seventh night of Styles’ tour stop at Madison Square Garden.

“putting that on the back the week of 9/11 is f–king insane 😭 it’s cute but i fear that was not a good idea,” one person wrote of the artwork, with another noting, “A little bold given its 9/11 week in NYC.”

“bro that’s wild,” a netizen wrote, with another chiming in, “literally who approved this 😭?”

Page Six has reached out to Styles’ representatives for comment.

This ticket fiasco comes as NYC is preparing to mourn the nearly 3,000 lives lost when the Twin Towers of the World Trade Center collapsed after two commercial airlines flew into them on Sept. 11, 2001.

The collective 9/11 attacks are the deadliest terrorist attack in United States history.

Styles is currently midway through a 30-night residency at Madison Square Garden that kicked off Aug. 26 and run through Halloween on Oct. 31.

He’s already performed in Amsterdam, London, São Paulo and Mexico City.

He’ll head to Australia for shows in Melbourne and Sydney after wrapping up his time in NYC — his only stop in the United States on his Together, Together Tour.

The tour is Styles’ third solo tour since his split from One Direction and is in support of his fourth studio album, 2026’s “Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally.”

The “Watermelon Sugar” singer emailed his fans Wednesday to share that he’ll be announcing 2027 dates for the Together, Together tour on Thursday, Sept. 10.

“When we decided to do the 2026 tour I knew it would be special but what you turned it into was far beyond what I could have imagined,” he began in the email, per Rolling Stone.

“I love playing music, I love being with all of you and being a part of the love and joy you all create together every night,” he continued. “That’s why I wanted you to be the first to know we will be adding 2027 dates.”

While Styles admitted the thought of adding additional dates was “daunting,” he shared that “being an artist is the only life [he’s] ever known” and that he feels “like the luckiest person in the world to still be sharing these moments with [his fans].”

The Grammy winner said the dates that will be announced are for the “final cities” for the tour.

“After that, I don’t know what comes next for me,” he revealed, “but I know that I’m excited to find out.”

Styles is currently engaged to actress Zoë Kravitz, so perhaps he’ll be turning his attention to wedding planning when he’s off the road.
