Former Israeli ambassador to US lays out three things Trump can do to speed up ‘inevitable’ collapse of Iranian regime

2026-11-18 10:30

The current “no deal and no war” state of the nearly 9-month-long conflict has put Iran in “actually a very bad situation,” the Israeli diplomat told “Pod Force One” host Miranda Devine.

This is because Trump’s military actions have eliminated the “existential” nuclear threat from Iran, while economic sanctions have put a “squeeze” on the regime, the likes of which it has “never faced,” Dermer said.

Iran is on the ropes, the ex-ambassador argued, and to usher in a new era of long-term peace, Trump must focus on three things.

“I think that collapse will be inevitable if you keep the pressure up … the three legs: credible military threat, crippling sanctions, and to engage the Iranian people as your partners will put tremendous, tremendous pressure on them,” Dermer said.

The third leg of the strategy is “critical” and something Dermer argued “hasn’t been done by any other previous administration.”

“Iran’s regime hates America. Iran’s leadership hates America. The Iranian people, actually, most of them love America – and they’re your partner,” he said, comparing what Trump must do to what “Reagan did” to bring about the end of the Soviet Union.

“You know, the worst moment for the Soviet Union under the Reagan administration was when he called them an evil empire,” he argued.

“[T]he people in the prisons, the dissidents in the prisons understood that once you had an American leader that would make clear right and wrong, good and evil, it was only a question of time before they were going to collapse,” Dermer continued.

“I’m telling you, if you see dissidents in the Oval Office, that will be as big a blow to the Iranian regime as dropping a thousand bombs on them because they understand they don’t have legitimacy.”

Dermer, who served as Israel’s ambassador to the US between 2013 and 2021, argued the strategy will work even without Trump in office.

“If Iran is staring down years and years of this type of pressure, no matter who wins two years from now in the presidential elections, they’re not going to be able to sustain it,” he said, calling for Trump to issue a policy directive to “the CIA to work with other intelligence agencies in the region and other agencies to do everything they can to help the Iranian people win their freedom.”

Along with engaging with Iranian dissidents, Dermer said the policy should include arming Iranians against the regime inside the country and providing them with communications equipment.
