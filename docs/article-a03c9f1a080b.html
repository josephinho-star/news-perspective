Is ‘Monday Night Football’ on ABC tonight? Where to watch tonight’s ‘MNF’ game live for free

2026-11-23 23:01

Here we go! Live from Northwest Stadium in Landover, Maryland, the Washington Commanders host the Cincinnati Bengals on Monday Night Football.

Cincy enters this Monday night showdown on a hot streak. Winners of five straight, the Bengals scored 38 points during their Week 10 Sunday Night Football win against the Pittsburgh Steelers. Joe Burrow threw for 409 yards, and Ja’Marr Chase had 11 receptions for 231 yards as Cincinnati improved to 7-2.

On the other side of the field, Washington (3-5-1) continues to hang around in the NFC East. The Commanders were on the right side of a 24-21 game against the New York Giants on Thursday Night Football. Will the extra rest help Washington score an upset win? We’re about to find out.

Is Monday Night Football on ABC this week? Where can you watch MNF live for free? Here’s everything you need to know.

Is Tonight’s Monday Night Football Game on ABC (November 23)?

Nope. ABC will simulcast ten Monday Night Football games during the 2026 regular season, with the other seven matchups airing only on ESPN.

Tonight’s Bengals/Commanders game is only airing on ESPN (and streaming on NFL+).

Where To Watch Tonight’s Monday Night Football Game Live Online For Free:

You can watch MNF live on ESPN or the ESPN app/website (with a valid cable login).

The only way to watch shows airing live without cable is by using a live TV streaming service. Our favorite is DIRECTV, which comes with a five-day free trial before payment begins.

DIRECTV offers a number of flexible subscription options, including Genre Packs, so you can best choose which streaming plans fit your needs and budget. Packs start at $19.99/month.

You can also watch MNF with an active subscription to fuboTV, Hulu + Live TV, YouTube TV, NFL+, or Sling TV. YouTube TV, fuboTV, and Hulu + Live TV offer free trials for new subscribers.

Is The ManningCast On Tonight?

You bet. Peyton and Eli are back on ESPN2 and the ESPN App for Week 11.
