‘Slow Horses’ is back for Season 6 — Here’s when and where to watch for free

2026-09-15 21:00

Watch 'Slow Horses' Season 6 for free

- When: Sept. 16 (new episodes weekly on Wednesdays)

At yesterday’s Primetime Emmys, Saul Metzstein, who directed all of “Slow Horses” Season 6, won outstanding directing for a drama Series. It was also honored with nominations in eight other categories, including outstanding drama series, outstanding lead actor (for Oldman), outstanding supporting actor (Jack Lowden), outstanding writing and outstanding direction.

When is Season 6 of “Slow Horses” coming out?

“Slow Horses” Season 5 premieres Wednesday, Sept. 16 on Apple TV. One new episode will drop every Wednesday through Oct. 21.

Apple TV has not confirmed a release time, but the streamer will often release new episodes of original series the night before their listed premiere date. In the case of “Slow Horses,” you’ll likely be able to watch new episodes as early as 9:00 p.m. ET on Tuesday nights.

Was ‘Slow Horses’ renewed for Season 7?

Yes! In July 2025, “Slow Horses” was renewed for a seventh season, so you can expect at least one more season of “Slow Horses” once Season 6 wraps up. Apple TV also announced a Season 8 renewal yesterday, on the heels of another Emmy win.

Is ‘Slow Horses’ based on a book?

Yes! The series, as a whole, is based on Mick Herron’s Slough House series. Each season takes inspiration from a book in the series. Season 6 of “Slow Horses” is based on both the sixth novel, “Joe Country” and book seven, “Slough House.”
