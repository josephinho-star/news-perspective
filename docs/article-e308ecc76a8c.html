Michigan Democrat Rep. Debbie Dingell: 'Painful to Watch' Haley Stevens Defend Her Endorsement of Mike Rogers

2026-11-02 16:25

Rep. Debbie Dingell (D-MI) believes Rep. Haley Stevens’ (D-MI) defense of her decision to endorse U.S. Senate candidate Mike Rogers (R-MI) over radical leftist Abdulrahman Mohamed El-Sayed (D) was “sad.”

Dingell spoke during an interview with CNN’s Anderson Cooper on Sunday moments after Stevens went on the same show to defend her decision, the Hill reported.

“First of all, I’m going to say I’m not sure I would have done this if I had known she was going to be on immediately before me. That was one of the most painful interviews I’ve ever watched. I have been worried about her all last week. She’s a human being. I’ve had multiple conversations with her. I’m far from being the only one that has,” Dingell said.

“I’m very concerned that this has all become a distraction that the people of Michigan do care about what’s happening to the cost of the groceries, they’re worried about whether they can make their mortgage payments, fill their cars with gas,” Dingell continued, adding she was “sick of ads on all sides.”

Her comments came after Stevens endorsed Rogers on Sunday over the Muslim socialist El-Sayed in the Michigan senate race.

“I can’t tell you how sad I am… It was just painful to watch,” Dingell said of Stevens’ interview just before her own.

She added, “I think that this is backfiring. Abdul has raised more money today, more than a million dollars. Talking to different people that have been at different polls, there have been more walk-ins at the end of the day, lines on the last day of early voting. People in the black community are really angry about who speaks for them. They’re not gonna have other people speak for them.”

“By the end of the day, I thought Abdul was gonna win, but I’ve felt a shift just being out, as I always am, this afternoon, so I think this race is going to be close,” she noted.

Meanwhile, Rogers applauded Stevens for her decision.

“Congresswoman Stevens showed immense courage in joining our campaign and standing up against hate today. Her never-ending commitment to put Michigan first deserves the highest praise, but instead, the media is doing what it always does — forcing division to get views. If you believe we have a state to save, join Congresswoman Stevens and me,” he wrote in a social media post:

President Donald Trump also praised Stevens in a Truth Social post on Sunday, writing, “Wow, this is BIG! Congresswoman Haley Stevens, a Highly Respected Democrat, who narrowly lost in the Primary to Abdul Mohamed El-Sayed, and who has been breathlessly wined and dined by the Dumocrats to help in their Michigan Campaign for the United States Senate against Mike Rogers, has just ENDORSED Mike Rogers for Senate!”

“Her following is huge, and the level of respect from the People of Michigan, especially our African American and Jewish populations, is virtually unparalleled. Haley said what few people are willing to say. She showed great and almost unprecedented loyalty to the Great People of Michigan with what she did. It’s a thing called bravery, and few people have it like just displayed by Congresswoman Haley Stevens! President DONALD J. TRUMP,” he concluded.
