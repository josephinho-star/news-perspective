Tayshia Adams used this $5 ‘secret weapon’ to recover from the NYC Marathon

2026-11-06 18:32

Tayshia Adams traded roses for racing shoes.

The former “Bachelorette” recently crossed the finish line at the 2026 New York City Marathon, marking her fourth time running the iconic race.

The reality star was all smiles as she greeted fans along the course, wearing a hot pink Maybelline T-shirt (she ran on behalf of the brand) and toting a mini belt bag.

As for what she kept in tow during the 26.2 miles? Below, she shares six essentials that helped her go the distance — and recover afterwards.

“I cannot say enough good things about Feetures socks! I’ve been wearing them for about three years now, and I swear I’ve convinced half the people in my life to try them. They have different levels of cushioning and sock heights, so you can really find what works for you. They’re supportive, stay exactly where they’re supposed to, and I never have to think about them while I’m running — which, honestly, is exactly what you want in a good running sock. Each training season, I look forward to replenishing my sock drawer with new pairs. They’re that good!”

“This little thing is my secret weapon, and I am not exaggerating when I say I have five of them scattered around my house. People underestimate how much tension we carry in our feet, and how much that can affect how the rest of our legs feel. Whether I’ve just finished a long run or spent the day running around in heels for work, I always roll out the bottoms of my feet before bed to work through any tight spots. It’s one of those tiny habits that’s made such a difference in how my feet feel, both with running and in everyday life. I even pack one when I travel. I’m never leaving home without it.”

“After training for four NYC marathons, I’ve learned that taking care of my body is just as important as putting in the miles. I think one thing we can all relate to is the everyday wear and tear that comes with just living life. Whether it’s a long run, a busy work day or traveling, minor aches and pains happen. I love having Tylenol Precise Pain Relieving Patches on hand for targeted, temporary relief of minor muscle and joint pain. What I especially love is that they’re clear and discreet, so I can put one on and go about my day without thinking twice about it. They stay on for up to 12 hours, which is amazing when you’re constantly on the go. It’s one of those little essentials I love having in my bag because you never know when you might need it!”

It leaves my skin looking so glowy, hydrated and supple, but somehow feels like I have absolutely nothing on my face.
