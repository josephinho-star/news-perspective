Prince Harry’s plans for movie in the UK revealed — and why Prince William is furious: report

2026-09-04 14:32

Daily Mail’s Senior Editor-at-Large Richard Kay alleged the Duke of Sussex, 41, “has been reaching out to his friends and others who knew” his mom, who died in August 1997 at 36, and “asking them to contribute.”

The royal expert noted, “Let’s not forget who Harry spent time with when he was here in July, it was Diana’s family at Althorp.”

The alleged project, which will “occupy a lot of [Harry’s] headspace over the next 12 months,” has left William, 44, with “intense frustration,” per Kay.

However, Harry’s plans for another movie will “throw up that old canard” with “people always link[ing] Harry to Diana,” Kay predicted.
