No 10 BYU survives Kansas in triple overtime thriller to keep College Football Playoff hopes alive

2026-11-21 22:20

It is better to win ugly than to lose pretty, and BYU's season-saving triple-overtime win was not pretty.

No. 10 BYU (9-2) staved off Kansas (4-7) in a 31-29 thriller on Saturday afternoon at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium to keep its College Football Playoff hopes alive. For a moment, it looked like the Cougars were going to come away with the win without requiring overtime.

Kansas tied the game at 16-16 with a touchdown with just over four minutes left in the game, but BYU quarterback Bear Bachmeier led the Cougars into field goal range, leaving kicker Matthias Dunn a 37-yard field goal attempt with time expiring.

However, instead of walking Kansas off in regulation, Dunn yanked the field goal attempt wide left as time expired, forcing overtime.

The Jayhawks got the ball first and quarterback Isaiah Marshall took the game over with his legs. On third-and-10, Marshall scrambled for 12 yards to keep the drive alive. BYU forced another third down, but Marshall again escaped the pocket and scored a touchdown, putting Kansas up 23-16 after the extra point.

It took BYU just three plays to respond, as a 23-yard pass from Bachmeier to wide receiver Kyler Kasper set up a 2-yard touchdown run from Bachmeier. After Dunn's extra point, the game was tied at 23-23.

BYU got the ball first in the second overtime and scored in just four plays. Running back LJ Martin converted from 11 yards out, but in the second overtime, both teams must go for two if they score. Bachmeier's pass on the two-point conversion fell incomplete, meaning Kansas could win the game if it scored and converted on the two-point conversion.

BYU's defense was on the brink of closing the game out, as it forced a fourth-and-5 on Kansas' opening series. However, Marshall threw up a prayer to the end zone as he was being pressured, and wide receiver Nahzae Cox went up and hauled it in for a 20-yard touchdown to tie the game 29-29.

Kansas squandered its opportunity to pull off the upset when Marshall was pressured in the pocket and threw the pass over the end zone.

In the third overtime, only two-point conversions can be attempted, and the Jayhawks went first. Marshall again found himself under pressure and was stripped of the ball in the pocket.

BYU, afforded an opportunity to walk off Kansas for a second time, capitalized in triple overtime. Bachmeier found Jojo Phillips in the end zone for the two-point conversion, sealing the win.

The roller coaster win saved the Cougars' College Football Playoff dreams. They will have to beat Cincinnati at home next week to strengthen their case to be in the tournament.
