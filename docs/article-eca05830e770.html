Cowboys fan runs over TV with truck in crazy meltdown after embarrassing loss to Buccaneers

2026-10-09 21:46

Being a Dallas Cowboys fan is not for the faint of heart. It should probably come with a warning label, a therapist on speed dial and now apparently an extended warranty on all televisions.

After watching America’s Team get ‘embarrassed’ 24-16 by the previously winless Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Thursday Night Football, one furious fan decided he’d seen enough.

Literally.

Wearing a Dak Prescott jersey, TikTok user @daniel1caudillo carried his television outside, hurled it onto his driveway and proceeded to crush it beneath the tires of his massive pickup truck.

Then he threw it in reverse and ran over it again just for good measure.

Apparently, one demolition wasn’t enough. Much like the Cowboys’ offense, the television never stood a chance.

The meltdown came after Dallas somehow managed to lose at home to an 0-4 Buccaneers team starting undrafted rookie quarterback Jalon Daniels in place of injured starter Baker Mayfield.

Prescott’s game-ending interception only added to the misery.

The humiliation spread quickly across social media. Former Cowboys receiver Keyshawn Johnson demanded head coach Brian Schottenheimer and defensive coordinator Christian Parker be fired. Stephen A. Smith gleefully trolled Dallas fans, while Skip Bayless called it the ugliest Cowboys loss he could remember.

Even Prescott acknowledged the embarrassment after a stadium worker heckled him following the game.

“Not that they’re wrong,” Prescott admitted.

But perhaps the funniest response came beneath the television demolition video.

“Now you have to buy another one to watch them lose again next week,” one commenter wrote.

That’s the cruel reality of Cowboys fandom. The television can be replaced. But the product the Cowboys are putting on the field every week isn’t getting a refund anytime soon.
