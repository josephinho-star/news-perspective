Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce go to extreme measures to keep nosy neighbors away from new Ohio mansion

2026-09-16 00:04

Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce aren’t taking any chances when it comes to feeling safe in their new Ohio mansion.

Page Six has exclusively learned that the couple have set up a warning alarm surrounding their new digs.

“A neighbor approached the house and got a voice warning about not passing onto the property after getting too close to the house,” a nearby neighbor shared.

Before getting married in July at Madison Square Garden, Swift and Kelce, both 36, purchased the 21,000 sq. foot lakefront mansion back in March for $5.35 million in Bratenahl — which is miles away from where Kansas City Chiefs player grew up in Cleveland Heights.

The eight bedroom, 11 bathroom property sits along Lake Erie and comes equipped with a game room and a theater.

Despite the couple making it clear that they don’t want people getting too close, they haven’t caused any sort of commotion moving in, we’re told.

“The village is a relatively secluded lakefront property. Something may have been set up at that particular home which is their private estate, but in terms of any additional fencing for the village, I’m not aware of that,” veteran realtor in the area, Patricia Knight Gary of Howard Hanna Realty, exclusively told Page Six.

According to Knight Gary, there’s already a security presence to keep residents safe in the area, though with the celebrities moving in, more may be on the way.

“It does have a small police force that gauge and patrol the village on a regular basis,” she added. “And then even at the condos [down the street], there is 24 hour security, so I would anticipate that there might be some discussion in terms of increasing that.”

The Ohio home is just the latest on the growing list of places Swift and Kelce own real estate, and with their busy schedules, it sounds like it won’t be their main one.

“Right now, Travis’ Leawood [Kansas] home is his and Taylor’s primary residence and a big home base for them especially while he’s still playing for the Chiefs,” a source exclusively told Page Six earlier this month.

Swift also has home bases in Nashville, Rhode Island and Beverly Hills, as well as her Tribeca apartment, according to the source, “So they have plenty of options depending on where they need to be.”

While it remains to be seen just how much time Swift and Kelce will spend at their new home, Knight Gary shared the neighborhood is excited to see if they can catch a glimpse of the famous newlyweds.

“We found out publicly when everyone else did [about their move],” Knight Gary told us.

She added, “We’re waiting to see what impact this will have in terms of the additional traffic, additional people, and the village, but I think we certainly understand that this is not their full-time home either, so we have no way of knowing at this point just what the impact will be. It’s almost a wait and see, quite frankly.”

According to the real estate agent, properties in the area could very easily rise in price thanks to their new neighbors.

“I would think that certainly the residents and those who are selling would certainly have some hope that this might do something to increase values, but it is very difficult to gauge and anticipate it at this point.”
