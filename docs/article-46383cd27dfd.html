Meghan Markle’s estranged dad Thomas blasts child porn allegations as ‘sick lies’

2026-10-23 13:19

“I’m shocked, I’m hurt and I’m very upset that my son is making these accusations against me,” Thomas Sr. told the Daily Mail Friday, slamming the “sick lies.”

“I could never do what I’m being accused of. It’s the worst thing anyone could accuse me of,” he continued.

“This is bulls—t. It’s just a bunch of lies. It’s sick. I have no idea why Tom is trying to destroy me with these lies.”

As Page Six previously reported, the Cebu City Police Office in the Philippines is investigating Thomas Sr., 82, after child sex abuse material was allegedly discovered in his home.

According to The Sun, Thomas Jr., discovered “hundreds, if not thousands” of child sex abuse images in four boxes at his dad’s home in the Philippines, and reported the alleged findings to the police and the FBI.
