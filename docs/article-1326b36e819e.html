‘Below Deck’ star Chef Ben speaks out about Joao Franco’s fatal fight that left teen dead as reality star remains in Turkish jail

2026-11-17 15:36

“Below Deck: Mediterranean” star Chef Ben Robinson defended co-star João Franco after the latter was involved in a fatal fistfight in Turkey that left a teenager dead.

“I’m so sorry to hear this,” Robinson wrote in an Instagram comment Monday, reacting to Franco’s arrest earlier this month.

“Clearly, João was trying to de-escalate the problem,” the 45-year-old claimed. “He faced five aggressors upon him, and it was clearly self-defense.”

He added that it is “so unfortunate that this minor lost his life.”

João, 36, is currently behind bars in Turkey following his and brother Karlos Franco’s altercation with the employees of a clothing store in the Bodrum district of Muğla.

Page Six exclusively obtained CCTV footage of the brawl, which appeared to show João stepping in after his sibling threw a punch.

While João seemingly attempted to defuse the scuffle multiple times, he did strike a 17-year-old, whom local outlets reported had hit his head upon falling.

After sustaining a severe head injury and suffering cardiac arrest, the teen was revived. He later died at the Muğla Training and Research Hospital.

João and Karlos were taken to Bodrum Courthouse, where we hear they are “awaiting the next steps.”

Reps for the police, prosecutor and courthouse in Bodrum have yet to respond to Page Six’s requests for comment. Bravo reps declined to comment.

Robinson is not the only “Below Deck” personality to show support after João was taken into custody.

“Having seen the video footage, my impression is that João appeared to be trying to defend himself and de-escalate an already escalating situation,” Colin Macy-O’Toole told Page Six last week.

“From what is visible, it doesn’t look to me like he was looking to start a confrontation,” the 39-year-old continued. “It looks like he was reacting to what was happening around him.”

Macy-O’Toole urged others to “take the full context of the footage into consideration before jumping to conclusions or assigning blame.”

He concluded, “This is an incredibly tragic situation, and I hope the facts are allowed to come out before anyone rushes to judgment.”
