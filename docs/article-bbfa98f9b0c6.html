Costco quietly drops massive Disneyland ticket deal with major perks

2026-10-02 16:39

Costco has quietly rolled out a new Disneyland ticket deal for its faithful shoppers, just in time for the holidays. The new deal comes with a 2-day Park Hopper ticket, a Lightning Lane Multi Pass, and a $60 Disney dining card, all for $329.99.

The deal is valid from Oct. 2 through Dec. 18. Park tickets expire 13 days after the first day of use or on Dec. 18, 2026, whichever comes first.

To use the tickets, parkgoers must make a park reservation.

Parkgoers are saving at least $150 by taking this deal. A standard 2-day Park Hopper ticket with Lightning Lane which goes for $503 for adults and $483 for children.

The deal was spotted at California Costco warehouse locations and does not have a residency requirement to purchase.

Social media users sounded off on the deal across Reddit posts.

“This is probably the best deal you’re going to get during the holiday season unless they somehow surprise us with a different offer,” one Redditor wrote.

“I remember when this was the cost of an annual pass,” one commenter wrote.

“Dang that’s a good deal. That’s around the same price I spent when I went on Monday for two people,” a third wrote.

This isn’t the first time Costco has rolled out a Disneyland ticket deal.

In July of 2025, Costco offered a 3-Day Park Hopper with Lightning Lane Multi Pass and a $30 Disney Dining Card for $499.99.

Last November, they introduced a 2-Day Park Hopper with Lightning Lane Multi Pass for $389.99.

The deal comes as fans anticipate Disneyland and Disney World’s annual price increase. For the past several years, Disney has raised ticket prices each October to coincide with the start of the company’s fiscal year.

Last year, Disneyland raised prices for their most expensive tickets to $224 for a single day, single park ticket and $314 for a Park Hopper ticket.
