‘DWTS’ winner Ezra Frech was prepared to finish freestyle dance on one leg after finale snafu: ‘I blacked out’

2026-11-25 16:12

“I was like, ‘I’m just going to do the rest of the dance on one leg.’ Like, I’ll literally do the entire thing on one leg,” the two-time Paralympian told Variety after his victorious Season 35 finale.

Frech, 21, explained that his pro partner, Daniella Karagach, always taught him to keep dancing no matter what, so he was willing to do so despite the snafu.

“She’s drilled in me since the beginning that no matter what happens, we keep pushing through, you never give up and you fight for it,” he shared. “In this case, regardless of the circumstance, I was going to do whatever I can to fight for it.”

Although he was ready to take the risk, Karagach was able to hand Frech his prosthetic for him to put back on in a split second.

“Then I heard her say, ‘Put your leg on!’ And I was like, ‘Yes, OK. Good idea, I’ll put it on right now,'” Frech playfully recalled.

“Last night was crazy. We started our freestyle without the prosthetic, that was a creative choice. And then things went a little off script,” Frech told viewers.

“I wasn’t able to suction my leg on and I knew it wasn’t on. Sometimes when that happens, it will re-suction, but it didn’t, it came off. She told me to put it on mid-dance, I kind of blacked out, and then we ended up finishing the dance.”

Karagach, 33, echoed Frech’s previous sentiments that he would have “done the whole dance without his leg” if she hadn’t told him to put it on.

He put the prosthetic on later in the dance in a quick-change moment, but soon lost it after jumping down the stairs and onto the ballroom floor.

The track and field star handled the moment like a dance pro himself, and was able to flawlessly get his prosthetic leg back on and finish the routine.

The astonishing number earned Frech and Karagach a 30/30, bringing their finale score to a perfect 90/90.

The dancing duo went on to win the season, taking home the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy.
