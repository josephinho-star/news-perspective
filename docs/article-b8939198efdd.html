Turkish deputy governor dies after being found with gunshot wound

2026-10-09 12:13

İhsan Ayrancı, the deputy governor of Turkey’s western province of Afyonkarahisar, died Thursday after being found with a gunshot wound to the head in his car, the DHA news agency reported.

Ayrancı, 46, who also served as secretary-general of the provincial administration, was found in the parking lot of a thermal hotel in the city center. He received emergency treatment at the scene before being taken by ambulance to Afyonkarahisar Health Sciences University Hospital, where he died.

The governor’s office confirmed his death in a statement Thursday evening and said criminal and administrative investigations were underway. It did not explain the circumstances of the shooting.

The Cumhuriyet newspaper, citing the İhlas news agency, subsequently reported that Ayrancı had died by suicide. The governor’s office statement quoted in the report did not confirm that account.

Born in the Suluova district of Amasya province in 1980, Ayrancı graduated from İstanbul University’s faculty of political science. He worked in the private sector before entering public service in 2008.

His career included posts as district governor in Kale, Çaykara, Gölbaşı and Mut as well as deputy governor of Erzincan. He was appointed deputy governor of Afyonkarahisar in November 2024.
