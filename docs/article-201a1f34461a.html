Iceland Rejects Restarting EU Membership Talks

2026-08-30 12:44

Icelandic voters rejected restarting EU accession talks, with opposition particularly strong outside Reykjavik as concerns over sovereignty, fisheries and agriculture outweighed arguments for closer European ties.  RÚV Iceland News Defense & Security Correspondent Ingólfur Sigfússon is on Bloomberg This Weekend and says says the result puts membership talks on hold and could shift attention toward Norway as Nordic countries reassess their relationships with Europe amid geopolitical uncertainty. (Source: Bloomberg)
