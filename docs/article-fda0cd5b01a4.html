US journalist jailed for 2 years for working as a Chinese agent

2026-09-02 05:07

Thomas Weir Pauken II, who was arrested in February, had worked for state media in Beijing and pleaded guilty to spying for China

Thomas Weir Pauken II, 51, was also sentenced to three years of supervised release and barred from overseas travel during that period, according to the US Justice Department. He also used the alias Tom McGregor. His father, Thomas Pauken, chaired the Texas Republican Party in the 1990s and later ran for governor.

Pauken has been in custody since his arrest in February, and he admitted in June to acting as an agent of China in the US without notifying the attorney general, as required under federal law.

The bureau added that he recruited potential intelligence assets, issued taskings, sent reports to MSS contacts, received at least US$100,000, and made several trips from China to the US to meet people who could provide information to him, and ultimately to Chinese intelligence.

Assistant attorney general for national security John A. Eisenberg in a statement said Pauken had “betrayed his country for money” and “exploited his US citizenship and corresponding ability to travel to the US without restriction”.
