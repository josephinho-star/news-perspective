Taylor Swift cheers on husband Travis Kelce with Caitlin Clark at Chiefs-Chargers game

2026-10-18 22:01

Taylor Swift made the trip from LA to Missouri to support husband Travis Kelce as Chiefs take on the Los Angeles Chargers on Sunday.

She was shown, alongside Fever star Caitlin Clark, on the CBS broadcast cheering on the Chiefs tight end after a catch over the middle for a big game in the second quarter. The duo previously watched the Chiefs take on the Texans at Arrowhead Stadium in January 2025.

The Chiefs had a bye last week, and Kelce and Swift celebrated the All-Pro tight end’s 37th birthday in New York.

Swift sparkled in a custom Dior gown for her first appearance at the Academy Museum Gala, which raises money for the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures, on Saturday night.

The 14-time Grammy winner performed her 2025 song “The Fate of Ophelia” for the very first time, and her contender for the 2027 Oscars, “I Knew It, I Knew You,” from the Pixar film “Toy Story 5″ — as well as her hit, “Love Story” which was released in 2008, and 2014’s “Blank Space.”

She made an outfit change for her stage performance and donned a navy blue dress from Ralph Lauren Spring 27 runway, which included a smocked bodice, drop waist, and crystal-embellished pleated tulle.

Swift was not in attendance for the Chiefs’ divisional win over the Raiders, 30-27, last week after she made a surprise appearance on “Saturday Night Live” in New York and crashed host Dakota Johnson’s opening monologue.

She missed the Chiefs’ 24-10 road win against the Miami Dolphins to attend the MTV Video Music Awards, during which she was honored with the inaugural Artist Director Honors and Video of the Year for “The Fate of Ophelia” on Sept. 27.

Swift was in Kelce’s suite at Arrowhead Stadium when the Chiefs beat the Broncos 31-10 in Week 1, followed by a 33-30 overtime victory against the Colts in Week 2.

The pair were married on July 3 at Madison Square Garden in New York City, nearly a year after getting engaged.
