Cardinals fire DC Rallis; Austin takes over on interim basis

2026-11-23 23:08

TEMPE, Ariz. -- The Arizona Cardinals fired defensive coordinator Nick Rallis on Monday, first-year head coach Mike LaFleur announced on Monday.

The move came a day after the Cardinals gave up 34 points to the Kansas City Chiefs, the second straight game Arizona allowed 34 points and the eighth time in 11 games this season that the defense gave up 30 or more points.

Senior defensive assistant coach Teryl Austin will take over as interim defensive coordinator.

LaFleur said the decision to fire Rallis was his alone and that it just felt right to make it Monday morning.

"I just felt in my heart this morning that we needed a change," LaFleur said. "We needed a fresh perspective, a fresh voice, albeit the foundation of what T.A.'s going to do is not necessarily what he was able to put in starting in April, but he's been around this a long time. He's been around obviously successful defenses, a successful head coach and Mike Tomlin for a lot of years.

"So, just thought it was the right time starting this week."

LaFleur called the conversation with Rallis "tough" but that the decision to fire Rallis was "in the best interest for this organization. And it's obviously not been good enough collectively, all phases as a team, and so I'm in the business and it's my job to find solutions and this is one that we're trying to find."

LaFleur said his focus is on this week before he can decide whether Austin will be a candidate for the role full time.

Arizona is not making other changes on the defensive staff, LaFleur said.

After Sunday's 34-7 loss to Kansas City, LaFleur said he felt the Cardinals' defense could be better.

"I thought they had a good plan with some things to beat the pressure so that when that's the case, we got to be able to rush with four and cover," LaFleur said Sunday. "And I know we're capable of doing that better. I know we're capable of stopping the run better than we did particularly early. I mean they only finished with 118, but it kind of started early where they were getting some of those gashes and I though we honed in there toward the end, but it was too late."

The 33-year-old Rallis was hired by former Cardinals coach Jonathan Gannon to be Arizona's defensive coordinator in 2023 and was retained by LaFleur in February.

Multiple sources told ESPN that Arizona had attempted to move on from Rallis during the offseason. However, with LaFleur being the last head coach hired during the 2026 coaching cycle, Arizona's options were limited with whom to replace Rallis.

After considering their options, "Nick was the one that made the most sense," a source said.

This season, Rallis guided a defense that was ranked 32nd in points margin and points margin per game, 31st in opponent points per game and opponent points, 29th in opponent yards, and 28th in yard margin, yard margin per game, opponent turnovers.

The Cardinals' defense was also ranked last in the NFL in opponent QBR and 31st in raw QBR. It was 27th in run yards allowed per game, and 26th in yards per game and yards per play.

As a baseline, LaFleur hopes to spark better results with a different defensive coordinator in place.

"It's just bigger than that," LaFleur said. "We need to go execute -- 11 as one. And I think when you start getting the cracks a little bit within a game, you got to stop the bleeding somehow. We just haven't stopped the bleeding enough. A lot of that has to do with, again, these guys want to do right, but when there is those cracks and you're trying to do a little bit more than maybe your job and you love that out of these guys that they're trying to make the play. But again, in this league, the margins are so small that if you go outside of what your job requirement is on any given play, that's where the breakdowns can happen."

Rallis' defense has struggled to stop offenses from scoring going back to last season.

Since Week 10 of last season, which was the first in a nine-game losing streak to end the season, the Cardinals' defense was ranked 32nd in opponent points, opponent points per game, point margin, point margin per game and opponent yards; was 29th in opponent yards per game; and 28th in yards margin and yards margin per game.

Arizona gave up 30 or more points in 14 of their last 20 games.

"Obviously these last three weeks have not been good collectively," LaFleur said. "Obviously we haven't held our end of the bargain on offense. I don't need to hear excuses of why. We just haven't. So yeah, I wouldn't say that. It just felt right today."

The Cardinals, winless (0-4) at home under LaFleur, play host to the Washington Commanders on Sunday at 4:25 p.m. ET.
