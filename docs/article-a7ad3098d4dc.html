‘Dutton Ranch’ Season 2 Release Date Info: When Does The Second Season Of ‘Dutton Ranch’ Come Out?

2026-09-20 14:00

Every week since Taylor Sheridan’s hit Yellowstone spin-off Dutton Ranch aired its intense Season 1 finale, fans have been begging for more Dutton drama. So is this finally the week that Dutton Ranch returns?!

Dutton Ranch Season 1 finale spoilers ahead.

When we last left Beth (Kelly Reilly) and Rip (Cole Hauser) in Episode 9, “El Padrino,” they were fearing for Carter (Finn Little), who was kidnapped by the cartel. Meanwhile, Beulah (Annette Bening) and Oreana (Natalie Alyn Lind) were grieving after Rob-Will’s (Jai Courtney) death. And Joaquin (Juan Pablo Raba) — who we’re led to believe killed his brother — was reckoning with the aftermath of his dad Mariano’s (Raoul Max Trujillo) return to Rio Paloma, Texas.

Dutton Ranch Season 1 left fans with a slew of burning questions about Sheriff Wade’s (Josh Stewart) next move, the true intentions of Miguel (Berto Colón), a possible Dutton Ranch and Marshals crossover episode, and more.

Naturally, at the top of every fan’s list of questions is still: “Will there be a Season 2 of Dutton Ranch?” and “When does Dutton Ranch return with new episodes? So we’re sharing the latest updates!

Curious if Dutton Ranch is new this weekend? What’s Dutton Ranch Season 2’s renewal status? When is Dutton Ranch Season 2’s release date? When does Marshals Season 2 come out? Will there be a Dutton Ranch and Marshals crossover? And where can you watch Dutton Ranch online? We’ve got answers. Here’s what to know about the future of Dutton Ranch.

Is Dutton Ranch New This Weekend?

Sadly, no. There’s no new episode of Dutton Ranch on Paramount+ this weekend. But if you head to CBS at 10:00 p.m. ET, you can watch a rerun of Luke Grimes’ Yellowstone spinoff Marshals to get your weekly dose of Dutton drama.

Is There A Dutton Ranch Episode 10? How Many Episodes Are In Dutton Ranch?

Season 1 of Dutton Ranch consists of nine episodes. Not eight episodes, not ten episodes, only nine! That means Dutton Ranch Episode 9, “El Padrino,” served as the Season 1 finale. So fans will have to wait for Season 2 to learn what the future holds for Beth, Rip, Carter, the Jacksons, and the rest of the Rio Paloma, Texas crew.

Will There Be A Season 2 Of Dutton Ranch? Dutton Ranch Season 2 Renewal Status:

You bet! On Wednesday, June 24, ahead of Dutton Ranch Episode 8, Paramount+ officially renewed the series! And it’s no surprise!

The series has a Certified Fresh 90% Tomatometer rating on Rotten Tomatoes, and according to Paramount+ press release, “Dutton Ranch cemented itself as a smash-hit by amassing 12.9 million viewers globally in the 7 days following its premiere — marking the biggest Original Series launch in Paramount+ history. The series also delivered 2.9 million total viewers during its two-episode debut on Paramount Network on May 15, making it the biggest series premiere on cable since 2023. With 1.85 million viewers for the premiere telecast alone, Dutton Ranch also ranked as the #1 cable entertainment telecast among both adults 18-49 and total viewers.”

“Dutton Ranch has resonated with audiences in a powerful way, building on the legacy of beloved characters, while establishing its own identity,” said Matt Thunell, President of Paramount Television Studios. “Thank you to our partners at Paramount+ and 101 Studios for a phenomenal first season. We look forward to bringing fans more stories from these unforgettable characters in Season 2.”

When Does The Second Season Of Dutton Ranch Come Out? Dutton Ranch Season 2 Release Date Info:

There’s no official Dutton Ranch Season 2 premiere date on the books yet, but The Hollywood Reporter confirmed that production is expected to begin next year.

“We’re slated to start early 2027 to film,” Reilly told THR in July. “But the room starts up pretty quick. I think we’re going to be very much involved in the room, so the next sort of six months is all about writing it.”

Assuming production sticks to the predicted timeline, here’s hoping Dutton Ranch Season 2 will premiere in 2027. Stay tuned for more updates!

Is Cole Hauser Leaving Dutton Ranch? Who Is Dutton Ranch‘s New Showrunner?

These are still popular questions from Dutton Ranch fans — especially after recent feud escalation rumors between Hauser and Sheridan — and though the two have a long history, as far as we know, Rip isn’t leaving Dutton Ranch, nor is Cole Hauser! The Yellowstone spin-off was specifically written to continue Beth, Rip, and Carter’s stories — plus, Hauser is an EP on the series. So we don’t expect to say goodbye to Beth’s partner in crime any time soon.

When Dutton Ranch returns, however, it will have a new showrunner in Benjamin Cavell, who joined the team ahead of Season 2. Season 1 showrunner Chad Feehan departed from the series just three weeks before its premiere.

When Does Season 2 Of Marshals Come Out?

While Dutton Ranch doesn’t have a Season 2 premiere date yet, Sheridan’s other Yellowstone spin-off Marshals does! Curious when you can see Luke Grimes/Kayce Dutton back on your screen in new episodes? Marshals Season 2 premieres Sunday, October 4, 20256 on CBS, with streaming on Paramount+. Here’s everything to know about Marshals Season 2.

Will There Be A Dutton Ranch And Marshals Crossover?

Grimes confirmed that we won’t see Beth and Rip on Marshals when the show returns for Season 2 this fall. That said, with Dutton Ranch expected to film Season 2 in 2027, there’s still hope that Kayce could appear on Marshals‘ sister series — especially after Grimes’ recent comments about a Dutton sibling reunion.

“It’s not out of the question that we might have some sort of crossover at some point. Beth’s basically Kayce’s only living family member other than Tate. It would make sense that down the line we’d at least talk,” Grimes told TV Insider. “[Kelly and I have] texted about it back and forth a lot, talked about how it’d be fun and necessary for our story arcs, however they’re going to end.”

The promising comments came just months after Reilly and Hauser shared their own positive sentiments, so if you’re in line for a Marshals and Dutton Ranch crossover episode, stay in line!

What Is Dutton Ranch Streaming On? Paramount+ Streaming Info:

While we wait for Season 2 news, if you’re looking to rewatch Dutton Ranch Season 1 it will be available to stream on Paramount+.

Paramount+ offers two subscription plans, Essential and Premium. Right now, you can get started with a seven-day free trial on the Premium plan.

The ad-supported Essential plan typically costs $8.99/month, while the ad-free Premium plan (which comes with Showtime titles and live CBS) costs $13.99/month.

While Dutton Ranch is a Paramount+ original series, you can Dutton Ranch on other streaming platforms like Hulu and Prime Video by adding Paramount+ for $13.99/month. Both streamers offer free trials for new subscribers.
