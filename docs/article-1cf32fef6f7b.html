TikTok star Bryce Hall arrested for felony domestic violence

2026-10-14 14:09

TikTok star Bryce Hall was arrested and charged following a domestic dispute in Las Vegas.

The influencer is accused of using or threatening physical force against someone in a domestic dispute, according to legal documents obtained by TMZ.

A source told the outlet that the alleged victim, Hall’s now ex-girlfriend Maria Palafox, has “documented” the injuries she claimed the social media star allegedly inflicted during their dispute on July 27.

Charges weren’t filed until Aug. 28, and Hall was detained until a judge issued an arrest warrant on Sept. 10.

Hall, 27, is facing two charges: felony coercion involving domestic violence and misdemeanor domestic battery.

He has been released from custody without posting bail and was ordered to stay away from Palafox, who goes by Mika Lafuente on social media.

A rep for Hall denied the allegations, telling TMZ, “These allegations are false, and we look forward to the facts coming out in court.

“We will be taking legal action against those responsible for spreading false and defamatory claims,” the spokesperson added.

Page Six has reached out to both Hall and Palafox for comment but did not immediately hear back.

The alleged domestic violence incident took place two days before Hall took to social media to announce he and Palafox split after two years of dating.

“I’m not the best at this because I don’t do this often but me and Mika broke up,” he said in the video.

“It’s not a joke. We are no longer a couple. She just moved out of my house. Now I have this big ass f–king crib to myself, which is kind of scary at night,” he added.

Hall said the breakup “wasn’t pretty” but said he “wished her the best.”

“Let’s move on. I’m only gonna shade her if she f–ks another dude. I’m just kidding,” the YouTuber concluded.
