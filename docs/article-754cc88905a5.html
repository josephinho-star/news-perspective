Kallas says Hormuz diplomacy has produced ‘no results’

2026-09-01 11:35

EU top diplomat Kaja Kallas said Tuesday that diplomatic efforts to defuse the crisis in the Strait of Hormuz had produced “no results at the moment.”

Speaking to reporters in Wicklow, Ireland, ahead of an informal meeting of EU defense ministers, Kallas said that “the freedom of navigation needs to be respected,” insisting ships should not be charged to use previously open routes.

“For us it is important that there are no tolls, no fees, for these routes that have been open before,” she added. Ministers will also discuss protecting critical undersea infrastructure.
