Palestinian woman shot dead by Israeli settlers near Jericho, witnesses say

2026-11-17 20:21

A 72-year-old Palestinian woman has been shot dead in an overnight attack by Israeli settlers in a village near Jericho where settlers have recently moved in after taking over several Palestinian homes.

Fadha Wadha was standing outside her home in al-Duyuk al-Tahta in the occupied West Bank when she was struck in the head by a bullet as Israeli settlers fired towards Palestinian houses, according to testimonies from residents. Several other Palestinians, including her son, were wounded by gunfire.

Abu Younis, a cousin of Wadha’s husband, told the Guardian that the violence had begun when a group of settlers attacked a young boy they accused of entering a courtyard seized from its Palestinian residents.

Dozens of Israeli settlers, some of them armed, moved into eight buildings in al-Duyuk al-Tahta two weeks ago with the backing of the settler organisation Nachala and a regional council representing illegal settlements in the central West Bank.

“A vehicle carrying settlers chased him,” Younis said. “There were about 15 settlers in several vehicles. They began firing indiscriminately, killing the woman.”

Witnesses said Palestinian residents began to throw stones to defend themselves, as more settlers arrived at the scene, shooting at civilians and injuring six men.

Wadha’s son, Arafat Fidhat, was wounded after he rushed to the scene after learning that his mother had been shot. His wife, Naama Fidhat, told Reuters he was in shock when he accidentally collided with a parked car belonging to one of the settlers.

Witnesses said the vehicle was empty at the time, but that settlers responded by opening fire, wounding Arafat in the torso.

Naama Fidhat said her husband was being taken to a hospital in Ramallah when Israeli soldiers stopped the ambulance and arrested him. She said Israeli soldiers also arrested their son, Wadha’s grandson, early the following morning.

“What did we do? We didn’t do anything,” she said. “This is our house, they were the ones who came with weapons … we were unarmed, we were sleeping.”

The Israeli military said a group of Palestinians in a car “accelerated their vehicle toward an Israeli vehicle and rammed it”. It said the Palestinians exited the vehicle and attacked the Israelis, who opened fire at the Palestinians. “There are Israeli and Palestinian casualties in the incident,” the military said.

The arrival of the Israeli settlers in al-Duyuk al-Tahta had raised fears among Palestinian residents who have faced repeated attacks in the area.

The Binyamin regional council, which covers 47 illegal settlements, claimed the properties in the village had been bought from Palestinians, but Jericho’s governor, Hussein Hamayel, disputed the claim, saying the land belonged to the state and could not legally be sold.

Human rights activists said settlers had assaulted and robbed two Israeli activists and shot a Palestinian man in the leg during the takeover earlier this month.

“Since the settlers moved into the area on 4 November our lives have been turned upside down,” Younis told the Guardian. “We used to live peacefully and safely on our farms, but since the settlers arrived, our lives have become a living hell. I own 53 dunams [5.3 hectares] of farmland with greenhouses, but we are no longer allowed to access them. We have suffered devastating financial losses, and now people are being killed as well.”

There has been a sharp rise in attacks by Israeli settlers on Palestinians in the West Bank, territory Israel captured in the 1967 six-day war. UN bodies and most countries deem the settlements illegal, which Israel disputes.

On Tuesday, Suleiman Abu Srour, a photographer for the Palestinian news agency Wafa, was beaten by Israeli soldiers while covering events in al-Duyuk al-Tahta. Wafa said he suffered fractures and bruises, was detained for six hours and had his phone confiscated before being released.

Since the 7 October 2023 Hamas-led attacks on Israel, there has been a surge of Israeli settlement expansion, ethnic cleansing and violence against Palestinians in the occupied West Bank. More than 1,100 Palestinians have been killed there by Israeli forces or settlers.
