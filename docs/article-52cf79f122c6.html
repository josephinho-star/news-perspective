Will There Be a ‘Lioness’ Season 4? Everything We Know About ‘Lioness’ Season 4

2026-09-20 15:00

Wow. The Season 3 finale of Lioness certainly didn’t disappoint. We’ll keep this article spoiler-free, but if you haven’t watched, we highly suggest making your way to Paramount+ to stream the final installment of the season.

After you pick your jaw off the floor, make sure to read Johnny Loftus’s Lioness finale recap coverage on Decider.

Now that the dust has settled, where do we go from here, Lioness fam? Has Taylor Sheridan’s beloved spy thriller been renewed for a fourth season? Will there be a Season 4 of Lioness on Paramount+?

Your next mission, if you choose to accept it, is to keep reading. Here’s everything we know about Lioness Season 4.

When Does The Lioness Season 3 Finale Drop on Paramount+?

The Season 3 finale debuted Sunday, September 20 at 3:01 a.m. ET. It’s now streaming on Paramount+.

How Many Episodes Are There In Lioness Season 3?

Lioness Season 3 consists of eight episodes.

Has Lioness Been Renewed For Season 4?

As of now… no. There hasn’t been an official Season 4 renewal. But the season just ended, so I wouldn’t be too worried about it.

Will There Be a Season 4 of Lioness?

The series hasn’t officially been renewed for Season 4, but based on the events of the finale, we fully expect that Lioness will be back for a fourth season. And we have some additional info to back that up.

A few weeks ago, writer Michael Idov posted on Instagram that he and his wife have joined the series as writers and supervising producers. The timing of the announcement has led many to believe that the duo will be working on a yet-to-be-announced fourth season. Series star Jill Wagner also added to the Season 4 hype during a recent interview with ScreenRant.

“We’re going to get a Season 4,” Wagner said. “When people see [The Season 3 Finale] what [Taylor Sheridan] wrote for the finale, we are getting a Season 4, or I will pay you $100.”

Lioness Season 3 Streaming Info:

Lioness streams exclusively on Paramount+.

Paramount+ offers two subscription plans, Essential and Premium. Right now, you can get started with a seven-day free trial on the Premium plan.

The ad-supported Essential plan typically costs $8.99/month, while the ad-free Premium plan (which comes with Showtime titles and live CBS) costs $13.99/month.

How To Watch Lioness For Free:

You can add Paramount+ to your Prime Video or Hulu account for an additional $13.99/month. The best part? Both streamers offer free trials!

Amazon offers both Paramount+ Essential ($8.99/month, no free trial) and Paramount+ Premium ($13.99/month and a seven-day free trial). You can also subscribe to Paramount+ through YouTube ($10.99/month for the first three months), which offers a free trial.

The season finale of Lioness is now streaming on Paramount+.
