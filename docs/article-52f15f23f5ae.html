Teen busted in West Indian Day Parade shooting is alleged attempted murderer let out on bail by lefty judge 1 month ago

2026-09-08 17:21

An accused shooter tied to Monday’s West Indian Day Parade violence had been out on bail at the time — because a lefty judge ignored prosectors’ pleas to lock him up over an attempted-slay rap just a month ago.

Nayshawn Wilkinson, 19, allegedly shot a 24-year-old man in the butt near the corner of St. Johns Place and Nostrand Avenue near the parade route Monday afternoon. It was not clear if the victim was his intended target, but the struck man has no prior arrests, law-enforcement sources said.

Wilkinson was on the streets despite being busted for a previous shooting only a month ago.

That’s when the teen menace appeared before Brooklyn Criminal Court Judge Janice Robinson for allegedly firing a gun twice at a man in front of the Callahan-Kelly playground in Brownsville on July 22, according to the criminal complaint.

The victim in that shooting suffered a bullet wound to the foot, which required surgery.

A 22-caliber bullet fragment was recovered from the victim’s foot, prosecutors said at the time.

Wilkinson was charged with attempted murder, assault and gun charges in the case — and prosecutors begged the judge to remand him.

The reputed soft-on-crime judge instead granted the alleged gunman $5,000 bail or $10,000 bond Aug. 4 — and he posted it, freeing him to allegedly strike again.

Wilkinson is awaiting arraignment in court Tuesday.

He is charged with attempted murder and other charges over the parade shooting.
