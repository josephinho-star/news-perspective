Jon Lovitz responds to Crystal Hefner after scathing Playboy ‘perv’ claims: ‘It’s a compliment’

2026-10-28 17:32

Jon Lovitz is taking Crystal Hefner’s “perv” claims about him as a badge of honor.

The comedian had a smug answer when asked what he thinks of Hugh Hefner’s widow’s recent claim that he was the Playboy Mansion’s “No. 1 customer.”

“Any time tells me I’m No. 1, it’s a compliment,” he said as he left a Los Angeles restaurant Tuesday night, per a video obtained by Page Six.

When asked if he “lurked around” the mansion, the actor shrugged off the claim.

“Hugh Hefner was one of the nicest, most generous people I’ve ever met and all of his friends know it,” he concluded. “That’s all I have to say about that.”

Hugh Hefner’s widow dubbed Lovitz, 69, “one of the biggest pervs” in Hollywood during Tuesday’s episode of the “Lisa Guerrero Unleashed” podcast.

“He would be up there all the time,” Crystal said of Lovitz at the mansion. “His eyes would go front to back [looking at] all the women that walked through the door at the party.”

She also labeled the “Saturday Night Live” alum a “lurker” whose dog “peed all over the place.”

“I remember him. Every time, every party,” she insisted.

“They used to call it Jack Nicholson’s drive-thru at one point because he would drive up, grab some food or whatever else he could get there and leave,” she said during an episode of her “After the Mansion” show.

Crystal, 40, who married the Playboy founder in 2012, claimed that her late husband was obsessed with rubbing elbows with Hollywood’s biggest stars.

“Any celebrity was treated extremely well whenever they visited. He would have Bill Maher for chats, hang with Berry Gordy of Motown and then you’d see plenty of other people there. Buzz Aldrin, the original Catwoman.”

Crystal claimed that the late magazine publisher raped her throughout their relationship and that she allegedly witnessed him force other playmates to perform sexual acts they weren’t comfortable with.

The couple remained married until he died from cardiac arrest and respiratory failure at the age of 91 in September 2017.

Since his death, she has been outspoken against her late husband, especially in her 2024 memoir, “Only Say Good Things: Surviving Playboy and Finding Myself,” in which she alleges Hugh attempted to control her by telling her to lose weight and what to wear.

Crystal has since moved on romantically, tying the knot with marine biologist James Ward back in April.
