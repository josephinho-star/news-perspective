Media industry still has a problem with sexual abuse, UK culture minister says

2026-11-08 18:35

Following comedian Russell Brand’s rape conviction last week, Lisa Nandy said it ‘is time for the media industry to take this seriously’

Brand’s conviction on Thursday renewed scrutiny of how Britain’s entertainment industry has treated well-known performers accused of abusing their power, with some women saying they feared speaking out could harm their careers.

Asked whether there was still a problem with exploitation and sexual abuse in the media and entertainment industries, Nandy told the BBC: “Yes, I do, and I think hand on heart I can’t say to you as the culture secretary that this is not happening today and in fact, if I had to wager, I would say it is.”
