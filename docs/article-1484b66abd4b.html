Hollywood makeup artists love this hydrating serum— and it has thousands of five-star reviews

2026-09-29 21:26

Hollywood beauty pros know great makeup starts long before foundation.

And when prepping their famous clients, multiple celebrity makeup artists have reached for Beauty Pie’s Triple Hyaluronic Acid & Lipopeptide Serum for a hydrated, camera-ready base.

The formula contains three molecular weights of hyaluronic acid to approach hydration from multiple angles; higher-weight molecules help create an immediately plumper appearance at the surface, while medium- and lower-weight forms support hydration deeper within the skin.

The result? A lightweight, fragrance-free formula that delivers a dewy look — without leaving behind the sticky finish often associated with hyaluronic acid.

Its ingredient mix caught the attention of Hollywood makeup artist Tonya Brewer, who layered the serum under Kate Hudson’s ’60s-inspired makeup for the 2026 Actor Awards.

“Kate looked absolutely perfect, and I love collaborating with Beauty Pie,” Brewer said in a press release.

She’s not alone in her love for the brand, as Reese Witherspoon’s makeup artist, Tracey Levy, used the same serum on her iconic client for the “Elle” premiere this summer.

The product’s appeal also extends beyond the red carpet. Beauty Pie says independent four-week panel testing found that 88% of participants reported healthier- and smoother-looking skin, 87% reported revitalized-looking skin and 82% reported firmer-looking skin.

What’s more, customer reviews similarly highlight its fast absorption and non-sticky texture, calling it things like “very impressive” and a “holy grail.” (On Beauty Pie’s site alone, it has over 3,900 five-star ratings.)

For best results, apply two to three pumps to clean skin morning or evening, ideally while skin is slightly damp, then follow with moisturizer. It can also be layered beneath sunscreen and makeup without separation or creasing.

The brand also makes a corresponding Beauty Pie Triple Hyaluronic Acid Elastic Lifting Eye Serum, which Brewer used for Hudson’s Actor Awards glam.

It’s designed to target dark circles and puffiness — and based on the over 1,200 five-star reviews on the brand’s site, it’s also a brand favorite.
