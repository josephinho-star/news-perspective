GTA 6 fans go wild for game’s un-PC moments from searing ‘white guilt’ radio ad to blue-haired protester slayings

2026-11-19 19:49

Despite internet incel outcry over Grand Theft Auto VI removing grisly features like the option to kill sex workers and focusing on a romantic storyline, some viral clips from the game suggest it’s a far cry from politically correct.

At last, the gaming gods have delivered, releasing GTA VIat midnight on Thursday to fanfare, praise, and sharp criticism.

The latest iteration follows criminal duo Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos through the fictional state of Leonida (a Florida-inspired state) and Vice City.

The game includes five chapters and 91 missions, each laced with the signature GTA holy trinity of expletive-rich dialogue, violence, and fraught interpersonal relationships.

And while players can no longer pay for sex, there is no shortage of blood-soaked satire.

Questionable content includes, but is not limited to, elder abuse, white guilt radio ads, eyebrow-raising OnlyFans references, the execution of endangered species, and animal liberation operations going seriously, and grievously, sideways.

In one viral clip, which has already amassed upwards of 8 million views, an animal rights activist is viciously mauled by a polar bear.

In the footage, a woman tries to soothe the animal whom she and her cohorts have rescued from the back of a box truck, calling her a “sweet sister of the North.”

As the polar bear attacks, she is heard saying, “It’s ok; physical pain is nothing compared to the pain of oppression.”

Underscoring that there is neither order, mercy, nor a species hierarchy in the GTA universe, the polar bear is subsequently killed by a speeding car.

“Damn, I’m going to buy this game just for this scene alone,” said one commenter.

“This is such a metaphor,” surmised another.

“Suicidal empathy in action,” said a third.

Another commenter illustrated the irony of the discourse surrounding the new game.

“Republicans: GTA 6 is so woke for not letting you buy prostitutes or shoot gay bars. Also, Republicans: GTA 6 totally owned the libs by having a bear maul them!”

In another clip, a truck is seen arriving at the shoreline where a lei-wearing activist liberates a captive orca by riding it into open water, only to have the whale hurl her into the air and thrash her to death.

“Just your daily reminder that they’re called KILLER whales for a reason,” said one commenter.

“All right, I was kinda starting to become anti-GTA6, but it’s stuff like this that kind of pulls me back in and makes me a little less upset about other things,” said a presumed convert.

For reference, tigers are considered an endangered species on the brink of extinction.

Aside from interspecies violence, the game also includes some shocking scenes of human-on-human warfare, notably the chance to shove an elderly thief out of her motorized wheelchair.

Moreover, in another jaw-dropping move, GTA also features an unhinged radio ad marketed to “liberal men suffering from white guilt.”

“Do women think you’re a vanilla villain?” the radio voiceover asks.

“Here at Brownie Points, we know that sometimes all you need is validation from the margin. We have black people nearby right now just waiting to acknowledge you in public.”

The ad promised to deliver “a range of urbanness from respected community leaders,” including a guaranteed “fist bump from a person of color.”

Those who want to upgrade to the “brownie points reparations package” can opt in to “have a black dude call you the N-word straight to your face.”

“Show this to people who call this game woke,” said one commenter.

“I can’t even tell who it’s making fun of at this point,” said another.

Someone blatantly unfamiliar with satire and reality posted, “Love that they admit white men are marginalized.”

Another commenter surmised that GTA is where democracy truly shines, “People arguing whether the game is right or left wing is getting boring. GTA has always made fun of both sides.”

An additional radio ad seemingly takes a satirical swipe at OnlyFans, urging women to “sell their bodies, not their souls” via the fictionalized “Peekhole” platform, which helps children in need…through nudity.

“Just imagine, if you’re hot enough, you could make enough money to rebuild little Miguelito School in Nicaragua,” the ad announces.

“Unironically, this could work,” said one commenter.

Ahead of Thursday’s drop, so many GTA fans—one in five American workers—warned they plan to skip work Thursday to hole up with the new game that economists project will cost US businesses $1 billion in lost productivity.

Despite, or because of, the controversy it has instigated, GTA VI is also on track to become the highest-reviewed and top-grossing video game of all time.
