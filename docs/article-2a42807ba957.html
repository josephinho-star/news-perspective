Chargers join exclusive club with win over Ravens that Eagles fans won’t be happy about

2026-11-17 05:35

The Chargers not only picked up their first win of the season, they did it on Monday Night Football.

With a 23-20 victory against the Ravens, the Chargers no longer have the longest active losing streak in the NFL, and have passed the bill to the Eagles.

The Eagles are riding a seven-game losing streak and have not won a game since their Week 2 24-20 overtime victory against the Tennessee Titans.

According to ESPN Insights, the Chargers became the second 0-8 team to enter a game on Monday Night and win. The last team to do it was in 2013 when the Tampa Bay Buccaneers beat the Miami Dolphins 22-19.

Los Angeles’ first win of the season came from an all-around team effort.

The Chargers offense, which has struggled all season, scored on its first offensive possession when quarterback Justin Herbert marched the team downfield, running six plays and capitalizing it with a 22-yard touchdown run.

The Chargers scored their second touchdown of the night during the final two minutes of the second quarter, a 1-yard run by Omarion Hampton to make it 16-7.

Hampton was the Chargers’ leading rusher, running the ball 22 times for 105 yards, while the offense finished with 160 yards on the ground.

Los Angeles piled on six more points from the legs of Cameron Dicker.

Herbert completed 12 of 18 passes for 129 yards and had his first game of the season in which he didn’t give up a turnover.

The Chargers defense held Baltimore to a total of 287 yards and forced two turnovers, including a forced fumble on Zay Flowers during the final two minutes of the fourth quarter, which helped run down the clock.

Baltimore got the ball back one more time but started on its own 20-yard line and was only able to run two plays before the game ended.
