Gruesome shark attack details emerge after failed migrant smuggling attempt in California Coast

2026-10-20 02:38

Mexican nationals involved in failed migrant smuggling attempt off the California coast suffered injuries consistent with shark bites, according to officials.

Sixteen of the 27 people were rescued after a boat capsized off the California coast early Friday morning fought off sharks in open waters and many were bitten.

“Sixteen people have been rescued and transported to Coast Guard Sector San Diego, where emergency medical services personnel transferred them to local hospitals for further medical evaluation. Two of the sixteen individuals rescued were confirmed to be Mexican nationals,” the United States Coast Guard (USCG) said on Friday.

At least one person was being treated for a suspected shark bite and two others were in critical condition.

The survivors were found after a call came in from a boat around 1 a.m. on Friday after the vessel capsized about 140 miles west of Ensenada, Mexico.

USCG also suggested that the ship may have been involved in a maritime smuggling operation.

“The vessel had a large number of people onboard and was following a route normally used in illegal maritime smuggling operations. Offshore conditions are unforgivable and can quickly result in death,” said Captain Robert Tucker, commander, Coast Guard Sector San Diego.

A San Diego City spokesperson also told NBC San Diego that a human smuggling boat was at the scene.

The Friday incident came after a series of smuggling operations off the Southern California coast occurred.
