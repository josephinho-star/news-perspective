LightStream stops accepting loan applications. Try these 6 lenders instead

2026-10-20 13:59

LightStream is no longer accepting new applications. Explore other options in our personal loan marketplace.

Online lender LightStream is no longer accepting applications for new loans.

In its third-quarter earnings call on Oct. 16, the New York City-based Truist Financial Corporation, operating as Truist Bank, announced it had agreed to sell $7.7 billion in LightStream unsecured loans, essentially the entire book, to Carlyle Group, a private equity firm.

LightStream, the online consumer lending division of Truist Bank, launched in 2013 and focused on fixed-rate unsecured personal loans, especially for borrowers with good-to-excellent credit. LightStream approved financing ranging from $5,000 to $100,000 and marketed loans for a variety of purposes, including vehicle financing, debt consolidation and home improvement.

"The sale of LightStream represents an exit from a non-core loan-only business that is generating returns well below our targets," Truist CFO Mike Maguire said on the call. "Exiting LightStream will also improve our credit risk, liquidity, and funding profiles."

So what changes for those who have LightStream loans? A note on the company's website indicates clients with an active LightStream loan can still sign in to manage their account. Beyond that, CNBC Select was able to confirm the following: "The LightStream origination platform will be retired," according to Truist spokesperson Catherine S. Matawaran, who added that Truist will no longer offer LightStream loans.

Matawaran did note Truist's ongoing commitment to issuing unsecured personal loans and debt consolidation services. "We'll offer these loans through our client-facing channels, which include 844-4TRUIST (844-487-8478) as well as branch locations," she says, directing questions regarding future plans to Carlyle.

A representative for the Carlyle Group told CNBC Select the sale would not change any terms for existing borrowers, saying "an orderly servicing transition is in place to ensure a smooth process."

While LightStream is no longer accepting applicants, you still have options for personal loans.

LightStream was known for offering borrowers with good credit flexible terms, fast funding and no upfront fees. While it is no longer offering loans, you can still look to these lenders for financing.

We like that SoFi has high loan caps and doesn't charge any origination fee, so borrowers get the full amount funded. The credit requirements can be stringent, however, and interest rates for weaker applicants are on the higher side.

We like that Citi combines a low maximum fixed APR with no origination or late fees. But higher loan limits and faster funding are reserved for existing Citi customers.

Its minimum loan amount is $1,000, making it a good choice if you don't need to borrow a lot.
