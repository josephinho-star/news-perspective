Atlanta Falcons are dealing with the worst quarterback situation in the NFL and it isn't even close

2026-09-20 21:52

The worst quarterback situation in the NFL right now? The Atlanta Falcons.

And it isn't very close at all.

The Falcons on Sunday started third-stringer Cooper Rush for the second time this season while Michael Penix Jr. and Tua Tagovailoa were designated as inactive due to ongoing health issues.

Penix Jr. has been recovering from knee surgery following an ACL injury last season.

And Tagovailoa was out for the second consecutive week after he suffered an unexpected oblique injury two weeks ago, this time in practice. Although no one touched him.

Anyway, Rush got the call and he promptly stunk.

Again.

On Atlanta's opening possession, Rush lost the ball on a fumble, which the Panthers recovered at the Atlanta 35-yard line. Carolina converted the turnover into a touchdown.

Rush threw an interception on the following series, then added a second one later in the game.

He completed 10 of 17 passes against the Panthers, which doesn't sound terrible except that you read the previous paragraphs that outlined Rush's, ahem, struggles with turnovers.

This followed a Week 1 performance against the Pittsburgh Steelers in which Rush also threw two interceptions.

"Obviously, extremely disappointed in that game and extremely disappointed in how that game started," Falcons coach Kevin Stefanski said. "And then [we] could not crawl out of that hole. And in the second half, it got away from us. So very disappointed.

"As you guys know, giveaways and takeaways are the key to this game and to turn it over that many times ... there's no way to win in the NFL like that."

Stefanski made those feelings known before he ever stepped up to the post-game microphones. Because he benched Rush before the end of the third quarter.

The coach had seen enough. Except the poor quarterback play was far from over.

Stefanski inserted fourth-string quarterback Jack Strand.

Strand threw an interception on his first pass attempt. And it wasn't just any interception. Strand threw a pick six when Devin Lloyd returned his pass 16 yards for a score.

This is Keystone Cops type QB play. The Falcons are used to QB issues, but this is something else again.

The Falcons are hoping that can end by Thursday when they play the Green Bay Packers.

They're hoping Penix Jr., who has been practicing and ramping up toward game action, can start that in Wisconsin. They hope that Tagovailoa will ultimately decide he can tolerate some pain and take the field, or receive an injection to help him do so.

The Falcons are doing a lot of hoping about their quarterback room. And so far, it hasn't paid off.
