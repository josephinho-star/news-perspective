Demi Lovato celebrates 34th birthday in colorful string bikini during Bahamas getaway

2026-08-28 21:04

Demi Lovato is keeping her birthday celebration going in the Bahamas.

The 34-year-old singer kept the birthday fun going Thursday, sharing new shots of herself riding a jet ski in a colorful bikini against the bright blue Bahamas water.

Lovato opened the Instagram carousel with a close-up selfie taken while sitting on a red jet ski.

She wore a red, black and yellow patterned string bikini with dark sunglasses and small hoop earrings. Her dark hair fell loosely around her shoulders as she leaned toward the camera for the selfie.

"Hi," Lovato captioned the post.

"Bye!" she added.

A second photo showed the singer from farther away on the same jet ski, giving a wider look at the colorful swimsuit and surrounding water.

Fans quickly flooded the comments with praise for Lovato's vacation photos.

One fan referenced Lovato’s 2015 hit, writing, "She’s really cool for the summer."

"You’re prettier than the prettiest water I’ve ever seen!" another added.

One commenter also pointed out how the look coordinated with the jet ski, writing, "The suit matching the jet ski 🙂↕️"

The new photos came just days after Lovato shared more highlights from the same Bahamas getaway.

"Someone’s gotta hold it down in the bahamas," she captioned the earlier post.

In one of those photos, Lovato stood in shallow water wearing a bright red string bikini with both arms raised above her head.

She smiled as the clear water surrounded her, while the tropical shoreline could be seen in the distance.

Another image captured Lovato floating on her back in the water in the same red bikini.

The singer stretched out across the surface with her arms relaxed at her sides as the blue water filled the frame.

Lovato turned 34 on Aug. 20.

The birthday getaway comes during a celebratory year for the singer, who recently marked her first wedding anniversary with husband Jordan "Jutes" Lutes.

Lovato and Lutes married in California in May 2025 after first going public with their relationship in 2022.

Earlier this month, Lovato opened up about what she has learned during her first year of marriage.

"When you’re able to communicate with each other what you need, what you want, it creates a safe space for the two of you to share your feelings with one another," Lovato told People at the Aug. 10 premiere of "Camp Rock 3."

"My love for Jordan grows every single day, and I’ve never been so happy in my life. So I think communication is a big part of that," she added.

The singer also recently returned to one of the roles that helped launch her career.

Lovato reprised her role as Mitchie Torres in "Camp Rock 3" and served as an executive producer on the film alongside the Jonas Brothers.

The original "Camp Rock" premiered in 2008, followed by "Camp Rock 2: The Final Jam" in 2010.

Lovato's Disney roots stretch back to her childhood, when she appeared on "Barney & Friends." She later broke out as Mitchie Torres in "Camp Rock" before starring in her own Disney Channel series, "Sonny with a Chance."

Those projects helped launch Lovato's acting and music career, making her return to "Camp Rock" nearly two decades later a full-circle moment.
