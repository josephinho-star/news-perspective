The best Advent calendars you’ll actually want to open in 2026 are selling fast

2026-10-16 18:23

The countdown to Christmas is even more exciting when every December morning brings a little surprise.

Our guide to the best Advent calendars 2026 has something for every kind of festive fan, from classic treats to luxurious beauty products and thoughtful gifts. Whether you’re shopping for yourself or looking for a fun way to build anticipation for Christmas, the right calendar can make the holiday season feel even more special.

- Best Kids Advent Calendar: Lego 2026 City Advent Calendar

- Best for Makeup Lovers: Benefit Cosmetics The Hotel Beneville

- Best Chocolate Advent Calendar: Vosges Haut-Chocolat 2026 Advent Calendar

- Best Wine Advent Calendar: Total Wine 2026 Advent Calendar

- Best Bathtime Advent Calendar: Lush 29 1/2 High Street Advent Calendar

For traditionalists, a chocolate Advent calendar is a timeless choice, while a beauty Advent calendar offers the chance to discover skincare, makeup and pampering treats throughout December. If you’re shopping for younger family members, a kids’ Advent calendar can turn the countdown into a daily moment of fun with toys, crafts, sweets or other age-appropriate surprises.

And it’s not just children who get to join in. An adult Advent calendar can be filled with everything from gourmet goodies and drinks to candles, self-care treats and collectible gifts. We’ve rounded up the standout options to help you find the perfect calendar, whether you want something indulgent, playful, practical or simply guaranteed to put a smile on your face each morning.

Best Beauty Advent Calendar: Anthropologie The 24 Days of Beauty Advent Calendar

Beauty lovers get 24 little reasons to look forward to December, with premium-size treats spanning skincare, makeup, haircare, fragrance and more. Standouts include Sunday Riley Good Genes, Verb Ghost Oil, Supergoop!, Boy Smells and Living Proof, so there’s plenty to discover from some seriously good beauty names.

The whimsical 2026 packaging, illustrated by Nephthys Foster, makes the whole thing feel like opening a tiny present every morning. With more than $300 worth of products inside, it’s a particularly fun pick for anyone who loves trying a little bit of everything.

Best Chocolate Advent Calendar: Vosges Haut-Chocolat 2026 Advent Calendar

Chocolate countdowns don’t get much more decadent than Vosges Haut-Chocolat’s 2026 edition, tucked inside the fantastical Grand Cacao Lodge. Behind the doors is a mix of dark and milk chocolates, chewy caramels, toffee, beloved favorites and brand-new creations.

And because 24 days apparently aren’t enough, there’s a special chocolate-filled moment waiting on Day 25. Consider it a holiday village where every tiny door leads to something delicious.

Another Chocolate Advent Calendar: Compartés Enchanted Castle Chocolate Advent Calendar

This chocolate calendar feels like a holiday fairy tale, complete with a snowy enchanted kingdom, towering castle, and a dragon lurking among the pines. Each door reveals a Compartés chocolate inspired by festive flavors such as buttered croissant, eggnog crème brûlée, pistachio marzipan and salted honeycomb.

The result is equal parts whimsical and indulgent, with enough variety to keep every day interesting. It’s basically a tiny chocolate adventure with a very yummy ending.

Most Unique for the Coffee and Skincare Lover: Beekman 1802 The Kindness Cafe Advent Calendar

The Beekman 1802 The Kindness Cafe Advent Calendar is a delightful way to count down to Christmas with 24 days of pampering and festive surprises. Designed like an adorable, dollhouse-inspired cafe, this charming calendar features a collection of Beekman 1802 favorites and new discoveries, including skincare, body creams, hand creams, lip balms, soaps and moisturizing mists powered by the brand’s signature goat milk ingredients.

With 12 full-size products, a retail value of $410 and a collectible Barista Goatie figurine complete with miniature café accessories, it offers a wonderful blend of everyday self-care and holiday *whimsy.* The reusable cafe design makes it a keepsake long after the holidays, while the variety of products makes it an especially thoughtful gift for beauty lovers or anyone who deserves a little extra kindness and indulgence during the season.

Best Kids Advent Calendar: Lego 2026 City Advent Calendar

Lego turns the countdown to Christmas into a miniature trip to the North Pole with its 2026 City Advent Calendar. Behind the doors are tiny builds and minifigure fun, including Santa and Mrs. Claus, elves, a snowman, and an adorable baby reindeer.

Piece by piece, the calendar creates a festive little scene that can be played with long after Christmas morning. It’s a great choice for kids — or grown-up Lego fans who still get excited about tiny builds.

Best for the Skincare Beginner: Clarins 24-Day Beauty Advent Calendar Gift Set

The Clarins 24-Day Beauty Advent Calendar Gift Set is a luxurious way to make the countdown to Christmas feel extra special, with 24 beauty surprises featuring the brand’s beloved skincare, body care and makeup favorites. Valued at $374 and priced at $155, this limited-edition collection includes four full-size essentials alongside a variety of travel-size treats, making it a wonderful opportunity to discover new favorites and enjoy familiar bestsellers.

Highlights include the Hydra-Essentiel Moisturizer, Lip Comfort Oil, Wonder Volume Mascara XXL and hydrating lip balm, along with nourishing body lotions, refreshing cleansers, radiance-boosting serums, eye care and makeup essentials.

Best Luxury Advent Calendar: Voluspa 12-Day Holiday Village Advent Calendar

For a countdown that smells as good as it looks, Voluspa fills 12 days with petite pedestal candles from its beloved Japonica and Holiday collections. Fragrances include festive favorites such as Crushed Candy Cane, Noble Fir Garland, Gilt Pomander & Hinoki, Sparkling Cuvée and Forbidden Fig.

Each candle comes in a decorative glass vessel and offers around 25 hours of burn time, while a gold-plated wick trimmer adds an extra luxe touch. The whole set arrives in a beautifully decorated holiday box that’s almost as giftable as what’s inside.

Best for the Fragrance Lover: Dedcool Advent Calendar

For fragrance enthusiasts who love discovering something new, the DedCool Advent Calendar offers a fresh and unexpected take on the traditional holiday countdown. Behind its 24 doors is an assortment of the brand’s signature scents and lifestyle essentials, ranging from fragrance and body care to laundry products and home fragrance.

Rather than focusing solely on perfume, this collection brings DedCool’s distinctive scent experience into everyday routines, making even the simplest moments feel a little more luxurious. So, if you’re hoping to find a new favorite fragrance, experiment with different scent profiles or give your self-care routine a seasonal refresh, there’s plenty to explore throughout December.

Best Foodie Advent Calendar: Bonne Maman 2026 Advent Calendar

Bonne Maman brings a little French-inspired sweetness to the holiday countdown with 24 miniature jars of jams and spreads. The 2026 assortment includes festive limited-edition flavors like Caramel with Salted Butter, Strawberry with Madagascar Vanilla and Raspberry with Dark Chocolate.

Each tiny box holds a different flavor, making breakfast, toast, pastries or midnight snacking considerably more exciting. Even the packaging gets a second life; the little boxes can be reused for stocking stuffers, place settings or tiny treasures.

Best Value: Liberty Beauty 2026 Advent Calendar

Liberty’s 2026 Beauty Advent Calendar is a serious beauty haul, with 29 hand-picked Beauty Hall favorites, including 21 full-size products — at a $1,700 value for $395. Expect an impressive mix of skincare, fragrance, makeup, haircare and body care from names such as Augustinus Bader, Sunday Riley, Sisley Paris, ILIA, Violette_FR and Trinny London.

There’s even a Liberty-print drawstring bag tucked into the experience, plus the chance to find a Golden Ticket. With a collectible illustrated box and an enormous assortment of beauty treats, this one is made for the person who considers opening a skincare delivery an event.

Best Wine Advent Calendar: Total Wine 2026 Advent Calendar

Wine enthusiasts can count down with 24 individually selected wines sourced from around the world. The assortment covers a broad mix of reds, whites, rosé, sparkling and lighter styles, with bottles such as Pinot Noir, Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, Cabernet Sauvignon and Bordeaux making appearances.

It’s a fun way to sample different varietals without committing to a full-size bottle of every discovery. Think of it as a 24-day tasting tour that happens to fit neatly into a holiday calendar.

Best Advent Calendar for the Chef: Williams Sonoma Spice Advent Calendar

Spice up the holidays — literally — with Williams Sonoma’s countdown featuring a collection of globally inspired seasonings. The calendar includes blends such as za’atar with thyme, sumac and sesame; paella seasoning with paprika and turmeric; and a cozy speculoos blend featuring cinnamon, nutmeg, clove, anise and ginger.

Every little packet offers a new excuse to experiment in the kitchen, from weeknight dinners to holiday baking. It’s especially fun for the cook who believes a pinch of the right spice can transform everything.

Best Bathtime Advent Calendar: Lush 29 1/2 High Street Advent Calendar

Lush takes inspiration from its famous 29½ High Street home for a calendar packed with bath-time treats. Alongside holiday favorites such as Sugar Plum Fairy, candy cane and reindeer-themed goodies, the collection includes six exclusives created especially for the countdown.

Expect playful bath bombs and bubble bars, including a carrot bath bomb and a mini Swirly Sticky Dates bubble bar, plus favorites like Unicorn Horn, Luna’s Butterfly and Sleepy. It’s essentially 25-ish days of turning your bathroom into a very festive spa.

Best Skincare Advent Calendar: Augustinus Bader The 12 Days of Bader

This is the Advent calendar for the skincare devotee who likes their holiday treats decidedly luxurious. The 12-day collection brings together Augustinus Bader favorites across cleansing, moisturizing, serums, body care, and more, including The Cream Cleansing Gel, The Essence, The Rich Cream, The Face Oil, Eye Patches and The Body Cream.

The 2026 assortment also includes newer additions such as Vitamin C Serum, The Elixir, Mineral Sunscreen SPF 50 and Hand Treatment. Twelve doors, one very high-end skincare routine.

Best Advent Calendar for Cheese Lovers: Williams Sonoma 12 Days of Cheese

Forget chocolate: Williams Sonoma’s 12 Days of Cheese is for anyone who would happily build an entire personality around a cheese board. Each day brings another cheese made by a family-operated Utah creamery using milk from a fourth-generation dairy. The assortment ranges beyond everyday cheddar and Muenster, with flavors incorporating ingredients such as wildflower honey, organic bergamot and cayenne pepper.

Pair the whole countdown with crackers, fruit and a very serious charcuterie board, and Christmas practically plans itself.

Best for the Spice King or Queen: Fly By Jing 2026 12-Day Advent Calendar

Fly By Jing brings a fiery twist to the holiday countdown with 12 Sichuan-inspired flavor surprises. Inside, expect a mix of chili crisp, noodles, Mala Spice Mix, a brand-new hot sauce, limited-edition goodies and other surprises the brand is keeping under wraps.

The 2026 edition is completely redesigned with a brand-new assortment, including products you won’t typically find elsewhere. It’s spicy, playful and a lot more exciting than another box of chocolates.

Best for the Type-A Guy or Gal: Papier 24-Day Advent Calendar

Papier’s 24-day countdown is a stationery lover’s dream, with a drawer full of useful little treats rather than throwaway trinkets. Inside are rollerball pens, fineliners, highlighters, notebooks, notepads, journals, sticky tabs, cards, stickers, paper clips and other desk-friendly surprises.

Some days bring practical supplies, while others reveal more unexpected goodies like a mini jigsaw puzzle or playing cards. Best of all, the beautiful drawer-style box can be reused long after the last door is opened.

Best for the Art Lover: The Met Museum Favorites Advent Countdown

The Met turns the holiday countdown into a miniature museum-shopping experience with its Museum Favorites Advent Countdown. Each day reveals a different surprise inspired by the museum’s collections, with the dates intentionally randomized so you never quite know what you’ll open next.

Among the featured designs are collectible Art Deco-inspired shoe ornaments based on the work of Erté. It’s an especially charming choice for anyone who likes their holiday décor with a little history, art and New York flair.

Best for the Crafter: Uncommon Goods Craft Project Advent Calendar

This 24-day calendar is essentially a tiny holiday craft studio hiding behind 24 doors. Each day brings an art supply, stationery surprise, or creative project, with activities ranging from watercolor painting and coloring to paper crafts and other festive projects. The instructions are beginner-friendly, so you don’t need an art-school degree — or even particularly impressive crafting skills — to join in.

Once everything has been opened, the gingerbread-inspired calendar transforms into a cheerful village display for the shelf.

Best for the Dry Skin Gal: Elemis The Ultimate Beauty Advent Calendar

Elemis packs 25 days of skincare indulgence into its 2026 Ultimate Beauty Advent Calendar, with a collection worth more than $947. The lineup includes three full-size heroes — Pro-Collagen Cleansing Balm, Pro-Collagen Marine Cream and Frangipani Monoi Body Cream — alongside cleansers, serums, masks, oils, exfoliators, moisturizers and body care.

There are also multiple versions of the brand’s beloved Pro-Collagen Cleansing Balm, plus Dynamic Resurfacing and Superfood favorites. And for an extra bit of holiday magic, 10 calendars contain a Golden Ticket worth a $1,000 ELEMIS shopping spree.

Best for Makeup Lovers: Benefit Cosmetics The Hotel Beneville

Benefit checks you into the Hotel Beneville for a 24-day stay filled with makeup favorites and beauty surprises. The lineup includes full-size icons such as BADgal BANG! mascara, 24-HR Brow Setter, Benetint and Cookie Golden Pearl Highlighter, alongside minis and fun-size versions of Hoola, POREfessional, Roller Lash, Gimme Brow+, They’re Real! and more.

There are even brow products, an under-eye mask, hair clips and a mirror tucked among the beauty treats. Basically, it’s a hotel where every room has a makeup product waiting inside.
