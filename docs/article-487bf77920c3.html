My first ‘nice’ rug came from Wayfair, and this 50%-off find has my attention

2026-10-23 13:28

Way Day is finally here, and boy, are there a lot of good deals to be found.

One of our favorite Wayfair finds is rugs. Seriously, my first-ever “nice” rug was from Wayfair, and it lasted me three years before I finally replaced it. Fun fact, it was also an on-sale Way Day buy.

Wayfair has tons of great colors and patterns (as well as sizes) on rugs to choose from. One that’s currently seeing a big discount is the Magnolia Home by Joanna Gaines x Loloi Banks rug, down to 50% off for a limited time in the 7’6″ x 9’6″ size.

This unique distressed rug has a bluish-brown hue and design, which the brand compares to oceans and spices. It’s a low-pile option and sits closer to the ground, but with an added rug pad, you can get both extra cushioning and grip to keep your rug from slipping around your floors.

The brand says the rug is also machine washable and stain-resistant, meaning it’s built to last for years. I only ever spot-cleaned my previous rug, and it lasted just fine.

If you’re looking for a standout rug that isn’t too loud with its design but is still sleek enough to elevate your living room, the Magnolia Home by Joanna Gaines x Loloi Banks rectangular rug is a great pick.
