Macklemore announces ‘Free Palestine’ music tour after Ed Sheeran debacle

2026-09-24 14:54

Macklemore announced that he and special guests will be traveling overseas for the “Free Palestine Tour” after his controversy with Ed Sheeran.

On Thursday, the “Thrift Shop” rapper took to Instagram to share that the tour will hit Dublin on Oct. 26, Paris on Oct. 29 and conclude with a stop in London on Oct. 30.

Dates for shows in the United States and “artist announcements” will be released soon.

“If these last couple of weeks have shown us anything, it’s that something has shifted over the last three years,” Macklemore, born Benjamin Haggerty, captioned his post.

“There has been a collective awakening around Palestinian liberation, the power of billionaires that try to censor that call, and what we expect from artists right now,” he added.

The “Glorious” emcee, 43, then appeared to take shots at Sheeran, who attempted to stay neutral on the war in Gaza.

“It’s 2026. Being apolitical during a genocide doesn’t work anymore,” Macklemore penned.

“People are asking artists to show up, educate themselves and be in community. To use the stage for more than entertainment. To push back when the call for a Free Palestine is met with attempts to silence it,” he shared.

“What happened to me is bigger than one artist or one stage. When the billionaire class can decide which voices are allowed on stage and which ones are too dangerous to hear, everyone should be paying attention.”

Macklemore said that since his “schedule freed up a bit,” he has been contacting “artists who have shown up and used their stages to stand in solidarity with the Palestinian people, regardless of the consequences.”

“Because of how quickly all of this has come together, a lot of the conversations, schedules and details are still being worked out,” he wrote.

“But this is a moment of momentum, and there is work to be done,” the three-time Grammy winner added.
