Elgato takes on DJI with a tiny wireless mic for creators on the go

2026-10-13 13:00

Elgato already dominates the market for hardware built for creators that use powerful desktop PCs, and it’s now turning its attention to creators on the go with its first wireless microphone. The $99.99 Wave Go clips onto your clothes just like DJI’s Mic Mini, with hands-free recording for vlogging and content creation.

The Wave Go has ‘broadcast-quality’ audio and a USB-C receiver designed for mobile.

The Wave Go has ‘broadcast-quality’ audio and a USB-C receiver designed for mobile.

The Wave Go is tiny, about the size of an AirPod, so it disappears on camera and only weighs seven grams. Despite the tiny size, Elgato is promising “broadcast-quality” audio, with a condenser capsule that supports EQ shift, auto gain, and noise cancellation.

The Wave Go clips onto clothes with a magnetic clip and plate, and a single button lets you power on the microphone or mute it. You can also double-press the button to launch the camera app on your phone.

Elgato has also created a Smart Handle for the Wave Go, a bundled accessory that turns it into a full-size interview mic. The Wave Go simply slots into the top of the Smart Handle, which has a power and mute button with an LED indicator for power, mute, and pairing status. The handle also acts as a charger for the Wave Go, adding five more charges, which at around eight hours per charge gives you 48 hours in total. You can also quick-charge in 10 minutes to get around an hour of recording time.

The USB-C receiver is made for phones, so it works with iPhones, Android devices, and iPads. It connects to the microphone over a 2.4GHz wireless signal, so there’s no Bluetooth here, and the latency is around 24ms or less. The Smart Handle, mic, and USB-C receiver can all be secured inside the included case, but the case doesn’t have an integrated battery, so the Smart Handle is the main way to charge on the go.

Elgato has also created a Wave Go mobile app that lets you monitor live audio levels, enable noise reduction, toggle automatic leveling, and check battery status. You can even use the Wave Go on a desktop PC with Elgato’s Wave Link 3.0 software, simply by plugging the receiver into a USB-C port.

The Wave Go is available today priced at $99.99 (€99.99 / £99.99), and a second mic transmitter is available for $29.99 if you want to record a second voice.
