Seahawks vs. Raiders prediction, props: Don’t expect a shootout in Fernando Mendoza’s first start

2026-11-15 17:00

It will be trial by fire for Fernando Mendoza when the No. 1 overall pick gets his first start against a ruthless Seahawks defense.

Following the trade of Kirk Cousins to Minnesota at Tuesday’s NFL trade deadline, Mendoza inherits an offense that ranks 31st in rushing yards per game (71.3) and last in yards per carry (2.89), leaving little ground-game support.

The Seahawks, who are laying 6.5 points, are allowing just 4.1 yards per play, the league’s lowest mark by a substantial 0.6-yard margin, while surrendering only 5.5 yards per pass attempt.

The total has already dropped three points from its opening number of 43.5.

Seahawks vs. Raiders prediction, best bet

The jury will be out on Mendoza, but he would have to make a stark impact with an offense that managed just 240 total yards and 24 rushing yards in last week’s 38-17 loss to San Francisco.

Las Vegas committed five turnovers in that defeat, including two in the 49ers’ end zone, and possessed the ball for just 21:42.

Of course, there’s always Brock Bowers, who is a matchup-proof receiving threat, having recorded at least 70 yards in seven consecutive games, but Seattle has the personnel to limit his impact.

Defensive back Nick Emmanwori held Arizona tight end Trey McBride without a reception in their individual coverage matchups last week.

Seattle’s offense presents its own limitations.

Sam Darnold remains sidelined with rib and quad injuries, leaving Drew Lock to start despite dealing with elbow and oblique issues.

Seattle should nevertheless find rushing opportunities against a Raiders defense allowing an NFL-worst 151.2 yards per game and 5.21 yards per carry.
