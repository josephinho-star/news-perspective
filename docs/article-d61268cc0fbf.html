Where to watch Rams vs. 49ers for free live from Australia: Time, streaming

2026-09-10 20:30

NFL history will be made tonight.

The second game of the 2026-27 NFL season between the Los Angeles Rams and San Francisco 49ers will be the league’s first in Australia. It marks the fourth continent the league has touched down on, after international debuts in Europe in 2007 and South America in 2024.

There’s been much talk of travel plans as the matchup drew closer, as the 49ers opted to fly down under a week before the game while the Rams left Los Angeles on Wednesday and touched down in Australia on Thursday so that players’ bodies wouldn’t feel the repercussions of jet lag.

Watch Rams vs. 49ers for free

- When: Sept. 10, 5:35 p.m. PT / 8:35 p.m. ET

- Where: Melbourne Cricket Ground (Melbourne, Australia)

- How to watch for free: Netflix via DIRECTV free promo

- TV Channel: streaming exclusive

The first NFL game in Australia will be an exciting divisional clash between NFC West rivals with the Rams entering the season as Super Bowl favorites.

They begin this season making more history with the first reigning MVP and Defensive Player of the Year combination ever on the same roster after 2025-26 DPOY Myles Garrett was traded to the Rams in June 2026 and MVP Matthew Stafford signed a contract extension in May to stay with the Rams.

Rams vs. 49ers start time

The Rams vs. 49ers game is scheduled to begin at 5:35 p.m. PT / 8:35 p.m. ET tonight, Sept. 10 (which is 10:35 a.m. on Friday, Sept. 11 in Australia).

How to watch Rams vs. 49ers for free

Tonight’s Rams vs. 49ers matchup is exclusive to Netflix, but with a new offer from DIRECTV, you’ll be able to stream everything on Netflix free for an entire year.
