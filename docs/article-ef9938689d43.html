DHS denounces TX officials for allegedly planning to end cooperation with ICE

2026-11-18 19:23

FIRST ON FOX: Secretary of Homeland Security Markwayne Mullin denounced Texas officials who announced plans to end local law enforcement's cooperation with Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) in an exclusive statement to Fox News.

Following the sweeping blue wave that saw Democrats take virtually every statewide office on the ballot on Nov. 3, incoming Democratic officials across Texas announced plans to end or severely limit their respective law enforcement offices' cooperation with ICE.

Most notably, Houston Mayor John Whitmire issued a Nov. 6 executive order telling the city's police officers that they are no longer permitted to notify ICE if they encounter an illegal immigrant during a nonviolent encounter.

Shortly after Whitmire's order, the mayors of Austin and San Antonio also issued executive orders limiting city police cooperation with ICE.

"If Texas were to stop cooperating with ICE and become a sanctuary state, it would be one of the worst derelictions of duty we’ve seen in a very long time," Mullin told Fox exclusively.

"Recent illegal alien arrests in Texas include murderers, child sex traffickers, terrorists, gang members, sexual assailants, and drug traffickers. Texas has long been a great partner with DHS in the fight to arrest and remove illegal aliens from our communities, so that we can save lives and make America safe again," Mullin continued.

"We are calling on Governor-elect Hinojosa and other leaders throughout the state to continue cooperating with us. If they do not, then crime and violence will plague the Lone Star State, and innocent Americans will suffer as more sanctuary politicians put illegal alien criminals over their own constituents. It’s no coincidence that 7 of the 10 safest cities in America cooperate with ICE law enforcement," he concluded.

Mullin also referred Fox to a long list of illegal immigrants ICE has removed from Texas after they committed crimes as severe as murder, child sex trafficking, manufacturing and trafficking drugs and even plotting an ISIS-linked terror attack on U.S. soil.

Giannys Alexandra Ramirez-Fernandez and Nelson Adrian Perez-Martinez, both illegal immigrants from Venezuela, were arrested in San Antonio and sentenced to 12 and 20 years in prison, respectively. The pair were convicted on charges of conspiracy to traffic a child and transportation of a minor with intent to engage in criminal sexual activity, after a Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) probe in San Antonio.

Another Texas illegal immigrant, Isaias Jose Rodriguez-Manzanares, was deported back to Honduras in June after DHS and ICE determined that he was affiliated with drug trafficking cartels and with the notorious MS-13 gang.

Officers from the Irving Police Department arrested Rodriguez-Manzanares in 2024 for a liquor violation, but, despite his known and admitted gang affiliation, authorities working under former President Joe Biden took no action to remove him from the country, according to DHS.

He was arrested again in 2025 for manufacturing or distributing a controlled substance and unlawfully carrying a weapon and deported in July 2026.

In September, ICE also deported Nazira Haji Zada, an illegal immigrant from Afghanistan who had been living in Fort Worth, Texas. Zada admitted to authorities being involved in a plot to commit an ISIS-inspired mass shooting on Election Day in 2024.

DHS removed her from the U.S. in September and made her permanently inadmissible to the U.S. in the first-ever case before the United States Alien Terrorist Removal Court (ATRC).
