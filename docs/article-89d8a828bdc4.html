Jake Ferguson’s wife Haley Cavinder’s cheeky ‘Kiss my Dallas’ outfit backfires after Cowboys embarrassing loss

2026-10-09 17:08

Haley Cavinder-Ferguson’s outfit for the Dallas Cowboys’ Thursday Night Football game against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers surely seemed like a good idea at the time.

But the outfit has not aged well, considering the Cowboys’ embarrassing 24-16 loss to the previously winless Bucs.

Cavinder-Ferguson, the influencer wife of Cowboys tight end Jake Ferguson, wore a navy long-sleeve top with matching pants that had feathered trim running down the outer sides for Thursday’s game.

Across the back of the pants, it read, “Kiss my Dallas.”

Cavinder posted a video of her wearing the outfit to her Instagram on Thursday with the caption, “kiss my dallA$$$$$$”.

Ferguson did not have a good game on Thursday. He finished the loss with three catches for 19 yards, and had two dropped passes that ended up being interceptions for the Buccaneers.

Cowboys fans are extremely frustrated with their tight end’s performance, and Cavinder’s outfit for the game aging as poorly as it has isn’t going to make them feel any better.

Dallas is now 2-3 on the season. Their offense isn’t usually the source of their troubles but was a problem when facing the Buccaneers at home for whatever reason.

Ferguson and the rest of Cowboys’ offense will look to get back on track against the Green Bay Packers next week. Cavinder-Ferguson will want to think wisely about the outfit she wears for that game.
