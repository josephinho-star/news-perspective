Raiders get a viral Kirk Cousins moment — but little else against Rams

2026-10-25 22:45

The NFL’s hot mic strikes again.

During the Raiders vs. Rams game at Allegiant Stadium on Sunday, quarterback Kirk Cousins fell victim to the hot mic near the end of the first quarter.

As Cousins pitched the ball to rookie running back Mike Washington, Cousins was heard immediately shouting, “GO! GO! GO! GO! GO!”

The NFL posted the clip of Cousins’ viral moment on X, and it was instantly jumped on by users who were waiting for the next Cousins soundbite.

In the comments section of the post, NFL fans were seen gathering and reacting with hilarious responses.

“the zoomies hit him hard,” @ZidaneZ08902030 wrote.

@ArmChairLogan wrote “this is the best part of Sundays,” referring to the broadcast catching another hilarious moment.

“There goes that man!” @jedwhelchel wrote on X.

While the live broadcast gave fans something to laugh at, Raiders fans might feel the other way, as they are in the middle of the third quarter, trailing the Rams 28-6, and on their way to their fourth straight loss.

The Raiders offense has yet to score a touchdown. They have only put together 133 yards of offense, the Rams have 241.

Cousins has completed 17 of 27 passes for 125 yards, while Washington Jr. is the team’s leading rusher, having 21 yards on four carries.
