Crowd erupts as Vance smacks down protester waving Mexican flag: 'Get your a-- over there'

2026-09-11 02:19

A protestor in the crowd at the Republican National Committee midterm convention throws a Mexican flag as U.S. Vice President JD Vance delivers his remarks on the second day of the event in Dallas, Texas.

NEWYou can now listen to Fox News articles!

Vice President JD Vance blasted a heckler waving foreign flags and yelling at him from the GOP convention floor during his primetime keynote address Thursday night.

The rabble-rouser emerged from the crowd waving Mexican and Palestinian flags, catching Vance's eye as he delivered his rallying cry to thousands of GOP voters at the American Airlines Center in Dallas. The incident wasn't captured by the convention's official television feed, but Vance described it for viewers at home.

"What the people at home missed is that we just had a protester interrupting us and waving the Mexican flag.," he said. "Well, my friend, if you love Mexico so much, get your a– over there. I'll buy you the plane ticket."

The crowd erupted into chants of "USA! USA! USA!" as the troublemaker was escorted out of the event.

Vance delivered a deeply nationalistic keynote focused on America First principles.

Vance used the opportunity to outline his vision for rebuilding the nuclear family – taking shots at Democrats. "I think about what the Democrats have said about our shared birthright as Americans, or frankly, the lack thereof in Michigan. One Democrat in particular, one candidate has said, and I quote ‘f the nuclear family.’ Should ask the nuclear family, what are you even talking about? These hateful words come not from a love of country," said Vance. "They come not from a desire to serve you or our fellow Americans, but they come from a very deep emptiness in the soul."
