Moment entire California lifeguard tower is washed into the ocean by massive swells

2026-11-28 00:20

A lifeguard tower in Laguna Beach was washed away into the Pacific Ocean as massive swells from king tides continue to pound the coastline.

The powerful waves blasted the tower fixed to the rocks that are located along the coast in the Southern California community.

In the footage from KTLA, the tower stands tall before it is completely ripped off its base by the destructive waves, leaving only shreds of the lookout post.

A clip posted on Facebook by Laguna Beach Unfiltered showed what was left of the tower on the rocks as the swell pounded it over and over again.

The only part that remained was the concrete base at the foot of the stairs down to the beach.

It comes after the city warned about the massive high tides that were coming to the area.

“King tides peak this week in Laguna Beach, with the highest tide of 7.0 ft on Wednesday at 8:33 AM,” a post on social media read.

“Surf is expected to peak Wednesday afternoon through Thursday morning, with waves forecast at 4 to 5 ft Wednesday and 5 to 7 ft Thursday.”

“Coastal flooding is possible during high tides. The Aliso Beach parking lot will likely see water during high tides and will stay closed to vehicles through the holiday weekend,” it added.

Officials also warned visitors to stay off the sea walls and rocks and to keep pets and kids far from the water’s edge.

People commented on the video posted online, noting the incredible power of the ocean.

“It’s amazing how the ocean can just eat up the things that humans make and swallow them into the sea,” one person wrote.

The destruction of the tower comes after part of the Pacifica Municipal Pier was washed away in the Bay Area.

Dramatic video captured the exact moment the large section of the pier fell into the ocean, as giant waves battered it.

The pier has been shut down indefinitely, according to ABC7. The city closed and repaired the pier back in June when large cracks were found in the structure.

The pier’s collapse and tower wash-out come as monster waves pound the California coast during a disastrous El Niño season.
