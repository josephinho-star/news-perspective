What Taylor Swift and Tom Cruise chatted about during Travis Kelce’s NFL win: lip reader

2026-09-15 17:37

Taylor Swift and Tom Cruise were spotted deep in conversation as they cheered on her husband, Travis Kelce, and the Kansas City Chiefs at Monday’s game.

Although what Cruise said next was somewhat indecipherable, the lip reader claims he told Swift, “You may not know it, but you do have a job.”

Swift then put her drink down, and allegedly said “right” while nodding calmly.

Following Swift’s nod, the lip reader claimed the “Mission: Impossible” star added, “I told her to stick to one and that it’s the last time,”

Swift nodded again, though it is unclear who Cruise was referring to.
