Bo Derek stands by falling for married John Derek at 16 despite 30-year age gap: 'It all worked out'

2026-11-21 18:01

Bo Derek believes "it all worked out" when it comes to her marriage to John Derek — the director who was 46 and married when she met him as a 16-year-old up-and-coming actress.

She spoke about their relationship — as did John's former wife, "Dynasty" star Linda Evans — in a new profile for The Hollywood Reporter.

John, best known for appearing alongside Charlton Heston in "The Ten Commandments," had shifted into filmmaking later in his career, and in the early 1970s, he'd set out to cast a movie he'd written titled "Fantasies" about a relationship between a teenage girl and her adoptive brother.

Bo, who went by her birth name, Mary Cathleen Collins, at the time, auditioned and was given the part. The film was shot in Greece, but John asked Evans to give Bo acting lessons before they departed.

As Evans recalled, Bo was "beautifully independent," and she had "zero, zero, zero" suspicions that anything romantic might develop between the teen and her then-husband.

"I was with him 25 years until he died. He was a very ridiculously honest, good man. And it all worked out, didn’t it?"

Bo herself told the outlet that her relationship with John and the age gap they shared is "not the natural way of things," admitting, "If it were my daughter, I would have kidnapped her and taken her away from him, of course."

She added, "At the same time, I was with him 25 years until he died. He was a very ridiculously honest, good man. And it all worked out, didn’t it?"

It was 1973 when Bo left with John for Europe to shoot the film. A few weeks later, Evans traveled to visit John on the set, which was when she'd learned that he'd fallen for Bo.

"To say I was devastated is an understatement," she said, but she maintained that Bo wasn't to blame for what happened.

"She didn’t break up my marriage," she insisted. "She did not go after him — he went after her."

THR wrote that Evans "doesn't hold much resentment for John, either," with the actress saying, "When he loved you, he was consumed with you."

She later added, "It was a favor they both did to me, because I ended up having an extraordinary life going off on my own."

And Bo, as she said, went on to have a long relationship with John that only ended when he died in 1998.

In a 1980 interview with the Washington Post, John said, "I had everything I wanted in Linda, so why I became interested in Bo I don't know. At that time she couldn't hold a candle to Linda, but something about her got me involved. She's an interesting girl, Bo is."

He said at the time that he was on good terms with Evans, and that she was the one who researched the legalities of returning to the U.S. with Bo – after wrapping up the film in Greece, they stayed in Germany for some time, because, as he explained, "In the United States you have statutory rape and the Mann Act to contend with."

"It was definitely more acceptable there," Bo admitted to The Hollywood Reporter.

They were married in Las Vegas in 1976, and three years later, "10" was released and Bo became an international sex symbol.

However, she wasn't quite sure she was cut out for the job.

"I remember sobbing and saying, ‘I’m so confused. I don’t know what to do,’" Bo told THR. "And my husband said, ‘Well, do what you want. Just because everybody would kill for this career doesn’t mean you have to love it.’"

She recalled telling him, "I just want to go make little films like we were doing before," referring to "Fantasies," which was finally released in 1981. She then starred in "Tarzan, The Ape Man," which John directed, as well as a couple of other films, also helmed by her husband.

Since then, she's done smaller roles here and there, saying, "I didn’t always make the right career moves. But God, it was fun."

In 2002, four years after John's death, she was set up on a date with actor John Corbett, known for his roles in "Sex and the City" and "My Big Fat Greek Wedding." Corbett had been given a list of names by a mutual friend and told to list his top five choices for a date, and he sent the list back with only Bo's name circled, saying, "It’s Bo or nobody."

The two hit it off, and as Corbett told The Hollywood Reporter, "We’ve kind of never been apart since then, really."

Bo married John in 2020, and when she was asked why they've worked so well together, she answered, "I fall in love. I fall hard, and I think that when someone else truly loves you, it’s such a gift to cherish. It’s kind of a miracle with all the billions of people in the world, so I cherish it."
