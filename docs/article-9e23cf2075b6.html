Trinity Rodman to miss World Cup qualifiers after stunning Ben Shelton breakup news

2026-11-25 16:03

Trinity Rodman has a minor shoulder injury and won’t be available for the United States in the upcoming CONCACAF W Championship tournament, which serves as qualifying for next summer’s Women’s World Cup.

U.S. coach Emma Hayes replaced Rodman on the 23-player U.S. roster for the tournament with Gotham FC forward Jaedyn Shaw, who has made 37 appearances for the national team with 10 goals.

The news comes just a day after the Daily Mail reported the soccer star and Ben Shelton split following nearly two years of dating.

The couple’s work schedules and long distance were cited to have made it difficult to maintain the relationship.

Shelton, 24, was not present at the NWSL championship game on Saturday between Rodman’s Washington Spirit and the Kansas City Current at Audi Field in Washington D.C.

On the same day, he lost to Carlos Alcaraz in the semifinal of the ATP Finals in Turin, Italy.

Rodman, 24, was injured in the opening minutes of the Spirit’s 2-1 loss to the Current. It occurred in the fifth minute when she collided with Current defender Laney Rouse.

Yet, she scored the team’s lone goal in second-half stoppage time.

Eight teams play this weekend in the quarterfinals of the W Championship, with the four winners earning the confederation’s four direct spots in the World Cup in Brazil.

The tournament also will determine the region’s teams for the 2028 Olympics.

Jamaica will meet Costa Rica before the United States plays El Salvador on Friday, with Canada to play Panama and Mexico facing Haiti on Saturday.

All four matches will be played at Texas Health Mansfield Stadium, in Mansfield, Texas.
