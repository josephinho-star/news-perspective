From field to court: Terrell Owens’ new Centerline styles might make you pick up pickleball

2026-09-22 21:00

Centerline doesn’t want you to have to choose between a sporty look and serious style.

And its latest collection with former NFL legend Terrell Owens is a testament to that.

The new collection features pieces designed to be worn both on and off the court, with sleek silhouettes and performance-driven materials that ensure you look and feel your best.

The sports apparel brand, which offers popular styles for both men and women, has just dropped three pieces for the recent collaboration: a quarter-zip henley, lined shorts and a classic top for easy styling. The pieces are available in Black, Glacier Gray and Azure Blue for a pop of color — and everything is priced under $95.

Owens’ silhouettes are simple, stylish, yet unmistakably built for competition. The fabric is intentionally breathable and lightweight, so it offers extra flexibility that moves with your body as you play. Plus, each design offers built-in UPF protection and invisible pockets for more secure storage.

Below, check out the three Owens-approved styles that just hit the shelves:

A true fall staple, the TO quarter-zip is crafted from a high-performance blend of 88% polyamide and 12% elastane. It features loose raglan sleeves with unrestricted underarm construction so you can stretch, serve and move without restriction. An engineered collar elevates the cut for a more polished look off the court, while targeted side vents maximize breathability when things get heated.

For the guy who lives (and breathes) in his shorts, this pair from the TO collection boasts all the comfy features: an ultra-wide waistband, adjustable drawcord, side vents, and an inner gusset for maximum mobility. They’ve also got dedicated phone storage and zip pockets that securely fit a pickleball or small essentials — so you’re set and secure without feeling held back.

Last but certainly not least is the TO Performance Quarter Zip Top. This handsome top is ideal for cooler conditions on the court. It’s constructed with the brand’s signature ripstop fabric that is built to withstand twists and turns. And it has all the smart touches — including zippered pockets and an adjustable draw cord at the hem that lets you find the perfect fit.
