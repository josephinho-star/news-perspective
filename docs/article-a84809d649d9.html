Sammy Kershaw responds to 'sellout' critics after attending 2026 CMA Awards red carpet

2026-11-21 22:59

Sammy Kershaw is addressing critics directly who suspect that he "sold out" for attending the 2026 Country Music Association awards.

Speaking to Fox News Digital on the red carpet, the legendary country music singer explained how he spoke with the association after taking to social media to air out his grievances about the association allegedly denying him and his wife access to walk the red carpet.

"Well, it went great, and I'm not going to tell you all the details of what was said. All I can tell you is that I know things were going in a positive direction. So, when we decided that I was going to come to the show and walk the carpet, there's a few people out there that said things like, 'Oh, he sold out' and this and that. But that's not how I look at it," Kershaw began.

"The way I look at it was, there was me and the CMA. And we came together and talked. We're moving in the right direction to a greater time in country music," Kershaw continued.

Without giving away details of the conversation, the country music star emphasized that both parties stayed for the whole conversation. "Nobody got up and walked away," he said.

Kershaw said that he didn't intend to hurt anyone with his Instagram post, but he was "heartbroken" by the association's decision.

"And then all of a sudden a lot of other people's fans came aboard. The fans, country music fans are country music fans, and they will bat for anybody in country music," Kershaw said. "Sometimes they'll get rough, but they came together for this because it was more than a carpet walk."

On Nov. 5, Kershaw uploaded a lengthy statement to Instagram, stating that the Country Music Association was not allowing him or his wife, Mendy, to walk the red carpet.

Kershaw claimed that he adjusted his tour schedule to accommodate this year's award show after being "strung along" for several months, assuming that they were invited.

"After more than 50 years in this business, many millions of records and singles streamed and sold, more than two dozen Billboard charted hits, a CMA Horizon Award nomination, being a lifetime CMA member, that they presented to me in 1992, and being asked to donate my time to play CMA Fest, I would hope I’ve earned the opportunity to walk the red carpet alongside my wife, as we’ve done before," Kershaw wrote.

He added, "I love country music, but never in a million years would I think that a successful country music entertainer (and I’m not the only one) would not be able to walk the red carpet. It’s extremely hurtful, embarrassing and very disappointing. I guess the next time I attend the CMA’s, it will be in tribute after I’m gone, if I’m lucky."

Country music stars poured into his comment section, supporting his message.

Riley Duckman wrote, "If you haven’t earned the right to be there then nobody has… disappointing to say the least."

Granger Smith added: "So sad for country music. I grieve for the old CMA. … many of us wouldn’t be here without artists like you…"
