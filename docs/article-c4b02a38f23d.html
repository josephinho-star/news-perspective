Sydney Sweeney wows in lingerie look as she keeps Scooter Braun close with familiar accessory

2026-09-29 21:11

Sydney Sweeney is once again keeping a possible tribute to boyfriend Scooter Braun close to her heart.

Days after the "Euphoria" star sparked buzz by wearing a necklace featuring the initials "SB" in a campaign for her lingerie brand, Syrn, Sweeney appeared to sport the same personalized jewelry in a new series of photos.

The brand shared two selfies of the 29-year-old actress modeling its black Smooth Cup Demi Bra in "After Dark."

"Syd selfies, starring the Smooth Cup Demi Bra in After Dark," Syrn captioned the Instagram carousel.

In the first photo, Sweeney smiled while taking a mirror selfie, with the two small charms visible around her neck. A second image showed the actress posing in the coordinating set while taking another photo in the mirror.

The delicate gold necklace featured two small letter charms hanging side by side, appearing to form an "S" and a "B." The understated piece rested just below her collarbone and stood out against the otherwise all-black look.

The necklace appeared to be the same piece Sweeney wore in Syrn photos shared last week, when the gold initials first drew attention because they matched the initials of her boyfriend, Scooter Braun.

Neither Sweeney nor Syrn has publicly explained the meaning behind the necklace.

This time, however, some followers zeroed in on the accessory themselves.

"The necklace, S & B," one person wrote in the comments.

"Cute necklace!" another added.

Another follower had a different reaction, writing, "Lose the necklace."

The repeat appearance comes just days after Sweeney gave fans another glimpse of her increasingly public relationship with Braun.

In a recent Instagram photo dump reflecting on the end of summer, Sweeney included a romantic nighttime shot of herself and the music executive sharing a kiss.

"Saw pumpkins on someone’s front porch so i guess it’s time for one last summer dump," she captioned the post.

The carousel offered a wide-ranging look at Sweeney's summer, including horseback riding, swimming underwater in a cave, painting, waterskiing, camping and several outfit photos.

One of the more personal images captured Sweeney and Braun sharing a kiss while embracing on a balcony overlooking a palm tree-lined cityscape at night.

In another shot, Sweeney posed for a mirror selfie in a flowing light blue gown that cinched at the waist and featured draped fabric, fluttering sleeves and a floral detail at the hip.

The actress traded the dressed-up look for a life jacket in another action shot, which captured her waterskiing across a lake while holding onto a tow rope.

The summer recap marks another instance of Sweeney choosing to put her relationship with Braun on her own social media after the couple initially kept their romance largely out of the spotlight.

Sweeney and Braun first connected in Venice in summer 2025 around the time of Jeff Bezos and Lauren Sánchez's wedding, according to People. The outlet initially reported that they were "casually" seeing each other that September.

Since then, the couple has increasingly documented their relationship themselves. Braun accompanied Sweeney to the "Euphoria" Season 3 premiere in April, while Sweeney has shared photos from their travels and other moments together on Instagram.

Their romance came full circle earlier this month when the pair returned to Venice roughly a year after first connecting there. They were photographed holding hands and kissing during the trip.

Braun then offered one of his most public declarations about Sweeney while celebrating her 29th birthday Sept. 12, calling their relationship a "true partnership."

"You make me smile harder, laugh louder, adventure more, and dream bigger," he wrote on Instagram. "You've made the ordinary feel exciting and the future feel like something I can't wait to get to."
