Carrot Top breaks silence after suicide attempt: ‘Overwhelmed by the outpouring of love’

2026-10-17 18:56

Carrot Top broke his silence following his suicide attempt with an emotional note to fans.

“I’m truly overwhelmed by the outpouring of love, support, prayers, and well wishes I’ve received over the past couple of weeks,” he wrote via Instagram on Saturday.

“Every message, text, and kind thought has meant more to me than you know,” he added, alongside a pic of himself onstage.

“While I haven’t been able to respond to everyone individually, please know that I’ve felt your love and appreciate each and every one of you from the bottom of my heart. Thank you for being there for me during this time. It truly means the world to me!”

He added an orange heart to the upload before noting that he returns to the stage at the Luxor for his self-named prop comedy show on Oct. 19.

“Hacks” star Hannah Einbinder was among the first to respond in the comments thread, writing, “love you scott❤️,” referring to the comedian’s given name, Scott Thompson.

A fan wrote, “The World is too dark to be without you,” while another chimed in, “Please know the world has your back !”

A third wrote, “The joy you bring this world is truly magical. Carrot Top forever ❤️🥕” while a fourth noted, “hope you truly did see all the love and you know that you have so many you could reach out to at any given moment. 🤍”
