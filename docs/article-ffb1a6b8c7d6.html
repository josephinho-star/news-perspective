Cowboys star Quinnen Williams repeats six word answer 12 times in presser after major coaching change

2026-11-19 01:45

"I'm gonna keep that to myself."

Those six words were the lone answer Dallas Cowboys star defensive tackle Quinnen Williams offered to reporters on Wednesday. Reporters kept peppering the four-time Pro Bowler with questions, and he just repeated those same six words 12 times over.

After the Cowboys' defense was gashed for 584 total yards by the San Francisco 49ers in their 46-20 loss on Sunday, the team stripped play-calling duties from defensive coordinator Christian Parker. Mike Pettine will now call the defensive plays for the Cowboys.

Williams' performance in his press conference is something the Cowboys' defense should try to emulate, as that was the first time they have shut somebody down this season.

The Cowboys' defense has been porous this season. They have allowed 28.2 points per game (30th in the NFL) and 393.8 yards per game (last in the NFL), yielding 239.7 passing yards per game (26th in the NFL) and 154.1 yards per game (last in the NFL). They also don't force turnovers, as their 0.8 takeaways a game are 30th in the NFL.

The rest of the NFC East has been ravaged with injuries and underperformance, yet the Cowboys, despite remaining largely healthy, have been unable to take advantage. The New York Giants lead the division at 5-3-1 despite having started three different quarterbacks after Jaxson Dart's injury.

The Philadelphia Eagles have lost seven games in a row, and Jalen Hurts is out for the season because of blood clots. The Washington Commanders lost Jayden Daniels for some time, among other key contributors for the season.

Yet the Cowboys are 4-6 because their defense can't stop a nosebleed. The team hopes Williams' stout performance with the reporters carries over to the field.

Williams has been a bright spot in what has otherwise been a terrible unit. He has 46 total tackles and six sacks, and leads the NFL with 15 tackles for loss.
