Horrifying moment boy, 10, shoots teen neighbor after being ordered by mom to grab gun

2026-11-20 09:13

Horrifying home security footage captured the moment a 10-year-old boy – following his mother’s orders – allegedly fatally shot his teenage neighbor who was punching his mother over a noise complaint on Halloween.

The unidentified boy aimed down sights on 16-year-old Keinerlein Plaza Rangel with his mother’s handgun and fired a pair of shots at the teen in their Albuquerque apartment complex at around 4 p.m. on Oct. 31, according to video obtained by KOB4.

The gun-toting minor’s mom, 40-year-old Elaina Gomez-Villeda, told her son to grab the weapon as she duked it out with Rangel and the teen’s mother.

Gomez-Villeda had allegedly complained to her neighbors about the loud music earlier in the day before the trio got into a hair-pulling, slugfest.

“He’s going to shoot you, look he’s going to f–king shoot someone, bitch,” the heavily tatted mom warned.

Only one of the two shots struck Rangel, killing her.

Security footage outside Gomez-Villeda’s home captured Rangel’s father, Keiner Plaza, going ballistic after he learned his daughter had been shot.

The outraged man ripped open his neighbor’s door and attempted to break in.

“You know this happened. They took our joy away. It was our biggest dream to see her grow…To see your daughter go like this, in this way. It’s not fair, man. It’s not fair,” Plaza told the outlet.

Gomez-Villeda, who was left bruised from the beatdown, had reported the shooting to police.

“These ladies outside were jumping me, and my son grabbed my gun, and he shot them,” Gomez-Villeda told a police dispatcher, according to KOB4.

Police officers arrived to a chaotic scene where they discovered Rangel on the ground, not breathing and Gomez-Villeda holding the gun.

One cop fired two shots towards Gomez-Villeda striking the wall outside the apartment.

Gomez-Villeda was charged with first-degree murder but the charges were downgraded to second-degree murder by a grand jury.

Charges against the boy may be held until he is 12, when he is old enough to be booked.

“We talked about this on scene. He’s not old enough to be booked into the juvenile detention center, and so this is something that we will continue to work closely with the juvenile district attorney’s office on as he turns 12 years old to continue to review the situation,” Albuquerque Police Chief Cecily Barker told the outlet.

Charges could be brought against Plaza for his behavior after the shooting, but he says he is the victim after Gomez-Villeda allegedly attacked his daughter to start the brawl.

“That woman attacked my daughter. She came to my house and pounded on the door. She attacked my daughter and also tried to attack my wife, who is pregnant, by hitting her in the stomach. She grabbed my daughter and told her own son to shoot her,” Plaza said.
