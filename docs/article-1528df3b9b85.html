Yankees’ Clarke Schmidt solid in ‘emotional’ first outing since Tommy John surgery

2026-09-17 05:47

Fourteen months and five days removed from Tommy John surgery, Clarke Schmidt made his return to a big league mound Wednesday.

Dealing with some nerves and much more adrenaline than he experienced on his rehab assignment, Schmidt struck out a pair and allowed two bloop hits in the seventh inning of what became a 5-4 loss to the Twins in 13 innings at Target Field.

With his dad in attendance, Schmidt threw 24 pitches, 13 for strikes, while topping out at 95.8 mph on his fastball.

Now that he has the first game under his belt, the Yankees are hoping Schmidt can settle into a bullpen role to give them another option to pitch important innings down the stretch and into the playoffs.

The right-hander will likely be limited to one or one-plus innings at a time, and he will not pitch on back-to-back days.
