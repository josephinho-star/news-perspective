Ecuador Court Convicts Former President Moreno of Taking Bribes

2026-08-28 22:08

Three judges in Ecuador found former President Lenín Moreno guilty of accepting bribes from China’s Sinohydro Corp. Ltd. when he was vice president in 2010, according to a unanimous verdict.
