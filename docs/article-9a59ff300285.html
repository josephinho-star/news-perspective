‘The Pitt’ Season 3 Release Date: Everything We Know About ‘The Pitt’ Season 3

2026-09-15 11:15

Noah Wyle did it again. For the second straight year, the man who portrays Dr. Michael “Robby” Robinavitch on HBO Max’s The Pitt with such profound pathos earned the Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series. And Wyle’s win wasn’t the only high-profile Emmy for the series because The Pitt also took home the Emmy for Outstanding Drama Series!

We’re all using an extra dose of hand sanitizer today to celebrate the show’s continued success.

The second season of The Pitt concluded back in April 2026. While a third season of the beloved medical drama was never really in question — the show was renewed for Season 3 ahead of its sophomore season — many fans are wondering when we’re heading back to the Pittsburgh Trauma Medical Center for another day of unpredictable medical mayhem.

When will The Pitt Season 3 premiere on HBO Max? Here’s everything we know about the upcoming third season.

Has The Pitt Been Renewed For Season 3?

Yes! HBO has already renewed The Pitt for a third season.

When Does The Pitt Season 3 Come Out on HBO Max?

The new season officially premieres in January 2027 on HBO Max.

Everything We Know About The Pitt Season 3:

We know that the third season will take place on November 12, 2026, a day after Veterans Day.

“Season 3 is about doctors benefiting from being patients,” star Noah Wyle told Deadline. “We’re going to see that [Robby]’s begun the therapeutic road; it’s had a positive effect. It’s fragile, and it’s not without risk and vulnerability, but it’s at least there’s forward progress.”

The show has also added seven new recurring characters for Season 3, including Stephen Chang (Bosch: Legacy), Barry Clifton (The Madison), Chris Diamantopoulos (Silicon Valley), Lawrence Kao, Brent Sexton, JB Tadena, and Hannah Ware.

How To Watch The Pitt Online:

The Pitt is streaming on HBO Max.

If you’re new to HBO Max, you can sign up for as low as $10.99/month with ads, but an ad-free subscription will cost $18.49/month.

If you want to stream even more and save a few bucks a month while you’re at it, we recommend subscribing to one of the discounted Disney+ Bundles with Hulu and HBO Max. With ads, the bundle costs $19.99/month and without ads, $32.99/month.

You can also add HBO Max to your Prime Video account for an additional monthly fee.
