Fox News star Ainsley Earhardt vows to ‘serve’ new husband Sean Hannity after wedding — as she proudly takes his name

2026-10-28 17:16

Ainsley Earhardt vowed to “love and serve” her new husband, Sean Hannity, for the rest of her life while revealing plans to take his last name.

“I am so proud to be his bride and I want to love him and serve him all the days of my life,” Earhardt gushed during her return to “Fox & Friends” on Tuesday, days after tying the knot with the fellow Fox News host.

The newlywed, 50, also revealed that she has already begun signing her text messages as “Mrs. Hannity.”

“I will take his last name for my personal life. I am so proud to be Mrs. Hannity, and I have signed every text, pretty much, that people have sent me, ‘Love, Mrs. Hannity,'” she shared.

However, Earhardt plans to keep her maiden name professionally.

“For TV, I think here at Fox, there’s only room for one Hannity, so I think I will be Ainsley Earhardt forever on TV,” she joked.

During the segment, she also shared new details about their Florida nuptials, which took place on Oct. 24.

“We had a blast,” she said, adding that she felt so much “support” from guests as they tied the knot.

“We were there with so many people from Fox, so many family members, so many friends, and it was everyone in one room that we love most,” Earhardt said. “It was just so special.”

She gushed over her new husband, and said the family was excited for the future.

The blended family includes their children from previous marriages. Hannity has two children — son Sean and daughter Merri — with ex-wife Jill Rhodes, while Earhardt shares pre-teen daughter Hayden with her ex, Will Proctor.

“I’m so in love with that man right there,” she said about Hannity, whom she started dating in 2020.

“He’s the most generous person, he’s so kind, he loves everyone at Fox, he’s just really, really, good,” she shared.

Earhardt and Hannity wed at the Church of Bethesda-by-the-Sea in Palm Beach, Florida, the same place where they got engaged in December 2024.

The wedding guests included many high-profile Washington, DC figures, like Lara Trump and her husband, Eric, as well as former United States Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt.

“Our hearts are overflowing with love and gratitude as we begin this new chapter together as husband and wife,” the newlyweds gushed in a statement.

They called their ceremony “more meaningful than [they] could have ever imagined.”

The couple continued, “Our faith has always been at the center of our relationship, and we truly believe God brought us together. We feel blessed by the outpouring of love and support from the wonderful people in our lives and are excited for the future.”

“Above all,” Hannity and Earhardt concluded, “we are grateful to God for bringing us to this moment and for the beautiful life we will continue to build together.”
