14 missing after Russian strike as Zelenskyy presses US over Patriot missiles

2026-11-22 09:49

A Russian drone strike partially destroyed a five-story apartment building near Kyiv, leaving 14 people unaccounted for, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Sunday.

Rescuers were searching the rubble in Dymer, north of Kyiv, where part of the building collapsed and a fire broke out. Nine people had been evacuated, Zelenskyy added, warning that others could remain trapped.

The attack prompted the Ukrainian president to renew pressure on Washington to approve deliveries of U.S.-made Patriot interceptor missiles financed by Ukraine’s allies, amid mounting Russian aerial attacks. “It is particularly difficult to understand the delays in supplying anti-ballistic interceptors to Ukraine,” Zelenskyy said on X.
