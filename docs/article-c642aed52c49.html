Here’s how to watch Texas A&M vs. LSU: Start time, livestream

2026-09-26 20:30

It’s time for a Saturday night rivalry game as Texas A&M travel to Louisiana for the 65th all-time meeting between the Aggies and the LSU Tigers.

Though it’s still early in the season, both teams are hoping to keep their College Football Playoff chances alive with a win tonight after losing their respective SEC openers last week.

LSU enters Saturday at 2-1 following a 32-24 road setback against Ole Miss in Week 3. The Tigers are looking to rebound in front of a home crowd after opening the season with dominant wins over Clemson and Louisiana Tech by relying on quarterback Sam Leavitt and tight end Trey’Dez Green to jumpstart an explosive passing attack.

Watch No. 23 Texas A&M vs. No. 10 LSU

- When: Sept. 26, 7:30 p.m. ET

- Where: Tiger Stadium (Baton Rouge, Louisiana)

- How to watch: Sling Select and Blue plans

Texas A&M also arrives at 2-1 following a disappointing 31-21 home loss against Kentucky last weekend, dropping their SEC opener after picking up early double-digit victories over Missouri State and Arizona State. Head coach Mike Elko will lean on quarterback Marcel Reed, running back CJ Baxter, and an aggressive front seven to generate relentless pressure and control the line of scrimmage early.

During last year’s meeting in Baton Rouge, the Aggies came out on top, 49-25.
