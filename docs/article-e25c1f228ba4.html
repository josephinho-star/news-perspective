Iconic supercar brand unveils new cruiser for LA’s ritziest police force: ‘You ain’t running from this’

2026-11-21 00:46

The Beverly Hills Police Department has teamed up with Ferrari to unveil a head-turning V12 Purosangue supercar, giving the ritzy city’s streets a high-octane new patrol vehicle.

“Catch it this weekend at the LA Auto Show and on road for a limited time,” the Beverly Hills Police Department (BHPD) captioned on Instagram.

The eye-popping ride will be on display this weekend at the 119th LA Auto Show, which runs from Nov. 20 through Nov. 29 at the Los Angeles Convention Center.

The V12 Purosangue is Ferrari’s first-ever four-door, four-seat vehicle. Unveiled in 2022, the model is essentially an SUV, though the Italian automaker refuses to use that label. It carries a price tag of roughly $440,000.

The flashy new cruiser has already hit the streets.

During Thursday’s annual Rodeo Drive Holiday Lighting Celebration, the Ferrari was spotted cruising around the upscale shopping district with a uniformed officer behind the wheel and someone dressed as Santa Claus riding shotgun.

The partnership is the latest chapter in Beverly Hills’ decades-long relationship with the exotic-car world, with the police department and its nonprofit charity organization, BHPOA, maintaining close ties to local supercar dealerships.

The city has long embraced its reputation as a playground for the world’s most expensive rides.

Since 1993, the city of Beverly Hills shuts down Rodeo Drive for the Rodeo Drive Concours d’Elegance which draws massive crowds and rare supercars. The event usually takes place on Father’s Day and is free to attend.
