Republican senator's husband dead: 'Our family is heartbroken'

2026-11-24 19:19

Sen. Deb Fischer, R-Neb., announced Monday that her husband, Bruce Fischer, died over the weekend.

"It is with a heavy heart that I share the passing of my beloved husband, Bruce," Fischer said in a statement posted on X. "Bruce was my partner in life, my greatest source of strength, and the heart of our family. His kindness, integrity, and quiet strength left a lasting impression on all who knew him.

"On November 22, Bruce passed away peacefully while doing what he loved most, spending time outdoors," the statement continued.

"Our family is heartbroken by his passing, but we find comfort in the life we shared with him. I will cherish the decades we spent building a family in the Nebraska Sandhills. Above all, Bruce was a loving husband, a devoted father and grandfather, a man of faith and a rancher."

Fischer met her husband when the pair were students at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, the Lincoln Journal Star reported. They married in 1972 and had three sons.

Utah Republican Sen. Mike Lee reacted by saying, "This is truly heartbreaking."

"Bruce Fischer was a good man, with whom Sharon and I have been privileged to interact over many years," Lee wrote on X.

Nebraska Gov. Jim Pillen also offered his condolences following news of the death.

"Suzanne and I are keeping Sen. Fischer and her family in our thoughts. We share our prayers as she deals with this great personal loss," he wrote on X.

"I am very sad to hear of the passing of Bruce Fischer," Nebraska Sen. Pete Ricketts said. "Bruce left his mark on this world through his love for family, his faith, and his ranch. Susanne and I send our deepest condolences to Deb, the entire Fischer family, and all their loved ones during this difficult time. The Fischer family is in our prayers."

Bruce Fischer's cause of death has not been disclosed.

Bruce Fischer went viral in January 2025 when he appeared to decline a handshake from then-Vice President Kamala Harris as his wife was sworn into office.

After administering the oath to Fischer and shaking her hand, Harris turned to Bruce Fischer to extend her hand. He appeared to respond with "Thank you" without extending his hand back.
