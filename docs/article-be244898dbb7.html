Sources: Tide to start QB Mack vs. Vandy; injured Russell available, if needed

2026-11-14 12:36

No. 3 Alabama is expected to start quarterback Austin Mack at Vanderbilt on Saturday as Keelon Russell deals with the lingering effects of an ankle injury, sources told ESPN's Pete Thamel.

Russell, who has started every game this season and been one of the most impactful quarterbacks in the country, is expected to be available off the bench, sources told ESPN. The expectation, however, is that Russell will be held out to let the injury heal, sources said.

He suffered the ankle injury during the second half of last week's win at LSU. He got the ankle wrapped and played through the pain.

The sophomore from Texas has thrown for 2,263 yards with 18 touchdowns and five interceptions. He also has rushed for 276 yards and five touchdowns. His 86.5 QBR is second only to Miami's Darian Mensah.

Russell beat out Mack for the starting job late in preseason camp.

Mack, a fourth-year player who followed coach Kalen DeBoer from Washington in 2025, has appeared in four games this season, completing 3 of 4 passes for 23 yards.
