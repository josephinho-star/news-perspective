Fox, USVETS renew 'Make Camo Your Cause' campaign dedicated to ending veteran homelessness

2026-11-05 09:00

Fox News Channel viewers can once again expect to see a lot of camouflage through Veterans Day on Nov. 11.

Fox Corporation is partnering with U.S.VETS for the "Make Camo Your Cause" campaign for the sixth straight year to help end veteran homelessness because too many American heroes who bravely served our country find themselves sleeping on the streets they swore to protect.

The "Make Camo Your Cause" campaign is dedicated to ending veteran homelessness in the United States because "veterans" and "homelessness" should never be uttered in the same sentence.

Fox News Channel hosts and anchors will wear camo to show appreciation and gratitude for veterans and their military service while raising awareness and funds to propel U.S.VETS' mission. Viewers can pick up Fox News-branded camo gear in the online store and wear it proudly on Veterans Day to show support for U.S. veterans.

FOX is the founding media sponsor of the campaign, which aims to bring visibility and awareness to the tragic issue in the United States while transforming what it means to wear camo here at home. The past five years have been wildly successful and resulted in significant donations to U.S.VETS and greater awareness of the critical work it does.

Viewers can make camo their cause by donating or shopping online here because veterans deserve every opportunity to live with dignity and independence, not on the streets.

The "Make Camo Your Cause" campaign offers a variety of camouflage products — from hats and T-shirts to mugs and duffel bags — designed to create awareness of the U.S.VETS Mission.

Every purchase helps veterans and their families successfully transition to civilian life by providing housing, counseling, career development and other critical support.

"Make Camo Your Cause" will also appear in on-screen graphics and across Fox News social media platforms and Fox News Digital.

Founded in 1993, U.S.VETS is now the largest nonprofit with boots on the ground fighting America’s veteran homelessness crisis. It provides housing, counseling, supportive services and career programs, in addition to supportive services for veterans to rebuild and thrive.
