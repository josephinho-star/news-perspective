Russian Diesel Deal Confronts Legal Uncertainty

2026-10-24 12:55

Oliver Wyman Geostrategy Practice Leader Daniel Tannebaum tells Bloomberg This Weekend that the first Russian diesel shipment under President Donald Trump’s deal with Vladimir Putin raises legal and compliance questions while offering limited relief for US fuel prices. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Tannebaum says private companies remain wary of doing business with Russia and warns that uncertainty over US sanctions and technology policy could push foreign governments and businesses toward alternatives to American suppliers.
