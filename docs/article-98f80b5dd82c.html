The week in whoppers: Brad Lander abuses Yom Kippur, Obama hypocritically slams Trump’s media ban, and more

2026-09-24 21:02

Diary of disturbing disinformation and dangerous delusions

“My confession must center on my, on our complicity in what I view as genocide in Gaza.” — Ex-NYC Comptroller Brad Lander, Monday (Yom Kippur)

We say: For Jews, Yom Kippur is a day of atonement, a day to “confess” personal sins and wrongdoings, not stage political theater.

Yet Brad Lander, likely a congressman come January, abused Jews’ holiest day to smear Israel as genocidal, pretending he thinks he and other Jews are to blame.

What a disgusting, blasphemous phony. Lander didn’t atone for his sins; he compounded them.

“The Media Begins to Turn Against Trump” — Heather Cox Richardson’s Substack, “Letters from an American,” Saturday

We say: Begins? Please. When have most media not been against the prez?

Most outlets have bashed him incessantly, often based on outright lies, since the day he came down that escalator to announce his 2016 campaign.

A real man-bites-dog news story would be when the media turn to support him. (By the way, Heather: “Media” is a plural noun.)

“I have never seen killing and destruction like Gaza in all my years as secretary general.” — UN Secretary-General António Guterres, Tuesday

We say: Yes, 70,000 people (many terrorists) were reported killed in the Gaza war that Hamas started.

But Guterres’ time as sec-gen has seen 600,000 killed in Ethiopia, 377,000 in Yemen, hundreds of thousands in the Ukraine war and 150,000-plus in Sudan (with 10 million-plus displaced). Oh, and more than a million Uyghurs sent to internment camps and prisons in China. Wonder why Guterres has only seen deaths in Gaza …

“What . . . if I had said . . . Fox News, you’re out of here? It’s unimaginable!” — Barack Obama, Sept. 18

We say: No need to “imagine”; it’s a matter of historical record: Obama’s administration did try to limit Fox News’ press access, albeit on a smaller level than President Trump’s recent ban on CNN, Politico and MS NOW.
