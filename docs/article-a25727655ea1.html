Josh Allen had important Hailee Steinfeld question after Bills’ dramatic win

2026-10-14 18:31

Josh Allen had a one-track mind.

After the quarterback did everything he needed to do following the Bills’ thrilling 37-34 overtime on Monday night, from shaking hands to media requirements, Allen went looking for his wife, Hailee Steinfeld, and asked SoFi Stadium staff for help.

“Is my wife in one of these suites somewhere?” the 30-year-old said the clip captured by Spectrum News 1 reporter Andy Young. “Does anybody know?”

Allen put his hands to his head to shield his eyes from the bright lights as he looked around for the Oscar-nominated actress.

He didn’t seem to find her from the field and walked off the field while greeting fans.

Steinfeld made a rare public appearance to support her husband Monday and was caught on the broadcast various times, including a moment that went viral of her cheering for the four-time Pro Bowler.

“Go, Josh! Let’s go, Josh! Let’s f–king go!!!” she could be seen yelling and pointing from a suite with other Bills families.

Steinfeld, 29, was also later caught seemingly ducking camera as the game neared the two-minute warning of overtime.

Things didn’t look great for the Bills in overtime when James Cook fumbled early in the extra quarter.

However, they received a second chance when linebacker Greg Rousseau’s outstretched hand blocked a 48-yard Rams field goal attempted.

Allen drove his team down the field and set up Tyler Bass for a game-winning 45-yard field goal.

The victory went down as Allen’s first overtime win in his nine-year career. He logged a combined 331 yards in the air and on the ground, plus two touchdowns.

Cook also ran in two scores.

Steinfeld, who typically supports Allen in private, has been linked to the 30-year-old since May 2023.

The two married in May 2025 and welcomed their first child together in April, daughter Harper Haize Allen.

The Bills (4-1) visit the Raiders in Week 6 before their Week 7 bye.
