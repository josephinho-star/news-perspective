How to watch ‘MobLand’ Season 2 premiere for free: Time, cast, episodes

2026-09-18 04:00

The Harrigan family is bloody back.

Paramount+’s hit series “MobLand” returns today, Sept. 18, for a second season where chaos reigns.

In Season 2, “the Harrigans (Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren) struggle to show a unified front as rising rivals threaten their fractured criminal empire – and Harry Da Souza (Tom Hardy, their street-smart and formidable ‘fixer,’ must walk a dangerous tightrope when tensions within the family intensify,” per the official synopsis of the season. “As violence spills into every corner of their lives, loyalties snap, safety proves temporary, and the battle for power leaves no room for mercy.”

- When: Sept. 18, 3 a.m. ET/12 a.m. PT

- How to watch for free: Paramount+ (seven-day free trial)

- TV Channel: Streaming exclusive

“MobLand” comes from executive producer Guy Ritchie, who has also found television success with Netflix’s “The Gentlemen.”

Brosnan and Mirren reprise their roles as the patriarch and matriarch, respectively, of the Harrigan crime family, and despite reports that he had been fired from the series due to on-set behaviors, Hardy returns as the Harrigan family fixer.

The series’ Season 3 renewal further confirmed that Hardy will remain a part of the series alongside Brosnan and Mirren.

‘MobLand’ Season 2 release date

“MobLand” Season 2 premieres Friday, Sept. 18. New episodes will drop on Paramount+ at 3 a.m. ET/12 a.m. PT.

Where to stream ‘MobLand’ for free

“MobLand” is a Paramount+ original series, so you’ll need a subscription to stream new episodes.

Paramount+ offers two plans, both of which include “Lioness.” The ad-supported plan costs $8.99/month, while the ad-free plan costs $13.99/month.

If you subscribe through Amazon Prime Video add-on subscriptions (which is what we recommend), you can take advantage of a seven-day free trial of the ad-free plan.

Season 2 of “MobLand” will have ten episodes, releasing weekly on Fridays at 3 a.m. ET.

- Episode 1: “I Wanna Be Your Dog” – Sept. 18

In addition to Hardy, Brosnan and Mirren, here’s who you can expect to see in Season 2 of “MobLand.”

- Paddy Considine as Kevin Harrigan, Conrad’s and Maeve’s second son, and Harry’s longtime working partner

- Joanne Froggatt as Jan Da Souza, Harry’s wife and Gina’s mother

- Lara Pulver as Isabella “Bella” Harrigan, Kevin’s wife and Eddie’s mother

- Anson Boon as Eddie Harrigan, Kevin’s and Isabella’s rebellious son

- Jasmine Jobson as Zosia, a friend and close associate of Harry

- Mandeep Dhillon as Seraphina Harrigan, Conrad’s daughter, Maeve’s stepdaughter, and Kevin and Brendan’s half-sister

- Janet McTeer as Kat McAllister, an international cartel operative connected to Harry

- Jordi Mollà as Jaime Lopez, the leader of a Mexican cartel involved in fentanyl production

- Toby Jones as Colin Tattersall, a retired DCI tasked with taking down the Harrigans

Will there be a ‘MobLand’ Season 3?

Yes! Ahead of the Season 2 premiere, Paramount+ renewed “MobLand” for Season 3.
