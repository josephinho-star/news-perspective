Iran war live: Saudi-led coalition downs Houthi missiles

2026-11-21 00:00

Saudi Arabia issued and quickly lifted emergency alerts for Riyadh for the third night in a row, two days after the capital’s Public Investment Fund skyscraper – the tallest building in the country – was hit by Houthi missiles.

The Houthis threatened a Saudi power station, Red Sea ports, including Jeddah, and Riyadh’s financial centre after the Saudi-led coalition supporting the Yemeni government said it hit Yemen‘s Houthi-held Hodeidah port and Sanaa earlier in the day.
