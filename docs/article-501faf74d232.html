Nottingham man jailed for 12 years for drugging and sexually assaulting partner

2026-11-26 19:54

A man has been jailed for 12 years for drugging his long-term partner and sexually assaulting her while she slept.

The man, who is in his 30s and from Nottingham, cannot be named in order to protect the identity of his victim. At a hearing in June, he admitted sexually assaulting the woman on multiple occasions while she was under the influence of sleeping pills.

He was sentenced at Nottingham crown court on Thursday for two counts of sexual assault, six counts of assault by penetration and one count of possession of a class B drug.

The man wept in the dock as Judge Watson told him his sex crimes were “depraved” and “playing out a disturbing fantasy”.

“For a number of months you abused the trust inherent in any relationship,” the judge said. “You made excuses and shrugged off her concerns. She built up trust in you slowly that you betrayed.”

The court was told the victim was “particularly vulnerable” because he had given her sleeping tablets and sometimes wine that he asked her to drink. This induced a deep state of sleep.

The judge said the man would be subject to a sexual harm prevention and restraining order for the foreseeable future because he presented a “danger to future partners”.

Isabel Wilson of the Crown Prosecution Service East Midlands said: “This man sought to gain power over a woman who should have been at her safest with him, treating her as an object for his own sexual gratification.

“These were deeply predatory offences that involved a shocking abuse of trust, and the devastating impact of that betrayal cannot be overstated.

“I hope this conviction provides some sense of justice for the victim and demonstrates that those who commit such appalling offences will be brought before the courts and held accountable.”

The offending came to light after the man told a woman that he had been having sex with a woman while she was unconscious.

He shared images and videos of the abuse with the woman, including sexual touching and penetration, which prompted her to report him to the police.

Detectives then discovered that the woman in the photographs and videos was the man’s long-term partner.

The victim also found a conversation between the man and another woman, in which he had shared an intimate video of her without permission.

The man had also posted sexual images and videos of his partner online without her consent, the court was told.

The case comes just a week after four men were convicted at Winchester crown court for sexual offences against Joanne Young.

She was drugged and abused by her former husband, Philip Young, who allowed other men to assault her in the family home.

He has admitted 48 sexual offences against her, while a fifth man pleaded guilty to rape and assault.

All six men are to be sentenced next month.
