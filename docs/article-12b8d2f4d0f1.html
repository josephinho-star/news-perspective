The Eagles Sphere residency starts again soon. How much are tickets?

2026-09-04 20:54

Don Henley and Joe Walsh just can’t quit each other.

After claiming The Eagles were wrapping their Las Vegas Sphere residency in April 2026, the Rock Hall of Famers have extended the record-breaking run multiple times.

Now, they’ll allegedly wrap their 68-concert Sin City stint — which began way back in September 2024 — with 14 more shows from September ’26 through January 2027.

If you’d like to see the legendary classic rockers do anything but “Take It Easy” live, tickets are available for all of their upcoming gigs at the visually-spectacular Sphere.

At the time of publication, the lowest price we could find on seats for any one show was $406 including fees on StubHub.

Other concerts have tickets starting anywhere from $409 to $529 including fees.

Based on our findings at Set List FM, the ’70s hitmakers stick to a pretty strict script at their live performances.

Records indicate they typically deliver 20-21 song shows chock-full of fan favorites like “Tequila Sunrise,” “Life In The Fast Lane,” “Desperado,” “Lyin’ Eyes” and, of course, “Hotel California.”

Other exciting tracks that often get worked into the mix include Henley’s timeless “Boys Of Summer” and Joe Walsh’s funky James Gang staple “Life’s Been Good.”

However, what makes this show so special isn’t just the music.

“Part of what makes the Eagles pairing with the Sphere so perfect is that the visuals — while breathtaking — never remove you from the music,” Ultimate Classic Rock spilled in a review of a March 2026 concert. “Whether it’s an eerie swamp setting for ‘Witchy Woman’ or the sight of lyrics falling from the sky during ‘Lyin’ Eyes,’ the visuals serve to amplify the music’s impact. The Sphere may be the spectacle, but the songs remain the star of the show.”

Who performs with The Eagles in 2026?

When you’ve been “taking it to the limit” since 1971, there’s bound to be a bit of shakeup in your group.

Still, after 50+ years together, 79-year-old founding member Don Henley (drums, vocals, also known for the timeless ’80s smash “Boys of Summer), continues to head the group.

At all Sphere dates, he’s joined by 78-year-old Joe Walsh (who also fronted The James Gang), 78-year-old Timothy B. Schmit (bassist and vocalist who played with Poco), 22-time Grammy winner Vince Gill and the late Glenn Frey’s 30-something son, Deacon.

Past members that are no longer with the band include Frey (who passed away in 2016) and Randy Meisner (he left this mortal coil in 2023). Bernie Leadon is still alive but hasn’t played with the band since 2016. As for guitarist Don Felder, he recently toured with Styx and REO Speedwagon’s Kevin Cronin, although The Post reported he suffered a medical emergency at a show in 2025.

Over the course of the group’s half-century together, The Eagles have received six Grammy Awards, performed over 1,000 concerts, and sold over 150 million albums.
