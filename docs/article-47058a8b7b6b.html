Rockstar Rebuilt Itself to Make GTA VI

2026-10-17 12:15

Bloomberg News video game reporter Jason Schreier tells Bloomberg This Weekend how Rockstar Games nearly doubled its workforce and overhauled its production culture while building the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI. Speaking with hosts Christina Ruffini and Lisa Mateo, Schreier explains Rockstar’s efforts to curb excessive overtime, the extraordinary commercial expectations for the game and why the company believes its next major release could take less time to develop.
