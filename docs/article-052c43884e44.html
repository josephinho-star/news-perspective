Travis Kelce passes Rob Gronkowski on all-time NFL list with two-touchdown game for Chiefs

2026-11-22 23:25

Travis Kelce had himself a day against the Arizona Cardinals, and with two more touchdowns on his Pro Football Hall of Fame resume, he made some NFL history.

The Kansas City Chiefs tight end’s eight-yard touchdown snag to get his team in the end zone first on Sunday at Arrowhead Stadium marked the 109th of his career.

Kelce moved past the great Rob Gronkowski for third on the NFL’s all-time tight end touchdowns list. They had been tied at 108 scores, which the New England Patriots star finished his career with.

Only Antonio Gates (118) and Tony Gonzalez (115), both Hall of Fame tight ends, are above Kelce on the list. He certainly has a chance to live atop the list entirely one day if he continues to get opportunities like he did on Sunday.

Quarterback Patrick Mahomes found Kelce later in this 34-7 blowout at home for an even more impressive 22-yard touchdown grab, where the veteran pass-catcher fought off two tacklers before falling into the end zone.

Kelce, with his superstar wife Taylor Swift in her usual suite looking down on the field, hauled in six of his seven targets for 74 yards with his two scores.

Mahomes himself had a tremendous day when he dropped back, going 27-of-39 for 324 yards with three touchdowns. With the game very out of reach in the fourth quarter, key starters like Mahomes and Kelce were getting some rest on the sideline.

This Sunday also marked Kelce’s 100th regular season game at the beloved Arrowhead Stadium, making him only the fourth player in team history to do so. He’s spent his entire career in Kansas City, which took him in the third round of the 2013 NFL Draft out of Cincinnati.

It’s unknown what the future holds for Kelce after this season, as retirement has been a topic of conversation in back-to-back offseasons. The question will likely pop up again after the 2026 campaign, though Kelce hasn’t given an indication of what he wishes to do just yet.

Until then, he’ll continue to impact the Chiefs, who are now 8-2 on the season, as they aim for another Super Bowl victory.
