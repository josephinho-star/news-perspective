The viral shirt Hailey Bieber and Sabrina Carpenter wear just got a chic update

2026-11-11 21:31

Dress me like one of your French girls.

Inspired by the iconic Jane Birkin, Cou Cou’s Rina Tee has become a must-have for fashion insiders.

Made with 100% organic cotton, the $68 style has been spotted on Hailey Bieber, Sabrina Carpenter and Kaia Gerber. (Likewise, I’ve started collecting Cou Cou tees — and can confirm they’re worth every cent.)

While the original Rina shirt previously inspired a dress and a short-sleeve tee, the brand just dropped two new versions that are worth shopping tout de suite: a gorgeous pointelle option and an elongated cut.

With bell sleeves and a dip hem, the pointelle Rina comes in baby pink, black or white and pairs nicely with higher-waisted or even low-cut bottoms (if you prefer to show a little skin).

The London-based design house is known for its intimates, and the ready-to-wear options reflect the same sensual coziness.

The tees are perfect for lounging or adding a little je ne sais quoi to any street style. They effortlessly give jeans a whole new French It girl vibe, so it’s no wonder Bieber’s worn them on repeat in multiple colors.

However, because of the slim fit and the potential for natural cotton fabric to shrink, many brand fans (myself included) size up for a more relaxed fit.

As such, Cou Cou also just debuted The Rina Extra Long Sleeve, which has a little more length in the hem and sleeves.

Just in time for cooler months (when you may not want much skin exposed), the long version features more subtle detailing, but the same understated elegance.

Available in black, white and navy, the Rina Extra Long comes in sizes XXS to XXL.

While imitation is a form of flattery, the many dupes the Rina tee has spawned just ain’t the same. Cou Cou’s are immeasurably soft and thick, wash well and have little details that bring joy to everyday outfits.

Besides the solid tee, Bieber has also been spotted in the Rina Raglan, while Zoë Kravitz has worn the Long Cami and the Slip Skirt on repeat.

In addition to the clothing, don’t miss Cou Cou’s luxurious organic cotton intimates. (Plus, you can even score 10% off two-piece sets.)
