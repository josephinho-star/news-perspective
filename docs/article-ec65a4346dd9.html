Peace has never smelled this good: Meet Bond No. 9’s newest scent

2026-09-25 17:14

Sometimes, peace smells really, really good.

Meet The Scent of Peace for All, the latest fragrance from Bond No. 9, a New York-born scent house known for bottling the energy, personality and unmistakable attitude of the city.

This time, Bond No. 9 is thinking bigger than a single block, borough or skyline. The new collection takes inspiration from a universal idea — harmony, connection and the hope that people can find common ground — and turns it into something you can actually spritz on your wrist.

And yes, it smells as inviting as the concept sounds.

The Scent of Peace for All opens with a bright, juicy combination of sparkling pear, tangerine and pink grapefruit, giving the fragrance an immediate burst of freshness. It’s energetic without being overwhelming — the olfactory equivalent of stepping outside on a crisp New York morning when the city is already buzzing.

At the heart, lentisque absolute, bran absolute and geranium oil add texture and character, bringing a green, aromatic and floral dimension to the composition. The trifecta of scent notes allows it to evolve rather than simply announce itself and disappear.

Finally, amber, musk and tobacco settle into the base, adding warmth and depth. This gives the bright opening somewhere to land, creating a scent that feels polished, modern and designed to linger.

The brand also ties the launch of this fragrance to New York’s role as a global meeting place, where cultures, languages and perspectives collide — sometimes literally — and coexist.

Bond No. 9’s The Scent of Peace Natural starts off bright and fresh, with lemon, blackcurrant bud and petitgrain. When I first sprayed it, the lemon really stood out, giving it a clean, refreshing feel, while the blackcurrant added a subtle fruity sweetness.

As it settled on my skin, I started noticing raspberry and Damask rose, which made the scent softer and a little more floral. To me, it smells fresh and natural without being too sweet or overpowering. It’s easy to wear during the day and instantly puts me in a good mood.

As the fragrance dries down, the brighter notes give way to ambroxan, cedarwood and musk. I liked this part because it adds warmth and keeps the scent from feeling too light. On my skin, I could still pick up hints of the lemon and raspberry, but they were mixed with a soft, woody and slightly musky finish. The fragrance stayed close to my skin and felt clean and understated.

Overall, The Scent of Peace Natural is an easy everyday fragrance — light enough for daytime, but with enough warmth and depth to keep it interesting throughout the day.

The Scent of Peace – For Him takes the collection in a deeper, more masculine direction, pairing freshness with a rich, confident character. Its aromatic composition balances crisp citrus and green notes with warmer woods and sensual undertones, resulting in a fragrance that feels refined while still having plenty of personality.

“The Scent of Peace — For Him” is a bit different for me in the Bond No. 9 lineup,” Frank Massaro, commerce partnerships manager, shared. “I have become accustomed to the leathery, deeper scents that permeate much of their product lines. The Scent of Peace for Him immediately stood out as bright and vibrant from the get-go, from the fun bowtie on the bottle to the fruity notes you get right away.”

Without fail, the scent settles in for a nice, subtle all-day wear. It also transitions effortlessly from the office to dinner, bringing a subtly confident finish to the Scent of Peace lineup.

It’s a fitting concept for a fragrance house whose identity has always been closely linked to New York. Bond No. 9 has built its collection around the city’s neighborhoods and landmarks, translating the character of NYC into perfume. With The Scent of Peace for All, the inspiration expands from a particular place to an idea that can travel anywhere.

The fragrance also arrives as New York prepares to welcome the annual United Nations General Assembly, when world leaders, activists and visionaries from around the globe converge on the city. Bond No. 9 founder Laurice Rahmé describes the scent as an invitation to embrace empathy, acceptance and hope.

Of course, perfume alone isn’t going to solve the world’s problems. But a fragrance can be a reminder (and this one comes in a pretty chic bottle).

The Scent of Peace collection is available in 50- and 100-milliliter sizes, giving fragrance lovers a chance to carry a little optimism wherever they go.
