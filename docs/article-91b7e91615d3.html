49ers fans involved in second consecutive week of fighting after Cowboys game at AT&T Stadium

2026-11-16 03:33

San Francisco 49ers fans were very present at AT&T Stadium on Sunday as they faced the Dallas Cowboys– two heated rivals in the NFL.

That rivalry spilled over in a very bad way with a massive brawl taking place at Texas Live, a large entertainment complex just outside the stadium between supporters of both fan bases after the 49ers’ 46-40 win in Week 10.

Police had to get involved to break things up, and likely book some people into the local jail as video from Dallas Texas TV social media accounts showcased the brutal altercation.

The video began with two police officers subduing one fan on the ground, though a fight continued to occur next to them.

A 49ers fan was seen tossing punches at a Cowboys fan, who was clearly fighting back. Some others around the incident were trying to break it up, but a quick camera pan showed another fight seemingly being de-escalated by a Cowboys fan restraining a 49ers fan who had been fighting.

Then, as police were escorting a fan away from the tense scene, another skirmish broke out. There were two women, one wearing a 49ers jacket and the other in Cowboys gear, were in each other’s faces as shoving seemed to be going on.

While it’s unclear what exactly caused the brawls to take place, 49ers fans have not been helping their reputation after what happened last week as they played another rival, the Las Vegas Raiders, at home in Santa Clara.

A massive parking lot brawl broke out last weekend between 49ers and Raiders fans, with one video going viral after fans were knocked out while throwing punches.

Nearly a dozen people were arrested and 45 were ejected from Levi’s Stadium for fighting, Santa Clara police told SF Gate.

"Incidents of violence at large-scale events in Santa Clara will not be tolerated," Santa Clara Police Department spokesperson Lt. Eric Lagergren told the outlet. "The Department strictly enforces the law and holds individuals accountable for criminal conduct. We will seek to arrest the individuals responsible for the fan-on-fan violence at the event."

Seven of the 11 people who were arrested were cited for disorderly conduct. Four were arrested for allegedly fighting in a public place and at least one received a drug possession charge.

It’s unknown how many arrests and ejections there may have been at AT&T Stadium, but these teams and fan bases certainly do not like each other and that is very apparent each time they match up during an NFL season.

Fox News’ Ryan Gaydos contributed to this report.
