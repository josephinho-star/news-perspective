Taylor Swift has fans spiraling over cryptic countdown and ‘hidden in plain sight’ clues

2026-09-22 17:50

Taylor Swift has the clock ticking – and Swifties are scrambling to figure out what happens when it hits zero.

Swift set the internet ablaze Tuesday after a countdown appeared on her website. The countdown was set to end at 2 p.m. ET on Sept. 22. It's unclear what will appear on the other side, but many fans have theories online.

The countdown comes after Swift's team seemingly added a third page to the letter Swift wrote to fans after buying back her life's work in May 2025. Her bio on Instagram and X also changed to read, "And, baby, that's sh0w business f0r y0u." Each o in the phrase was replaced with a 0.

Fans are speculating this could mean the release of the re-recording of her debut album, "Taylor Swift." Others believe it might have something to do with her latest album, "The Life of a Showgirl" – whether it's a tour announcement or a deluxe album release has not been agreed on by fans.

Swift has seemingly been dropping hints at something big since the Emmy Awards. The singer-songwriter appeared in a pre-taped "Law & Order: Special Victims Unit" sketch alongside Mariska Hargitay.

The squad jokingly investigated whether Swift would attend the ceremony, combing through a board packed with references and Easter eggs before Swift herself joined the case. During the bit, Swift suggested the answer was "hiding in plain sight" before rattling off a cryptic string of clues that quickly caught fans’ attention.

"Saccharide, Ares, From the vineyard, North or South," Swift said, which has now been widely dissected on social media.

Some fans claim rearranging and decoding the words reveals the message, "My first record's re-record is out Nov ten ha ha ha ha."

Others have zeroed in on the newly added third page of Swift's letter, claiming the third letter of each word spells out "Encore."

"My Prediction: I think it's a Showgirl Deluxe with vinyls (warehouse), a few more singles - 1 or 2 with music videos (MV Promo) - alongside some new merch to close era out," one social media user wrote. "Then VMAs performance with new singles. Then country music awards Debut announcement and Debut TV era starts."

"There's legitimately nobody more fun to stan like sorry if you don't get it," another Swiftie wrote on X.

"I love that I have 20 different theories on my timeline of what this could be and I haven't felt this alive since the podcast episode, WE ARE SO BACK," one user wrote.

What happens when the clock runs out remains a mystery, but Swift has a history of keeping fans on their toes with carefully timed announcements, surprise reveals and cryptic clues.

Swift used a countdown on her website to announce the release of her 12th studio album, "The Life of a Showgirl."
