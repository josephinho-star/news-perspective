Giants betting odds revealed after unfortunate Jaxson Dart injury update

2026-09-22 21:00

It appears that Jaxson Dart’s knee injury is far worse than initially thought, and oddsmakers wasted no time axing the G-Men’s betting outlook.

Their odds to make the playoffs opened at +310 on BetMGM and moved to +260 before Week 1.

Following a dominant home victory over Dallas, those postseason odds narrowed to +165.

However, with reports that Dart may require season-ending surgery, FanDuel drastically tanked Big Blue’s playoff odds to +560 Tuesday afternoon.

The Giants’ projected win total hovered between 7.5 and 8.5 in early markets and stood firmly at 8.5 prior to Monday’s 28-6 loss to the Los Angeles Rams.

Now sportsbooks have set their win line at 5.5 (-170), though bettors targeting sweet value can back over 6.5 wins at +105.

Their odds to secure a Super Bowl title have now cratered to +8000, signaling a dramatic shift from what once looked like an incredibly promising campaign.

“Evaluations are ongoing. We haven’t got any final decisions yet…we’ve learned a lot more. I wouldn’t want to share too much until decisions get made, ” head coach John Harbaugh shared with reporters during a Tuesday press conference.

With Dart out of the picture, NFL journeyman Jameis Winston will be the Giants quarterback moving forward.

The 12-year veteran took over Monday night, but he couldn’t thread the needle offensively, finishing just 11-of-27 for 111 passing yards and an interception.
