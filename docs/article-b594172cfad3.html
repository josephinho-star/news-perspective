Zay Flowers’ fumble could change everything at top of hyped 2027 NFL Draft

2026-11-17 15:20

The Titans (1-8) have now dropped below Los Angeles and hold the rights to the No. 1 pick, with the Chargers (1-8) sitting at No. 2.

The Dolphins (1-8), Jets (2-8) and Eagles (2-7) round out the top five in that respective order.

The 2027 NFL Draft features a projected generational receiving talent in Ohio State receiver Jeremiah Smith, while there are varying opinions about the quarterback class.

While it would have been a complete stunner had the Chargers gone winless this year, their win changes the outlook of the bottom-feeding teams due to remaining schedules.

Los Angeles hosts the Jets this weekend in a game with major implications, and then visits the Dolphins in Week 16.

The Jets then visit the Dolphins in Week 12, meaning their next two weeks will very likely influence whether they stay in the top five and vie for the top pick.

The Titans holding the No. 1 pick is an intriguing wrinkle since they used their top picks in the last two drafts to select quarterback Cam Ward first in 2025 and receiver Carnell Tate fourth this year.

Tennessee, should it have the No. 1 pick, would have to decide whether to dump Ward for the top quarterback in the class, use back-to-back top-five picks on receivers — a strategy most teams avoid — pick the best defensive player or potentially trade back for a possible king’s ransom.

A trade-back scenario could be good for a team like the Jets, who have three first-round picks all currently projected to be in the top 18.

The Eagles are the only non-AFC team in the projected top five, and their fate will likely be decided in Weeks 13 and 14 when they visit the Cardinals (3-7) and host the Colts (3-7), in that order.

As the NFL teams jockey for position, the final few weeks of the college football regular season, plus the bowl games and College Football Playoff, will help shape the prospect rankings.

The quarterback class had once been projected as an era-defining group, but inconsistency from Arch Manning, a season-ending injury suffered by Dante Moore and mediocre play from other top names suddenly has raised questions about the group.

The fascinating element with Smith being the consensus top prospect is that only one receiver has been drafted No. 1 overall in the last 30 years — Keyshawn Johnson by the Jets in 1996.

Teams usually go for the franchise-changing quarterback over the franchise-changing receiver, but in this draft there may not be any sure-fire star signal callers.

The 2024 NFL Draft also is a cautionary tale for those believing Smith is a can’t-miss talent as fellow Buckeye Marvin Harrison Jr. entered the draft viewed as an instant star.

He’s instead been mediocre at best through two-and-a-half seasons, already traded from the Cardinals to the Texans and struggling to find footing with his new team.
