Justin Herbert ruled out against Patriots as DJ Uiagalelei gets first career start for Chargers

2026-11-28 00:15

The brutal season for the Los Angeles Chargers continues with news that quarterback Justin Herbert will miss this weekend's game against the Patriots.

In his place will be second-year QB DJ Uiagalelei, who will make his first career start.

Herbert played all of last weekend's game against the New York Jets, which the Chargers lost 27-24, but he wound up in concussion protocol.

Seeing as he hasn't been cleared yet, the keys to the Chargers' offense have been handed to Uiagalelei.

He'll have his work cut out for him trying to help the 1-9 team get another win this year, but sometimes that infusion of new blood is precisely what a team needs.

Even with a fresh face under center, though, it’s fair to wonder if anything can save a season that’s been this brutal.

The team is currently the 31st-most potent offense in the NFL, while Herbert is dealing with the worst season of his career. He's got a career-low 62.4% completion percentage for 1,851 yards, 8 TDs (two of which came against the Jets), and 11 interceptions.

Herbert has not dealt with too many injuries during his NFL career. In fact, this will mark the first start he has missed since the 2023 season, during which he missed the last four games of the year due to a broken finger.

Uiagalelei has made one other appearance this season in a game against the Houston Texans in which he went 2-for-7 and threw for 24 yards.

He'll be backed up by Jake Browning and will coincidentally be starting against another backup as the Patriots plan to go with Tommy DeVito.
