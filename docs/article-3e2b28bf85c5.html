Amr Diab to make Türkiye debut, support Palestine aid

2026-10-02 08:07

One of the biggest stars of Arab pop music worldwide, Amr Diab, will take the stage at Ataköy Marina Arena on Saturday, Oct. 3, marking his first concert in Türkiye. Another significant aspect of the concert is that a portion of the profits from the event will be donated to humanitarian aid efforts in Palestine.

Having crossed the boundaries of Middle Eastern music decades ago, Diab’s songs have reached audiences across a vast region, from Cairo to Dubai and Beirut to Europe. The singer will finally bring his music to Istanbul, where he will perform at Ataköy Marina Arena.

The concert is particularly notable given Diab’s place in the Arab pop music scene. By blending Arab pop with modern pop, electronic production and Mediterranean influences, he has developed a distinctive musical identity and established himself as one of the Middle East’s leading pop figures.

The Istanbul event will also have a social solidarity dimension. According to a statement from the organizers, part of the profits will be donated to humanitarian aid efforts in Palestine.

The long-awaited Istanbul debut comes as Diab continues to draw audiences across the region and beyond, bringing his distinctive blend of Arab pop and contemporary sounds to Türkiye for the first time.
