Savannah Chrisley is ‘in for a rocky ride’ with much older baby daddy Jason Galanis, his own brother warns: ‘His biggest con yet’

2026-10-28 22:03

Savannah Chrisley is her fiancé Jason Galanis’ “biggest con yet,” according to the convicted fraudster’s own brother.

Galanis, 56, — a career criminal and ex-business associate of disgraced former first son Hunter Biden — was identified Tuesday as the mystery man engaged to reality star Chrisley, 29, and expecting a baby with her.

“It’s his biggest con yet,” Galanis’s estranged brother, Derek Galanis, claimed to Page Six in an exclusive interview. “This girl’s in for a rocky ride.

“He’s not a changed man, he’s the same dude he always was.”

Once dubbed “Porn’s New King,” he was sentenced to 189 months in prison in Sept. 2020 for his role in multiple security fraud schemes.

He served time at Federal Prison Camp in Pensacola, Fla., in 2023, alongside Savannah’s dad Todd Chrisley, before the pair were pardoned by President Trump last year.

“My brother is one year younger than her father,” Derek said. “They were in prison together, right?

“Both my brother and her father were pardoned by Trump. This is a continuation. It’s wild.”

“Chrisley Knows Best” star Savannah announced her pregnancy and engagement to a man named “Jason” — later identified as Galanis — in an Instagram post Tuesday.

“I’ve prayed for this life. I’ve cried for this life. And there were moments I truly wondered if this life would ever be mine,” she captioned the video with Jason and her growing baby bump.

“You are not a headline. You are not a controversy,” she wrote. “You are not defined by anyone’s past, anyone’s mistakes, or anyone’s opinions.”

Jason was a business partner of Hunter’s from 2012 to 2015, he told the House Oversight Committee in 2024.

Derek, 54, was convicted of fraud in relation to a securities conspiracy led by his brother Jason and their white-collar criminal father, John Galanis, between 2009 and 2011, and later wrote about it in his book Greed and Fear: The Galanis Crime Family.

He said the last time he spoke to his brother was in 2016 while they were locked up together at the Metropolitan Correctional Center in New York.

“My noise about the Hunter Biden stuff is what got him out of prison,” Derek said.

“You’d think he’d picked up the phone to say, ‘Hey, Derek, thanks for that.’ No, it’s off to the next thing.”

Hunter Biden, 56, and Jason were both equity partners at Burnham Financial Group (BFG), where Hunter served as vice chairman.

Through BFG subsidiary Burnham Securities, Jason orchestrated a $60 million fraud scheme to sell fake municipal bonds to the Wakpamni Lake Community of the Oglala Sioux Tribe in South Dakota, according to federal court documents.

Jason cooperated while behind bars with House Republican investigators who were looking into his dealings with the 46th president’s son.

President Trump commuted Jason’s sentence last year while he was locked up at a corrections center in Montgomery, Ala.

In his 2024 testimony to the House Oversight Committee, Hunter played down his ties to Jason.

“I think that 10 years ago for 30 minutes I was introduced to Jason Galanis, and that’s only – the only time I ever recall meeting him,” he said.
