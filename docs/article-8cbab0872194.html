Our guide to every Week 10 game: Matchup previews, predictions, picks and nuggets

2026-11-14 12:25

Week 10 of the 2026 NFL season is here. The trade deadline came and went, with some surprising moves. QB Kirk Cousins is expected to be the Vikings' starting quarterback once again, the Chiefs added WR Josh Downs and ... RB Jonathan Taylor is still in Indianapolis. A few teams added and others subtracted from their rosters. So, now it's time to dive back into the games.

We're here to get you ready for the action. First, our NFL Nation reporters take you inside the locker room with the best thing they heard this week, and ESPN Research provides a key stat to know. Then, analytics writer Aaron Schatz makes a bold prediction for each matchup, fantasy analyst Eric Moody shares fantasy football intel and betting analyst Pamela Maldonado hits betting insights for each contest. We also have Football Power Index (FPI) game projections, and Maldonado, Moody and Schatz all give us final score picks for every game.

Let's get into the full Week 10 slate, which culminates with a "Monday Night Football" matchup between the Chargers and Ravens on ESPN. (Game times are Sunday unless otherwise noted, and all odds are via DraftKings.)

What ESPN Analytics says ...

Patriots (5-4) at Lions (6-2) in Munich

9:30 a.m. ET | Fox | DET -2.5 (51.5 O/U)

What we're hearing on the Patriots: New England is hoping to rebound from a poor defensive effort last week. Coordinator Zak Kuhr said CB Christian Gonzalez had some plays he would want back, but also said there were other plays where he could have been the closest defender because of breakdowns elsewhere. Kuhr acknowledged that very little went right for the defense but thought the unit took steps Wednesday in a spirited practice that featured better communication and connectivity. Kuhr added that the team's belief in Gonzalez hasn't wavered. -- Mike Reiss

What we're hearing on the Lions: It's not every day that the Lions are selected to play in an international game. In fact, it hasn't happened since 2015, so this is a huge deal for the franchise. WR Amon-Ra St. Brown's mother, Miriam, hails from Germany, and he frequently visited the country as a child. This will be a new experience for coach Dan Campbell. "As far as I've been across the pond is London, and that was for [a game]," Campbell said. "And I already mentioned, I had an extra pair of underwear and socks and that was it, all right. And a toothbrush, but that's as far as I've been over there." -- Eric Woodyard

Stat to know: Lions QB Jared Goff has had consecutive games with fewer than 200 passing yards for just the second time over the past five seasons. -- ESPN Research

Bold prediction: Patriots QB Drake Maye will not throw an interception against the Lions. His 11 interceptions this season are the second most in the league. In general, about 20% of passes defensed turn into interceptions. It's been a little lower leaguewide this season (16%). For Maye, it has been 34%. So he has been unlucky and shouldn't be as interception prone going forward. -- Schatz

Fantasy nugget: Corey Kiner could be the Patriots' lead RB against the Lions. Rhamondre Stevenson is expected to miss the game with a knee injury, and TreVeyon Henderson has been ruled out. Kiner has topped 50 rushing yards in three of his past five games despite playing a reserve role. Detroit is a difficult matchup, allowing the second-fewest fantasy points per game to RBs. However, Kiner could see enough volume to be a viable flex option. See Week 10 rankings. -- Moody

Betting insight: The Patriots have failed to cover four straight games, tied for the NFL's longest active streak and their longest since a five-game stretch in 2024. Detroit can keep that going. New England allows 10.6 yards per pass attempt against play-action, which Goff uses constantly. St. Brown can attack the slot, while New England's battered offensive line leaves Maye dodging pressure. Read more. -- Maldonado

Maldonado's pick: Lions 28, Patriots 23

Moody's pick: Lions 31, Patriots 24

Schatz's pick: Patriots 27, Lions 24

FPI prediction: DET, 52.6% (by an average of 0.7 points)

Matchup must-reads: Pats' Stevenson expected to miss time with knee tendon issue

1 p.m. ET | CBS | BUF -8.5 (50.5 O/U)

What we're hearing on the Bills: Coming off a short week following "Monday Night Football," the Bills are still easing LT Dion Dawkins and LG Alec Anderson back into the lineup. But the Bills are plenty aware of Jets coach Aaron Glenn's aggressive tendencies and standout rookie edge rusher David Bailey's recent hot streak. Coach Joe Brady and offensive coordinator Pete Carmichael said they'll have to be aware of where Bailey lines up every play, and the task of keeping him out of the backfield is urgent: "He's already a star right now," Brady says, "but he's going to be a problem for a lot of years." -- Nick Suss

What we're hearing on the Jets: The spiraling defense has allowed 32 points per game over the team's current four-game losing streak. Glenn said he's "looking at everything" in an attempt to halt the slide. He doesn't have many personnel options, though it appears that disgruntled edge rusher Will McDonald IV will get more playing time than usual. The Jets have another long interception drought brewing -- five straight games without a pick, 15 shy of the league record (set by them just prior to the current five-game slump). -- Rich Cimini

Stat to know: Bills RB James Cook III has had 75-plus rushing yards in seven straight games, the longest active streak in the NFL and the longest by a Bills player since Travis Henry in 2003 (eight). -- ESPN Research

Bold prediction: Jets WR Garrett Wilson (concussion) will return and set a season high with at least 110 receiving yards. Buffalo currently ranks 27th in DVOA (Defense-adjusted Value Over Average) against WR1s, and Wilson is the Jets' clear top option. -- Schatz

Fantasy nugget: Jets RB Breece Hall returned from a five-game absence last week and led the Jets' backfield with 18 touches and 11.7 fantasy points. Before a quad injury, Hall had been the clear lead back on early downs, but he split that work much more evenly with Braelon Allen in Week 9. Allen also played more snaps and ran twice as many routes. Hall could see his snap share increase this week and has a favorable matchup against a Bills defense that has allowed the ninth-most fantasy points per game to RBs. See Week 10 rankings. -- Moody

Betting insight: The Jets are 1-7 ATS (against the spread) in division games since the start of last season, worst in the NFL. Their defense has been getting picked apart lately, and now QB Josh Allen gets his turn. Buffalo scores on more than half its possessions. The Jets struggle to finish drives with touchdowns, and trading field goals for touchdowns makes keeping up much harder. Read more. -- Maldonado

1 p.m. ET | Fox | NO -2.5 (48.5 O/U)

What we're hearing on the Panthers: Interim defensive coordinator Todd Wash won't mince words when he talks about his philosophy: "We're going to load the box. We're going to stop the run and make you throw to beat us." Rookie defensive tackle Lee Hunter, a second-round pick, should be a big part of that plan against the Saints. Hunter played only 13 snaps against Denver, but Wash says the lineman's playing time will be a "major, major point of emphasis" moving forward. -- Mike Kaye

What we're hearing on the Saints: The run game could look different for the second straight week. One week after claiming Audric Estime, the Saints could get Travis Etienne Jr. back from a hamstring injury that has kept him on IR since September. They'll also have another offensive line shuffle the week after getting LT Kelvin Banks Jr. back, as Erik McCoy (biceps) is out. RG Cesar Ruiz will replace him at center, the position Ruiz played at Michigan. -- Katherine Terrell

Stat to know: Saints WR Chris Olave has 734 receiving yards on the season, ranked sixth in the NFL. However, New Orleans is 0-3 when he records 100-plus receiving yards in a game. -- ESPN Research

Bold prediction: Panthers WR Tetairoa McMillan will have a 100-yard receiving day. New Orleans ranks 30th in DVOA against opposing WR1s. They allow an opponent-adjusted 87 yards per game to opposing WR1s but only 27 yards per game to opposing WR2s. -- Schatz

Fantasy nugget: Panthers QB Bryce Young is coming off a strong performance, finishing with 20.3 fantasy points. His production has been inconsistent, but he's still a top-12 fantasy QB. Young also has a talented receiving duo in McMillan and Jalen Coker. This week, he faces a Saints defense that allowed Browns backup QB Shedeur Sanders 22.6 fantasy points last week and has given up 17.9 fantasy points per game to QBs this season. See Week 10 rankings. -- Moody

Betting insight: The Panthers are 10-2 ATS following a loss since the start of last season, best in the NFL. Young just showed he can get the ball out quickly and also attack downfield. That will come into play against New Orleans' pass rush. The Saints allow 7.8 yards per attempt, giving Young and McMillan plenty of room to make them pay. Read more. -- Maldonado

What we're hearing on the Jaguars: There's still some uncertainty about how much -- or if -- newly acquired DT Jordan Davis, S Jessie Bates III and DT Davon Godchaux will play Sunday. Godchaux practiced Wednesday and Thursday, but Bates and Davis did not practice Wednesday. Coach Liam Coen said he'll evaluate their progress late in the week before making any decisions. -- Michael DiRocco

What we're hearing on the Titans: Tennessee has found success running the ball over the past two games, with Tony Pollard going over 100 rushing yards against both the Browns and Bengals. Pollard will look to hit triple figures for the third consecutive game, but the Titans might be without LG Peter Skoronski, who has missed practice time this week because of a hamstring injury. This week will be a bigger challenge against the Jaguars, who have allowed the fifth-fewest rushing yards per game (89.3) but gave up 172 yards on the ground last week. -- Turron Davenport

Stat to know: The Titans have grabbed multiple takeaways in three straight games, their longest streak since 2021 (three straight). A fourth straight game would be their longest single-season streak since 2008 (five). -- ESPN Research

Bold prediction: Jaguars edge rusher Josh Hines-Allen will get a sack. He has only two sacks this season, but he'll get at least one more against Tennessee LT Dan Moore Jr., who ranks 55th out of 66 qualified tackles in pass block win rate. -- Schatz

Fantasy nugget: Jaguars QB Trevor Lawrence and WR Parker Washington weren't asked to do much in Jacksonville's blowout win over Baltimore, finishing with 13.5 and 14.1 fantasy points, respectively. Both have a great opportunity to bounce back. The Titans have allowed the eighth-most fantasy points per game to QBs and the most to WRs. Lawrence has also averaged 23.4 fantasy points in his past four games against Tennessee. See Week 10 rankings. -- Moody

Betting insight: The Jaguars are 3-0 ATS as favorites of 10 or more points since the start of last season, tied for the NFL's best record. Tennessee keeps giving up big passing plays, and Lawrence has the receivers to exploit that. Jacksonville has jumped on teams early, while Tennessee keeps falling behind. That could leave QB Cam Ward playing catch-up. Read more. -- Maldonado

1 p.m. ET | CBS | KC -3 (46.5 O/U)

What we're hearing on the Chiefs: WR Josh Downs is expected to make his debut with the Chiefs following Tuesday's trade from the Colts. Before his first practice Wednesday, Downs explained how excited he is to be playing with QB Patrick Mahomes. "I mean, who wouldn't love playing with a quarterback of his caliber? You can't really put it into words. I've been following Mahomes for years. When people are playing in college, that's the quarterback that everybody is like, 'I want to go play with Mahomes.' It's truly a blessing. He makes plays in the pocket but out of the pocket as well. Having that scramble aspect is really big, and he makes a lot of plays out of that." -- Nate Taylor

What we're hearing on the Falcons: The Chiefs are middle of the pack in blitz rate and QB pressure rate, but don't be surprised if they dial things up against the Falcons like they did in Week 9, when they had a 50% pressure rate. Atlanta's Michael Penix Jr. completes a league-low 34% when pressured, which is the second lowest by a qualified QB in a season since at least 2018, per NFL Next Gen Stats. The Falcons have yet to figure out how to make blitzing teams pay, and RB Bijan Robinson has just one touchdown since Week 4. -- Marc Raimondi

Stat to know: The Chiefs' offense has had five games with 150 or more rushing yards, which is tied for the most in a season under coach Andy Reid. -- ESPN Research

Bold prediction: Robinson will have more than 50 yards as a receiver. The Chiefs rank 28th in DVOA against RBs as receivers, allowing an (opponent-adjusted) average of 40 yards per game, and Robinson is about as good a receiving back as there is. -- Schatz

Fantasy nugget: Falcons TE Kyle Pitts Sr. hasn't reached 25 receiving yards in any of his past three games, despite seeing at least five targets in each. Atlanta's offense has struggled during that stretch, averaging the fifth-fewest total yards per game. Pitts has a more favorable matchup against the Chiefs, who have allowed the sixth-most fantasy points per game to TEs while giving up the second fewest to WRs. That could lead to more opportunities for Pitts in the passing game and give him a chance to bounce back. See Week 10 rankings. -- Moody

Betting insight: The Chiefs are 5-13 ATS in November over the past five seasons, worst in the NFL. Kansas City's defense allows 2.18 points per drive on the road, compared with 1.32 at home. Penix has played better at home, too. If Robinson can stay consistent, the Falcons can make this a competitive fourth-quarter game. Read more. -- Maldonado

1 p.m. ET | CBS | IND -3.5 (38.5 O/U)

What we're hearing on the Dolphins: QB Kyle McCord will start for at least the next four weeks while Malik Willis is sidelined with a groin injury -- and the first snaps McCord takes Sunday will be his first with the first-team offense in a game situation. Miami traded for McCord in August after the preseason ended, but the team feels comfortable with his grasp of the playbook, despite limited reps. McCord said he prides himself on being able to run anything OC Bobby Slowik wants to call. At the same time, coach Jeff Hafley said the Dolphins don't have time to install a new offense, so there will be a learning curve for all parties as they figure out what works. -- Marcel Louis-Jacques

What we're hearing on the Colts: Indianapolis completed its midseason roster overhaul by trading WRs Josh Downs and Keenan Allen, and that has left a shell of a group remaining at the position. The Colts receivers who will take the field Sunday have a combined 27 receptions and 375 yards. Downs and Allen had a combined 73 catches for 705 yards but now will be playing for the Chiefs and 49ers, respectively. New slot receiver Anthony Gould, who replaces Downs, has four career catches and has played only two offensive snaps this season. -- Stephen Holder

Stat to know: The Colts have scored 23 or fewer points in five straight games. A sixth straight game would tie their second-longest single-season streak in the past 30 seasons (11 straight in 2017, six consecutive in 2011). -- ESPN Research

Bold prediction: Chop Robinson will have a pair of sacks. The Dolphins' edge rusher is the surprising midseason leader in pass rush win rate (32.7%), while the Colts have an average (13th) pass-blocking offensive line. -- Schatz

Fantasy nugget: The Dolphins could lean more heavily on Ollie Gordon II and the running game since Willis is on IR. Gordon hasn't scored double-digit fantasy points since Week 7, but he has had at least 17 touches in each of his past three games. That workload is encouraging heading into a favorable matchup against a defense that has allowed the eighth-most fantasy points per game to RBs. See Week 10 rankings. -- Moody

Betting insight: The Colts are this season's first favorite with a losing streak of at least five games. Those teams went 1-3 ATS last season. Indianapolis can buck that. Miami is starting McCord, and losing Willis removes any scrambling threat from the position. The Dolphins allow 8.1 yards per pass attempt, giving Daniel Jones a chance to finally get something going. Read more. -- Maldonado

1 p.m. ET | Fox | GB -3 (42.5 O/U)

What we're hearing on the Vikings: The Vikings celebrated the acquisition of QB Kirk Cousins this week, hoping he can pick up where he left off when he last played for them in 2023. (His last game wearing their uniform was, coincidentally, at Lambeau Field.) Specific to Sunday, can they protect Cousins? Vikings QBs this season have been pressured an NFL-high 46.9% of the time. Some of that has been the result of QB indecision, but the scheme has also struggled to adjust to stunts and keep pass rushers out of the backfield. -- Kevin Seifert

What we're hearing on the Packers: The Packers aren't ignoring their 39-22 loss to the Vikings to open the season, but they're not dwelling on it in advance of the rematch, either. The Packers' offensive line was a mess that day, and there's no worse place to have offensive line issues than at the raucous U.S. Bank Stadium. "You might as well throw Week 1 out the window in most instances," coach Matt LaFleur said. "There's a lot of craziness that goes on -- unscouted looks and you just never know what you're going to get. I do think it's going to be helpful. That place is such a hard place to play, and it caused us some issues where we didn't have everybody on the same page, especially from an offensive standpoint." -- Rob Demovsky

Stat to know: The Packers' 19 takeaways are the second most in the NFL (Buccaneers, 21). -- ESPN Research

Bold prediction: Vikings kick returner Myles Price will return at least one kickoff past the 50, maybe for a touchdown. The Packers are currently 30th in my net kickoff value metric, while the Vikings rank fourth in kick return value. -- Schatz

Fantasy nugget: The Vikings have scored only 11 offensive touchdowns in eight games, tied with the Chargers for the fewest in the league. Cousins' return could help turn things around. He spent two seasons playing under coach Kevin O'Connell and already has an established connection with Justin Jefferson and Jordan Addison. There could be an adjustment period, but both WRs belong in lineups against a Packers defense that has allowed the sixth-most fantasy points per game to the position. See Week 10 rankings. -- Moody

Betting insight: The under is 6-1 in Vikings games, 4-0 on the road. That's without having an efficient QB. Cousins helps, but him joining midweek brings adjustments. Green Bay defends deep throws well and limits touchdowns off play-action, a big part of Cousins' success. Both offenses have problems to work around here. Minnesota's pressure can trouble QB Jordan Love behind a banged-up line. Read more. -- Maldonado

What we're hearing on the Texans: The Texans have some RB questions. Woody Marks (knee) didn't finish practice Wednesday and didn't participate Thursday, while David Montgomery (groin) hasn't played since Week 5. But Montgomery did practice Thursday, so that's promising. Rain is expected in Cleveland, so while Houston's passing attack has been humming, it'll likely need the ground game to supplement the offense. -- DJ Bien-Aime

What we're hearing on the Browns: The Browns' defense might face its toughest test to date against QB C.J. Stroud. Cleveland plays Cover 1 on a league-high 39.8% of passing plays, but Stroud has feasted on that coverage; his 8.3 yards per attempt against Cover 1 are eighth most among qualified passers. "He's been slinging the ball all around the field, getting those guys the ball and just a really good team," CB Denzel Ward said. "So we're definitely going to have our work cut out for us this week, and I think we're up for the challenge, though." -- Daniel Oyefusi

Stat to know: The Browns have scored a touchdown on 39% of red zone possessions (last in the NFL). -- ESPN Research

Bold prediction: Browns QB Deshaun Watson will be sacked by both of Houston's edge rushers (Will Anderson Jr. and Danielle Hunter). Watson has the worst adjusted sack rate (12.0%) of any regular starting QB in the NFL this season. -- Schatz

Fantasy nugget: Backup QB Shedeur Sanders gave Cleveland's passing game a lift last week. WR KC Concepcion finished with 85 yards and a touchdown. Denzel Boston drew a team-high seven targets and finished with a career-high 98 yards. Deshaun Watson should be back in the lineup, but the receiver matchup against Houston is favorable, with the Texans allowing the second-most fantasy points per game to WRs. Both rookies are worth considering as flex options. See Week 10 rankings. -- Moody

Betting insight: The Texans are 9-3 ATS against the Browns since 2005. Cleveland's protection problems give Houston another opening. The Browns allow sacks at the league's highest rate, while Houston has the second-highest sack rate. That means stalled drives for an offense already struggling to finish near the goal line. Stroud has also handled man coverage without throwing an interception. Read more. -- Maldonado

That said, Darien Porter and rookies Jermod McCoy and Hezekiah Masses should get their fair share of opportunities against the reigning Offensive Player of the Year. The Raiders have allowed 234.2 passing yards per game (26th). -- Ryan McFadden

Stat to know: Bowers has had seven straight games with 70 receiving yards, which is tied for the longest streak by a TE in history (Rob Gronkowski). -- ESPN Research

Bold prediction: Seahawks RBs Jadarian Price and Zach Charbonnet will each run for at least 70 yards. The Raiders are dead last in run defense DVOA and also give up a ton of long highlight runs. Las Vegas can't lean on a run game averaging 2.9 yards per carry. With Drew Lock dealing with elbow and oblique injuries, Seattle can keep things simple and run against this Raiders' defense. Read more. -- Maldonado

Garrett and Donald have combined for 6.5 sacks and 10 pressures across six games against QBs making their first career start. -- Sarah Barshop

"You look at their defense just as a whole, they're super talented," Beck said. He went on to praise the entire unit, not mentioning Garrett or Donald by name. "What they're able to do on defense, it causes problems for offenses, and it's definitely going to be a challenge for us," Beck said. "I think the biggest thing is watching the film, seeing what we want to do, and then ultimately trying to go out there and execute our plan." -- Josh Weinfuss

Stafford is missing Nacua and Adams, and the Rams scored only 16 points in their first meeting with both receivers available. Their depleted passing game could keep this one close again. Read more. -- Maldonado

4:25 p.m. ET | Fox | SF -3.5 (55.5 O/U)

What we're hearing on the 49ers: San Francisco is embarking on its second 10-plus day trip of the season, as the 49ers head to Dallas before spending next week in Colorado Springs for altitude acclimation before meeting the Vikings in Mexico City. It's a lot of time away from home, but LB Fred Warner said it's easy to keep the focus on the Cowboys given the history the franchises share and what another win can do for San Francisco's hopes to land the NFC's top seed. "I love the energy around the old rivalry of Dallas versus the Niners," Warner said. "In my mind, this is the Super Bowl this week." -- Nick Wagoner

What we're hearing on the Cowboys: Dallas is still trying to earn a signature win in 2026. If the Cowboys can beat the 49ers, then perhaps they can turn a corner and put pressure on the Giants in the NFC East. The Cowboys have lost their only game to a team with a winning record this season (Green Bay) and are 2-4-1 overall against winning teams under coach Brian Schottenheimer.
