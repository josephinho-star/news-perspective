Get on the bus: New mobile medical clinic aims to keep NYC cops healthy and prevent sudden deaths

2026-09-19 15:03

A new health screening vehicle being rolled out by one of the city’s police union aims to catch officers’ health problems before they’re too late, The Post has learned.

“We’re out here doing what we have to do to make sure the city’s safe,” said Bronx Det. Carlos Losada, a trustee with the Detectives Endowment Association. “But we tend to forget ourselves in that whole process.”

The 38-foot RV will go to police precincts throughout the city to screen members for heart and other health problems, the union said.

“They get to go on work time to get a full comprehensive physical,” Siciliano said. “We try to focus on professions like fire and police that normally have a higher risk of heart disease.”

The company also provides the service to police and fire departments in Suffolk County and Nassau County, the New York State Police and in multiple jails, he said.

Det. Lydell Harrigan, who’s 47 and has been on the job for 20 years, was one of 21 cops who signed up for appointments on the van Friday while it was at One Police Plaza in Lower Manhattan.
