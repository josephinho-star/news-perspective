Will There Be A ‘Ted Lasso’ Season 4 Episode 11? How Many Episodes Are In ‘Ted Lasso’ Season 4?

2026-10-07 00:00

When the end credits roll on Ted Lasso Season 4, Episode 10, we can’t imagine fans of AFC Richmond wanting to say goodbye to Apple TV’s Emmy-winning feel-good comedy yet.

The official synopsis for Ted Lasso Episode 410 — which is either called “The Departing Lass” or”Being Alive,” depending on whether you check the press site or the streaming platform — promised viewers “Big changes in bunches.” So will it deliver?

The eventful episode will follow the devastating loss of the Crown & Anchor pub owner Mae Greene (Annette Badland), the reveal that Zelda Southport (Tracey Ullman) is conspiring to steal the club from Rebecca (Hannah Waddingham), Roy (Brett Goldstein) and Keeley’s (Juno Temple) big baby news, and another major win for Ted (Jason Sudeikis) and the Lady Greyhounds.

So what happens next?!

Curious how many episodes are in Ted Lasso Season 4? Will there be a Ted Lasso Season 4, Episode 11, or is Episode 10 the Ted Lasso Season 4 finale? And if so, will there be a Ted Lasso Season 5?

Read on to learn more about the future of Ted Lasso, including Season 4’s finale date, episode count, Season 5 info, and beyond.

How Many Episodes Are In Ted Lasso Season 4?

Ted Lasso Season 4 consists of 10 episodes.

Will There Be A Ted Lasso Season 4, Episode 11?

Sadly, no. Ted Lasso Episode 10 is the Season 4 finale. If you were expecting more episodes, that could be because Ted Lasso Season 2 and Season 3 had 12 episodes each.

Will There Be A Season 5 Of Ted Lasso? Or Is Ted Lasso Over?

Ahead of Ted Lasso‘s Season 4 finale, Apple TV has yet to officially renew Ted Lasso for a fifth season, but that’s not to say Ted Lasso Season 5 isn’t a possibility. The Emmy-winning series is one of Apple TV’s most popular original shows. Heck, that’s part of the reason why it returned for Season 4! And when Decider spoke with stars Abbie Hern and Tanya Reynolds ahead of the finale, they expressed interest in and hopes for Season 5.

Given the success of the series and the large fanbase, we can certainly see Apple greenlighting more seasons of Ted Lasso, especially since there’s more of the Lady Greyhounds’ story to tell, and Sudeikis told The Hollywood Reporter he revived the series with another three-season arc in mind. So stay tuned for more updates!

When Will Ted Lasso Season 5 Premiere? Ted Lasso Season 5 Release Date Info:

Since Ted Lasso has yet to be officially renewed, there’s no Ted Lasso Season 5 premiere date on the books yet. And considering there was a three-year gap between Season 3 and Season 4, who’s to say how long it could take to get more new episodes? It seems like that’s in Sudeikis’ hands. But based on the original three-season run, Season 1 and Season 2 premiered a little less than a year apart in 2020 and 2021, and Season 2 and Season 3 had around a year-and-eight months-long gap between them.

So if renewal, writing, and production happened without major delays, here’s hoping we could see new episodes in fall 2027, if not early to mid 2028. Again, stay tuned for updates.

How To Watch Ted Lasso: Apple TV Streaming Info

Since Ted Lasso is an Apple TV original series, you’ll have to subscribe to the platform to watch new and existing episodes.

Apple TV comes with a seven-day free trial for new subscribers and has just one ad-free streaming plan available for $14.99/month.

A bundle with Apple TV and Peacock is also available starting at $14.99/month.
