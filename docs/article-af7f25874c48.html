Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum protests new DEA tip line for reporting criminals, corrupt officials

2026-11-28 07:08

Published Nov. 27, 2026, 11:08 p.m. PT

See more of our coverage in your search results.

Mexico has filed a formal complaint with the United States over a new Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) tip line that allows anonymous callers to report corrupt government officials and cartel operatives, Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum said Friday.

Sheinbaum, who has repeatedly pushed back against US actions targeting Mexican drug cartels, announced the diplomatic protest during her daily morning briefing.

“The United States has a responsibility to its people to address drug use, as well as to arrest members of the drug cartels operating in the United States,” Sheinbaum said. “We cannot have this view that the responsibility lies with Mexico.”

Her remarks came days after Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as “El Chapo Isidro,” an ally of a Sinaloa Cartel faction, was captured in Mexico during a joint US-Mexican operation.
