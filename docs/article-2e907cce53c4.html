Gulf tech firms turn to Hong Kong and Singapore as Iran war grinds on

2026-09-23 00:00

Analysts point to growing adoption of a ‘dual-hub’ model, using secondary Asian bases to hedge against geopolitical risk

Paul Bratby had already drawn up plans for expanding to Hong Kong when war broke out in the Middle East earlier this year.

But the founder of xBratAI, a Dubai-based AI-powered trading signals platform, soon brought that timeline forward as the Iran conflict dragged on and widened in scope.

“Our Hong Kong entity had been scheduled for a few years later, but we established it in early 2026,” he said.
