Adored SoCal mom of seven struck and killed trying to save dog hit by car

2026-10-13 03:15

An 86-year-old California mother of seven was hit and killed on the street after sprinting into traffic to save her injured dog.

Mary Wear was helping her injured dog when she was fatally hit by a car driven by a 70-year-old woman in Apple Valley in the Inland Empire.

Wear –– who was also known as “Meeka” –– was struck at around 8:15 p.m. on Waalew Road while attempting to save her pet dog Benji, who had just been hit by another vehicle.

“Meeka was a beloved mother of seven children, grandmother of four, and great-grandmother of nine. She was the heart of her family, a treasured friend to many, and a woman whose warmth, kindness, and love touched the lives of everyone fortunate enough to know her,” a GoFundMe created on her behalf said.

“Even at 86 years young, Meeka continued to embrace life with enthusiasm and a wonderful sense of purpose. Most recently, she had been helping her daughter and granddaughter bring a special dream to life through the opening of the Buc N Doe Restaurant in Fawnskin,” the fundraiser continued.

The GoFundMe was created to help create a “beautiful and lasting” memorial for Meeka and her two sons who died before her. The 86-year-old grandmother had already made arrangements for her own cremation.

The driver who hit the grandmother remained at the scene and cooperated with the investigation. Drugs and alcohol were not a factor in the incident according to KTLA 5.

Prior to her death, Meeka had told her daughter to “cremate me and spread my ashes at the Buc N Doe” when she died.

The GoFundMe has raised over $1,000 of its $1,500 as of Monday evening.
