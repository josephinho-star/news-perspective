Inside ‘lucky’ Kai Trump’s luxe apartment as University of Miami freshman starts college

2026-09-07 14:45

Kai Trump gave a tour of her Florida apartment as her freshman year at the University of Miami begins.

Vanessa Trump and Donald Trump Jr.’s daughter told her YouTube subscribers Saturday that she’d decided to “show it off” since “so many [people] were, like, dying to see” the space.

The 18-year-old called her residence “pretty cool,” gushing, “I’m very, very lucky to have this obviously as a freshman. I love it. It’s very nice coming home to no one.”

She started the tour by walking into the kitchen, which she dubbed her “favorite part” because she “eat[s] a lot.”

The college student highlighted a framed black-and-white photo of gondolas in Aspen, Colorado, since she “love[s] skiing” — as well as a New York City skyline shot in the living room.

Kai panned over the white couch and TV before pointing out, “[There’s] my fireplace, which is, like, I think, the coolest part of this.”

The rest of her tour featured a guest bathroom, an office for her homework, and a bedroom, complete with a walk-in closet and an attached bathroom.

Along the way, she showed more framed photos, from a car to a golf cart picture with President Donald Trump, her grandpa.

Elsewhere in the upload, Kai opened up about how “crazy” it is living alone — but confessed she has gone home to Jupiter, Florida, “a few times already.”

She explained, “I’ve got all my golf stuff and my trainers and coaches all in Jupiter, so if I have an off day, I try and go home. It’s been great.

“College life is fun,” she continued. “When you’re a student-athlete, that makes it hard. … I haven’t really explored Miami yet because I’m so busy with the golf, but I’m here for golf, I’m not here to party in Miami, so I guess that’s OK.”

Kai, who committed to the school in August 2024, added that she has been “going through it” health-wise.

“I ended up getting strep throat last week. On top of strep I had a sinus infection,” she said. “Last night I think I had food poisoning because I ate sushi and then I threw up. Not fun.”

In her last YouTube vlog, Kai documented her move to Miami — and broke down in tears over leaving her loved ones.

“I’m not a very emotional person at all, so this is like really weird for me to cry,” she said in the Aug. 29 video. “I think knowing that I have to say goodbye to everyone tonight really, really sucks.”
