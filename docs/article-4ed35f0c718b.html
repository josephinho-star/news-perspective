Investors are fleeing this data center stock. I see an opportunity

2026-11-24 19:15

I’m a momentum trader, and I’m always looking for stocks where the potential reward outweighs the risk. Today I’ve got my eye on Vertiv Holdings . I already own a position in Vertiv and I'm looking to buy more. The Ohio-based company provides essential equipment for data centers, including power management and cooling systems that keep servers running. Key points Strong third-quarter earnings and surging orders show demand is still high. Local restrictions could delay data center construction, but they haven't translated into canceled orders. Vertiv generates free cash flow without taking on the same financial risks as its customers. Vertiv shares plunged 25% in the first two trading days after the November midterms amid growing concerns that opposition to data center development could lead to construction restrictions. Shares have continued to slide and are now trading roughly 55% below their May high of $379.94. What's interesting is that while the political rhetoric is getting worse, Vertiv's underlying business is getting stronger. This creates the kind of asymmetric trade I'm looking for. Why I'm buying Business is booming despite the backlash In Vertiv's third-quarter results, revenue surged 61% from a year earlier to $4.31 billion, while adjusted earnings per share came in at $2.21, beating Wall Street's estimate of $1.82 by roughly 21%. Adjusted operating margin reached 25.2%, and organic orders more than doubled from a year earlier. Management also raised its full-year guidance. Those are impressive numbers for a company whose shares have been cut nearly in half. A delay doesn't mean a cancellation There is political opposition to data centers, but there's a key distinction between local opposition and a nationwide slowdown in data center construction. So far, restrictions have been limited to the local level, with bans in towns across six states. There are no statewide bans. I think states and communities that don't want data centers may ultimately reject them, while those that welcome the investment will attract more development. That could change where data centers get built, but it doesn't necessarily eliminate the demand for them. An overlooked advantage Vertiv sells the power and cooling infrastructure that those companies need. It doesn't carry the same construction-related debt burden as the customers financing these massive projects. The company generates positive free cash flow and receives advance payments from customers. That matters in an environment where investors are increasingly questioning the enormous amounts of money being spent on AI infrastructure. VRT YTD mountain Vertiv Holdings, YTD Management also raised its dividend by 60% on November 16, another sign of confidence in the company's cash generation. Why now? Wall Street's consensus price target for Vertiv is approximately $329, implying roughly 92% potential upside from current levels. Given the stock's recent volatility however, I would use stops to protect capital if shares continue to weaken. Bottom line The biggest risk is that the political backlash gains enough traction to produce statewide restrictions or more widespread delays in data center construction. Vertiv's stock is also trading well below its major moving averages, so the technical damage is real. But right now, the company's results aren't showing the deterioration that its stock price suggests. Steven Grasso is the CEO of Grasso Global Inc.
