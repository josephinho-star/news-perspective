Kevin Costner sparks romance rumors after grocery run with lifestyle coach

2026-09-20 18:00

Kevin Costner sparked romance rumors when he was spotted out shopping recently.

The 71-year-old "Yellowstone" star was photographed shopping at a local Albertsons in Santa Barbara, California, with a woman the Daily Mail identified as 40-year-old lifestyle coach Jennifer Milburn.

The pair were photographed smiling as they walked down the aisles of the grocery store and through the parking lot together, taking turns pushing the shopping cart around.

Costner kept it casual in a pair of white jeans and a white collared shirt, which he paired with flip-flops and black sunglasses.

Milburn opted for a pair of cutoff denim shorts and a long-sleeve white button-up shirt, which she paired with brown sandals and sunglasses. She wore her long blonde hair in a ponytail.

Fox News Digital has reached out to a representative for Costner for comment.

Before stepping out with Milburn, Costner sparked romance rumors with country singer Jewel in December 2023, when photos obtained by TMZ showed her speaking into a microphone and appearing to sit on Costner’s lap, with his arms around her waist.

The "Horizon: An American Saga" star cleared up the rumors during an appearance on "The Howard Stern Show" in June 2024, explaining the photos were taken on Richard Branson's private island, Necker Island, and that he didn't know she was going to be there until he got on the plane.

"No, Jewel and I are friends. We've never gone out ever. She's special, and I don't want, I don't want these rumors to ruin our friendship because that's what we have," he said. "She's special to me. She's beautiful enough to go out with."

Costner was married to Christine Baumgartner for 18 years before she filed for divorce in May 2023, citing the date of separation as April 11.

The former couple share three children together: Cayden, Hayes and Grace.

"It is with great sadness that circumstances beyond his control have transpired which have resulted in Mr. Costner having to participate in a dissolution of marriage action," Costner's rep said at the time.

"We ask that his, Christine’s and their children’s privacy be respected as they navigate this difficult time."

The handbag designer took Costner to court over child support and the validity of their prenuptial agreement. The former couple eventually agreed to joint legal and physical custody, according to documents obtained by Fox News Digital.
