MTR Corporation halts unauthorised remedial works at station after column concerns

2026-11-07 09:20

Rail giant says it has ordered contractor to submit a rectification plan after routine inspections last month uncovered incomplete works

Hong Kong’s rail giant, the MTR Corporation, has ordered a contractor to fix substandard columns erected on the platform of a station under construction after discovering unauthorised remedial works to fill gaps of up to 30cm to the ceiling.

The corporation said on Saturday that routine inspections last month uncovered the incomplete works and that it had immediately instructed the main contractor, Bouygues-Dragages Joint Venture, to suspend the remedial works.

“During routine inspections of incomplete works in October 2026, we found that remedial works involving the filling of patching materials had been carried out at the heads of columns at the platform level of the Tung Chung West station construction site without the MTR’s prior approval,” a spokesman said.
