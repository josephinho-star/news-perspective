Nordstrom Rack is having its own anniversary sale, with 100+ underrated deals

2026-09-15 17:42

It’s Nordstrom’s birthday, but we’re the ones getting the gifts.

While the Nordstrom Anniversary Sale is done and dusted, Nordstrom Rack’s now getting in on the once-a-year fun. The more accessibly priced retailer just launched 125 Deals, a bargain blowout in celebration of its parent company’s 125th anniversary.

With savings of up to 65% off on brands like Rag & Bone, Adidas, Madewell and more, the under-the-radar sale isn’t to be missed. Below, we combed through all the deals to pick our top 10 favorites, including several from brands we often spot on celebrities.

Scoring 62% off Rag & Bone is practically unheard of — but not at Nordstrom Rack. Everyone from Martha Stewart to Bethenny Frankel wears the brand, and these deeply discounted jeans will sell fast.

This suede Madewell bag is the epitome of autumn chic — but it’ll carry you (and your daily essentials) well into winter, too.

Everyone from Harry Styles to Gigi Hadid has stepped out in Adidas Gazelles over the years, so you’ll be in stylish company with this on-sale pair.

Kris Jenner and Kathy Hilton have both raved about this mascara; the latter even called it “better than any eyelash extension.”

Gorgeous earrings for under $20? Tell me about it, stud.

This limited-edition lip combo works out to less than $10 a product. It comes with MAC’s iconic Velvet Teddy shade, plus a matching liner and shimmery gloss to top it all off.

These swipe-and-go sticks deliver a smoky or shimmery eyeshadow look in seconds — and they last for up to 24 hours of wear.

Vince’s knits are a Page Six team favorite, and it’s rare to find styles for prices like these.

No one makes denim quite like Levi’s. The heritage brand is deeply discounted at Nordstrom Rack right now, with style standouts including this versatile layer.

From Kendall Jenner to Alix Earle, Frame has plenty of famous fans. This ’70s-inspired flare is on trend for fall — and marked down to under $100.
