Kristin Juszczyk debuts bold upside-down skirt for 49ers vs Dolphins game

2026-09-20 20:47

The San Francisco 49ers being back on the football field means more fascinating outfits from 49ers fullback Kyle Juszczyk’s wife, Kristin.

Kristin Juszczyk has become one of the NFL’s most prominent WAGs because of her unique game day outfits, which often includes her transforming traditional sports gear into high-fashion, custom streetwear.

These DIY outfits became the inspiration for Juszczyk’s Off Season clothing brand, which the NFL has granted an apparel license for.

Juszczyk didn’t disappoint with her outfit when the 49ers face the Miami Dolphins on Sunday.

She posted an Instagram video of her cutting up two 49ers shirts and turning them into an upside-down 49ers skirt that featured oversized team graphics.

She finished the look with a red tie-front crop top, red heels, and a black bag.

She captioned the post, “It’s an upside down skirt kinda day! Let’s go Niners! Feels so good to be back at it!!”

The outfit already received Kyle Juszczyk’s stamp of approval, as he commented on his wife’s post and wrote, “The scissors are back!!”

San Francisco is expected to dominate the Dolphins on Sunday, which would give them a 2-0 start to the regular season after beating the Los Angeles Rams in Australia last week.

Regardless of the 49ers’ record, fans can expect Kristin Juszczyk to keep bringing the heat with her outfits each weekend.
