Chris Hemsworth’s multi-million dollar beach retreat targeted by burglars who stole three cars

2026-11-12 21:24

Chris Hemsworth’s multi-million-dollar property in Australia’s Broken Head was reportedly the target of burglars who stole three vehicles — but the cars have since been located.

According to TMZ, authorities were called to the beach property around 7:30 Wednesday morning for reports of auto theft.

Authorities in New South Wales were reportedly told that three cars had been stolen after four unidentified thieves had entered the property earlier that day.

7 News reported that burglars made off with a Ram, a Raptor ute, and a BYD in the alleged heist — and that the “Thor” actor’s wife, Elsa Pataky, had woken up after hearing dogs barking around 2:00 a.m.

After ignoring the noise, the outlet claimed, Pataky saw the four men wearing balaclavas — with two of them holding knives — via their CCTV cameras the next morning.

The couple notified authorities.

“Officers attended and were told four unknown people had entered the property and stole three vehicles earlier that day,” cops told the outlet, noting that “no injuries were reported.”

According to TMZ, however, two of the vehicles have since been recovered at Coraki and Tabulum, with a third being found on Thursday in Lennox Heads.

Police sources said the vehicles are currently undergoing forensic examination after having been seized, the outlet reported, and the investigation is ongoing.

Local authorities did not immediately respond to Page Six’s request for comment on Thursday. A rep for Hemsworth also did not immediately respond to our request for comment.

Hemsworth’s expansive luxury estate, which is located near Byron Bay in the actor’s native Australia, was reportedly purchased by the longtime couple in 2014 for $7 million.

In 2021 comments to the Daily Mail, a local luxury builder called the mansion “ginormous” and noted that it has a four-car garage.

The outlet reported the home’s estimated worth to be $65 million at the time.

Hemsworth, 43, and Pataky, 50, married in 2010 and have since welcomed three children — twin sons Sasha and Tristan Hemsworth, 12, and daughter India Rose Hemsworth, 14.
