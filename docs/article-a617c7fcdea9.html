Trump will have historic meeting with Xi, Putin in China to talk nuclear arms control

2026-11-13 21:06

WASHINGTON — President Trump will hold a trilateral summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin next Thursday on the sidelines of the annual APEC conference in Shenzhen, The Post has learned — with the discussion expected to focus largely on developing an ambitious nuclear arms control pact.

A White House official told The Post that discussions in the Chinese boomtown near Hong Kong also could touch on the war in Iran and North Korea.

Trump hasn’t met with Putin in person since a summit last August in Alaska focused on his efforts to broker an end to the war in Ukraine, which is another potential topic of conversation. He hosted Xi at the White House in September.

US special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner have met with Putin repeatedly this year attempting to make progress toward ending Putin’s nearly five-year conflict with Kyiv — with their most recent Kremlin sit-down occurring Thursday.

Trump already all-but confirmed plans for the summit among the three nations that control most of the world’s nuclear weapons.

“One of the meetings, I think, will be with President Putin and President Xi, and that’ll be interesting,” Trump told reporters on Sunday. “We’ll talk a lot of things, including denuclearization, but we’ll be talking a lot of things. We’re trying to end the war in Iran, and we’re doing well.”
