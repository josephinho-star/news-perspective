Adam22 and Lena the Plug reconcile after hoax divorce filing

2026-10-26 01:59

Lena the Plug and Adam22’s ongoing divorce “hoax” has apparently officially ended for good — for the time being, at least.

The couple appear to be solidly back together, based on a reconciliation post shared on Instagram Saturday. The pair, who were apparently at rapper Drake’s 40th birthday party that night, showed off party outfits as they cuddled for the camera. Lena wore a plunging-neckline yellow satin dress, while Adam kept it more casual in a gray turtleneck and dark jacket plus a pair of sunglasses.

“Really gave this man a second chance. Pls don’t mess it up 💋 Happy 10 years @adam22 ❤️,” Lena captioned the reel.

The duo’s bizarre back-and-forth relationship first made headlines in June when Lena (born born Lena Nersesian) claimed that a mystery man filed paperwork in her name to try to end her marriage to Adam, alleging financial and physical abuse.

According to the June 1 petition previously obtained by Page Six, a person going by Lena’s name filed for divorce from Adam — born Adam Grandmaison — and requested primary legal and physical custody of their daughter.

The individual also listed the pair’s date of separation as April 15 and disclosed their alleged assets, including $1.15 million they hold in real estate.

In July, Adam was officially served divorce papers at his and Lena’s home in Studio City, California, reportedly by the person impersonating Lena.

Lena told Page Six at the time that the divorce petition was not her doing but rather “the work of the crazed fan.”

However, the following month, Lena filed to dismiss the dissolution of marriage in Los Angeles Superior Court, Page Six exclusively confirmed. She notably requested to dismiss the case without prejudice, giving her the opportunity to refile in the future.

The drama continued in September. “I would like to formally announce that my wife and I are officially separated,” Adam said on an episode of his “No Jumper” podcast that month, explaining that the couple “have ran into some issues” and decided to put their “relationship on ice” for a period of time.

Adam, who married Lena, 35, in May 2023 after they began dating in 2016, explained that their relationship changed after they welcomed daughter Parker, now 5, in 2020.

“Will we end up back together? It’s certainly a possibility,” the 42-year-old YouTuber added, at the time.
