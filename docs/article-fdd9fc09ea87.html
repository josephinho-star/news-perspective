Live updates: America heads to the polls as Trump fights to protect his agenda

2026-11-03 20:40

Election Day is here, with President Trump’s political capital on the line as Democrats eye a potential blue wave and Republicans scramble to defend their congressional majorities.

Trump has campaigned across the country in recent months, holding 28 in-person rallies in addition to a series of telerallies. He’s made stops in states including Ohio, Texas, Iowa, Kansas and Alaska, as well as deep-red states like Mississippi and South Carolina, where polls have shown unexpectedly competitive races.

The Cook Political Report on Monday shifted its rating of the Michigan Senate race from a toss-up to lean Democratic, while Decision Desk HQ is forecasting Democratic victories in Texas, Alaska and Maine that could put the party in a strong position to reclaim the Senate. Political analyst Nate Silver also predicted a “blue tsunami” is on the horizon. Here is your election night cheat sheet.

In the Middle East, the U.S. military targeted radar detectors and missile launchers on Qeshm Island as the Trump administration weighs renewing strikes on Iran after the midterms.

Trump is set to participate in an election night viewing reception as voters head to the polls to decide the balance of power in Congress next year. Earlier Tuesday, he criticized a Democratic lawmaker and news outlet over their response to his final pre-midterms speech a day earlier, when he joked that his administration considered renaming the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool after him.
