'Danger seems greater all the time': Ukrainians search for missing after Russian strikes

2026-11-22 17:57

At least three bodies have been recovered the site of a residential building that was hit by a Russian strike in the Kyiv region, Ukraine's State Emergency Services said.

A woman in her forties and a teenage boy have so far been identified as rescue workers continue to search the rubble for those who are still trapped.

Continued warnings of further drone threats near the site are hampering efforts, the service added, but some people were successfully evacuated earlier in the day.

"We are living a nightmare... only God knows when it will end," one woman told the BBC's Sarah Rainsford, who's reporting from Ukraine's Kyiv region.
