Tom Brady pays touching tribute to children with personalized gold necklace

2026-10-10 20:14

Tom Brady has forged a way to keep his three children close to him, even when they’re not nearby.

The legendary quarterback and seven-time Super Bowl champion took to Instagram to show off a personalized gold necklace on Saturday.

He is folding a gold necklace with a flat, multi-sided pendant hanging from it. On one side, the necklace features the names of his three children: Jack, Benny and Vivi.

“Always and forever ❤️❤️❤️,” the post was captioned.

Jack is Tom’s oldest child. He turned 19 in August, which Brady celebrated by posting a photo of Jack dunking with the caption, “Don’t think I could do that at 19….” Jack is the son of Bridget Moynahan, whom Brady dated from 2004 to 2006.

Benny (Benjamin) is Brady’s 16-year-old middle child and the first one he had with his ex-wife, Gisele Bündchen.

Vivian (Vivi) is 13 years old and Brady’s youngest child (also his only daughter). She seems to be an elite athlete as well, as Brady has praised her volleyball abilities in several social media posts.

All three of these children are busy with their own lives, but Tom spends as much time with them as possible.

And now he has a way to keep them close to his heart, even when calling NFL games on the road.
