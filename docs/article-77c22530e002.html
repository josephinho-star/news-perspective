Melania Trump’s sleek and stunning Xi state dinner outfit is stylish tribute to Chinese culture

2026-09-25 02:20

The stylish peony-motif brocade Dolce & Gabbana pants, bow-adorned Saint Laurent cotton poplin blouse, black silk Dolce & Gabbana cummerbund and sky-high black Christian Louboutin heels she wore to welcome Xi and his wife, Peng Liyuan — took the internet by storm.

The first lady’s simple outfit had a more complex message — and it wasn’t that she’s the one who wears pants in the White House.

In choosing pants with a subtle peony pattern, Melania Trump was paying tribute to the “King of Flowers,” as the peony is known in Chinese culture. It symbolizes wealth, nobility, prosperity and romantic beauty.

The Chinese consider the peony the embodiment of feminine charm and perfection.

The first lady has been on a menswear-inspired kick as of late. She wore a black Alexander McQueen military-style suit to the official welcoming ceremony for the Chinese first couple Thursday morning.

She also donned a Dolce & Gabbana tuxedo suit for her official White House portrait last year.

For her part, Madame Peng Liyuan wore a deep red gown with a black clutch with a gold clasp for the state dinner.

The two women appeared to get on well when they visited the Smithsonian National Museum of Asian Art.

They even shared a whisper and a smile as they listened to a group of students from the Hope Chinese School — dressed in white with sparkling blue vests — serenade them with English and Chinese songs.

Peng, who was a famous singer in China before she married Xi, bopped her head and sang along. The two women also saw the famous Peacock Room in the museum, created by James McNeill Whistler.

At the state dinner, the two first couples were joined by Apple’s Tim Cook, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and his wife Lori, and Advanced Micro Devices CEO Dr. Lisa Su and her husband Daniel Lin at the head table.
