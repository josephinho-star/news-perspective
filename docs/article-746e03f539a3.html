Free agents: Replacing Drake Maye? Trust in Jordyn Tyson?

2026-11-16 20:44

The New England Patriots are one of six teams that will not be playing in Week 11, as bye weeks continue. However, even when the defending AFC champions resume playing in Week 12 versus the Los Angeles Chargers, QB Drake Maye (ankle) will not be on the field. Maye was injured in Sunday's loss to the Detroit Lions. So, while fantasy managers may have already had a plan to cover for Maye in Week 11, they are now likely to be scrambling for aid beyond that point.

Las Vegas Raiders rookie Fernando Mendoza (19.5% rostered) was under constant pressure in his first NFL start on Sunday, a loss to the excellent Seattle Seahawks defense. He scored a rushing touchdown and finished with 13.92 points. Mendoza was sacked six times and did not have the services of star TE Brock Bowers for most of the contest.

Still, it sure looks like Mendoza (the first overall NFL draft pick) may be special, even this season. The Raiders face a Denver Broncos defense in Week 11 that has not been as awesome as expected, permitting 16.9 QB fantasy points per game (19th in NFL). Prepare to spend at least 3% of your FAB to acquire Mendoza.

Other quarterbacks to consider

Aaron Rodgers, Steelers (37.2%, 2% FAB): Rodgers salvaged his Week 10 outing on Sunday night against the Cincinnati Bengals with a pair of fourth-quarter TD passes, ending up with 15.62 fantasy points. More of those points should be coming when the Steelers face their intrastate rivals in Week 11, with the sputtering Philadelphia Eagles riding a seven-game losing streak.

Cam Ward, Titans (4.5%, 1% FAB): Ward entered this week outside the top 20 fantasy quarterbacks for 2026 scoring, but then he put up 19.14 points in Sunday's loss to the Jacksonville Jaguars, completing a season-best 71.8% of his passes. Ward boasts four TD passes over his past two games, and he will face the terrible -- and it really is awful -- Dallas Cowboys defense in Week 11.

J.J. McCarthy, Giants (27.6%, 1% FAB): McCarthy faces a tough Week 11 home matchup with the Jaguars, a defense that permits only 12.5 fantasy points against them per game. McCarthy is coming off a solid outing with 17.12 points in Week 10 -- and remember, six starting QBs will be on a bye.

Deep-league options/streamers

Baker Mayfield, Buccaneers (19.6%, 1% FAB): Mayfield (foot/ankle) is preliminarily listed as questionable for Sunday's road outing against the Detroit Lions, but if he is able to start, he will face a defense that permits the most QB fantasy points and a unit that will be missing two-time Pro Bowl DE Aidan Hutchinson (knee). If Mayfield cannot play, fantasy managers may wish to consider rookie backup Jalon Daniels, a runner who has made three starts, reaching double-digit fantasy points each time.

Several top wide receivers will not play in Week 11, including Seattle's Jaxon Smith-Njigba (fantasy's leading scorer), Green Bay's Christian Watson and Matthew Golden, and Atlanta's Drake London. For those needing some assistance at that position, may we offer up a rookie for your consideration?

The New Orleans Saints expected WR Jordyn Tyson to be one of the top rookie performers this season when they used the eighth overall pick on the Arizona State product. A preseason hamstring injury kept Tyson sidelined for the first half of the regular season, but Tyson debuted in Week 9 with four receptions for 30 yards. It was clear that better statistical days were pending -- if Tyson could remain healthy. That has been a problem for him.

Well, a healthy Tyson caught 11 passes for 80 yards in Sunday's last-second loss to the Carolina Panthers, earning 13 of Tyler Shough's 35 targets. Shough is among the league leaders in passing attempts, completions and passing yards, in part because the team's running game is poor. Tyson was the team's only wide receiver to earn more than four targets. Chris Olave investors should not panic, but Tyson (rostered in only 42.2% of ESPN standard leagues) needs to be far more popular for Week 11 against the Chicago Bears and beyond. This may be your last chance to act.

Ryan Flournoy, WR, Cowboys (26.0%, 2% FAB): Flournoy continues to thrive for a team that scores myriad points and permits a whole lot more than that. He scored a season-best 19.1 points in Sunday's 46-40 loss to the San Francisco 49ers. That makes five double-digit scoring outings in Flournoy's past six games, even with Dallas featuring WRs CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens. The Cowboys face the struggling Tennessee Titans and Eagles during the next two weeks.

Makai Lemon, Eagles (49.7%, 3% FAB): Lemon, the No. 20 pick in the most recent draft, has stepped up with teammates DeVonta Smith (hamstring) and Dontayvion Wicks (ankle) both sidelined, reaching double-digit fantasy points in three consecutive games. The Eagles come out of their bye week to face the Pittsburgh Steelers. Even if Smith and/or Wicks play, we can assume Lemon, with 19 receptions in three games, figures to remain heavily involved in this struggling offense.

Malik Washington, Dolphins (33.5%, 2% FAB): Washington scored 13.3 points in Sunday's loss to the Indianapolis Colts, but he reached double-digit scoring for the second time in three weeks, doing so with second-year QB Kyle McCord starting. Washington is averaging just over 10.0 fantasy points per game this season and with myriad receivers on a bye this week, that may be enough to consider him for active lineups.

Xavier Hutchinson, Texans (8.7%, 1% FAB): Hutchinson scored a season-best 15.8 points in Sunday's loss to the Cleveland Browns, notching a TD reception for the third consecutive game. The nine targets were a great sign, too. Hutchinson isn't Houston's top wide receiver option, but QB C.J. Stroud throws enough to make others a reasonable flex option -- especially this week versus the Colts.

Deep-league options/streamers

Antonio Williams, Commanders (17.7%, 1% FAB): Williams scored 12.2 points in Week 10, the second time in three weeks he has reached double digits, although he got there only because of a TD reception. Williams saw just two targets. He should see more in Week 11 against the Bengals.

Laquon Treadwell, Colts (1.2%, 1% FAB): Treadwell, 31, is a journeyman catching passes for his sixth NFL team. However, with the Colts trading away both Keenan Allen and Josh Downs last week, he led the team's receivers with 11.4 points in Sunday's win. Treadwell caught only three passes. Still, that matched what Ashton Dulin and Anthony Gould achieved. This week, the Colts face a Houston Texans defense that permits the second-most WR fantasy points.

Seth McGowan, RB, Colts (10.9%, 3% FAB): McGowan is no longer just a curiosity. He outscored star Jonathan Taylor for the second consecutive game, turning 12 rushing attempts into 77 yards, a goal-line touchdown and 13.7 points. Taylor, notably not traded at the league deadline, earned 18 touches and tallied 103 yards, but he did not find the end zone. Even on an awful Colts team with a top-10 RB (though Taylor is fading), McGowan is becoming a reasonable flex option.

Kenny Gainwell, Buccaneers (49.4%, 2% FAB): Gainwell has been notably disappointing so far, but he scored double-digit fantasy points in his past two games entering the team's Week 10 bye week, where he slipped under our 50% roster threshold. It's not too late for Gainwell to help, especially after Buccaneers coach Todd Bowles told reporters the team plans to lessen volume for star RB Bucky Irving, on pace for more than 300 touches. Gainwell totaled only 122 scrimmage yards over the first six games, but he has a solid 304 yards during the past three contests.

Kaelon Black, 49ers (35.1%, 2% FAB): Black, the backup to star Christian McCaffrey, made the highlight shows with his bruising 25-yard TD run in Sunday's win over the Cowboys, finishing with 14.6 points. McCaffrey continues to thrive with modest volume -- he scored a ridiculous 46.3 points on just 17 Week 10 touches -- yet Black is averaging double-digit fantasy points during the past three games. He is more than insurance for another McCaffrey injury, and perhaps your best option for flex.

Deep-league options/streamers

Austin Ekeler, Commanders (7.1%, 1% FAB): Ekeler led the team's running backs with 9.2 points in a Week 10 win over the New York Giants, mainly due to his season-high four receptions. Rachaad White earned more rushing attempts, but he caught only one pass. Neither Commander comes truly recommended for Week 11 versus the Bengals (nor beyond), but this is not a great week for those needing to add a running back.

Emari Demercado, Texans (0.4%): Demercado, claimed off waivers by the Texans just a few weeks ago, scored only 2.5 points Sunday. Even so, he may be the team's starter for this Thursday against the Colts with David Montgomery (groin) and Woody Marks (concussion) both questionable. Demercado, 27, has averaged 6.2 yards per rush in his career as (mostly) a backup.

Chig Okonkwo, Commanders (16.1%, 1% FAB): Okonkwo scored only 6.4 points in the Week 10 win over the Giants, but he finished second to WR Stefon Diggs with eight targets, which is a good sign. Okonkwo has yet to score a touchdown this season, but perhaps he finds the end zone this week versus the Bengals. Though they have improved in terms of being stingy with TE fantasy points, they're still among the 10 friendliest for the position.

Michael Mayer, Raiders (18.5%, 1% FAB): Mayer scored only 3.8 points on Sunday against the Seahawks in Mendoza's first start, but he figures to see more attention moving forward if star TE Brock Bowers (ankle) misses time. Mayer boasts four double-digit fantasy outings this season.

Lions D/ST (32%): This unit has scored double-digit fantasy points in consecutive outings and at least 6.0 points in five straight games. Things will be more difficult sans star DE Aidan Hutchinson (knee), but the Lions have a Week 11 home game against a Buccaneers offense that is among the bottom 10 NFL teams in total yards and is unsure of who will start at quarterback.

Bears D/ST (40.2%): The Bears come out of their bye week for a home game against a Saints team that has scored more than 24 points in just one of their past six games. This unit has scored the fifth-most fantasy points, including double digits in three of the past four games.
