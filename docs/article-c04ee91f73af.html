What Time Does ‘The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power’ Season 3 Come Out?

2026-11-11 04:00

Two years after it last aired, The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power is back on Prime Video for Season 3 in just a few hours!

Set five years after Season 2, The Rings of Power picks up at the height of a war between the elves and the Dark Lord Sauron.

Per Amazon’s official synopsis of the new season, “deep in the land of Mordor, in his newly completed tower of Barad-dûr, the Dark Lord toils day and night, obsessed with harnessing a power that will bring the last of his enemies to their knees: One Ring to rule them all… Now, all the free peoples of Middle-earth – Dwarves, Elves, Men, and Wizards alike – must find a way to come together, in a race against time to prevent Sauron from achieving his goal of utter domination of all life.”

Watch 'The Rings of Power' for Free

- When: Nov. 11, 3 a.m. ET (new episodes Wednesdays at 3 a.m. ET)

- How to watch for free: Prime Video (30-day free trial)

- TV Channel: Streaming exclusive

DECIDER’S Meghan O’Keefe declared that the new installment is “finally truly epic” after two “directionless” seasons.

“The Rings of Power is proudly an idiosyncratic, raucous, nerdy, and extremely metal show,” O’Keefe wrote. “By fully committing to its creative swings, it finally cuts through the cynicism that persnickety Tolkien fans (like yours truly) had watching seasons past.”

If you want to tune in to the “pure fantasy magic” of The Rings of Power Season 3, here’s what you need to know.

When Does Season 3 of The Rings of Power Come Out?

Season 3 of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power comes out on Nov. 11.

What Time Does Rings of Power Air?

New episodes of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power drop on Prime Video at 3 a.m. ET / 12 a.m. PT.

Where to Watch The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power for Free

As an Amazon Original, you’ll need a Prime Video account to watch The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power.
