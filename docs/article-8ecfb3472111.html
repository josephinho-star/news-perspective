New footage shows missing DoorDash driver’s last pickup before phone lost connection as heartbreaking final text revealed

2026-11-12 21:07

The last pickup a DoorDash driver made before he vanished in Pennsylvania has been caught on camera — as the devastating final text he sent his wife was revealed.

Newly released footage shows 32-year-old Zachariah Schuck picking up the food delivery from the New Sheffield Café on Brodhead Road in Center Township.

In the clip Schuck, wearing a red and dark blue plaid shirt, picks up two bags of food and carries them to his car.

North Sewickley Township investigators said his phone lost connection around 8 p.m. later that evening.

In a statement provided to The US Sun, the family of Schuck’s wife, Jenn, shared details about the DoorDash driver’s private life, including the chilling text message she received from her husband before he disappeared.

Schuck texted his wife, “Everything will be okay,” after calling to tell her he was heading home after his final delivery.

Jenn’s family also claimed her husband had hidden financial woes from his wife for months before he vanished – including the loss of his job and the family’s health insurance coverage.

“Zach’s employment ended in April 2026, a fact he concealed from his wife and in-laws,” the family said in the statement.

“It was not until the morning following his disappearance, when his wife contacted his employer directly, that she learned he had not been employed with the company since April 2026.”

On Monday, Schuck’s father told The Post that his son had welcomed a new baby at the same time he lost his job as a manager for Penske truck leasing branches in Pennsylvania and Ohio.

Schuck’s last-known location was in Beaver County, Pennsylvania, when he was dropping off a delivery.

His car was seen in a neighborhood around 90 minutes later, with black-and-white doorbell footage obtained by WPXI showing someone parking the vehicle. The person in the video then got out of the vehicle and walked down the road — however, it’s unclear if this was him.

Investigators received information about the car on Oct. 24 — two days after Schuck’s last sighting.

Police said the keys were left in the ignition and there was no gas in the tank.
