NFL Week 11 early predictions, odds, picks: Titans vs. Cowboys, 49ers vs. Vikings

2026-11-16 20:55

Favorites are running the world in the NFL right now.

Underdogs have been downright embarrassing in Weeks 9 and 10, as the league really looks more lopsided than ever.

Favorites are 24-4 straight up since Week 9 and 21-7 against the spread.

This is something we have to follow closely.

Is the league separated by too much at this point in the season between the teams that are trying to win games?

We have five spreads in double figures in Week 11, insane stuff as books look to adjust to the changing landscape in the NFL.

We dive into that ahead of Week 11 of the NFL season, where we look to beat the line and perhaps snag some winning bets.

NFL Week 11 early predictions

Very quietly, the Tennessee Titans are playing better football. The group led by the bizarre combination of Robert Saleh and Brian Daboll has the sixth-best yards-per-play average in the NFL over the last three weeks (5.8 YPP).

They have scored at least 23 points in four straight games and even went blow for blow with the Cincinnati Bengals at one point.

The Cowboys are a disaster in every sense of the word, ranking No. 31 in DVOA, behind only the Tennessee Titans.

This is a spot where I think the Titans could win outright, and the over of 53.5 points is another good look for this week.

49ers -2.5 (-125, Polymarket) vs. Vikings

Kirk Cousins really raises the floor for Minnesota, but let’s not get crazy here.

Most of the betting market outside of Polymarket has moved to three points for this spread and some are even at 3.5, so this is an obvious middle opportunity to win both ways if the 49ers take the win by a field goal.
