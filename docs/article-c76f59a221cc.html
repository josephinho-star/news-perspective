‘Little House on the Prairie’ star felt ‘an inch tall’ delivering food in the ’90s despite childhood fame on iconic show

2026-11-25 02:25

“Little House on the Prairie” star Patrick Labyorteaux recalled feeling humiliated when he once delivered food to musician Joe Walsh.

The former child star, 61, shared the story in an Instagram video, in which he recounted that he had a few “lean years” in the ’90s between his gig on NBC’s “Little House on the Prairie” and getting a role on CBS’ “JAG.”

Labyorteaux said he worked for a company that delivered food from different restaurants, before the more modern food apps like Grubhub or DoorDash existed.

He recounted that during one delivery, a woman who “had the biggest head of sex hair” opened the door, wearing a big robe. As he entered the home to set up the food, she recognized him from “Little House on the Prairie.”

Labyorteaux noted that getting recognized while delivering food was his biggest fear at the time, and it didn’t help when the woman asked him, “What happened?”

“Well now, I’m about 3 inches tall. I don’t have any witty comeback,” he said. “I just kind of stare at her and go, ‘I just needed a job.’”

He shared that a man then came down the stairs, wearing a tiny robe that barely covered him. He immediately recognized him as Walsh.

“So the girl points to me and then turns to Joe Walsh and says, ‘Oh my god Joe! The guy delivering our food? He used to be on ‘Little House the Prairie!'” he recalled.

Labyorteax admitted, “Now, I’m like an inch tall.”

But it ended up being a positive interaction thanks to Walsh’s matter-of fact reaction. Labyorteaux said Walsh bluntly responded, “I used to be in the Eagles.”

Labyorteaux laughed and cracked, “God bless you, Joe Walsh. I didn’t feel that bad about myself. I was able to leave there with my head held high and a pretty nice tip.”

The actor got his big break playing Andrew “Andy” Garvey on “Little House on the Prairie” from 1977 to 1981.

He later had a long-running role on “JAG” as Lt. Cmdr. Bud Roberts from 1995 to 2005.

He also appeared in popular television series like “21 Jump Street,” “Living Single,” “Lois & Clark: The New Adventures of Superman,” “The Love Boat,” and “iCarly.”

His film roles include 1987’s “Summer School” and 1988’s “Heathers.”

Labyorteax’s latest television role was a 2022 appearance on “NCIS,” which is a spinoff of “JAG.”
