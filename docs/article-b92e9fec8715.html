When is the 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade?

2026-11-26 11:00

There’s never a dull moment in New York City. For the first time ever, Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade has been postponed due to extreme weather conditions.

Thanks a lot, AL ROKER.

Heavy rain and strong winds are expected on Thanksgiving Day as a major nor’easter is set to wreak havoc on the East Coast. It’s important to note that the parade is postponed, not canceled.

“Due to the extreme weather conditions, the city of New York, Macy’s and NBC have decided, in the interest of safety for both the participants and spectators, to move the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday, Nov. 28,” an NBC spokesperson said in a statement.

The annual event has never been canceled due to weather, per the NY Post, but it was “squashed for three consecutive years during World War II.” Today’s telecast will feature a replay of the 2025 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade.

It’s a tough day to be a giant Snoopy balloon, but we persist. When will the 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade air on NBC? Here’s everything you need to know.

What time does the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade start today (November 26)?

The official telecast airs from 8:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. in all time zones on NBC and Peacock. But, as we mentioned above, due to “extreme weather conditions,” NBC has postponed the parade and will instead be reairing the 2025 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade.

The parade will be followed by the 2026 National Dog Show at 12:00 p.m. ET.

Did Macy’s cancel the Thanksgiving Day Parade?

Nope. It’s just postponed. The 2026 edition will air this weekend.

When does the 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade air on NBC?

A Saturday Thanksgiving parade? Why not!? This year’s Macy’s parade will now air Saturday, November 28 at 8:30 a.m. on NBC and Peacock.

Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade 2026 live stream info:

You can watch the 2026 Thanksgiving Day Parade live on NBC, NBC.com (with a cable login), or the NBC app.

How to watch the 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade live without cable:

The only way to watch shows airing live without cable is by using a live TV streaming service. Our favorite is DIRECTV, which comes with a five-day free trial before payment begins.

DIRECTV offers a number of flexible subscription options, including Genre Packs, so you can best choose which streaming plans fit your needs and budget. Packs start at $19.99/month.

You can also stream this joyous holiday event with an active subscription to fuboTV, Hulu + Live TV, YouTube TV, Peacock Premium, or Sling TV. FuboTV, YouTube TV, and Hulu + Live TV offer free trials for new subscribers.

How to watch The 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade live on Peacock:

Peacock currently offers two subscription types: Premium with ads and Premium Plus ad-free. Peacock Premium costs $12.99/month, while Premium Plus costs $19.99/month.

You can save a bit by subscribing to one of Peacock’s annual plans, which give you 12 months for the price of 10. These cost either $129.99 with ads or $199.99 without ads.

Peacock Premium Plus is also available to subscribe to via Prime Video with a seven-day free trial that you can’t get by subscribing directly on Peacock.

You can also bundle Peacock and Apple TV for $17.99/month or $22.99/month (ad-free on Peacock).

Does Peacock offer a free trial?

Peacock doesn’t directly offer a free trial, but thankfully, you can sample the streamer through Prime Video. Amazon offers a seven-day free trial of Peacock Premium Plus, which renews at $199.99/year until canceled.

You can also score a free subscription to Peacock through Instacart+, which offers a free trial. Finally, if you’re a DIRECTV subscriber, you can also get two months of Peacock Premium by adding the Movies Extra Pack.
