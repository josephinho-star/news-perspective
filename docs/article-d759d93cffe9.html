Anthony Richardson gets final Colts’ chance after ominous NFL evaluation

2026-11-20 19:21

This may be Anthony Richardson’s final chance to impress both the Colts and NFL.

Following Indianapolis’ stunning decision to release Daniel Jones on Friday, Richardson has been named the starter beginning in Week 12 against the Giants.

This six-game audition is notable since longtime Colts reporter Bob Kravitz reported Thursday night that the Colts have tried to trade the former first-round pick, but “nobody was interested.”

Richardson now has up to five games — vs. Giants, at Eagles, at Titans, vs. Bengals, at Browns and vs. Jaguars — to prove his worth before he becomes a free agent at season’s end.

A good showing could, in theory, help him land a contract, perhaps with the Colts or a quarterback-needy team instead of being viewed as a potential practice-squad option.

Richardson will at least have the chance to change his team’s opinion after it seemed he couldn’t get off the bench no matter how bad Jones played.

Plenty wondered why the Colts had not benched Jones in a lost 3-8 season and the their resistance did not speak well on the former Florida star.

Once viewed as the franchise cornerstone after being selected No. 4 overall in 2023, Richardson experienced highs and lows while making 15 starts over the 2023 and 2024 seasons.

His career seemingly nosedived after he asked out of a game for a play in the 2024 season since he felt tired and he later would be benched for Joe Flacco.

The Colts then signed Jones to be their starter and Richardson appeared in just four games over the last two years with no starts.

He appeared in mop-up duty during Thursday’s 26-3 road loss to the Texans.

There had been some speculation as to why Richardson didn’t replace Jones since the team hesitated to say on the record.

One idea had been that going to Richardson is a move that ends Jones’ tenure, and perhaps that was a top consideration since the team did release Jones while clearing the path for Richardson.

The Colts obviously need a long-term quarterback and perhaps he can win them over, although he’ll reportedly be looking to change the perception of the 31 other teams, as well.

It’s certainly notable that Richardson reportedly didn’t draw trade interest since there are many instances of teams taking fliers on early-round flameouts.

The Cowboys traded a fourth-round pick for Trey Lance. The Giants sent the Vikings a fifth-round for J.J. McCarthy. The Jaguars swapped a sixth-rounder for Mac Jones.

Richardson certainly has similar pedigrees to those other quarterbacks, yet they have received second chances and he’s only now getting off the bench after two years.

With a strong showing in these last six games, he may work his way into teams’ plans.
