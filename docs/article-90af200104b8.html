Search underway for suspected gunman outside Notre Dame football game vs Michigan State

2026-09-20 01:12

U.S. Marshals announce the arrest of one 16-year-old suspect in Colorado, connected to the murder of Penn State student Billy Schmidt. Philadelphia police confirm both 16-year-olds will be charged as adults for the June 6 robbery and shooting. Kaiseem Smith, the second suspect, remains a fugitive as authorities issue a wanted poster.

A search is underway Saturday night for a suspected gunman near the University of Notre Dame campus after gunfire was reported in the nearby Eddy Street Commons area. The incident occurred while Notre Dame's football team took on Michigan State.

The university’s emergency alert system said the suspect was last seen along the southern edge of campus as police searched the area.

People inside Notre Dame Stadium were instructed to remain there, while others on campus were told to shelter in place.
