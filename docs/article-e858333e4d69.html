When Does ‘Mayor of Kingstown’ Season 5 Come Out? ‘Mayor of Kingstown’ Netflix Release Date Info

2026-10-04 16:00

We have some exciting news for Mayor of Kingstown fans: Multiple seasons of the series are about to drop on Netflix! But what’s the latest on the fifth, and sadly final, series of the Jeremy Renner-led fan favorite? Season 4 spoilers ahead.

The final installment follows FBI agent Russell Hardy (David Morse), who arrives in Kingstown following the murder of Mike’s (Renner) sister-in-law. Per Paramount, Agent Hardy “threatens to disrupt the tenuous balance of power in Kingstown.”

Nestor Carbonell has also joined the cast for Season 5, with Edie Falco, Laura Benanti, Hugh Dillon, Taylor Handley, Tobi Bamtefa, Derek Webster, David Morse, Necar Zadegan, Nichole Galicia, and Lennie James all returning.

When does Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 premiere on Paramount+? When will Mayor of Kingstown be on Netflix? Here’s everything you need to know.

Will There Be a Season 5 of Mayor of Kingstown?

Yes! Earlier this year, it was announced that Mayor of Kingstown was renewed for its fifth and final season.

How Many Episodes Are There of Mayor of Kingstown Season 5?

The final season will consist of eight episodes.

When Does Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 Come Out?

The Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 release date has yet to be announced, but production on the final season concluded in early July. Season 4 production ended at the beginning of June 2025, with the season debuting on October 26, 2025.

The show’s return date has a lot to do with Paramount+’s remaining 2026 schedule. MobLand has returned; Tulsa King and Dexter: Resurrection will be back later this month; and the final season of Yellowjackets drops in November. The third season of Landman just started production, so that won’t be back until the middle of 2027 at the earliest.

Based on all the above information, our best guess is that Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 (and most likely The Madison Season 2) will debut in December 2026 or early 2027 on Paramount+.

When Will Mayor of Kingstown Be on Netflix?

No Paramount+? No problem! Multiple seasons of Mayor of Kingstown premiere Monday, October 5 on Netflix.

Mayor of Kingstown Season 5 Cast Info:

There’ll be a few new faces for the final season of Mayor of Kingstown. David Morse has joined the cast as seasoned FBI agent Russell Hardy, and Nestor Carbonell will portray Enrique Molina, a “boss who comes to Kingstown to avenge a relative who has gone missing.”

How To Watch Mayor of Kingstown On Paramount+:

The first four seasons of Mayor of Kingstown are streaming on Paramount+.

Paramount+ offers two subscription plans, Essential and Premium. Right now, you can get started with a seven-day free trial on the Premium plan.

The ad-supported Essential plan typically costs $8.99/month, while the ad-free Premium plan (which comes with Showtime titles and live CBS) costs $13.99/month.

How To Watch Paramount+ on Amazon and Hulu:

You can also add Paramount+ to your Hulu or Amazon accounts for $13.99/month. Both streamers offer free trials for new subscribers.
