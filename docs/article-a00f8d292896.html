Rescheduled Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade set for Saturday — but organizers will still face weather challenge

2026-11-26 20:18

While Saturday looks to have a “much improved forecast” with “lighter winds,” there is still set to be a breeze, said AccuWeather senior meteorologist Matt Benz.

“You could still see gusts of up to or maybe over 25 mph,” he told The Post.

The wind gust threshold that would stop the balloons from being allowed to float down Midtown is anything over 34 mph.

“I can only speculate what they’ll do with the balloons,” Benz added. “Maybe there is a window that they could be lowered, but it’s tough to say what they’ll do at this point.”

“The big difference between Friday and Saturday is on Friday, I think you could still see some gusts up to 30 mph, and the wind direction is probably a little bit more key,” he said.

“On Friday, the winds are out of the southwest, which when it aligns with the avenues in Manhattan, can create a canyon effect,” Benz explained.

Further storms are expected on Sunday night, delivering more rain, which could linger into Monday morning, according to the top meteorologist.

Next week, however, is expected to be far calmer.

Macy’s did not respond immediately to requests for comment.

The postponement of Thursday’s parade, the 100th of its kind, marked the first time Macy’s hadn’t held the iconic event on Thanksgiving since 1944 — which was the last of a three-year spell of cancellations due to World War II.

The parade — which follows a 2.5-mile route through Manhattan, starting near West 77th Street and Central Park West and ending at Macy’s Herald Square on West 34th Street — will begin at 8:30 a.m. Saturday.
