Taylor Swift shows her team spirit in red at Chiefs vs. Chargers game

2026-10-18 21:44

From the Academy to Arrowhead.

Taylor Swift supported husband Travis Kelce Sunday afternoon at Arrowhead Stadium in Kansas City less than 24 hours after performing at the Academy Museum Gala 2026 in Los Angeles.

At the game, Swift showed her spirit in a bright red Louis Vuitton turtleneck bodysuit and black pants with matching lipstick and gold nail polish.

She styled her hair in loose waves and wore gold Louis Vuitton hoop earrings, joined in the suite by close friend and Chiefs fan Caitlin Clark as well as Este Haim.

The Grammy winner, 36, performed at the glittering gala Saturday night, first hitting the red carpet in a sparkling blue Dior ballgown featuring exaggerated hips and a bead-covered bow.

She looked radiant, adding Chopard jewelry, including cluster earrings that totaled more than 32 carats of diamonds.

While inside the star-studded event, she performed a medley of hit songs like “The Fate of Ophelia” — the first live performance of her “Life of a Showgirl” single — as well as her “Toy Story 5” original, “I Knew It, I Knew You” and “Blank Space.”

For the performance, Swift again went sparkling blue, changing into a strapless Ralph Lauren gown and sapphire and diamond Cartier jewelry.

Meanwhile, Kelce was not in attendance ahead of the game against the Chargers. Swift has dutifully supported him at his home games this season, with this marking her third appearance.

She chose a red checked polo dress from Guest in Residence for her first game, hanging with Tom Cruise in the suite, and memorably shouted “that’s my husband!” when Kelce scored a touchdown at the Sept. 20 matchup against the Colts.

While she wore a $40 Chiefs tank top to the latter game, she pointed to her wedding ring stack, which cost more than $1 million.
