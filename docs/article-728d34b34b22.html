Wavytalk’s wet-to-straight flat iron is only $36 before Black Friday

2026-11-10 13:59

Your next good hair day could cost less than a dinner delivery, thanks to this Wavytalk flat iron that’s currently on sale for just $36 ahead of Black Friday on Nov. 27.

If your usual hair routine involves blow-drying, straightening and wondering how you somehow lost an entire hour in the bathroom, it’s time to meet your potential new favorite styling tool. The Wavytalk Wet to Straight Flat Iron is designed to take some of the hassle out of getting sleek, smooth hair, and right now, it’s a budget-friendly $36 on Amazon. That’s a *pretty sweet deal* to score before the Black Friday shopping frenzy officially kicks off.

What makes this flat iron worth a spot in your beauty lineup? For starters, it’s designed to work on both damp and dry hair, meaning you can streamline your styling routine without juggling multiple hot tools. It features 10 ventilation holes that help release moisture as you style, allowing you to go from damp hair to a straighter finish with fewer steps.

Wavytalk also claims the design can cut styling time by up to 20%, while providing 50% more protection.

The 1.75-inch-wide plates are another major perk. Their extra-wide design lets you tackle larger sections of hair with each pass, which can be a real time-saver if you have long, thick or naturally curly locks. Instead of spending forever working through tiny sections, you can get more coverage as you go.

There’s also plenty of room to customize your styling experience. The flat iron has nine temperature settings ranging from 290 to 450 degrees Fahrenheit, so you can choose a heat level that suits your hair and styling needs. It heats up in about 30 seconds, too, making it a convenient option when you’re rushing to get ready. Other thoughtful features include dual-voltage compatibility for travel, an eight-foot swivel cord, and a safety lock for easier storage.

And let’s talk about that price. At just $36, down from its $50 list price, the Wavytalk Wet to Straight Flat Iron is a tempting beauty bargain. The limited-time Amazon deal is available ahead of Black Friday on Nov. 27, so there’s no need to wait for the big shopping event to give your hair routine an upgrade.

Does it have an automatic shut-off and travel features?

Yes, it includes a 60-minute automatic shut-off safety feature, a handle lock and worldwide dual voltage support.

Is this flat iron suitable for short hair or curling?

The extra-wide 1.75-inch plates are designed for long or thick hair and are not ideal for tight curls or very short hair.

What accessories come in the box?

The box includes the 1.75-inch titanium flat iron, one heat-resistant styling glove and a flannel storage pouch.

What is the warranty and return policy?

It includes a one-year manufacturer warranty, extendable by 12 months upon registration, and is backed by standard 30-day retailer return policies.
