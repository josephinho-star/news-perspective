New details emerge about Tom Brady and ‘secretive’ Candice Swanepoel’s romance

2026-10-25 17:47

Tom Brady and Candice Swanepoel reportedly go through great lengths to not be seen together amid their whirlwind romance.

“They both live in Miami, so it’s very convenient,” an insider told People Sunday.

“They’re spending a lot of time together. Candice and Tom have been dating for months,” the source continued. “She’s been very secretive and selective about who she’s told about their dating.”

“When they have met up, they’ve gone [to great] lengths not to be spotted,” the source added.

A rep for Brady, 49, and Swanepoel, 38, weren’t immediately available to Page Six for comment.

The NFL alum and the Victoria’s Secret model’s whirlwind romance was revealed on Saturday, after an insider told Us Weekly they had been together for “a while.”

Brady has been romantically linked to many models and celebrities since his 2022 divorce from Gisele Bündchen, including Irina Shayk and Brooks Nader.

He’s also rumored to have had flings with Kim Kardashian, Sofía Vergara, Alix Earl and Yael Cohen.

However, the retired athlete has shied away from detailing his dating life, as he continues to amicably co-parent his and Bündchen’s two kids, Benjamin, 16, and Vivian, 13. He also shares son Jack, 19, with his ex-girlfriend Bridget Moynahan.

In January, Brady alluded that he was too busy to focus on dating after he was seen cozying up with Earle, 25, on New Year’s Eve.

“You know what? I don’t have much time for a personal life or much time for myself, but I love working and I love my kids,” he told People at the time.

“I like to be busy and try to really stay fulfilled in the things I’m doing,” he continued. “So I’m working on some really cool projects and I’m involved with some great teams of people that are doing some really positive things.”

As for Swanepoel, she is the mother of two sons, Anacã, 10, and Ariel, 8, with her ex-fiancé, Hermann Nicoli. The exes were together for more than 10 years before they called it quits in 2018.

“Life is a constant search for balance, for me at least, and yes I do sometimes feel overwhelmed, but I’m very in tune with my children’s needs, then my own personal needs, which are also important,” she said, explaining that she “realized” she needed “to be a better mother.”

“I know I need to also take care of myself, whether that be about my [physical] health or mental health, or being able to excel in my career and business because it brings me joy and so my children have begun to understand that too,” Swanepoel continued.
