Ukraine death toll hits 22 as rescuers pull bodies from rubble after Russian strike

2026-10-08 09:35

Published Oct. 8, 2026, 5:35 a.m. ET

See more of our coverage in your search results.

Rescuers searched through the rubble of a residential building hit by a Russian missile in the northern Ukrainian town of Pryluky on Thursday as the death toll rose to 22, including five children, Ukraine’s State Emergency Service said.

The strike Wednesday in the country’s Chernihiv region also injured 54 people, seven of them children, the agency said. In the immediate aftermath, rescuers had pulled 15 survivors out, including a child.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said that a cruise missile directly hit the building in Pryluky, some 150 kilometers (93 miles) east of Kyiv, the country’s capital.

“This is the kind of ‘gift’ Putin gave himself for his birthday,” Zelensky said in a nightly video address Wednesday. “The killing of children, the killing of people.”

Ukraine’s energy company DTEK said the national grid operator Ukrenergo had ordered emergency power outages in Kyiv, and explained that this is separate from regular, rolling outages scheduled across the country and would not be applied during those shutdowns.

Russia and Ukraine are locked in a long-range aerial war while their armies remain largely deadlocked on the front line, more than 4 1/2 years since Moscow’s full-scale invasion.

Late Wednesday, Russian drones struck Kyiv’s Obolonskyi and Desnianskyi districts, police said.

In Obolonskyi, north of central Kyiv, a drone hit a courtyard between apartment buildings, killing two people and injuring seven, including a 12-year-old boy and a 16-year-old girl.

In Desnianskyi, on the capital’s northeastern outskirts, a drone hit a residential building, killing two women and injuring five, among them girls aged 1 and 6.
