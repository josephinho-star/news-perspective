Kris Jenner stalking suspect arrested outside her Los Angeles home

2026-09-09 21:04

A suspect who has been accused of stalking Kris Jenner was reportedly arrested on Monday near the Los Angeles home of the reality TV icon.

According to NBC4 Los Angeles, 25-year-old Zenwilliam Robson was detained Monday evening by a security guard.

Robson was said to have been caught well before he was able to reach the mogul’s property, which is located in the Santa Monica Mountains.

According to the news outlet, security personnel told authorities that he’d attempted to contact the “Kardashians” star, 70, on multiple occasions in the past.

Per the outlet, Robson was subsequently arrested on suspicion of stalking by deputies from the Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department, and later charged with one count of trespassing and refusing to leave private property, a misdemeanor.

It’s unclear if the “Keeping Up with the Kardashians” personality was home at the time of the incident.

Reps for Jenner did not return our request for comment.

Though the individual’s identity was not revealed, ex-husband Kanye West once claimed via Instagram that “YESTERDAY KIM ACCUSED ME OF PUTTING A HIT OUT ON HER.”

Kendall Jenner, meanwhile, obtained restraining orders against separate individuals in 2018 and 2021.

In the latter case, she was granted a temporary order after Noe Flores allegedly showed up to her Los Angeles home several times, sent her unsolicited gifts and attempted to scale a fence at her residence.

As for Kris, Robson isn’t the first alleged stalker to be arrested. Back in 2017, a fired security guard named Joshua Jacobs was arrested after allegedly breaking into her gated Hidden Hills, California community in an attempt to reach her.
