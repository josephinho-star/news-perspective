Chinese engineers reduce cargo ship fuel use by 5% with high-speed torpedo tech

2026-11-08 12:00

Bubble-lubrication technology allows torpedoes and other projectiles to travel at extreme speeds under water in a gas-filled cavity

Beyond torpedoes, supercavitating projectiles can streak through the water at blistering speeds to intercept naval mines, incoming torpedoes and unmanned underwater vehicles, and even combat divers.

Now, Chinese engineers are applying a related drag-reduction principle to commercial shipping, with a bubble-lubrication system cutting net energy consumption.
