Ariana Grande to lay bare personal life in deeply intimate project — months after taking extended hiatus from public work

2026-11-24 19:35

Ariana Grande is set to release “The Eternal Sunshine Diaries: The Making of the Tour” after taking an extended hiatus from public work.

The “Petal” songstress’ forthcoming intimate two-part documentary, set for release via Peacock on Dec. 11, will show the hitmaker’s first 30 days leading up to the start of her “Eternal Sunshine” tour, the streaming platform announced Tuesday.

Viewers will watch Grande prepare to return to the stage on June 6 in Oakland, California, and wrap up production of her Grammy nominated album “Petal.”

The pop star, 33, released her eighth studio album on July 31 while she was touring.

The second part of the documentary will follow Grande throughout her tour, which marked the first since her “Sweetener” world tour in 2019.

Peacock described the project as “a cinematic celebration of healing, connection, and the music that has defined [Grande’s] life.”

The streaming platform claimed that audience members will see “through the collapse of [Grande’s] reality,” including “a cosmic rebirth, a reconnection with her inner child, and the memories that shaped her.”

The “We Can’t Be Friends (Wait for Your Love)” songstress wrapped up her tour in September and briefly took a step back from the limelight as she faced public scrutiny.

“Ariana will be taking a step back from visibility after she completes the ‘Eternal Sunshine’ tour,” her rep told People in August.

“She looks forward to finishing the tour and ending it on a high note, both healthily and happily, and then taking a much-deserved break from public-facing work and appearances, which has led to endless, ongoing public scrutiny,” the spokesperson added.

Grande’s rep described her tour as “a beautiful experience for her,” saying, “She loves her fans and has loved every minute of this tour so much.”

At the time, the “Yes, And?” singer also pulled out of the West End revival of “Sunday in the Park with George.”

That same month, Grande reassured fans at her Chicago tour stop that she had the hiatus planned for a while, saying the decision wasn’t an “impulsive thing.”

The actress has spent her off-time with her boyfriend, Ricky Álvarez, whom she reconnected with around June. The couple initially dated from 2015 to 2016.
