The life of a model is a 24-hour job that is demanding, brutal and restless

2026-09-10 03:05

Model’s life no easy feet

Women drive cabs, run businesses, make babies, con guys — and now come by-the-part.

Today, models push any product. Like, to hustle hair goo or thigh cream, you can now rent models that specialize in upper leg parts and thighs.

Agencies now rent specialists in ankles, fingers, scalps, teeth. Name it, they package it.

Hustling shoes? Shove feet that look like a dented fender, find a pair with a ballerina’s toes.

These specialists make good money. One whose lower half decorated almost every monthly magazine for stockings, nail polish, body lotion, hair removers, shorts, panties, razors, bathing suits told me: “So much work that I can’t handle it.”

Those great legs climbed up to her earrings. She received me strictly in the john, saying, “When horse trainers want to tone muscles of their thoroughbreds, they run them in the surf. Icy, salty water is the best toner. Summers it’s the beach. Winter, I stand in the shower and my hose attachment shpritzes water against my legs.”

Hand model Martha: “It’s 24-hour care. More babying than other parts of your body.”

She offered her wet wrist to shake amid a dozen bottles of nail colors, oils, cotton, files, emery boards, scissors, nippers, lotions, orange sticks, cuticle cream, base coat, sealer, fast drying spray and surgical equipment.

“I do my nails daily. Reapplied polish eight times one day in back of a cab. Oval was popular, now it’s squared tips. Housewives want pink with half moons showing. For glamour it’s rounded and pearlized.”

She flicked the light switch with her elbow, dialed the phone via a pencil’s eraser, manipulated a girdle with the balls of oiled cuticles, creamed incessantly with something her mom cooks up from cocoa butter, and carries in her evening bag.

Hair model Pat: “Shampoo every other night so it doesn’t get limp, stringy, lose bounce. Sundays oil treatment. Nightly brush with a special English pack of natural bristles set in hard rubber. Stiff nylon creates electricity, which snaps hairs off. Never brush or comb when hair’s wet. Breaks the hair. Forget sun. Never leave hair uncovered or it can burn. Use only rubber tip bobbies that don’t tear. Get a French teasing comb which has soft teeth.

“Also allow an hour and a half to dress hair for work and same amount to get it undressed for bed.”

Foot models require an extra encyclopedia. It’s corns off toes, callouses off heels and clumsy guys off their hoofs.

First rule? Dangle in soapy water. Elevate it to shrink its size. Prop against a wall, stick them up for 10 minute intervals, or the flesh puffs up around it like yeast. Otherwise, they may have to cut you physically out of your shoe.”

The foot model’s one wish?

ENGRAVED on a Long Island tombstone: Women come into your life with a roar. Noisy, excitable, unpredictable. And then — when they leave — they take your house.

Maybe possibly could be only in New York, kids, only in New York.
