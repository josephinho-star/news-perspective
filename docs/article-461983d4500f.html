Is the Dyson Airwrap worth the money? Its early Prime Day sale price might make you buy it

2026-09-29 17:03

Whenever people see me with that hair tool with all of its attachments spread out on my vanity, I always get asked, “Is it worth it?”

It’s a valid question. After all, our moms didn’t have a turbo-curl spinner that cost upwards of $650 back in the day. They likely used things like hot rollers, but that’s a conversation for another article.

My short answer to the above query? Yes. And now is the time to find out for yourself because the viral hair styler is on sale.

Before October Prime Day falls upon us on Oct. 6, the Dyson Airwrap i.d. Multi-Styler is 20% off, which is a sign the universe wants us to upgrade our at-home styling tools. I’ve tested more than I can count and this highly praised system is durable, yields bouncy waves and allows you to skip a day or two of styling because your hairstyle will really hold.

Sorry, your straightener and curling iron may be left on read in your bathroom cabinet. Encased in a gorgeous organizer that makes traveling a breeze, the Dyson Airwrap is lightweight, comes with a variety of attachments to suit all hair types and doesn’t require any other tool to be used before or after.

I put the Dyson Airwrap head-to-head (or should I say hair-to-hair?) with the Shark FlexStyle in my review, and both are excellent. I’d recommend picking up the Dyson Airwrap when it’s on sale. You’ll save, plus it’s a bit more powerful than Shark’s styler.

Why spend $50 each year on a random blow dryer, only for it to break or fry your hair after a few months?The ceramic pink color makes getting ready feel like you’re in a 90s movie and it’s a worthy upgrade to simplify your routine.

With any beauty investment, the choice is yours to make. But if you want to skip the salons (side note: I don’t remember the last time I had a blowout appointment!), then the Dyson Airwrap is completely justifiable. Even more so now that it’s one of the best early Prime Day beauty deals.

What attachments come with the Dyson Airwrap i.d. Straight + Wavy set?

This model includes six styling attachments: a Conical Barrel, 1.6-inch Long Barrel, Round Volumizing Brush, Soft Smoothing Brush, Dryer + Flyaway Smoother and Fast Dryer.

How does the Bluetooth i.d. curl feature work?

It pairs with the MyDyson app via Bluetooth to automatically control wrapping time, active heat styling and cold shot duration.

How do you use the Airwrap i.d. to get a sleek, straight blowout?

Pre-dry hair to 80%, lift and smooth sectioned hair with the Soft Smoothing Brush, and lock it in with a cold shot.

Why is it best to buy the Airwrap before Prime Day?

Coveted colors sell out quickly, and early promotional price drops typically match the official Prime Day discounts anyway.

Why is the Dyson Airwrap worth the investment?

It protects hair shine using Coanda airflow instead of extreme heat and replaces six separate styling tools.

The device includes a two-year manufacturer’s limited warranty, and purchases on Amazon are subject to Amazon’s standard 30-day return policy.

Your Prime Big Deal Days FAQs, answered

What is Prime Big Deal Days and when is it?

Prime Big Deal Days — fondly known as October Prime Day — is Amazon’s biggest fall sale, offering massive discounts before the holiday rush of Black Friday and Cyber Monday. October Prime Day runs from Tuesday, Oct. 6 to Wednesday, Oct. 7, 2026.

Prime Day vs. Prime Big Deal Days

Prime Day and Prime Big Deal Days are both Prime member-exclusive seasonal sales with massive discounts on rotating bestsellers. Unlike July’s Prime Day, Prime Big Deal Days runs every October as a precursor to the busy holiday season, allowing shoppers to start saving on gift shopping early.

Do you have to be an Amazon Prime member to shop?

October Prime Day is exclusively available to Prime members. However interested shoppers can grab a 30-day free trial to try Prime, and its many benefits with no strings attached, and gain access to exclusive deals and the convenience of fast, free shipping.
