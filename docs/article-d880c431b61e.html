Keegan Allen posts painful photo after getting brutally robbed in LA: ‘I’m on the mend’

2026-10-12 01:36

The “Pretty Little Liars” alum posted a close-up photo of his neck while wearing a necklace with a cross on Instagram Sunday; his skin still a painful-looking red from the armed robbery he fell victim to.

Allen wrote, “Thank you all for such kind and caring messages. I’m on the mend.”

“Really grateful to God, law enforcement, medical and my friends and following for being so supportive,” he added.

The actor, 37, said he would keep his fans updated and concluded, “God bless and be safe.”

Allen also posted a pic of himself at the hospital on his Instagram Stories, thanking the doctors, nurses and security at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center.

On Thursday night, he posted shocking video of his bloodied face after getting robbed outside of high-end grocery store Lauren Supply.

“These two dudes hit me in the head and pointed a gun at me with a knife and then took off,” Allen, appearing dazed and out of breath, said on Instagram moments after the ambush.“They had masks on and took my watch. They took my watch. Dammit.”

“West Hollywood is not safe anymore,” he captioned the video. “Robbed at gunpoint and knifepoint by two young men that threatened to KILL me over my watch and would have!”

“God said no tonight,” he continued. “My life for a watch.”

Security footage of the robbery Allen posted shows one suspected robber wearing a mask striking him in the head multiple times with a gun, while the other holds him at knifepoint.

The two men fled once they snatched only Allen’s watch, though did not take his bracelet and his ring.

On Saturday, Allen showed the stitches he had to get above his left eyebrow after the terrifying attack. The “Waker” star shared that he was “healing.”

“Thank you to medical team at [sic] and law enforcement and everyone reaching out ❤️,” he wrote.

A rep for the LASD told Page Six that a call for service was placed for a “robbery.”

“Upon arrival, deputies contacted a male adult who stated he was approached by two male adults dressed in black clothing. One suspect pointed a silver handgun at the victim while the other pointed a knife and demanded the victim’s watch and bracelet,” the rep said in part.
