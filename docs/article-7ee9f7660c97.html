Carney to make first bilateral visit to Turkey by a Canadian leader

2026-10-07 09:01

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney will visit Turkey on October 21-23, his office confirmed Tuesday, in the first bilateral visit by a Canadian leader to the NATO ally.

Carney will hold talks with President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan in Ankara and meet business leaders in İstanbul.

He has sought to expand Canada’s economic ties overseas to reduce its dependence on the United States.

In July, Carney made the first visit to Saudi Arabia by a Canadian prime minister in 25 years. He has also visited India and China, seeking to repair ties strained during the tenure of his predecessor, Justin Trudeau.

Carney called Turkey “an important NATO ally and G20 partner, with enormous potential for greater economic cooperation.”

“We are deepening our partnership to build greater certainty, security, and prosperity for both our peoples,” he said in a statement.
