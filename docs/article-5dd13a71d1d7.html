Who is Savannah Chrisley’s fiancé Jason Galanis? All about the fraudster once dubbed ‘Porn’s New King’

2026-10-27 21:17

On Oct. 27, the reality TV star announced that she was engaged to a man named Jason, who was later identified by Us Weekly. She also shared that they are expecting their first child together.

The two have been engaged since December 2025, after he popped the question just five months after they started dating.

“I’ve prayed for this life. I’ve cried for this life. And there were moments I truly wondered if this life would ever be mine. 🥹🤍,” Chrisley, 29, wrote in the caption of her Instagram announcement, accompanied by a video of her and her fiancé celebrating her pregnancy.

“The past few years have taken me through fires I never imagined I’d have to walk through. Seasons of heartbreak, loss, and moments when I didn’t know if I would ever make it to the other side,” the reality star, 29, added.

In the emotional caption, she called Jason her “protector” and “fiercest fighter,” as well as her “most loyal person.”

But it seems like her new beau has quite the colorful past, as he was dubbed “Porn’s New King” for taking part in a $60 million scam and has served years of federal prison time.

So, who is Jason Galanis? Here’s everything you need to know about Chrisley’s new beau.

Galanis was convicted of running several financial fraud schemes

One of the schemes involved defrauding the Oglala Sioux tribe in South Dakota. Galanis and his co-conspirators, which included his father, John, persuaded a native American tribal entity to issue them bonds, promising that they would invest the money appropriately for them.

However, instead of investing the money as promised, Galanis and his co-conspirators diverted more than $38 million into an account that he controlled. Galanis personally spent more than $8.5 million on homes, cars, jewelry and travel.

In addition, he was convicted of running two other schemes. One involved manipulating Gerova Financial Group’s stock and defrauding clients of an investment advisory firm.

He also participated in a separate scheme to defraud investment advisory firm TAG Virgin Islands, Inc.

He was ordered to pay nearly $162 million in restitution, according to the New York Times.

Galanis’ prison sentence was commuted by President Donald Trump in 2025

The President’s order authorized for the “immediate release” of Galanis, who was serving his sentence at a corrections center in Montgomery, Alabama.

The commutation also ensured that he was relieved of financial penalties.

Chrisley’s parents, Todd and Julie, were also pardoned by Trump in May 2025 after they were convicted in May 2022 of conspiring to commit bank fraud and evade taxes.

He has ties to Hunter Biden

Galanis was an associate of Hunter Biden and testified in March 2024 before the House Oversight Committee in March 2024 as part of the House Republican’s inquiry to impeach then-President Joe Biden.

Galanis, who testified virtually from behind bars, previously worked with Hunter and his business associate, Devon Archer, on investment ventures.

During his testimony, he said that he was present for a May 2014 phone call that took place between former President Joe Biden, Russian billionaire Yelena Baturina, and other business associates.

Galanis claimed that the former President told Baturina to “be good to my boy,” in reference to Hunter.

He also made new claims about the Biden family’s relationships in China and Ukraine, including their investments.
