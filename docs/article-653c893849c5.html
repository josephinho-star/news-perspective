Green Leader Zack Polanski pitches for Keir Starmer’s old seat

2026-09-03 08:33

LONDON — Green Party Leader Zack Polanski announced Thursday he wants to stand as a candidate in the Holborn and St Pancras by-election to succeed Keir Starmer.

Polanski, who took over the left-wing party last year, told the Camden New Journal he hopes to represent the Greens in the central London contest — a shot across Labour’s bows in what has previously been considered a safe seat for the governing party.

“I have lived and worked in this community over many years, I know it well and I love it,” the Green leader said. “It represents so many of the things that make London so special. I am so excited by the prospect of representing this place in Parliament.”
